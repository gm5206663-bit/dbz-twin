## COLD OPEN

The end of the stretcher disappears through the aircraft door. Beyond the broken crater, the last light lies across a ridge of bare stone.

**THE YEAR AHEAD**

## 1 · THREE INJURIES

**NARRATOR:** Raditz is dead, but his warning remains. Two more Saiyans are coming, and Earth has approximately one year to prepare.

Roshi and Krillin lift Goku the rest of the way into the aircraft. Gohan holds the blanket clear of the door while Goshin steadies the frame with his good hand.

Goku catches his breath as they set the stretcher down. Bulma fastens it to the floor, then checks the strap across his legs. Gohan pulls the blanket clear of Goku's ribs, leaving his father's hands free above it where he can reach them.

"We'll go back to the island," she says. "I can land where we took off."

"What about Father?" Gohan asks. "His ribs still hurt."

"I know," Krillin says. "We need to get him a Senzu Bean."

Goku opens his eyes. "Korin."

"Right. I'll go to the tower and ask him." Krillin sees Goshin looking at him. "What is it?"

"Will he have any?" Goshin asks.

"I don't know how many he's got," Krillin says. "I'll find out."

Roshi folds a blanket beneath Goku's shoulder. "We'll take the family to Kame House. Meet us there as soon as you can."

"And call Chi-Chi the moment you get in."

"We will."

Gohan comes closer to Krillin. "Can the beans help Goshin too?"

"They helped your father after the tournament. I'll ask for enough for all three of you."

Goshin holds up his bandaged hand. "And my head?"

"Tell me where he hit you," Krillin says.

"Tell me where he hit you," Goshin repeats. He touches the side of his hair with his good hand. "Here. I was asleep when he carried us away."

Krillin kneels beside him. Goshin parts his own hair, and Krillin looks at the sore place without touching him.

"I'll tell Korin that too."

Bulma closes the medical case. The scouter and its cell are inside, wrapped separately in her handkerchief.

"Keep that thing off," Krillin says.

"The cell is out," Bulma answers. "I'm not putting it back until I know what the transmitter does."

Goshin looks at the case. Bulma catches his eye. "What is it?"

"Can it find the other two Saiyans?"

"Raditz used it to find strong fighters," Goku says. "It found us on the island."

"If I can make it safe, maybe I can find Yamcha and Tien with it," Bulma says. "First I have to work out what the writing means."

Gohan touches the Four-Star Ball on his hat. "They heard about Grandpa's ball."

"And the others," Roshi says. "We mustn't let them get all seven."

Goshin reaches toward the closed case, then stops.

"Let's get your father settled first," Bulma says. "Then we can make a plan."

Krillin steps back through the aircraft door. The crater wall rises behind him, cut by the path of Piccolo's final attack.

Goku lifts a hand from the blanket. "Thanks, Krillin."

"Save your breath. I'll be back."

Krillin rises above the crater and turns toward Korin Tower. Gohan watches until he passes over the ridge.

## 2 · THE ARM

On the far side of the crater, Piccolo stops against a shelf of rock. The cloth he held against his left shoulder is dark and torn.

His sleeve hangs empty from the shoulder down. From where he stands, the crater wall hides him from the people at the aircraft.

Piccolo plants his feet. His right hand closes, and the stone beneath his boots begins to shake.

A new arm pushes out from his shoulder. He draws a sharp breath through his teeth as the sleeve tears farther open, then drops to one knee with both hands against the ground.

Dust settles around him. He flexes the fingers of his left hand and stands, slowly, using the wall for support.

His right fingers leave four narrow grooves in the rock as he pushes himself upright. The new arm hangs loose for a moment before he lifts it level with his shoulder.

From above the ridge comes the sound of an aircraft engine. Piccolo turns toward it and sees the vehicle still on the ground. He pauses until the engine settles into a steady rhythm, then moves toward the edge to see who remains below.

"Kakarot."

He raises his new arm and turns the hand over. The fingers close once more.

"One of your sons already knows how to use his power. The other nearly broke Raditz in half."

Piccolo looks toward the aircraft again.

"Those two Saiyans won't wait for them to grow up."

He lifts from the rock and flies back over the ridge.

## 3 · A REQUEST

A stamp comes down on a sheet of paper, and a clerk slides another forward before the ink has dried.

At the end of a long desk, King Yemma looks over a line of figures waiting beneath him. Kami stands apart from the queue, holding his staff in both hands.

The desk is piled with ledgers, and red stamps hang from hooks along one side. When the line advances, a clerk slides the next page beneath Yemma's hand.

"Earth's guardian," Yemma says. "You aren't here for judgment, are you?"

"No, Great King. I have come to ask for help."

"That's a different window."

Kami remains where he is. "A Saiyan called Raditz was killed on Earth. Did he come before you?"

Yemma puts down his stamp. "Long hair? A temper?"

"That would be him."

"He came through here, all right. He tried to fight me when I gave my judgment."

"And what happened?"

"I held him down and sent him to Hell. Next question."

Kami's hands tighten around his staff. "Piccolo was the one who killed him. His spirit reached you?"

"I just said it did. Do you want me to fetch the record?"

"No. Thank you."

Yemma turns a page. "What is it you want, Guardian? There are other people waiting."

"Two more Saiyans are coming. The fighters on Earth barely stopped the first. I hoped King Kai might help us prepare."

"King Kai?"

"A man named Goku has defended Earth before. He is strong, and he will train as hard as he can."

Yemma runs a finger down the page. "Son Goku. I don't have him here."

"He is still alive. He was badly injured in the fight."

Yemma looks down at Kami. "You brought me a request for a man who isn't dead?"

"I am not asking you to declare him dead. I am asking whether he might be allowed to seek King Kai's training."

A figure in the queue coughs. Yemma reaches past the ledger and stamps a waiting page without looking at it.

"I'm not promising your man a road today," he says. "He'd have to want it, and he'd have to be fit enough to make the trip."

"I understand."

"If you want me to consider it, come back when you can tell me both."

Kami bows his head. "May I at least see the road?"

"You can see the beginning. That is not the same as permission for him to use it."

Yemma calls for a guide. A small figure appears at the side of the desk, carrying a case under one arm.

"Show him the entrance," Yemma says. "Then bring him back."

"Yes, Great King."

Kami steps aside as the next person reaches the desk. The stamp comes down again.

## 4 · PICCOLO RETURNS

The aircraft engine is running when a shadow crosses its open door. Goshin turns from the stretcher as Piccolo lands between the vehicle and the crater wall.

Roshi steps down onto the ground. "What do you want?"

"Where are the boys?"

"With their father."

Gohan comes to the doorway beside Goshin. Bulma follows them, and her hand catches the edge of the door when she sees Piccolo's left arm.

"Your arm grew back. Like a lizard's tail."

Piccolo looks past her. "I intend to take both boys with me."

"We're taking Goku home," Roshi says. "You're not taking his children anywhere."

"Your home won't stop the Saiyans."

"Neither could we, without everyone else," Goshin says.

"You saw what Raditz did. The next two are stronger. If you want this planet to survive, you need training."

Goku raises his head from the stretcher. "Leave them alone, Piccolo."

"You can't even get up. How much time do you think you have to teach them?"

"That's for their parents to decide," Bulma says.

Gohan moves back toward Goku. "I want to stay with Father."

Gohan glances back through the open doorway, where his father's hand has tightened around the blanket beside the stretcher.

Piccolo raises his restored arm. "The one who flies can use his power already. The other only brings his out for an instant. He comes first."

Gohan's feet lift from the ground. His hands close around his collar as he rises toward Piccolo's outstretched fingers.

"Let me down!"

Goshin steps between Piccolo and the aircraft. "Put him down."

Piccolo turns his hand. Gohan moves with it, still above the ground, and Goshin watches the turn of Piccolo's fingers.

Goshin reaches for his brother's sleeve but stops short. For an instant Gohan hangs between them, his feet pointing toward the open crater.

Goshin raises his own unbandaged hand and copies the motion. Gohan jerks toward him, but Piccolo snaps his arm toward the empty ridge. Gohan's hat tilts as the motion pulls him sideways, and he reaches toward Goshin without letting go of his collar.

Piccolo's eyes follow Goshin's hand as his restored fingers close. Gohan shoots across the crater.

"Gohan!" Goku calls.

Goshin flies after him. He reaches with his open hand again, and Gohan's course pulls sideways, just enough to carry him away from the centre of the rock face.

The ridge rushes toward them. Gohan throws both arms in front of his head and twists, trying to turn away from the stone.

Light bursts from him.

The cliff breaks open. Goshin catches Gohan before he reaches it, and both boys tumble through the air as a cloud rises behind them.

## 5 · THE FALLING STONE

Goshin carries his brother clear of the first falling rocks. Gohan keeps one arm around him while pieces of stone strike the crater floor below.

A great slab rolls from the broken ridge and drops toward the aircraft. Bulma reaches the door as Roshi moves beside her.

"There!" Gohan points. "It'll hit Father!"

Goshin lands him on a clear patch of ground. Gohan gets to his feet and waves both arms toward the aircraft.

"Get down!"

Roshi pulls Bulma behind the door. Goshin lifts into the air beside the falling slab, with an empty wall of the crater beyond it.

He turns before he fires, keeping the aircraft behind his shoulder. His hands come together at his side, and light spreads between his fingers.

"Kamehameha!"

The wave catches the slab from the side. Stone breaks apart under the force and veers away from the aircraft, followed by the wide blue stream.

The wave wavers. Its edge cuts a gouge through the empty wall before Goshin turns his wrists and sends it farther across the crater.

Fragments strike the ground beyond the broken pod. A second piece follows the slab toward the aircraft, but it breaks against the ground well short of the open door. Goshin lowers his hands, and the singed end of his bandage flutters against his wrist.

Gohan runs to him. "Are you hurt?"

"My hand. Are you?"

"I think so. I broke that rock."

Goshin looks at the torn ridge, then back toward Piccolo. "He threw you at it."

Piccolo stands at the edge of the field. He watches both boys return together, then glances at the broken stone.

"You see?" he says. "That power comes out when you're frightened, and then it disappears. You need to learn to use it."

Gohan stops beside Roshi. His shoulder is marked with dust, and his hands shake when he lowers them.

"You threw me."

"And you're alive."

"You could have hurt him," Goshin says.

"Raditz would have killed him. The next two won't give him time to ask nicely."

Gohan looks at his brother, then faces Piccolo again. "I don't want to be a fighter. I want to be a scholar."

"Be one after the Saiyans are dead. There won't be a school left if they destroy the planet."

"I know they're coming. I'm still allowed to say no to you."

Piccolo's new hand closes. "Your father is too soft to teach you what you need."

"He didn't throw me at a cliff," Goshin says.

"A few tricks won't save you."

Goshin raises his unbandaged hand. "You didn't get Gohan."

Gohan steps level with him. Roshi comes up on their other side, with Bulma and the aircraft behind them.

Piccolo looks at the raised hand and then at the scorched bandage. His restored fingers tighten and tremble; he watches the brothers standing together and lowers his hand.

"You don't have a year to waste," he says. "When the Saiyans arrive, don't expect me to rescue you."

"We didn't ask you to," Gohan says.

Piccolo rises from the crater. He passes over the broken ridge without turning toward the aircraft and disappears beyond it.

Goshin keeps his hand raised until he is gone. Roshi crosses the space between the boys and the aircraft, looking up at the ridge and the broken stones beneath it. Then Goshin bends his fingers and winces.

"Both of you, inside," Bulma says.

"Both of you, inside," Goshin repeats, and follows Gohan into the aircraft.

## 6 · OVER THE WATER

Gohan reaches the stretcher first. Goku looks at the dust on his clothes and grips the edge of the blanket.

"Are you hurt?"

"My shoulder," Gohan says. "Goshin caught me before I hit the rock."

Goshin stands on the other side of the stretcher, watching his father.

"Piccolo tried to take you because of those Saiyans," Goku says.

"He said he was going to train us."

"I told you he wasn't after you. I was wrong. I'm sorry."

Gohan puts his hand over Goku's on the blanket. "We came back."

"You did. But Piccolo doesn't get to take either of you."

"He said I have to fight," Gohan says.

"We have to do something about those Saiyans. But your mother and I will talk to you both about what happens next."

"Even if I don't want to be a fighter?"

"Even then."

Bulma closes the door. The aircraft lifts over the broken crater and turns toward the sea.

Roshi unwraps Goshin's bandage while the boys sit beside Goku. Gohan sits where he can see his father and the window, keeping one hand on the stretcher whenever the aircraft turns. The cloth is scorched, but Goshin can still bend his fingers.

"I used my hands again," he says. "There was a big rock."

"I saw it. Let me put a clean wrap on."

A roll of gauze slips from Roshi's knee and starts toward the floor. Goshin opens his good hand and turns it the way Piccolo did.

The roll lifts a little, wobbles, and drops against his fingers. He catches it before it falls.

Roshi looks at the roll. "How did you do that?"

"Piccolo moved his hand this way," Goshin says. He repeats the small turn.

"That's what you did when Gohan was in the air?"

"I tried."

Roshi takes the gauze and wraps Goshin's palm again. Outside the window, the last of the land disappears behind them.

"Is there a telephone in here?" Gohan asks.

"No," Bulma calls from the cockpit. "I'll get us back to Kame House as fast as I can."

"Then we'll call your mother," Roshi says. "She'll hear what happened from us."

## 7 · THE MACHINE

At the mountain house, Chi-Chi sets the washed dishes in their places. The Ox-King stands in the doorway, turning his hat between his hands.

The returned test paper remains where she left it, with the red circle showing at the edge. A chair stands a little apart from the table, its back turned toward the door. Chi-Chi smooths the corner of the paper before going to the telephone, then checks that the door latch is fastened.

"I'll call Master Roshi's house," she says.

"That's a good idea. They'll tell you when they're coming."

Chi-Chi lifts the receiver and dials. The wheel returns after each number, clicking against the stop.

The line rings. The Ox-King comes a little closer when a voice finally answers.

"*You have reached the house of Master Roshi. Nobody is available to speak to you at present.*"

Chi-Chi holds the receiver against her ear. The recorded voice continues, mentioning messages for the household and for Krillin, and then the tone sounds.

She puts the receiver down.

"The machine," she says.

"Then they're out," says the Ox-King. "Or they're all at the table and haven't heard it."

"Goku knows what time he was supposed to bring them home."

"A few hours with his old friends won't hurt him."

"I didn't say I was worried about him having friends."

Chi-Chi folds a cloth on the edge of the table. The Ox-King sets his hat down beside it.

"They're probably teaching the boys those rough games again," she says. "Goku calls it playing, and then somebody gets kicked."

"A little rough play is good for children."

"What if one of them gets kicked into the sea?"

"Goku can swim."

"And if a shark comes after them? They could wash up on an island, and then they'd be hungry, and one of them would reach for a banana."

The Ox-King looks at the folded cloth. "There are worse things to reach for."

"Not if there's a snake in the tree. Suppose it bites them, and the poison goes all the way to their heads."

"Chi-Chi."

"They've worked hard at their lessons. What happens if they can't remember anything afterward?"

Her father reaches for his hat again. "They're with Goku. He'll look after them."

Chi-Chi looks at the telephone. She picks it up and dials the exchange to ask about the line, and a woman answers her.

"The line's all right," the woman says. "I rang your house myself this week."

"Then why is nobody answering at Kame House?"

"You said the machine answered. There's a lot of those about."

Chi-Chi thanks her and replaces the receiver. The telephone hangs against the wall, silent again.

"I didn't leave a message," she says.

"You can call back."

"And what would I say?"

## 8 · TWO ROADS

The guide opens a gate. Beyond it, a great serpent's head rises above the clouds, its mouth open toward a road that winds out of sight.

Kami steps through and stops beside the guide. The clouds below the road move without showing what lies beneath them.

The path bends behind the clouds and returns to view farther away, narrower than the guide's finger. There is no railing along its edge, only scales rising from the long curved surface.

"That is Snake Way," the guide says. "It leads to King Kai."

"How far?"

"The tradition says about a million kilometres."

Kami looks along the first curve. "Has anyone reached the end?"

"I believe King Yemma is the only one to do so in the past hundred million years."

"Only Yemma?"

"As far as I know."

The guide places a hand on the gatepost. "If someone falls into the clouds, there is Hell below. Please tell your fighter not to fall."

"If he is given permission."

"Yes. If he is given permission."

Kami rests both hands on his staff. The road disappears into the distance, then appears once more as a narrow line above the clouds.

"One year," he says. "And first he must recover."

"Would you like to go farther?"

"No. Earth needs its guardian there."

The guide closes the gate behind them.

Far below another stretch of sky, Krillin flies up alongside Korin Tower. Its narrow column reaches through the clouds, and the round roof above him grows larger as he rises.

He lands at the edge of the platform. The clouds thin below him, leaving the tower's column and the green forest visible for a moment beneath his feet.

Krillin grips the railing and catches his breath before calling, "Master Korin!"

Korin comes out from behind a pot. Yajirobe follows him, wiping his mouth on his sleeve.

"Krillin?" Korin says. "What brings you here?"

"Goku's been hurt. We need Senzu Beans."

Yajirobe stops wiping his mouth. "Goku? What happened to him?"

"A Saiyan called Raditz attacked him and took his sons. We got the boys back, but all three of them were hurt."

Korin sets his staff against the wall. "Where are they now?"

"Bulma's taking them to Kame House. Can you spare three beans?"

"Let me see what I have."

Korin opens a jar and reaches inside. He counts three beans into a small cloth bag, then pulls the string tight. Korin replaces the lid on the jar before handing over the bag, and Krillin checks that the string is tight.

"Here. Make sure each of them eats one."

"Thank you."

"You said a Saiyan," Korin says. "Is he still after them?"

"Raditz is dead. He said two stronger ones are coming in about a year."

Yajirobe lowers his hand. "Two more?"

"I'll explain everything when Goku's safe."

Krillin tucks the bag inside his shirt and rises from the platform. Korin watches him drop below the roof and turn toward the sea.

## 9 · THE FIRST TELEPHONE

Bulma brings the aircraft down on the same stretch of sand where it took off. Roshi opens the door before the engine is completely still.

"We're back," he calls.

Turtle comes around the corner of Kame House. He stops when he sees the stretcher.

"What happened?"

"Goku's hurt," Roshi says. "Move those chairs so we can get him to the bed."

Gohan and Goshin go ahead to open the house door. Inside, Turtle moves the chairs while Bulma and Roshi carry Goku to a bed.

Gohan tucks the blanket around his father. Goshin takes the medical case and places it beside the bed, keeping the wrapped scouter inside.

"The telephone," Gohan says.

"I'm going now," Roshi answers.

He picks up the receiver and dials the mountain house. Gohan stands on one side of him, and Goshin stands on the other.

A telephone rings at the mountain house.

Chi-Chi lifts the receiver before the second ring. "Hello?"

"Chi-Chi? It's Master Roshi."

"Where are they?"

"Goku and both boys are here with me. They're alive."

The Ox-King turns toward her. Chi-Chi grips the cord with her free hand.

"What do you mean, alive? What's happened?"

"A man attacked us. He took Gohan and Goshin, but we got them back. Goku was badly hurt in the fight."

"How badly hurt?"

"His ribs were crushed. Krillin has gone to Korin Tower for Senzu Beans. We're looking after Goku until he gets back."

The Ox-King steadies the table as Chi-Chi pulls the telephone cord taut, her other hand pressed against the wood.

"And the boys?"

"They're awake. Gohan hurt his shoulder. Goshin burned his hand and was knocked unconscious by a blow to the head."

"Let me speak to them."

Roshi lowers the receiver to Gohan. The cord stretches across the edge of the table.

"Mother?" Gohan says.

"Gohan. Can you hear me?"

"Yes. Father is here. He told us to call you."

"Does your shoulder still hurt?"

"A little. Goshin is here too."

Gohan passes the receiver across. Goshin takes it with his unbandaged hand.

"Mother?"

"I'm here. Does your head hurt? Are you dizzy?"

"It hurts where Raditz hit me. I'm not dizzy now."

"And your hand?"

"Master Roshi wrapped it again."

"Both of you stay close to your father. Don't go anywhere with anybody else."

"Both of you stay close to your father. Don't go anywhere with anybody else," Goshin repeats. "Mother, the man was Father's brother. His name was Raditz."

Chi-Chi's hand leaves the cord. "Goku's brother?"

"He won't come back," Goshin says. "Piccolo killed him."

Roshi reaches for the receiver. Goshin gives it to him, and Chi-Chi listens without putting hers down.

"Raditz said two more Saiyans are coming," Roshi says. "He said they'll be here in about a year. We don't know the exact day."

"Are they there now?"

"No. Those two aren't here."

"What about Piccolo?"

"He helped fight Raditz. Afterward he tried to take the boys and threw Gohan toward a cliff. Goshin brought him back. Piccolo has gone, but he isn't our friend."

"He did what?"

"The boys are with us, Chi-Chi. I won't let them go with him."

The Ox-King finds a pencil and flattens an envelope on the table. Chi-Chi gives him the receiver, and he listens while Roshi repeats the part about Goku.

"We're coming," she says when her father hands it back. "Father has his truck, but we'll need to get across the water."

Bulma comes to the table. Roshi passes her the receiver.

"I'll pick you up," Bulma says. "Where should I meet you?"

"The ninth marker on the mountain road. It's just past the village."

"I'll look for a place to put down near there."

The Ox-King leans toward the receiver. "If there isn't room for both of us, take Chi-Chi first."

"We'll work it out when I get there," Bulma says.

"Tell Goku I'm on my way," Chi-Chi says. "And tell him I expect him to stay where he is."

"I'll tell him."

Chi-Chi puts down the receiver. Her father is already reaching for his hat.

"Get the truck," she says.

"I'll bring it to the gate."

She takes her coat from its peg and reaches for the house key. Outside, the truck engine starts.

## 10 · THE BEANS

At Kame House, Goku listens while Roshi tells him about the call. Gohan remains beside the bed, and Goshin sits on the floor with the medical case in his lap.

"She knows about Piccolo?" Goku asks.

"I told her what he did."

"And she's coming here?"

"Bulma's going to fetch her. Chi-Chi also said you were to stay where you are."

Goku glances at the blanket. "Everyone keeps saying that."

"They have a reason."

A sound comes from outside: feet landing on sand, then a hurried knock at the door. Goshin gets up and opens it.

Krillin stands there with a cloth bag in his hand. "Korin had some."

Gohan moves aside to let him through. Krillin reaches Goku's bed and loosens the string on the bag.

"Three," he says. "One for each of you."

Krillin keeps the bag open while Goku takes the bean, and Gohan watches from the other side of the bed.

Goku eats it. He draws a deep breath, throws the blanket aside and springs out of bed. His crushed ribs and the rest of his injuries have healed, and his strength has returned.

"I'm completely fine!" He twists from side to side, then raises both arms. "No pain, and I'm not tired anymore."

Gohan reaches for his hand. "Father?"

"All healed. The bean brought my strength back too. Gohan, would you like yours?"

Gohan takes a bean from Krillin and eats it. He raises his arm and turns his shoulder all the way around. The pain and his other injuries are gone.

"Me too," he says. "I'm all healed, and I'm not tired anymore."

Krillin offers the last bean to Goshin. Goshin takes it and turns it over in his good hand, but he doesn't eat it.

"What's wrong?" Krillin asks.

Goshin looks at the bean. "I didn't see it made."

"Korin grows them at the tower. I got this one out of his jar."

Goshin lifts the bean toward the window, then sets it gently back in the open cloth bag without bringing it to his mouth.

"He won't eat it if he hasn't watched it being made," Goku says. "We'll have to ask Korin about that."

Roshi checks the bandage again. "How is your head now?"

"It still hurts where Raditz hit me."

"Tell me if it hurts more, or if you feel dizzy again."

Goshin looks away, then back at Roshi. "Tell me if it hurts more, or if you feel dizzy again. Can I go and see Korin?"

"We'll ask him together," Goku says. "If your head gets worse, tell us and we'll go right away. Otherwise, we can bring your mother too."

"If your head gets worse, tell us and we'll go right away," Goshin repeats.

Krillin closes the bag around the remaining bean. "Korin also wanted to know about the other Saiyans. I told him what Raditz said."

"We'll all have to get ready," Goku says. "Korin may know someone who can help us."

Goshin sets the case down and puts both feet on the floor. He shifts them apart, then adjusts the left one until both heels are flat.

"What are you doing?" Goku asks.

"My feet moved when I fired the wave at Raditz. The light went wide."

"Does your head hurt when you stand?"

"A little."

"Then stay sitting for now. We can practise when you feel better."

"Then stay sitting for now," Goshin repeats. He moves his feet again without getting up. "I want the wave to stay where I aim it every time. I'll start with my feet."

Goku puts his own feet flat and shifts his weight. Goshin watches, then tries the movement slowly beside him.

"Like that?"

"That's it. When you're better, we'll practise that wave until it goes where you want."

Gohan looks at the door. "What about Mother?"

"I'm going to meet her," Bulma says. "You stay here with your father."

She takes the aircraft keys from the table. Beside them, the scouter remains wrapped in the case, with its cell separate and its lens dark.

Goshin looks at Goku. Goku catches his eye. "Anything else?"

"Can we find the other Dragon Balls before they come?"

"We can try. But first we tell your mother everything."

Bulma opens the door and heads back toward the aircraft. The engine starts again, and the sound carries across the water.

## 11 · WHAT PICCOLO SAW

Piccolo lands on the far side of the broken ridge. The crater lies behind him, and the aircraft is out of sight.

He opens his new hand. The fingers answer him, though his arm trembles once before it is still.

"One of them caught the other," he says. "And the other broke the cliff."

Dust settles on the empty rocks between his boots. Piccolo looks toward the sky where the aircraft went.

"If they learn to control all of it, they could become worse enemies than their father."

He closes his hand.

"If they don't, there may not be an Earth left for any of us."

Far inland, the Ox-King's truck comes down the mountain road. Chi-Chi sits beside him with her coat across her knees, watching the markers pass. Below the road a row of trees moves past the windows, and Chi-Chi counts each marker she can see.

"The ninth one," she says. "Bulma will meet us there."

"I've got it."

"Drive carefully."

"I am."

"A little faster, then."

The truck rounds the bend. Above the sea, Bulma's aircraft turns toward the mainland.
