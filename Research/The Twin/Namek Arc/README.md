**Research collection · 7 November 2026 · major original-Z spoilers**

This collection brings the Namek journey, the conflict on the real planet, and its immediate aftermath together for the author of *The Twin*. The principal baseline is the **original Japanese-numbered Dragon Ball Z television series**, beginning with the Saiyan-battle aftermath in episode 36 and following episodes **37–107**. Episode 108 begins the separate Garlic Jr. story; episodes 118–123 are identified only when a later consequence answers a question left open at the end of 107. English releases and Kai do not necessarily use the same numbering, titles, editing, dialogue or music.

**For a quick overview:** the source story begins as a rescue effort for the friends killed during the Saiyan invasion. Three Earth travellers seek another set of Dragon Balls, only to find that Freeza and Vegeta also want them. The contest becomes an invasion, a desperate shifting alliance and a fight over both the planet and the right to make wishes. Goku defeats Freeza, Namek is destroyed, and the surviving Namekians eventually find a new home. Those are **original-story outcomes, not predictions for the divergent novel**.

## Evidence and interpretation

- **Original-Z baseline:** the existing 4 November reviews report a complete read of all *retained caption cues* in episodes 37–107, including recaps, previews and trailing material; those reports were reused for this collection. On 7 November the retained Toei Japanese episode synopses for these 71 episodes were reviewed, selected local cues were rechecked, and one published Toei episode-page still for each Z 35–107 was inspected. Exact episode pages are linked in the chronicle and inventory. The source ZIP and episode-member identifiers are in the inventory and original reviews.
- **Important limitation:** no complete original-Z episode audio or video was examined for this collection. The redistributed Japanese SRT archive's original source and release cut are unverified. Its timestamps are local cue positions, not validated broadcast timecodes. Captioned wording is provisional; it cannot establish silent action, appearance, music, exact visual framing, an unlabelled speaker or every scene in an episode. Toei's short synopses are official but incomplete and contain identifiable website errors; see the source file.
- **Separate versions:** the Dragon Ball Official Site features cited here are often **manga-based retrospectives**. They corroborate or qualify a point within their own media and should not silently replace the original-Z TV account. Community episode guides are secondary aids. Kai, films, later Dragon Ball works and game-only material are not automatically the same continuity.
- **The Twin:** facts of the selected story are attributed to the actual, **unaccepted** Chapters 33–42 and current author directions. Original-Z futures are reference material only. Research is not a new chapter, an author acceptance, or a requirement that the branch follow the source's casualties, wish choices, power readings or ending.

## Completeness statement

This is a substantial, navigable collection across the **whole chosen original-Z Namek/Freeza journey**, not a claim to possess every possible fact. The world and character entries are organized around documented material and practical writing use. Full audiovisual verification, a licensed edition-by-edition comparison, every manga chapter, production record, dub, guidebook, film, game and later franchise appearance remain outside what has been independently examined.
