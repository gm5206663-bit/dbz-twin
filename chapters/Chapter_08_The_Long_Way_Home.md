## COLD OPEN

A truck climbs a mountain road beneath the last of the daylight. At a bend, a white post leans toward the ditch, its black number visible between the trees.

The truck passes it and continues toward the next bend. Far above the road, an aircraft comes out of the clouds.

**THE LONG WAY HOME**

---

## 1 · THE NINTH MARKER

**NARRATOR:** Raditz is dead, but two more Saiyans may reach Earth in about one year. Chi-Chi has heard the warning. She has not yet seen her family.

The Ox-King slows the truck beside the ninth marker. The road beyond it runs straight for a short distance, with a grassy verge on either side.

A fence follows the road toward the village. There are no other vehicles on this stretch, and the trees leave a gap above the marker.

Chi-Chi opens her door before the truck has stopped. "Is that Bulma?"

An engine answers above them. The aircraft drops below the treetops and circles once, looking for a place to land.

"Move the truck off the road," Chi-Chi says. "Give her the whole stretch."

Her father eases the truck onto the grass. Chi-Chi gets out and waves both arms, and the aircraft settles onto the empty road a little way beyond the marker.

Bulma opens the door as the engine slows. Chi-Chi runs toward her, with the Ox-King coming down the verge behind her.

"Where are they?" Chi-Chi asks.

"At Kame House. Goku and Gohan are completely healed."

"Completely?"

"Krillin brought Senzu Beans. They each ate one, and all their injuries healed. Their strength is back too."

Chi-Chi grips the edge of the open door. "I need to see them."

"That's why I'm here. Goshin wouldn't take his bean, though. His head and his hand still hurt."

The Ox-King reaches them. "Is the little one awake?"

"He was talking when I left. Roshi is watching him."

"Then let's go," Chi-Chi says.

The Ox-King looks into the cabin. "Will all of us fit?"

Bulma checks the weight marking inside the door and looks from the bench to her passengers. Then she moves a bag from the back bench to the front.

"The aircraft can carry you. Try getting in, and we'll see how much room you leave."

He turns sideways at the door and ducks his head. One shoulder catches the frame, so he backs out, takes off his hat and tries again.

The bench creaks when he sits. He draws his knees up and folds his arms close to his chest, leaving a narrow space beside him.

"I can move over a little," he says.

"You can't," Bulma says. "But Chi-Chi can fit there."

Chi-Chi climbs in beside her father. Bulma checks the door and helps them fasten the belts before taking the pilot's seat.

"What about the truck?" the Ox-King asks.

"It'll be there when we come back for it," Chi-Chi says. "Go on, Bulma."

The aircraft rises from the road. The marker and the truck fall away beneath them, and Chi-Chi keeps her hands on the belt across her lap until the trees are out of sight.

---

## 2 · THE DOOR

At Kame House, Gohan hears the engine first. He leaves the table and goes to the door, where Goshin is already standing with his bandaged hand against his chest.

"They've come back," Gohan says.

Roshi follows them outside. Goku is a step behind him, with Krillin and Turtle coming around the side of the house.

The aircraft settles on the sand. Bulma opens the cabin door, and Chi-Chi comes down before her father has finished undoing his belt.

Gohan runs to her. She drops to her knees and catches him, then draws back to look at his shoulder.

"Can you lift your arm?"

He raises it all the way. "It doesn't hurt at all. The bean healed everything."

"And you're not tired?"

"No. I feel strong again."

Chi-Chi puts a hand against his cheek. "Let me look at you."

"I'm here, Mother."

Sand has caught in the hem of his trousers. She brushes it away and finds a tear in one knee, then runs her hand down his leg to make sure he is standing steadily.

She holds him again, then sees Goshin waiting behind him. His hands go behind his back as she turns toward him.

"Goshin. Show me your hand."

"Show me your hand," Goshin repeats.

He brings it forward. The clean bandage covers his palm and passes between his thumb and fingers.

"Master Roshi wrapped it," he says. "It hurt again when I moved the rock."

"Roshi told me it was already burned."

"Yes. Raditz hit my head."

"Does it still hurt?"

"Here." He points above his ear with his good hand. "Not as much as before."

"Do you feel sick? Is the ground moving?"

"No."

She looks over his face, then sets her hand on his uninjured cheek.

"I'm going to put my arms around you now."

He steps close enough for her to do it. Gohan moves in beside them, and Chi-Chi keeps one arm around each boy.

The Ox-King climbs down from the aircraft. His hat catches on the door, and Bulma pulls it free for him before he reaches the sand.

"My grandsons!"

Gohan looks up. "Grandpa!"

"You've both grown since I last saw you. Did you see the things I brought?"

"We haven't been home yet," Gohan says.

"That's right. I left them at the house."

The Ox-King crouches beside the family. He reaches for Goshin, then stops with his hands open.

"May I pick you up?"

Goshin looks at his mother, then at the large hands. "Not yet."

"All right. How about when your head feels better?"

"All right."

Goku comes forward. Chi-Chi gets up with a boy on either side of her and looks at him from his face down to his ribs.

"Roshi said your ribs were crushed."

"They were. The Senzu healed me completely and brought all my strength back."

She puts her palm against his side through his shirt. Goku takes a deep breath and turns easily beneath her hand.

"You should have heard the telephone call," she says. "I didn't know if you'd still be breathing when I got here."

Goku lowers his eyes to her hand. "I'm sorry."

"Don't do that again."

"I'll try not to."

"You'd better do more than try."

He takes her hand from his ribs and holds it. Chi-Chi lets him keep it for a moment before turning back to the boys.

## 3 · THE CALL

Inside, Goshin sits on the bed where Goku had been lying. Gohan brings him a cushion and sets it behind his back without being asked.

Chi-Chi kneels beside the bed. "How long was he unconscious?"

Krillin looks at Roshi. "Raditz hit him on the island. By the time we reached the crater, he'd woken up. I can't give you an exact time."

"He was still out when we got to the pod," Gohan says. "Then he woke up, and we got outside."

"Did he fall again?"

"No," Goshin says. "My head hurt when we were flying back."

Chi-Chi turns to Bulma. "There's a doctor in the village near our house. She's seen the boys before. Can we call her?"

"Of course. Do you have her number?"

The Ox-King takes a folded envelope from his pocket. Roshi hands him a pencil, and he writes the number across the back.

Bulma dials. Chi-Chi stands beside her and takes the receiver when a woman answers.

"It's Chi-Chi. My son was struck on the head and lost consciousness. He's awake now, but his head still hurts, and his hand was burned."

She listens, then looks at Goshin. "No, he says he isn't dizzy. He hasn't been sick."

The boys watch her. Chi-Chi turns slightly so she can see both of them while she listens again.

"We're at Kame House, out on the island. Bulma can bring you here."

Bulma takes the receiver. "I just landed near the ninth road marker. Could you meet me there?"

She waits for the answer. "I'll leave now. Bring whatever you need to examine him."

When the receiver goes down, Goshin looks from Bulma to his mother. "Is she coming here?"

"Yes," Chi-Chi says. "She wants to look at you."

"Will I have to go away?"

"Not without me. We'll hear what the doctor says first."

Goshin puts his good hand on the edge of the cushion. Gohan sits beside him, leaving enough room for the bandaged hand between them.

Bulma picks up the aircraft keys. "I'll be back as soon as I can."

"Thank you," Chi-Chi says.

"Keep him resting until then. And if anything changes, tell the others right away."

The engine starts outside. Gohan grips the edge of the cushion as the sound rises over the roof. He does not let go until it has moved away across the water.

Chi-Chi sits on the other side of him. "What is it?"

"Raditz's ship made a noise before it opened. I thought someone was coming in."

She looks toward the window, then reaches across to close it. Gohan watches her fasten the latch; his hand stays on the cushion beside his brother.

"That's Bulma," she says. "She'll bring the doctor back."

"I know. I heard it, and I thought of the ship."

Goshin shifts the cushion between them so Gohan can hold it without twisting his wrist. Gohan leaves his hand there, and Chi-Chi takes the chair beside the bed.

## 4 · ABOVE THE CLOUDS

Clouds pass below the Lookout. Kami stands at its edge with both hands resting on his staff, while Mr. Popo waits a little way behind him.

"You went to see King Yemma," Popo says.

"I asked about Raditz. He reached the place of judgment."

Popo comes to stand beside him. "Piccolo killed him."

"Yes. In the past, a soul killed by the Demon Clan would have wandered without finding rest. Raditz did not."

"Then Piccolo has changed?"

"Something is different. That does not mean we can trust him."

Kami looks down through the clouds. The broad surface below is hidden from him for a moment, then the wind draws a gap through the white.

"He fought beside Goku because the Saiyan threatened them both. I do not know what he will do next."

"Afterward, Piccolo tried to take the children," Popo says. "I watched from here while you were away. They are with their parents now."

"Then they stopped him. And Goku is alive."

"Krillin brought Senzu Beans. Goku and Gohan each ate one. They are fully healed, and their strength has returned. The other boy refused his."

Kami lifts his staff and sets its end down on the stone. "I asked Yemma whether Goku might be allowed to seek King Kai. He gave no permission."

"Could Goku make that journey?"

"His strength has returned, but he must want to go, and Yemma must agree. First we must ask him."

Popo waits. Kami does not move away from the edge.

"There is something else," Kami says. "I have a feeling that my life has about a year left."

"One year?"

"If I die, Piccolo dies with me. If he is killed, I die as well. Our lives are joined."

Popo looks at the staff between Kami's hands. "The Saiyans are coming in a year."

"They may kill him. Or my own life may simply be near its end. I cannot tell which, and I hope my feeling is wrong."

"And the Dragon Balls?"

"If I am right, they may be used only once more. If I die before that, they will not be available even once."

The wind lifts the edge of Popo's vest. He presses it flat and looks toward the clouds below the platform, where the light is fading over the land.

Popo folds his hands. "Then the fighters need to know."

"They need to prepare, whether my feeling is right or not. Korin has already heard of the Saiyans. I will send word to him."

"And Goku?"

"I want him to hear about the road. I do not want him promised a journey Yemma has not granted."

Popo nods. The gap in the clouds closes beneath them.

## 5 · THE EMPTY WASTELAND

Piccolo lands among tall pillars of bare stone. He has left the crater behind, and no houses are visible from the ridge where he stands.

His new hand opens and closes. He raises it toward a narrow pillar, gathers light in his palm and fires.

The shot cuts through the top of the pillar. Stone slides down its side, scattering smaller pieces across the ground.

Piccolo watches where they fall. Then he moves to another position and fires again, this time at a smaller piece of rock balanced above the ground.

The stone breaks apart. He drops his hand before the fragments land.

He crosses the ridge and examines the pieces. The smallest lies beside a crack in the ground, beyond the place he had aimed. Piccolo sets it upright against another stone and returns to his first position.

He fires once more. The upright piece splits down the middle, leaving the stone behind it standing.

"Not enough."

A low sound comes from beyond the pillars. A large animal pushes its head around the stone and looks toward the dust.

Piccolo turns. The animal pulls back, and its footsteps move away through the rocks.

He looks up at the sky. "Two of them are coming, and I couldn't even take those boys while their father lay hurt."

He spreads his feet, raises both hands and gathers light between them. Dust lifts around his boots, then settles as he draws the light inward.

The next shot goes farther into the wasteland. Its impact sounds long after it has left his hands.

Piccolo remains where he is until the echo has gone.

## 6 · THE EXAMINATION

The aircraft returns after dark. Bulma helps the doctor down onto the beach, and Chi-Chi meets them at the door of Kame House.

The doctor carries a case in one hand. She wipes her shoes on the mat before coming inside.

Goshin is sitting on the bed with Gohan beside him.

"Hello, Goshin," she says. "Your mother told me what happened."

Goshin looks at the case. "Did you bring the little light?"

"I did. May I use it?"

Goshin looks away, then back at the doctor. "Yes."

She shines the light briefly toward each eye.

"Follow my finger without moving your head."

"Follow my finger without moving your head," Goshin repeats. His eyes follow her finger.

"Tell me if anything looks strange."

"Tell me if anything looks strange. It doesn't."

"Do you know where you are?"

"Master Roshi's house. On the island."

"And who is this?" She points to Gohan.

"My brother."

"Does your head hurt more than it did before?"

"Less. But I can still feel it."

The doctor turns to Chi-Chi. "I'd like to see where he was struck. Can you part his hair for me?"

"I'll tell him first."

Chi-Chi sits beside Goshin. "I'm going to move your hair so she can look. Is that all right?"

He turns toward her and holds still. She parts his hair carefully, and the doctor bends close without putting her hand on his head.

"There's a tender place here," Chi-Chi says.

"I can see it. Goshin, is there pain anywhere else?"

He shows her his bandaged palm. Roshi brings clean water and fresh cloth while the doctor loosens the old wrapping.

"Can you open your hand for me?"

"Can you open your hand for me?" Goshin repeats. He opens it slowly. The doctor checks the burn and asks him which fingers he can feel when his mother touches them.

"All of them," he says.

She cleans the hand and covers it again. "Keep this clean, and don't make him use the sore palm."

"What about his head?" Chi-Chi asks.

"A blow that knocked him out needs watching. I can't promise from this examination that everything is fine. He needs rest, and I want to check him again."

"Tomorrow?"

"Tomorrow, if he stays as he is. If he gets more sleepy than usual, seems confused, vomits, or his pain gets worse, take him for urgent care immediately. Don't wait for me to come back."

Goku moves closer to the bed. "I can carry both of you to the mainland."

"Then I'll come with you," Chi-Chi says.

"Please do," the doctor says. "Tonight, keep him where you can check on him, and no fighting or hard exercise while he's recovering."

Goshin looks at her mouth. "No fighting or hard exercise."

"That's right. You can rest. Tell your mother if anything changes."

"Tell your mother if anything changes. For how many days?"

"I can't tell you yet. I'll look at you again before we decide."

He looks away, then back at her. "All right."

The doctor writes the warning signs and her telephone number on a sheet of paper. Chi-Chi reads it before folding it once and setting it beside the bed.

Gohan pulls the cushion forward when Goshin leans back. The doctor closes her case and turns to Chi-Chi.

"Call me if you need me. Don't worry about the hour."

## 7 · WHAT THEY DID

The doctor accepts a cup of tea before Bulma takes her back. Chi-Chi sits at the table with the boys, while Goku and Krillin stand on the other side.

"I heard that Raditz was your brother," she says to Goku. "You never told me you had a brother."

"I didn't know. He came here and told me what he was."

"And he took our sons?"

"Yes. He wanted me to help him kill people. When I wouldn't, he took them."

Goku pulls the chair farther from the table so Goshin has room to keep his hand on the cushion. For a moment, nobody touches the cup the doctor has left beside the sink.

"Piccolo killed him," Gohan says. "Even though he was Father's brother."

Goku looks at the boys. "I wish it hadn't come to that. But Raditz was going to hurt you again."

Chi-Chi looks at Gohan. "Did he hurt you?"

"He put us in his ship. Goshin couldn't wake up at first."

Her hand closes around the edge of the table. Gohan puts his own hand over it.

"I stayed with him," he says. "When he woke up, we got out."

"You stayed with him?"

"I hid with him under a truck before Raditz found us. Before that, I tried to pull him away from Raditz on the beach."

Goshin touches the cushion beside him. "He got me under the truck."

Chi-Chi looks at each of them in turn. "I'm glad you were together."

Gohan glances toward his father. "When Raditz was hurting Father, I hit him. I don't know how I got there so fast."

"You hit him hard," Goku says. "I didn't know you had that much strength."

"Did I hurt him?"

"You broke his armour. You helped me get free."

Gohan lowers his eyes to the table. "I was scared the whole time."

"I know," Goku says. "You still came."

Chi-Chi keeps her hand beneath Gohan's. "And Piccolo threw you at a cliff. Roshi told me that much."

"I thought I was going to hit it. The rock broke, and Goshin caught me."

"You broke it?"

"I think so. I couldn't stop myself."

Goku pulls out a chair and sits across from him. "You saw the big rock falling afterward. You warned us before it could hit the aircraft."

"Goshin moved it."

"And he needed to know it was there. Both of you helped us."

Chi-Chi looks at Goku. "You told me Piccolo had changed after the tournament."

"I thought he wouldn't come after the boys. I was wrong, and I told them so."

"He doesn't get another chance to take them."

"No," Goku says. "He doesn't."

Roshi clears his throat. "There's something else. Raditz spoke about the full moon on the island. I should have explained it then."

Chi-Chi turns toward him. Gohan's tail curls around his ankle under the chair.

"Goku had a tail when he fought in the tournament," Roshi says. "He looked at the full moon and changed into a giant ape. I saw it."

Goku stares at him. "That was me?"

"Yes. You didn't remember afterward."

For a moment Goku says nothing. His hand tightens on the edge of the table.

"Grandpa used to warn me not to go outside when the moon was full. He said there was a monster. When he died, I thought the monster had killed him."

Roshi looks down at the table.

"Could it have been me?" Goku asks.

"I don't know what happened there. I wasn't with you that night." Roshi raises his head. "But I should have told you what I saw at the tournament. I'm sorry, Goku."

Chi-Chi reaches across the table and takes Goku's hand. He holds hers, still looking at Roshi.

"Will it happen to us?" Gohan asks.

"I don't know," Roshi says. "You and your brother have tails like your father had. Until we know more, neither of you should look at a full moon."

Chi-Chi looks from one boy to the other. "You knew about this when you saw their tails?"

"I knew about Goku. I didn't understand why it happened until Raditz spoke. But I should have told you both."

"Yes," she says. "You should have."

She goes to the window and draws the curtain closed. The other window has a curtain too; she checks that it will cover the glass. The room grows a little darker, and she leaves the lamp on.

"Can we find out more?" Goshin asks.

"We can ask," Roshi says. "Not by putting you under a full moon to see what happens."

Gohan moves closer to Goku, who puts an arm around him. Chi-Chi comes back from the window and rests her hand on Goshin's shoulder.

"You're my sons," she says. "Neither of you is a monster. We'll work it out together, and until we do, we'll keep you safe."

Roshi sets a small cloth bag on the table. He opens it just enough for Chi-Chi to see the remaining bean.

"Korin gave Krillin three. Goku and Gohan ate two."

Chi-Chi looks at Goshin. "You didn't want this one?"

"I didn't see it being made."

"He gave it back to me," Krillin says. "We haven't used it."

"Can I watch Korin make one?" Goshin asks.

"We can ask him," Goku says. "I don't know how long it takes to grow them."

Goshin studies the bag. "This one was already in the jar."

"It was," Krillin says. "We'll keep it safe."

Roshi closes the string and puts the bag safely in his pocket.

## 8 · THE MEAL

The Ox-King opens a cupboard in the kitchen. He finds rice, onions and a tin of curry powder, then turns toward his daughter with the tin in his hand.

"Do you suppose the old master would share?"

"The old master is standing right here," Roshi says.

"Then there's no need to suppose."

Chi-Chi ties an apron around her waist. "I'll cook. Father, wash the rice for me."

Goshin climbs onto a chair beside the table. Gohan puts the bowl within his reach, and Goshin watches the water cloud around the rice as their grandfather turns it with his fingers.

"You don't have to sit in here," Chi-Chi says.

"I want to see."

"Then stay where you can rest your hand."

"Then stay where you can rest your hand," Goshin repeats. He rests it on the table.

She cuts the vegetables while the Ox-King rinses the rice again. Krillin brings a pot from the cupboard, and Turtle shifts a chair so Chi-Chi can reach the stove.

Gohan watches the lid go onto the pot. "That's the curry we have at home."

"Roshi had the powder," Chi-Chi says. "I don't know if he had any idea what to do with it."

"I knew it belonged in the cupboard," Roshi says.

Gohan smiles, then puts a hand against his stomach. "I don't think I can eat any."

Goku looks down at himself. "Me neither. That bean really fills you up."

The Ox-King stares at his son-in-law. "You're turning down dinner?"

"Just for now."

"Write that on the calendar," Bulma says.

The room laughs. Goshin looks around at everyone, then smiles too.

Chi-Chi stirs the curry and lets the steam rise before she replaces the lid. Goshin stays on his chair through the chopping, the stirring and the wait for the rice.

"You can have some when you're hungry," she tells Gohan. "I'm not throwing it away."

"Could I have a little now?" Goshin asks.

"When it's ready."

He keeps watching the pot. The Ox-King sets out bowls, including one for Goku and one for Gohan, then returns them to the shelf when both shake their heads.

"This is the strangest meal I've had in years," he says.

When Chi-Chi serves the curry, Goshin takes his spoon in his good hand. She puts a cloth beside him so he can rest the bandaged one.

"Is it too hot?" she asks.

He blows on the first spoonful. "Not now."

He eats, and she fills his bowl again when he holds it out.

After the meal, Gohan helps his grandfather stack the bowls. Goku dries them while Chi-Chi brings the doctor's instructions from beside the bed and lays them on the cleared table.

A breeze comes through the broken wall and moves the paper. Gohan catches it before it slides onto the floor. The opening is still there from Raditz's arrival, with loose plaster at its edges.

Roshi fetches a hammer and a few boards from beneath the porch. The Ox-King sets the first board across the gap, and Gohan holds it while his grandfather drives in a nail.

The hammer rings against the nail head. Gohan flinches, then settles his hands and keeps the board in place for the next blow.

"You can go inside," the Ox-King says.

"I want to help."

The two of them fit another board below the first. Goku carries a third from the porch and holds it against the remaining opening while Gohan passes his grandfather the nails.

There are narrow spaces between the boards when they finish. The wall will need proper repair, but the wind no longer moves the paper on the table.

Chi-Chi hangs an old sheet over the boards on the inside, covering the gaps. Gohan holds one corner while she fastens it. Then he picks up the doctor's instructions and takes them back to her. She smooths them flat and reads them once more under the lamp.

## 9 · THE YEAR

Goshin sits on the cushion with his feet flat on the floor. He shifts one foot, then stops and looks at the closed door.

"What is it?" Goku asks.

"If Piccolo comes back, will he take us again?"

"He won't take you while I'm here. And if you see him, you tell us right away. You don't have to go anywhere with him."

"And if you see him, you tell us right away," Goshin repeats.

Chi-Chi puts down the cloth. "We need to decide what to do about the others. Raditz said they're stronger than he was."

"That's what he said," Krillin answers. "We don't know exactly when they'll arrive."

"Or how strong they are," Roshi says. "But we saw what one of them could do."

Gohan takes off his hat and places it on the table. Light from the lamp passes through the ball on top, showing the four stars inside.

Goku looks at the ball Grandpa left him. His hand stays on the edge of the table.

"They heard Piccolo talking about the Dragon Balls," Gohan says. "We have two more at home."

Chi-Chi looks at the ball on the hat. "Are they going to look for them there?"

"They might," Goku says. "We don't know how much they heard about where the balls are."

"If we find the others first, they can't make a wish," Gohan says.

"That's a good start," Bulma says. "But we'd still have to keep any we find away from them."

Goshin looks at the hat. Chi-Chi sees him watching it.

"What is it?"

"Can your radar find the rest?"

"It can find the balls. I'll need to take it with me when I go looking."

Chi-Chi moves the hat to the cleared end of the table. "The two at home aren't going anywhere tonight. We'll decide where to put them before we leave."

Gohan brings the last bowl to the table. "Father?"

"What is it?"

"I don't want to be alone with someone like Raditz again. I want to know what to do if he comes for us."

Chi-Chi draws him beside her. "You shouldn't have to fight grown men."

"I know. But if they come to our house, I want to help you."

She looks at Goku. He sets the bowl down and turns it so it will not roll off the cloth.

"We can all prepare," he says. "That doesn't mean we're sending either boy away."

"What about my studying?" Gohan asks.

"You can keep studying," Chi-Chi says. "Being able to protect yourself doesn't mean giving that up."

"Could I learn with everybody nearby?"

"Yes," Goku says. "We'll talk with you and your mother before we make a plan."

Gohan leans against Chi-Chi's arm. "I want to be a scholar."

"You still can," she says.

Goku looks at Goshin. "What about you?"

Goshin turns his feet the way he had on the floor beside the bed. "I want the wave to go where I point it. Every time."

"The one you used against Raditz?"

"And the falling rock. My feet moved."

"We can work on your footing and your aim," Goku says. "The wave is something you already know. We'll make it steadier."

Goshin adjusts one heel. "Can I move my feet while I'm sitting?"

The doctor watches him from across the room. "A little, if it doesn't make your head hurt. No workout, and stop if you feel unwell."

Goku puts both feet flat beneath his chair. He moves one heel outward, settles his weight and waits until Goshin has seen the position.

"Put both feet down," he says. "Then check that they feel steady."

"Put both feet down. Then check that they feel steady."

Goshin sets his feet, pauses, and moves the left one back into place. He lifts that heel a little, puts it down again and checks the space between his shoes.

On the next try he leaves it where it lands. He looks at his father, who nods once, and then Goshin rests against the cushion again.

"Does your head still feel the same?" Chi-Chi asks.

"The same."

"Then that's enough for tonight."

"Then that's enough for tonight," Goshin repeats.

Goku takes his own feet out of position and stretches his legs beneath the table. "We'll do the rest when you're well."

Chi-Chi looks down at Goshin's bandage. "And not by firing at the house."

"I wasn't going to," Goku says.

She raises an eyebrow. "I was talking to both of you."

Krillin takes the doctor's folded instructions from the table and places them beside the bed. The lamp lights the writing clearly enough for anyone sitting there to read.

## 10 · A MESSAGE NOT YET SENT

In Otherworld, cups and saucers crowd a café counter. A customer counts her change twice, then holds up one finger to the server.

"I'm short one coin."

The server checks the counter and finds it under a saucer. At the next table, Fortuneteller Baba watches the coin change hands before turning back to the guide sitting opposite her.

"You were telling me about a visitor," she says.

"Earth's guardian came to King Yemma. He asked about training with King Kai."

"For himself?"

"For a man called Son Goku. He was alive and injured when Kami asked. His Majesty didn't grant the request; he said he would consider it if Goku recovered and wanted to make the journey."

Baba sets down her cup. "Do you know how Goku is now?"

"No. Kami didn't tell me what happened after he left. I showed him the beginning of the road, but nobody was sent along it."

"That boy has never been satisfied with the teachers he's already found."

"Please don't tell anyone the king has given permission. He hasn't."

"I heard you. I'll tell my brother that Kami has a question for Goku. The guardian can give them the rest."

The guide reaches into his case. Baba puts a coin on the table before he can open it.

"You brought me the news," she says. "I'll buy the tea."

## 11 · THE ROAD BACK

Bulma lays the scouter on a folded cloth. Its lens is dark, and the removed cell lies on a separate square of paper.

"I'm taking these to my lab," she tells Krillin. "I won't put the cell back until I know how to keep it from sending anything."

"Can you read it yet?"

"No. I haven't even turned it on."

She wraps the pieces separately and puts them in different compartments of her case. The doctor waits beside the door with her own bag.

"I'll bring her back to the village," Bulma says. "Then I'll go home. I'll call when I know more about the device."

Chi-Chi walks them outside. "Thank you for coming."

"Remember what I said," the doctor tells her. "If you're worried that he's getting worse, don't wait."

"I won't."

Bulma and the doctor board the aircraft. Chi-Chi remains on the beach until it rises above the roof and turns toward the mainland.

Inside, Roshi brings out blankets. Gohan spreads one beside Goshin's bed, and the Ox-King finds a place against the wall where he can sit without blocking the door.

"Your truck's still at the marker," Gohan says.

"It can wait for me. I haven't seen you two in a year."

"You brought us presents?"

"A truckload, and something wrapped just for you. I left everything at your house."

Gohan looks up at Chi-Chi. "Can we see them when we go home?"

"Of course. First we'll make sure your brother is well enough to travel."

Goku sits on the edge of the bed. "How's your head now, Goshin?"

"It still hurts a little. Not more."

"Tell us if that changes."

"Tell us if that changes."

Gohan lies down beside the bed with his blanket pulled up to his chin. Chi-Chi sits where she can see both boys, and Goku draws a chair close enough to reach Goshin's hand.

On the mountain road, the truck waits beside the ninth marker. Its door is locked, and the road beyond it is empty.

At Kame House, the door stays open a little longer. Then the Ox-King gets up and closes it for the night.
