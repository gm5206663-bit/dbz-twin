# Twin project — review package

**Start with `00_REBUILD_PLAN_AND_AUDIT.docx`.** It combines the proposal and chapter-by-chapter audit in one reading copy. This package is research and planning only; it does not contain a replacement chapter or authorize changes to the story.

## Files

- **`00_REBUILD_PLAN_AND_AUDIT.docx`** — the recommended staged rebuild, the rules to preserve, decision request and audit of all ten current chapters.
- `01_REBUILD_PROPOSAL.md` — the proposal as editable text.
- `02_CHAPTER_BY_CHAPTER_AUDIT.md` — the full audit as editable text.
- `03_EPISODE_BEAT_COMPARISON.md` — ordered original-Z 1–10 comparison and 11–35 synopsis-level navigation, with branch/dependency notes.
- `04_FRANCHISE_AND_FILM_MAP.md` — the wider franchise, 21 individual animated theatrical films, other versions and continuity conflicts.
- `05_SOURCE_AND_PROVENANCE.md` — what was actually read, how each source may be used, and what remains unverified.
- `06_EPISODE_AND_FILM_INDEX.xlsx` — searchable navigation for 659 DB/Z/GT/Super/DAIMA episode entries and 21 films. English title hints are mostly machine translations and are **not** official or verified. All film decisions remain pending.
- `sources/INDEX_EXCEPTIONS.md` — extraction exceptions for Z 189/209 and the combined Super 109/110 page.
- `audit/ORIGINAL_PROJECT_SHA256.txt` and `audit/VERIFICATION_2026-11-01.txt` — before/after verification for the original living project.
- `DELIVERABLE_SHA256.txt` — checksums for the other files included in this package.

Raw local subtitle files, complete official-synopsis caches and promotional stills are not included in this review ZIP. The source ledger identifies the working locations and online sources; a source cache is not a reviewed episode.

## Key status

- Chapter 10 has been **rejected**; its authored additions are not accepted continuity.
- The updated Chapters 1–3 await the reader’s review. Chapters 4–9 are provisional. Preservation or implementation of a revision does not itself mean acceptance.
- All ten draft bodies were read, Toei’s Z 1–35 synopses were examined, and the local Z 9 and 10 captions were reread. **No full episode or film footage was watched** in this audit. Hundreds of short official synopsis pages were indexed, not individually reviewed or watched.
- The separate prior ZIPs, Drive snapshots and GitHub history remain unchanged. The latest public/cloud copies still predate the rejection and were not relabeled.
- Any proposed opening would be written in a separate candidate folder only **after approval**, reviewed as prose, and would not automatically replace or publish existing work.
