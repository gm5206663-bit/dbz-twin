## Standing author directions preserved

- Goshin is never less capable than Gohan where compared; Gohan nevertheless grows, chooses and acts independently. Temporary condition or tactical disadvantage does not erase capability.
- After their previous development **and the full shared chamber year**, both boys comfortably outclass this era's Piccolo in an ordinary spar. This is an author-directed qualitative branch checkpoint, not a sourced canon multiplier, permanent universal ranking or predetermined Nappa/Vegeta outcome (`O170`, `O181`).
- Goshin's tail remains. His future SS4 follows his ape/Golden Ape road, and his very first Great Ape is aware and controlled; no compulsory fear or loss-of-control arc is imposed (`O035`, `O046`).
- Deliberate training prioritizes mastering existing abilities and serious foundations. Instinctive copying in an emergency remains allowed (`O104`, `O105`).
- Both brothers' lived chamber year counts for each. The selected landing occurs later the same morning as the exit; the exact length of the larger pre-room interval and their birthdays are not invented.
