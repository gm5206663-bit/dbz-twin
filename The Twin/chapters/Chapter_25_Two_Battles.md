**TWO BATTLES**

**NARRATOR:** Four Saibaimen remain in the quarry. As the fighters struggle to keep them apart, Vegeta prepares another attack.

## Between the Shelf and the Tracks

Vegeta fires toward Krillin.

Gohan reaches the blast above the tracks. Its light fills his vision as he strikes its side with both hands. The force shoves him toward the rails, but he twists at the waist and drives the attack upward. It clears the quarry wall and bursts over the empty ground beyond, shaking dust from the loading platforms.

Krillin shields his eyes. A Saibaiman springs toward him through the falling grit, and he jumps back over the rails. Its claws rake the space where his face was a moment before.

"Thanks, Gohan! I've got this one."

"Watch the other two!" Piccolo calls from the western wall.

The pinned Saibaiman twists beneath Goshin's hand, claws scraping for a hold in the quarry floor. He keeps its head turned away from Tien and Chiaotzu as they fly clear. Then he presses his palm down. A short flash lights the ground beneath him; when he lifts his hand, the creature is gone, leaving a scorched mark in the stone.

Vegeta steps off the shelf. "Three left, Nappa."

"I can see that."

Gohan drops toward Krillin, but Vegeta moves after him. Goshin rises from the eastern ridge and crosses Vegeta's path. Their forearms meet with a crack that scatters grit across the quarry.

Vegeta drives forward. Goshin holds him above the tracks, their locked arms shaking under the pressure. The old rails shudder below them. Goshin changes the angle of his shoulder and pushes Vegeta back toward the shelf, keeping the Saiyan's line of fire away from Krillin.

"You're fighting me?" Vegeta asks.

Goshin looks past him at the others. "You're not attacking them again."

"You stopped one shot."

Vegeta twists and sends a quick punch toward Goshin's face. Goshin turns it aside. Vegeta kicks from below, but Goshin lifts his knee and catches the blow. Before Vegeta can pull the leg back, Goshin reaches for his ankle. Vegeta spins free, his boot passing close to Goshin's head, and both rise above the shelf.

They separate in the air, with the fighters and the remaining Saibaimen spread across the ground below. Vegeta's scouter sounds as he watches Goshin: **16,300**.

"Still holding that power," Vegeta says.

Below them, Nappa steps toward the fighters gathered by the western wall. He glances once at Gohan before he moves.

"Nappa," Vegeta says. "Take the others. Keep one alive to tell us about the Dragon Balls."

"The boy won't let me walk past him."

"Then make him follow you."

Nappa launches himself across the quarry toward Yamcha and Chiaotzu. Gohan turns and flies after him.

"Goshin!"

"I've got Vegeta!"

Vegeta smiles at that. He rushes Goshin before the boy can look away again.

## Gohan's Opponent

Nappa raises a hand toward Chiaotzu. Gohan catches his wrist and pulls it upward just as the blast leaves his palm. Light streaks over the ridge; the heat brushes Gohan's cheek before the attack clears him.

Nappa swings his free arm. Gohan ducks beneath it and drives his shoulder into Nappa's middle. Their bodies cross the quarry together. Nappa claws at Gohan's back, trying to turn him, but Gohan keeps both arms tight around his waist until they hit the empty loading platform.

Timbers snap. Nappa rolls through the fallen boards and comes up on one knee, scattering splinters with a sweep of his arm. Gohan lands between him and the other fighters, checking over his shoulder that Chiaotzu has reached Tien.

"Leave them alone," Gohan says.

Nappa spits dust from his mouth. "You already told me that in the city."

"You didn't listen."

"I remember your hand on my arm. You and your brother are stronger than Raditz said."

Gohan keeps his hands raised. Nappa gets up and takes a step to one side. Gohan moves with him, keeping the broken platform at his back.

"Did Kakarot teach you to fight?" Nappa asks.

"He taught us some things before he left. Mother and our friends helped too. Goshin and I trained together."

"Your mother?"

"She knows how to fight."

Nappa gives a bark of laughter. Gohan remembers how Chi-Chi caught Tien during their spar and makes no attempt to laugh with him.

Nappa looks over Gohan's shoulder. Tien and Chiaotzu have reached Piccolo and Yamcha; Krillin is still on the tracks with a Saibaiman circling him.

"But you can't stand in front of all of them."

He flicks two fingers toward the ground beside the western wall. Stone erupts between Piccolo and Yamcha.

"Up!" Piccolo shouts.

The fighters lift clear as another explosion tears through the place where Chiaotzu stood. Gohan is already moving. He catches Nappa's raised hand before the Saiyan can point again.

Nappa pulls him close and swings a knee toward him. Gohan shifts to the side. The knee passes his hip, and Gohan plants a foot against Nappa's armour.

He pushes.

Nappa flies backward through the remains of the platform. His shoulder hits the quarry wall, cracking the stone around him. Gohan follows and stops a short distance away.

"Don't make me do that again."

Nappa pulls himself out of the wall. The dent Goshin left in his armour is deeper now, and one strap hangs loose.

"You think one kick will finish me?"

"I don't want to hurt you more. I want you to stop."

Nappa charges out of the broken wall and catches the front of Gohan's gi. His other hand reaches around toward the boy's tail. Gohan turns inside the grip—the step he and Goshin practised beside the marker sticks—and drives the heel of his palm against Nappa's wrist. The fingers open. Gohan slips free and springs back before the larger hand can close again.

Nappa looks at his empty fist. "You learned that on this planet?"

"Yes."

Nappa raises both hands. Two blasts shoot toward Gohan, and a third passes him toward the fighters. Gohan knocks the first aside, ducks the second and turns after the third.

He reaches it above the broken platform. His palm meets the blast and sends it over the empty ridge. The explosion lifts dust against the sky.

Nappa comes through the dust behind him. Gohan feels him approach and turns. He catches Nappa's punch against his open hand, pivots and sends him down into the quarry floor.

Nappa lands hard. Gohan descends facing him, his tail moving clear of his feet.

## The Western Wall

A Saibaiman springs from the wall toward Piccolo. He stretches an arm across its path and catches it by the ankle. The creature claws at his hand as he swings it away from Yamcha.

"Tien!"

Tien moves beneath it. The creature twists in the air and claws at Piccolo's extended arm. Piccolo tightens his grip and pulls it farther from the others.

"Don't let it reach the wall," he says.

Chiaotzu moves around Tien and raises both hands. The creature stops just above Tien's reach. Its legs kick against the hold, and Chiaotzu's small hands tremble as he keeps it suspended.

"I have it, Tien!"

Tien gathers light in his palm, but the Saibaiman twists toward Chiaotzu, wrenching against both restraints. Its head parts down the middle. Piccolo pulls its leg back, holding it clear of Chiaotzu, while Tien shifts his aim until neither of his friends is behind the creature.

"Now!" Chiaotzu calls.

Tien fires. Light fills the space between the wall and the tracks. The Saibaiman disappears in the burst. Chiaotzu lowers his hands, breathing hard, and Piccolo draws his arm back.

"You all right?" Tien asks.

"Yes. It was harder to hold while it was moving."

"You gave us enough time," Piccolo says. He looks across the quarry.

Two creatures remain.

Krillin's opponent runs along the tracks on all fours. Yamcha faces the other near the western wall. It leaps at his head; he ducks and drives a punch into its side.

The creature hits the stone and scrambles up again. Its head opens, and a stream of liquid sprays toward Yamcha.

"Move!" Piccolo calls.

Yamcha jumps clear. The liquid strikes the wall behind him, and the stone hisses. A dark channel runs down the rock where it touched.

"That would have hurt," Yamcha says.

"Don't let it get close," Tien tells him.

"I'm working on it."

Krillin's Saibaiman darts across the tracks. Krillin throws a short burst after it, but the creature rolls beneath the light and comes up beside a broken rail.

"This one's quick!"

Chiaotzu flies toward him. "I'll help!"

"Watch its head," Yamcha calls.

A heavy impact rolls across the quarry. Everyone looks up. Goshin and Vegeta have crossed above the western ridge, leaving a long break in the stone below them.

Piccolo raises a hand toward the fighters. "Keep your eyes on what's in front of you."

The Saibaiman at Yamcha's feet springs again.

## The Family at the Bus

Bulma brings her car back to the junction. Chi-Chi opens the rear door for the mother, father and daughter waiting beside the bus.

"There are seats for all three of you," she says.

The girl climbs in between her parents. Her father keeps one hand on the door while her mother settles her against the seat.

"The boy who helped us," the girl says. "Is he coming too?"

Chi-Chi looks west. A pale flash rises beyond the city, followed by a sound that reaches them a moment later. She grips the car door until the sound dies away.

"He's helping his friends," she says. "We're taking you to the gym."

The girl nods, though she keeps looking out of the window. Bulma closes the door and turns to Chi-Chi.

"The volunteers have made room inside. A doctor was coming when I left."

"Good. Ask them to send someone back for the driver if they can. His arm was hurt at the crossing."

"I'll find out where they took him."

Puar emerges from the building beside the bus with two people following. One carries a small bag; the other is holding a cloth against her forehead.

"The stairs are clear," Puar says. "They were waiting for someone from the next floor."

A man comes out behind them and raises his hand. "I'm here. We can walk."

Chi-Chi points toward the junction. "Stay together and follow that road. The police are keeping it open."

Popo's carpet descends beyond the bus. He helps the woman with the cloth climb aboard, then makes room for the man beside her.

Puar looks west. "Yamcha's with them, isn't he?"

"He went with Krillin," Chi-Chi says.

Puar folds both hands together. "I'll help here until they come back."

"Krillin and Yamcha followed the boys west," Chi-Chi tells Bulma. "They stopped here on the way."

"I'll tell Roshi when I find a phone," Bulma says.

She starts the car. Chi-Chi watches it turn onto the clear road, then helps the next group down from the bus.

## A Call from the Gym

A volunteer meets Bulma's car outside the gym. The father gets out first and lifts his daughter from the back seat. Her mother follows, carrying the girl's coat.

"There's water inside," the volunteer tells them. "The doctor is in the office with someone now."

"A delivery driver hurt his arm near the crossing," Bulma says. "Do you know whether he got here?"

"A driver came in with an injured arm. He's being looked after."

Bulma leads the family through the doors. Blankets cover part of the floor, and another volunteer is writing down names at a table.

"Can I use a telephone?" Bulma asks.

The volunteer points to an open doorway beyond the table. Bulma goes inside and calls Kame House.

Roshi picks up on the first ring. "Hello?"

"It's me. I'm at the school gym. Chi-Chi, Popo and Puar are helping people near the crossing."

Oolong moves his chair close to Roshi's. "Ask her about Yamcha!"

Bulma hears him through the receiver. "Chi-Chi saw him and Krillin on their way west. They went after the boys and Piccolo."

"Do you know what's happening there?" Roshi asks.

"I saw a flash from the city. I can't see the fight from here."

Roshi is quiet for a moment. "Stay with the people who need you. Call again when you can."

"I will."

Bulma puts down the receiver. Outside the office, the girl from the crossing has found a place on a blanket between her parents. A volunteer brings her a cup of water.

Bulma returns to the car. More people are still waiting at the bus.

## Above the Quarry

Vegeta comes at Goshin with a high punch, then changes direction before their arms meet. His knee strikes Goshin's side and pushes him across the air. Goshin folds around the blow, breath driven from him, and catches himself above the ridge before he can strike the rock.

Vegeta follows at once, firing two small blasts to either side of him. Goshin knocks the nearer one toward the empty slope and slips over the other. It bursts behind him, scattering stone across the crest.

Vegeta is waiting above the flash. He clasps both hands and strikes down. Goshin crosses his arms in time, but the blow drives him onto the ridge. His feet cut two furrows through loose stone before he stops. Pebbles bounce against his shoes as Vegeta lands opposite him.

Goshin draws a breath. His side aches, and below the ridge he can hear another blast from the fight on the tracks. He keeps Vegeta in front of him.

"You're better than Raditz," Vegeta says. "That doesn't make you a match for me."

Goshin looks down at the marks under his shoes. He rolls one shoulder, then raises his hands again. The blow hurt; it also showed him a gap in his guard. He wants to see Vegeta try it now that he knows.

"You hit harder than he did." Despite the ache in his side, Goshin can't keep the excitement out of his voice.

"I've been fighting since before Kakarot was sent to this planet."

Vegeta moves first. Goshin meets him halfway, turns the next punch and reaches for his arm. Vegeta pulls free and steps toward Goshin's tail.

Goshin catches his wrist.

"Nappa tried that too."

Vegeta jerks his hand back. His scouter still shows **16,300** as Goshin settles on the ridge.

Goshin lets more of the ki he developed during his year with Gohan flow through him. He had kept it in reserve when they entered the city. The loose stones around his feet begin to lift.

Vegeta's scouter sounds. The display climbs and stops at **19,400**.

"Nineteen thousand four hundred?"

Goshin rises above the ridge. "You were right. I wasn't using all of it."

Vegeta's fingers tighten at the edge of the scouter. Before leaving for Earth, he had warned Nappa what Kakarot's children might become. Now the number on the lens is higher than the power he has been using.

"A child of Kakarot," he says. "Do you think I'll let you stand above me?"

Goshin glances at the furrows his shoes cut through the ridge. His side still aches, but he raises his hands.

"You said I wasn't a match for you. Try knocking me back again."

Vegeta lifts the scouter from his ear and clips it to his armour. His gaze stays on Goshin as his own ki rises, throwing the loose stones from the ridge.

"Gladly."

Goshin flies forward. Vegeta meets him with both hands raised. Goshin's first punch strikes his guard and pushes the Saiyan back through the air. Vegeta turns with the next blow, letting it slide past his shoulder instead of meeting it head-on.

Vegeta turns low and drives a fist toward Goshin's stomach. Goshin catches the wrist, pulls him past and lands a blow against his armour. The impact bends Vegeta backward and sends him through the ridge. Stone follows him down the far side.

Goshin flies after him. Vegeta comes out of the falling dust with an energy blast in each hand. He throws them wide, then cuts between their paths. Goshin twists after him, shoulder brushing the edge of one blast, and closes the distance. Vegeta turns in time to catch his next punch against his palm.

They hang above the broken slope, one small fist locked in Vegeta's hand, both watching for the other's free arm.

For an instant neither gives ground.

Vegeta's fingers tighten around Goshin's fist. "What did Kakarot do to you?"

"Father's been away. Gohan and I trained together."

Vegeta shoves him back. "In a year?"

"We trained before that too."

Vegeta sweeps his foot toward Goshin's ankle. Goshin lifts clear, but Vegeta follows the movement and drives an elbow toward his guard.

Goshin blocks it and feels the force run down both arms. He pushes Vegeta away and circles above the broken ridge.

"You learned to fly here?" Vegeta asks.

"I could fly when we fought Raditz."

"Not like this."

Vegeta sends a burst toward the open ground on Goshin's left, then another to his right. Goshin rises between them. Vegeta is already coming up through the gap.

Goshin turns in the air and meets him with a kick. Vegeta blocks, but the impact sends him back against the ridge. He pushes off the stone and comes straight at Goshin again.

Vegeta fires at close range. Goshin brings his free hand across the blast and turns it upward. The light tears through the dust above them.

Vegeta follows the opening and lands a punch against Goshin's cheek. Goshin turns with the blow, catches Vegeta's sleeve and swings him toward the bare ground beyond the ridge.

Vegeta tears his arm free before he hits. He lands on one hand, slides through the dirt and rises again.

He is no longer smiling.

## Yamcha's Ball

Yamcha rises over the Saibaiman's next lunge. He holds one hand above his head, and a ball of light forms over his palm.

"You remember this one, Krillin?"

Krillin ducks under the creature chasing him. "I'd rather see it from over here!"

Yamcha sends the ball toward his opponent. The Saibaiman jumps left, but the light turns with Yamcha's hand and strikes it in the side. It tumbles across the quarry floor.

"Got you."

The creature lies still beside a broken rail. Yamcha keeps his eyes on it as he moves toward Krillin. The ball of light hangs behind him, waiting for the movement of his hand.

The Saibaiman opens one eye. It springs from the dirt and wraps both arms around Yamcha's waist.

"Hey! Get off!"

Yamcha catches one of its hands and pulls. The grip holds. Krillin turns toward them, while Chiaotzu rises away from the track.

The creature's body swells against Yamcha's back. Its ki grows sharply, and light begins to flicker through its skin.

Yamcha looks over his shoulder. The creature's face is pressed close to his, its grip locked around his waist.

"Krillin, get back!"

He snaps his free hand upward. The waiting ball curves behind him and strikes the Saibaiman from below, prising it off before its swelling body can burst. Its arms break apart, and the light carries it high over the empty side of the quarry.

Yamcha dives toward the ground. Krillin catches his arm and pulls him behind the broken loading wall; they hit the dirt together as the creature explodes above the ridge. The shock blows loose boards across the tracks and rattles the old wall over their heads.

A splinter skims Yamcha's forearm. He keeps his face down until the last boards clatter onto the rails.

Yamcha lies still for a moment, then pushes himself up. His sleeve is torn, and a thin line of blood runs down his forearm.

"Yamcha?" Krillin asks.

"I'm all right. That thing blew itself up."

Chiaotzu lands beside them. "I felt its ki. I didn't know it could do that."

"Neither did I," Yamcha says.

He looks up at the smoke and takes a breath. His fingers shake before he closes them.

"Don't tell Puar how close that was," he says.

"You can tell Puar yourself," Krillin says. "After we get out of here."

Yamcha manages a shaky laugh. "Yeah. He'd know I was lying anyway."

The final Saibaiman comes over the broken wall behind Krillin. Piccolo's arm stretches past Chiaotzu and catches it around the middle.

"Krillin!"

Piccolo throws the creature into the open air above the tracks. Krillin fires before it can turn back. The burst catches it, and the light clears with nothing left above the rails.

Krillin lowers his hand. "That's all of them."

Tien helps Yamcha to his feet. "Can you use that arm?"

Yamcha bends his elbow and closes his hand. "Yes. It caught the sleeve more than me."

Piccolo looks toward Nappa and Gohan. "Keep clear of those two. Gohan has him away from the rest of us."

A streak of light crosses above the western wall. Goshin and Vegeta are still fighting beyond it.

## The Road Goku Runs

Goku rounds another bend on Snake Way. Clouds drift across the road ahead, then part around his shoulders as he runs through them.

He checks the supply capsule inside his clothes without slowing. It is still there.

"A whole year," he says.

Gohan's message had come through Kami. Goku remembers the room and the weight that pulled at him during his own visit.

"You two must have a lot to show me."

His smile fades when he thinks of what might be happening while he runs. He pumps his arms harder.

"Hang on. I'm coming."

The road bends again. Goku leans into the turn and keeps running.

## Nappa's Last Shot

Nappa gets to his feet. Gohan waits several paces away, watching both of his hands.

"You can leave," Gohan says. "Take your ship and go."

Nappa glances toward Vegeta. A blow flashes above the western ridge, and Vegeta shoots out of the light with Goshin following him.

"Leave him here?"

"He can leave too."

Nappa laughs, then winces as he straightens. "The prince won't run from a couple of children. And neither will I."

He raises his hands and fires a spread of blasts across the ground. Gohan jumps above them. The blasts break the rails behind him, throwing lengths of metal into the air.

Gohan catches the nearest rail with his ki and guides it down away from Krillin and Yamcha. Two more pieces fall against the empty platform.

Nappa rushes beneath him, heading for Piccolo. Gohan drops into his path and plants both feet on the ground.

Nappa pulls up short. "You were watching me instead of the metal?"

"I could feel where you were going."

"Then feel this."

Nappa pounds the ground with both fists. A shock races toward Gohan, lifting a line of stone between them. Gohan rises over it and sends a short burst into the ground behind the Saiyan. Nappa turns toward the sound, and Gohan lands beside him.

"You missed," Nappa says.

"I wasn't aiming at you."

Gohan takes hold of his arm before he can turn back toward the others. Nappa twists and swings his free elbow; Gohan releases the arm and steps clear.

Nappa comes forward without raising his hands. Gohan shifts to meet him. Nappa swings high, then drives his other fist toward Gohan's ribs.

Gohan turns the first punch aside and stops the second against his palm. Nappa bears down, but Gohan holds his ground.

"You're not moving me," Gohan says.

Nappa jerks his arm free and drops toward the ground. His fingers rise again, aimed toward Piccolo and the others beyond the tracks.

Gohan catches him around the waist and lifts him off the quarry floor. Nappa strikes at his back; Gohan turns and carries him away from the fighters.

"Put me down!"

Gohan throws him toward the bare slope. Nappa lands on his feet, digs two trenches through the stone and stops himself before he reaches the wall.

Light gathers in his open mouth.

Gohan sees it and rushes him. He drives his palm beneath Nappa's chin, forcing his head upward as the beam fires. It tears past Gohan's shoulder and climbs into the empty sky. Heat stings his hand; he keeps Nappa's head turned until the last of the light has passed the fighters below.

The blast shakes the air above the quarry. Nappa reaches for Gohan's tail, but Gohan is already moving around him. He catches Nappa's arm, turns it across his chest and pushes him back toward the bare wall.

"You keep aiming at my friends!"

Nappa pulls free and charges. Gohan steps inside his reach. His fist strikes Nappa's armour, and a crack spreads across the breastplate.

Nappa staggers but stays up. He raises his hand toward the fighters again.

Gohan cups his hands at his side. Blue light gathers between them.

"Kamehameha!"

The wave catches Nappa squarely and drives him into the empty wall. Stone breaks around him, and his cry is lost beneath the rush of the attack. Gohan ends the wave as Nappa strikes the rock, letting the last light break apart against the wall instead of feeding more into it.

Nappa drops to the ground at its foot. A slab slips above him; Gohan lifts it with his ki and lowers it beside him instead. Smaller stones patter against the damaged armour.

Nappa's hand opens. He does not get up. Gohan waits until he sees the man's chest move. The relief catches him by surprise: a moment ago Nappa was still trying to hurt his friends. Gohan lowers his hands a little.

Gohan backs away, keeping his eyes on him. Krillin and Piccolo approach from the tracks.

"Is he finished?" Krillin asks.

"He's breathing. I don't think he can fight now."

Piccolo looks at the broken wall, then toward the ridge. "Vegeta can."

Gohan turns. Goshin's ki is still moving above the quarry.

## What Goshin Has Learned

Vegeta lands on the far side of the ridge. Goshin comes down opposite him, with the quarry spread out below them.

"Your brother has beaten Nappa," Vegeta says.

Goshin looks toward the wall. Gohan is standing clear, with Piccolo and Krillin nearby.

"He stopped him."

"Nappa should have killed the others while he had the chance."

Goshin's hands close into fists. "You tried to hurt them too. Gohan wouldn't let Nappa near them."

"Neither of you understands what you are," Vegeta says.

"We're Goku and Chi-Chi's sons."

"You're Saiyans. You could have taken this planet for yourselves."

"We live here," Goshin says. "We don't have to take it."

Vegeta fires a blast past him toward the quarry floor. Goshin turns and knocks it above the ridge before it can reach the others.

Vegeta is already behind him. He strikes Goshin across the back, sending him down through the broken stone. Goshin catches himself before he reaches the floor and rises through the falling dust.

Vegeta follows with a rapid series of punches. Goshin turns the first, slips past the second and catches the third against his forearm. Vegeta changes his angle again, trying to draw him away from the quarry.

Goshin follows the movement, but this time he steps toward Vegeta instead of retreating. His palm strikes the Saiyan's chest. Vegeta is driven back across the ridge, boots skidding over its broken edge.

The stone at Vegeta's feet breaks as he lands. Goshin comes after him. Vegeta fires from his palm, forcing Goshin to turn the shot, then closes in behind it. His fist catches Goshin on the forearm before the redirected blast has cleared the ridge.

Goshin rolls with the impact. He comes out of the turn with his tail tucked close and his other hand already raised. Vegeta swings again. Goshin meets his fist; the pressure drives their arms between them and sends a crack through the rock underfoot.

Vegeta pushes. Goshin sets his feet and pushes back. Vegeta twists to free his hand, and Goshin lets him go rather than be pulled off balance. They break apart above the ridge, then come together again, trading blows through the falling dust. Each time Vegeta angles toward the quarry, Goshin turns with him.

Goshin feels his ki growing stronger as they fight. When they separate, the strength is still there, and the discovery thrills him. It comes behind his next step, carrying him to Vegeta before the Saiyan can rise from his crouch.

Vegeta blocks the first blow and sends a kick toward Goshin's side. Earlier, a strike from that direction pushed Goshin back. Now he catches Vegeta's ankle and holds it still.

Vegeta's eyes widen. Goshin turns and throws him over the empty ground.

Vegeta arrests his flight and lands beyond the tracks. His armour is cracked where Goshin hit it. He wipes blood from the corner of his mouth and looks at the boy again.

"You changed while we were fighting."

Goshin lifts his guard. He wants another exchange, another chance to find out what his body can do now, but he keeps the fighters below in the corner of his sight.

"Maybe I'll surprise you again."

Vegeta gives a sharp laugh. "Then watch closely."

His ki rises. Wind drives dust along the tracks and pulls at Piccolo's cape. Vegeta comes at Goshin again, no longer watching the fighters behind him.

Goshin meets him over the quarry. Their hands strike and part. Vegeta feints toward his tail, then turns a punch toward his face. Goshin catches the arm and strikes back.

Vegeta blocks, but Goshin drives him upward. They pass above the ridge, trading blows over the empty ground beyond it. Vegeta catches Goshin's wrist, twists under his arm and lets the boy's own momentum carry him past. By the time Goshin turns, Vegeta has shot high above him.

The prince faces down toward the quarry and brings his hands together at his side. Violet light gathers between his palms. Goshin starts after him, but Vegeta is already drawing more and more ki into the light. The broken rails below shudder against the stone.

Vegeta came for the Dragon Balls, for the wish that would make him immortal. Now Nappa is unconscious, the blood at the corner of Vegeta's mouth is his own, and Kakarot's son is still growing stronger.

"To hell with the Dragon Balls! I'll destroy you and this entire planet!"

The light swells as Vegeta draws every bit of power he can muster into the attack. Goshin flies faster, reaching for him, but the prince thrusts his palms forward before the boy can close the distance.

"Galick Gun!"

The violet beam roars down toward Goshin and the Earth beneath him. Its force tears stone from the ridge. Gohan looks up from the quarry floor, feeling the immense ki in the descending column.

"Get clear of its path!" he shouts.

Tien catches Chiaotzu's arm and flies toward the western wall with Yamcha. Krillin reaches Piccolo as Piccolo lifts Nappa; together they carry the unconscious Saiyan away from the line of fire.

Goshin turns beneath the attack and cups his hands. Blue light flares between them.

"Kamehameha!"

His wave strikes the Galick Gun above the ridge. The collision stops the descending beam for an instant, then drives Goshin backward through the air. He holds on, feeding ki into his wave, but the place where the two attacks meet begins to sink toward the quarry.

Gohan flies up to his brother's left. He draws on the power he has trained, bringing his hands to his side as he comes level with Goshin.

"I'm with you!"

Goshin glances at him. "Let's send it back!"

"Kamehameha!" Gohan calls.

Gohan's wave surges beside Goshin's and joins it against the violet beam. The air shakes around them. For a moment the Galick Gun still presses down. Gohan draws more ki into his wave, and Goshin drives upward beside him. The descent stops.

Their arms tremble as they force the meeting point higher, away from the quarry. Vegeta strains to keep his attack aimed at the planet, but the blue light keeps advancing.

Stone breaks from the ridge and scatters across the tracks. The force tears a length of rail loose and sends it tumbling above the western wall. Tien pulls Chiaotzu down beside him, and Yamcha knocks the rail away from them. Krillin and Piccolo shield Nappa from the falling fragments.

Goshin and Gohan keep pushing, their waves rising together through the sky. As the clash nears Vegeta's hands, he cuts off the Galick Gun and throws himself sideways. The combined light rushes past him and climbs into the open sky. The brothers hold their aim until the violet is gone, then end their waves.

In the city, the western sky flashes violet and blue above the stopped bus. Chi-Chi grips the door and looks toward the distant quarry. She has seen that blue light before, in the clearing where Goshin taught Gohan. The rumble follows, rattling glass in a damaged window. She cannot see her sons.

"Come back to me," she whispers.

A passenger is coming down the steps. Chi-Chi reaches up and steadies her until she is safely on the road.

Vegeta catches himself above the far ridge. His breath comes hard, and his hands shake as he raises them again. Goshin closes on him before he can fire. His punch strikes Vegeta's guard and drives him back over the quarry.

Gohan follows and rises beside his brother. Below them, Piccolo has laid Nappa against the western wall; Krillin is with him. Tien, Chiaotzu and Yamcha stand together beyond the tracks.

"Why are we saving him?" Krillin calls.

"Nappa told us about Namek," Piccolo answers. "I still have questions for him."

Krillin looks from the unconscious Saiyan to Vegeta. "Let's stay alive long enough to ask them."

"They're clear," Gohan says.

Goshin touches his cheek, then turns to Gohan. "Are you all right?"

"Yes. Nappa won't get up right now."

"Good. I don't want him near the others again."

Vegeta hovers across from them. He looks once toward Nappa, then back at the brothers.

Gohan watches Vegeta's hands. "You can still leave. Take Nappa with you."

"Leave? After two children put their hands on a Saiyan prince?"

Vegeta raises both arms. Light swells around him, and the broken rail below begins to shake.

Gohan moves to Goshin's right. "I'm staying with you."

Goshin nods. "Let's keep him away from the others."

Vegeta rushes them. Goshin meets him head-on, catching the descending arm before it reaches Gohan. Vegeta drives against his guard; Gohan turns toward the prince's open side, forcing Vegeta to twist between them.

Their first impact rolls across the empty quarry. Dust lifts from the tracks below as all three fighters press into the next exchange.

**NARRATOR:** Nappa is down, but Vegeta has not given up. Can the brothers stop him together?
