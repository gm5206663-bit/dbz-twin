## Someone Faster

While Bubbles picks at the grass beneath the tree, Goku stretches his arms above his head and turns to King Kai.

"What do I do next?"

"You're in a hurry."

"You said there were two of them. I'd like to be ready."

King Kai glances toward Bubbles. "And you think catching him has made you ready?"

"No. That's why I'm asking."

King Kai clears his throat and calls toward the far side of the house.

"Gregory! Come here a moment!"

A small figure darts over the roof, too quick for Goku to make out until he stops in front of King Kai. Gregory is a green, grasshopper-like creature with folded wings and long limbs; he hangs in the air as comfortably as Bubbles sits in the tree.

"What can I do for you?" Gregory asks.

Goku leans closer. "Hey, you talk."

"Of course I talk. My name is Gregory, and I assist King Kai."

"I'm Goku. You live here too?"

"I do, and I work very hard. Please don't mistake me for an ordinary grasshopper."

"I wasn't going to."

Gregory folds his arms. "You were looking at my wings."

"They're good wings."

King Kai laughs. Gregory turns toward him.

"Sir, why did you call me?"

"Goku needs another lesson. He's going to catch you."

"With his hands?" Gregory asks.

"Not exactly."

King Kai walks to the side of the house and pulls the hammer from its hook. The handle comes free first. He lowers the head onto the grass beside Goku.

"You have to hit Gregory with this."

Goku takes the handle in both hands and raises the head a little before it drops back onto the grass.

"That's heavy."

"It isn't meant to make the job easy."

Gregory backs away. "King Kai, perhaps I should explain how fast I am before he agrees."

"Go ahead."

"Very fast. Much faster than Bubbles."

When Bubbles makes a noise from beneath the tree, Gregory points at him.

"You know I am."

Goku bends his knees and lifts again, this time getting the hammer clear of the ground. His arms tremble as he brings it across his body.

"Do I have to hit him hard?"

"You have to hit him," King Kai says. "Gregory can look after himself."

"I'll still try not to hurt him."

Gregory rises above Goku's head. "You'll have to reach me first."

"And when I do," Goku says, "will you teach me yourself?"

"That's the agreement."

Goku shifts his hands on the handle. Gregory zips past his left shoulder.

The first swing misses. The hammer hits the ground and pulls Goku down with it.

## The Visit

At the mountain house, Gohan folds his library drawing along its old crease. Goshin holds the edge of the paper while Chi-Chi packs the food she has made for the trip. His black hair rises in the same wild spikes as Goku's. His face is almost his father's in miniature, with Chi-Chi's eyes. His tail curls around one leg as he looks up from the drawing.

"Hatch said they added three doors," Gohan says. "I want to see where they put them."

"And the stairs," Goshin says.

"Those were already there."

"I want to see them again."

Chi-Chi closes the basket. "We'll have time to look. I also want to speak with the director about next week."

Gohan puts the drawing in his pocket. "They can still use the room this week, can't they?"

"Yes. That's what she told us. She hasn't promised what happens after it."

When an engine approaches outside, Goshin moves to the window before the aircar settles beside the gate.

Bulma gets out and opens the rear door. "I brought the car with the good heater. Nobody complain about my driving."

"Your driving wasn't the problem last time," Chi-Chi says.

"Good. Then we'll have a peaceful trip."

Gohan and Goshin climb in. Chi-Chi sets the basket between their feet and takes the front seat.

As they leave the mountain road, Gohan leans forward. "Did you find out whose machine it is?"

"No," Bulma says. "It's still covered in the lab. I'm not taking it apart until I know how to do it without setting anything off."

"Will you tell us when you do?"

"I'll tell you what I find. I won't promise to find it today."

Goshin watches her in the mirror. "Is it making that picture?"

"Not while it's covered. Nobody's seen it make another one."

Goshin sits back. Gohan takes the folded drawing from his pocket and checks that the corners are still flat.

## The Hammer

While Goku pulls the hammer from a shallow mark in the grass, Gregory lands on the roof of King Kai's house.

"You need to pick it up before you can swing it," Gregory calls.

"I'm working on it."

Goku raises the handle to his shoulder. Gregory lifts off and travels around the planet. Goku follows, but the weight drags his arms to one side.

He corrects his footing, turns and swings. Gregory slips underneath the head. Goku stops the hammer before it reaches King Kai's car.

The handle bumps his shoulder.

"That was close," King Kai says.

"I didn't hit it."

"I was talking about my car."

Gregory settles on the car's roof. Goku lowers the hammer instead of swinging again.

"Come away from there," he says.

"Why?"

"Because I don't want to hit the car."

Gregory looks down at King Kai. "He may be here a very long time."

"Then you'd better have lunch before it gets cold," King Kai says.

Goku looks up. "Is it lunchtime already?"

"For Gregory. You can have yours when you put the hammer down."

"I'm putting it down."

Bubbles takes Gregory's place on the car roof while Goku sets the hammer carefully on the grass. Gregory passes close enough to touch Goku's hair on his way to the house.

Goku reaches up. Gregory is already through the door.

## A Place for the Doors

The six children are together in the common room when the family arrives. Hatch spreads the large boat drawing over the table. Ena places a book on one corner to keep it from curling.

"We made the doors," she tells Gohan.

Hatch points to the first door beside the library, the second at the top of the stairs and the third on a deck at the back of the boat.

"If you're reading," Hatch says, "you don't have to go through the classroom to get outside."

Gohan unfolds his smaller drawing. "I left space for the book we talked about. But Mina hasn't said anyone else can borrow it."

"Then let's leave the space," Ena says. "We can choose something later."

Rom pulls a chair up beside Gohan. "Can you get to the back deck without climbing the stairs?"

Hatch studies the page. "You could take the lift."

"What if it breaks?"

"You asked that last time," Chiko says.

"It could still break."

Goshin takes a pencil from the table and places it along the boat's side. "Could this be a slope?"

Hatch shifts the pencil. "There isn't room on the page."

Gohan finds a second sheet in the basket. "We could draw the side of the boat here. Then the slope wouldn't have to fit on the first picture."

Ena takes the sheet. "I want a rail on both sides."

"For the whole way?" Goshin asks.

"For the whole way."

Goshin draws two long lines. Hatch adds the deck above them. Ena puts a door where the slope reaches it, and Rom draws a square at the bottom.

"That's where you turn," Rom says. "So you don't roll into the water."

Yordon brings two cups of water from the kitchen, and Jink takes one and puts the other beside the pencils. Bulma pulls her chair closer when Hatch asks whether a real boat could carry a school.

"That depends on how big you build it," she says. "And how many people you put inside."

"All of us," Yordon says.

"You might need a bigger boat than this page."

Hatch spreads both hands over the paper. "We'll get more pages."

"Is Pigero coming this afternoon?"

Chiko takes hold of the doorframe. "Can I ask them something too?"

"You can write it down, and I'll put your question with the forms."

Chi-Chi offers her the pencil from the basket. Chiko takes it to the table and asks Ena for a corner of the spare page.

"Do you want help spelling anything?" Gohan asks.

"I'll ask if I do."

Chiko writes slowly, then carries the page to the director. "I want to know if Rom and I can go in together on the first day."

"I'll send your question with the forms," the director says. "I can't answer for the school."

Rom comes to read the page. Chiko holds it down while he follows the words with one finger.

Chi-Chi turns back to the director. "And after this week?"

"I'm still asking about the room and the school places. I won't move anyone without speaking to the children first."

"Please tell me when you know. If you want me to make a call, I'll ask you and the children before I give anyone their names."

"Thank you. I may take you up on that."

Before they leave, Gohan gives Ena the library page to hold beside the new side view.

"Can these stay together?" Ena asks.

"Yes." Gohan writes his name on the back of the library page. "That's the one to give back when you're finished."

Goshin puts the pencil beside Hatch's hand. "I'll ask C-6 if he wants to see the slope."

"We might add something first," Hatch says.

"Then I won't take it yet."

Pigero arrives as Bulma opens the car door. Chiko runs to meet him. Rom waves from the window, and Pigero waves back before going inside.

Chi-Chi and the boys get into the car. Bulma takes them back to the mountain house before turning toward the city.

## The Edge of the Clearing

A few days later, Gohan leads Chi-Chi and Goshin up the path to the broken ground above the clearing. Chi-Chi stops where she can see the low rock shelf.

"You can use that side," she says. "Stay where I can see you."

Goshin follows Gohan toward the shelf, glancing back at Chi-Chi.

Gohan places two sticks in the dirt. "I want to start here and land beyond the second one."

Goshin picks up a loose pebble and rolls it between his fingers.

A shadow moves across the shelf as Piccolo lands beyond the sticks, away from Chi-Chi.

"You're back," Gohan says.

"I felt you practising."

Piccolo looks at the marked ground. Gohan steps beside the first stick and points to the second.

"I'm trying to land there."

"That ground is uneven."

"That's why I chose it."

Chi-Chi sets her basket on a flat stone. "They chose this exercise themselves. Let's see what they make of it."

Piccolo looks at Gohan. "Do you want me to move the target?"

"No. I want you to try to make me miss it."

Goshin puts the pebble down. "I want a turn after him."

"I'll go first," Gohan says. "Then it's Goshin's turn."

Piccolo takes a position between the sticks. Gohan raises his hands.

Gohan steps left. Piccolo reaches for his shoulder, and Gohan ducks under the arm. His next step lands on a tilted stone. He twists away from Piccolo but misses the far marker, dropping into the shallow cut beyond it.

Gohan checks his fall with his ki, catches the edge with both hands and finds the rock below with his feet.

Chi-Chi moves closer. "Are you hurt?"

"No. I can get up."

Goshin kneels above the cut. "There's a place for your foot on the left."

Gohan looks down. "I see it."

He sets one foot on the ledge, pushes and reaches the top. Piccolo waits without offering a hand. Gohan stands, brushes his knees and moves the tilted stone aside.

"Again," he says.

This time Gohan steps toward Piccolo, then turns sharply across the marked ground. Piccolo pivots to follow, but Gohan reaches the second stick and lands with both feet.

"Better," Piccolo says. "You didn't look at the place you wanted to go until you moved."

Gohan smiles and steps back for Goshin.

"Will you try it with me too?" Goshin asks.

Piccolo raises one hand. "Come on."

Goshin lifts from the ground and moves across the shelf. Piccolo reaches toward him. Goshin turns in the air, drops below the hand and lands beyond the second stick.

Piccolo sends a quick punch toward Goshin's guard. Goshin catches the wrist, turns with the movement and releases it before either of them reaches Chi-Chi's basket.

Piccolo moves between Goshin and the first marker. Goshin takes a step toward him, then rises over his outstretched arm. Piccolo turns to follow, but Goshin lands beside the stick and holds his ground.

"You could have kept flying," Piccolo says.

"Gohan chose a landing place. I wanted to land there too."

"Then don't let me move you off it."

Piccolo presses forward. Goshin braces and catches his hand between both palms. Goshin pushes back, but his shoes skid a hand's width through the dirt. The stick trembles behind his heel.

He digs in and pushes harder. Piccolo's arm keeps coming forward, steady enough to make Goshin choose between holding the position and slipping away. Goshin gathers ki in his hands, but the pressure still drives him back. Piccolo reaches with his other hand, and his fingers tap Goshin's shoulder before he can turn.

"You were watching that hand," Piccolo says, keeping the first one in Goshin's grip.

Goshin looks at it, then raises his eyes to Piccolo's face.

Before he changes his footing, more strength runs through Goshin's arms. His ki swells with it, and his heels stop sliding. Fine cracks spread beneath his shoes. He pushes forward, forcing Piccolo back three steps across the shelf; the loose pebble skips away from their feet.

Piccolo's eyes narrow. He plants a foot and comes at him again, faster this time, reaching for the same shoulder. Goshin rises from the ground and catches his wrist before the hand arrives. He turns in the air and touches Piccolo's shoulder, then lands beside the stick.

Piccolo looks down at the tracks, then at Goshin. "You weren't moving like that when we started."

Goshin looks at the marks his shoes left, then back at Piccolo. "Can we do it again?"

Piccolo lowers his hand. "Next time I won't let you turn past me so easily."

Goshin steps back, breathing hard but grinning. "Then I'll find another way." Gohan brings him the pebble from the first marker.

"You left this."

Goshin takes it. "Will you show me that turn you used?"

Gohan plants his feet and shows him. Goshin follows the movement, then returns to the first stick to practise it on the ground.

Chi-Chi comes over and points to Gohan's shoulder. "You showed him the turn before your feet moved. Try it without the warning."

Gohan tries again, and Goshin steps in to meet him. After watching for another moment, Piccolo tells Gohan to keep working on his landing and rises over the ridge. The boys finish their exchange and gather the sticks with Chi-Chi.

## Dinner and a Story

On King Kai's planet, Goku eats with the hammer propped against the outside wall. Gregory sits on the table, rubbing his arms.

"You haven't touched me once," Gregory says.

"I know."

"I thought you might have lost count."

King Kai serves another helping. Goku finishes it before asking for the pot.

"You're moving better under the gravity," King Kai says. "That doesn't mean your opponents will be easy."

Goku sets down his bowl. "You knew about the Saiyans before I came here. What else do you know?"

Gregory rises from the table. "King Kai knows a great deal."

"Let me answer him," King Kai says.

Gregory sits again.

"Have you heard of Planet Vegeta?"

"Raditz said that was the Saiyans' planet. He didn't tell me much about it."

"Saiyans lived there with another people called the Tsufurians. The Tsufurians had cities and advanced machines. The Saiyans were fewer, and they were strong fighters."

Goku rests his hands on the table. "Were they fighting each other?"

"The Saiyans began a war against them. The Tsufurians had weapons, but the Saiyans kept attacking. When a full moon appeared on that planet, the Saiyans could become enormous apes."

Goku looks down at the place where his own tail used to be.

"Roshi told us about that. He saw me change once."

"On Planet Vegeta, a full moon was rare," King Kai says. "That doesn't tell you when Earth's moon will be full."

"My boys still have their tails. Their mother knows to keep them from looking at it."

King Kai waits while Goku fills his bowl again.

"The Saiyans won," King Kai continues. "The Tsufurians were destroyed, and the Saiyans took their technology. Later they travelled to other worlds, fought the people there and sold the planets they conquered."

Goku stops with his spoon halfway to his mouth. "Raditz said they killed people and sold their worlds. Did they do that over and over?"

"That's the account I've heard."

"Raditz said a giant meteor destroyed Planet Vegeta. Do you know if that's true?"

"It was destroyed. One story says an angry god drew a great meteor into it. I didn't see it happen, so I won't tell you that part is certain."

"Raditz said there were only four of us left. Could there have been others?"

"I heard that a few Saiyans survived. I can't give you a reliable count of everyone alive now."

Goku lowers the spoon. "Raditz hurt Gohan and Goshin. He wanted me to leave with him and help attack other people."

Gregory's wings settle against his back.

"Were there children on those other planets?" Goku asks.

"There were families," King Kai says. "They had their own homes, just as the Tsufurians did."

Goku looks at the empty bowl. "My sons didn't ask Raditz to come after them. I don't suppose those families asked for it either."

He sets the bowl down and turns toward the open door. Beyond it, the hammer rests against the wall where he left it.

"I won't let the ones coming do that here," Goku says. "My family and my friends are on Earth."

"Then keep training," King Kai says. "You still have work to do before you face them."

Goku finishes his meal and takes the bowl to the door. "Thanks for telling me."

Gregory follows him outside. "Are you ready to try again?"

"Are you?"

Gregory shoots over the tree. Goku takes up the hammer and runs after him.

## Later That Week

Gohan sets an old board against the empty slope beyond the clearing. Goshin helps him brace it with two stones. Chi-Chi watches from the path while the boys return to their starting marks.

"I'll go first," Gohan says. "I want to hit the left side."

He brings his hands together and calls the wave. Blue light travels across the clearing and strikes the board where he aimed. The board falls backward against the slope.

Gohan runs to look. His mark has crossed the line he drew on the left, but it has not touched the right side.

"It's better than yesterday," he says.

"You held it longer before you sent it," Goshin says.

Gohan carries the board back and braces it against the slope again. The round hollow Goshin made when he taught Gohan the wave is still visible above it.

"Your turn," Gohan says.

Goshin sets four pebbles in front of the marker. He lifts them with his ki and sends them along separate paths while he cups his hands. The strength that rose in his arms against Piccolo comes more readily now. He draws it into a wave as narrow as the one he showed Gohan before.

"Kamehameha!"

The wave strikes the edge of the old hollow. Last time it knocked a small chip free. This time it cuts a deep channel beyond the old mark, and a crack runs up the bank. A slab wider than the board begins to tip forward.

Goshin closes his palms to stop the wave and catches the slab with his ki before it can fall onto the board. The four pebbles keep moving while he lowers the slab onto clear ground beside the bank.

Gohan walks over to the slab, staying clear of the cracked stone. "That went farther in than before."

Goshin joins him and looks at the new cut. Gohan holds his own hands together. "Will you show me how you made it narrow?"

Goshin cups his hands beside Gohan's. "Like this."

Chi-Chi studies the crack from the path. "We'll put the next target against a solid part of the slope. The board will do again, and I'll cut another after lunch so you can each have one."

Goshin sets the four pebbles in a line beside his marker. Together the boys carry the board down to the house, and Gohan draws a new target on its other side.

The telephone rings while Chi-Chi is cooking. Gohan reaches the house before the second ring and calls her to the receiver.

The director tells Chi-Chi that the children can keep the room for one more week while she follows up the placement questions; she has sent the school forms with Chiko's question, but no places have been confirmed.

"Did you speak to the children?" Chi-Chi asks.

"I did. They want to stay together. Pigero may visit again this week, and I've told him the times."

"Thank you for calling before this week's arrangement ended."

Gohan waits beside the table. Chi-Chi covers the receiver.

"Do you want to ask something?"

"Can I speak to Hatch?"

A moment later Hatch comes to the telephone. Gohan asks about the boat. Hatch says Ena has added another rail and Jink wants a place to dry wet shoes.

"Did you show Pigero?" Gohan asks.

"Yes. He said the deck needs a roof. We haven't decided if he's right."

Gohan looks toward the door. Goshin is waiting there with the pencil in his hand.

"Can Goshin talk to you too?"

Hatch agrees. Goshin takes the receiver with both hands.

"Did the slope fit?"

"On the second page. We had to make it longer."

"Did you keep the rail?"

"Both of them."

Goshin looks toward Gohan, then back at the receiver. "All right."

After the call, Chi-Chi returns to the stove. Goshin stands beside her while she adds vegetables to the pot. Gohan takes the board outside and finds a dry place to keep it.

## What Baba Cannot See

At Kame House, Roshi sets two cups on the table. Baba sits opposite him with her crystal ball between them.

"I want you to look at Earth's future," Roshi says.

"That's a large request."

"I know. Two Saiyans are coming, and I'd like to know what we're facing."

Baba places her hands near the crystal. The surface clouds, then clears without showing an image.

She tries again. Roshi leans forward, but the ball stays empty.

"I can't read it," Baba says.

"Not even a little?"

"Not this time. I can't tell you what will happen."

Roshi sits back. "That doesn't mean it's bad?"

"It means I can't see it."

As the sea sounds through the open window, Roshi lifts one cup and offers it to her.

"Goku asked me about Grandpa Gohan," Roshi says. "He wanted to know whether he could have hurt him when he changed under the moon."

"What did you tell him?"

"That I wasn't there, and I didn't know. Could you try to find Gohan and ask him?"

Baba turns the cup in her hands. "I can try, but I can't promise when I'll find him or whether he'll answer."

"That's all I'm asking. Goku would want to hear whatever he chooses to say."

"Then I'll let you know if I speak to him."

Baba takes a drink. Roshi leaves the other cup on the table until she has finished looking at the crystal.

## The Second Week

While Gregory circles the tree on King Kai's planet, Goku follows with the hammer across his shoulder, taking shorter steps than he did on the first day.

Gregory dives toward the ground. Goku begins a swing, sees the change in direction and stops. The head of the hammer settles into the grass instead of striking the tree.

"You nearly had me," Gregory says.

"You went under it again."

"I can go over it next time, if you prefer."

Goku lifts the hammer. "Don't tell me which one."

Bubbles runs across the open ground. Goku waits for him to clear the path before starting after Gregory again.

The days pass. Goku practises lifting the hammer, stopping it and changing direction without letting its weight pull him off his feet. Gregory chooses new paths around the house and over the tree. They stop for meals and begin again.

After two weeks, Gregory flies low over the grass. Goku follows without swinging. When Gregory turns toward the house, Goku steps across his path and raises the hammer.

Gregory darts upward. Goku turns with him and brings the head down.

Tap.

Gregory drops onto the grass and stares up. Bubbles claps beside the tree.

"You got me," Gregory says.

Goku sets the hammer down. "Are you all right?"

"You didn't need to be so gentle. I'm harder than rock."

"You've got a bump."

Gregory touches his head. His hand stops over a small swelling.

"I didn't have that before."

King Kai comes out of the house. Gregory points at Goku, lowers his hand and rubs the bump instead.

"I could have hit him harder," Goku says, "but I didn't want to hurt him."

"He did hit me," Gregory tells King Kai. "That was the agreement."

King Kai looks at the mark on the grass and then at Goku.

"Yes, it was."

Goku lifts the hammer by its handle. "Then it's your turn to teach me?"

"It is. Don't expect it to be easy."

"I don't."

"I want more than a strong body. You'll need to pay attention to your mind and your control as well."

"All right. What do we do?"

King Kai points to the house. "First, Gregory will bring us some tea."

Gregory looks at him. "Now?"

"After you find something for that bump."

Goku laughs. Gregory gives him a look and goes inside. Goku follows, leaving King Kai and Bubbles beside the tree.

King Kai looks toward the hammer. "Perhaps he can learn the Kaiō-ken," he says quietly. "And the other technique, if there's time to teach it."

Bubbles reaches for the grass at King Kai's feet. King Kai waits until Goku calls from the house, then goes in for his tea.

## The First Direct Lesson

King Kai stands in front of the house after tea. Goku places the hammer back on its hook, with the head resting on the grass.

"Gather your ki," King Kai says. "Hold it steady while you move. Then raise it without letting it spill outward."

Goku sets his feet. Light gathers around his hands and spreads across his arms. He steps across the grass and turns, keeping the light steady. Bubbles moves behind King Kai to watch.

"Like that?" Goku asks.

"That's your starting point. Now raise it again, and keep it tight through the turn."

Goku draws on more ki. His boots press deeper into the grass, but the light stays close to him. He takes a step, then turns.

"I can hold this!"

The light flares as he finishes the turn, and loose leaves lift around his feet. Goku brings the ki back to the level he held before.

King Kai lowers the hand he raised against the wind. "You recovered it, but the higher level surged. Keep that level even while you turn."

Goku shifts his feet. "All right. I'll try again."

He moves forward, raises his ki and starts the turn once more.

Far away, after more days of practice at home, Chi-Chi sets a fresh board against a solid part of the mountain slope. Gohan brings the marking sticks. Goshin follows, keeping four pebbles moving above his open palm.

"Are we starting from the first stick?" Gohan asks.

"From the first stick," Chi-Chi says.

Goshin guides the pebbles down beside the first stick. Gohan takes his position, and Chi-Chi steps back onto the path.

On King Kai's planet, the hammer hangs beside the house. The hook bends under its weight, but the head does not move.

**NARRATOR:** Goku's direct training has begun. On Earth, the others are still preparing, and no one has been given a certain day for the Saiyans' arrival.
