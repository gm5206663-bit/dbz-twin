## Growth and the readings

Vegeta infers that Saiyan/Earthling hybrids can be powerful and fears their continued growth (ep. 5, 22:22–23:13; `C142`). Gohan then grows in multiple ways: training, latent ability, temporary surges, recovery after severe injury and later rituals. The **1** immediately after his Raditz outburst is not a lifelong baseline. His enraged Masenko reads **2,800** in ep. 28; episode 65 reports a momentary **over 20,000** after a Masenko call, interpreted in context as Gohan's but not target-named in that cue (`C146`). At ep. 80, 18:07–18:11, Vegeta explicitly links Gohan's increase after Dende's healing to Saiyan blood. `canon/RESEARCH.md §2` separates the readings and their conditions.

**Goshin's ~400** is our scouter snapshot at Kame House before Raditz takes both boys (`O005`), not his later permanent floor. His exceptional supernatural Talent and deepening whole Saiyan–Earthling hybrid inheritance support cumulative whole-being growth: raw physical and ki power, recovery, instincts, senses and techniques can develop together, including rapidly in combat (`O004`). The Talent is not just technique copying; gains already integrated become part of his later foundation. A suppressed, tired or injured reading can fall without erasing those gains. His standing capability is never beneath Gohan's by the user's direction, not by a canon equation. No Nappa ceiling, mandatory canon death, automatic ranking above all other hybrids, or fixed later number is imposed.

## Tail, flight and damage

- Piccolo considers taking Gohan's tail in ep. 8; it is missing in ep. 9, and in ep. 18 he says he pulled it. Goshin's kept tail is authored (`O046`). Vegeta and Nappa trained away their tail weakness (ep. 27), not by merely seeing a grip once.
- The natural moon's destruction does not end every ape route: ep. 18 has a pod-generated projection and ep. 31 has Vegeta's artificial moon. Natural restoration and future SS4 continuity need their own verification. The serial's ruled future route stands as authored.
- Piccolo flies. In ep. 6 it is **Goku**, not Piccolo, who says fatigue prevents further flight on Snake Way. Ordinary ki and flight are allowed now; **god ki is not**.
- Episode 22 reports East City's disappearance after Nappa's greeting. His guidebook value of 4,000 is not an on-screen measurement of the blast. Piccolo's 408 was a separate earlier reading, not a reading of the moon attack. No numerical formula converts battle power into collateral or guarantees victory.

## Later continuity and the clock

The Earth set is tied to Kami, not Namek's set. Piccolo's fusion with Kami removes Earth's balls until Dende revives them. In ep. 173, about **100 days** is the estimate to **make new balls**, whereas the old stones and dragon model permit immediate revival; the group selects a **two-wish** arrangement for group resurrection. Goku dies fighting Cell, not on Namek, and later chooses not to be revived in canon.

Raditz's opening date is a retrospective guidebook assignment, not episode dialogue. Trunks's three years begin with his warning, not Raditz's arrival. Ep. 171's anime dialogue says Gohan became **11 in the time chamber**; other time/age references conflict. The author directs Goshin to spend a physical training year in the chamber before the Saiyan arrival (`O155`); unaccepted Chapter 22 shows **both boys** living that year, and unaccepted Chapter 23 stages the landing later the same morning as the boys' exit. This early stay does not itself decide whether he accompanies Gohan during the later Cell-era visit. Account for both boys' added lived time without inventing an exact birthday or transferring the later Cell-era visit. The guidebook date pair is 12 October Age 761 to 3 November Age 762, nearly 13 months; anime countdowns and this branch's approximate timing remain separate (`C067`). Exact later ages remain a source-and-divergence question (`C140`, `canon/RESEARCH.md §6`).

## Goten and the parents

Goten is canonically a prodigy, not an untrained replacement. In ep. 206 he says **Chi-Chi taught him sparring**, and he can transform before he can fly. Gohan teaches him flight in ep. 207. Gohan does not cease to grow or matter because Goshin is capable. Chi-Chi tells Roshi and the others that she has come to learn what happened; **Roshi tells her in ep. 8**, not Krillin in ep. 6 (`C120`, `C121`).
