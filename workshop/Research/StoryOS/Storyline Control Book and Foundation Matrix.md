III. 3-Tier Canon & Fact Classification Protocol
Every detail introduced into a story chapter must be categorized into one of three strict tiers:
1. Tier I: Confirmed Canon: Verified primary source facts directly witnessed or stated in official media (episodes, chapters, books, official lore).
2. Tier II: Reasonable Inference: Logical, necessary extensions required to bridge gaps where primary source material is silent, strictly adhering to world rules.
3. Tier III: AU Speculation & Divergence: Explicit non-canon changes or butterfly effects caused by original character actions or alternate timeline setup.

V. 12-Point Master Release Gate Audit Checklist (storyos-master-auditor)
Before accepting or locking any draft chapter, execute a full audit against these 12 points:
1. Canon Accuracy: Verified against primary source facts? (3-tier classification strictly maintained).
2. Character Consistency: Knowledge firewall intact? Voice, speech cadence, and motivations authentic?
3. Timeline Consistency: Wall-clock time, weather, travel durations, and locations verified?
4. Technical Realism: Scientific laws, physical limits, and engineering vocabulary verified?
5. Dialogue Authenticity: Natural hesitations, broken thoughts under stress, zero villain over-explaining?
6. World Activity: Off-screen faction movements and ambient living-world texture reflected?
7. Butterfly Effects: Logical cause-and-effect chain intact without forced canon restoration?
8. Emotional Continuity: Trauma, grief, trust stages, and psychological recovery respected?
9. Plot Logic: Zero convenient inventions (portals, secret databases, instant cures)?
10. State & Inventory Persistence: Active wounds, fatigue, biological reserves, clothing damage, weapons, and tools tracked continuously across turns?
11. Future Setup: Subtle setup for upcoming canon events and long-term narrative payoff established?
12. Micro-Pacing & Dramatization: Full beat-by-beat dramatization (~1800&ndash;2500 words per chapter) with zero summarized key interactions.
