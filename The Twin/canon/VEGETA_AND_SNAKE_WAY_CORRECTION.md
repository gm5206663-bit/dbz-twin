## What prompted the work and what was actually reviewed

The author identified a serious characterization failure and delegated research and correction of other supported problems. Vegeta's earlier portrayal left him hostile but too passive about Freeza, his own survival and the Dragon Balls. This pass checked the selected Vegeta, Nappa, watch, medical, communication and return sequences across the manuscript, read Chapters 23 and 28 and substantial relevant scenes individually, searched the selected chapters and living records for related information, and compared targeted source cues. It was **not a fresh sequential read of all 44 chapters**, a complete audiovisual episode review or a guarantee that no other mistakes remain. Earlier broader reviews are separate dated work, not evidence of this pass's scope.

## Applied story revisions

- **Chapter 6:** Vegeta privately wants immortality as a possible way to survive and overthrow a stronger Freeza, while keeping the plan from a potentially transmitting scouter. This is an authored early motive, not proof every transmission was or was not received; Chapter 36 later shows receipt of particular spoken fragments, not his private thoughts.
- **Chapters 23–28:** King Kai gives an uncertain tomorrow estimate, explicitly allowing an earlier arrival. Goku warns Roshi and Kami, intends to finish training and return, and remains with King Kai. Kami tries unsuccessfully to alert him when the Saiyans land. Goku's training intercuts with the city and quarry scenes; only after the final test does King Kai discover the error and the stopped fight. Goku leaves immediately, uses his established Kaiō-ken, meets Kami at Yemma's end and reunites with his family. The Moon/tail memories occur in the reunion. Popo brings the bags earlier, and Chi-Chi transfers the blanket to Launch's car before Bulma departs.
- **Chapter 29:** Goku says he did not eat on the urgent return, and the capsule description no longer implies repeated checks on a long road. His Chapter 29 refusal of Dr. Mori remains.
- **Chapters 32 and 34–36:** The family talks beyond the Saiyans' hearing while the separate guards remain visible. Vegeta still refuses examination through Chapter 32. In Chapter 34 he independently requests limited assessment to recover for his own ends: he sets boundaries, consents to the stethoscope, can stop it, and does so; the doctor retains a safe route and cannot diagnose his unfamiliar anatomy or promise recovery. In Chapter 35 he accepts food, considers how Goshin might serve his anti-Freeza aims, and suspects the ship's destination without a confirmed ball set. He drinks the offered water in Chapter 36 and declines another examination. Nappa's relationship with Vegeta remains hostile and his consent is separate.
- **Chapters 37–41:** Chi-Chi's nearby wording does not turn the planned destination into a confirmed report. In Chapter 38 Vegeta connects his earlier suspicion about a Namekian creator to a possible further set; he does not rely on an unshown statement heard by Goku. He admits Freeza is stronger and wants the wish for himself, bargains for his ship, provides limited scouter/search information and argues against Freeza gaining the wish first. Nappa and his guards are accounted for across the tracks. In Chapter 39 Vegeta uses a distraction to attempt escape when Goku is the only guard; the injured prince is intercepted by Goku using the established Kaiō-ken. Tien and Chiaotzu remain responsible for Nappa, who does not assist Vegeta. In Chapters 40–41 the prince tries to exploit the boys' fight and the guards evaluate the attempted escape. No successful escape, automatic victory or redemption is inserted.
- **Chapter 44:** Krillin actually relays Bulma's identification of Namek within Vegeta's hearing. Vegeta proposes conditionally hiding a ball to deny Freeza the wish, while privately intending to take the rest and wish for himself. He does not know a Namekian set, its number, its rules or current custody. Goku asks King Kai to pass the warning if he can; King Kai agrees to try, but actual delivery is not shown. The inlet cliffhanger and the independent actions and existing gains of both boys remain.

## Knowledge and future-use boundaries

1. Vegeta already knew Piccolo was called a Namekian and that Namek is a planet. Quiet local remarks and Goku's later comments allow suspicion about the travellers' destination; his Chapter 35 thought is a deduction, not confirmation. The family conversation in Chapter 32 and King Kai's coordinates remain private. Krillin's Chapter 44 relay confirms where Bulma says they are.
2. Gohan, Dende and Bulma have seen or learned of Muri's ball locally; their knowledge is not automatically given to Vegeta or Goku. Vegeta's plan in Chapter 44 is conditional and does not name a total or claim an existing possession. Goku's request that King Kai pass the warning has not yet resulted in an on-page delivery to Namek.
3. Vegeta knows and resents Freeza before any revelation about Planet Vegeta. The disclosure to the sons in Chapter 40 does not teleport to the Earth quarry. The prince is still injured, dangerous and guarded; the provisional watch and the unopened pod do not establish secure custody, a safe spacecraft or transport.
4. The chronology repair relocates the late warning and urgent return instead of slowing Goku. No precise speed, exact travel time, afterlife cap or time dilation is imposed. The historical approximate road length remains an approximation. The new sequence is a proposed literary repair for author review, not a mathematical or canon certificate.
5. Goshin's authored appearance, entire hybrid bloodline and extraordinary cumulative whole-being growth remain; temporary Kaiō-ken is not retained growth. Gohan retains independent choices and growth. Neither scouter snapshots nor source matchups are treated as ceilings. The boys' and Vegeta's tails and the natural Moon remain as established.
