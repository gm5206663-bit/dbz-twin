# Chapter 7 candidate — source coverage and continuity review

**Spoilers for the candidate.** This is a review aid, not part of the story and not an acceptance of Chapters 6 or 7. Episode base: Dragon Ball Z episode 6. The complete local Japanese episode-006 caption file was read before the chapter was drafted; its provenance and exact cut are unverified. Toei's and Kanzenshuu's episode summaries were also consulted. **The complete episode video was not watched, and the manga was not reread for this candidate.** Dialogue captions cannot establish every silent image or its choreography, and the new plane chase and other detailed divergence are authored rather than presented as source footage.

The pre-prose plan at `00_EPISODE_6_BEAT_PLAN.md` put the episode's major beats in source order, but grouped some individual exchanges. The more granular check below was completed during revision, after the first prose pass; it should not be mistaken for a claim that every line of this table was separately listed before drafting. The existing detailed `SCENE_COVERAGE_006.md` in the protected project was also consulted before drafting.

## Authored consequences and open threads

- **What changed on the page:** Piccolo intercepts an aircraft already in flight. Gohan deliberately uses Nimbus to draw him away from the injured Goku; Goshin follows, fights Piccolo, and repeatedly helps his brother. Gohan pilots, contributes to their escapes, bandages Goshin, warns him about falling rock and makes clear what he wants. They are now together in the woods with Piccolo following—not safely home and not placed in a predetermined year-long custody arrangement.
- **Parents and allies:** Goku contributes two restrained blasts despite injury and sets out with Roshi; Bulma brings the damaged aircraft down and stays to assess it. Chi-Chi learns of the original kidnapping from Turtle, not of the later rescue or current Piccolo attack, and travels with Ox-King. Turtle can know what he saw at Kame House, not the subsequent crater outcome. Popo leaves the Lookout to help; he has not arrived. Kami's request is unanswered. Krillin changes course toward the children's and Piccolo's powers. The next chapter must follow these live threads causally, without letting arriving adults reduce the boys to passengers.
- **Senzu:** Korin has three ready in this authored branch and gives them to Krillin. None is eaten or applied in this chapter. A Senzu completely heals and restores the person who eats it; no partial healing is implied. Goshin has not watched these beans' preparation and cannot eat one under his food rule. The count and timing are authored, not episode-6 evidence.
- **Injuries and capability:** Goku's rib-area/arm injuries continue to limit him and both uses of ki have costs. Gohan's shoulder and Goshin's head, burned palm and newly grabbed ankle still matter. No exact diagnosis, permanent disability, new scan, numerical damage formula, free observational power increase, transformation or god ki is asserted. Goshin uses established flight and ki; he does not copy telekinesis. He remains capable alongside Gohan, who makes independent choices.
- **Habits:** applicable spoken instructions to Goshin are repeated before he complies; he waits to be invited into his father's conversation; he takes the downhill side beside Gohan on the path. Chi-Chi does not examine him and he does not eat on page, so those two habits are not manufactured as scenes.
- **Objects:** Gohan keeps the four-star hat. Bulma takes the wrapped, unpowered scouter and its separate cell into different pockets. Roshi carries the medical case with the Dragon Radar. Bulma has not proved the scouter safe. No hospital is introduced.
- **Future scope:** The existing Chapter 8 and Chapter 10 cannot be attached unchanged to this ending. In particular, the rejected C-6 adventure remains a later, separate repair; neither the robot's outcome nor the boys' future location is fixed here.
