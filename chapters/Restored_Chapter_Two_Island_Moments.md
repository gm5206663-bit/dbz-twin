# Restored Chapter Two scenes — Kame House

**Historical proposal, integrated 1 November 2026.** The user authorized the five passages to be incorporated into revised Chapter Two, with contextual adjustments. The chapter body is the current reading; this file preserves the earlier separate proposals and does not describe additional events. Originally, these scenes restored missed moments without silently changing the accepted text. The sweets fit in section two after Krillin speaks about Launch; the crab fits during the training conversation in section three; the stones fit just before section four. In section six, the valley passage replaces Roshi's single paragraph about Goku's childhood, immediately before Goku says, “So that's me.” The Saiyan-business passage fits at the end of section six, after Goku says he is standing in Raditz's way.

## The sweets

Krillin reaches into Bulma's box and takes another manju.

"Save some for Goku," Bulma says.

Krillin puts it in his mouth. He closes the lid and slides the box toward the middle of the table.

"There are plenty left."

Roshi reaches for the box. Bulma catches it first and holds it out of his way.

From above the beach, Goku calls to them.

---

## The crab

Gohan reaches toward Turtle's shell, then stops before touching it.

"May I?"

"Go ahead," Turtle says.

Gohan puts a hand on the shell. Goshin is already sitting beside Turtle, and he moves over to make room. Gohan climbs onto the broad back and holds on while Turtle walks toward the water.

"Don't go too far," Goku calls.

"We won't," Gohan says.

A crab emerges from behind the shell and raises its claws. Gohan leans forward to look at it.

"Hello."

The crab opens and closes one claw. Gohan looks back at Goshin.

"Do you want to play?"

Goshin crawls onto the other side of Turtle's shell. The crab turns toward him, then back toward Gohan.

"Rock, paper, scissors," Gohan says.

Gohan makes a fist. The crab holds up its open claw.

"I won!"

The crab blows a spray of bubbles into Gohan's face. Gohan wipes his eyes, and Goshin laughs. Turtle stops at the edge of the water.

"My turn," Goshin says.

Goshin opens his hand. The crab raises its claw again.

"You lost," Gohan says.

"I know."

The crab blows more bubbles. Goshin tries to catch one, and it bursts against his palm. Gohan holds out his own hand for the next one, but the crab retreats behind Turtle's neck.

"You can come back," Gohan tells it.

Turtle turns toward the house. Both boys hold on while Turtle carries them up the beach.

---

## The stones

Krillin walks down to the water with Goku. Gohan and Goshin follow, and Roshi comes to the door to watch them.

Krillin picks up a flat stone and throws it across the water. It skips twice before it sinks.

"I used to be better at that," he says.

Gohan crouches and looks through the stones at the edge of the beach. He finds a flat one and gives it to Goshin.

"You can try."

Goshin throws. The stone touches the water once and disappears. He picks up another and turns it in his hand while Krillin finds one for Gohan.

Gohan throws his stone with both feet planted in the sand. It lands in the shallows and sends a little water over his shoes.

Goku takes a stone from beside his boot and throws it out to sea. It crosses the water and disappears from sight.

Krillin shades his eyes. "You haven't changed much."

Roshi steps down from the doorway. "I thought living with a family might have made you take it easy."

"I still train," Goku says.

Goshin offers him another stone. Goku takes it and lets Goshin pick one too.

"Can you show me?" Gohan asks.

Goku crouches beside him and places a flat stone in his hand. He turns Gohan's fingers around its edge, then points across the water.

Gohan throws. This time the stone skips once.

"It did it!"

Goshin throws his next stone. It skips twice, and he looks at Gohan. Gohan picks up another one and holds it out to him.

Goku reaches for a stone of his own, then stops.

## The valley

Roshi steps forward from the broken wall.

"Your grandfather told me about the day he found you, Goku."

A round pod lies in a deep valley. Grandpa Gohan pushes branches aside and kneels beside it. Inside is a baby with a tail.

"He took you home and tried to raise you."

Grandpa Gohan holds the baby in his arms. Goku cries and pushes at him. A bowl of food falls from the table, and Grandpa Gohan bends to pick it up.

"You were a difficult child. Then you fell into the valley and struck your head."

Grandpa Gohan carries Goku home. He lays the injured child down and stays beside him.

"You nearly died. After you recovered, you were a different boy."

Grandpa Gohan offers Goku his hand. This time Goku takes it.

On the island, Roshi looks at Goku. Gohan presses against Bulma, and Goshin holds his brother's hand.

---

## What the Saiyans do

Raditz looks past Goku at the house.

"We find planets worth selling," he says. "We kill the people living there, then sell the empty planets to someone else."

Round pods land on another world. Armoured Saiyans step out. Their attacks tear through buildings as the people below run for cover.

A line of pods rises from the ruined ground and disappears into the sky.

Krillin gets to his feet beside the broken wall. "You sell the planets?"

"When the people on a planet are strong, we send adult warriors. On a weak planet, we send a child. Kakarot was sent here to clear this one."

Gohan takes Bulma's hand. Goshin stays beside him, looking at Raditz.

"You send a baby away alone to do that?" Krillin says. "That's worse than Piccolo."

"It doesn't matter where I came from," Goku says. "I'm not helping you."

Raditz turns back to him. "You haven't heard why I came."

Bulma pulls both boys a step farther back. Gohan keeps hold of Goshin's hand.
