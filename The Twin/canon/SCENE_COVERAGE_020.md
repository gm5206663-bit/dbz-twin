## Authored handoff and continuity limits

- Goku completes the Gregory task after two weeks and begins the promised direct lesson. King Kai privately mentions the Kaiō-ken and another unnamed technique; Earth is not shown hearing of the meeting and Goku is not shown hearing that private thought. King Kai's meteor explanation is character dialogue with explicit uncertainty, not independent verification.
- The promised visit takes place. The children keep their shared room through the earlier permission and receive one further week while the director follows up placement. She sends the school forms with Chiko's question, but no places are confirmed. Pigero visits and receives the next week's visiting times; longer placement remains unresolved.
- Hatch's original drawing is last shown under Gohan's hat at home. Gohan lends his smaller library page to the children beside their larger boat drawing and writes his name on it; the children also keep the new side-view page. Mina's bridge book is last shown on loan to C-6; completion, return, permission for onward lending and completed arm repair remain unshown. The unidentified machine remains covered in Bulma's lab by her report, with no new diagnosis or activation.
- Both boys choose their own training and Piccolo exchanges. Gohan checks the difficult fall with ki, recovers and aims his wave. Goshin's strength and ki rise during his exchange; after further practice his narrow wave cuts a deeper channel while four pebbles move and he safely lowers the slab. Chi-Chi offers tactical advice and chooses a solid part for later targets; she does not end each round. No scan, new form, automatic immunity or guaranteed outcome is asserted.
