## Fight scale, growth and endpoint

- In Chapter 24 Gohan was read at **9,200**, and at **11,600** while charging his earlier defensive wave; Goshin was at **16,300** sustaining more of his already-held power after carrying the slab. These branch values are condition-specific, not original-Z numbers or maxima (`O187`).
- In Chapter 25 Vegeta again sees **16,300**, then **19,400** when Goshin intentionally releases more ki he and Gohan developed before the fight. This 3,100-point change is explicitly existing reserve becoming output, not fresh development awarded by one move. Vegeta then removes and clips on the scouter. Later, after sustained exchanges, Goshin feels further ki and physical development, and his improved response is shown in action; no numerical gain, trigger requirement, new form or ceiling is asserted (`O192`). A scouter alone cannot identify the cause of a change (`O188`).
- Gohan decisively controls the Nappa fight, avoids attacks directed at the others, turns the mouth blast into the sky and knocks Nappa unconscious at the bare wall. Nappa is breathing; neither death nor permanent incapacitation is established. Vegeta is still fighting and neither he nor Goshin has a demonstrated maximum (`O191`, `O192`).
- All six Saibaimen are accounted for: two destroyed in Chapter 24, then the pinned one, Tien/Chiaotzu's opponent, Yamcha's self-destructing opponent and the final Piccolo/Krillin opponent in Chapter 25. Yamcha's arm is cut, not established as seriously impaired (`O190`).
- At the new endpoint Gohan has redirected Vegeta's later blast and joined Goshin. Their first impact against Vegeta is shown. Piccolo moved the unconscious Nappa from falling stone and wants to question him about Namek. Tien, Chiaotzu, Krillin and Yamcha are beyond the tracks. Goku is still running Snake Way with his capsule; Kami's meeting with him and Yajirobe's arrival are not shown. Both tails remain and no Great Ape or other form appears (`O194`).
- In the city, the family of three reaches the gym. A volunteer says a driver with an injured arm is being treated but does not establish that this is the particular delivery driver. Chi-Chi, Puar and Popo continue helping; Bulma returns to the car after her call. The overall evacuation and casualty totals remain unknown (`O193`).
