# Chapter Fourteen — The Long Way Up

An apple hangs above the field gate. The branch dips when a bird lands beside it.

**THE LONG WAY UP**

**NARRATOR:** The Saiyans are on their way to Earth. Goku wants to train with King Kai, and the other fighters are gathering to prepare.

---

## The Last Page

Gohan holds Mina's book open beneath the laboratory lamp. The page shows a long tunnel with tracks running through it.

"That's the end of the chapter," he says.

C-6 turns his glass eye toward the picture. "They still haven't found the lower stair."

"Maybe it's in the next chapter."

"Then they'll need a better map."

Gohan laughs and puts Mina's paper marker back between the pages. Beside the book lies the separate drawing C-6 helped him make. Raditz's scouter rests on another bench, with its power cell kept separate.

Dr. Brief lifts the listening instrument from C-6's casing. "I heard that sound again when your working arm moved."

"The clicking?"

"Yes. I can't tell yet whether it comes from the leak. May I move the instrument to this side?"

"You may. Leave the support where it is."

Dr. Brief moves the instrument without disturbing the cushion beneath the bent arm. C-6 watches his hands until the instrument is in place.

Goku looks toward the laboratory window. "I want to go and ask Kami about King Kai myself."

Chi-Chi turns to him. "Now?"

"Yeah. Two Saiyans stronger than Raditz are coming. I need to get stronger too, and I want to see how strong King Kai really is!"

"What if Kami hasn't heard from Yemma?"

"Then I'll ask Kami to train with me until he does. I'm not going to sit around waiting."

Gohan closes the book. "I want to get stronger too. Can we finish our match?"

"Sure! Come on outside."

Goku reaches the door before Gohan has put the book down. Chi-Chi takes it from him and wraps it with the separate drawing.

"No blasts near the laboratory," she says.

"No blasts near the laboratory," Goshin repeats.

In the yard, Goku sets his feet beside the path. Gohan stands opposite him and raises both hands.

"One touch on the shoulder," Goku says. "Ready?"

"Ready!"

Gohan darts left. Goku turns with him, but Gohan changes direction and reaches from the other side.

Goku catches Gohan's wrist. When Gohan starts to turn him, Goku releases the wrist and steps inside, touching Gohan's shoulder before Gohan can reach his.

"You got me," Gohan says.

"That turn nearly worked again! Keep practising it. We'll have another match when I get back."

Gohan looks toward the sky. "Can I train while you're gone?"

"You bet! Kami invited Krillin and the others. You could ask him to train you too."

Goku looks at Goshin. "What do you want to do?"

"I want to train too. Can I keep working on my Kamehameha?"

"Sure! I want to see what you can both do when we meet again."

Chi-Chi steps beside the boys. "I'm going with them. Piccolo is still out there, and Gohan's studies aren't stopping."

"That's fine," Goku says. "I want them to be ready too."

Dr. Brief comes out carrying two capsules. "This one contains food and water for a journey. The other is a spare aircar, if you'd like to borrow it."

"Thanks! I'm still full from the Senzu, but I might need those supplies later."

Goku takes the first capsule. Chi-Chi accepts the second and slips it into her pocket.

"I'll return Mina's book," Gohan says. "And ask about the bridge book for C-6."

Goku rests a hand on each boy's shoulder. "Train hard, both of you!"

He hugs them, then steps back. Chi-Chi catches his arm before he rises.

"Come back to us, Goku."

"I will. And I'll be ready for those Saiyans!"

Goku lifts into the air and heads for Kami's Lookout. Gohan watches until he passes above the roof, while Goshin keeps his eyes on the patch of sky where he disappeared.

## Asking Kami

Goku climbs through the clouds under his own power. The white platform of Kami's Lookout emerges above him, and he lands beside the garden.

Kami stands at the edge with his staff. He turns as Goku comes running toward him.

"Kami! Have you heard from Yemma? I want to go to King Kai as soon as I can."

"I have. Yemma has agreed to receive you in the Other World while you are alive. He will let you take Snake Way to King Kai."

Goku's face lights up. "That's great! Let's go!"

"King Kai will decide whether to teach you when you reach him. The journey itself will be long."

"Then I'd better start!"

Kami lifts his staff. A pale opening appears beside the garden, wide enough for both of them to pass through.

"Yemma arranged this passage," Kami says. "Come with me to his office."

Goku steps through without waiting for another invitation. Kami follows, and the opening closes behind them.

## Across the Mountains

Bulma's aircar rises above the waterfall. Krillin sits in front with the map on his knees, while Launch leans between the seats and looks through the windscreen.

Tien and Chiaotzu fly beside the car. Below them, the damaged table is a small patch of white among the trees.

"Will they be all right down there?" Krillin asks.

"The cooks left before the earthquake," Bulma says. "Tien checked the house before we took off."

Launch rests her chin on the seat. "I'm not worried about the house. How high is this tower?"

"High enough that you can't see the top from the ground."

"And Kami lives above that?"

"That's what Yajirobe told us."

Launch looks up through the glass. "Tien's not getting away from me just because he can fly."

Krillin checks the small bag inside his shirt, then settles it back against his chest.

"Still there?" Bulma asks.

"One Senzu. I don't want to lose it before anyone needs it."

Tien moves ahead of the aircar, with Chiaotzu keeping pace at his shoulder. When Krillin points west, both turn with him.

"I've been there before," Krillin calls. "I'll tell you when we're close."

The mountains give way to forest. Far ahead, a thin white pillar rises through the clouds.

## King Yemma's Office

A stamp comes down on King Yemma's desk. The clerk beside him gathers a stack of papers while a line of souls waits across the hall.

Goku looks up at the desk. "You're even bigger than I thought!"

"And you're the living Saiyan Kami told me about," Yemma says. "I can see you've recovered."

"Thanks to a Senzu. Did Raditz come through here?"

"He tried to fight his way past me. He didn't get far."

Goku grins. "You stopped him? You must be really strong!"

"I have work to do. You're here to meet King Kai."

"That's right! I want him to train me so I can stop the other Saiyans."

Yemma sets his stamp down. "I sent word about you. You may take the road while alive, but King Kai will speak for himself when you meet him."

"Then I'll ask him myself. Which way is it?"

"A guide will take you to the entrance. The road is long, and the drop beneath it is dangerous."

"Got it."

Kami looks at Goku. "Do not forget why you are going."

"I won't! I'll get stronger and come back before they reach Earth."

A guide opens a door at the side of the hall. Goku waves to Kami and follows him out. Yemma picks up his stamp again.

## The Field Gate

The borrowed aircar descends beside the village road. Chi-Chi stops it near the gate, and Gohan steps out with Mina's book held against his chest. Goshin follows, closing the door behind him.

The gate to the sheltered field is shut. Beyond it, the injured dinosaur stands under a tree beside the fence, pulling leaves from a low branch.

Mina is sitting on the fence. When she sees Gohan, she slides down and runs to the gate.

"You brought it back! Did C-6 see the pictures?"

"I read them to him. He says the map left out a stair."

Chi-Chi hands Gohan the folded drawing. He opens it on the fence rail while Mina holds the book beside it.

"Here," he says. "C-6 told me where to put it."

Mina follows the pencil line with one finger. "And it goes down?"

"That's what he said. He wants to know if you have a book about bridges too."

"I do! I'll look for it."

Mina's father comes out of the house carrying a pail. He sets it beside the gate and looks at the drawing.

"You've given him some reading to do."

"He gave me something to draw," Gohan says.

Sato comes around the side of the field with her case in one hand. The dinosaur lifts its head when she approaches.

"I'm glad you're here," she tells Gohan. "Your friend is putting more weight on that leg."

"Can it go back to the river?"

"Not yet. I want it to rest here while the wound closes."

Mina takes the case from Sato, who kneels beside the fence. The dinosaur turns toward her, brushes its side against the bars and stops.

Sato waits until it settles before checking the edge of the dressing. She stands again without asking the animal to move.

"It's holding," she says. "I'll come back to see how it walks."

The dinosaur lowers its head toward the pail. Mina's father opens the gate enough to carry the water through, then closes it behind him.

Gohan watches the animal drink. "Could I get it some leaves?"

"The low branches by the fence are fine," Sato says. "Don't make it chase you."

"I won't."

Mina goes with him to the fence. They pull down a leafy branch together, and Gohan snaps it where Mina points. He carries it back and holds it through the bars.

The dinosaur steps over and takes a mouthful. Gohan moves his hand away from its teeth, then offers the branch again.

"It remembers you," Mina says.

The animal takes another mouthful. Gohan keeps the branch steady until the leaves are gone.

## The Base of the Tower

The aircar settles in a clearing beside Korin Tower. Its shadow crosses the base of the white pillar, and Launch climbs out before the engine has stopped.

Tien lands beside her, with Chiaotzu close behind. Krillin steps onto the grass and looks up.

"I remember that view."

A man comes along the path from a house among the trees. He carries a bundle of wood beneath one arm and stops when he sees the group.

"Bora!" Krillin calls.

Bora lowers the bundle. "Krillin. It has been a long time."

"We're here to see Korin. These are Tien and Chiaotzu. Kami wants to train us."

"Then you've come to the right place."

Upa appears behind his father and looks from the aircar to the climbers. He is carrying a coil of rope over one shoulder.

"Is Goku coming too?" he asks.

"Not with us," Krillin says. "Last I heard, he was with his family."

"Family?"

"He has two sons."

Upa smiles. "I would like to meet them."

Tien places one hand against the tower. "How far does it go?"

"Farther than it looks," Bora says.

Bulma opens the car door again. "I can take everyone to the platform."

Tien studies the pillar. "I'd rather climb it. Goku made this climb when he was young. I want to try it myself."

"I'll climb with you," Chiaotzu says.

Krillin folds the map and passes it back to Bulma. "I'm coming too. It'll be good practice."

"Suit yourselves. I'm going up to see Yamcha."

Launch grabs the edge of the door. "Take me all the way to Kami's place."

"I don't know where it is. I can take you to Korin, but the others will have to find their own way from there."

"I need a plane I can take wherever they go. Have you got another capsule?"

"Not with me. I brought this car to find Tien."

Launch looks from the car to the tower. "Then I'll find something else."

Tien jumps onto the pillar and catches a narrow seam in the stone. Chiaotzu takes hold beside him, and Krillin follows on the other side.

"Tien!" Launch shouts. "Don't think you've seen the last of me!"

Tien looks down. "I didn't."

He climbs, and the other two move up with him. Bulma watches until they have cleared the lower branches, then gets back into the aircar.

## Apples for Later

Mina brings out the bridge book and leaves it on the table by the gate. Its cover has dried, although one corner still curls upward.

"Can C-6 borrow it?" Gohan asks.

"Yes. I want it back after he's finished."

"I'll tell him."

Gohan wraps the book in the cloth Chi-Chi has brought. Mina's father carries the empty pail back out of the field, and Sato closes her case.

"The dressing can stay until I return," she says. "If the animal stops eating, send for me."

"I will," Mina's father says.

Gohan looks up at the apples above the gate. "Could the dinosaur have one?"

"We're saving the ones on the tree for market," Mina's father says. "There are windfalls behind the fence if you want some for the animal."

Gohan finds three apples in the grass. He carries them to Mina, who checks them and sets the sound ones in a basket beside the gate.

Gohan takes one back out and holds it through the bars. The dinosaur stretches its neck, takes the apple and settles again beneath the tree.

Goshin watches Mina shut the basket lid, then looks at the branch above the fence. "Can I help with another one?"

"Yes," Chi-Chi says. "Keep it clear of the gate."

"Keep it clear of the gate."

Goshin rises to a low fork in the tree. He holds a leafy branch still while Mina's father cuts it free, then carries it down to Gohan.

The dinosaur takes the branch through the bars. This time Gohan leaves it leaning against the fence so the animal can eat without following him.

"We'll bring the other book when we visit C-6 again," he tells Mina.

She nods. "I'll keep the drawing until then."

## Up the White Pillar

Tien reaches for the next seam. His fingers close around the edge, and he pulls himself above a band of smooth stone.

Chiaotzu climbs to his right. Krillin is below them, keeping the bag inside his shirt as he moves one hand at a time.

"I thought there would be more places to grip," Krillin says.

"There's one above your left hand," Chiaotzu calls.

Krillin stretches. His fingers find the seam, and he pulls himself level with them.

"Thanks!"

Tien reaches across the pillar to test a wider crack before trusting it with his weight. The edge holds, and he calls the others toward it.

Krillin moves first, leaving room for Chiaotzu to take the next hold without crossing his path. Chiaotzu follows, then points out another seam above Tien.

Wind pushes against their backs as they climb beyond the trees. Chiaotzu's foot slips on the smooth surface. He steadies himself in the air, finds another hold and continues climbing.

Tien pauses where the stone widens. "We could fly the rest of the way."

"Do you want to?" Chiaotzu asks.

Tien looks up. The platform is still hidden by cloud.

"No. Not yet."

Krillin takes a breath and reaches for another hold. "Then I'm staying with you."

They climb through a bank of mist. Water beads on Tien's forearms, and the ground disappears beneath them.

"Goku used to tell me about this place," Krillin says. "He came here after Tao beat him."

"He came back stronger," Tien says.

"And then he kept training."

Tien looks at his own hand against the stone. "So will we."

## Inside the House

Launch walks around Bora's house. A basket stands near the entrance, and a folded length of cloth hangs beside it.

She lifts the cloth and looks behind it. Then she goes inside.

"There has to be something I can use."

She opens a box, finds only tools and pushes it aside. A second box contains folded blankets. Launch pulls them out, checks beneath them and lets them fall to the floor.

Bora steps through the doorway behind her.

"What are you doing?"

Launch turns. "I'm looking for a plane capsule. I'll pay for it."

"We don't have one. Put those back and leave."

"Everybody's got capsules. Where do you keep yours?"

"There is no plane here."

Launch draws her gun. "Then find something else that flies."

Upa enters behind Bora. "Father?"

"Stay back," Bora tells him.

Upa sees the gun and moves toward the side of the room. Launch swings the barrel toward him.

"I said stay where you are!"

The gun fires. Upa drops behind the doorway as a shot tears into the wood outside.

Bora catches the barrel and pushes it away before Launch can fire again. She pulls against his grip, and a tassel on his arm brushes her nose.

"Get that out of my—"

Launch sneezes.

Her hair turns blue. She blinks at Bora's hand on the gun, then at the blankets scattered across the floor.

"Oh! What happened?"

Bora takes the gun from her. "You came into our house and fired at my son."

"Did I hurt him?"

Upa steps back into view. "No. You hit the door."

Launch puts both hands over her mouth. "I'm so sorry."

She bends to gather the blankets. Bora keeps the gun pointed toward the floor.

"Leave those," he says. "Come outside first."

Launch follows him through the doorway. Upa picks up the box she knocked over and sets it upright before he comes after them.

## A Place to Practise

A path slopes away from Mina's field to an open bank above the river. Goshin walks on Gohan's downhill side, and Chi-Chi follows them to the flat ground. She puts the wrapped bridge book beneath a tree, well clear of the water.

"Can we practise before we go to Kami?" Gohan asks.

Goshin steps onto the flat ground. "I want to."

Chi-Chi checks the slope and points toward the empty bank. "Keep your blasts away from the field and the road."

"Keep your blasts away from the field and the road," Goshin repeats.

Gohan sets his hat beside the book. The Four-Star Ball catches the light beneath the tree. Both boys free their tails from around their waists as they take their places.

"First to touch the other's shoulder twice?" he asks.

"All right."

Chi-Chi raises one hand. "Start!"

Gohan runs in low. Goshin turns his reaching arm aside, but Gohan plants one hand in the dirt and changes direction beneath it.

Goshin jumps, and Gohan's fingers brush his sleeve. Goshin lands behind him and taps Gohan's shoulder.

"One for Goshin," Chi-Chi calls.

Gohan turns at once. Goshin comes forward again, and Gohan catches his wrist with both hands, pulling him toward the river.

Goshin releases a short pulse of ki against the ground and changes direction in the air. Gohan follows the movement instead of the dust and reaches up.

His hand touches Goshin's shoulder.

"One each!" Chi-Chi says.

Goshin lands beside the river and raises both hands. Gohan takes a breath and comes after him.

## The Platform

Bulma's aircar rises above the clouds. Korin Tower's platform appears ahead, with a small building at its centre.

Yamcha is moving across the open floor. He jumps over a low stone bench, plants one foot on the wall and pushes off again.

Puar floats beside the doorway with a bundle of loose cloth. Korin watches from the shade, his staff resting against his shoulder.

"Not near the pots," Korin says.

"I haven't touched them!"

"I'd like to keep it that way."

Yamcha lands as the aircar reaches the platform. Bulma opens the door and steps out.

"You're here," Yamcha says.

"I said we'd find them. Tien, Chiaotzu and Krillin are climbing."

"Climbing? They could have come up with you."

"They wanted the exercise."

Puar gathers Yamcha's cloth bundle closer. "Should I go down and see if they need help?"

"They asked to climb," Bulma says. "If they want a ride, they'll call."

Korin looks toward the edge. "It will do them good to choose how they reach me."

Bulma watches Yamcha retrieve a length of cloth from Puar and wrap it around his wrist.

"Are you going to wait for them?"

"I'm going to keep moving until they get here. I don't want Tien saying I spent the whole time sitting around."

"Then don't break Korin's house before your training starts."

"I'll try not to."

Yamcha smiles, and Bulma reaches up to straighten the end of the cloth at his wrist.

"Take care of yourself," she says.

"You too."

Bulma rests a hand on the aircar door and looks up through the clouds. "Goku told me King Kai was even stronger than Kami. He wanted to train with him."

Korin taps his staff against the floor. "That sounds like Goku. He never stopped looking for a better challenge."

## One Point Each

Gohan and Goshin circle each other on the riverbank. Gohan moves toward the water, and Goshin follows without turning his back on the open ground.

Gohan kicks loose dirt toward Goshin's feet and runs around it. Goshin rises above the dust, but Gohan jumps after him and reaches for his shoulder.

Goshin turns in the air. His palm meets Gohan's, and the pressure sends both boys apart before either can make the touch.

Gohan lands on one foot and springs forward again. Goshin catches his wrist, then lets go when Gohan tries to turn him.

"Father did that!" Gohan says.

Goshin does not stop. He moves inside Gohan's reach and raises one hand, but Gohan ducks and runs past him.

A loose stone lifts under Goshin's ki. He sends it across the ground in front of Gohan and follows from the other side.

Gohan jumps over the stone. Goshin is already moving, and his hand touches Gohan's shoulder as Gohan lands.

"Two for Goshin," Chi-Chi says.

Gohan looks down at the stone, then back at his brother. "You made me jump where you wanted."

"You nearly reached me first."

"Next time I'll go around it."

"Then I'll try something else."

Chi-Chi comes over and checks the dirt on Gohan's hands. She brushes grit from his palm and looks at Goshin next. His hands go behind his back.

"Did either of you get hurt?"

"No," Gohan says.

Goshin brings one hand forward. A thin red line crosses his palm, already fading at one end.

"It doesn't hurt."

Chi-Chi checks it, then points toward the clear water between the rocks. "Wash your hands before you touch the book."

"Wash our hands before we touch the book," Goshin repeats.

The boys rinse their hands. Gohan retrieves his hat from beneath the tree and puts it on, checking that the Four-Star Ball is still secure.

## The Road to King Kai

The guide stops beside a stone arch shaped like a serpent's head. Beyond it, the road rises into the clouds, curving out of sight.

"This is Snake Way," he tells Goku. "Tradition says it is about a million kilometres long."

"A million?"

"King Yemma has made the journey himself. Be careful not to fall from the road."

Goku looks along the first bend, then back at the capsule in his hand. He tucks it securely inside his clothing.

"King Kai is at the other end?"

"Yes. I cannot tell you how long the journey will take."

"Then I'll find out. Thanks!"

Goku lifts from the stone and flies through the arch. The guide watches him rise above the road and shoot around the first bend.

The road stretches on. Goku flies close above its surface, then climbs over a long curve and drops down the other side. One bend vanishes behind him, then another, as his wake rolls through the clouds.

He reaches for more speed. The air presses against his face, and his ki brightens around him as he drives forward.

After a long flight, his breathing grows heavier. Goku lands without stopping and breaks into a run, his boots striking the road in a steady rhythm.

"I can still move faster than this."

He pumps his arms, lengthens his stride and races up the next slope. When his pace starts to falter, he shortens the steps until his breathing steadies.

Then he pushes again.

Goku turns his shoulders as he runs, practising the release that helped him escape Goshin's grip. His first step carries him too close to the edge, and he shifts back toward the middle.

He tries it again without slowing. This time his foot lands where he wants it.

"All right!"

Goku springs into the air and flies around another bend. The road still fills the distance ahead, and the clouds move beneath it.

"King Kai, I'm coming!"

He drives forward.

## The Wasteland

Piccolo stands above the broken pyramids. A crack from their earlier impact runs across the desert beneath him.

He raises one hand. Three slabs lift from the rubble, shedding sand as they rise.

Piccolo turns the first slab, then the second. The third begins to tilt, and he stops it before its edge reaches the others.

He holds all three in place. The slabs tremble, but none falls.

"Again."

Piccolo lowers them to the empty ground and raises them once more. This time the third remains level as he moves it between the others.

Far beyond the ridge, a faint burst of ki rises and falls. Piccolo recognizes one of the boys' powers and turns his head toward it, then closes his hand.

The slabs stop moving.

"Train while you can, kid."

He sends the first slab across the desert and brings it to a halt just above the sand.

## Waiting Below

Upa kneels by the damaged doorway, fitting a short piece of wood over the hole. Bora holds the board in place while he drives in a peg.

Launch stands outside with the folded blankets in her arms. Her gun is on a high shelf just inside the doorway, out of her hands.

"Is there anything I can do?" she asks.

"You can put those on the bench," Bora says.

She does, then looks toward the tower. The gun remains on the high shelf, and Bora keeps it there when she turns back.

"Please don't give that to me while I'm here," Launch says. "I don't want anyone else getting hurt."

Upa looks at the hole in the old board before laying it beside the new one. "You frightened us both."

"I know. I'm sorry."

She picks up the loose peg that rolled beneath the bench and offers it to him. Upa takes it and sets it with the other tools.

"Tien and Chiaotzu went up there?"

"With Krillin. They intend to train with Kami."

Launch shades her eyes. "I wanted to see Tien before he left."

Upa sets down the small hammer. "They were still climbing when I last saw them."

"Then I'll wait here for a little while, if that's all right. I won't go into your house again without asking."

Bora looks at the repaired section of the doorway. "You may wait outside."

"Thank you. And I'm sorry, both of you."

Upa presses the board once to test it. It stays in place.

"The door will hold," he tells his father.

Launch sits on the end of the bench, leaving the blankets beside her. The tower rises beyond the trees, and she keeps looking up at it.

## The Island

Yajirobe stands outside Kame House with his sword against one shoulder. Turtle comes up the steps from the beach, carrying a shell that the tide left behind.

"Krillin and Bulma ought to have found the others by now," Yajirobe says.

"They said they'd go to Korin's tower after they did."

"Fine for them. I still don't know how I'm supposed to get there from here."

A shadow passes over the sand. Popo descends on his carpet and brings it to rest beside the steps.

"Kami asked me to bring you to Korin," Popo says. "The others are on their way."

Yajirobe looks at the carpet. "You could have told me that before you left me here."

"Kami sent me when he was ready."

Roshi opens the door. "Going already?"

"Apparently."

Yajirobe steps onto the carpet and sits, keeping his sword across his knees. He catches the edge when Popo lifts the carpet above the beach.

"Tell Korin I said hello," Roshi calls.

"You can tell him yourself next time!"

The carpet rises toward the clouds. Turtle watches it go, then puts the empty shell on the step beside Roshi.

## The Last Stretch

Tien's hand reaches the lip of Korin's platform. He pulls himself up, rolls onto the stone and gets to his feet.

Chiaotzu reaches the edge beside him. Tien offers a hand, but Chiaotzu finds a hold and lifts himself up.

"I made it!"

Krillin's head appears between them. "A little room, please!"

Bulma comes over from the aircar while Yamcha reaches the edge. Krillin brings the small bag out from under his shirt and tucks it safely back again.

"You even carried that all the way up?"

"Of course I did. It's not yours to eat."

"I didn't say it was!"

Puar floats over with a folded cloth. Chiaotzu takes it and wipes the mist from his face, then passes it to Tien.

Korin steps out with his staff. "Welcome. Bulma said you were coming."

Tien looks beyond him at the open sky. "Where is Kami's place?"

"Higher still. You've reached my tower, not his home."

Chiaotzu leans back to look upward. "Can we see it from here?"

"Not from this side of the clouds. Rest for a moment. We'll see about the next part."

Krillin sits on the edge of the platform and looks down at the mist they have climbed through.

"I forgot how long that took."

"You made it," Yamcha says. "Now we can get started."

Chiaotzu passes the cloth back to Puar and looks across the platform at the space beside Yamcha. "Will we all be training together?"

"I hope so," Yamcha says. "I want to see what you and Tien have learned since the tournament."

"We've been practising at the waterfall," Chiaotzu says. "I can show you what I was doing with the stones."

"And I want to see what Kami can teach us," Tien adds. He sets his hand against the pillar and looks up through the moving cloud.

Krillin gets to his feet again. "We came all this way. He won't have to ask me twice."

"When Kami's ready for you," Korin says. "You're still on my tower, not his Lookout."

Yamcha folds his arms. "Then let's hope he's ready soon."

## A Meeting Above the Clouds

Popo's carpet appears through the clouds with Yajirobe seated behind him. It settles on Korin's platform, and Yajirobe steps off with his sword.

"You all got here before me?"

"We climbed," Krillin says.

"Then you can carry me up the next part."

A second aircar rises through the clouds and lands beside Bulma's. Chi-Chi climbs out first, followed by Gohan and Goshin.

"Chi-Chi?" Bulma says. "Where's Goku?"

"He went to ask Kami about King Kai. The boys want to train too, and I'm staying with them."

Gohan carries the wrapped bridge book against his chest. Goshin looks at Korin and then at the doorway behind him.

"Are you Korin? Father told us he trained here."

"He did," Korin says. "He chased me around the tower until he took the water."

"And that made him stronger."

"All that climbing and chasing certainly helped. He worked hard for it."

Popo steps toward the edge. "Kami invited the others to the Lookout. The boys are welcome to come and train as well."

Gohan looks at Chi-Chi. "Can we?"

"You both chose to train. We'll go together."

Tien and Chiaotzu rise from the platform. Krillin and Yamcha follow them, and Puar floats after Yamcha. Yajirobe settles back onto Popo's carpet.

Bulma climbs into her own aircar. "I'm going back to the lab. Father and C-6 might have found something I can help with."

"Tell C-6 Mina lent him a bridge book," Gohan says. "I'll bring it when we visit."

"I will."

Chi-Chi starts the borrowed car, and the boys climb in beside her. Popo's carpet leads the group upward through the clouds.

---

## Training Begins

The fighters land on Kami's Lookout. White tiles spread across the open platform, with trees and a building beyond them. Kami has returned from Yemma's office and waits beside the garden.

"Did Goku find you?" Chi-Chi asks.

"He did. Yemma gave him permission to take Snake Way, and he has already begun. He was eager to go. He intends to ask King Kai to train him. I will help you prepare here."

Gohan smiles. "I knew he'd go as soon as he could!"

Chi-Chi looks up at the sky. "Then we'll have to work hard while he's away. Both of you keep up with your studies too."

"Keep up with our studies too," Goshin repeats.

Yajirobe steps off the carpet. "I came to see Korin, not to promise I'd do all this." He moves into the shade with his sword while Puar settles near Yamcha.

Popo places four flat stones on the ground. "Move them across the circle without letting them touch the floor. You may choose how."

Chiaotzu raises his hands. Two stones lift, and he guides them across the circle. Tien taps a third into the air and keeps it up with quick strikes as he moves beside it.

"I'll get this one across," Tien says.

Krillin and Yamcha face each other farther away. Krillin sends a small burst toward the edge of the platform, and Yamcha turns it upward with his own ki.

"Again," Yamcha says. "This time I'll move before you fire."

Gohan steps toward the remaining stone. "Can I try?"

"Of course," Popo says.

Gohan flips the stone into the air with his foot and jumps after it. He catches it against his palm before it falls, then sends it across the circle to the clear tiles beyond.

"I got it over!"

"You did," Chi-Chi says. "Keep your eyes on where you want to land."

Chiaotzu and Tien place their stones beside Gohan's. Goshin looks at all four, and Popo notices him.

"Would you like a turn?"

"Yes. Can I use all four?"

Goshin raises one hand. The four stones lift at once and turn in the air above the circle.

One begins to drift toward the edge. He adjusts the pressure of his ki, brings it back into line and carries all four across without touching them.

Popo watches the stones settle. "Try it again while they are moving in different directions."

"All right."

Goshin lifts them again. This time he sends each stone around a different side of the circle, then brings them together at the centre.

Gohan points to the open space beside him. "Can you throw one for me too? I want to try catching it without stopping."

"Yes."

Goshin sends one stone toward him. Gohan jumps, catches it and keeps moving across the tiles, landing beside Chi-Chi.

"Again!" he says.

Across the platform, Tien and Chiaotzu reset their stones while Krillin and Yamcha change places. Popo moves between them, watching each exercise.

---

## On the Road

Goku runs through another bend. Sweat darkens the back of his gi, and his legs have begun to ache.

He looks toward a line where two broad sections of the road meet. "I'll sprint to that one."

Goku lowers his shoulders and drives forward. He reaches the seam, throws three quick punches as he passes it and turns away from an imagined counterattack. Each strike cracks through the empty air.

His foot slips on the curve. He catches his balance before reaching the edge, then runs on.

"Too wide. Try it again."

At the next seam he repeats the punches. This time he keeps his step shorter and turns from his hips. He stays near the centre of the road and accelerates out of the turn.

The road rises. Goku races up the slope, then plants one hand on the stone and pushes himself up on his fingertips. After a long set he switches hands and does another, then springs up and sprints to the next rise. He repeats the sequence through bend after bend, adding punches and a turn to each sprint.

By the time his breathing grows ragged, his fingers are trembling. He pauses at the bottom, then pushes himself up once more.

"Still got one!"

Goku stands, takes a breath and looks at the road disappearing into the clouds. He cannot see King Kai's planet from here.

"I want to be stronger when I get there, not just tired."

He starts running again, setting a pace he can hold. At the next bend he increases it, then lifts into the air and drives forward until his shoulders burn.

He lands, keeps his balance and runs on. The bend is behind him now, and another waits ahead.

"Come on!"

Goku pushes toward it.

---

## Above the Gate

The bird returns to the apple branch above Mina's field. It settles beside the fruit, and the branch dips under its weight.

**NARRATOR:** Goku has begun the long journey to King Kai. On Earth, his sons and friends have started their own training for the Saiyans' arrival.
