# Chapter Twelve — The Planet Between

*Revised story draft; not yet accepted.*

Two round space pods cross the dark side of a planet. A red indicator begins to blink on one of their hulls.

## Two Fish

Mina's father closes the field gate behind the injured dinosaur. Sato has left fresh dressings at the farmhouse and promised to return. The dinosaur keeps its head low as it pulls leaves from a branch.

Gohan waves through the bars. The dinosaur lifts its head, then goes back to the leaves.

"It heard you," Goshin says.

"I think it just wants the branch."

"It can do both," Mina's father says.

Gohan watches until the animal has pulled the last leaves within reach. It still favors its injured side, but it is standing, and Sato has promised to come back.

Mina's father picks up the recovered parcel and tucks it under his arm. Gohan has Mina's other book in his bag. The Four-Star Ball shines on his hat as he turns toward the road.

"We'll bring it back after C-6 sees it," he says.

"Mina will want to hear what he thinks."

Goku looks toward the river. "Now, where are those fish?"

Chi-Chi folds the empty rice cloth and puts it away. "You said two."

"Gohan said two. I was going to catch one really big one."

"Two really big ones," Gohan says.

Goku grins. "Even better."

The high path bends above the water. The bank below it is still torn from the flood.

A narrow pool has formed beyond the broken ground. Fish move beneath the surface, turning around a flat rock where the current cannot reach them.

"Stay on this side of the pool," Chi-Chi tells the boys. "The mud near the river is loose."

Goshin stays on the firm bank, eyeing the fish.

Goku sets the empty cloth beside Chi-Chi. "Let's see who can catch one."

Gohan kneels at the water's edge. Goshin stands beside him, with both hands ready over the pool. A fish noses out from behind the flat rock.

Gohan reaches for it. The fish darts away, and Goshin's hands close around water.

"It went under you," Gohan says.

"I felt it."

Goku crouches on the other side. Their reflections ripple together: Goshin has his father's black, spiked hair and nearly the same face, made small, with Chi-Chi's eyes. He pulls a face at the water, and Goku pulls one back. A fish breaks the surface between them, scattering both reflections.

"Don't grab where it is," Goku says. "Watch where it's going."

Goshin follows a second fish with his eyes, his tongue between his teeth. It turns around the rock, and he cups both hands in its path. Water runs through his fingers as he lifts it clear.

"Got one!" Goshin says as the fish wriggles.

Goku laughs. "Hold tight!"

Chi-Chi opens a bag, and Goshin lowers the fish inside. Gohan moves farther along the pool, keeping his shadow away from the surface.

A fish glides toward the rock. Gohan waits until it begins to turn, then brings both hands together behind it. The fish wriggles against his palms, splashing his sleeves.

"Two," he says.

Goku leans over to look. "That one's even bigger!"

"Mine was longer," Goshin says.

"We'll see when we get home," Chi-Chi says.

Gohan starts toward the bag, but a branch cracks beyond the pool. He turns before he reaches Chi-Chi.

The carnivorous dinosaur pushes its head between two trees. Its teeth show as it opens its mouth, and the broken ends of branches drag across its back.

"Is that the one that took our fish?" Gohan asks.

"It looks like it," Goku says. "Move away from the mud, Gohan!"

Gohan jumps sideways to the flat rock as the dinosaur lunges. Its jaws close on the spot he has left, sending water across the path. He keeps hold of the wriggling fish.

The dinosaur's tail swings toward Chi-Chi and the bag. Goshin reaches out, and his ki turns it aside; the tail tears through reeds instead.

Chi-Chi lifts the bag clear. "Goshin, come up beside me."

He flies to the path while Gohan springs from the rock onto dry ground. Gohan passes his fish to Chi-Chi, who slips it into the bag. The dinosaur turns after him, its feet sinking into the soft edge of the pool.

"It's going to get stuck," Gohan says.

"Then let's give it another way out," Goku says. He moves beside Chi-Chi, leaving the higher ground open.

Gohan spots a broad branch and shakes it beyond the pool. The dinosaur follows the movement, but the wood catches on a stump when he pulls it back. Goshin lifts the end free with his ki, and Gohan drags the branch toward firm ground between the trees.

The dinosaur climbs out after it. Gohan lets go and leaps back onto the path; the animal bites the moving wood and pulls it toward the trees.

Goku claps his hands. The dinosaur drops the branch and backs away, watching them over its shoulder before it vanishes into the leaves.

Gohan watches until the shaking leaves settle. "We still have both fish."

"And it already had our first one," Goku says.

Chi-Chi kneels beside Goshin.

"Did its tail touch you?"

"No. I moved it away."

She checks Gohan's wet sleeve and finds only water beneath it. Goku picks up the bag of fish, holding it out of Gohan's reach for a moment.

"Hey, want to race me to the top of the path?"

Gohan looks at the swinging bag. "With the fish?"

"Sure!"

"Goku," Chi-Chi says, "put the bag down first."

He puts it beside her. "Okay. Ready?"

Gohan plants his feet. Goshin crouches beside him.

"I'm racing too!"

"To the big stone," Goku says. "No flying."

"Then you'd better run fast," Goshin tells him.

Goku pushes off beside the boys. Gohan and Goshin reach the stone together and slap it, but Goku vaults straight over it and keeps running.

"Hey, that was the finish!" Gohan calls.

Goku skids around and runs back toward them. "Come on, let's go again!"

Chi-Chi comes up the path with the fish and the book. "You can all race after you've carried these home."

"We'll put them inside first," Goshin says, grinning at Gohan.

## The Aircar

Bulma's aircar passes above a lake. Korin Tower stands beyond the far shore, a thin line rising through the clouds.

Yamcha leans forward between the seats. "Kami is really going to train us?"

"That's what Korin's message said," Krillin replies.

"I can't wait. It's been too long since I've had a proper challenge."

Puar floats over Yamcha's shoulder. "With Master Kami helping, you'll be ready for those Saiyans."

Bulma keeps her hands on the wheel. "We don't know that yet."

"We'll get a lot stronger," Yamcha says. "Goku managed it."

Krillin looks out at the tower. "We still have to find Tien and Chiaotzu."

"We'll ask Korin where to start," Bulma says. "I'm not turning that scouter on just to look for them. Not until I know whether it can transmit."

"You took it apart?"

"Enough to know I need more time with it. The cell's in a different drawer."

Yamcha settles back and stretches his arms. Puar has to duck to avoid an elbow.

"Sorry, Puar. Let's get to the tower."

The aircar climbs toward the clouds.

## The Detour

A signal sounds inside Nappa's pod. He opens his eyes and touches the scouter beside his face.

"Vegeta? Are we at Earth already?"

"Not yet. I set the timer early. There's a planet nearby that might be worth selling."

Nappa looks through the small window at a brown sphere below. "A stop sounds good. A year in one of these things would make anyone stiff."

"We'll see what the place has to offer."

The pods turn together and descend. Fire streaks behind them as they break through the clouds and strike a plain of red stone. The impact throws two rings of dust across the cracked ground.

Nappa climbs out first, stretching his shoulders in the thin, unfamiliar air. Dust rolls past his boots. A broken tower rises on the horizon, with dark fields spreading around its base and the remains of walls half buried between them.

"This is the planet you wanted?"

Vegeta steps out of his pod. His scouter gives a short signal as he looks toward the ruins.

"It used to be valuable. Let's find out what happened."

The ground shudders. Two long, many-legged creatures rise from beneath the stones, each carrying an armed rider. The riders draw their mounts to a halt above the Saiyans.

"Visitors," one says. "Lord Moai will want to see them."

Nappa folds his arms. "And who is Moai?"

"The king of Arlia. You will come with us."

"What if we don't?"

Both riders open their mouths. Red beams strike the Saiyans, throwing dust from the ground around them and over the pods.

When it thins, Nappa and Vegeta stand where they were. Nappa brushes grit from his shoulder and grins up at the riders.

"My turn."

He takes a step, but Vegeta touches his scouter. "Wait, Nappa. They'll take us straight to their king."

Nappa looks at the two armed riders, then holds out his wrists. "All right. Lead the way."

The riders exchange a glance. One jumps down with a chain while the other keeps his weapon raised. Vegeta allows his own wrists to be bound; Nappa tests the links once, then lets them hang loose. The guard hastily steps back.

## A Gift for Tien

A siren winds through a city street. Launch's blond hair whips across her face as she drives around a corner with a canvas sack bouncing on the seat beside her. Notes spill through its open mouth and scatter across the road.

"Hey! Get back here!"

She catches the sack before it falls out. Two police cars follow her through the turn, their lights flashing against the shop windows.

"Stop!" an officer calls through a loudspeaker. "You're under arrest for robbing the bank!"

Launch glances over her shoulder. "I heard you the first time!"

She snatches up the weapon beside her and fires behind the car. The blast strikes the road between the pursuing vehicles. One brakes sharply, and the other swerves around it through a cloud of dust. A loose banknote lands against its windshield; the driver peels it away just in time to see the turn.

The first car skids against a stack of empty crates, and the officers duck as pieces tumble across the road. An officer from the second car jumps out to help clear the turn, but Launch is already out of reach.

Launch laughs. At the next junction she swings into a narrow lane, leaving the sirens on the other side of a row of buildings.

She pulls the sack onto her lap and ties it shut. A few notes still cling to the edge of the seat.

"Hang on, Tien. I'll buy you a meal that'll keep you going."

She gathers the loose notes, stuffs them into her jacket and drives toward the hills.

---

## The Cell

The riders take Vegeta and Nappa along a road cut through the ruins. Arlians watch from doorways and from behind a damaged wall. One pulls a child back as the procession passes.

Farther along the road, two Arlians strain against the harness of a cart while a guard walks behind them. One wheel sinks into a rut, and the guard strikes the harness with his staff until both Arlians pull again.

A bundle falls from the cart. A child reaches for it, but the guard picks it up and tosses it back among the other bundles.

At the castle gate, guards take the chains and lead the Saiyans down a stone stair. A heavy door opens onto a crowded cell.

"Inside," a guard says.

Nappa ducks beneath the lintel. The door closes behind Vegeta, and a bar drops into place on the other side.

An Arlian seated against the wall gets to his feet. A narrow gold bracelet circles one wrist.

"My name is Atla. I've never seen anyone from another planet before."

"Now you have," Vegeta says.

Atla looks at the chains. "I'm sorry you came while Moai is king. He takes whatever he wants, and his soldiers do the same."

"Then why don't you stop him?" Nappa asks.

Atla glances toward the door. "The people who tried are here, or they're dead."

Another prisoner shifts on the floor. Atla gives him a little water from a cup and waits until he has swallowed. The cup is almost empty when he sets it down.

"On the day I was married, Moai's men took my wife to the castle. Her name is Lemuria. I haven't been allowed to see her since."

He turns the bracelet around his wrist. Its gold has been rubbed dull where his fingers keep finding it. "We exchanged these before they came. I don't know whether she still has hers."

Atla points toward the stair. "There's a passage beside the courtyard. It leads up to the balcony. I heard the guards talking about it when they brought me in."

"Then why are you still here?" Nappa asks.

"The door is barred, and the guard keeps the key. I can't get to Lemuria from inside this cell."

Atla looks at the other prisoners. "If that door opens, I'll help them out. Then I have to find her."

Vegeta rests against the wall. "Do whatever you want. Just stay out of our way."

Nappa sits on a block of stone. "Does this king have any fighters worth meeting?"

Atla stares at him. "He has an army. And something under the arena."

A shout carries down the stair, followed by the sound of a horn. Atla steps toward the door.

"He makes people fight for him. If they lose, his guards throw them into the pit."

Vegeta looks up at the ceiling. Dust falls through a crack between the stones.

"The arena is above us?"

"Across the courtyard. Please, if they call for you, don't give him a reason to send you there."

Nappa smiles at Vegeta. "I hope he calls soon."

## The King's Arena

Two Arlians fight with blades on the stone floor. Above them, Moai watches from a raised throne. Lemuria sits beside him, her hand closed over a gold bracelet.

One fighter's blade knocks the other to his knees. The defeated Arlian drops his weapon and presses a hand against his wounded arm.

Lemuria takes a cup from the table beside her and starts down the steps. A guard blocks her way before she reaches the arena floor.

"He needs water," she says.

Moai raises a hand to stop the guard from moving aside. Below them, two more guards drag the defeated Arlian toward a square panel in the floor. Lemuria tries to get past, but the guard catches her arm.

"Enough! He's already lost."

Moai does not look at her. "The winner deserves to see what happens to the loser."

A guard opens the panel. The fighter struggles, but they push him through. It closes over his cries.

The cup tips in Lemuria's hand, spilling water across the steps. A deep growl rises from beneath the floor. The guard takes the cup and guides her back to her seat; she grips the edge of the chair until the sound stops.

Moai notices the bracelet. "You're still wearing that?"

"Atla gave it to me."

"Atla is in my prison. Forget him."

"I won't."

Moai reaches toward her wrist. Lemuria draws her hand back beneath her sleeve.

A guard hurries up the steps and bows. "Your Majesty, the patrol has brought two creatures from another world."

"Bring them here. Perhaps they'll be amusing."

The guard runs back toward the courtyard. Beneath the arena floor, the growl fades.

---

## The Ridge

Piccolo stands alone on a ridge above a dry valley. He raises two fingers, and three boulders lift from the ground around him.

He sends the first against the cliff. Stone bursts across the valley floor. The second strikes the third, and both break apart before they land.

Another boulder rolls down the ridge toward him, dislodging loose stones that scatter across the empty valley. Piccolo stops it with one hand and closes his fingers until the rock splits in two.

He brushes dust from the torn place on his clothes where Goshin struck him, then drops the broken halves beside his feet. Beyond the trees, two familiar currents of ki rise and settle.

Piccolo lowers his hand. He turns toward the distant line of trees.

"Those boys."

The bend in the river is hidden beyond the hills, and Goku's ki remains close to theirs. Piccolo stares that way for a moment, then closes his hand again.

"Enjoy keeping them beside you, Goku."

He lifts from the ridge and drives his fist through the next boulder. Fragments tumble across the empty slope as he flies away from the river.

## The Way Home

Goku pauses at the top of the path and looks toward the hills.

"Piccolo?" Chi-Chi asks.

"Yeah. He's out there, training."

Gohan moves closer to her. Goshin looks along the ridge, but the trees hide the other side.

"Is he coming here?" Gohan asks.

Goku waits a moment. "No. I can feel him moving farther away."

Chi-Chi takes the bag from him. "Then let's get home."

Goku looks toward the ridge again. "He wants to get stronger too, huh?"

"Goku."

"I didn't say I was going over there!"

Gohan smiles. Chi-Chi keeps her eyes on the hills until Nimbus drops beside them.

Goku takes Chi-Chi on his back and lifts the bag of fish. Gohan climbs onto Nimbus with the book, and Goshin sits beside him. They rise above the river and turn toward home while the sun is still high enough to light the road.

At the house, Chi-Chi opens the door and sets the book on the table. Gohan takes off his hat and places it carefully beside the window. His tail curls behind him as he passes the table.

Goku goes to the cupboard. "Hey, the other Dragon Balls are in here, aren't they?"

"You put them away," Chi-Chi says.

"Oh, right."

He reaches behind a folded cloth and brings out two orange balls. Three red stars show inside one, and six inside the other. Light from the window passes through them onto the table.

"They're still here."

Gohan comes over to see. "Raditz's friends heard about them."

"Yeah," Goku says. He puts the balls back beneath the cloth and closes the cupboard. "We'll be ready for them."

Goshin leans close to the cupboard. "Can the dragon hear us when it's inside?"

"I don't think it's inside the balls. It only appears when you call it."

Gohan looks at the cupboard again. "Do we have to call it to ask?"

"Not today," Chi-Chi says.

Goku laughs. "We'll find out another time."

Goshin listens at the cupboard door anyway. Gohan puts an ear beside his, and Chi-Chi has to move both of them before she can set the fish on the table.

## The Visitors

Atla hears a metallic snap. Nappa pulls the chain from his wrists and drops it on the floor. Vegeta presses one hand against the barred door.

"Move back," Atla tells the other prisoners.

The door breaks outward. The two guards beyond it raise their weapons, but Nappa steps through the dust and knocks them aside. Vegeta walks past him into the passage.

Atla brings the injured prisoner up from the floor. "Can you walk?"

"I think so."

"Come with me."

Atla and the prisoner follow as far as the stair. The courtyard above is full of guards running toward the Saiyans. Atla pulls the prisoner into a side passage while Nappa meets the first guard.

By the time Atla reaches the courtyard, the guards are down. Vegeta and Nappa have already gone through the doors beneath the arena seats.

Atla looks toward the high windows. "Lemuria."

He finds a second stair and begins to climb.

Inside the arena, Moai rises from his throne. His messenger has not returned, but the two Saiyans stand at the foot of the steps.

"Where are my guards?"

Nappa looks back toward the doors. "They didn't stop us."

Lemuria gets to her feet. Moai holds out an arm to keep her beside the throne.

"So these are my visitors. I thought you might make good pets, but you're too ugly for that."

"Then let's not waste time," Vegeta says.

Moai points toward the winning fighter. "Beat my champion, and I'll give you whatever you want."

The champion steps between the Saiyans and the throne. He raises his blade and charges Vegeta.

Vegeta lifts one finger. A narrow flash crosses the arena and strikes the champion before he reaches the steps. The blade falls from his hand, and he drops to the floor.

Moai grips the arms of his throne. "What did you do?"

"You said I could have anything," Vegeta replies. "I'll take this planet."

"This planet belongs to me!"

"Not for much longer."

Guards pour through the doors and spread around the Saiyans with their blades raised. Nappa rolls his shoulders and smiles.

"We let your patrol catch us," Vegeta tells Moai. "It was easier than finding you ourselves."

Moai points at them. "Kill them!"

The guards rush forward. Nappa drives one back with his forearm, then catches a second by the wrist and throws him across the arena. Vegeta rises above a swinging blade and lands behind its owner.

More soldiers close in. Nappa plants his feet as light crackles around him. The floor shakes beneath the guards.

"You wanted all of us," a guard shouts. "Now you've got us!"

Nappa raises his hand. A burst of ki sweeps across the floor. Blades and pieces of stone strike the walls, and the soldiers fall among them.

Moai shields his face. When he looks again, the arena is quiet. Lemuria stands beside the throne, her sleeve pressed against her mouth.

"Yedi," Moai says. "Release Yedi!"

## A Leaf and a Book

Gohan opens his schoolbook at the table. Beside it lies Mina's book about machines, with a scrap of paper marking the page C-6 wanted to hear.

The school page asks about animals living near a river, and Gohan writes down the fish they caught before drawing the injured dinosaur in the margin. He adds a small bandage where Sato placed the dressing and turns the book so the drawing faces the window.

Goku cleans the fish at the kitchen counter while Chi-Chi puts rice on the stove. A silver scale sticks to his nose.

Goshin points at it. "You missed one."

Goku looks down at the fish. "Where?"

Goshin touches his own nose. Goku rubs his and finds the scale, and both boys laugh. Chi-Chi takes the cleaned fish for the pan while Goshin fetches chopsticks for the table.

When she serves the fish and rice, Goshin blows on a mouthful before tasting it. He reaches for more rice at once.

Gohan closes his schoolbook. "I've finished this page."

"Show me," Chi-Chi says.

He carries it to her. She reads what he has written and points to one line. Gohan looks, takes the book back and corrects a character.

"Can we practise outside now?" he asks.

"After you put your books away."

Gohan stacks them on the shelf by the door. "I want to take Mina's book when we see C-6."

"Yeah! Let's take it tomorrow," Goku says. "I want to see how he's doing."

Goshin finishes his fish and slides down from his chair. "Can we use the leaf again?"

"Sure! But first, let's see how steady you can stand when the ground moves," Goku says.

Goku picks up a round piece of firewood and carries it into the yard. The boys follow him past the gate while Chi-Chi clears the table.

## Yedi

The panel in the arena floor opens. A platform rises from the pit, carrying an Arlian far larger than the guards. Yedi's head nearly reaches the balcony as the platform stops.

Moai points at the Saiyans. "Destroy them!"

Nappa steps forward. "Let me have this one."

Vegeta moves aside.

Yedi swings at Nappa. The fist strikes the floor and cracks the stone, but Nappa has jumped onto Yedi's arm. He runs along it and kicks Yedi across the face.

Yedi's eyes flash. Two beams cross the arena, and Nappa drops beneath them. They strike the wall behind him and tear a banner from its pole.

The banner falls across the steps between Moai and the arena, and Lemuria pushes it aside before its burning edge reaches her dress. Moai stares at the black groove that the blast has left in his wall.

Yedi roars and sweeps a hand toward the balcony. Lemuria drops behind the throne as the blow tears a piece from the railing.

Moai scrambles down the steps. "Watch what you're doing!"

Yedi brings both hands down toward Nappa. Nappa catches one enormous fist above his head. His boots sink into the broken floor, and the arm stops.

"Is that all?"

Yedi reaches for him with the other hand. Nappa seizes one of its fingers and tears it away. Yedi pulls back, knocking a column loose as it turns. The column crashes between the throne and the arena doors.

Moai covers his head as pieces of stone rain down the steps. "Yedi! Stop breaking my palace!"

Nappa rises into the air and gathers a ball of light in his hand. He throws it into Yedi's chest. The blast fills the pit and shakes the balcony.

When the light clears, Yedi is gone. The broken platform settles beneath the opening.

Moai stumbles behind his throne. Vegeta lands on the steps and lifts a piece of shattered stone from the floor with his ki.

"Wait!" Moai cries. "You can have the planet!"

Vegeta sends the stone into Moai's chest. Moai falls against the throne, and its damaged back breaks away and pins him beneath it. He does not move again.

Lemuria crawls out from behind the fallen railing. She looks once toward Moai, then runs for the arena door.

## On the Firewood

Goku sets the round piece of wood on the packed earth and places one foot on top. He rolls it forward, steps off, then rolls it back with the other foot.

"I wonder how King Kai trains," he says. "If he agrees to teach me, I want to see every move he's got!"

"Could he balance the log on one finger?" Gohan asks.

"Maybe I can!"

Goku lifts the log upright and balances its end on one finger. He walks it across the yard, then catches it with his other hand before it reaches Chi-Chi's flowerpot.

"Ha! I wonder if King Kai can do that with two logs."

He puts the log down in front of the boys. "Your turn. Try keeping your balance without stopping it."

Goshin points to Gohan. "You go first."

Gohan steps onto the wood. It turns beneath him, and he spreads his arms. He shifts his weight and keeps both feet on top as it rolls a short distance.

"I did it!"

"Now turn around," Goku says.

Gohan bends his knees, lifts one foot and turns. The log rolls toward the gate, and he jumps clear before it reaches Chi-Chi's flowerpot. He catches it with both hands and sets it back in the yard.

"I can do it again."

Goshin steps onto the wood. It rolls under his feet, and he adjusts his stance as it moves. When he turns, his tail swings behind him and he steadies himself without stopping the log.

Goku grins. "That's it! Gohan, try it once more."

Gohan gets back on, watching the gate. This time he turns with the log and steps down before it reaches the flowerpot. He looks over at Goshin, pleased, and Goshin gives him a thumbs-up.

Goshin picks up a fallen leaf. "Father, can I try hitting this while I'm moving?"

"Let's find out!"

Goku pins the leaf beneath a stone at the far side of the yard. Goshin gets back onto the rolling log and raises one finger. A thin flash touches the edge of the leaf without moving the stone.

Gohan sets another leaf on the ground and picks up a pebble. He waits for the log to roll, then throws while balancing on it. The pebble strikes the leaf and skips across the packed earth.

"I want another turn," Gohan says.

"Me too," Goku says. "You two can throw the pebbles at me next!"

Chi-Chi opens the window. "Goku! You can help me wash the dishes first."

"Aw, just one more round?"

"The dishes!"

"Okay, okay. Don't put the log away, boys!"

"We can move it out of the way," Goshin says to Gohan.

Gohan and Goshin carry it out of the path while Goku goes inside.

## The Empty Sky

Atla climbs over fallen stone on the last steps and reaches the arena just as Lemuria runs through the doorway. He stops, then holds out the wrist with the gold bracelet.

"Lemuria?"

She pushes back her sleeve. The matching bracelet catches the light.

"Atla!"

They run to each other across the broken floor. Atla holds her hands. "I couldn't get to you."

"I know." Lemuria presses his wrist against her own, the two bracelets touching.

Atla looks toward the Saiyans. "You defeated Moai. Our people can be free again."

Vegeta touches his scouter. A signal sounds high above the castle.

"You came from so far away," Atla says. "We'll always welcome you here."

The two pods descend into the courtyard. Nappa walks toward them with Vegeta, glancing back at the Arlians gathering by the gate.

"They think we came to rescue them."

Vegeta does not answer. He climbs into his pod, and Nappa follows him into the other. The hatches close, and both craft rise above the castle.

Atla leads Lemuria into the courtyard. Arlians are coming out of the side passages, helping the wounded and lifting the fallen gate.

Beyond the gate, people step out of their houses and look toward the palace for the first time since the fighting began. One of them lifts a bundle of clean cloth over the fallen gate, and others hurry forward to carry it to the wounded.

A prisoner calls from the stair. Atla waves him into the courtyard, where another Arlian is helping the wounded sit in the shade.

"There are more people in the cells," Lemuria says. "Moai kept the keys behind his throne."

Atla looks back at the broken doorway. "I'll get them. Can you take the others down to the stair?"

She catches his hand. "We'll get the keys together. Then we can bring everyone out."

He looks at the two bracelets against each other, and for the first time since the cell door opened, he smiles without looking back toward the guards.

Above the clouds, the pods stop. Their hatches open, and Vegeta floats out with Nappa beside him. Below them, Arlia's broken cities and dark plains curve toward the horizon.

"Nobody would pay much for that," Nappa says.

Vegeta raises two fingers. "Then we won't leave it for anyone else."

A bright beam descends through the clouds. Its light reaches the courtyard as Atla and Lemuria turn toward the stair where the prisoners are still waiting. They look up. Their bracelets touch as they reach for each other.

The light swallows the castle, the streets and the fields beyond. Arlia breaks apart beneath the Saiyans. For a moment fragments hang against the glare; then the world and everyone on it are gone.

Nappa watches until the light fades. "I hope Earth's worth more."

"We'll collect the Dragon Balls first," Vegeta says. "After that, if it disappoints me, it can disappear too."

"And the wish?"

"Eternal life."

Nappa climbs back into his pod. Vegeta follows, and the hatches shut.

Two round space pods cross the dark where Arlia had been. Their red indicators are still.
