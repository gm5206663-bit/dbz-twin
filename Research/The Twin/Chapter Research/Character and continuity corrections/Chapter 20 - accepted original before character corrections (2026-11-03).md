# Chapter Twenty — The Weight of a Step

*Author-accepted chapter, continuing the selected Chapter 19 review draft.*

An apple rolls across a little planet and stops against Goku's boot.

**THE WEIGHT OF A STEP**

**NARRATOR:** Goku has reached the end of Snake Way. On Earth, his sons have begun teaching each other, but the Saiyans are still on their way.

---

## The Man by the House

Goku picks up the apple with both hands. The man behind the house watches him straighten up.

"Are you King Kai?" Goku asks again.

The man scratches his cheek. "My cheek is itching. I suppose you could call me the itchy king."

Goku waits for him to finish scratching.

"I'm King Kai," he says.

"You are?" Goku looks toward the tree. The monkey swings down from a branch and lands beside the house. "Then who is he?"

"That's my pet, Bubbles."

Bubbles makes a noise and hops around the apple tree. Goku laughs and rubs the back of his head.

"I thought he was you."

"I saw. You were following him around the tree."

"He moved easy, so I thought he might teach me how."

King Kai puts his hands on his hips. "First, tell me what you thought of my joke."

"Your joke?"

"The itchy king. You didn't laugh."

"Oh." Goku gives him a quick smile. "I didn't know it was a joke."

King Kai folds his arms. "Then you may as well go home. I don't train people who can't laugh."

Goku looks toward the edge of the planet. Snake Way is far below them.

"I came a long way to find you," he says. "Kami said you might teach me."

"I might. Can you make me laugh?"

Goku puts the apple down and takes a careful step. His boot sinks into the grass. He pulls it free, then looks from King Kai to the tree.

"Bubbles is funny," he says.

"Bubbles isn't taking the test."

Bubbles tips his head and makes a sound. King Kai looks at him.

"Don't encourage him."

Goku takes another step and stops when his feet come close together. "If I keep walking this way, I'll get back to your house."

"Eventually."

"And then I'll be back where I started."

"That's what walking around a planet does."

Goku points at the ground. "So it's a round trip."

King Kai stares at him. His mouth opens, closes, and opens again. Then he bends over with both hands on his knees.

"A round trip!" he says. "On a round planet!"

Bubbles jumps onto the roof while King Kai laughs. Goku grins and waits for him to stand up again.

"Does that mean you'll teach me?"

"It does." King Kai wipes his face. "I can show you some excellent jokes."

"I want to learn how to fight."

King Kai's smile drops. "Martial arts?"

"Yeah. Two Saiyans are coming to Earth, and they're stronger than the one we fought. My family and my friends are getting ready too."

King Kai looks at him for a moment, then steps away from the house.

"Show me how you move."

---

## The Heavy Ground

Goku sets his feet and raises his hands. When he steps forward, his knee bends farther than he intends, and he catches himself before he falls.

King Kai waits. Goku tries a punch, but his arm drags downward before it reaches King Kai.

"Something's wrong," Goku says. "My whole body feels heavy."

"This planet has much stronger gravity than Earth. About ten times as much."

Goku looks down at his hands. "Even though the planet's this small?"

"That's right. Try jumping as high as you can."

Goku bends his knees and pushes. His boots leave the grass, but he comes down almost at once. The ground thumps beneath him.

"That's all?" he says.

King Kai watches the mark his boots have made. "You got off the ground. Let's see how you do after some practice."

Goku brushes dirt from one knee. "Can you tell where the Saiyans are?"

"I'll look."

King Kai closes his eyes. His antennae remain still while Goku waits beside him.

At last King Kai opens his eyes again.

"There are two of them travelling toward Earth. Their course can change, so I won't give you a day and pretend it's certain."

"Then I ought to get started."

"From what I can sense, they're stronger than I am. Even if I train you, I can't promise you'll defeat them."

Goku looks toward Snake Way, then back at King Kai. "My boys are training too. I told Gohan we'd have another match when I got home."

"How many boys?"

"Two. Gohan and Goshin."

"Then you'd better pay attention to your own training. They may be hard to catch when you get back."

Goku looks toward Bubbles. "I haven't caught him yet either."

"You'd better be able to get home for it."

"That's why I came here."

Bubbles runs between them, snatches the apple and carries it to the other side of the tree.

King Kai points after him. "First, catch Bubbles."

Goku watches Bubbles take a bite. "That's the training?"

"If you can move well enough to catch him here, you'll have learned something about the gravity. Don't hurt him."

"I won't."

Bubbles drops the apple core and runs. Goku starts after him, planting one foot carefully before he lifts the other.

Bubbles reaches the house while Goku is still beside the tree.

---

## Breakfast and a Telephone Call

At the mountain house, Chi-Chi lifts the lid from the rice pot. Goshin stands beside her with his hands resting on the edge of the table. He has watched her wash the rice, add the water and set the pot over the heat.

Gohan brings two bowls from the cupboard. Chi-Chi gives them to Goshin.

"Set these on the table, please."

"Set these on the table, please," Goshin repeats. He places the bowls beside the third one.

Gohan moves his library drawing out of the way. The page has a boat, a school above it and an unfinished row of shelves.

"I want to show this to Hatch and Ena," he says.

"We can ask about a visit when we call," Chi-Chi says. "First I want to know where they're sleeping this week."

The telephone rings before they sit down. Chi-Chi answers it while Gohan holds the drawing flat beneath his hand.

"I got the machine to the lab," Bulma says. "It's in a separate room, still covered. I've kept everyone out while I work out what to do with it."

"Did it make that picture again?"

"Not while I was bringing it here. I'm not opening the cover just to see if it will."

Gohan comes to the telephone. Chi-Chi holds the receiver where he can hear.

"Do you know if it's Father's?" he asks.

"Not yet. I want a proper look before I tell you whose it is, and I want to do it safely."

Goshin moves close to Gohan and watches Chi-Chi's mouth as she repeats Bulma's answer for him.

"It's still covered," he says.

"It is," Bulma answers. "And I'll call before I try anything that might make that light come back."

Chi-Chi tells her about the children's home and the drawing. Bulma offers to come on another visit if the children want her there. They agree to speak again after Chi-Chi has called the home.

When the rice is served, Goshin finishes his first bowl and holds it toward the pot. Chi-Chi gives him another helping, then fills Gohan's bowl.

"Can we practise after breakfast?" Gohan asks.

"Finish your schoolwork first," Chi-Chi says.

"Finish your schoolwork first," Goshin repeats.

"Tell me before you go beyond the clearing."

"Tell me before you go beyond the clearing," Goshin repeats.

Gohan looks at the page beside him. "I'll finish the shelves first."

After breakfast, Goshin opens his schoolbook beside Gohan's.

---

## Catch Bubbles

Goku lunges for Bubbles and reaches a patch of empty grass. Bubbles has already turned around the house.

Goku stops himself with both hands before he falls onto the path. King Kai watches from the doorway.

"You're trying to run before you can turn," he says.

"He turns pretty fast."

"He lives here."

Goku pushes himself up and follows the track Bubbles has made in the grass. This time he takes shorter steps. At the corner of the house he bends his knees, turns and keeps moving.

Bubbles looks over his shoulder, waits until Goku comes closer, and darts beneath a low branch.

Goku reaches after him. His fingertips brush the leaves.

"Almost!"

Bubbles climbs the tree and sits on a branch. Goku puts both hands against the trunk, breathing hard.

King Kai comes around the house. "You can stop and rest. Bubbles isn't going anywhere."

"I can see that."

Goku leans against the trunk while Bubbles swings his feet overhead. After a while Goku takes a bottle of water from the supply capsule he brought along Snake Way. He drinks, puts the bottle away and secures the capsule inside his clothes again.

"My stomach's making more noise than Bubbles," he says. "Do you have anything to eat?"

"You already had an apple."

"That was a while ago."

King Kai looks up at the remaining fruit. "Come inside. I'll find you something."

---

## A Meal and More Weight

King Kai puts a bowl in front of Goku, then fetches another when the first is empty. Bubbles sits on the table with a piece of fruit.

"You weren't joking about being hungry," King Kai says.

"The road was long."

"Did you eat on your way here?"

"I had supplies. I saved some for the trip back."

King Kai sits across from him. "You'd better save some room for training too."

Goku finishes the second bowl and puts it down. "That was good."

"Of course it was. I prepared it."

Bubbles reaches for the empty bowl, and King Kai moves it away before it tips.

After the meal, King Kai opens a cupboard and brings out two broad training bands. He sets them on the floor. The boards creak beneath them.

"Try these when you go back outside," he says. "They're weighted."

Goku picks one up with both hands. "These are heavy even here."

"They'll make the running harder. You can take them off to rest."

Goku fastens the bands around his wrists and gets to his feet. He keeps his elbows close to his sides until he has his balance.

"The Saiyans are used to gravity like this," King Kai says. "A world where they lived had a pull about as strong as this planet's."

"It did?"

"It's one reason they're difficult opponents. They're used to fighting, too."

Goku looks down at the bands. "I'm a Saiyan."

King Kai leans forward. "You are?"

"Raditz was my brother. He told me before we fought him."

Bubbles makes a noise from the table. Goku gives him the last piece of fruit, then looks at King Kai.

"I'm still going to stop the others from hurting my family."

King Kai studies him, then points toward the door. "Bubbles is waiting."

Goku opens it with both hands. Bubbles goes out first.

---

## The Clearing

Gohan puts his schoolbook on the shelf and meets Goshin in the yard. Chi-Chi comes with them, carrying a water bottle and a cloth to spread beneath the trees.

On the path, Goshin crosses behind Gohan when the slope changes. In the clearing, they set two sticks apart to mark the sides of a lane. Gohan walks to one end.

"You stand there," Gohan says, pointing halfway between the sticks.

"You stand there," Goshin repeats. He steps into the lane.

"I'll try to get past you without going outside them."

Gohan comes forward. Goshin shifts to meet him, and Gohan changes direction before they touch. Goshin turns, reaches past Gohan's shoulder and taps his back.

"You got me."

"You nearly got past."

Gohan returns to his mark. On the next attempt he sends a small push of ki toward the dirt at Goshin's left. Dust lifts there, but Gohan moves right.

Goshin watches Gohan's feet. He steps across and catches Gohan's wrist gently before Gohan reaches the edge.

"The dust didn't make you look," Gohan says.

"I saw your foot."

Gohan looks down at the tracks. "Then I'll change that part."

They try again. This time Gohan places his foot as though he will go right, then turns back across the clearing. Goshin catches his sleeve, but Gohan slips out and reaches the mark.

"I got through!"

Goshin looks at the sleeve in his hand, then releases it. "Again?"

"Again."

A shadow passes over the clearing. Piccolo lands beyond the two sticks, keeping his distance from Chi-Chi.

Gohan turns toward him. Goshin lets the sleeve fall and stands beside his brother.

"You came back," Gohan says.

"I said I might."

Chi-Chi rises from the cloth. "They have schoolwork and a call to make. What do you want?"

"To see whether they can use what they practised yesterday."

Gohan looks at Goshin. "I want to try."

"I want to try too," Goshin says.

Chi-Chi looks at Piccolo. "One short round with each of them. When I call a stop, you stop. No one leaves this clearing."

"When I call a stop, you stop. No one leaves this clearing," Goshin repeats.

Piccolo folds his arms. "If they stop when an enemy tells them to, they won't last long."

"You're not an enemy in this exercise," Chi-Chi says. "Those are the conditions."

Piccolo looks at the marks in the dirt. "Fine. Which one first?"

Gohan steps between them. "Me."

Chi-Chi moves beside the water bottle, where she can see the whole clearing. Goshin waits beyond the right-hand stick.

Piccolo takes one step toward Gohan. Gohan moves left and raises his guard. Piccolo reaches for his shoulder, but Gohan turns under the hand and drives a straight punch toward Piccolo's middle.

Piccolo catches it against his palm and slides half a step back.

"Good thrust," he says.

Gohan smiles, but Piccolo is already moving. Gohan raises his other arm to block. Piccolo turns the block aside and taps Gohan's shoulder with two fingers.

Gohan backs away, resets his feet and comes forward again. A short push of ki lifts dirt beside Piccolo's boot. Piccolo looks down, and Gohan changes direction.

Gohan reaches Piccolo's side and touches his arm before Piccolo can turn fully. Piccolo pivots and sends Gohan stumbling toward the left-hand stick.

Gohan catches himself inside the mark. His heel leaves a long line in the dirt.

"Stop there," Chi-Chi says.

Piccolo lowers his hand. Gohan rubs his shoulder and walks back to the cloth for a drink.

"I got his arm," he tells Goshin.

"You moved your foot the other way."

Gohan nods and offers him the bottle. Goshin drinks, then goes to the mark Gohan has left.

Piccolo advances. Goshin slips past his first reach and rises just above the ground, turning toward Piccolo's open side. Piccolo follows him into the air.

Goshin lifts two loose stones with his ki. One passes to Piccolo's right; the other hangs below his left hand. When Piccolo turns toward the first, Goshin sends the second across his path and reaches for Piccolo's shoulder.

Piccolo knocks the stone aside and blocks the touch. Goshin lands near the boundary, catches the falling stone before it reaches Chi-Chi's cloth and lowers it beside the other.

Piccolo lands in front of him. Goshin meets the next reach, turns beneath it and presses his palm against Piccolo's forearm. Piccolo's arm moves off line, opening a clear path to his side.

Chi-Chi raises her hand. "That's enough."

Piccolo steps back. Goshin lets his arm fall and returns to the cloth.

Chi-Chi checks Gohan's shoulder, then turns to Goshin. His hands go behind his back.

"Any pain?"

"No," Goshin says.

"My shoulder's sore," Gohan says. "I can still move it."

Chi-Chi looks at it, then gives him the bottle again. "We'll put a cool cloth on it at home. No more rounds today."

Piccolo watches Gohan drink. "You kept moving after the first hit. Keep doing that."

Gohan lowers the bottle. "Will you fight Father again?"

"Our fight isn't finished."

"You helped us with the machine," Gohan says. "You didn't have to."

Piccolo looks away toward the ridge. "The Saiyans are still coming. Your father and I can settle our business after that."

Goshin picks up one of the marking sticks and smooths the dirt from it with his thumb.

"Will you come back?" Gohan asks.

"Keep practising. If I come back, I'll expect more."

Piccolo rises above the trees and flies away. Chi-Chi gathers the cloth and the bottle.

Gohan picks up the second stick. He walks beside Goshin down the path, taking the uphill side when the path bends.

---

## The Room This Week

After they return home, Chi-Chi puts a cool cloth on Gohan's shoulder. Then she telephones the children's home.

The director comes to the receiver. Chi-Chi asks about the room before asking anything else.

"The six children can use the common room this week," the director says. "We're still discussing a longer arrangement with the people responsible."

"Have they been able to tell you what they want?"

"Rom asked to keep a bed where he can see the door. Chiko wants to stay near him. We'll speak with all six before we make a change."

Gohan sits beside Chi-Chi while she writes the answer down. Goshin stands at her other side, watching her pencil.

"May I talk to them?" Gohan asks.

The director calls across the room. After a moment Hatch takes the receiver.

"I made the shelves," Gohan tells him. "There's room for the bridge book, if Mina says the others can see it after C-6 is finished."

"Ena wants a table in the library," Hatch says. "So the books don't fall on the floor."

Gohan adds a small rectangle beneath the shelves. "Like this?"

"I can't see it."

Gohan laughs. "I'll bring it when we visit."

Chi-Chi asks whether the children would like Bulma to come too. Hatch calls the question to the others, and several voices answer at once.

"They said yes," Hatch reports. "Yordon wants to show her how much he can eat now."

"I heard that!" Yordon calls.

Hatch gives the telephone to Chiko. She tells Gohan that Pigero has visited again and that she and Ena have been taking turns with a blue pencil.

"Rom's here too," she says. "He wants to know if you still have the boat."

"Hatch's first drawing? It's with my hat."

"Tell him the door is still on it," Goshin says.

Chiko passes the message across the room. Gohan hears Rom answer, but he cannot make out the words.

When the call ends, Chi-Chi writes down a time to speak with Bulma about arranging the visit. Gohan folds the library drawing and puts it beside Hatch's original.

"They can stay together this week," he says.

"This week," Chi-Chi agrees. "We'll ask again after that."

She calls Bulma, and they arrange to go the following afternoon.

---

## A Page for the Boat

Bulma brings an aircar to the mountain house the next day. Gohan folds his library drawing into his pocket, leaving Hatch's original under his hat. Chi-Chi and Goshin come out to the car with him.

"The machine is still covered," Bulma tells them as they climb aboard. "I haven't opened it."

The children's home comes into view beyond the shore. Pigero is at the gate, and he walks beside the car as Bulma brings it down.

"They've been waiting for that drawing," he says when Gohan gets out.

Inside, Hatch spreads the larger boat drawing across the table. Ena holds one corner while Gohan unfolds his page beside it. Chiko pulls up a chair for Rom, then sits on the bed where she can see both pages.

"There's the library table," Gohan says.

Ena traces its outline without touching the paper. "Can people sit on both sides?"

"If we move the shelves back."

Hatch finds his pencil and draws two chairs on the larger boat picture. Goshin holds the paper flat while Hatch adds another one.

"There's room for the teacher too," Goshin says.

Bulma looks over the table. "You'll need a way to get books up to that floor."

"A lift!" Yordon says.

"Stairs," Rom says. "The lift could break."

Hatch draws the stairs and lift behind the library door. Jink asks for a place to put wet coats, and Ena adds hooks along the wall. Chiko points out that the teacher needs one too.

"We asked about school," Hatch says. "The director said they're checking for places."

"Tell me when you hear," Chi-Chi says.

Pigero looks toward the six beds. "They've kept this room for the week," he tells Chi-Chi. "The director and I are still talking about what comes after."

"We'll keep asking," Chi-Chi says. "You can tell me if you want help with a question."

Before they leave, Gohan shows Ena the space on his smaller page.

"I haven't asked Mina about her book yet," he says. "C-6 still has it."

"Then leave the space," Ena says. "We can choose a book later."

Gohan folds his page again. The larger boat stays with the children, and Bulma takes the family home in the aircar.

## Another Way Around

Bubbles runs past the house with Goku behind him. The weighted bands pull at Goku's arms, but he keeps them close and makes the turn around the tree without falling.

"I got farther!" he calls.

"You did," King Kai says. "You haven't caught him yet."

Goku takes another step. His foot slides, and he sits down heavily on the grass.

Bubbles circles back and reaches toward one of the bands. Goku lifts his hand, and Bubbles jumps away.

"You want to try them?"

Bubbles makes a sound and runs behind King Kai.

"I don't think he does," King Kai says.

Goku takes the bands off and sets them together beside the tree. After a rest and another drink, he puts them on again. He follows Bubbles around the house twice before King Kai calls him in for food.

On another day, Goku plants one foot and turns without bracing himself against the house. Bubbles changes direction at the tree, and Goku changes with him. The monkey escapes through the gap between Goku's reaching hands.

"That was closer," Goku says.

"Your feet stayed under you," King Kai answers. "Do it again after you've caught your breath."

Goku lies back in the grass. Above him Bubbles climbs onto the roof and peers down over the edge.

"Next time," Goku says, pointing at him.

Bubbles drops a leaf onto Goku's face.

Goku lifts it away and looks at the path around the house. His footprints and Bubbles' smaller prints cross near the tree. He traces the two tracks with one finger when he gets up.

"He keeps going around the house," he says.

"You've been chasing him around it," King Kai replies.

Bubbles climbs down and takes the leaf from Goku's hand. He runs toward the tree, pauses to look back and runs on when Goku follows.

This time Goku keeps his arms low through the turn. He gets close enough to reach, but Bubbles doubles back before his hands come together. Goku stops short of the tree and puts one palm against the trunk.

"He can turn both ways," Goku says.

King Kai picks up his cup. "You might want to try that too."

## The Things They Practise

At home, Gohan stands in the yard with his hands cupped at his side. Chi-Chi watches from the doorway, with Goshin beside the target they have set against the bare bank.

Gohan gathers a small light. He moves his feet, aims at the target and sends the wave forward. It strikes the centre of the marked board and knocks it against the bank.

Goshin catches the board with his ki before it tips onto the ground. He turns it upright and sets it back against the bank.

"I hit the middle," Gohan says.

"You did. Now let your hands rest," Chi-Chi tells him.

Gohan opens his fingers and lets the light fade. Goshin moves four pebbles into a slow circle and keeps them there while he steps around the target. He lowers them into a row at Chi-Chi's feet.

"Hold the board still," Chi-Chi tells Goshin.

"Hold the board still," Goshin repeats. He holds it while Gohan fetches a stone and wedges it against the bottom.

Chi-Chi waits until Gohan moves his fingers clear. Goshin releases the board, and it stays upright.

"Now the next shot won't knock it over," Gohan says.

Later, the boys practise the turn Gohan used in the clearing. Goshin offers his wrist, and Gohan catches it. Goshin shifts his weight and turns free, then lets Gohan try the same movement.

Gohan gets his foot across but loses his balance. Goshin catches his arm before he falls, and they reset together.

At the table that evening, Gohan shows Chi-Chi the shelves he added to the drawing. She checks the spelling beneath the picture before handing his pencil back.

"You left room for another book," she says.

"Ena might want a different one there."

Goshin looks at the open space. "A big one."

"Then the table has to be bigger too," Gohan says.

He draws another line.

## Three Weeks

Three weeks have passed since Goku began chasing Bubbles. The grass around King Kai's house has a narrow track worn through it.

Bubbles runs along the track. Goku follows with the weighted bands secured at his wrists, placing each foot before he makes the turn. King Kai sits near the tree with a cup in his hand.

"What do you call a joke about this planet?" he asks.

Goku goes past him. "What?"

"A world-class joke!"

King Kai laughs. Bubbles darts around the house, and Goku follows.

When they come back, King Kai sets down his cup. "You missed the best part."

"Tell me again after I catch him."

Goku reaches for Bubbles near the tree. Bubbles ducks beneath his hand and races toward the house. Goku turns and follows, but Bubbles is already around the far side.

Goku slows at the corner. The track continues all the way around the house and back to the tree.

Bubbles comes past the tree again, glances over his shoulder and finds Goku following him. He speeds up. Goku keeps the same distance through the next turn.

At the house, Bubbles cuts left. Goku plants his right foot, turns and follows without losing his balance. He gets close enough to touch Bubbles' back, but Bubbles slips beneath his arm.

They go past King Kai again.

"He's still ahead of you," King Kai says.

"I know!"

Bubbles runs behind the house. Goku follows him out of sight, then slows where the path curves around the wall. Bubbles reaches the tree and looks back.

Goku is not there.

Bubbles comes cautiously along the path toward the house. Goku steps out from behind the corner and reaches for him.

Bubbles jumps aside. Goku misses, turns on the planted foot and runs after him. Bubbles makes for the tree, but Goku is close now. He reaches with both hands and catches Bubbles around the middle before he can climb.

"I got you!"

Bubbles squirms. Goku loosens his grip and sets him down at once. Bubbles looks up at him, then scrambles onto his shoulder.

King Kai comes over, smiling. "You finally learned to move on my planet."

Goku takes a breath. "Bubbles made me work for it."

"He did. And you kept working."

Goku holds out his hands. Bubbles climbs from his shoulder into them, and Goku carries him toward the tree. He puts Bubbles down beside a fallen apple.

While Goku is on the far side of the tree with Bubbles, King Kai watches from the house.

"The Kaiō-ken," he says quietly. "Perhaps, in time."

Goku does not hear him. Bubbles picks up the apple and runs, and Goku follows at an easy pace.

---

## A Call and a Bowl

At the mountain house, Chi-Chi hangs up the telephone and returns to the table. Gohan and Goshin wait beside their schoolbooks.

"That was the director," she says. "The six have kept the common room since we last spoke. They can use it for another week, and she'll tell them before any change."

"Is Pigero still visiting?" Gohan asks.

"He is. They agreed on times for this week. The longer arrangements still aren't settled."

Gohan looks at the library drawing. "Can we go and see them?"

"Bulma can take us tomorrow afternoon. I've told the home, and the children said they want us to come."

Goshin touches the edge of the page. "And the three doors?"

"We'll ask Hatch and Ena to show us."

Goshin follows Chi-Chi to the stove. She slices the carrots while he holds the bowl, then adds them to the pot and stirs until they are tender. He watches while she fills the bowls.

Gohan sets Goku's empty bowl in its place before sitting down. Goshin carries his own to the table.

Outside, the evening light reaches the practice board. The stone at its foot holds it upright.

**NARRATOR:** Goku has caught Bubbles, and his sons continue their own practice on Earth. Their next lessons are still ahead of them.
