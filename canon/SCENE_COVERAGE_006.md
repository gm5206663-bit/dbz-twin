# Episode 6 — ordered beat inventory for Chapter Seven

**Owns: the source-order comparison for the episode-6 adaptation.** The complete local Japanese episode-6 captions (`W-30`) were reread before the original draft; their provenance and cut remain unverified, and dialogue does not establish silent frames. `W-24`, `W-51` and `W-52` are secondary episode summaries, not a full-video inspection. Rows below are in **source order**, not necessarily the chapter's screen order.

**31 October reader correction:** the first Chapter Seven draft invented a hospital and a landing strip. Neither was a sourced episode-6 event or an established story location. That detour and the doctor-trip lines in the unaccepted Chapter Six ending have been replaced. In the baseline, the group intends to return to Kame House and Piccolo intervenes before that return (`W-24`); Goku is dead. The revised divergent chapter keeps Goku alive and adapts the events causally, rather than asserting the altered sequence is canon.

## Additional resources and current continuity

- **The Senzu trip is authored, not an episode-6 event.** The pre-Z Korin/Tower/Senzu background is supported by the official manga-based `W-53` and secondary `W-54`; Krillin's pre-Z flight is reported by `W-55` (`C172`, `C173`). No source establishes Korin's bean count at this divergent moment or Krillin's travel time. In this draft Krillin asks for three; Korin has three to spare. He flies from the crater to the tower and on to Kame House. No specific distance, speed or travel duration is asserted.
- At the island, Goku and Gohan each eat a bean and are completely healed and restored, without lingering battle pain or fatigue. Goshin declines the third because he has not watched it being made, consistent with `STATUS §5`; only his hand and head remain sore. The bean is kept, and the family plans to ask Korin about it. No new scan or numerical recovery increase is asserted (`O103`).
- The user directs that Goshin first master or perfect what he already has in deliberate training and seriously focus on foundations (`O104`, `O105`). The user expressly permits instinctive copying during an emergency, so the crater rescue remains. At Kame House, a short added beat shows Goshin adjust his footing and want to refine the wave he already knows; he does not conduct full practice while injured. Neither technique is newly declared perfected.
- Chapter Three is revised by explicit approval: the same §10 unanswered call remains, while the contradictory date, countdown and overnight outcome are removed. No one waits until morning to preserve them.
- Roshi calls after Piccolo's crater assault. Chi-Chi hears both boys and is told about Goku's initial injury, the earlier knockout, Raditz, Piccolo's attack and the approximate one-year threat. She and the Ox-King are driving to meet Bulma at the ninth road marker; they have not yet reunited. Bulma leaves in the same aircraft already established in Chapter Four, with no claim about a new landing strip.
- A wrapped scouter and separate cell are not evidence that its transmitter has been safely disabled or its language decoded. Previous scan values are momentary snapshots, not caps, formulas or substitutes for conditions and choices.
- Complete episode footage, exact original broadcast cut and silent choreography remain unverified. The chapter remains a draft pending reader review, not evidence of acceptance.
