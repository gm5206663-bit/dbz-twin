# Chapter 28 — ordered source comparison before drafting

**Purpose:** Compare the original-Z aftermath with the actual unaccepted Chapter 27 endpoint before writing the next local continuation. The source is a reference, not a required sequence of deaths, escape, travel or family decisions. This plan does not constitute an accepted chapter.

## Evidence and limits

On 5 November 2026 I reread all of the retained spoken-caption text for Z episode 36 (`Dragon_Ball_Z_036.srt`, member SHA-256 `00a879d6afe4397a0d453a9f12007b53ea78417823be8192ebbfeb6dbbaf2ad6`) and episode 37 (`Dragon_Ball_Z_037.srt`, SHA-256 `69a756dc0d75c422e65ed8944824fe24cee5eafe99b8aeaa061a5d96e75755b1`) from the local subtitle ZIP, including recaps, previews and trailing fragments. Episode 37 was read in three bounded passes so the tool output did not truncate the spoken text. Both were among the 125 previously recorded full-caption reads; this is not an increased episode count. The original caption source and release cut remain unverified. Text does not establish uncaptioned movements, timing, delivery, facial expression or audio. No full episode video or audio was examined.

I also read Toei's short synopses for [36](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/36/), [37](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/37/), [38](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/38/) and [39](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/39/), plus the secondary Kanzenshuu guides for [37](https://www.kanzenshuu.com/episode/dbz/eps-037/) and [38](https://www.kanzenshuu.com/episode/dbz/eps-038/). ## Branch promises carried into the draft

- Start with Piccolo's unanswered question and Nappa's guarded viewpoint; distinguish his observations from assertions he cannot know. Vegeta's attempted attack on Nappa has witnesses; Nappa is not obliged to become a friend.
- The boys have different choices and feelings. Goshin enjoyed his conscious, controlled giant form and retained the real growth earned in the fight. Gohan's independent contributions and his decision not to transform matter; neither brother should be reduced to a witness of the other. Preserve both tails and the remaining bean.
- People still need help. Civilian danger, transportation, injury, reporting, the six children's temporary shelter and Saiyan custody require concrete choices; neither a complete casualty account nor an effortless long-term prison may be invented.
- The scouters are separately located and their transmission state is unknown; if no one uses one to scan, no new numerical reading is appropriate. The pods' status is unknown until someone credibly reaches or reports the landing site.
- Chi-Chi, friends, Popo, Launch and Yajirobe act from their established positions. If Popo retrieves the boys' bags, they were last under the Lookout shelter; their day-count page is separately under a stone on the bench.
- King Kai has promised to notify Kami near Yemma's end. Any Goku return must physically progress along Snake Way, show Kami's agreed meeting and passage and then Goku's travel to his family; no resurrection or instant travel from King Kai's planet. His supply capsule remains with him.
- Preserve unresolved family promises, the accepted original Chapter 20, predecessor bytes and local/private boundaries. A new chapter remains an unaccepted draft; do not publish or upload.
