## Character and branch checks

- **Talent and growth:** the prose gives observable changes in recovery, fighting adjustment, ki control, non-contact force and strength. It does not say Goshin only copies. No fixed bloodline percentage, universal Saiyan healing timetable, new numerical ceiling or unproved near-death Zenkai is introduced. The wider native Talent, deepening Saiyan heritage and future possibilities are in `00_CHARACTER_RULES_AND_SCOPE.md`.
- **Great Ape:** Goshin's first transformation, when it happens, is to be controlled from the start. In this sequence it has not happened. Roshi relates what he saw happen to Goku; the family cautiously avoids the moon because they do not yet know how either child would respond. Piccolo overhears Goku's warning. This is not evidence that Goshin must lose himself, or that Gohan must respond the same way. The later ordinary-body use of Great Ape power and possible combinations remain future directions, not achievements already shown.
- **Gohan and C-6:** Gohan is independently active and can learn and grow; no scene is intended to establish Goshin as less capable than Gohan. C-6's choices and limitations are his own. The robot's survival, location and mechanical cost are authored divergences from the episode, not claims about canon.
- **Food and Senzu:** Goshin watches Chi-Chi prepare his food, watches the apple preparation and C-6's cooking, and does not eat the unwatched bean. Krillin retains the last whole bean. Goku and Gohan do not remain injured merely because Goshin declines one.
- **Standing mannerisms:** the revised instructions to stop pressing his head, show his hand, stay safe at the bridge, follow the doctor's advice, and stay near Gohan are repeated by Goshin before compliance. The existing instruction exchanges in the cave are preserved. His downhill position, hands behind his back when Chi-Chi examines him, and waiting for his father's conversational invitation remain part of the branch. The changed passages were reviewed in context; a string check cannot prove every semantic instruction in five chapters has been caught.
- **Medical boundary:** the fictional doctor notes unexpectedly rapid healing, examines and follows up after the earlier unconsciousness, and gives conditional warning signs. She is not asserting that a human child with a comparable head injury should resume activity on the same schedule.
