## Standing instructions for future work

Read the actual relevant chapters and the author’s latest corrections. Goshin visually resembles Goku extremely closely, with Chi-Chi's eyes; the design is distinct from his habits and is not erased by the old R-22 prose note. The assistant-invented automatic repeat-back requirement is withdrawn; the author did not direct it. Older drafts repeated food-watching, paternal-invitation and maternal-inspection practices with incompletely recoverable provenance; the selected correction removes mechanical recurrence while retaining contextual choices, not unverified absolutes. Goshin is emotional, plays fully and has independent agency. He is never less capable than Gohan, while Gohan retains agency and growth. His Saiyan bloodline becomes purer and deeper as he grows, without assuming his human heritage disappears. In the unaccepted Chapter 26 Goshin's first Great Ape is aware and controlled from the start; the form and its future applications develop with him. Do not force canon outcomes, present authored readings as canon or fixed maxima, or mark drafts accepted without the author’s decision. When a scouter is measuring someone, show the relevant reading and its scene-specific consequences.

For delegated corrections, investigate and repair related issues autonomously; do not stop or require the author to reread each change. Keep revised text clearly unaccepted unless the author accepts it, but do not treat that status as a workflow gate. After a chapter or revision, update the foundation, timeline, relationships, status or status delta, world/knowledge and source notes that actually changed. Keep one clearly identified working version and preserve necessary earlier versions in the designated Research backup locations; existing older Drive copies are separate, not automatically synchronized. Check before deleting, replacing or syncing anything. Do not claim checks prove literary quality or full video review.
