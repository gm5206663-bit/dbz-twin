# Chapter 32 — The Road Beyond Earth

*Anime episode reference: Dragon Ball Z, Episode 38 (preparation, training and departure, adapted for the living-character branch).*

## The Empty Place

Goshin carries the food container home, holding it against his chest. Gohan flies beside him with their folded letter tucked into his shirt. The sun is low over the mountains, and their house appears between the trees before Goshin has finished telling his brother what he thinks a toilet on Namek might look like.

"We don't even know if they have houses like ours," Gohan says.

"Kami had a house."

"It was a spaceship."

"Then maybe their toilets are spaceships too."

Gohan laughs so hard he drops a little way in the air. Goshin waits for him, and they land together beside the path.

The mountain air smells of the evening cooking fire. For a moment the flight to the quarry feels as ordinary as a trip across the yard.

Chi-Chi is at the door. "I was just about to come looking for you."

"We came straight back," Goshin says, holding up the container.

"After you went straight there?"

"Yes."

She takes the container and looks inside. "Did everyone get something?"

Goshin can see the relief she tries to hide when she finds them both on the doorstep.

"Krillin and Tien did," Gohan says. "Father offered Vegeta food and water, but he didn't want any. Nappa took some."

Chi-Chi nods. She sets the container down and draws them both inside.

The rice is warm, and three bowls wait on the table. Goshin looks at the empty place beside them.

"Dad said we could talk," Gohan tells her. "All four of us. First he wants to work out how to look after things at the quarry."

"I want that too." Chi-Chi fills the boys' bowls. "Did you tell them what the hospital said?"

"About the team?" Gohan sits down. "I told Father they wanted to speak to the guards."

"Good. I'll call the hospital after we eat."

Gohan lays the letter beside his bowl. Its corners have grown soft from being opened so often, but the drawing inside is still clear.

Goshin reaches for a piece of fish. "Can we ask Bulma about the ship tomorrow?"

"You can ask her questions. That doesn't mean you're leaving tomorrow."

"I know." He puts the fish back on his plate. "I want to know how the beds stay on the floor when the ship moves."

"I want to know how it finds the way back," Gohan says.

Jupiter had filled the window. Namek is much farther away, beyond anything he knows how to find by looking.

Chi-Chi sits with them. "So do I."

They eat. The boys tell her about the book Goku noticed in the letter, and Goshin admits he had drawn the target board crooked. Gohan says the board itself had leaned after a particularly hard practice. Goshin says Gohan was the one who hit it.

"You were standing on it," Gohan replies.

Goshin opens his mouth to protest, then remembers exactly where his feet were. His brother sees his face and starts laughing again.

"That wasn't why it leaned."

Chi-Chi listens to them argue over the drawing. She wants Goku at the table, reaching for another bowl and asking to hear the whole story from the beginning. He has only just come home, and already she is setting out food for him to eat somewhere else.

After supper she calls the hospital. The coordinator has spoken to Dr. Mori and found a small portable examination unit. They want to see the place and speak with the fighters before bringing it close to Nappa.

"I'll meet you there," Chi-Chi says. "Please don't bring anyone who hasn't been told what happened."

When she hangs up, Gohan has the letter open again. Goshin is adding a tiny line beside the leaning board.

"What's that?" she asks.

"Dad. So he can see it when we bring the letter back."

"He's already seen it," Gohan says.

"He hasn't seen himself."

Chi-Chi watches the pencil move across the page. There was no way to send their father the letter from inside the room; now his figure has a place on it.

Chi-Chi lets Goshin finish. Then she closes the shutters while the boys put their bowls away. Neither brother goes to look out into the dark sky.

## The People Watching

The next morning, Gohan and Goshin carry Chi-Chi to the quarry. A hospital vehicle is already waiting beyond the broken tracks. Dr. Mori stands beside it with another woman and a wheeled case. Tien is explaining where Nappa lies, while Chiaotzu watches from the mouth of the gully.

Goku is beside Vegeta with Krillin. The prince's tail lies across the stone behind him, its end twitching when he sees the arriving boys.

Goshin lands and lets Chi-Chi step down. "Did Dad sleep?"

"Some," Krillin says. "We took turns watching."

Goku stretches one arm over his head. "I heard you coming."

"You heard Goshin telling me to hurry," Gohan says.

"You were going the long way around the mountain."

"Mother asked me to avoid the wind."

"Oh. That was a good way, then."

Chi-Chi goes to Dr. Mori. The doctor introduces the woman from the hospital and shows Chi-Chi the compact machine in the case.

"We can look at some injuries here," Dr. Mori says. "I can't promise we'll recognize everything in someone from another world. We'd like to ask Nappa before we start."

"And if Vegeta moves," Goku says, "I'll be between him and you."

Dr. Mori looks from him to the prince. "Thank you. I still want the vehicle facing the way out."

Tien helps the other woman turn it. Nobody opens the gully to traffic or invites the city team closer than the doctor has requested.

Nappa watches them come. The scrape Dr. Mori cleaned is visible on his arm, and he braces his other hand against the rock.

"More of you," he says.

"One more," Dr. Mori replies. "We brought equipment. Would you like us to look at your side?"

"Can you fix it?"

"I can try to find out what's wrong. I won't give you a medicine without telling you what it is."

Nappa looks past her at Vegeta. The prince says nothing. Nappa turns back and shifts enough to let the doctor reach him.

"No needles."

"All right."

The examination takes time. Tien and Chiaotzu stay where they can see Nappa's hands, and Gohan watches the doctor's face rather than the screen he cannot read. When Nappa flinches, she stops and asks him whether to continue. He tells her to go on.

Gohan remembers the village doctor examining Goshin. He hasn't forgotten the city; he is glad Dr. Mori asks Nappa anyway.

At last she closes the case. "I can see an area that needs more attention, but I want another opinion before I tell you what it means. I'll speak to the hospital about what we can bring here."

"I don't want to be carried into your city."

"I heard you."

Vegeta gives a short laugh. "You're asking them for help now?"

"You weren't going to help me," Nappa says.

The prince's expression hardens. Goku steps closer, and Vegeta's eyes follow him. He does not rise.

Tien stays at Nappa's side rather than trusting the argument to end on its own.

Dr. Mori comes back along the tracks. "I can ask him again," she says, looking toward Vegeta, "but only if he wants to speak to me."

"He said no yesterday," Krillin tells her. "He hasn't changed his mind."

"Then I'll leave him the choice. If he has trouble breathing, or if he asks, call us."

While the doctor and her colleague pack their equipment, Goku takes Chi-Chi and the boys farther up the empty slope, beyond the Saiyans' hearing. Krillin stays with Vegeta; Tien and Chiaotzu remain beside Nappa. The family can still see them below. Chi-Chi watches until the hospital vehicle has room to leave.

"Bulma thinks the trip could take months," she says. "She also says she hasn't finished checking the ship. The boys want to go."

"They told me," Goku says. He looks at his sons. "I want to see Namek too. King Kai said there are people there, and Vegeta says there are strong fighters out in space. I'd like to meet them."

Goshin brightens. "You could come with us!"

Goku glances back toward the quarry. "I'd like to. But somebody has to stay here while those two are hurt. If Vegeta gets up and goes after people again, I want to be close enough to stop him."

"We can watch too," Gohan says.

"You can," Goku agrees. "But you can't watch from another planet."

Goku looks up, but the two men below are real and close.

Gohan looks toward Vegeta. Wanting to leave won't make the prince less dangerous.

"I still want to go," he says. "I want to meet the Namekians, and I want to help Piccolo ask about Kami's family."

"I want to learn about the ship," Goshin says. "And if we find people who need help, I can help them."

Chi-Chi puts a hand on his shoulder. "I know what both of you want. I want to see that ship again, and I want Bulma to tell me how you're supposed to come home."

"I'll ask her," Gohan says.

"I'll ask her myself. You can come with me."

Gohan looks up. She has not said yes.

Goku grins. "Ask her whether there's room to train in there too."

Chi-Chi turns to him. "They are still sore."

His grin fades just enough for the boys to notice. "I know. I meant later."

"I meant you too."

"Then I'll wait for later."

Goshin leans against his father. "You promised you'd show me the Kaiō-ken."

"I haven't forgotten." Goku ruffles his son's hair. "I haven't forgotten Gohan's match either."

Below them, Krillin calls Goku's name. Vegeta has shifted against the quarry wall, and the loose stone beneath him scrapes down the slope. Goku is beside Krillin before the last piece stops moving.

"I'm getting tired of this wall," Vegeta says.

"Then we'll find you another place," Goku replies. "But you don't get to go looking for one on your own."

Vegeta's hand closes on a broken piece of armour. Goku stays where he is until the prince lets it fall.

A covering can keep the sky out of Vegeta's view, Goku thinks, but it will not keep the prince from deciding to make his own light.

## The Words for Going Home

Yunzabit is colder than the mountain house. Gohan brings Chi-Chi a wrap before she has to ask, and Goshin lands beside the round ship with a gust that sends dust across Bulma's boots.

"Hey!" Bulma lifts a foot. "I just cleaned those."

"Sorry." Goshin looks at the dust. "I could clean them."

"Not with that wind, you couldn't."

Dr. Brief is kneeling beneath one of the ship's supports. A measuring instrument clicks beside him. Popo stands at the open platform with a notebook, and Piccolo has his arms folded near Kami.

"I brought questions," Chi-Chi says.

"Good," Bulma replies. "I've got answers to some of them."

They go inside. The curved room is still recognizable as the place where Kami once lived, but Bulma has marked spaces on the floor with strips of tape. One shows where she wants to put a bed; another outlines a bank of equipment.

Kami follows the lines with his eyes. The room that once held a lonely child is being measured for several people, all talking at once.

"We need more secure places to sleep," she says. "A longer trip means more food and water, and I want a way to check the air without guessing. Dad is looking at a reserve supply."

"Can you put all that in without damaging the ship?" Kami asks.

"I won't cut into the controls or the part that makes it fly until I understand them. The beds and monitors can be fastened to the cabin. We'll show you before we change anything else."

Kami lays a hand against the wall. "It was my home for a long time. I would like it to carry people to Namek, if they choose to go. You have my permission to make the changes you have described. Ask me before you do more."

"I will." Bulma points at the panel. "The part I can't promise yet is the route. King Kai's direction gives us a place to begin, but we have to translate it into something this ship understands."

Gohan studies the marks she has written in her notebook. "What about coming back?"

"We'll enter Earth separately," Bulma says. "And test the return command near home before I trust it far away."

She shows Chi-Chi both entries.

"What if the machine that translates breaks?" he asks.

"Then somebody aboard needs to know the important words." Bulma turns toward Popo. "Which is why he's teaching us."

Popo opens his notebook. "Pikonatto means hello."

"Pikonatto," Gohan repeats.

"Pikonatto," Goshin says. "What means food?"

"You already know that word," Bulma tells him.

"Not in Namekian."

Piccolo takes the notebook from Popo and looks at the page. "Teach them the words for stop and return first."

"And open," Gohan says.

"And food," Goshin adds.

Piccolo gives him a sidelong look. Goshin has no intention of withdrawing the suggestion.

Popo smiles. "Popo will teach those too."

Gohan writes the sounds carefully on a separate sheet. He asks Popo to say each one again, then points to the mark beside it. Goshin tries a word, and the panel gives a questioning beep. Nothing moves.

"Did I say it wrong?"

"You gave it a word for a person, not a command," Popo says.

"Good," Bulma says. "I haven't fastened the bed down yet."

Goshin tries again. A compartment opens beside the wall. Bulma catches the edge before it reaches her notebook.

"That's the bed," he says proudly.

"Yes. Now learn to close it before it eats my work."

Gohan finds the corresponding word on his sheet and says it. The compartment slides shut.

Chi-Chi runs her hand along the round window. "What about the moon?"

"We didn't look until Piccolo checked for one," Gohan says. "We can't always tell what's outside."

"Then we'll cover the window," Bulma says. "A solid cover, not a picture of the view. Somebody without a tail can check before you look."

"And if the ship turns while we're looking?" Goshin asks.

"We close it again." Bulma writes that down. "The cover needs a catch we can reach from every seat."

Gohan remembers the white light Vegeta made above the battlefield. The thought of that light appearing inside the ship makes the narrow room feel suddenly smaller.

Chi-Chi watches her add it to the list. The ship is remarkable; it has carried her sons to Jupiter and back. It is also a room that could carry them far beyond any place she can follow by looking for their ki.

"I want to see it tested," she says.

"So do I," Bulma replies. "That's why we're still here."

## A Match at Last

Over the following days, Bulma and Dr. Brief work on the ship with Kami's permission. Popo teaches them words, and Piccolo checks what he understands against the commands Popo remembers. A recorder repeats the sounds until Bulma can enter them without asking him every time. She calls Chi-Chi with progress and with problems, including a monitor that gives two different air readings until Dr. Brief finds a loose connection.

Bulma stays beside the reconnected monitor through the test before recording the result.

At the quarry, the watches continue. The doctors bring what they can and ask Nappa before each examination. Vegeta accepts no examination. A covering is put near his position, but he can tear it away, and it cannot stop him making another light of his own. Goku and Krillin keep watching his hands and the sky; nobody calls the arrangement a secure prison.

One morning the boys wake without the pulls in their shoulders. Goshin twists from side to side and waits for the soreness that had caught him before. It does not come. Gohan raises his arms and turns them in a slow circle.

"Mine doesn't hurt either," he says.

Chi-Chi makes each of them show her again. She remembers the doctor's warning and asks whether anything has become worse, whether either has trouble breathing or feels dizzy. Both say no.

"All right," she says. "You may fight your father. Away from the city, and you stop if anything hurts."

Gohan shares a look with Goshin. Their father has been waiting for this as eagerly as either of them.

Goku hears the news when they reach the quarry. "Both of them?"

"Both of them," Chi-Chi says.

He is on his feet before she finishes. "Come on!"

"Goku."

He stops and looks back. "Away from the city. Stop if anything hurts."

"And don't leave the Saiyans unwatched."

"I wasn't going to."

Krillin raises his hand. "Piccolo's here, Tien and Chiaotzu have Nappa, and I'll stay right beside Vegeta. You can use the empty ridge where we can all see you."

Piccolo steps between Vegeta and the slope. "If he moves, you'll know."

Goku looks at the prince. Vegeta is watching the boys, his face tight with a different kind of interest now.

"Don't get any ideas," Krillin tells him.

"I have plenty," Vegeta says.

"Then keep them to yourself."

Goku takes the boys above the empty ridge, where their attacks can go into open sky. Chi-Chi remains below with the others, far from the line of their movements. Gohan faces his father first. The wind moves his hair away from his eyes, and his tail curls behind him.

"Last time you touched my shoulder," he says. "Before you went to King Kai."

"I remember. Same rule?"

"Same rule."

Goku raises his hands. "Then come and get me!"

Below them, Chi-Chi braces herself against the wind. She can tell by Goku's grin that he has no intention of standing still and letting Gohan touch him.

Gohan moves first. He crosses the space between them and strikes toward Goku's left shoulder. Goku catches his wrist, just as he did at Bulma's house, but Gohan turns beneath the grip instead of letting it stop him. His free hand comes around from the other side.

Goku ducks. Gohan's fingers skim his hair, and Goku's answering hand flashes toward his shoulder. Gohan lifts out of reach. Their movements crack the air over the ridge, and a fan of dust races away across the empty rock.

Gohan comes down again, but this time he stops short. Goku moves to meet him. Gohan changes direction in midair, using the turn he and Goshin practised in the room, and reaches toward his father's shoulder from behind.

Goku spins with him. Their hands meet; Gohan feels his father's strength and pushes harder. Goku laughs and slips around the pressure, but Gohan has already let his own hand fall. His other palm touches Goku's shoulder.

The touch is light. The movement that earned it has carried them across the sky and back before the dust has settled on the ridge.

"Got you!"

Goku looks down at the hand, then at Gohan. "You did! You made me turn the way you wanted."

Gohan's face lights up. "I thought you might remember the first move."

"I did. I didn't expect the second one." Goku touches his shoulder. "You wanted to see the Kaiō-ken in a match, didn't you?"

Gohan nods. They face each other again, and Goku draws his hands in close.

"I'll double the power I'm using for this round. Kaiō-ken, times two!"

Red light springs around him. He moves, and the air cracks where he has been.

Gohan feels him coming before he can turn. He drops beneath the reaching hand and swings around, sending a short burst of ki across Goku's path. Goku checks his movement and rises over it. Gohan is already following, but his father doubles back and taps his shoulder.

The red fades as Goku comes to a stop. Gohan looks from his shoulder to his father, breathing hard with excitement.

"I almost got around you."

"You made me change direction!" Goku says. "We've got to do that again."

"Can I have my turn first?" Goshin asks.

"You bet!"

Gohan moves clear, still smiling. Goshin takes his place, his black hair lifting in the wind. For an instant the resemblance between him and Goku is so close that Krillin laughs; then Goshin looks down toward Chi-Chi, and her eyes are there in his small face.

Goku holds out both hands. "Show me how much stronger you've gotten."

Goshin remembers lifting the stairs for Ren's crew, and the great wave he showed his father afterward. This time Goku will feel the strength himself. Goshin keeps much of his power in reserve for their match; the others are still below the ridge.

Goshin goes straight at him. Goku meets the first punch with an open palm. The impact drives him back through the air, and his heels cut two lines across the empty ridge when he lands. He springs up before the broken rock can fall around him.

"That's strong!"

Red light flashes around Goku. "Kaiō-ken, times two!"

He darts forward. Goshin turns with him, but Goku's hand reaches the edge of his guard first. Goshin yields with the push rather than driving back through it, then catches himself in the air. The force behind his father's palm is unmistakably greater than before.

He rushes back. Goku changes direction; Goshin follows. Their fists meet above the ridge, and a rolling shock spreads into the high clouds. Goku lets the red light go, and the two of them separate before either crosses the line above the guards.

Goshin cups his hands and sends a Kamehameha past his father. Goku dodges, but Goshin is already moving, turning the wave upward through the clouds. He closes his hands and ends it while still in flight. The technique he learned from Goku and later taught Gohan answers him as readily in motion as it did when he stood on the ground.

"You can steer it while you're fighting!" Goku calls.

Goshin beams. "Gohan can too!"

Piccolo looks up as the cloud bank parts. He felt Goshin's strength after the first transformation; Goku is beginning to feel it for himself.

Goshin stares at his father's hands. "You got faster when the red came."

"Stronger too. The Kaiō-ken multiplies my power. At times two, it's twice what I was using before. That makes my punches and my guard stronger, and my speed and ki rise too."

"What about three?" Gohan asks. "Is that three times the double?"

"Three times what I started with, not three times the double." Goku grins. "King Kai warned me it can hurt if I lose control or push more than my body can handle."

Goshin looks toward the high clouds where he sent his wave. "Do I have to use everything I've got?"

"No. You can hold back for a match, even when you're using the Kaiō-ken. And I'd like you to."

Goshin remembers his father's face at the great wave.

"Will you show me?"

"I said I would." Goku holds out his palms. "First, feel what your ki is doing before you try to make it rise. Don't just throw it everywhere."

"I want to try too," Gohan says. "Can I watch first?"

"Sure! Then we'll have another turn together."

Gohan comes closer to listen. Goshin settles in the air and gathers his energy. He knows how to direct a wave and stop it; now he tries to hold the strength close while lifting it through his body. The first rush escapes around his shoulders in a flash.

"Too much at once?" he asks.

"You let it go before you had it together," Goku says. "Try the way you set your stance before a punch."

Goshin plants his feet on the stone and tries again. This time the pressure gathers more steadily, though it still breaks apart when he reaches for the sudden rise. He lets it settle instead of forcing it.

He has gathered ki for a wave and moved it in several directions at once. Keeping it gathered while he lifts his power through his whole body takes a different kind of attention.

Gohan watches the place where the dust has moved. "It spreads from your feet first."

Goshin looks down. "I didn't notice."

"Neither did I," Goku says. "Good eye, Gohan."

They keep practising until Piccolo calls from the quarry. The hospital team is due back, and Goku returns to the watch. Goshin goes with him, still thinking about the moment his ki came together.

## The Strength That Stays

The next time Chi-Chi and the boys visit, Goshin asks Goku to show him again. Gohan practises the turn that won his match, changing the direction of his own ki as he moves. Goku takes turns with them, stopping whenever the guard needs him. The work does not make the quarry safe by itself; Piccolo and Krillin remain with Vegeta while Tien and Chiaotzu watch Nappa.

Goshin sets his feet. He has been practising the foundations Piccolo taught him for a long time: his balance, his breathing and the way he draws ki into a movement. His father shows him how to gather that strength before asking it for more.

"Now," Goku says, "let it rise. Keep hold of it."

Goshin draws a breath. The energy gathers, firm beneath his skin. He remembers the pressure of Goku's palm and the red light that carried his father forward. He keeps much of his strength in reserve for the match, brings the ki he has chosen together and drives it upward.

"Kaiō-ken, times two!"

Red flares around him. His power rises to twice what he was using without the technique, and the ridge drops away as he launches toward Goku.

Goku's eyes widen. He calls on the power he has chosen for their match, and red flashes around him in answer.

"Kaiō-ken, times two!"

He catches Goshin's hands, but the force behind them bends his arms and drives him backward across the open sky. Goku tries to turn them upward; Goshin feels the change and sees the line above the quarry drawing close. He lets go and turns his own flight sharply up and away. Goku follows, stopping beside him.

They let the red fade. Goshin looks at his father, then at his own hands.

"I did it!"

"You sure did!" Goku flexes his arms, laughing. "I thought I had you, and you carried me halfway across the ridge!"

"I was holding back."

"I could feel that. You were right to do it."

Gohan reaches them. "It looked different from the wave. All your ki rose together."

"It felt different too." Goshin opens and closes his hands. "Nothing hurts. I want to try three."

Goku watches him for a moment, then smiles. "All right. Keep it clear of the quarry, and tell me if anything changes."

Gohan flies higher and looks along the empty side of the ridge. "There's room above the clouds."

Goshin gathers the same ordinary ki as before, leaving the rest in reserve. The strength he gained fighting Vegeta and in his giant form remains with him, even in this small body.

"Kaiō-ken, times three!"

His power climbs to three times the unboosted strength he gathered. Red light streams close around him. His body answers the greater rush, adjusting as he moves, and he shoots upward past Gohan.

Goshin punches into the empty sky. He directs the force away from Earth, and a vast ring tears through the cloud bank above the ridge. He stops his fist and the rush together, then circles back toward his father and brother.

Below them, Piccolo looks up. He remembers the planet-threatening ki in Goshin's ordinary body and feels it rise now. The boy has kept much of that strength in reserve.

Goshin lets the red light go. "Did you see that?"

"I felt it all the way down here!" Krillin calls.

Goku grins at his son. "You held it together at three!"

Goshin presses against Goku's palms as at the start of practice; his father's hands shift farther back. Their work has made Goshin's body and ki stronger, a gain that remains without the red light.

Goku feels it. He presses back, and Goshin's arms hold steady.

"You grew while we were doing that," Goku says. "Next time I want to see how much further we can go."

"Me too!" Goshin says.

Gohan grins at them both. "You still owe me another match. And a turn learning the Kaiō-ken."

"I owe you lots of matches," Goku says. "I'll show you the technique too."

"And me?" Goshin asks.

"You too."

Chi-Chi calls up from below. "You can make a list after you come down!"

Goku lowers his hands. "Your mother's right. And Goshin, the Kaiō-ken can put a strain on you if you push it carelessly. Practise it; don't assume one day's work means you've found everything it can do."

"I won't." Goshin lands beside Chi-Chi. His tail curls around his waist as he looks back at Goku. "Can I try it again later?"

"That's what practising means," Gohan says.

Goshin nudges his brother. Gohan nudges him back, and both of them laugh.

## Four Places

At Yunzabit, the ship sits with its window covered. Bulma has installed the catch Goshin asked for, along with seats that hold fast when the floor moves. Dr. Brief points to two air monitors and shows Chi-Chi how the spare supply is connected.

"We ran them through a long cabin test," he says. "They agree now. There's more air and water packed than Bulma's estimate requires, but an estimate is not a promise about everything that might happen."

"And the way home?" Chi-Chi asks.

"We tested the return command on a short flight," Bulma says. "Popo gave it the destination, and then Piccolo and I gave it the return command separately. The ship brought us back here each time."

She shows Chi-Chi the recorded readings from the test. Gohan follows a line across the page and asks which mark is the turn home; Bulma points it out.

"Not from Namek."

"No. Not from Namek." Bulma closes her notebook. "I've put Earth's direction and the return words in the recorder as well as the translator. I'll know more when we're out there, but I won't tell you that removes every risk."

Gohan stands beside Chi-Chi, holding the sheet of words he has copied. Goshin has his own page, with a drawing of the bed beside the word that made it open. Piccolo is near the doorway, and Popo waits beside Kami.

Chi-Chi looks at the four marked seats. "You want to go," she says to Piccolo.

"Yes."

"And you want to go," she says to Bulma.

"I do. I'm not letting Dad have all the fun with that engine."

Dr. Brief peers around the panel. "I can still come if you want."

"You can look after the laboratory. And the other pod. Without switching anything on."

"Ah. That drawer again."

Chi-Chi turns to Piccolo. "You know why Gohan wants to be there."

"He told me."

"He and Goshin aren't your responsibility because they asked to go. They're mine and Goku's." She looks at the boys. "But if they go with you, I need to know you'll listen to them. And I need to know they'll listen to you."

Piccolo meets her eyes. "I'll watch out for them. You know they can look after themselves, too."

Chi-Chi remembers Gohan telling her how Piccolo checked the window at Jupiter before the boys turned around. She had thanked him for bringing them back.

Gohan remembers the artificial light over the quarry and the way Piccolo had listened when he said he did not want to look. "I want to go with him, Mother."

"So do I," Goshin says. "And with Gohan and Bulma."

"You'd better want to go with me," Bulma says. "I'm the one who knows where I put the beds."

Chi-Chi draws the boys closer. She has watched them carry a year she did not see, stand against two Saiyans, come home hurt and ask to leave again. She cannot make the space between Earth and Namek small. She can ask the people going what they know, and she can decide what she is willing to trust.

"I've thought about going with you," she says. "I would like to see you every day. But your father is staying here, and I want to be here with him. I want our home to be waiting for you when you return."

Goshin takes her hand. "Will you be all right?"

"I'll miss you. That isn't the same thing as not being all right."

Gohan leans against her side. "We'll miss you too."

"Then you can tell me about everything when you come home." She looks from one son to the other. "Yes. You may go, with Bulma and Piccolo."

For a moment neither boy moves. Then Goshin's arms go around her, and Gohan joins him from the other side. Chi-Chi holds them tightly enough that Bulma looks away and pretends to inspect the seat straps.

Kami steps toward Piccolo. "If you find anyone who remembers the child sent here, ask them what you can."

"I will."

"And ask your own questions."

Piccolo's expression softens for an instant. "I said I would."

Popo hands Bulma the recorder. "There are words in here Popo has not yet taught the boys. Listen carefully."

"I'll make them listen carefully," Bulma says.

"I heard that," Goshin says.

"Good. You're learning already."

---

## Before the Sky

Goku stays at the quarry. He would rather see the ship leave, but Vegeta has not agreed to stay where he is, and Nappa still needs care. Krillin remains beside him with the last whole Senzu; Tien and Chiaotzu are across the tracks. The guards have arranged to share the watch and call one another, not declared the two Saiyans harmless.

The boys go to Goku before they leave. Chi-Chi comes with them, carrying the folded letter. Goku reads the new drawing of himself beside the crooked board and laughs.

"I'm taller than the board!"

"You are taller than the board," Goshin says.

"I wasn't sure you'd noticed."

Gohan takes the letter back. "We'll keep practising on the ship. Bulma says there isn't room to break anything."

"Then don't break anything." Goku crouches to bring his face level with theirs. "But when you get back, I want to see everything you've learned. Both of you."

"You promised more matches," Gohan says.

"I meant it."

Goshin presses his hands against Goku's. "I'll practise the Kaiō-ken too."

"Good. Pay attention to how it feels." Goku draws both boys into a hug. "And take care of each other."

"We will," Gohan says.

Chi-Chi waits until they let go. Goku stands and looks at her.

"I really do want to go," he says.

"I know."

"But I want them to have this chance too."

She takes his hand. "Then help me make sure there's a home for them to come back to."

"I will."

The letter rests between their sons. Chi-Chi smooths its fold once, then lets Gohan put it away.

Vegeta watches from the stone. "You think sending the children away will protect them?"

Goku turns toward him. "They aren't going because we're hiding them."

"There are people out there who would make your little family wish it had never left this planet."

Goku's eyes narrow. "Who?"

Vegeta smiles. "Find out for yourself."

Goku wants an answer. He wants to know how strong those people are and where they might be. He also remembers the city after Vegeta's attack and the boys standing in front of the injured Nappa. He takes a step closer to the prince, leaving Chi-Chi and the children behind him.

"If somebody comes here to hurt people, we'll stop them," he says. "And if you try it again, I'll stop you too."

Vegeta looks away first, but he does not take back the smile.

At Yunzabit, the wind pulls at Chi-Chi's wrap. Kami and Popo stand beside the platform. Bulma checks the fastenings on a case of tools while Piccolo carries the boys' bags aboard.

"Mine has the books," Gohan says. "Please don't put it under the water."

"Then carry it yourself," Piccolo replies, handing it back.

Goshin follows with his own bag. Chi-Chi stops him long enough to straighten his collar. His hair springs up around her hand, and he looks so much like Goku that she has to take another breath before she speaks.

"Eat properly. Both of you. And if either of you feels ill, tell Bulma."

"We will," Gohan says.

"Even if it isn't me?" Goshin asks.

"Especially then."

They hug her again. Gohan holds on for a moment after Goshin has stepped back. Chi-Chi kisses his hair, then draws Goshin close once more when he tries to pretend he is already in a hurry.

"Come home," she says.

"We will," Goshin answers.

Popo checks that the window cover is latched. The boys climb aboard without looking through it. Piccolo follows; Bulma is last, after showing Chi-Chi the return recorder one more time.

"I'll keep it where I can reach it," she says.

The platform rises. Gohan stands beside the door and waves until it closes. Chi-Chi keeps her hand raised against the wind.

Inside, Bulma points everyone toward a seat. "Straps. All of you. I'm not catching anybody this time."

"I caught you last time," Goshin says.

"Exactly. Sit down."

Piccolo checks both boys' fastenings, then his own. Gohan holds the letter on his lap. Goshin grips the arms of his seat and looks at his brother.

"Ready?"

Gohan nods. His heart is beating quickly. He thinks of the people King Kai sensed and of the questions Piccolo has carried since the Saiyans arrived. Then he thinks of his mother outside and his father at the quarry, looking after the world they are leaving.

"Ready," he says.

Bulma checks the course they have entered from King Kai's direction. It is their best conversion, not a sighting of Namek itself. She speaks the prepared words, and the panel answers with a clear tone. For a moment the floor hums beneath their feet.

The ship rises from the hollow.

The straps press against their shoulders. This time Gohan has his feet braced, and Goshin holds on to his seat.

Chi-Chi stands with Kami and Popo as the round craft climbs above the ridge. It gathers speed, becoming a bright shape against the blue. A moment later it is gone.

At the quarry, Goku feels his sons' ki moving away. He tilts his face toward the sky, smiling even as he wishes he were going with them.

"Bring back a good story," he says.

Krillin follows his gaze. "And maybe a souvenir."

Across the tracks, Nappa asks for more water. Tien brings it while Chiaotzu keeps watch. Vegeta says nothing. His eyes remain on the empty sky.

Far above them, the four travellers sit together in the ship. Goshin reaches for Gohan's hand, and Gohan takes it. The covered window gives them no view yet, but beyond it is a road none of them has travelled before.
