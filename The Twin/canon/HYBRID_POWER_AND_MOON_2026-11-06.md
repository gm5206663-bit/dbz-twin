## What the source establishes

- **Vegeta's artificial moon:** Dragon Ball Official's manga-based Vegeta profile describes the Power Ball mixing with atmospheric oxygen to create a miniature artificial full moon, and says only select Saiyans use it ([1](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_3929.html)). Selected retained original-Z episode 31 caption cues (21:23–22:33) separately give Vegeta's explanation and Goku noticing Vegeta's ki decline (`C295`). The captions' broadcaster origin and cut are unverified; no complete video or audio was examined for this correction.
- **Gohan's emotions:** Dragon Ball Official's manga-based Gohan profile describes his anger at Goku being hurt releasing latent power against Raditz, followed later by another angry surge after Piccolo's sacrifice ([2](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_503.html)). Selected episode 5 captions (05:15–07:24) separately give 1,307, then 1, and Raditz's observation that power changed with emotion (`C100`, `C239`). The low reading is not a lifelong ceiling, nor does a power spike prove a permanent increase.
- **Hybrid potential:** selected episode 5 captions (22:22–23:19) have Vegeta infer from Gohan that Saiyan–Earthling children can possess extraordinary power and worry about their later growth (`C142`). That is his inference, not a numerical rule that every hybrid exceeds every other Saiyan. The official Frieza-arc profile separately identifies Gohan as a hybrid and describes another angry response and his continued development ([9](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_1201.html)). The biologist's heterosis discussion on the official site is explicitly a speculative thought experiment, not confirmed Dragon Ball genetics.
- These sources do not say pure Saiyans lack emotions, that every hybrid's personality is identical, or that canon Gohan has Goshin's separate Adaptation Talent. Training, choices and relationships matter alongside latent power; rage is not the only route to growth.

## Author direction and prose changes

1. **Current artificial-moon capability (`O229`):** the author expressly says Goshin remembers Vegeta's technique and can create a moon like his. Chapter 26 now lets Goshin see the forming light and sense ki in Vegeta's hand before the flash and throw. In Chapter 29 he tells his family he remembers and can create the light. He lets a tiny bead of ordinary ki fade: **he has not yet created an actual Power Ball on the page**. This is not a reason to downgrade the author-confirmed ability to a hypothetical, impose a teacher or waiting period, or fabricate an earlier full use. Gohan independently decides whether and when he wants to look.
2. **Hybrid emotions (`O230`):** Chapter 6 connects the already-authored 1,500 surge to Goshin's fear and anger for Goku and Gohan; Gohan's original 1,307 and independent actions remain. Chapter 25 gives Goshin an observable ki surge in his existing struggle against Vegeta's planet-threatening attack, and Gohan still independently joins with his own Kamehameha. Neither passage sets a fixed multiplier, makes anger compulsory or automatically turns every momentary surge into permanent growth.
3. **Bloodline and Talent (`O230`, `O243`):** as Goshin grows, his **whole Saiyan–Earthling hybrid bloodline becomes purer, deeper and stronger through his developing Adaptation Talent**. Both parental heritages are part of that developing whole, not merely a Saiyan component with the human side left unchanged. This is Goshin-specific authored continuity, not a canon genetic claim. The corrected Chapter 27 makes strength inherited from both parents deepen while his body adapts during the already-conscious, controlled first giant-form fight. His substantial real ordinary-body and ki growth from that fight and experience still remains after reversion (`O214`); the first-form control and Gohan's choices are unchanged.

The standing author comparison with Gohan still applies without making Gohan passive or denying his own growth (`O056`). The earlier time-dependent space possibility, already-demonstrated ordinary flight and unshown state of unaided space survival remain unchanged (`O228`).
