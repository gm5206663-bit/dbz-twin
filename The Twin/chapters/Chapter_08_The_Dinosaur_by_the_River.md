# Chapter Eight — The Dinosaur by the River

*Revised story draft; not yet accepted.*

As the brothers hurry through the trees, a low branch catches Goshin's bandage and pulls loose the knot Gohan tied beside the ravine.

## The Other Bank

Gohan frees the end and winds it around his brother's palm again, but his shoulder hurts when he lifts his arm, so Goshin holds the cloth while he ties it.

"The rocks fell everywhere," Gohan says. "I didn't mean to break the cliff."

"He shouldn't have thrown you," Goshin says. He looks back through the trees, toward the place where Piccolo disappeared.

Gohan pulls the knot snug. "Can you see Nimbus?"

Through the branches, Goshin sees a yellow edge moving above the trees.

"He's up there."

"Nimbus! Come down!"

As the cloud dips toward them, a blast flashes through the branches and Nimbus darts aside. Splinters fall around the boys.

Piccolo steps between the trees behind them, brushing splintered branches aside.

"You can't hide in the trees forever."

Goshin stands in front of Gohan. "We only have to get past you."

"You haven't managed it yet."

Gohan looks toward the river. "Father's down there."

"Your father can barely stand."

"We're going to him."

Piccolo folds his arms. "You want to survive the Saiyans? Spend six months out here first. Find your own food. Learn what hunts you. Then I'll teach you to fight."

"Six months?" Gohan says. "What about Mother? What about school?"

"If you can't stay alive, you won't need school."

"And where are we supposed to sleep?"

"On the ground."

Goshin looks at the mud beneath his shoes. "It's wet."

"Then find somewhere dry."

"Mother has beds," Gohan says. "And books, and supper. She'll be waiting for us."

"Your mother won't be there when the Saiyans come."

Gohan takes Goshin's good hand. "She'll be with us."

Piccolo's expression hardens, and he raises a hand. Goshin pulls Gohan behind the tree before the blast strikes; the trunk shudders, showering them with bark.

"Go toward the river," Goshin says.

Gohan runs down the sloping path, and Goshin rises and flies beside him, keeping pace.

Piccolo comes over the fallen branches and raises his hand again, but a heavy crash sounds farther ahead. Another follows it, and the ground shakes under Gohan's feet.

A tree bends as something pushes through it.

Gohan stops so abruptly that Goshin nearly flies past him.

A dinosaur's head rises above the ferns. Its pebbled hide is streaked with mud, and water hangs in strings from the teeth filling its open jaws. One clawed foot sinks into the bank as it pushes forward.

"Goshin," Gohan says.

"I see it."

As the dinosaur lunges, Goshin catches Gohan around the waist and lifts him clear. Its jaws snap shut beneath their shoes; its head hits the tree behind them, shaking loose a rain of leaves.

Goshin carries his brother above the first row of branches, where Piccolo is waiting over the canopy.

"I'll stop you here."

"There's something with teeth down there!" Gohan says.

"Then stay up here."

Piccolo reaches for him. Goshin twists away, and all three drop through the leaves toward the riverbank.

Goshin sets Gohan on his feet and puts his weight on the ankle Piccolo gripped; the red mark has already faded. Gohan scrambles beneath a fallen trunk, and Goshin follows with both hands free.

The dinosaur's claws rake the bark above them.

"It can't get under," Gohan says.

The trunk rolls.

"It can move it!" Goshin says.

They hurry out the other side, and the dinosaur tears the trunk away and comes after them along the bank.

Goshin fires at the ground beside its feet, and mud bursts against its legs, making the dinosaur turn toward him. Gohan waves both arms from behind a rock.

"Over here!"

The dinosaur swings back, and Goshin flies to the other side so the boys can lead it around the rock instead of toward the river.

Gohan's foot slides on the wet stones. He grabs a hanging vine and swings clear of the dinosaur's jaws, straight into a patch of nettles.

"Ow!"

"Are you bitten?" Goshin calls.

"No. The plants got me."

The vine bounces back and taps the dinosaur on the nose, drawing an indignant roar.

The dinosaur reaches past the rock, and Gohan ducks, but its teeth catch the vine above him. It pulls, shaking leaves down over his hat.

Goshin fires a narrow burst at the end of the vine, breaking it above the dinosaur's jaws. The animal recoils with a mouthful of leaves, and Gohan runs clear.

"That wasn't lunch," Gohan calls back.

"Don't tell it!" Goshin says.

As the dinosaur roars and stomps after Gohan, Goshin circles behind it, but Piccolo drops from the trees into his path.

"Are you going to fight it or run?"

"Both," Goshin says.

He darts beneath Piccolo's arm and joins Gohan behind the rock. Piccolo lands on the higher bank, watching the dinosaur swing its head toward the boys.

Gohan stops when a thin cry comes from behind the rock.

"Did you hear that?"

The dinosaur charges again. Goshin pushes Gohan clear, and its snout strikes the rock between them.

The cry comes a second time.

---

## The Aircraft

Bulma kneels beside the damaged tail, where a bent control rod juts from its housing with blackened wiring wrapped around it.

"Come on," she tells the rod, pulling with a pair of pliers. It doesn't budge.

She cuts the wire away and tries again, pulling the rod free and leaving a smear of soot across her cheek.

"That's one problem. Now I only have the rest of the aircraft."

Bulma looks up as a shadow passes over the clearing.

Mr. Popo descends on a carpet and lands beside her.

"Popo?"

"Kami sent me to help. Where is Goku?"

"He and Roshi went up the river. Piccolo's after the boys."

Popo looks toward the trees. "I'll find them."

He reaches the carpet, then pauses when Bulma takes the wrapped scouter from her pocket.

"Can you fix it?" he asks.

"I can try. I need tools that fit this casing."

She shows him the edge, but none of the drivers in her hand matches the tiny slots.

"I'm not putting its cell back in while it might be transmitting. I'll take it to my lab when I can."

Popo nods and rises above the clearing. Bulma wraps the scouter again, puts it away from the separate cell, and returns to the bent rod.

---

## The Road

Ox-King's truck rounds a bend on the way to the coast. Chi-Chi has been watching the sky above the trees; when a figure in orange flies over the road, she opens the window.

"Krillin!"

Krillin turns at her voice and drops beside the truck as Ox-King brakes hard enough to scatter gravel.

Chi-Chi is out before the wheels have stopped. "Where are Goku and the boys? Turtle told me someone took them."

"They got away from Raditz," Krillin says. "Goku and Piccolo beat him."

"Are the boys hurt?"

"They were both awake when I left. Gohan hurt his shoulder. Goshin hurt his head and hand."

"And Goku?"

Krillin touches the bag inside his shirt. "He's alive, but he was hurt badly. I went to Korin for beans."

"How badly?"

"His arm was burned, and he was holding his ribs. He was talking to us when I left."

"When was that?"

"Before the aircraft took off."

For a moment Chi-Chi grips the open door. The last time she saw Goku, he had a sack of rice over one shoulder and their boys climbing onto his cloud. She had called after them to bring their shoes home.

She catches Krillin's arm. "Take me to them."

"I'm going there now. I can feel the boys, and Piccolo's near them, but I don't know what he's doing."

"He went after the man who took them, didn't he?"

"With Goku. They fought Raditz together."

"Then let's find out why he's still with my sons."

Ox-King climbs down from the truck. "I'll follow as far as the road goes."

He reaches into the cab and gives Chi-Chi the first-aid box, then catches the folded blanket against his chest when it slips out after it.

"I'll bring this too."

"If you see Turtle, tell him we're going to the boys."

"I will."

Chi-Chi keeps hold of the box as Krillin lifts her, and they rise above the trees.

"Which way?" she asks.

Krillin turns toward the mountains. "There."

The truck starts below them.

---

## The Small Cry

Gohan edges around the rock to a narrow channel cut by the river between the bank and a fallen slab. Beneath a thick root, a small dinosaur struggles to pull its leg free, giving another thin cry with each tug.

The larger animal roars on the other side of the rock. Gohan looks from the trapped youngster to the teeth he has just escaped.

"There's a little one," he tells Goshin. "It's stuck."

The big dinosaur charges around the rock, and Goshin flies between it and Gohan and fires into the water. Spray hits its face, and it turns away long enough for Gohan to reach the channel.

Gohan touches the edge of the slab. "This might have come from the cliff. When I broke it."

"Let's get it out."

Goshin crouches beneath the slab and heaves, rock scraping against rock. Water rushes into the space he opens, but the root is wedged into a crack and still pins the little dinosaur's leg.

"I'll lift it higher," he says.

The small dinosaur snaps at Gohan when he reaches underneath. He jerks back, then sees a broken branch floating beside the bank. He pulls it out, pushes the end into the crack and levers at the root.

It moves, but not enough, so Gohan plants a stone under the branch and bears down again. This time the root lifts, and the little dinosaur drags its leg free.

"There! Come on!"

Instead of coming out, the youngster scrambles farther under the slab. Gohan crawls after it, his injured shoulder brushing the stone.

The big dinosaur roars behind them, its footsteps shaking the bank. Goshin braces the slab against his shoulder and thrusts his free hand toward the animal. His burst strikes the water at its feet, throwing up a sheet of spray. The dinosaur slips sideways, catches itself and comes on again.

Gohan reaches for the youngster. It snaps at his sleeve; he folds the cloth over his hand and tries again.

"I'm not going to hurt you. Please come out."

The large dinosaur opens its jaws above them. Goshin starts to raise his hand again, but a blast tears into the bank in front of it. Mud flies up between the teeth and the boys. The dinosaur skids back, roaring.

Piccolo stands among the trees, smoke curling from his fingertips.

"You're no use to me dead."

Goshin blinks mud from his eyes. "We didn't ask you."

"I didn't ask you to stop for an animal."

The slab shifts. Goshin sets his feet and pushes it up while Gohan catches the small dinosaur around the middle. It kicks and wriggles, but he holds on and pulls it out, his shoulder aching.

"Goshin, it's clear!"

Goshin lifts the slab away and drops it in the empty channel with a splash, then comes around it as the large dinosaur breaks through the falling mud.

Gohan sets the young dinosaur down and backs away. It limps once, finds its footing and runs to the larger animal. For a moment both dinosaurs look at the boys. Then the adult turns and pushes through the ferns, with the youngster hurrying after it.

Gohan sinks into the wet grass, watching the ferns sway long after the dinosaurs have gone.

"It got out."

Goshin offers him a hand. "You got it out."

"We did. I was afraid it would bite you."

"It nearly bit you!"

Gohan looks up the bank. "Its mother found it."

Goshin gives his hand a tug. "We'll find ours too."

They hurry toward the river, and Gohan snatches a red apple hanging above the path. He takes a bite without stopping.

His face folds. "Sour!"

"Let me try." Goshin takes the apple, bites it and gives it back. His face folds too. "It is sour."

"Mother would make curry."

"With potatoes," Goshin says.

"Or steamed buns. Or shumai."

Gohan takes another small bite and makes the same face. Goshin laughs, then touches the place where Raditz struck him.

"It doesn't hurt now," he says.

"Good," Gohan says. "Don't press it again."

Goshin takes his hand away. "I won't."

Piccolo lands across the channel.

"Finished?"

Gohan drops the apple. Goshin turns toward Piccolo.

"You helped us," Gohan says.

"I stopped the animal. You still have a year before the Saiyans arrive."

"We don't want to go with you."

"I know."

Piccolo's arm stretches toward Gohan. Goshin strikes the wrist aside. Gohan calls Nimbus, and the cloud comes through the broken branches.

Piccolo reaches again, and Gohan jumps onto Nimbus and steers it between two trees. Goshin flies beside him.

Piccolo's arm follows them through the gap, then curls around the nearer trunk. His hand catches Gohan's sleeve.

Gohan turns Nimbus sharply around the other side. The sleeve tears free, and Piccolo's arm tightens against the trunk. Goshin kicks the trapped wrist as Piccolo pulls it back. Bark strips away, and the hand misses Gohan by inches.

Piccolo rips his arm clear and rises above the branches. A blast comes after them. Goshin turns his hand and sends it down into the river. Water erupts behind them, hiding the bank.

"Father!" Gohan calls.

A voice answers through the trees.

"Gohan! Goshin!"

Nimbus carries Gohan toward it, with Goshin flying close beside him.

---

## The Carpet

Goku leans on Roshi as they follow the river, then takes another step and nearly falls at the sound of Gohan's voice.

"I'm coming!" he calls.

A carpet drops through the gap above them with Popo standing on it.

"Get on," he says. "I'll take you to them."

Roshi helps Goku onto the carpet and climbs on with the medical case, and Popo lifts them above the water.

Nimbus comes around the bend, carrying Gohan, with Goshin flying beside him. Goku reaches out. Gohan guides Nimbus alongside the carpet, and Goshin catches the edge to keep the two level.

"Father!"

Gohan steps across and flings his good arm around him. Goshin lands at his other side. Goku gathers them both close; the movement hurts, but he doesn't let go until Gohan pulls back to see his face.

"Are you hurt again?" Goku asks.

"My shoulder. The dinosaur didn't bite us."

"Dinosaur?"

"There were two," Goshin says. "Piccolo stopped the big one."

"Then he chased us again," Gohan adds.

Goku feels Piccolo close behind them, and a moment later the green figure rises above the trees.

Roshi puts the medical case between Goku and the edge of the carpet as Popo turns to face Piccolo.

"Leave them alone," Goku says.

"You can't protect them lying down."

Goshin moves beside his father while Gohan takes his hat off and holds it against his chest.

A second voice comes from above the river.

"Goku!"

Krillin descends with Chi-Chi. He lands on the bank, and Chi-Chi is running toward the carpet before he has both feet on the ground. Popo brings it beside the bank.

"Mother!" Gohan calls.

Krillin takes a bean from the bag and tosses it to Goku, who catches it, puts it in his mouth and swallows.

He takes a full breath. He moves his arm, then rises from the carpet without Roshi's help. The pain is gone, and his strength is back.

Piccolo watches him.

"You're not taking my sons," Goku says.

Chi-Chi reaches the carpet and steps aboard. She draws Gohan close with one arm and Goshin with the other, gathering damp, torn cloth and muddy children against her. Gohan buries his face in her shoulder. Goshin catches her sleeve but keeps looking past her at Piccolo.

She follows his gaze.

"You stay away from them."

"When the Saiyans come, that won't keep them alive."

"Then we'll prepare for them," Goku says. "Together."

Piccolo looks at the recovered Goku, then at Krillin on the bank and Popo and Roshi on the carpet. Both boys are watching him too.

"Don't expect me to save you."

He flies above the trees and disappears over the ridge. Goshin watches until he is gone, then turns back toward his mother.

Chi-Chi catches Goku's hand. Turtle had told her he was hurt, and Krillin had gone for the beans; a moment ago she had seen him lying on the carpet. Now his hand is warm and steady against hers.

"You frightened me," she says.

"I'm sorry."

"All three of you."

Gohan presses closer. Goku puts his arm around them, and Chi-Chi holds on.

Across the ridge, Piccolo lands among the stones and touches the place on his chest where Goshin struck him, then lets his hand fall.

"Goku's recovered. Both boys are with him."

He faces a tall rock pillar and raises his new arm. Light gathers in his palm, and his shot cuts through the stone. The upper half crashes into the ravine, taking a smaller boulder with it.

Piccolo looks at the broken end. Against Raditz, his best attack had needed time he could scarcely find. The two Saiyans coming to Earth would be stronger.

He sets his feet and gathers his strength again.

---

## Three Beans

Chi-Chi kneels in front of the boys, her hands shaking as she sets the first-aid box on the carpet.

"You were taken away," she says. "Turtle told me."

Gohan presses against her. "Goshin couldn't wake up. I stayed with him."

"He was there when I woke up," Goshin says.

Chi-Chi holds them both for a moment. "Turtle didn't know where that man had taken you. I didn't know if I'd see you again."

Goku rests his hand on Chi-Chi's shoulder. Goshin catches her sleeve.

"I wanted you to come."

"I came as soon as I could."

Chi-Chi's face crumples for an instant. She hugs him again, taking care of his hand, then draws back to look at it.

"The middle is better," Goshin tells her.

He brings the bandaged palm forward, and Chi-Chi unwraps the dirty cloth. New pink skin covers the middle of the burn, though the edge is still red.

"It was bleeding at the crater," Gohan says.

Chi-Chi turns the hand toward the light. Goshin spreads his fingers for her. She takes a clean dressing from the box and wraps it loosely to keep the palm clean.

"Your head too?"

"It hurt where Raditz hit me. It stopped by the river."

"Were you sick? Did the ground move?"

"I wasn't sick. The room tilted when I first stood up in the pod, but it stopped."

"We'll tell the doctor that too."

She looks at Krillin. "How long was he unconscious?"

"Raditz knocked him out on the island. He was awake when we reached the crater. I don't know exactly how long."

Chi-Chi closes the box. "I'm having someone look at him today. A blow that knocked him out isn't something I'm guessing about."

Goku nods. "We'll take him."

Krillin offers Goshin the second bean. "This would heal your hand and your head."

Goshin studies the bean. "How many are left?"

"Two, after the one your father ate."

Goshin looks at Gohan's shoulder, then opens and closes his own hand. "Give one to Gohan. My hand is getting better. What if somebody else gets hurt?"

"Are you sure? You can have one too," Chi-Chi says.

He touches the side of his head. "I know. I still want the doctor to look. We can keep the last bean in case somebody needs it."

Chi-Chi draws him close. "Then the doctor looks, and you tell me if anything changes."

"Did you see where Korin grows them?" Goshin asks Krillin.

"He took these out of a jar."

"Can I see the jar?"

"I expect he'll show you that much," Krillin says. "We can ask about the rest."

Goku smiles. "He might even give you a tour."

"If Korin wants to," Chi-Chi says.

"If he wants to," Goku agrees. Krillin keeps one bean ready for Gohan and folds the bag around the last one.

Gohan moves closer to see the bandage around Goshin's palm. Chi-Chi checks that the cloth is not pulling against the new skin before she settles the first-aid box beside her.

Chi-Chi brushes the hair back from Goshin's cheek. "For now, I want you close enough that I can see you."

Goshin sits beside her on the carpet and leans against her arm.

Krillin holds out the bean to Gohan, who looks at his brother.

"What about you?" Gohan asks.

"I can wait," Goshin says. "The doctor will look at me."

Gohan takes it and eats it, then raises his arm and turns it all the way around.

"It doesn't hurt."

"And your strength?" Chi-Chi asks.

Gohan gets up. "That's back too."

Goku grins at him. "Good."

Goshin watches Gohan swing his arm, then looks at the bag. The bean worked so quickly that his brother is already moving as if nothing had happened.

"You really don't mind saving the last one?" Gohan asks.

Goshin shakes his head. "We might need it later."

"I'll come with you to ask Korin about the jar," Gohan says.

"You don't have to."

"I want to."

Goshin leans against him while Krillin ties the bag shut and puts it away.

"I'll keep it safe," he says.

## Above the Clouds

Popo lifts the carpet from the bank with the family and Roshi aboard. Roshi keeps the medical case beside him, and Krillin flies alongside as Popo carries them back toward the clearing.

When the damaged aircraft appears below them, Bulma waves from its tail.

"They're together," Popo says.

He brings the carpet down. Goku steps off first and steadies Chi-Chi as she lifts her first-aid box. Goshin hops down and offers his good hand to Gohan, who jumps beside him.

"The rod's bent," Bulma says. "I can mend it, but not in one minute."

"You don't have to," Goku says. "We can get everyone home."

Popo looks toward the clouds. "I should tell Kami what happened."

"Thank you for finding us," Goku says.

Popo bows his head and lifts away on the carpet.

At the Lookout, Kami waits with his staff as Popo lands beside him.

"The boys reached Goku," Popo says. "Piccolo tried to take them. He left when the others came."

"He still intends to rule this world," Kami says.

"He also stopped an animal from reaching the boys."

Kami looks down through the clouds. "That is not the same as letting them choose."

"You spoke to King Yemma?"

"Raditz reached his judgment. In the past, souls killed by the Demon Clan wandered without finding rest. Raditz did not. Something about Piccolo is different."

"Does that mean he is good?"

"I don't know. We have seen what he did to the children."

Kami rests both hands on his staff.

"There is something else. I have a feeling my life may have about a year left."

Popo turns toward him. "Because the Saiyans are coming?"

"Perhaps. Or it may be my own age. I cannot tell, and I may be wrong."

"If Piccolo dies, you die too."

"And if I die, so does he. Our lives are joined. If my feeling is right, the Dragon Balls may have only one use left."

Kami looks toward the world below. "Perhaps Piccolo feels it too. Perhaps he wants to leave something behind, even if it's strength in Goku's sons."

"That doesn't excuse what he did."

"No. It doesn't."

Popo looks toward the clouds. "The others should know."

"They should. I'll speak to Goku."

Kami lifts his staff and walks toward the edge of the Lookout.

## A Cup of Tea

In Otherworld, a customer orders French toast and milk while another counts her change at the café counter.

"One coin short."

The server finds it beneath a saucer. At the next table, Fortuneteller Baba watches the coin change hands, then turns to the guide opposite her.

"You said Earth's guardian came to Yemma?"

"He asked whether King Kai might help a living fighter named Son Goku. His Majesty said he would ask."

"Did he give permission?"

"No. I heard him say he couldn't promise an answer."

Baba sets down her cup. "Goku is my brother's pupil. Roshi should hear that Kami asked about him."

"Please tell him exactly that."

"I'm old, not deaf."

She puts a coin on the table and climbs onto her crystal ball.

"Where are you going?" the guide asks.

"To find my brother. Someone has to pay attention down there."

As the crystal ball rises past the counter, the server catches a cup before it tips.

## The Way Back

Gohan sits beside Chi-Chi at the edge of the clearing, while Goshin sits on her other side with his wrapped hand in his lap.

"A little dinosaur was caught under a rock," Gohan says.

"The big one wanted to eat us," Goshin adds.

"It wanted its baby," Gohan says.

"It wanted to eat us too."

Chi-Chi looks from one to the other. "You helped it?"

Gohan nods. "The rock might have fallen when the cliff broke. We couldn't leave it there."

Goku sits beside them. "You got away from Piccolo and helped it. You did things I didn't know you could do."

"I was scared," Gohan says.

"I would have been too."

"He stopped the big one from biting us," Gohan says.

Chi-Chi looks toward the ridge where Piccolo disappeared. "After he shot at you?"

"Yes."

Goku looks at Goshin. "Did he leave you alone afterward?"

"He still tried to take us."

Goku's smile fades. "I'm sorry he got near you again."

Goshin presses against Goku's side, and Goku puts an arm around him.

"Can I still study if we have to train?" Gohan asks.

"Of course you can," Chi-Chi says. "You're not giving up your books."

Goku rests a hand on Goshin's shoulder and closes his eyes for a moment. "Your ki feels stronger than it did at the crater."

Goshin looks at his palm. "Even after all that?"

"Even after all that."

Gohan straightens. "What about mine?"

"It's growing too." Goku grins. "We'll all keep getting stronger."

Goshin lifts his hand. "I want to practise aiming. I want to hit the right place even when everything's moving."

"That's a good thing to practice," Goku says.

"I want Mother's curry," Gohan says.

"You just ate a Senzu," Krillin says.

Gohan puts a hand on his stomach. "Oh. I'm not hungry now."

Goku looks surprised too. "Neither am I."

Bulma stops working and stares at him. "Someone write that down."

Chi-Chi laughs, and Gohan laughs with her. Goshin smiles, then tucks his head against Goku's arm.

Roshi sets down the medical case. "There's something I should have told you on the island. Raditz spoke about the full moon."

Goku looks at him. "He said Saiyans change."

"I saw you look at a full moon during a tournament. You became a giant ape. You didn't remember afterward."

Goku's hand stops on Goshin's shoulder. "That was me?"

"Yes."

Goku stares at him. "Grandpa used to warn me about the full moon. I thought a monster killed him."

Roshi takes off his sunglasses.

"Could it have been me?" Goku asks.

"I wasn't there. I don't know." Roshi turns the glasses in his hands. "I should have told you what I saw at the tournament. I'm sorry."

The river murmurs beyond the clearing. Goku looks down at his hands, the same hands Grandpa Gohan once guided through a fighting stance. Chi-Chi reaches across the boys and takes one of them.

"You were a child," she says. "And you don't know what happened."

"He warned me."

"He loved you, Goku."

Goku holds her hand for a moment, then looks up at Roshi again.

Chi-Chi draws the boys closer. "Could it happen to them?"

"I can't say for certain. They have tails. Until we know more, don't let them look at a full moon."

Gohan's tail curls around his ankle. "Are we monsters?"

"No," Chi-Chi says at once. "You're my sons."

"What if I look by accident?"

"If we close the curtains, we can't see it," Goshin says.

"How will we know when it's full?" Gohan asks.

"We'll find out," Chi-Chi says.

Goku puts his arm around both boys. "We'll watch out for each other. You won't have to work it out alone."

Gohan leans against Chi-Chi, who keeps one arm around him and her other hand in Goku's. She kisses the top of his head and draws Goshin close enough to kiss him too.

Bulma puts the damaged rod in her toolkit and closes it. She presses the aircraft's capsule switch; the vehicle contracts into its capsule, which she slips into her case. The scouter remains wrapped in her pocket, with its cell separate.

"I can work on both of them at home," she says. "For tonight, let's get out of the forest."

Goku holds his arms out to Goshin. "Want me to carry you?"

"Yes."

Goku lifts him carefully. Gohan takes his mother's hand. Behind them, Nimbus waits above the grass.
