A delivery cart lies across the broken crossing. One wheel turns against the pavement, slows and stops.

**SIX SEEDS**

**NARRATOR:** Vegeta and Nappa have landed in East City. Goku is still with King Kai, while Gohan, Goshin and their friends hurry toward the two Saiyans.

## The Crossing

The little girl clings to her father's coat. He keeps her in the doorway while a man helps the delivery driver away from the overturned cart. A woman joins them and takes the driver's other arm.

"Can you walk?" the woman asks.

"I think so. My arm's hurt."

"Come with me. There's a clinic on the next street."

Nappa steps out of the crater and onto the crossing. He looks at the people hurrying past the shops.

"They don't know what to make of us."

Vegeta touches his scouter and turns toward the sky. He scans two approaching signals: **9,200**, then **12,400**. Several others are farther away.

Nappa touches his own scouter and looks at its display. "Both stronger than me? Are those the ones Raditz fought?"

"We'll find out," Vegeta says. "Their power changed while he fought them. Don't trust the readings to stay where they are."

A man across the road raises a camera. The flash goes off, and Nappa turns toward him. The man lowers it and backs against a wall.

"You should have asked me to smile," Nappa says. He bares his teeth at him.

Nappa looks from the people to the approaching signals. "If those fighters care about this place, they'll come straight to us."

Vegeta glances at the buildings. "Don't ruin the planet. We may still want to sell it."

"I'm only saying hello."

As light gathers in Nappa's hand, the man drops his camera and runs. The girl cries out, and her father pulls her farther inside the doorway.

Above the city, Gohan and Goshin draw level with Piccolo. Goshin keeps some of his power in reserve as they pass over the crowded streets. The light in Nappa's hand reaches the rooftops below them.

"There are people right there!" Gohan calls.

Goshin pulls ahead and drops toward the street. Gohan follows, turning toward the doorway as Nappa lifts his arm. Piccolo comes down behind them.

Goshin strikes Nappa's forearm from below, knocking it upward as the blast leaves his hand. The beam tears through the edge of a roof and climbs into the open sky, lighting the windows along the avenue.

Glass falls across the pavement as the street shakes. A hanging sign breaks loose and crashes down across the fleeing people's path.

A slab breaks from the damaged building and tips toward the doorway. Gohan catches it with his ki above the father and daughter, but smaller pieces scatter around their feet.

"Come out!" Gohan calls.

The slab shifts in his grip. He steadies it while the father carries his daughter underneath, then guides it down into the road, clear of them. He lifts a fallen sign from their path.

The delivery driver has fallen beside the crossing. The man and woman with him are trying to get him up. Gohan flies to them and takes the driver's uninjured arm. Together they bring him to his feet.

"Can you reach the next street?" Gohan asks.

"We can," the woman says. "What about you?"

Gohan looks back at Goshin and Nappa. "I have to stay here."

She puts the driver's arm across her shoulders, and the man helps her lead him after the father. Gohan waits until they have passed the fallen glass, then turns toward the Saiyans.

Nappa stares at his arm. Goshin lands in front of him, with the two pods behind them and the people running toward the next crossing.

"Don't fire at them," Goshin says.

Nappa's hand closes into a fist. "You knocked that aside?"

He swings at Goshin. Goshin catches the punch in one hand. Nappa drives forward, but Goshin's arm doesn't move. Nappa's grin vanishes. Goshin's feet stay planted beside the cart, and for a moment the larger man can only stare at the hand holding his fist.

Goshin lets go, steps inside Nappa's guard and drives his palm into the armour. Nappa flies off his feet and crashes into the crater, breaking stone beneath him.

Goshin stays between him and the people escaping down the street.

Nappa pushes himself up on one arm, gasping. He looks at the dent in his armour, then at Goshin, who is still standing beside the cart.

"Kakarot's son?"

Gohan comes up beside his brother. The wind lifts Goshin's black, spiked hair from a face that closely resembles his father's, with Chi-Chi's eyes fixed on Nappa.

"They got out of that doorway," Gohan says.

"There are more people down the road," Goshin says.

Vegeta watches the brothers. His scouter sounds as he looks at Gohan on the broken crossing: **9,200**. He turns toward Goshin beside the cart, and the display jumps to **12,400**.

"The two signals," Vegeta says. "Nine thousand two hundred and twelve thousand four hundred."

Nappa looks from him to the boys standing between the Saiyans and the fleeing civilians. "Kakarot's sons? Both of them?"

"Those weren't the numbers Raditz sent us. They've grown."

Nappa gets to his feet. When Goshin glances toward the people at the corner, Nappa lunges for his tail. Goshin turns and catches his wrist before he can reach it.

Nappa pulls against Goshin's grip, but cannot make him give ground.

Nappa raises his other hand toward the fleeing people. Gohan darts in and catches that wrist. Nappa tries to bring his arm down, but Gohan holds it pointed at the sky.

"Leave them alone!" Gohan says.

The woman and the man guide the injured driver into the next street. The father reaches the corner with his daughter in his arms.

Nappa strains against both boys, but neither lets him move his hands.

"Enough, Nappa," Vegeta says. "You can't beat them like that. Stand down."

Nappa glares at him. "They're just children!"

"Children who are stronger than you. Come back."

Nappa stops struggling. Goshin and Gohan release him, and he backs toward Vegeta, rubbing his wrist. The brothers stay between the Saiyans and the people at the corner.

## A Voice They Remember

Piccolo lands on the broken road. His cape settles around him as he looks at the Saiyans, then at the brothers.

"Gohan. Goshin. Keep them away from the buildings."

"I remember that voice," Vegeta says. "You're the one who killed Raditz."

"You heard me through his scouter."

"And I heard about the Dragon Balls."

Gohan looks at Vegeta's scouter. "You want them too?"

"That's why we came. Tell us where to find them."

"No," Gohan says.

Nappa looks past him at the people leaving the street. "You can't guard every one of them."

Piccolo steps between the Saiyans and the road the civilians are using. "You heard the boy."

Nappa looks closely at him. "Vegeta. He's a Namekian, from Planet Namek."

Piccolo's eyes narrow. "A what?"

"A Namekian," Vegeta says. "I wondered where the Dragon Balls came from. Some of your people have unusual abilities."

Piccolo repeats the unfamiliar name under his breath. He has known himself as Piccolo all his life. Now this stranger claims to know where he came from.

"I don't know any Namekians."

"You made the Dragon Balls, didn't you?"

"I didn't. Fighting is more my kind of work."

Vegeta's gaze shifts toward Gohan and Goshin. "Then someone else made them."

Goshin keeps his hands raised. "We're not giving them to you."

"Raditz called one of you Broc. Which one?"

"My name is Goshin."

"Raditz had trouble with you."

"He took us away from our family."

Nappa looks toward the open street. "Where's Kakarot? I wanted to see him."

Gohan takes a step forward. "He's coming."

"Then we won't have to look for him," Vegeta says.

A siren sounds beyond the next block. Piccolo glances toward it, then back at Vegeta.

"There's an old quarry west of here. No houses near the working ground. If you want to fight, come there."

"And if we don't?" Nappa asks.

Goshin looks at Nappa's hand. "We'll stop you here."

Vegeta's scouter chirps as more signals approach from different directions. Four figures flash across the lens: **1,120**, **1,360**, **1,410** and **700**. He looks back at the brothers.

"Your friends are coming. Those are well below you two. They'll follow you to the quarry."

"Vegeta?" Nappa says.

"We came for the Dragon Balls. I want to know which of them can tell us where they are. And I want room to see what those boys can do."

Nappa presses a hand against his dented armour. "They could be hiding even more."

"I know. So watch them."

Goshin looks down the road, where the last of the people have reached the corner. "Let's get them away from here," he tells Gohan.

Piccolo rises above the crater. "The quarry is this way."

Vegeta lifts off after him. Nappa follows, watching the brothers. Gohan and Goshin stay between the Saiyans and the rooftops until all of them are clear of the avenue.

Below, the cart's loose wheel lies still.

## The Road Out

Chi-Chi sees the broken roof from Popo's carpet. Smoke drifts across the street, and people are gathering at the corner beyond it.

"There," she says. "Bring us down beside that bus."

Popo lowers the carpet to a patch of pavement. A bus driver has stopped across the side road and opened both doors. Passengers climb out while he checks the damage beneath the front wheel.

"The road's blocked," he tells Chi-Chi. "I can't take them any farther."

"Can they walk to the next junction?"

"Most of them. One woman hurt her ankle."

Chi-Chi goes into the bus. A woman sits near the door with her foot raised on the opposite seat. Chi-Chi kneels to look at it.

"Can you stand if I help you?"

"I can try."

Popo steadies her on one side, and Chi-Chi takes the other. They help her down the steps and onto the carpet. The father and daughter from the doorway are waiting beside the bus; the girl has both arms around his neck.

"My daughter won't let me put her down," he says. "I can walk."

"Then stay with her," Chi-Chi says. "We'll carry the people who can't."

A car horn sounds from the next junction. Bulma pulls up beside the group and opens the back door.

"There's a school gym on the road out," she says. "The police are directing people there. I can take three at a time."

Popo brings the carpet alongside the car. Bulma and Chi-Chi help the woman with the injured ankle into the back seat. The driver guides two more passengers in after her.

"We'll be back for you," Bulma tells the others.

The father shifts his daughter higher. "Those men went west. Is that where the fighting will be?"

Chi-Chi looks up. Far beyond the roofs, five figures are moving away from the city.

"My sons are with them," she says. "We're getting everyone away from this street."

The girl lifts her face from her father's shoulder. "The one in the road helped us."

Chi-Chi closes her hand on her own sleeve. One of her boys was in that street when the roof broke. She brushes glass from the girl's coat. "I'm glad you got clear."

Bulma starts the car. Popo lifts the carpet just high enough to pass over the stalled bus and follows her toward the junction.

Chi-Chi stays with the people still waiting. She takes the end of a fallen barrier and drags it aside so the group can use the pavement. Through an open doorway, she sees people gathered above a stairwell in the next building.

Krillin descends beyond the bus. Yamcha lands beside him, with Puar close behind.

"Chi-Chi!" Krillin calls. "Did you see them?"

"The boys and Piccolo led them west. There was an attack here, but Goshin turned it away from the street."

Yamcha looks at the broken roof. "We felt it from the coast."

Puar floats toward the waiting passengers. "Can I help you get them out?"

"There's another group inside that building," Chi-Chi says. "The stairs are clear as far as I could see."

"I'll look."

Puar turns toward Yamcha, who gives a quick nod. "Stay with Chi-Chi. We'll find you when we can."

"Be careful," Puar says, and flies through the open entrance.

A woman comes around the bus, calling for her husband. Chi-Chi points her toward the crossing where the father is waiting with his daughter.

"He came away from the damaged street," Chi-Chi says. "His daughter is with him."

The woman runs to them. The girl reaches for her, and her father lowers her into her mother's arms. All three move back beside the bus.

Yamcha looks toward the roof of the damaged building. "We'll keep the fighting away from here if we can."

"Get everyone back," Chi-Chi says. "Including yourselves."

Yamcha gives her a quick smile. "That's the part I was planning to take care of first."

"See that you do."

Krillin looks west. "Come on, before she gives us homework too."

Krillin lifts into the air. "We can feel them moving."

Yamcha follows him over the roofs. Chi-Chi watches until they are past the smoke, then turns back to the bus.

## The Long Road

At Kame House, Roshi waits beside the telephone while Oolong turns up the radio. The report describes damage around the landing site and asks people to keep the roads clear.

"It doesn't say where those two went," Oolong says.

"They're away from the city now," Roshi says. "I can feel the others moving with them."

"Goku's boys?"

Roshi closes his eyes for a moment. "Yes."

Oolong pushes the radio closer. "Maybe they'll say something else."

The announcer repeats the warning. Roshi looks toward the door, then returns to his chair.

"Can you call Bulma?" Oolong asks.

"I reached her laboratory before she left. I don't know where she's stopped the car."

"She could call us."

"When she has the chance."

Oolong pulls a second chair beside him. Turtle brings them water and leaves both glasses on the table. Neither of them reaches for one.

The telephone rings. Roshi picks up the receiver.

"Hello?"

A man asks if the line is clear. Roshi tells him he has the wrong number and gently puts the receiver down.

"Well?" Oolong asks.

"It was the wrong number."

"Couldn't you have told him to call back later?"

Roshi looks at the silent receiver. "I did."

Oolong looks out across the sea. "How much longer does Goku need?"

"I don't know."

On King Kai's planet, Goku holds a small sphere above his hands while Gregory circles him. He turns to follow the quick green figure, keeping the light away from the house and the car.

"I'm over here now," Gregory calls.

Goku turns too sharply. The sphere wobbles, and he lets it spread harmlessly into the air.

"Again," King Kai says. "Move your feet before you turn your shoulders."

Goku plants them and raises his hands. Neither he nor King Kai has seen the ships land.

## The Quarry

The quarry opens between two rocky ridges. Old tracks cross the lower ground, and a row of empty loading platforms stands beside the western wall. No workers are in sight.

Piccolo lands on the flat ground at the centre. Gohan and Goshin come down to his right. Vegeta settles on a stone shelf opposite them, and Nappa lands below him.

Krillin and Yamcha arrive moments later. They take positions beside Piccolo, leaving room for the brothers to move.

"I'm glad you came," Gohan tells them.

"So am I," Krillin says. He glances across at the Saiyans. "I was hoping we'd have a little more time first."

Yamcha looks from Nappa to the damaged cliffs. "They did that in the city?"

"Nappa fired," Goshin says. "I turned his arm, and Gohan got people out of the way."

Krillin studies Vegeta and Nappa. "You're both much stronger than Raditz, aren't you?"

"He didn't leave much of a fight for the rest of us," Nappa says.

Piccolo's cape moves in the wind. "You heard how his fight ended."

"And we've come prepared for you," Vegeta says.

Gohan looks up at Piccolo. "Have you ever heard of that planet?"

"No."

Gohan looks at him again. Piccolo is still watching Vegeta, but one hand has tightened beneath his cape.

"Do you think Vegeta knows where it is?"

Piccolo lowers his voice. "He might. I've met someone who resembles me, but neither of us has used that name."

"We can ask Kami later," Gohan says quietly.

"If there is a later."

Gohan looks back at the Saiyans. "There will be."

Piccolo keeps his eyes on Vegeta. "Don't let either of them get behind you."

Nappa points his scouter at Gohan. "Nine thousand two hundred."

He turns toward Goshin. Watching the Saiyans across the open quarry, Goshin lets some of the ki he held back in the city flow out. The display flashes **12,400**, then climbs to **13,600**.

"Thirteen thousand six hundred? He was at twelve thousand four hundred in the city!"

Vegeta watches the number rise as Goshin stands beside his brother at the centre of the quarry. "They can change their power. Raditz trusted the readings until it was too late."

"Yours is still on."

"I'm watching how fast they change. Take yours off before you let it decide how you fight."

Nappa lifts the scouter from his ear and clips it to his armour. Vegeta keeps his own in place but steps down from the shelf.

"The Dragon Balls," Vegeta says. "Where are they?"

"You're not getting them," Piccolo says.

"You don't have them here, do you?"

Gohan says nothing. Vegeta watches his face, then smiles.

"We'll have to make someone talk."

Goshin moves half a step forward, an eager grin breaking through his anger. "You can try fighting us instead."

"I intend to. But you and your brother could have a much better life than Kakarot chose. You're Saiyans. Why protect a planet full of strangers?"

"We live here," Gohan says. "Our mother and father live here too."

"Your father was sent here to conquer it."

"He told us what Raditz said," Goshin answers. "He didn't do it."

"And you?"

Goshin looks across at the Saiyans. "I want you to leave the people alone."

Nappa snorts. "They won't learn anything talking to us."

"Perhaps they'll learn when we fight," Vegeta says. He turns to Nappa. "How many Saibaiman seeds do you have left?"

Nappa reaches inside his armour and brings out a small container. He opens it and counts.

"Six. They won't last against those two."

"I know. The others are weaker. I want to see what the boys do when their friends are attacked. Plant them."

Krillin looks at Yamcha. "What's a Saibaiman?"

"We're about to find out," Yamcha says.

Nappa walks to a patch of loose soil between the Saiyans and the fighters. He presses six small seeds into the ground, then pours a little liquid over each place.

Beyond the seeds, the old tracks divide around a long trough cut into the quarry floor. One branch ends against the western wall, while the other leads back toward the loading platforms. Gohan moves to the right, giving Goshin room to turn on his left and leaving the tracks open behind them. Krillin follows Yamcha toward the clearer ground beside the trough; Piccolo stays between them and the Saiyans. Goshin watches the six dark patches while Nappa puts the container away.

Gohan watches his hands. "Are those supposed to grow here?"

"Earth has good soil," Nappa says. "They won't take long."

He returns to Vegeta, and both rise onto the shelf.

The ground shifts beneath the first dark patch. A crack runs between two of the seeds, and dirt rises in a small mound.

Goshin lifts off the ground. "Everyone, move back."

Piccolo steps to one side, away from the quarry wall. Krillin and Yamcha spread out with him; Gohan rises beside Goshin.

A green hand pushes through the soil.

## At Korin Tower

Korin stands at the edge of his platform. Yajirobe sets down his bowl when Korin's ears lift.

"What is it?"

"Two unfamiliar powers came down on Earth. I can feel the others moving toward them."

Yajirobe goes to the rail and looks into the clouds. "The Saiyans?"

"I think so."

"And Goku?"

"He hasn't returned yet."

Yajirobe takes his sword from beside the pillar and fastens it at his waist.

"I'm going down."

Korin looks up at him. "To fight?"

"To find out where they are. I haven't promised to get killed."

He swings a leg over the rail and catches the pole beneath the platform. His bowl remains beside Korin's staff as he starts down the tower.

## What Came Out

Six green creatures climb from the soil, dirt sliding from their ridged heads and narrow shoulders. Their clawed feet dig into the ground as they spread across the quarry. One crouches with its hands against the dirt; another springs onto a low rock and bares its teeth. Their heads turn together toward the defenders.

Krillin takes a step back. "There are six of them."

"You grew those?" Gohan asks.

"They're Saibaimen," Nappa says. "Watch what they can do."

The creature on the rock shrieks. Another answers it, and the others turn toward the fighters.

Goshin lowers his feet to the ground beside Gohan. "They're watching us."

"I see them."

Two more powers approach over the eastern ridge. Tien and Chiaotzu appear above the quarry and land behind Krillin and Yamcha.

"Sorry we're late," Tien says. "We felt that blast."

"There are still people getting out," Yamcha says. "Chi-Chi and the others are helping."

Chiaotzu looks at the six creatures. "Are those the Saiyans?"

"Those two are," Krillin says, pointing to Vegeta and Nappa. "These came out of the ground."

Tien moves in front of Chiaotzu. "Stay close to me."

"I will. I'm fighting too."

Tien looks at him, then nods. "Watch the ones at the sides."

Krillin eyes the closest creature as it opens its mouth. "I liked them better when they were seeds."

The Saibaiman shrieks at him. "All right, all right. I liked you better underground."

Vegeta points past the brothers. "Attack the others."

The Saibaimen spring forward.

One races past Gohan toward Yamcha. Gohan catches it before it reaches him and throws it high above the quarry. A short burst from his hand hits the creature in open air, and it disappears.

"Thanks!" Yamcha calls.

Another Saibaiman springs from the rock behind Chiaotzu. Goshin reaches it first, strikes it away from him and follows it into the air. The creature turns toward Goshin, but his next burst catches it before it can attack. The light clears, and it is gone.

"Two," Nappa says. His hand tightens around the edge of his armour.

Four Saibaimen are still moving across the quarry. Piccolo stretches an arm toward one and catches its leg before it can reach Krillin. He throws it onto a ledge.

"Keep them apart!" Piccolo calls.

Krillin ducks beneath another creature's kick and punches it away. Yamcha moves beside him while Tien and Chiaotzu face an attacker near the eastern ridge.

Vegeta raises his hand, sending a blast from the shelf toward Piccolo and Krillin.

Gohan flies across its path, cups his hands and gathers ki. Vegeta's scouter sounds: **11,600**.

"Kamehameha!"

Gohan's wave meets the blast above the quarry floor. He holds it away from Piccolo and Krillin, but Vegeta feeds more power into his attack. The meeting point moves toward the western wall.

"Goshin!" Gohan calls.

Goshin is already turning. Blue light gathers between his hands, and Vegeta's scouter flashes **15,200**.

"Kamehameha!"

Goshin's wave strikes the side of Vegeta's blast. The three streams flare where they meet, throwing the fighters' shadows across the quarry. Together the brothers force the attack upward, over the western wall and into the empty sky. Vegeta lowers his hand. Gohan and Goshin turn their waves clear of the quarry and stop them.

For an instant the glare hangs above the rim. Then the shock reaches it and breaks a section loose.

"Above you!" Goshin shouts.

A slab drops toward Piccolo and Krillin. Piccolo pushes Krillin clear and turns to jump, but Goshin flies beneath the stone and catches it with both hands. His ki brightens as he holds the slab above the quarry floor.

Smaller rocks continue to fall around it. Gohan flies under the edge and sends two of them toward an empty corner with short bursts.

"That side's clear!" he calls.

Goshin guides the slab away from Piccolo and Krillin. It is wider than the loading platform below it. As he carries it over the wall, he keeps his eyes on Vegeta and lets more of the power he had held in reserve flow, ready to move if the Saiyan attacks again. He lowers the stone beyond the old tracks, onto unoccupied ground.

The impact sends a shudder through the quarry.

Goshin lowers his arms. The light around him settles, but he keeps that greater output ready while Vegeta remains above them. Vegeta's scouter holds at **16,300**, and his smile disappears.

"Sixteen thousand three hundred?" Nappa says. "Was he hiding that much in the city?"

Vegeta keeps watching Goshin. "He wasn't using all of his power. Don't assume that's everything he has now."

"Thanks," Krillin calls.

"Are you both all right?" Goshin asks.

Piccolo nods toward the remaining Saibaimen. "We are. The others still need us."

## Four

Tien meets a Saibaiman near the eastern ridge. It darts left, then springs toward Chiaotzu. Tien turns after it, but Chiaotzu raises both hands first.

The Saibaiman stops in midair. Its arms shake as it tries to move.

"Now, Tien!"

Tien strikes it across the side. The creature flies into the slope, breaking loose a spray of stone. Chiaotzu lowers his hands as it scrambles out again.

"I can hold it for a moment," he says.

"That's enough. Stay with me."

On the quarry floor, Yamcha advances on another Saibaiman. He feints high and drives two punches into its body, then sends it skidding across the dirt with a kick.

"Krillin, on your right!"

Krillin drops as a green hand swipes over his head. He plants a palm against the ground and kicks upward, knocking the attacker away from Yamcha. It lands on all fours and circles back.

Piccolo's opponent leaps from the ledge. Piccolo catches it by the arm, pulls it past him and hits it against the wall. It drops, then springs up again with a shriek.

Vegeta watches from the stone shelf, and Nappa has stopped smiling.

"They took two and stopped your attack," Nappa says.

"I saw. They moved to protect the others."

Nappa looks toward the fighters. "The others won't be as hard to kill."

"The boys won't leave them to you. Wait for an opening."

Gohan lands beside Goshin. He looks from the four Saibaimen to the Saiyans behind them.

"They're all still coming."

"Then let's help the others," Goshin says.

The brothers fly in different directions. Gohan goes toward Krillin, who is backing away from the creature circling him. Goshin heads for the eastern ridge, where Tien and Chiaotzu are keeping another Saibaiman away from the edge.

Vegeta folds his arms. "Show me what else you've learned."

## The School Gym

Bulma's car stops outside the gym. A police officer helps the passengers out and guides them through the doors, where volunteers are spreading blankets on the floor.

"The next load is waiting by the junction," Bulma says.

"We'll keep the road open," the officer tells her. "Tell the driver of the bus not to bring it through the damaged crossing."

"I'll tell him."

Bulma turns the car around. Popo's carpet has already returned to the bus, and Chi-Chi is helping Puar guide another group down the steps.

Puar carries a child who cannot put weight on one foot. Chi-Chi walks beside them, holding the child's shoe.

"It was caught under the seat," the child says. "I pulled it out."

"You did well to get free," Chi-Chi says. "We'll find someone to look at your foot."

The father and mother wait with their daughter beside the bus. The daughter has stopped crying, but she keeps hold of both their hands.

"Is there room for us?" the mother asks.

"Bulma's coming back with the car," Chi-Chi says. "You can go together."

The child in Puar's arms points toward the sky. "Are they coming back?"

Chi-Chi follows the pointing hand. The air above the city is clear of the Saiyans.

"I don't know," Chi-Chi says. "My sons and their friends are trying to stop them. We're taking you somewhere safer, and your family can come with us."

A woman hurries down the steps and takes the child's hand. Puar lowers them carefully onto the carpet, and Popo makes room for the woman beside them.

"I'll take them," he says. "Then I'll come back."

Chi-Chi gives the child the shoe and steps away as the carpet rises. She looks west for a moment, then returns to the bus door.

## The Two Saiyans

Nappa steps off the shelf toward the quarry floor. Vegeta holds out an arm to stop him.

"They're not finished."

Nappa watches Goshin cross the quarry. "He's showing more power than he did in the city. If there's still more he's holding back, he'll be trouble for both of us."

Vegeta's eyes narrow. "I know. We still need someone alive to tell us where the Dragon Balls are."

"The others aren't as strong. I could go around the boys."

"And the moment they see you, they'll come after you. Wait until they're occupied."

Nappa stays on the shelf, his gaze moving between the twins and their friends.

Below them, a Saibaiman lunges at Krillin. Gohan catches its arm before it reaches him and throws it into the open ground beyond the tracks. It rolls, then scrambles to its feet.

"Thanks," Krillin says.

"I'll keep it away from you."

Near the ridge, the creature facing Tien and Chiaotzu springs forward. Goshin catches it in midair, turns and pins it facedown against the ground. It claws at the stone but cannot get up.

"Go help the others!" Goshin tells them.

Tien and Chiaotzu fly toward Piccolo and Yamcha. Goshin keeps hold of the creature while he looks back at Vegeta.

Light gathers in Vegeta's raised hand.

"Gohan!" Goshin calls.

Gohan lifts off toward the shelf, putting himself between Vegeta and Krillin.

Far away, Goku gathers the light again. He has not heard what is happening at the quarry.
