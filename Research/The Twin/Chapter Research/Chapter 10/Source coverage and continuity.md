## Branch mechanics and character checks

- **Why both children are there:** the doctor re-examines Goshin the next morning, retains the no-training/no-fighting restriction, and permits only a short supervised trip with him riding and a stop if movement worsens symptoms. They search a visible river bend for Mina's lost books with both parents present; a stick meets apparently firm sand before the hidden bank fails. Goku catches the first slab, and both boys use their actual flight/cloud abilities. The blocked, moving passage—not a made-up anti-flight barrier—keeps them inside.
- **Direct relationship before the danger:** both children introduce themselves, answer C-6, return to him, deal with the scorpion, ask about his sight and history, speak over the apple, draw the ruin with him, and offer him a view of the sky. Gohan follows the book and helps cook; Goshin asks about the surveyors and wipes the glass with consent. Their relationship precedes and matters to the rescue. C-6 is initially irritable and retains the right to refuse touching or extraction.
- **Physical rescue, roles and material:** the *stone beam* crosses C-6 and reaches the arch; the separate *metal measuring bar* becomes a prop. Gohan loosens a wooden wedge against advice, making the beam and roof shift. Goshin catches a falling stone. Goku senses both boys' ki, also hears them, and stops disturbing the bank when warned; Chi-Chi notices air and identifies the cliff as a possible second approach. Outside, Goku holds the shifting exit slab. Inside, Goshin identifies and supports the beam; Gohan brings and seats the bar; C-6 agrees to leave and directs the red/blue connection. Gohan clears the debris, Goshin and Gohan lift C-6, Gohan holds the slipping beam from Nimbus, and C-6 uses his remaining arms to protect Gohan. Goku raises the outside slab after the interior is ready. The brothers and C-6 do the decisive robot rescue; the parents are competent, present support, not remote substitutes.
- **Costs and consent:** C-6 expressly agrees to extraction and chooses to spend reserve power despite the leak. His right arm bends, his light dims, the remaining energy is uncertain and the passage collapses. He is not confirmed repaired or guaranteed to survive another shutdown. Bulma is only proposed as a possible person to ask; she has not yet been called about him or agreed to repair him. This is an authored branch outcome, not a claim about the episode.
- **Medical status:** the head ache has eased but persists at the morning check; the hand is re-dressed and the ankle still stings. The rescue aggravates the headache and hand. Goshin reports the worse pain to Gohan and then his parents, and the doctor rechecks him, replaces the soiled dressing and gives renewed monitoring and follow-up advice. No bean, magical cure, new diagnosis or clearance for training/fighting is given. His brief emergency flight is not a training session or proof of recovery.
- **Standing behaviors:** Goshin repeats the doctor's foot instruction, no-training/no-fighting restriction and worsening-pain instruction, Chi-Chi's ride and visibility instructions, Gohan's `Look` and `Show C-6`, and C-6's applicable quiet, switch, eye, stone, drawing, digging and lifting instructions before complying. He relays `Don't hit it!` to the outside. He waits until Goku addresses him before joining the morning book conversation; Goku later directly asks him about the beam. He takes Gohan's downhill side on the sloped road, riverbank and passage, and puts his hands behind his back when Chi-Chi looks him over on both occasions. He watches the washing, cooking, apple cutting and C-6's reheating before eating. These are manually reviewed in context as well as checked for repeated strings.
- **Both boys remain capable:** Gohan reads, proposes actions, handles Nimbus, confronts his mistake and makes multiple physical contributions. Goshin uses existing flight and strength, advocates for C-6 and has his own conversations and choices. Injury affects his immediate comfort and restrictions; the chapter does not establish him as innately less capable than Gohan, invent a numerical growth formula, confer a technique automatically, or introduce a golden transformation or god ki.
- **Objects and other threads:** the Four-Star Ball stays on Gohan's hat; C-6 hears it called a Dragon Ball but is not told what it grants. Both boys retain their tails. The Three- and Six-Star Balls remain last known at home, unchecked. Mina's borrowed book stays with Gohan; one damp parcel with two books reaches her father, a second is last seen caught at the fallen tree and he plans to look for it, while the loose book is swept away. The scouter's cell remains separate and it stays off; its removed cover is beside Bulma's notes. Krillin still carries the last whole Senzu. Yamcha does not answer and neither he nor Tien is found on the page.
- **Deferred rather than silently resolved:** Piccolo was last heading toward his training ridge after learning of the moon danger in Chapter 9; he is not shown here. The home Dragon Balls, Korin Tower summons, Roshi's case and radar, Popo/Kami, Baba's report and King Kai's unanswered question do not change merely because they are absent from this chapter. The old adult-led robot materials do not supply missing facts.
