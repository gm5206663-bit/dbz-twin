## Reading, scale and continuity audit

- Dodoria's **22,000**, Gohan's **27,600** and Goshin's **62,400** are expressly authored scouter snapshots for this scene. Dodoria’s figure is informed by a guidebook value, not a spoken source-anime scan; none is a limit, fixed conversion to damage or numerical proof of the separate body-and-ki development. Freeza hears the soldier say the comparative figures. The earlier 36,800 was seen on the broken landing scouter; Zarbon's conscious witness reports the earlier Gohan 430/Goshin 520, the lower recheck and Gohan’s later 14,600, and says Goshin’s fighting number rose; he does **not** supply 36,800 (`O278`, `O280`, `O281`).
- Goshin carries his real prior first-ape and later development into the fight. His body strengthens even as he holds his then-chosen output; his ki deepens with it, he forces Dodoria's arms apart and his stronger body/ki remain afterward. He guards his tail and has an actual advantage over Dodoria. The scene imposes no injury requirement, fixed rate, compulsory weakness, new form, Kaiō-ken use, maximum or fixed relation to Freeza's full capacity (`O280`, `O283`). His whole hybrid bloodline and developing Talent remain broader than this one expression.
- Gohan independently intercepts Dodoria, escorts and reassures Dende/Cargo, discovers and helps cover the ball, defeats a later soldier's attack and redirects Freeza's first shot. He remains capable and makes his own choices. Muri takes risks and directs residents; Piccolo protects and asks about his own origins; Bulma protects the children and reasons about the ship. Source casualties are not guaranteed by the absence of a canonical rescue route (`O278`, `O279`, `O283`).
- **Knowledge paths:** the two children and Gohan cross an exposed gap that Freeza can see from his chair, but he does not see Bulma. He targets that bank later without mysteriously identifying her. The last soldier audibly reports Zarbon's discovery; Goshin and Bulma can hear it. Gohan's knowledge of the ball comes from sight, touch and Dende; Piccolo hears Muri's later statement, not the private conversation in the house. Piccolo reports Kami's actual letter and wait, not a claimed memory of the journey. King Kai's return to Goku contains only what Piccolo told him, not the later village encounter (`O279`, `O281`–`O283`).
- **Exact ending:** Freeza has left his chair and stands on the path facing Gohan and Goshin; his attacks were deliberately limited and his full capacity is not established. Muri and the older villager are moved back along the house under Piccolo's direction; other residents' complete safety is unknown. Bulma, Dende and Cargo shelter behind the bank rock, and the covered ball remains in the house. Dodoria is alive on one knee with damaged armour. One chair-side scouter functions; at least the restrained soldier is active and the soldier Piccolo knocked down was last held at the house. Zarbon and one soldier watch the intact, closed ship; the other attends the two landing scouts. Goku watches Vegeta alone after Krillin departs to find Chi-Chi; Tien and Chiaotzu watch Nappa. No Krillin arrival, Chi-Chi answer, Brief result, second ship or departure is shown. The boys' tails remain and Gohan avoids the unchecked sky; Vegeta's tail remains (`O278`–`O283`).
- Raiti and Zākuro remain at the earlier wreck with limited supplies and a locally tested transmitter. The brothers' promise to seek help is unfulfilled; survival and rescue are not guaranteed. The refugee children's later safety, all long-term Saiyan custody and the Saiyan pod's usability remain open.
