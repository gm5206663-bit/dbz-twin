## What was actually checked

The complete retained Japanese caption files for original-Z episodes **3–11** were reread, including their separately identifiable ending, preview and unrelated post-ending material. Their redistributor, broadcast origin and exact cut remain unverified (`W-30`). They record dialogue and labelled sounds, not every silent action, voice delivery or visual frame. The short official Toei episode synopses for [3](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/3/), [4](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/4/), [5](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/5/), [6](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/6/), [7](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/7/), [8](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/8/), [9](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/9/), [10](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/10/) and [11](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/11/) and the existing secondary coverage informed the comparison. The first chunks of Daizenshuu EX's episode 6–8 written guides were also retrieved; its episode-8 page begins with a mismatched original-*Dragon Ball* heading, so that opening is not used as episode-8 identification. The attempted episode-9 guide URL returned 404. Earlier inventories record selected stills separately; no new full motion or voice performance was examined.

These are rereads of episodes already counted in the project's **125 distinct full spoken-caption reads**, not nine newly distinct episodes. No complete episode video or audio was examined. Dialogues, thoughts, gestures, twin actions, rescue choreography and divergences introduced in the prose are authored unless a narrower source claim is specifically documented. The anime baseline is not a mandate to reproduce casualties or isolation after the branch has changed.

## Living prose and consequential handoffs

| Chapter | Revision and preserved branch |
|---|---|
| [3 — Eight Hours](../chapters/Chapter_03_Eight_Hours.md) | External camera throughout. Chi-Chi has pride, care and agency; the school paper, Ox-King visit and first unanswered call belong to one continuing day. The eight hours describe schooling, not an exact crisis clock. |
| [4 — The Rescue](../chapters/Chapter_04_The_Rescue.md) | The island fight earns Goshin's knockout before Raditz carries him; Gohan pulls him under the truck, both enter the pod, and Goshin wakes and escapes with Gohan. Turtle witnesses the departure from the shallows, supporting his limited later account. Avoid canon-character interiority in this chapter. |
| [5 — Piccolo's New Attack](../chapters/Chapter_05_Piccolos_New_Attack.md) | Goku's fear and nonlethal intent and the children's independent choices matter. The 1,450 wave scan is **authored**, a momentary attack reading, not a ceiling. Gohan deliberately targets the scouter; Raditz cuts his own tail. Avoid canon-character interiority in this chapter. |
| [6 — The Brothers' Attack](../chapters/Chapter_06_The_Brothers_Attack.md) | Both boys contribute; living Goku asks Piccolo to wait, but Piccolo kills the mortally wounded Raditz. Raditz taunts and warns them; he does **not** attempt a final attack after this conversation. `O091` and [episode-5 coverage](SCENE_COVERAGE_005.md) now match the selected page; the old `O091` statement is kept as historical metadata. |
| [7 — The Sky Between Them](../chapters/Chapter_07_The_Sky_Between_Them.md) | Piccolo intercepts the aircraft and tries to take the conscious boys; they resist and escape into the trees. Chi-Chi's later separate call reaches Turtle, then she and Ox-King leave. Krillin obtains three beans. Goku is alive; no source death or forced six-month abandonment is imported. |
| [8 — The Dinosaur by the River](../chapters/Chapter_08_The_Dinosaur_by_the_River.md) | Gohan's concern about the damaged cliff and both boys' dinosaur rescue lead to family reunion. Goku and Gohan each eat a whole Senzu. Goshin chooses medical care and asks that the last bean be saved; Krillin holds it. This particular choice does not make food-watching a character rule. |
| [9 — The Bridge Under the Moon](../chapters/Chapter_09_The_Bridge_Under_the_Moon.md) | A doctor examines Goshin and gives warning signs. Both boys help save Mina. A full moon rises, but neither boy looks at it or transforms; Piccolo hears the warning. Kami's inquiry about living Goku is unanswered. A repeated bandage-removal sentence was corrected. |
| [10 — The Robot Under the Sand](../chapters/Chapter_10_The_Robot_Under_the_Sand.md) | The brothers meet C-6 under the bank; Gohan's unauthorized movement destabilizes the roof. C-6 chooses to use his reserve and agrees to rescue; Goku and Chi-Chi help from outside. He is awake with an injured arm and uncertain energy, not automatically repaired or shut down. |
| [11 — The Branch in the Rain](../chapters/Chapter_11_The_Branch_in_the_Rain.md) | Gohan, Goshin and Chi-Chi help the wounded dinosaur; its recovery is not guaranteed. Yamcha learns the changed situation and leaves with Krillin. C-6 consents only to the proposed outside inspection, and his condition remains unresolved. |
| [12 — The Planet Between](../chapters/Chapter_12_The_Planet_Between.md) | The family goes home, checks the two home Dragon Balls and trains together. Arlia's independent source-baseline destruction remains; Atla and Lemuria's reunion and the Saiyans' predatory motives are not converted into a family-side event. The boys plan to take Mina's book to C-6. |
