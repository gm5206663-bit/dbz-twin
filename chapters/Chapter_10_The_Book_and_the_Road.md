## COLD OPEN

The doctor sets her bag on the mountain-house table. Goshin sits beside it, with Chi-Chi at his shoulder. When she looks him over, he puts his hands behind his back. Gohan has brought the machine book to the next chair, but he leaves it closed.

"Hold still, please," the doctor says.

"Hold still, please."

Goshin keeps his head level while she checks his eyes. He brings his injured hand forward when the doctor is ready to examine it. Then she unwraps the dressing. The skin beneath it is still red.

"Does it hurt as much as it did yesterday?"

"Not as much. It hurts when I bend it."

"Then we'll put a clean dressing on."

Goku waits by the door with the Ox-King. On the shelf behind them, Gohan's hat rests beside a folded blanket. The Dragon Ball in the hat catches the morning light.

"Can we go to the tower?" Gohan asks.

The doctor lays out fresh gauze. "That depends on how your brother is doing and how you plan to get there. I want to hear both."

**THE BOOK AND THE ROAD**

**NARRATOR:** Goku and his sons survived their battle with Raditz, but two more Saiyans are coming. Raditz warned they would arrive in about a year. With Goshin still recovering, the family must decide how to reach the tower—and what to do when they get there.

## 1 · THE DOCTOR'S ANSWER

The doctor finishes the dressing and sets the used gauze aside.

"Please stand," she says to Goshin.

"Please stand."

He rises from the chair and waits while she checks his balance. Gohan moves the machine book out of the way.

The doctor watches Goshin take one careful step on the level floor.

"Has your head hurt this morning?" she asks.

"A little when I woke up. Not now."

"Any spinning? Any sickness?"

"No."

Chi-Chi puts a hand on the back of his chair. "Yesterday you said a gentle journey might be possible after another look."

"A gentle ride as a passenger," the doctor says. "He must not make the journey under his own power. If the movement makes his head hurt or brings back dizziness or sickness, stop and bring him home for another examination."

Goku points through the window. "The boys rode Nimbus when we came home. It didn't bounce. I can carry Chi-Chi beside them."

"We can stop," Chi-Chi says. "I'll watch him the whole way."

The doctor turns to Goshin. "Travel only as a passenger."

"Travel only as a passenger."

"Tell me if your head hurts or spins."

"Tell me if your head hurts or spins."

"And no running, flying on your own, heavy lifting, or training until I see you again."

"And no running, flying on your own, heavy lifting, or training until I see you again."

Goshin looks at his hand. "Can I read?"

"For a while. Stop if reading makes your head hurt. Rest when you need it."

"Stop if reading makes your head hurt. Rest when you need it."

The doctor closes her bag. "I want to see him again. A better morning is not the same as a finished recovery."

Chi-Chi offers the doctor a chair while she writes down the warning signs. "We'll come back before it gets late."

The Ox-King picks up the folded blanket. "I'll take my truck."

"It's a long way by road," Goku says. "You'd be by yourself."

The engine of an aircar sounds above the yard. Yajirobe lowers the vehicle beside the truck and climbs out, one hand over his stomach.

Dust settles around the car's runners while the doctor carries her note to the table.

"I went all the way back to Korin," he calls. "Then I remembered your father mentioned lunch."

Chi-Chi steps onto the porch. "I said perhaps. You've arrived before I've started it."

"Good timing. I hate cold food."

The Ox-King gestures at the car. "Is that second seat still free?"

Yajirobe looks from him to the seat. "You fit in it yesterday."

"I would like to go with my family."

"All right. But if she packs a meal, mine goes in first."

## 2 · FOOD FOR THE ROAD

Chi-Chi sets uncooked rice, whole vegetables, a whole uncooked fish and an uncut lemon on the table. Goshin takes his chair where he can see her hands.

She rinses the rice, then washes and cuts the vegetables while he watches. She cleans the fish and lays it in a pan. The lemon goes into the travel bag whole.

The Ox-King brings water from the pitcher and pours it into the pot.

Gohan opens the machine book beside his brother. The stove stands beside the table, and Goshin watches the pot and pan while Gohan reads aloud. On one page, a broad-shouldered robot holds a tray. On the opposite page, a smaller drawing shows a row of switches across its chest.

"That's the picture I saw," Gohan says. "I couldn't remember which book it was in."

"What does it do?" Goshin asks.

Gohan follows a line of print with his finger. "It says some old capsule robots could work at digging sites. Some cooked, too."

"Both?"

"Maybe not at the same time."

Yajirobe leans over the page. "A cook who can dig would be useful. You could bury the plates when you're finished."

Chi-Chi turns the fish in the pan. "You can wash your plate."

The rice finishes cooking. Chi-Chi breaks the fish into portions, checks it for bones, and mixes vegetables into the rice. Goshin watches her fill the parcels and wrap them. She keeps two portions on plates for the boys, puts the family parcels in her travel bag, and sets Yajirobe's parcel beside the door.

Goshin takes his plate after the food cools. Gohan eats beside him, balancing the open book against his knee. The Ox-King carries a separate portion to the doctor, who has stayed to write down the return instructions.

"What's that building?" Goshin asks, pointing with his good hand.

"A temple," Gohan says. "It says Nemurian. There's another picture of a machine they found near one."

Goshin looks at the drawing. "The opening is behind its head."

"I thought it was in front."

They turn the page and find a side view. Gohan traces the roof, then the robot's head. "You're right. I was looking at it upside down."

The doctor hands Chi-Chi a note and leaves in her car. Chi-Chi puts the note in her pocket. Her gaze moves over Goshin, and he places his hands behind his back while she checks his face and bandage.

"How is your head after reading?"

"Still all right."

"We'll take the book, then. If you feel worse, it goes away for a while."

Gohan closes it carefully. The Ox-King puts the dishes in the sink, including Yajirobe's empty plate. Yajirobe picks up his packed meal before Chi-Chi can point to them.

## 3 · THE CLIMB

Chi-Chi shuts the cupboard containing the two Dragon Balls. She tests the latch and joins Goku on the porch.

"We're leaving them here?" she asks.

"Until we can ask Kami what to do with them. We don't know a safer place yet."

"We don't know if Piccolo will look here either."

Goku looks along the road. "No. We stay together, and I watch for him. I can't promise he won't find us."

"If he does, the boys stay with me."

"I'll be beside you."

Gohan takes his hat from the shelf and puts it on before leaving.

"Both of you, stay together on the path," Chi-Chi says.

"Both of you, stay together on the path," Goshin repeats.

On the sloping path to the yard, Goshin walks on Gohan's downhill side. Chi-Chi stays close enough to reach them both. Gohan carries the book under one arm and the blanket under the other.

The brothers remain side by side as they reach the level ground. The Ox-King folds himself into Yajirobe's passenger seat. Yajirobe sets his parcel safely between them.

Nimbus descends beside the porch. Gohan climbs onto the cloud and steadies the blanket while Chi-Chi helps Goshin sit next to him. Their tails curl against their waists.

"Tell me if the movement bothers your head," Chi-Chi says.

"Tell me if the movement bothers your head."

Goku lifts Chi-Chi, and the cloud rises beside them. Yajirobe's aircar follows across the valley.

At the first broad ridge, they stop. Goku lands with Chi-Chi, and Nimbus settles beside them. Yajirobe brings the car down without disturbing his parcel.

Chi-Chi turns to Goshin. He puts his hands behind his back while she looks him over.

"Any worse?"

"No. Can I finish the page at the tower?"

"If your head stays comfortable."

Gohan keeps the book closed for the rest of the ride. The tower rises ahead, its narrow shaft disappearing into a ring of clouds.

The aircar keeps alongside Nimbus as both vehicles approach the open floor above.

At the top, Korin waits by the railing. The cloud sets the brothers down on the level stone. Goku lands beside them with Chi-Chi, and Yajirobe brings the aircar in behind the group.

"You took longer than I expected," Korin says.

"I stopped for lunch," Yajirobe answers. "I'm bringing some back, too."

Korin examines the parcel. "I see you considered my needs."

"I considered mine. The extra is for emergencies."

Goshin watches Korin set the parcel aside. "May I ask about the beans?"

"Go ahead."

"Could I watch you make them, from the beginning?"

Korin's tail moves once across the floor. "I can show you what I do when I start the next batch. That won't be today."

"How long will it take?"

"I can't give you a day for it. The one Roshi kept was already grown before you arrived."

Goshin nods. His bandaged hand rests against his sleeve. "Then I still won't eat that one."

"That's your decision," Korin says. "I won't tell you that watching part of the work is the same as watching all of it."

A carpet descends from above the cloud line. Popo stands on it with his hands folded. He bows to the family.

"Kami is ready to receive you," he says. "The carpet will be gentle."

Goshin puts his hands behind his back while Chi-Chi checks the blanket around him before they board. The Ox-King steps onto the carpet beside Yajirobe, leaving the aircar at the tower. Popo takes them up through the clouds.

## 4 · QUESTIONS FOR KAMI

Kami meets them on the broad white platform. Popo brings chairs from inside, and Chi-Chi settles Goshin near her. Gohan places the machine book across both boys' knees.

"I wanted to ask you about the Saiyans," Goku says. "Raditz said two more were coming."

"And I cannot tell you the day they will arrive," Kami answers. "We have the warning he gave us. We should use the time without assuming we have every day he promised."

Goku glances at Chi-Chi. "Piccolo tried to take Gohan. He went after Goshin too. We need a way to keep the boys safe while we prepare."

"I know what he attempted at the crater."

"Can you stop him from coming here?" Chi-Chi asks.

Kami's hands close around his staff. "I cannot promise that. Popo and I will help you, but you must understand the danger. If Piccolo dies, I die as well. The Dragon Balls would vanish."

Kami rests the end of his staff against the stone.

"I have a feeling my own life may last about a year. I don't know why, and I may be wrong. If I die before the balls are gathered, they cannot grant another wish."

"And if you live long enough for a wish?" Goku asks.

"If my feeling is right, they might be used once more. It is not a certainty."

The Ox-King looks toward the edge of the platform. "Then we don't put the boys somewhere and assume no one can get to them."

"No," Chi-Chi says. "And nobody makes a plan that leaves one of them behind."

Gohan presses his finger against the closed cover of the book. "We can help decide what we do."

"Yes," Kami says. "You can."

Goku tells him what Raditz said about a full moon and a tailed Saiyan. Kami listens without interrupting. Gohan's hand moves to the tail around his waist, while Goshin adjusts his blanket.

"We have kept the windows covered at night," Chi-Chi says. "We don't know when it will be full."

"Keep using that precaution," Kami says.

"Keep using that precaution," Goshin repeats.

"I won't tell you it answers every question," Kami adds.

Goku asks about King Yemma and King Kai. Kami repeats the answer he gave Popo: Yemma had considered the request, but had not granted passage to a living Goku. Baba has gone to try to carry another question to Grandpa Gohan; there is no reply yet.

"Then I can't leave for that road now," Goku says.

"And we have no plan for the boys if you do," Chi-Chi adds.

"We'll make one together before I agree to anything."

Gohan opens the machine book to the temple picture. "Do people still study this place? I'd like to know what the robots did there."

"There is a survey office in that region," Popo says. "It gives a public radio bulletin. We can listen."

Popo places a radio on a low table and turns the dial. Its antenna extends above the open book. After a burst of static, a recorded bulletin begins.

A survey crew has reported a fresh opening near an old desert excavation. Loose ground has forced the crew out, and a piece of old machinery can be seen in the damaged passage. Workers are waiting for equipment to make the site secure.

Gohan lifts his head. "Did they say a temple?"

The bulletin repeats the name of the site.

"Nemurian," he says. "That's the place in the book."

## 5 · THE MESSAGE FROM THE DESERT

Popo turns the radio down. The broadcast has given a location but has not said that anyone is trapped.

"It might just be a broken machine," Goku says.

"It might," Gohan answers. "If it's one of the robots in this book, it could be able to talk."

Goshin turns to Gohan and holds the page flat with his good hand. "And the ceiling might come down if somebody digs in the wrong place. The picture says the old tunnel goes behind the machine."

Yajirobe pulls his parcel closer. "The workers have equipment coming. Why don't we let them use it?"

"We should ask them what they need," Gohan says. "Not just go in."

Chi-Chi looks from the book to Goku. "If you go, we're apart. We came here because Piccolo is still out there."

Goku nods. "That's true. We can leave the site to the workers."

Gohan keeps his finger on the drawing. "Can we talk to them first? If nobody needs help, we come home."

Kami looks across the platform at the open sky. "You can remain here with Popo and me while Goku asks. It is not a guarantee, but you would not be alone."

"I stay with the boys," Chi-Chi says.

"So do I," the Ox-King adds.

Yajirobe tears a corner off his parcel. "I'm not digging. I can stay here."

Chi-Chi turns to Goshin. He puts his hands behind his back while she checks his face and the edge of his dressing. "How is your head now?"

"All right. I'd like to listen."

"Then we'll listen. You won't go into a damaged tunnel."

"You won't go into a damaged tunnel," Goshin repeats.

He looks back at the book. "I can help find the right page."

Popo brings a second radio from inside. He gives the portable set to Goku and keeps the other on the table. The sets connect after a crackle; Goku answers from the far side of the platform, and Popo hears him clearly. Popo warns that the signal may weaken at the site.

"If you can't reach us?" Chi-Chi asks.

"I come back," Goku says. "I won't keep you waiting without an answer."

Gohan finds the survey location on the printed map in his book. Popo checks it against the broadcast. Goku points toward the desert, then looks at his family once more before rising from the platform.

"We'll be here," Chi-Chi says.

Goku heads across the clouds. His figure passes the edge of the platform and disappears beyond the cloud bank.

Gohan turns the book back to the robot's page, and Goshin rests the weight of it on the table instead of his bandaged hand.

## 6 · UNDER THE STONE

The survey workers have marked the broken ground with rope. A canvas shade shelters their equipment, and a second rope blocks the mouth of the excavation. Wind carries sand across the nearest marker.

A shallow trench lies open beside the rope, exposing a line of older stonework.

Goku lands outside the rope. A woman in a dust-covered hat steps toward him.

"This area's closed," she says. "The ground shifted."

"I heard your report. Is anyone inside?"

"Everyone on our crew is out. We found a metal object below the old roof, but we can't tell what it is until the supports arrive."

"Would it help if I looked? I can hold up a lot of weight."

The woman studies him. Goku moves to a fallen block beside the rope and raises one edge, slowly, without shifting the smaller stones around it. He sets it back where it was.

The woman looks at the block. "That would help. It won't tell us which stones are holding the roof, though."

"I'll follow your directions."

She brings him to a diagram. Two workers explain the old entrance and the new opening. The exposed metal is farther inside, where a support has fallen across the passage. One worker gives Goku a small lamp and points out the line they have already tested.

On the radio, Gohan hears the plan from Goku. "Please tell them about the tunnel behind the robot, if there is one."

Goku relays it. The woman compares the book's description with her diagram. "There was a passage there on the old survey. We don't know if it's open now."

Goku turns the radio toward himself. "Goshin, what do you see in the picture?"

Goshin's voice comes over the set. "The picture in our book is a side view. The opening is behind the head, not under the feet."

The woman marks the possible route with a pencil. "That gives us somewhere to look."

Goku lowers himself through the opening with the lamp. The workers remain outside the unstable passage and watch from the entrance. He follows the tested line to a broad metal head projecting from the sand.

The lamp illuminates a cracked lens, a strip of metal and the slab above them.

"There's a round plate at the front," he says. "Two rings on it. And a switch."

Pages move across the radio. Gohan stops at a picture. "The SSS type has two rings. Is there a number?"

"I can't see one. There's sand over the side."

The metal is wedged beneath a slanting slab. Goku does not pull it. He brushes loose sand away from the plate, exposing an old switch and a cracked lens.

"Can it be turned on?" Gohan asks.

The woman calls from the entrance. "If that switch is intact, try it gently. Don't move the body."

Goku touches the switch. A light flickers behind the cracked lens, then fades. A rough voice comes from the metal head.

"Who's there?"

Goku lowers his own voice. "My name is Goku. We're looking at the old excavation."

"Don't shout. The sand is balanced against the roof."

On the platform, Goshin turns to Chi-Chi. "Don't shout."

"All right. Are you hurt?" Goku asks C-6.

"My body is trapped. My visual sensor is damaged. Turn me off again. I'm saving energy."

Gohan leans close to the radio on the platform. "I'm Gohan. Are you an SSS-type capsule robot?"

The voice crackles through the set. "Number C-6. Who is asking?"

"I read about your type in a book. We didn't know anyone was in there."

"Now you know. Please turn me off."

Goku's hand stays beside the switch. "I will. May I ask one question first? If you don't want to answer, I'll switch you off now."

There is a pause under the stone. Sand ticks against the lamp.

"One question," C-6 says.

"Is there a safe way out for you?"

"Behind my head there was an exit. It was open when the roof fell about eighty years ago. I don't know whether it is open now."

The lamp throws a narrow stripe across the buried metal. C-6 continues without being asked another question.

"We were excavating the temple. The ground shook, and the ruins fell around me. I intended to save enough power and get out myself, but digging could bring the roof down. Can your workers hold the stone?"

"Eighty years?" Yajirobe says into the radio. "That's a long time to miss lunch."

Goku describes the slab above C-6's body. The survey woman listens at the entrance. "We might be able to support it," she says. "We need to know where his body ends."

Gohan turns another page. "There's a diagram here. C-6, would you like us to try to get you out?"

"You might make the roof fall."

"Then we won't pull you until you agree and the workers have a plan."

The lens flickers again. "Can they hold the slab while I move?"

Goku tells him about the workers and the support equipment. C-6 asks how much room lies above his head. Goku measures it against his hand and gives the answer.

"I would like to see the surface," C-6 says. "If they can hold that stone, I will try."

## 7 · THE OTHER SIDE OF THE PAGE

The survey woman and her crew fit a brace across the entry. Goku takes its weight while they set its feet on stable ground. C-6 tells them which part of the buried frame he can feel pressing against him.

On the platform, Gohan follows the diagram with a pencil. Goshin points to a second drawing on the facing page.

"That one is the same model on its back," he says. "The first picture is standing up."

Gohan turns the book sideways. "The shoulder joint faces the entrance now. Father, the plate might be on the left of the head, not the right."

Goku finds it under a crust of sand. The plate has a narrow handle, but a strip of stone pins it shut.

"I can lift that piece," he says.

"Only after the brace is set," the survey woman answers.

C-6 makes a low sound as the crew tightens the support. "My sensor has been cracked since the cave-in. Can you see whether the lens is covered?"

"There's sand on it," Goku says. "May I clear it?"

"A little. Do not press the glass."

Goku uses the edge of his sleeve. More of the lens appears, but the crack remains. C-6 reports a patch of light and the shape of Goku's hand, no more.

"It's not fixed," Gohan says.

"I know," C-6 replies. "It is still more than I had."

A tremor passes through the ground. At the entrance, a worker catches the diagram before it blows away. Inside, sand slips down the slab and strikes Goku's shoulder.

"That wasn't the brace," the woman says. "There was another movement under the site."

A second tremor cracks the old roof. The workers retreat beyond the rope.

One of the markers falls flat as sand spreads across the ground outside. Goku puts both hands under the slanting slab and holds it where it is.

"I can hold this," he says. "The sand above it is moving."

He shifts the weight onto his left palm and braces his feet against the floor. His right hand is free beside the robot's head.

C-6's voice sharpens. "The old balance is gone. Leave before the passage closes."

"What about you?" Gohan asks.

"My legs cannot move under that stone. Save yourselves."

Goku plants his feet. "If I lift the slab, can you move clear?"

"Not with the power I have."

The radio hisses. Gohan waits for C-6 to answer again, then looks at the diagram. "Is there another way to use what power you have left?"

"There is an emergency connection. The red wire and the blue wire in my head. But it will use my remaining energy gas."

"Will it hurt you?"

"I may not be able to wake again."

Goku keeps the slab above C-6's shoulder. A narrow gap opens beneath it, but the buried frame does not move.

"I can try to carry you," Goku says. "I won't leave you here if you want to come."

C-6's lens turns toward the light. "I want to come. Open the plate."

Goku lifts the strip of stone away. He hooks the handle with one finger and opens the plate. Several wires lie behind it.

"Red to blue," C-6 says. "The ends are loose beside the switch."

Goku joins them. The light behind the cracked lens flares. C-6's shoulder shifts; sand runs into the space it leaves.

"Full power," C-6 says.

Goku raises the slab with his left hand, freeing the shoulder. C-6's buried arm moves toward the opening, pushing against the sand. Goku catches the metal frame with his right hand and draws it clear.

He steps backward toward the entrance, then releases the slab behind them. The roof breaks above them. Goku pulls C-6 through the entrance, holding the robot against his chest. The crew runs beyond the marked ground as the slab falls into the passage. A column of sand rises after them.

When it settles, the entrance is blocked.

The survey woman counts her workers again before approaching the canvas shade.

## 8 · THE SURFACE

Goku sets C-6 on a folded canvas beneath the shade. The survey woman checks that every worker is outside. One of them retrieves the radio Goku dropped beside the rope.

"Father?" Gohan's voice comes through the static. "Are you there?"

"I'm here. We're both outside."

C-6 turns his cracked lens toward the light. A thin line shows across it, then disappears.

"This is the surface," he says. "I remember the sun being brighter."

"Your sensor is still damaged," Goku says. "The sky's clear."

"My energy gas is almost gone."

Gohan grips the edge of the table. "Can someone fill it again?"

"I don't know what would be safe," C-6 says. "Please don't connect anything else without asking me."

On the platform, Goshin turns toward the radio. "Please don't connect anything else without asking me."

The survey woman crouches beside him. "We have a machinery specialist in the city. I can send for her."

"How long?" Goku asks.

"She can get here tomorrow, if the road stays open."

The radio crackles. Popo's voice reaches the site. "He could rest here while we ask Bulma whether she knows the model. We cannot promise a repair."

"Would you like that, C-6?" Gohan asks.

C-6's lens moves toward the radio. "Would there be digging?"

"No digging," Gohan says.

"And nobody will turn you on to ask for lunch," Yajirobe adds.

"You asked about lunch," the Ox-King says.

"I'm making sure he knows the rules."

C-6 makes a sound that catches halfway through a word. "I would like somewhere quiet."

The survey woman gives Goku her contact details and records where C-6 will be. She asks if he will speak to the machinery specialist when she calls. C-6 agrees. Goku lifts the canvas with C-6 on it and takes the radio in his free hand.

At the platform, Chi-Chi moves a chair away from the doorway so Goku can set C-6 down.

A little sand falls from the canvas onto the white stone. Gohan kneels beside the canvas without touching the cracked lens. Goshin remains seated next to his mother, the book open in his lap.

"You found the right type," C-6 says to Gohan.

"The book helped. You told us where to find you."

Goshin turns the book to the sideways diagram. "I thought this was the front at first. The other picture showed us."

"I am accustomed to standing," C-6 says. "Being on my back for eighty years made the instructions difficult."

Yajirobe lowers himself onto another chair. "You should have said something sooner."

"I was switched off."

For a moment the platform is quiet. Then Gohan laughs, and even Yajirobe looks down at his parcel.

## 9 · A TASTE OF HOME

Popo offers the family a place inside to rest and warm their food.

"May we carry you indoors?" he asks C-6. "It is quieter there."

"Yes, please."

Goku and Popo lift the canvas together and carry C-6 into the room. Chi-Chi and the brothers follow across the level floor.

Chi-Chi brings out the parcels she prepared at home. Goshin sits where he can see them opened and watches while she puts their contents into a pan and warms them. Gohan sets the book within C-6's narrow patch of light.

"You said your type could cook," he tells the robot.

"There is an oven in my head. It would need power."

"Could you tell us how to make something taste like Mother's food?"

Chi-Chi turns from the pan. "It is Mother's food."

"I know. I meant, can he tell us how he would do it?"

C-6 considers the pan. "I can suggest a seasoning. I cannot tell what is already in it from here."

Chi-Chi names the ingredients and takes the whole lemon from her travel bag. The cracked sensor catches its outline. C-6 suggests a little of its juice.

Goshin watches while Chi-Chi washes the lemon, cuts it and squeezes juice into a clean cup.

"How little?" Chi-Chi asks.

"One spoon."

"That's a large spoon," Gohan says.

"Then half a spoon," C-6 answers.

Chi-Chi tips the measured juice into a separate dish. Goshin watches her stir it through the rice and vegetables. She serves a little to each brother, keeping the rest of their meal as she originally made it.

Gohan tastes his portion. His mouth closes, and he takes a long drink from his cup.

"It doesn't taste like Mother makes it," he says.

Goshin tries his own watched portion and turns to Gohan. "It tastes like the lemon."

C-6's lens dims. "I did not know how sour it was."

"Neither did I," Chi-Chi says. She tastes a grain and reaches for the water. "We can use less next time."

Yajirobe finishes the last of his parcel. "Or none. None would be good."

Gohan draws the dish back toward himself. "I can still eat it."

C-6's voice has grown softer. "I would like to switch off now."

"Will you be able to wake up?" Gohan asks.

"I don't know. It gives me the best chance to save what remains."

Gohan looks at the switch. He leaves his hand on the table. "All right."

"The switch is on my left," C-6 says. "Thank you for asking."

Goku reaches toward it, then waits. C-6 gives a small movement of his lens. Goku turns the switch off.

Popo covers C-6 with a light cloth, leaving the switch and lens free. He sets the survey woman's contact details beside the canvas. The book stays open nearby, with a pencil marking the diagram.

Outside, the family gathers for the return journey. Chi-Chi looks Goshin over, and he puts his hands behind his back. His dressing is still in place.
