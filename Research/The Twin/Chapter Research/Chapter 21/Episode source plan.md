## Dated continuity correction after the pre-draft plan

The Baba row above followed an older project note that attributed a promise to her in Chapter Nine. Before finalizing the continuation, the selected Chapter Nine and its protected pre-correction copy were checked: neither shows that request or promise. The cause of the older conflicting note is unknown. Chapter Eight does show Goku asking Roshi whether he could have harmed Grandpa, with no answer known. Chapter 21 therefore has Roshi make a new, bounded request to Baba; it does not claim she had already tried or that Goku asked her on page. `O115` now reflects the actually selected Chapter Nine scene and `O116` is a historical unsupported note. Neither earlier chapter has been changed.
