# GOSHIN — STATUS PANEL

**Owns: Goshin’s complete working status at the current Chapter 19 review checkpoint.**

**Checkpoint:** home with Chi-Chi, Gohan and Ox-King after teaching, the machine incident and a bounded Piccolo exchange. Goku has reached the little planet, but no news has reached Earth on-page. Chapter 20 remains an unaccepted later draft on hold; its three-week training span is not the current Chapter 19 handoff. Chapters 6–20 are review drafts, not accepted chapters.

## At a glance

| | Current picture |
|---|---|
| **Age / nature** | Four; Gohan’s twin, a half-Saiyan child with his tail. Mortal, in his ordinary body. |
| **Talent** | Innate, inseparable, always active Adaptation Talent. It supports the development of his whole being—not just copying—and works with his Saiyan body and ki rather than replacing them. |
| **Power** | Stronger than the crater checkpoint by Goku’s observation in Chapter 8. His **current exact reading and maximum have not been measured**; the old scans below are real milestones, not a present-day cap. |
| **Fighting kit** | Flight, ki blasts and deflection, Kamehameha, non-contact ki manipulation, physical combat and rapid learning. He can combine abilities rather than use only one at a time. |
| **Growth** | Faster observed recovery; a narrower shot; improved control during a beam contest; simultaneous control of four stones while using his Kamehameha. Chapter 18 includes a *mental* wall-piece-and-wave application; Chapter 19 separately shows four falling rocks and a wave in physical space. Neither establishes a numerical maximum. |
| **Condition** | Chi-Chi checks him after the bounded spar and he says nothing hurts. The earlier room experience was mental; the doctor had cleared normal movement with instructions to report concerning symptoms. Goshin did **not** eat the unwatched Senzu. |
| **Forms** | No transformation has occurred. His first eventual Great Ape is to be **aware and controlled from the start**. Golden forms and divine progression belong to a later era. |

## Growth that must remain visible

1. **Chapter 8 — strength:** Goku says Goshin’s ki feels stronger than at the crater. This is an actual qualitative observation, not merely a possibility of future growth.
2. **Chapter 9 — precision and recovery:** Goku notices the rescue shot is narrower than the earlier crater wave; the doctor observes how quickly the burn has closed.
3. **Chapter 13 — combat integration:** Goshin uses flight, strikes and ki manipulation in a real ring match. During the wave contest he narrows his spreading Kamehameha, adjusts its pressure and redirects it upward when the family is endangered. He wins the ring-out; both fighters were directing and limiting their waves for safety.
4. **Chapter 15 — parallel control:** he keeps **four stones moving**, fires a Kamehameha at one and catches its fragments while the others stay aloft. One stone drifts and the others wobble, so the scene shows both achievement and room for refinement.
5. **Chapters 16–17 — applied control and strength:** he keeps the branch raised while lifting Gohan from the water, adjusts its position against the wind, uses ki during the ball exercise and bends both cuff locks at Pigero’s request. These are meaningful feats and applications; they are not automatically a new named power.
6. **Chapter 18 — practice and a distinct mental experience:** at the Lookout he moves four stones on different paths. In the room experience, he holds four larger wall pieces while firing the retained Kamehameha and protects Chiaotzu. That scene alone is not an independent physical measurement.
7. **Chapter 19 — teaching and physical application:** he teaches Gohan and holds four falling rocks while firing the retained wave, then lowers them away from the family. The event is physical in this draft, not a numeric reading or proof of a new form.
8. **Chapter 20 — later draft on hold:** he flies and controls two stones in a bounded Piccolo round and later practises four-pebble control. These are conditional later-draft applications of retained skills, not a new scan or measured maximum.

## Later path — planned, not present

- **Great Ape:** the eventual first transformation is conscious and controlled from the beginning. The form and its power develop with Goshin and his deepening Saiyan bloodline, rather than remaining obsolete. The older suggestion that the ape itself must frighten him does not override the later correction. The first trigger is separate from control and has not been fixed beyond the known Saiyan possibilities.
- **Ape power in the ordinary body:** the author’s later direction allows Goshin to access Great Ape power without the giant body as his mastery develops, and to combine it with anger or, once earned, Super Saiyan. Its first appearance, exact mechanics and degree are not established. This is his own evolving power, not an automatic copy of Broly and not a current Chapter 19 feat.
- **Golden line:** Super Saiyan 1, 2 and 3, and the distinctive Rage path, are later developments in the stored design. Their eventual perfected presentation is not an ability he possesses now. Their arrival must follow the author’s rulings and the story’s causes, not a defeat forced to preserve canon.
- **Ape road and Super Saiyan 4:** a later, earned route through the ape lineage; neither the golden ape nor SS4 has appeared in this branch.
- **Divine crossing:** the design reserves a late, one-way promotion to a divine baseline, followed by the authored God 1–3 and God 4 paths. Divinity must be genuinely reached through a valid path; the Talent does not conjure it from nothing. No god ki exists at the current checkpoint.
- **Excluded for Goshin:** Super Saiyan Blue. The future God 4 design is his own path, not an automatic copy of someone else’s Ultra state. The crossing must preserve his identity, emotions and relationships.

The detailed authored future design is in [`../STATUS.md`](../STATUS.md), especially §§11–16. Exact scenes and timing remain unwritten; do not treat a plan as a current feat.
