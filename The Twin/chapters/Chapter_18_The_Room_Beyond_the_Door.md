A folded drawing rests beneath Gohan's hat. Morning light crosses the paper, stopping at the door Hatch drew on the side of his boat.

**THE ROOM BEYOND THE DOOR**

**NARRATOR:** Goku is still travelling along Snake Way. On Earth, his family has promised to visit the children they met by the sea, but first there is training to do.

## The Drawing on the Table

Gohan opens Hatch's drawing beside a clean sheet of paper. The boat has a school on its roof, a great wheel at the back and a door beneath a row of windows.

"I'm going to make it bigger," he tells Goshin. "Ena wanted room for a teacher."

Goshin rests his chin on the table. "Does the teacher need a chair?"

"And a desk. Otherwise the books will fall into the wheel."

He starts with the boat, using Hatch's drawing to count the windows. Goshin finds a short ruler in Gohan's schoolbag and holds it against the page. Gohan draws a line along its edge, then adds a second deck above it.

Goshin leaves the ruler with Gohan and brings his stool to the stove. He pours rice from the sack into a bowl, and Ox-King helps him rinse it and tip it into the pot. Beside them, Chi-Chi washes and cuts the vegetables.

"There's enough for another bowl," Ox-King says.

"There's enough for three more," Chi-Chi answers. "I've seen how much you give them."

"They have training to do."

"I know. I packed lunch as well."

Chi-Chi slides the carrots and greens into the rice.

"Will Rom and Chiko still be in the same room today?" Goshin asks.

"I don't know yet," Chi-Chi says. "The director offered the six of them one night together. We'll ask what happened after that."

Gohan looks up from his drawing. "And Pigero?"

"We'll ask about him too."

The telephone rings before anyone can say more. Chi-Chi wipes her hands and answers it. A moment later she holds the receiver out to Gohan.

"Bulma wants to know whether we're coming past the laboratory."

Gohan takes the receiver in both hands. "I have Hatch's drawing for C-6. We'll bring it after we see the children."

"I've been looking at that click in his arm," Bulma says. "We're not taking anything apart until he's ready, but he can have visitors."

"Can he still hold Mina's book?"

"With his working hand, yes. He's been asking Father about one of the bridges."

Gohan smiles. "Hatch put a door on the new boat. C-6 wanted one."

"Then he'd better see it. Give your mother the telephone for a moment."

Chi-Chi listens, then looks through the window at Dr. Brief's aircar beside the house.

"We meant to return the car yesterday," she says. "I'm sorry we kept it overnight again. May we use it to visit the children first?"

"Father says that's fine. Bring it back when you come to the lab."

"Thank him for me. We'll take good care of it."

When Chi-Chi hangs up, Ox-King takes the lid off the pot. Steam rises around his hand, and Goshin leans closer to see the rice.

"It's nearly ready," Ox-King says.

"How many more stirs?"

"I was going to leave it alone."

"Then can we put a kitchen on the boat too?"

Gohan laughs. "If it fits."

Goshin returns to the table and draws a small window beside the school. Above it he adds a chimney with smoke curling from the top.

"Now everyone can smell lunch while they're studying."

When the rice is ready, Chi-Chi fills their bowls. Goshin finishes his first helping and holds his bowl out for another. Ox-King gives him a generous spoonful, then helps Gohan find room on the page for the teacher's desk.

## A Moving Target

In the wasteland, Piccolo stands among the cuts his last attacks left in the rock. His double waits on the opposite side of a shallow crater.

"Come at me from whichever side you want," Piccolo says.

The double flies low across the crater. Piccolo touches two fingers to his forehead, but the double strikes a loose slab with his foot. The stone rises between them, hiding the double from view.

Piccolo moves around its edge. The double is already beside him, reaching for his raised arm. Piccolo twists away, catches the wrist and drives a knee toward the double's ribs.

The double blocks and pulls free. Both land on the broken ground.

Piccolo looks at the slab, which has fallen between them. He steps behind it and raises his fingers again. This time, when the double comes around the stone, Piccolo sends a short blast from his other hand.

The blast strikes the double's shoulder and turns him sideways. Piccolo fires from his forehead while the opening remains. The beam cuts past the double's leg and bores into the empty ridge beyond him.

A strip of rock drops from the ridge. Piccolo and his double watch it strike the ground.

"Closer," Piccolo says. Next time he will aim for where his opponent is going, not where he has been.

The double rises. Piccolo takes his place across the crater again.

## On the Road

Goku runs along Snake Way with the supply capsule tucked securely inside his clothes. The road bends ahead, then disappears into a bank of cloud.

He reaches a seam in the stone and turns around it, trying the step he practised after his match with Goshin. This time he plants his foot too close to the edge and has to put out a hand to steady himself.

"That one wouldn't have worked. Goshin would laugh at me!"

He takes the next seam at a slower pace, turns and keeps running. At the third he changes direction without touching the ground with his hand.

"Better!"

He looks ahead again. The clouds lift from the road, revealing another long curve.

"King Kai's got to be somewhere up there."

Goku leans forward and picks up speed.

## Four Stones and One Ball

By the time Chi-Chi lands the aircar at the Lookout, Krillin and Yamcha have already claimed the clear tiles near the edge. Yamcha sends a small ball of light toward Krillin, then lifts his hand and turns it before Krillin can catch it.

"You moved it!" Krillin calls.

"That was the idea."

Krillin jumps after the light. Yamcha guides it over his head, but Krillin twists in the air and taps it with the back of his hand. The ball changes direction and fades above the tiles.

Puar claps from the shade. Yajirobe, sitting nearby with his sword across his knees, pulls his feet out of Krillin's way as Krillin lands.

"You could do that farther from me," Yajirobe says.

"You could sit farther from us."

Yajirobe looks at the empty tiles around him. "I was here first."

Gohan leaves his hat and both drawings on the bench with Puar. Goshin sets four flat stones in a square, with enough room between them for Gohan to stand.

"Are you ready?" Gohan asks.

Goshin lifts the stones with his ki. Two travel around Gohan in opposite directions while the other two cross in front of him. Gohan follows the nearest stone, then holds out one hand.

His ki touches it. The stone slides off its path and moves toward the edge of the square. Goshin draws it back while the other three continue moving.

"You nearly had it past me," Goshin says.

"I wanted it to go between those two."

Gohan changes his footing. This time he sends a quick push against the second stone, then steps to the side. Goshin moves the first stone away from it, but Gohan has already redirected the second through the opening.

The stone reaches the far side of the square and drops into Gohan's waiting hands.

"That was the space I meant."

Goshin lowers the other stones and comes over to look. The one Gohan caught has a chipped corner from an earlier exercise.

"Can you do it when I change their places?" Goshin asks.

"I want to try."

Popo watches them reset the square. Nearby, Chi-Chi finishes a short spar with Tien. She turns his reaching hand aside, steps in and touches his shoulder before he can withdraw.

Tien lowers his guard. "You caught me looking at your feet."

"You caught me the last time," Chi-Chi says. "I thought I'd give you something else to watch."

Chiaotzu brings them both cups of water. Chi-Chi thanks him and drinks while Tien wipes his forehead with a towel.

Popo comes to the boys. "Gohan, close your eyes. Tell me when Krillin moves."

Gohan closes his eyes. Krillin steps quietly across the tiles, stopping a few paces away. Gohan turns toward the scrape of his shoe.

"Now."

Krillin takes another step, lifting his foot clear of the tiles. Gohan keeps facing the place where he heard the first sound.

"I'm over here," Krillin says.

Gohan opens his eyes. "You didn't make the same noise."

"There are other things to notice," Popo says. "Try again."

On the next attempt Krillin raises his ki slightly as he moves. Gohan turns after him, uncertain at first, then points toward the right side of the square.

"There?"

"That's where I am," Krillin says.

Gohan opens his eyes, pleased, and asks for another turn. Goshin sets the stones aside so Krillin has more room.

## What Goku Learned

After the exercise, Yamcha rolls his shoulders and looks toward Kami, who has been watching from beside the building.

"We've been working hard," he says. "Is there something else we can try? Something Goku did when he trained here?"

"I taught him almost nothing," Kami says.

"Almost nothing?" Krillin stares at him.

Kami's eyes crinkle. "Popo gave him work, and Goku found plenty for himself."

Yajirobe shifts his sword onto his other knee. "If you've got a shortcut to that kind of strength, this would be a good time to tell us."

Kami smiles. "If there were a shortcut, Goku would probably run past it to see where the longer road went."

Krillin laughs. "That sounds like him."

Yamcha looks at Popo. "All right. What would you give us?"

Popo points toward the building. "There is a room I can show you."

Gohan goes to the bench for his hat, but Popo raises a hand.

"You won't need to carry anything inside."

Gohan leaves the hat beside Hatch's drawing. Puar settles onto the bench, keeping one paw on the paper when the breeze reaches it.

"I'll watch these for you," Puar says.

"Thank you. There's another drawing underneath."

"I'll watch both."

## The Room Beyond the Door

Popo leads them along a corridor to a closed door. Yajirobe follows at the back, carrying his sword.

Inside, a pattern of circles and lines covers the floor. A broad pendulum hangs above it, swinging from shadow into light and back again. The room is cool and still; its measured movement makes a faint sound each time it passes overhead.

"Goku trained here," Popo says. "This room can take your minds to another time. Your bodies stay on the Lookout."

Krillin looks from the circle to the door. "Where would we go?"

"You may see a place and people you don't know. What happens there can feel real, even though your bodies remain here."

Chi-Chi takes Gohan's hand. "Can you bring us back if we ask?"

"Yes. I will listen from here."

"All of us?"

"All of you."

Gohan looks up at Chi-Chi. "I'd like to go."

Goshin looks at the circle. "Will we be together?"

"You can enter together," Popo says. "Once you're there, stay near one another."

Goshin takes Gohan's hand. "I don't want us to get lost."

Chi-Chi looks from one son to the other, then at the fighters waiting beside them. "If they go, I'm going too."

"I'd rather stay here," Yajirobe says. "Someone ought to make sure this door doesn't close on you."

"It won't close on us," Yamcha says.

"Good. Then I won't have much to do."

Tien and Chiaotzu step toward the circle. Krillin joins them, followed by Yamcha. Chi-Chi brings the boys to the edge and gives each of them a moment to look around the room.

"Call for Popo when you want to return," Popo tells them.

Goshin makes sure he can see Gohan from inside the circle.

"Are you ready?" Chi-Chi asks.

Gohan nods. Goshin looks at her, then steps beside his brother.

Popo waits until everyone has found a place. "Close your eyes."

Goshin closes his eyes, keeping his hand against Gohan's.

The pendulum passes above the circle. Light spreads across the lines on the floor, climbing around the seven figures standing inside it.

## A Street Without People

Gohan opens his eyes beneath a red sky. Broken buildings rise on both sides of a wide street, their windows dark and empty. Pieces of masonry lie across the road ahead; fine dust moves between them in the wind. The place feels solid beneath his shoes, though Popo said their bodies would stay on the Lookout.

Goshin stands beside him. Chi-Chi is close enough to take either boy's hand.

Tien, Chiaotzu, Yamcha and Krillin are a few steps away, looking up at the damaged walls.

"This isn't the Lookout," Krillin says.

A small device lies half buried beside a broken doorway. Its coloured lens catches the light when Goshin crouches near it.

"Bulma has one like that."

Gohan remembers the lens against Raditz's face, and Father pinned beneath him. His tail curls close to his waist as he looks up at the broken roofs.

"Could it still see us?" he asks.

"I don't know," Chi-Chi says. "Leave it there."

Goshin scans the rooftops too as he steps back beside Gohan.

A short electronic sound comes from above them. Chiaotzu turns toward the broken roof across the street.

"There's someone up there."

Two figures stand against the sky. One has a bare head and armour over his chest. The other has long dark hair, a tail around his waist and a device over one eye.

Krillin moves in front of Chiaotzu. "They're dressed like Raditz."

The long-haired fighter touches the side of his device. **2,840** appears across the lens, a combined reading for all seven. His gaze stops at Gohan's tail and Goshin's.

"Two thousand eight hundred and forty together," he tells the other fighter. "You brought children with you?"

"We came with them," Gohan says.

The bald fighter drops from the roof and lands in the middle of the street. Dust rises around his boots. He looks past the brothers toward Tien and Yamcha.

"All seven? Good. Which of you wants to start?"

"We didn't come here looking for a fight," Chi-Chi says.

The long-haired fighter descends to the street behind him. "Then you chose a poor place to visit."

He raises his hand. A burst of ki strikes the pavement between the group and the doorway. Stone breaks upward, and everyone moves away from the spreading cracks.

Goshin lifts off beside Gohan, holding one hand near his brother as the fragments fall. Chi-Chi steps to their other side.

"Stay together," she says.

Goshin moves closer to Gohan and watches both attackers.

The bald fighter runs through the dust toward Chiaotzu.

## The Broken Wall

Tien reaches Chiaotzu first, meeting the fighter's arm before it can close around him. The impact pushes Tien back across the street. Chiaotzu rises above them and sends a blast toward the fighter's shoulder.

The fighter turns, letting the blast strike the wall behind him. Goshin flies across the street and hits him in the side before he can follow Chiaotzu.

The fighter slides several paces, his boots cutting two tracks through the dust. He looks at Goshin, then grins and charges again.

Goshin moves to meet him, keeping the fighter away from Chiaotzu. He catches the first punch with an open palm and turns it aside. The second catches his forearm and knocks him upward. He rolls in the air, comes down behind the fighter and drives both feet into the back of his armour.

The fighter strikes the wall. Bricks break away and tumble toward Chiaotzu.

Goshin raises one hand. The largest pieces stop above Chiaotzu, and Goshin sends them into an empty stretch of street. Chiaotzu flies clear before the smaller fragments land.

"Thanks, Goshin!"

Across the road, the long-haired fighter rushes Yamcha. Yamcha blocks a blow to his chest, then ducks beneath a second strike and sends a ball of light into the fighter's ribs.

The fighter catches it against his arm. Yamcha turns his wrist, guiding the ball around the fighter's back. It strikes the other shoulder and drives him through the edge of a doorway.

Krillin follows with a burst of ki. The fighter jumps over it, lands on the wall and kicks a heavy piece of masonry toward Krillin.

Gohan pushes the stone sideways with his ki. It hits the road beside Krillin instead of striking him.

"I could have ducked," Krillin says.

"You were looking the other way!"

"All right, then. Thanks!"

The long-haired fighter drops from the wall. Chi-Chi pulls Gohan clear of his landing, and Tien moves in beside Krillin. The fighter swings at Tien; Tien blocks, but the blow drives him back against a broken pillar.

The bald fighter tears himself free of the wall and comes at Goshin from behind. Gohan sees his hand reaching for Goshin's tail.

"Goshin, behind you!"

Goshin turns, bringing his tail close around his waist. The fighter catches his sleeve instead. Goshin twists his arm free and strikes the fighter across the chest, sending him back toward the crater in the road. His uncle had reached for that same tail. He won't leave it dangling for this man.

The long-haired fighter plants a foot against the pillar pinning Tien and drives it down. Tien rolls clear, but the pillar breaks across the street, throwing a cloud of dust between him and the others.

Gohan can see only part of the opposite wall. A shoe scrapes behind it.

He turns toward the sound, just as Krillin showed him on the Lookout. The bald fighter comes through the dust, heading for Chiaotzu again.

"He's on that side!" Gohan calls.

Chiaotzu rises. Tien clears the broken pillar and catches the fighter's next punch before it reaches him.

## The Opening

Tien and the bald fighter exchange blows in the middle of the street. Each strike pushes loose stone away from their feet. Chiaotzu hovers above the broken pillar, watching for an opening.

The long-haired fighter climbs onto the roof again. He points his hand toward Tien and Chiaotzu, and light gathers in his palm.

"Above you!" Chi-Chi calls.

Krillin flies toward the roof, but the fighter fires before he reaches it. The blast strikes the ground between Tien and Chiaotzu, throwing both away from the pillar. Chiaotzu hits the pavement and skids toward the wall.

Goshin catches Chiaotzu with his ki before he hits the wall. Chiaotzu steadies himself in the air while Goshin turns toward the roof.

The long-haired fighter fires again. Goshin meets the blast with his palm and sends it upward. It crosses the red sky and bursts above the empty buildings.

Yamcha brings his hands together. A ball of light forms between them, larger than the one he used on the Lookout.

"Tien, get clear of that wall!"

Tien jumps away from the bald fighter.

"Sokidan!" Yamcha sends the ball across the street, then curves it sharply toward the long-haired fighter. The fighter leaps from the roof, but Yamcha changes the ball's course again.

It strikes the fighter and drives him into the side of a tall building. Cracks spread through the wall above him.

"We've got him!" Krillin says.

The wall bursts outward. The long-haired fighter emerges through the dust, with a tear in one shoulder of his armour. He looks down at Yamcha's glowing ball, which has returned to hover above Yamcha's hand.

"Do that again," the fighter says.

Yamcha takes a step back. "I was planning to."

Gohan watches the cracked wall. Four broad pieces have broken loose and are starting to slide toward the street where Chiaotzu and Chi-Chi stand.

"Mother, the wall!"

Goshin lifts all four pieces with his ki. They stop above the road while he shifts them away from Chi-Chi and Chiaotzu. One turns on its edge; he steadies it and keeps the other three apart.

The long-haired fighter sees him holding the pieces and dives toward him. Gohan sends a small burst against a loose brick in the fighter's path. The brick strikes the fighter's arm and changes his course just enough for Krillin to meet him.

Krillin catches the fighter around the waist. They hit the ground together and roll past the broken pillar. Krillin keeps his arms locked as the fighter braces an elbow against the street.

"I can't hold him for long!" Krillin calls.

Goshin moves the four pieces above an empty section of road. Keeping them aloft, he brings his hands to his side and gathers light between his palms.

The fighter pushes Krillin away and turns toward him. Krillin rolls behind the broken pillar as Goshin shifts above the street, making sure there is empty ground behind the fighter and no one between them. Only then does he fire his Kamehameha.

The wave strikes the fighter in the chest. He drives his heels into the ground, cutting two grooves through the dust, but the force carries him backward through the broken doorway and into the empty building behind it. The upper wall breaks apart as the wave goes out, and the sound rolls down the street.

Above the road, the four pieces wobble. Goshin spreads his fingers and steadies them, then lowers them onto the empty ground, away from the others. Dust rolls across the street, and a piece of the doorway falls beside the fighter, who is on one knee among the rubble.

The bald fighter looks toward him, then turns on Gohan.

## A Way Back

Gohan moves away from the fighter, but the broken pillar blocks the direct path to Chi-Chi. He sends a burst of ki into the loose paving stones at the fighter's feet.

The stones jump under the fighter's feet. As he slips and reaches for Gohan, Chi-Chi darts in from the side, draws Gohan out of reach and hooks a foot behind the fighter's already sliding leg. He reaches for balance as Krillin rushes in from the other side.

Krillin's shoulder strikes the fighter's chest. The fighter stumbles past Gohan instead of reaching him.

"Over here!" Chiaotzu calls.

Chiaotzu sends another burst toward the fighter's back. It strikes his armour and makes him turn. Tien divides into four figures around him, each moving toward a different opening in his guard.

The fighter swings at one Tien. Another catches his arm, but the fighter drags him a step forward. Yamcha sends his ball low across the street, striking the fighter behind the knees.

Goshin drops from above, takes the fighter's other wrist and turns with him. Together, Tien and Goshin drive him into the broken pavement. The remaining copies of Tien disappear as he releases the fighter and steps away.

The fighter tries to rise. Goshin lands beside Gohan, between him and the fighter, while Yamcha's ball waits above the street.

The long-haired fighter is still on one knee in the ruined doorway. He braces one hand against the ground and looks toward his companion.

Chi-Chi reaches Gohan and Goshin. "Is anyone hurt?"

"I'm all right," Gohan says. He looks at Chiaotzu. "Are you?"

"I hit the ground, but I'm all right."

Krillin rubs his shoulder and glances toward the doorway. "They're still getting up."

"We can keep going," Tien says, watching the fighter in the road.

Gohan looks at both opponents, then at the people beside him. The fight has felt real, down to the pavement breaking beneath them. Chiaotzu hit it hard, and the men are getting up. Rom and Chiko are waiting to see them.

"We said we'd visit Rom and Chiko after training."

Chi-Chi keeps her eyes on the fighters. "Do you want to leave?"

Gohan nods. "I want to know what happened to them."

Goshin looks from the broken building to Gohan. "Me too."

Yamcha lowers his hand, and the ball of light fades. "We learned something here. I'd rather talk about it before we start again."

Tien helps Chiaotzu down beside him. Krillin stays near the boys.

"Popo!" Goshin calls. "We want to come back."

"Please bring us back," Chi-Chi says.

The red sky fades. The broken street and the two fighters disappear beneath a bright light.

## Back on the Lookout

Gohan opens his eyes inside the circle. Popo stands by the pendulum, and Yajirobe is at the door with his sword still across his shoulder.

Chi-Chi puts an arm around each boy and gives them both a moment to settle after the exercise.

"Does anything hurt?"

Goshin flexes the arm that blocked the fighter's punch. "It hurt there when he hit me. It doesn't now."

Gohan touches his own shoulder. "I felt the stones break, but I don't have any marks."

"Your bodies stayed here," Popo says. "The things you experienced still felt real to you."

Chiaotzu touches his shirt where he struck the street. There isn't a mark. He looks at Goshin and lifts his thumb; Goshin grins back.

Yajirobe looks around the circle. "You were standing there the whole time. I couldn't tell whether anything was happening."

"Plenty was happening," Yamcha says.

Tien turns to Chiaotzu. "When the wall came down, I should have stayed where I could see you."

"Goshin caught the pieces," Chiaotzu says. "And you stopped the other one."

"Four of you kept him looking in different directions," Krillin says.

"Each body has only a quarter of my strength and speed," Tien says. "I needed Yamcha's attack and Goshin's hold to bring him down."

Goshin looks toward Popo. "Will those two get up?"

Popo pauses beside the pendulum. "I can't tell you what happened to them after we brought you back."

"Were they the Saiyans who are coming here?" Gohan asks.

"I don't know exactly whom the room showed you," Kami says. "Raditz told us two others were coming, but he didn't show us their faces. Don't assume the next fight will be the same as this one."

Krillin looks at his friends. "We had seven people in there. The next fight may not give us the same ground or the same chances."

"Then we'll keep working together," Tien says.

"And separately," Yamcha adds. "I'd like another turn with Krillin before he decides he can catch everything I throw."

"You owe me one that doesn't change direction."

"No, I don't."

Both boys smile at Yamcha.

Chi-Chi takes the boys' hands. "We promised to see the children today. We'll come back for more training."

Outside, Puar has kept both drawings beneath Gohan's hat. The Four-Star Ball catches the light as Gohan picks it up.

## The Room by the Sea

The aircar lands near the home in the newer part of town. Chi-Chi sees Pigero standing outside the gate, with his sack beside his feet. He waves when he recognises them.

"You came back."

"We said we would," Chi-Chi answers. "How are the others?"

"They're inside. I stayed until supper was over, then went back to our old house. I came here again this morning."

Gohan climbs out with both drawings. "Did they all sleep in the same room?"

"They did. Chiko wanted the bed by the window, and Rom wanted to make sure he could see the door."

A woman opens the gate when Chi-Chi reaches it. She is the worker who drove the children to the home the day before.

"They've been asking when you might come," she says. "The director is inside with them."

The common room has two windows, a long table and six beds arranged along the walls. Chiko is sitting on one with Ena while Hatch draws at the table. Rom stands beside the other window, watching Jink and Yordon examine a box of coloured pencils.

Chiko jumps up when she sees Gohan. "You found us!"

Gohan runs over to her, grinning. "Pigero was at the gate."

"I told him to wait there. He thought you might go to the old house first."

Rom comes closer, keeping one hand in his pocket. "You came after training?"

"We did," Chi-Chi says. "How was your night?"

"Chiko talked until the lights went out."

"Ena was talking too," Chiko says.

Ena looks at her. "You asked me questions."

"I wanted to know if you could see the window from your bed."

Goshin looks from one bed to the next. "Could you?"

"Yes," Ena says. "And I could see Chiko's feet."

Hatch holds up his pencil. "They let me keep it. And they have more paper."

Gohan unfolds the larger drawing they made at home. Hatch comes over at once, with Ena and Yordon behind him.

"You put the school on a second floor," Hatch says.

"Now there's room for the teacher's desk."

Ena points to a blank space beside the windows. "Could there be a library too?"

"You can put one there," Gohan says.

Ena takes a pencil from the box and draws a small door, then adds shelves behind it. Hatch takes a blue pencil and makes a line of water beneath the boat.

Rom watches for a moment, then turns to the director, who has come over from the other side of the room.

"Can we stay together tonight too?"

"You can use this room again tonight," she says. "I need to speak with the people responsible for arranging the rooms before I can promise anything beyond that."

"Will Pigero be allowed to come back?" Ena asks.

"He can visit tomorrow afternoon. We'll talk with him about the days after that."

Pigero rests a hand on the back of Ena's chair. "I'll be here tomorrow."

Chi-Chi looks at Rom. "You can tell me if there's something you want help asking. My offer to make room at our house still stands, but we'd need to discuss it with everyone involved."

Rom glances toward Chiko. "I want to know where she'll sleep."

"Then we'll keep asking," Chi-Chi says.

At the table, Gohan takes Hatch's original drawing from his bag and shows him how carefully he folded it.

"I'm taking this one to C-6. I haven't asked Mina about her bridge book yet, so I can't bring that."

"Tell C-6 the new boat has two doors," Hatch says.

Goshin bends over the larger drawing. "The library door counts."

"Then there are three," Ena says, adding a door at the front of the boat.

## The Door on the Boat

At Capsule Corporation, Chi-Chi returns Dr. Brief's aircar. Bulma meets them outside the laboratory and offers to take the family home in another car after the visit.

"C-6 is awake," she says. "Let's see whether he wants company."

C-6 agrees, and Gohan brings the folded drawing to his table. Mina's bridge book lies open beside him, with a strip of paper marking a page farther in.

"Hatch drew this one for you to see," Gohan says. "He added a door."

C-6 studies the boat. "And a school. Does the teacher have to hold on to the wheel?"

"That was where the teacher sat before," Gohan says. "We made another drawing with a proper desk. Ena put a library on it."

"Hatch said the new boat has two doors," Goshin tells C-6. "Ena put in another."

"Three doors? I'd like to see that one too."

"We left it with them," Goshin says. "We can ask next time."

C-6 lifts his working hand toward the picture, then stops when a click sounds in his other arm. Dr. Brief looks up from the next bench.

"Would you like me to check the support?" he asks.

"Yes, but please tell me before you move it."

"I will."

While Dr. Brief checks the padding beneath C-6's arm, Bulma brings Gohan a clean sheet of paper. He draws the second floor and its library, leaving space for Hatch and Ena to add more when they next meet.

"Did the children like the room?" Bulma asks.

"Some of it," Chi-Chi says. "They're together again tonight. We don't know what the director will decide after that."

"Then keep me informed. I can come with you next time if they want me there."

Gohan looks up from the drawing. "You could help us work out how to make the wheel turn."

"Ask Hatch if he'd like that," Bulma says. "I've got a few ideas."

Gohan folds his new drawing and puts it beside his hat. C-6 looks at the door on Hatch's boat once more before Gohan puts the original away.

"That should make getting aboard much easier," C-6 says.

Bulma drives Chi-Chi and the boys back to the mountain house before returning to her work at the laboratory.

---

## The Road and the Window

Goku crests a rise in Snake Way and sees the road curling into the distance. He stops just long enough to look, then leans into the downhill run.

"You're a long way off, King Kai!"

At the mountain house, Gohan places Hatch's drawing beneath his hat again. The new picture lies beside it, with a space left for the library shelves.

"We'll take this when we see them," he says.

Goshin puts one finger on the edge of the paper. "And ask about the doors."

Chi-Chi closes the window before the evening breeze can lift either drawing. Gohan moves the hat aside, and the door Hatch drew is visible again.

**NARRATOR:** The family has kept its promise to visit the children. Goku continues toward King Kai, while the friends on Earth prepare for the Saiyans in their own ways.
