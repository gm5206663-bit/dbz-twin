# Goshin's Great Ape: control and evolving Saiyan power

**Separate continuity note, 1 November 2026.** This records the user's new character direction and its implications. It does not amend or accept the protected project, the first five chapters, the delivered Chapter 6–10 candidates, or any remote copy. It supplements `SAIYAN_ADAPTATION_REVIEW.md` without silently changing that delivered report.

## The user's direction

Goshin's Great Ape is **not uncontrollable**. It is an ability he can use. His Saiyan bloodline and his exceptional Adaptation Talent both matter: inherited Saiyan nature supplies the Great Ape potential, and his native supernatural Talent interacts with and develops that nature. Do not reduce the Talent to copying techniques or treat this as a universal rule that every Saiyan or hybrid automatically has the same control.

As Goshin grows stronger, his Saiyan bloodline becomes purer and deeper, and his traits and other capacities develop. His **Great Ape and its power develop as well**, rather than remaining an isolated, obsolete transformation. Later, as he unlocks or masters that power, he can use Great Ape power in his ordinary body and combine it with other powers, including Super Saiyan and anger. The user likens some possibilities to Legendary Super Saiyan, without specifying that Goshin must literally be Broly, have an identical form, or lose control like Broly. Other developments remain possible.

## Working interpretation for future writing

### 1. Control from the first Great Ape event

When Goshin transforms into a Great Ape, he remains himself and retains awareness and control of his actions. He can recognize and protect people, make choices, and use the form; do not make an initial mindless rampage, forgotten transformation, or loss of self a prerequisite for this ability. This applies to the **first** transformation too, not only to a later mastered state.

Being able to control the form is not the same as having already explored every application of it. His Talent can make him exceptionally effective very quickly, and experience or training can develop further precision, strength and applications. Do not invent a long compulsory struggle before he is allowed to remain himself. Conversely, do not claim an ability or transformation has already occurred in a chapter where it was not shown.

His emotion remains his own: anger can increase power without automatically turning him into a mindless monster. Ordinary circumstances may still frighten, surprise or challenge him; fear of his own Great Ape and loss of control are not mandatory story beats.

### 2. The form evolves with him

A stronger underlying Saiyan produces a stronger Great Ape, and the user's rule goes further: Goshin's evolving bloodline, body, traits and Talent may improve the form itself and how effectively he uses it. Depending on the scene, its developing qualities could include bodily coordination, strength, durability, senses, instincts and ki control. These are examples of possible applications, not a list of already acquired feats or a set of limits.

His adaptation can affect the tail and other inherited traits too. The extent and timing of any particular change have not been set. Do not assume that his human inheritance is erased, invent a blood percentage, make his tail automatically invulnerable, or impose a new fixed multiplier, weakness, exhausting cost or stage of development.

### 3. Great Ape power without the giant body

As his mastery develops, Goshin can access Great Ape power while remaining in his normal-sized form. This can be a meaningful part of his Saiyan development, not merely a visual effect or copying another person. The specific degree, first appearance and mechanics remain to be shown in the story; the user did not set a fixed fraction or require that it unlock only after a long training period. The Talent can make further, faster or broader applications possible.

The official profile for *Dragon Ball Super: Broly* explicitly says that Broly uses Great Ape power in his regular body. That is a **precedent for the concept**, not proof that Goshin has Broly's biology, power level, rage, limitations or eventual forms. [1](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_941.html)

### 4. Combining powers later

Goshin's refined Great Ape power can interact with other powers as they become available, including Super Saiyan and anger, in a manner distinctive to him. A forceful, legendary-like expression is possible; it does not have to be identical to either film's Broly or carry Broly's loss of self. Do not automatically label it a pre-existing canon form, dictate an aura or hair color, assign an exact multiplier, or create a new named rung without a story decision.

The existing late Great-Ape/SS4 path and later divine-promotion direction remain in place where they do not conflict with the user's latest instruction. This new possibility does **not** mean a golden form, god ki, SS4 or an ape/Super Saiyan combination has already appeared in the current chapters. Great Ape itself is normal Saiyan biology and is not barred by the existing early-golden-form restriction.

### 5. First transformation versus its trigger

**Keeping control after transforming** is the settled character rule. Whether Goshin can initially trigger a transformation without a full moon or equivalent stimulus is a separate issue the user has not fixed. The ordinary Saiyan baseline includes tail and moon/Blutz-wave conditions, and an artificial source can work. His Talent may expand applications later; do not declare a permanent moon requirement or invent unshown moon-free use now. Official profiles discuss both the Great Ape and Vegeta's artificial moon. [2](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_503.html) [1](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_3929.html)

## What this changes in the provisional story

- **Neither twin has transformed in the separate Chapter 6–10 candidates.** Both tails and the moon remain. In Chapter 9 a full moon appears at the bridge, and both boys avoid looking at it; Chapter 10 explicitly continues the following morning.
- The family's caution in Chapters 8–9 can still make sense from what the family knows: Roshi saw Goku's uncontrolled transformation, and no one has seen either twin transform. Their uncertainty is not proof that Goshin would lose control. Gohan's own experience is a separate question; do not remove his agency or assume he automatically has Goshin's Talent.
- If a later scene gives Goshin his first Great Ape transformation, the writing should show a **conscious Goshin making meaningful choices from the start**. The family can react to what they see and learn, rather than the narration imposing an obligatory rampage. His age, character and capacity to grow remain intact.
- The protected `STATUS.md` describes him as comfortable as a Great Ape, but some older future-SS4 wording says the ape road 'costs him fear'; `OPEN.md` already flags that tension. The user's present control rule overrides any reading that would require him to fear or lose himself to his Great Ape. Do not retroactively edit the historical files or infer that all fear of any kind is forbidden.
- Preserve the broader corrections: supernatural and all-encompassing Talent, unusually capable Saiyan recovery, evolving bloodline, retained acquired qualities, Goshin never less capable than Gohan, Gohan's separate growth, and no forced early golden or divine forms.
