# Original-Z episode chronicle — the entire Namek journey

**Latest Chapter 43 branch:** [Source and branch handoff](../../../The%20Twin/canon/SCENE_COVERAGE_031_082_084_088_CH43.md) records the altered fight, choices and bounded relay; the source chronology below does not mandate outcomes. The earlier caption counts and audiovisual limits are unchanged.

**Later Chapter 42 branch:** [Ordered source and branch reconciliation](../../../The%20Twin/canon/SCENE_COVERAGE_080_083_087_088_CH42.md) follows the actual changed cast, Dende's on-page chosen awakening, the lake pursuit and Freeza's contested new change. Selected relevant cues and Toei reports were rechecked without a new complete-caption count or full audiovisual viewing. The source chronology below is not a mandated story outcome.

**Original-story spoilers.** This is a source chronology, not the sequence already completed in *The Twin*. Original Japanese Z numbers are used. Each episode number links to its official Toei page. The entries synthesize the retained Japanese caption reviews and Toei's brief Japanese synopses; they are paraphrases, not a complete transcript or full audiovisual account. Local timestamps and individual member hashes are in the [source reviews and inventory](07_SOURCES_AND_GAPS.md). Parenthetical notes distinguish characters' beliefs from demonstrated results.

## 1. The reason to leave Earth

**Earlier prerequisite, [35](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/35/):** In the source Saiyan battle, Gohan's tail regrows and he transforms after seeing Vegeta's artificial moon; Vegeta cuts the tail and is crushed by the falling ape. Yajirobe had already cut Vegeta's tail. Toei's episode title uses “Super Saiyan” for this earlier Gohan scene, but the synopsis describes a Great Ape, not the later golden transformation. In *The Twin*, Gohan and Vegeta both keep their tails and Goshin has an independently controlled first Great Ape; do not import the original cuts. The Toei synopsis and the [secondary episode guide](https://www.kanzenshuu.com/episode/dbz/eps-035/) are short accounts, not a full audiovisual comparison.

- **[36](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/36/) — aftermath and possibility.** After the original Saiyan battle, surviving friends recover the wounded. Piccolo and Kami have died, taking Earth's Dragon Balls with them; Krillin remembers what the Saiyans said about Piccolo's origin and the possibility of another Dragon Ball set on Namek. Hope to restore the dead grows out of those specific deaths. In *The Twin*, the relevant people survive, so this motive is altered.
- **[37](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/37/) — Yunzabit.** The group looks for a way to reach the distant planet. An attempt to operate the remaining Saiyan craft ends in its destruction. Mr. Popo helps Bulma find Kami's old spacecraft at Yunzabit Heights; it responds to Namekian commands, and a test carries them to Jupiter and back. The unverified-origin captions contain the reporter's account of the pod's destruction, while Toei's synopsis describes Bulma's failed attempt.
- **[38](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/38/) — the original crew.** Brief's people work on Kami's ship. Bulma estimates a roughly one-month flight and first discusses leaving in five days, then revises the preparation to ten days for the language work. Popo cannot leave the Lookout; Bulma goes to operate the craft, Krillin as protection, and Gohan insists on helping revive Piccolo despite Chi-Chi's fear. They depart. These timings are source estimates, not automatically the branch's timetable.

## 2. The voyage: the children and false Namek

- **[39](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/39/) — the hidden ship.** On the seventh day Bulma is bored and Gohan and Krillin image-train. A mysterious attack leads them to a huge mirrored vessel and a series of traps. The travellers find that their apparent attackers are children; Bulma is held in the source encounter. Goku's hospital situation and Vegeta's separate damaged-pod journey form source cutaways.
- **[40](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/40/) — distrust, rescue and route.** The children fear that the visitors work for Freeza's forces, which destroyed their home. During a meteor emergency the fighters protect children and Bulma helps steer. Rescue does **not** instantly settle their distrust: afterward Bun still treats the visitors' extraordinary power as evidence against them, while Zeshin argues that the act of saving them distinguishes the travellers from the invaders. The children explain their losses and intended refuge and offer a shortcut towards Namek in direction KS162, but a warning about that route is left unfinished. The narrator's 18-day clock concerns Vegeta's arrival at a Freeza base **after leaving Earth**, not the travellers' time in space.
- **[41](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/41/) — apparently helpful hosts.** After a space current, the travellers reach what seems to be Namek. Two apparent Namekians, Raiti and Zākuro, help them search for apparent Dragon Balls and face local hazards. The apparent setting and discoveries must be read with episode 44's revelation, not filed as genuine Namekian history.
- **[42](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/42/) — parallel recovery.** The apparent Dragon Ball hunt proceeds and the travellers seem to make rapid progress. On Earth Goku has recovered enough to train. Vegeta reaches Planet Freeza No. 79 and receives medical treatment. A line about a tail that could not be restored concerns Vegeta in this source context, not Gohan.
- **[43](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/43/) — a too-easy quest.** The apparent hosts guide the party through further dangers, including an icy cave, until the supposed set is nearly complete. Vegeta recovers and learns that Freeza is pursuing Namek's Dragon Balls. The strangers' helpfulness conceals another purpose; do not count the illusory balls as part of the real set.
- **[44](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/44/) — deception and arrival.** Raiti and Zākuro are exposed as impostors using deception to obtain the travellers' spaceship. The group regains the craft and finally reaches the **real** Namek; Vegeta also arrives pursuing the balls. The source narrator places the Earth party's real arrival 34 days after *its own departure*. The planet, its people and the looming powers are now separate from the previous illusion.

## 3. Three sides seek seven balls

- **[45](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/45/) — Freeza and Cui.** Freeza's party has arrived before the Earth travellers to seek immortality; Vegeta intends to obtain it for himself. Vegeta defeats and kills Cui, formerly a peer in Freeza's service. Freeza's men attack the Earth ship and destroy it, leaving Bulma and the boys without that way home. Source scouter figures include brief readings around 1,500 and Vegeta's 24,000 in particular contexts, not fixed values for another story.
- **[46](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/46/) — Goku's six-day ship.** Gohan and Krillin hide from Freeza and feel a frightening presence. On Earth Goku learns of the situation and departs in a second craft built around his old pod; Dr. Brief describes the six-day trip and adjustable gravity, up to 100 times Earth's. Goku begins with 20G. The source describes recovery and rebuilding of the previously damaged pod; the original pod's exact visual aftermath was not independently checked here.
- **[47](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/47/) — the village.** Krillin and Gohan follow Freeza's group and watch a Namekian village threatened for its Dragon Ball. Local fighters raise their apparent power and resist. The village elder recognizes that the soldiers use scouters to locate people and destroys the devices. Freeza's forces respond brutally.
- **[48](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/48/) — Dende's rescue.** Dodoria kills resisting Namekians and the elder despite the surrender of the ball. When the remaining children are threatened, Gohan breaks cover; Krillin joins him and they carry the surviving child, Dende, away. Dodoria pursues, and Krillin uses Solar Flare in the escape. Gohan's choice and Krillin's help both matter.
- **[49](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/49/) — Vegeta and Dodoria.** Dodoria loses the fleeing group and attacks the area, believing them destroyed. Vegeta intercepts him; Dodoria reveals that Freeza, not a natural disaster, destroyed Planet Vegeta and bargains for his life. Vegeta kills him. Dende is alive and grateful to his rescuers. This is information given to Vegeta here; the fact that some other character later learns it is a separate event.
- **[50](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/50/) — threat on two routes.** On Namek, Vegeta uses ki-sensing while the Earth fighters suppress their power; he seeks another village. Bulma brings news that Goku is on his way. In the original-Z ship subplot, damage and a dangerous approach to a hot celestial body threaten Goku's voyage; he uses a Kamehameha and repairs the emergency. One occurrence does not define a universal travel or training rule.
- **[51](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/51/) — the Elder's home.** Vegeta attacks a Namekian village and hides its Dragon Ball in water. Dende talks about his people's history and the ajissa trees and takes Krillin to the Great Elder, while Gohan stays with Bulma. At King Kai's planet, the dead Earth fighters have arrived and seek training; Goku continues gravity work. Freeza's party has five balls at this stage.
- **[52](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/52/) — warning and Zarbon.** King Kai identifies Freeza's enormous ki and warns Goku not to confront him. Piccolo and the other dead fighters ask King Kai to train them. Vegeta challenges Zarbon, one of Freeza's principal attendants, while Krillin and Dende continue towards the Great Elder.
- **[53](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/53/) — transformation.** Zarbon reveals a more powerful form that he dislikes for its appearance and defeats Vegeta, leaving him near death. Dende and Krillin meet Nail and the Great Elder. Goku describes adaptation to heavier gravity as well as his need for rest; his individual training is not a formula for everyone else.
- **[54](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/54/) — awakened potential.** The Great Elder reads Krillin's intentions, gives him his Dragon Ball and draws out dormant strength. Krillin asks about bringing Gohan. Freeza needs the location of Vegeta's hidden ball, so Zarbon retrieves the injured Saiyan for treatment; Freeza also calls the Ginyu Force. The Elder's stated potential-unlocking ability is not a measured multiplier.
- **[55](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/55/) — recovery and theft.** Yamcha, Tien and Chiaotzu train under King Kai and spar a weighted Piccolo; the exchange is stopped before an unweighted rematch. Vegeta recovers in Freeza's medical machine and escapes. His recovery matters to his next fight, but a single recovery does not establish a fixed increase for all Saiyans.
- **[56](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/56/) — the set splits again.** Vegeta steals the five Dragon Balls Freeza had collected. Krillin carries the Great Elder's ball, and Zarbon pursues Vegeta. Gohan independently discovers the ball Vegeta concealed in the lake. During Goku's voyage a magnetic storm disrupts the gravity controls and exposes him to 100G under emergency conditions.
- **[57](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/57/) — Zarbon's defeat.** After medical recovery Vegeta defeats transformed Zarbon. Krillin gives up the Elder's ball under threat, while Gohan keeps the lake ball hidden. Goku escapes the gravity emergency, reports broken bones, eats a Senzu and continues training. The violence, medicine and resulting recovery are particular source events.
- **[58](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/58/) — a dangerous secret.** Gohan conceals the lake ball during an encounter with Vegeta; Vegeta later realizes it is missing. Gohan and Krillin head for the Elder so Gohan can have his own latent power drawn out. Freeza's summoned Ginyu Force travels towards Namek, and King Kai senses their approach.
- **[59](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/59/) — Bulma on her own.** Bulma deals with the four-star ball falling into the water, local danger and capture by Freeza's men Blueberry and Raspberry. Her efforts to recover and protect the ball, and later to deceive her captors, show a separate struggle rather than passive waiting. Goku continues his voyage and training, including Senzu use.
- **[60](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/60/) — deception and distance.** Bulma tries to turn the soldiers' greed and local threats against them. Gohan and Krillin fly towards the Elder while controlling their detectable power; Vegeta senses them. Gohan refers to nearly four days without specifying the starting point in that cue. The Ginyu Force approaches, and Goku has worked through the 100G challenge.
- **[61](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/61/) — Gohan's awakening.** Gohan reaches the Great Elder and has his dormant strength released; Krillin senses the increase and Vegeta judges it still insufficient against him. Before the awakening, Krillin privately questions an earlier optimistic comparison. Nail explains that the Namekian dragon can grant three wishes. The rapidly approaching five powers signal a new emergency.

## 4. Ginyu Force and the first use of Porunga

- **[62](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/62/) — the uneasy alliance.** Vegeta, Krillin and Gohan cooperate against the Ginyu Force, considering immortality for Vegeta as a desperate counter. The squad arrives before they can use the balls and seizes all seven. Its members choose opponents; Guldo confronts Gohan and Krillin, and Recoome is matched with Vegeta.
- **[63](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/63/) — Guldo.** Although physically outpaced by the two Earth fighters, Guldo uses limited time-stopping and a distinct paralysis technique to put them in danger. Vegeta interrupts and kills him. A separate Earth-side line places this on the sixth day since Goku departed; it should not be equated with episode 60's unspecified four-day starting point.
- **[64](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/64/) — Recoome's violence.** Vegeta attacks Recoome but is severely beaten; a scouter reports a rising reading near 30,000 before the exchange. Krillin and Gohan intervene to save him, and Krillin is badly hurt. The exact fight dynamics and numerical readings belong to these source participants and conditions.
- **[65](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/65/) — Gohan's stand.** Gohan faces Recoome after the others fall. His Masenko fails to finish the opponent and Gohan is gravely injured. Freeza now has the seven balls but still needs the method of using them. Chi-Chi tries to reach her son in an Earth-side subplot. Goku arrives at the battlefield as the crisis peaks.
- **[66](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/66/) — Goku arrives.** Goku gives Senzu to Gohan and Krillin and uses the last one for Vegeta; he learns enough of the situation and chooses to face the Ginyu Force. He defeats Recoome with one attack. The captioned information-gathering moment does not by itself prove a particular touch or a general mind-reading power.
- **[67](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/67/) — Jeice and Burter.** The two attack together, with their scouter initially showing Goku at 5,000. Their attacks do not have the result they expect. Vegeta theorizes that Goku sharply raises power at the moment of action; Gohan notices a discrepancy between the fight and his father's sensed ki. These are observations and inferences, not a permanent limit on Goku.
- **[68](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/68/) — mercy and calculation.** Goku incapacitates Burter and wants the defeated opponents taken away; Jeice flees. Vegeta kills the fallen Recoome and Burter, angering Goku. Vegeta's cooperation remains tactical rather than a promise of friendship. Ginyu and Jeice return, and Goku sends Gohan and Krillin to search for the balls.
- **[69](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/69/) — Goku and Ginyu.** Vegeta leaves, hoping to gain the balls while Goku and Ginyu weaken one another. Bulma repairs her communicator and pursues the clustered balls; Gohan and Krillin seek her radar. Goku confronts Ginyu and calls Kaiō-ken as the observed scouter figure rises through 110,000. The 180,000 line belongs to the next episode's main scene, not this one's preview.
- **[70](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/70/) — a frightening reading.** Ginyu reports Goku at 180,000 after the Kaiō-ken call; Jeice has described Ginyu's own maximum as 120,000. Freeza reaches the Great Elder. In this anime caption account three additional Namekians identify themselves as warrior-types before facing Freeza; this differs from the wording of a separate manga-based profile and must not be silently averaged.
- **[71](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/71/) — Nail and the exchange.** Nail draws Freeza into a delaying fight so Dende can reach the others. Freeza cites 42,000 for Nail and 530,000 for himself; after Nail regenerates a lost limb, Freeza observes that his strength has dropped. Elsewhere Ginyu wounds his own body and uses Change to trade bodies with Goku. Gohan and Krillin locate Bulma and take the radar.
- **[72](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/72/) — the wrong face.** Gohan and Krillin find the seven balls but the Earth summoning words do not work. Ginyu approaches in Goku's body; Gohan warns that the apparent father is not Goku. Goku, trapped in Ginyu's injured body, explains the exchange and the difficulty of matching a body and its power. Gohan's recognition is not proof of an unrestricted identification technique.
- **[73](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/73/) — fighting the stolen body.** Jeice reports 23,000 for Ginyu in Goku's body. Gohan hesitates to strike his father's apparent body but then fights alongside Krillin; Ginyu begins adjusting, while Vegeta confronts and defeats Jeice. Ginyu attempts to take Vegeta's body next. The reported number is neither Goku's fixed base nor a rule for every swap.
- **[74](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/74/) — restored bodies and a frog.** Goku intercepts the proposed exchange and gets his own body back; a further Change attempt is diverted and Ginyu is left in a frog. The separate manga-based official account describes Goku throwing the frog; the captions alone do not independently establish every visual step. Vegeta helps Goku reach a medical machine and provides battle clothes because he needs him against Freeza.
- **[75](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/75/) — the secret words.** Gohan enjoys the new clothes even in a frightening situation. While Vegeta rests and Goku is treated, Krillin meets Dende and learns that both the summoning phrase and wishes require Namekian. The group moves the balls and Dende summons Porunga. Freeza realizes that Nail's resistance has bought time and returns.
- **[76](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/76/) — two wishes, an interruption.** Porunga offers three wishes but, at this time, only one person can be revived per wish. Through King Kai, Piccolo requests revival first and transport to Namek second; his return also restores linked Kami and Earth's Dragon Balls. A suggestion to use the last wish to bring Piccolo to the group is followed by Vegeta's demand for immortality. Facing Freeza's approach, Krillin reluctantly asks Dende to grant Vegeta's request, but the Great Elder dies before it is granted and the dragon disappears. The caption of the earlier suggestion is not speaker-labelled. Freeza reaches the party.

## 5. Freeza's transformations and the alliance's losses

- **[77](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/77/) — Piccolo and Nail.** Freeza is furious at being denied his wish. Piccolo encounters the dying Nail, who offers Namekian assimilation and a large increase in power while assuring Piccolo that he will remain himself. Piccolo agrees. Freeza and Vegeta discuss Planet Vegeta; Krillin realizes the tyrant destroyed the Saiyan world. Freeza prepares to transform.
- **[78](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/78/) — over one million.** An anime retrospective presents King Vegeta's failed confrontation with Freeza. Freeza takes his larger second form and claims a power above one million. He attacks the fighters and Krillin is gravely pierced. The flashback is this television version; its full visual presentation and comparison with other Bardock/Planet Vegeta versions remain separate questions.
- **[79](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/79/) — Gohan's anger.** Krillin falls after his injury; Gohan attacks Freeza in outrage and briefly surprises the others with his strength. Freeza remains dangerous and turns on him. Dende says Krillin is still alive. Vegeta's comments about Gohan's potential are interpretations of the immediate exchange, not a measured permanent base.
- **[80](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/80/) — Dende heals; Piccolo arrives.** Krillin distracts Freeza and buys time, including with Solar Flare and a cutting attack. Dende heals the living Gohan; Vegeta comments on the resulting increase. Piccolo arrives and asks the others not to interfere. Healing, ordinary recovery and an individual's later growth are related source events, not one universally quantified mechanism.
- **[81](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/81/) — Piccolo's challenge.** Gohan trusts his teacher's judgment; Krillin is initially anxious, then sees Piccolo contend with Freeza. In the retained captions a voice addressing Freeza is tagged as Nail before Piccolo discusses the assimilation. Without audio and visuals this does not settle exactly how separate awareness is staged or how long it persists.
- **[82](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/82/) — more forms.** Piccolo removes his heavy outer clothing and shifts the fight, but Freeza reveals that further transformations remain and begins another. Vegeta asks Krillin to wound him in the hope that Dende's healing and Saiyan recovery will produce the legendary Super Saiyan. That is Vegeta's plan, not a proven guarantee.
- **[83](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/83/) — sacrifice and hesitation.** Third-form Freeza beats Piccolo; Gohan intervenes with a strong attack, which Freeza repels. Krillin reluctantly wounds Vegeta. Dende refuses at first to heal the killer of his people, heals Piccolo first and ultimately heals Vegeta after appeals. Freeza proceeds towards his final form.
- **[84](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/84/) — the healer targeted.** In his final form Freeza kills Dende; Piccolo infers that Freeza had noticed his healing. Gohan, Krillin and Piccolo cannot stop the new assault. Vegeta, recovered and much stronger, claims to be a Super Saiyan, but Freeza outpaces him. Krillin's statement about recovery is a character's account, not an unconditional multiplier.
- **[85](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/85/) — Vegeta's despair.** Freeza overwhelms Vegeta and easily handles his attack. Vegeta's confidence breaks and he questions his belief about the transformation. At the same time Goku's medical treatment finishes and he senses he has become stronger. Neither scene supplies a complete measured present-day base for the altered branch.
- **[86](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/86/) — death and a plea.** Goku reaches the battlefield. Freeza recalls a Saiyan who resisted him; Vegeta tells Goku that Freeza destroyed Planet Vegeta and killed their fathers, and asks him to defeat the tyrant. Freeza fatally shoots Vegeta; Goku buries him and prepares to fight. Goku's response reflects both his Earth upbringing and the suffering of Saiyans and Namekians.

## 6. Goku, Freeza and the failing planet

- **[87](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/87/) — the duel begins.** Piccolo asks Gohan and Krillin to leave the immediate danger; Gohan asks his father to survive. Goku and Freeza exchange attacks, including Freeza's psychic manipulation of the environment. Goku begins inferring that Freeza cannot sense ki in the way the Earth fighters do. Such a judgment concerns this opponent and fight.
- **[88](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/88/) — hidden strength.** Goku exploits Freeza's apparent reliance on sight and uses successive Kamehameha attacks, but Freeza remains largely unhurt and counters with psychic force. Piccolo suspects the tyrant is still holding back and could destroy the planet. On Earth, Chi-Chi keeps trying to reach Gohan and Brief reports that the rescue craft is being repaired.
- **[89](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/89/) — a second Body Change.** Freeza taunts Goku and sets himself a handicap. Bulma meets the Ginyu frog and makes a translation device; the device voices Change and the two exchange bodies. On Earth Brief explains a conversion of the rescue ship and the energy needed to launch it. This frog/Bulma episode is original-Z TV material; do not assume it is present in every version.
- **[90](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/90/) — mounting danger.** Freeza offers Goku a place in his force and makes claims about the fraction of his power needed. King Kai says Goku is already using tenfold Kaiō-ken. The Earth rescue launch fails. Ginyu, now in Bulma's body, reaches Gohan and the others and threatens another exchange. A character's percentage claim cannot be used to reconstruct an unmeasured base.
- **[91](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/91/) — ×20.** Ginyu tries to exchange with Piccolo but is returned to the frog; Toei credits Gohan with foiling the switch. Bulma regains her own body. Goku uses a twentyfold Kaiō-ken Kamehameha against Freeza. This is an explicit later use by source Goku, not a factor automatically acquired by either branch child.
- **[92](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/92/) — last resort.** The attack hurts Freeza's hand but does not stop him; King Kai judges Goku to have pushed beyond his limit in that particular fight. Goku begins gathering a Genki-dama from nearby celestial bodies. A captioned subjective sequence includes a reference to Bardock as Goku's father; its visual framing and transmission are not settled by text alone. The dead Ginyu Force members appear at King Kai's planet.
- **[93](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/93/) — time for the Genki-dama.** Goku continues to gather energy, and Freeza discovers the plan. Piccolo asks Gohan and Krillin for some of their remaining ki, leaving some for them, and intervenes to buy Goku time. Their explicitly captioned contribution goes to Piccolo in this sequence; it should not be silently reclassified as direct energy for the sphere. King Kai's planet has a separate TV-only confrontation.
- **[94](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/94/) — the enormous attack.** Piccolo, then Gohan and Krillin, help create a chance while Goku finishes gathering and releases the Genki-dama. The others initially believe Freeza has been defeated. On King Kai's planet the dead Earth fighters fight the dead Ginyu Force. The gathering time and support are circumstances, not universal technique requirements.
- **[95](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/95/) — Super Saiyan.** Freeza survives, grievously injures Piccolo and kills Krillin. Gohan wants to help; Goku says Piccolo is still barely alive and, furious at Krillin's death and the other losses, becomes a Super Saiyan. The Earth fighters defeat the four dead squad members in the TV afterlife subplot. The anger, casualty and transformation are source-specific; none is an obligatory branch outcome.
- **[96](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/96/) — protect Piccolo.** Goku tells Gohan to take the still-living Piccolo away, noting his linked life with Kami. Gohan worries about his father but follows through. Goku confronts Freeza with the new transformation and retains protective intentions even while struggling with intense anger.
- **[97](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/97/) — the five-minute threat.** Freeza attacks the planet's core and says Namek will explode in about five minutes; the official synopsis says he held back to avoid destroying himself immediately. Gohan looks for Bulma and a way out. Kami's survival is used as evidence that Piccolo remains alive. The on-screen episode sequence stretches the declared countdown; do not treat screen runtime as a stopwatch.
- **[98](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/98/) — the fight Goku chooses.** King Kai urges an attack before Freeza reaches full power. Goku chooses instead to face that power, wanting to defeat Freeza and avenge Krillin while believing Gohan can escape. His motive involves both grief and the desire to test himself; neither element cancels the other.
- **[99](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/99/) — two dragons, one plan.** Mr. Popo gathers Earth's restored balls. Kami explains that Earth's dragon can revive a group matching a condition, with the discussed group-wish limit of deaths within one year and no natural deaths. King Kai proposes reviving those killed by Freeza's group to restore the Great Elder and obtain the unused Namek wish. Kami's suggestion that an unnaturally shortened life might return is tentative until tested. Gohan insists on bringing Piccolo and asks Bulma to wait for Goku.
- **[100](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/100/) — Gohan returns.** Shenron confirms revival of those on Namek killed by Freeza's group; the Great Elder and Dende return and Porunga reappears. Believing Goku dead, Gohan asks Bulma to take Piccolo away and goes back to delay Freeza rather than lead him to Earth. Goku reappears and tells him to leave. A character's mistaken belief is not the same as a death.
- **[101](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/101/) — the third wish.** King Kai intends to evacuate everyone except Freeza. Goku insists that he too be excluded so their fight can be settled; the change is accepted, and Dende makes the Namekian-language wish. Porunga transports those on Namek other than Goku and Freeza to Earth. The Elder says he will soon die naturally again.
- **[102](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/102/) — two fighters left.** On Earth the survivors worry about Goku; Vegeta instead hopes to become strongest and invites Gohan to join him. Gohan refuses and affirms his bond with Goku and Earth. The Elder discusses the contemporary Earth dragon's limitation on reviving the same person twice. On Namek the dramatic countdown continues below two and then one minute.
- **[103](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/103/) — victory without execution.** Goku observes that Freeza's maximum-power ki is fading. Feeling he has proven the point, he offers to leave rather than kill him; Freeza refuses to accept the defeat. Goku's mercy exists alongside his earlier anger and his desire for the contest.
- **[104](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/104/) — the tyrant's own attack.** Freeza's pursuing energy discs cut him instead of Goku. Freeza begs for help and Goku gives him some energy. A marked retrospective has Nappa tell Vegeta and Raditz that Freeza destroyed Planet Vegeta; Vegeta says he already knew. That account is in tension with Vegeta's reaction to Dodoria's episode-49 revelation, and the captions alone do not settle the framing.
- **[105](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/105/) — succession and final attack.** On Earth the revived Great Elder reaches his natural end and names Mūri his successor. Vegeta identifies a village he killed as missing and attributes its exclusion to the wording of the resurrection wish. Dende explains that Namek's dragon can revive someone more than once, probably not after natural death. Goku tells Freeza to escape, but Freeza attacks from behind and Goku retaliates.
- **[106](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/106/) — Namek explodes.** Goku seeks escape in Freeza's damaged ship as the planet breaks apart. King Kai concludes that Freeza has died and that Goku also failed to escape; Yamcha carries the latter news to Earth. Gohan refuses to accept Vegeta's claim about his father and resists him. These are the observers' assessments, not conclusive proof of either fighter's ultimate fate. Toei's live episode-106 synopsis is truncated, so it cannot by itself supply its ending.

## 7. The delayed aftermath

- **[107](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/107/) — the living and the relocated.** Vegeta proposes bringing the supposedly dead fighters' souls to Earth before revival, avoiding resurrection in empty space. The Namekians live temporarily on Earth. After **130 days**, the first successful Porunga wish brings Krillin's soul and reveals that Goku is alive; the second revives Krillin, and a proposed transport for Goku fails because he refuses. Yamcha is revived with the third successful wish. After another 130 days, Chiaotzu and Tien are revived individually and the third wish relocates the Namekians to a new home. Goku's precise escape route is not explained here. The end of Toei's live synopsis contains an unrelated fragment from the episode-66 Recoome account; exclude that corruption.
- **[108](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/108/) and later — separately labelled context.** Toei places a new Garlic Jr. story after the Namek events. Original-Z [119](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/119/) later reveals that Freeza survived and returned with King Cold; [123](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/123/) explains Goku's escape in a Ginyu Force pod, his arrival on Yardrat and his learning of Instant Movement. These are **later source revelations**, not knowledge that the people at episode 106 or the present branch possess. No systematic full-audiovisual review of these episodes is claimed here.

## Reading clocks safely

The narrator's 34 days in episode 44 starts at the original Earth party's departure. The 18 days in episode 40 starts when Vegeta leaves Earth. Goku's six-day trip is a different journey; episode 63's sixth day is tied to his departure. Episode 60's nearly four days lacks a specified starting event in the cited line. Episode 107 has two separate 130-day intervals after Namek's destruction. Freeza's approximately five minutes is an in-story statement in an extended television sequence. Do not combine them into one exact calendar without further evidence.
