## Ordered source inventory prepared before drafting

### Z 49

1. Recap: Gohan attacks in anger; Krillin flees with the saved child and blinds Dodoria. Dodoria searches the hiding place without a scouter, blasts the area, assumes the children are dead and departs. The trio survive, confirm Dende can fly and exchange thanks.
2. Bulma reaches Dr. Brief by communication and hears that a ship carrying Goku has launched and is expected in six days; Goku eats and continues gravity training aboard it.
3. Vegeta ambushes Dodoria, recognizes the value of the lost scouters, destroys his own and applies the ki-sensing technique he learned from the Earth fighters. The enemies argue about power and Vegeta's recent growth.
4. Dodoria bargains for his life by revealing that Freeza, not a meteor, destroyed Planet Vegeta because he feared Saiyan growth and collective resistance. Vegeta is angry at being used, not presented as simply grieving his lost world; he kills Dodoria. Goku trains; preview and ending follow.

### Z 50

1. Recap and Vegeta's satisfaction in defeating Dodoria; he senses and investigates two approaching energies. Gohan, Krillin and Dende approach Bulma's cave and hide with suppressed ki. A fish's energy diverts Vegeta; the three survive and reunite with Bulma.
2. Bulma has a capsule house and relays Goku's arrival estimate and his refitted infant Saiyan ship; Gohan and Krillin rejoice.
3. Goku trains, receives a warning from Brief, goes outside with a tether and magnetic boots to repair a hull, becomes stuck in glue and redirects a collision course by Kamehameha. Oolong and Puar react at Brief's end.
4. Vegeta senses an intact Namekian village, approaches its elder and demands the ball. The village refuses; Vegeta attacks. Preview and ending follow.

### Z 51

1. Recap and Vegeta's continuing attack on the other village, including resisting adults and children.
2. Bulma offers food; Dende explains Namekians need water and grow ajissa trees to restore the world after an ancient climate disaster. Gohan and Krillin sense the other village's dying ki; Dende asks who the visitors are and asks them to save his people.
3. Vegeta overcomes the elder, takes the other village's ball and hides it underwater. Freeza, Zarbon and another soldier discuss Dodoria's absence and search strategy; Freeza departs with the balls he already holds in this original storyline.
4. The visitors explain Earth, Kami and their revival motive. Dende says the Great Elder survived the disaster and proposes going there. They discuss the remaining ball and the danger posed by Vegeta and Freeza; Krillin takes Dende toward the Great Elder while Gohan and Bulma remain.
5. Goku continues gravity training. King Kai contacts him about the four dead Earth fighters who have reached his planet; Yamcha, Piccolo, Tien and Chiaotzu speak, and Goku welcomes their future training. Preview and ending follow.

### Z 52

1. Goku and King Kai continue their discussion; Goku identifies the danger, King Kai locates Freeza, warns strongly against fighting him and asks the trainees to promise not to go. Piccolo privately has other plans.
2. Dende and Krillin travel to the Great Elder. Dende gives a five-hour estimate; Vegeta senses their movements, prompting the pair to hide. He is diverted toward Zarbon instead; Krillin then carries Dende faster.
3. King Kai's living-comedy material: the trainees must tell a joke; Yamcha coaches Tien, King Kai begins training and Piccolo wins approval through an unintentional pun. Bubbles is set as their first task.
4. Vegeta confronts Zarbon, claims he has killed Dodoria, explains his rivalry with Freeza and attacks. They exchange blows; Zarbon begins to disclose an unshown greater power. Preview and ending follow.

### Z 71 — separate, brought-forward first-form Freeza reference

1. Recap: Goku's extraordinary output and Ginyu; Freeza approaches the Great Elder. Freeza threatens Nail to coerce the elder about the wish procedure. Nail chooses a more distant battlefield to protect the elder and buy time for Dende; Freeza accepts only so much distance.
2. Freeza hears Nail's 42,000 and states his own first-form 530,000, then says he will use one hand. Nail attacks; Freeza mutilates him; Nail regenerates an arm, with the scouter showing reduced strength, and continues fighting. These particular figures, wounds and outcome do not automatically transfer to this branch.
3. Elsewhere Bulma encounters dinosaurs; Gohan and Krillin rescue her and take her radar. She learns Goku has arrived.
4. Ginyu deliberately wounds himself, uses Body Change on Goku and leaves with Jeice. Goku, now in the injured body, struggles to pursue and worries about his friends and Chi-Chi. Preview, ending and unrelated trailing fragment follow.

### Z 77 — separate, brought-forward group-fight and revelation reference

1. Recap and an enraged Freeza confronting Vegeta, Gohan, Krillin and Dende after the source wish opportunity is lost. Vegeta insists the three fighters can win together, speaks of Gohan's increasing potential and confronts Freeza.
2. Piccolo meets dying Nail; they discuss Kami, identity and voluntary assimilation. Nail asks Piccolo to stop Freeza and Piccolo accepts the fusion; his power changes. These source prerequisites are not present at the Chapter 39 frontier.
3. Freeza attacks the group; Vegeta presses him and demands transformation. Freeza says he attacked Planet Vegeta and did not need to transform even against its king. The source transformation begins. Preview, ending and trailing fragment follow. A branch disclosure must travel by whoever actually hears it.

### Z 88 — separate, brought-forward psychokinesis and Earth-parent reference

1. Recap of Goku and Freeza fighting. On Earth Chi-Chi insists on rescuing Gohan while the others argue; Dr. Brief reports a repaired ship and the group prepares to go. These original transport premises are not established in the branch.
2. Freeza praises Goku's strength but declares superiority. Goku realizes Freeza is relying on his eyes rather than sensing ki; from underwater he sends two Kamehamehas as distractions and strikes. Freeza is marked by dust but not injured in that source action.
3. Freeza uses psychokinesis to attack, lifts a large island-like mass and later immobilizes Goku in a sphere. He attacks and detonates the trap; Goku escapes at speed. Piccolo, Gohan and Krillin react, and Piccolo notes that both combatants still have more to give. Preview, ending and an unrelated trailing fragment follow. A particular escape does not establish universal psychic immunity, and the source's final-form level is not automatically the branch's first-form output.

## Physical, knowledge and power audit

- **Geography:** the boys begin on Freeza's two sides of the village path. Goshin draws the fight over open ground to a newly shown broad lake; Gohan returns to join him. Muri and Piccolo collect the wrapped ball from the house through the rear door. Muri, Bulma, Cargo and the older resident move along the wall toward a hollow; two people have emerged, but other houses and outlying workers are not fully accounted for. Muri has the ball, not Freeza, and no wish has been attempted.
- **Soldiers and ship:** Piccolo breaks the last working *village* scouter. The nearby soldiers are knocked down or left still, but none is confirmed permanently confined. Dodoria is unconscious and breathing at the stony rise; Gohan does not bind him. At the closed intact ship Zarbon retains his own scouter, sends his sole nearby companion to the village, briefly tracks that soldier at 1,200 and stays by the hull. Piccolo intercepts that soldier on Dende's route after the man voice-reports a Namekian at 240 and a child at 18 to Zarbon. Piccolo’s displayed rise to 2,600 is not voiced before the scouter breaks; possible additional telemetry is unknown. No route or Great Elder destination is spoken. The other Zarbon-assigned soldier remains last shown tending the original landing scouts. None of this guarantees future ship or traveller safety.
- **Exact relay:** Piccolo reports that Freeza attacked both boys, Goshin was fighting him, and Gohan had stopped another fighter and was then with Piccolo. He says the ship was found, Bulma was with two children, and the elder's people were being moved. King Kai tells Goku, Chi-Chi and Krillin precisely this bounded, already-dated report; he did not hear either boy directly, and Piccolo did not specify whether they were hurt. They do not hear Freeza's later planet claim. Chi-Chi asks King Kai to relay a message if he can; that message's delivery is not shown.
- **Growth and technique:** Goshin's previously retained first-ape and later gains remain. While controlling the separate pieces of a lifted shelf and fighting, his body responds more quickly and his body and ki become stronger; the unnumbered gain already exists before his ×10 use and is distinct from it. The Kaiō-ken temporarily multiplies chosen unboosted output tenfold, not his permanent strength or a previous tripled state. Gohan blocks, disables Dodoria, breaks and redirects rock, independently attacks Freeza and gives Piccolo information. Freeza says his own battle power is 530,000 and that he is holding back; after his small cheek cut he raises his ki and pushes Gohan but cannot push the boosted Goshin back. The figure is his self-statement, not a branch scouter measurement or a numerical reading of current output. The chosen unboosted output is more than the earlier 68,600, so ×10 implies more than 686,000 on the same scale; this is arithmetic, not a new scouter display (`O293`).
- **Tails and unresolved capabilities:** both brothers remain ordinary-sized and tailed. They keep the unexamined sky out of view. Goshin considers his already-known artificial-moon ability and chooses ×10 in that moment, partly because he would first warn tailed Gohan; the choice is neither inability nor a fixed delay. No complete Goshin-made moon, new transformation, universal psychic immunity, precise sensing radius, unaided-space survival or compulsory weakness is shown.
- **Final frontier:** beside one another over the lake, Gohan and Goshin move against Freeza's raised hand after he has increased his pressure; Goshin's red ×10 aura remains active. His claimed 530,000 and destruction of Planet Vegeta are heard by them; the past is not independently witnessed by them, and no next strike or fight result is shown. Piccolo and Dende are flying toward, not at, the Great Elder. Muri has his wrapped ball, and Bulma, Cargo and the older resident are moving with him; wider village safety is unsettled. Zarbon is beside the ship, his sent soldier lies unconscious in the trees after the partial report. Goku still watches Vegeta and calls Tien to discuss the guard; Chiaotzu remains with Nappa. Chi-Chi and Krillin travel toward the laboratory while Puar watches her telephone. There is no ready second vessel, secure Saiyan custody or completed Namek rescue.
- Raiti and Zākuro's promise remains unfulfilled. The refugee children's later safety, the broader Namekian outcome and acceptance remain open.
