# Z episodes 1–10: ordered comparison and immediate forward map

This is a **planning inventory**, not a scene-perfect transcription. A retained item is present in a current draft, not necessarily approved by the reader. The summaries do not prove the quality of a scene or the exact on-screen choreography. Chapters 4–9 are provisional; the revised wording of 1–3 awaits review. **Chapter 10 is rejected**, so none of its invented consequences is credited as accepted coverage.

## Evidence key

- **CAP:** local Japanese SRT dialogue, narration or labelled sound. Its original provider and precise cut are unverified. It cannot establish silent visual action; timestamps below belong to this particular local file, not a verified broadcast master.
- **TOEI:** official short episode synopsis and, where explicitly noted, its one promotional still. A short synopsis is not a full episode.
- **SEC:** independent secondary guide/rewatch description. It may be incomplete or wrong; separate the particular report from what captions establish.
- **AU:** authored two-twin material or altered outcome.
- **Shown / changed / earlier / not yet validly adapted** describe the *current drafts*, not story approval. A branch-dependent baseline event can be inapplicable without the story being required to recreate it elsewhere; independent threads should still be considered, not silently forgotten.

For the detailed existing inventories and exact source IDs see `dbz-twin/canon/SCENE_COVERAGE_001.md`, `SCENE_COVERAGE_002_003.md` and `SCENE_COVERAGE_004.md` through `009.md`. The table below condenses their source order and adds editorial judgment; it does not convert a secondary source into a primary one.
