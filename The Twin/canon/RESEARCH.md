## 2 · Hybrid potential: what Vegeta really says

At **Z 5, 22:22–23:13**, Vegeta calls Gohan's reading abnormally high even compared with Saiyan children, infers that mixing Saiyan and Earthling blood can produce children with powerful strength, and worries about those children growing and gaining still more power. He refuses Nappa's proposal to breed more because they could threaten the Saiyans' position (`C142`). This is direct support for the user's point: **hybrid potential and continued growth are part of the story, not an invention made for Goshin.**

It is also Vegeta's inference from limited evidence. It is **not** a formula, an assertion that every hybrid outranks every full Saiyan, proof that every hybrid beats every other hybrid of the same age, or a fixed growth rate. The official site's biologist explicitly offers heterosis as a *possible thought experiment*—useful commentary, not a canonical genetic mechanism [9](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_831.html).

## 3 · What this means for Goshin

These are **authored serial rules**, not discoveries about a nonexistent canon character:

1. At Kame House, **before Raditz takes both children**, the scouter reads **about 400** on Goshin (`O005`, Chapter Two). This is one snapshot. My earlier claim that it occurred in the first training week was wrong. It is neither a permanent level nor an absolute minimum.
2. His Saiyan/Earthling potential, training, experience, recovery and adaptation can contribute to continued growth. **Six months of survival may strengthen him**, even before formal lessons begin. No fixed acceleration rate or automatic end-of-arc number has been established.
3. The Adaptation Talent lets him learn from a demonstrated technique unusually quickly and retain what he has learned. He **flies**, has techniques, and shows them on the page. It is more than a passive “does not forget” clause. Observation does not supply a free exact battle-power number, an unknown technique's full hidden prerequisites, or immunity to an injury.
4. Retained skill is not constant performance. Suppression, fatigue, injury, distraction or a tail grip can lower a reading or interrupt a technique. A learned flight technique is not forgotten if a tired child falls.
5. The user's standing direction is that Goshin is **never less capable than Gohan**. That is an authored requirement, not an equation supposedly proved by Vegeta's comment. It does not erase Gohan's training, agency or contribution, and it does not automatically set Goshin above Goten, Trunks, Piccolo, Nappa or every future opponent.
6. **No Nappa ceiling or mandatory canon deaths.** A second capable child changes the danger, available choices and timing. Work out consequences from the actual scene. A canon fighter's original role is not a wall that forces the same outcome.
7. **Era lock remains:** the initially four-year-old boy has ordinary ki, not god ki; outside time and the unaccepted chamber year do not make a new exact present age known. The separately ruled future divine progression, SS4 route and divine children remain future authored rules; this research does not erase them.
8. **Mastery and foundations, by user direction:** Goshin's intentional training first masters or perfects what he already has and seriously develops fundamentals (`O104`, `O105`). The user clarified that instinctive copying in an emergency remains possible. Mastery can actually be achieved; the former contrary blanket claim is withdrawn (`R-26`). These are creative rules for the OC, not claims about canon or a numerical power multiplier.

The earlier `O059` and `O060` extrapolations imposed Nappa as a ceiling and a universal ranking against all hybrids. They were mine, unsupported, and are withdrawn as constraints.

The [Kanzenshuu anime power guide](https://www.kanzenshuu.com/battle-power/anime/) separates on-screen cases from guidebook numbers. Its episode-22 and episode-65 entries are particularly useful. The original Japanese subtitle says **over** 20,000, even where a heading in that guide rounds the figure.

**Collateral is not calculated by dividing a landscape by a scouter number.** Piccolo says he blew away the moon in ep. 8; Nappa makes his “greeting,” and the episode later reports East City gone and attributes the disaster to Saiyans (ep. 22, 04:30–05:33, 07:11–07:20, 09:30–10:25). The speech establishes those claims, not every frame of the attack. Nappa **4,000** is a guidebook value, not an on-screen reading of that particular blast. Piccolo's **408** was read in a different scene before the moon incident; it is not an attack reading taken at the moon. Those incidents show why simplistic number-to-damage conversions fail; they do **not** prove bodies always cause little damage, that all large feats need a long charge, or that a larger number guarantees victory.

For each fight, decide the combatants' current condition, demonstrated capability, attack used, target, control, environment and consequences. A localized hit can leave a small mark; a deliberately expansive attack can devastate a city. Both require clear cause and effect, not an arbitrary rule that children may only crack stones.

## 5 · Training, flight, injury and the tail

**Training is not a single switch.** Piccolo tells Gohan to survive alone for six months before lessons (ep. 7, 04:54–06:45). At roughly six months, the formal sparring starts and Piccolo teaches him to sense his target (ep. 17, 04:16–05:37). By ep. 19, 10:26, **158 days** remain before the Saiyans. Goku's year includes the journey to King Kai; it is misleading to say all of that year was spent on the small planet.

Vegeta says Saiyans grow stronger through fighting and after surviving the edge of death (ep. 49, approximately 17:34–20:44); in ep. 83, 09:41–10:41, he asks Krillin to injure him before Dende heals him and says injuring himself will not produce the intended effect. Those are character statements about the case, not a prescription that every injury buys a fixed gain. Goku's dangerous gravity training and senzu recovery are separately shown in the ep. 59 captions, 17:12–18:22. Severe wounds can kill, recovery takes resources, and no evidence establishes a universal numerical increment.

**Flight.** My former `R-24` wrongly gave Goku's line to Piccolo: **Goku**, on Snake Way in ep. 6, says at 24:23 that he has used too much stamina to fly. This demonstrates fatigue, not Piccolo's inability. Piccolo's own flight is independently established earlier. In ep. 207, 04:22–06:19, Gohan describes flight as controlling ki and teaches Videl; Goten starts floating and then flies while Videl is also learning (16:25–19:54). At ep. 208, 09:15–09:18, Gohan says Videl has become fairly free in flight after about ten days. These individual examples do not set a universal learning time.

**Tail.** Piccolo considers pulling Gohan's tail in ep. 8, 23:35–23:46. In ep. 9, 05:18, Gohan finds it missing; in ep. 18, 09:14–09:19, Piccolo says he pulled it out six months earlier and it has regrown. The old conclusion that Piccolo left it attached was false. Vegeta says he and Nappa overcame the tail-grip weakness through **training** (ep. 27, 07:48–07:57). Goshin keeping his tail is our divergence; watching a grip once does not automatically give immunity. He can learn guards and train resistance, while a successful grip may still matter.

**Moon and Great Ape.** In the unchanged anime baseline Piccolo says he destroyed the moon (ep. 8); in the current authored branch it remains intact (`O119`). A moon projection from Goku's pod appears in ep. 18; Vegeta creates an artificial moon in ep. 31, and Gohan's regrown tail allows another transformation in the ensuing fight. Thus the destroyed natural moon did **not** eliminate every Great Ape route. Whether and when the natural moon is restored in a given continuity requires its own evidence. GT's tail/Golden Ape path, *Daima*'s Neva-enabled portrayal and the serial's authored future SS4 route must be kept distinct.

## 6 · The Dragon Balls, the clock and separate continuities

- **Earth, Saiyan arc:** Piccolo's death also affects Kami and Earth's Dragon Balls; these are not the same set as Namek's. In ep. 21, 17:53–18:09, Shenron says a wish to defeat the Saiyans exceeds his creator's power.
- **Fusion:** Piccolo and Kami fuse during the Android/Cell period; ep. 172, 20:27–21:35, Goku says this caused Earth's Dragon Balls to disappear and seeks a new guardian. Do not misplace the fusion at ep. 172 merely because that later dialogue discusses it.
- **Dende:** At ep. 173, 16:09–17:25, Dende says *new* Dragon Balls would take around **100 days to make**, but the old stones and dragon model let him revive the existing set immediately. He explains a three-wish possibility and a **two-wish configuration** for restoring many people at once. At ep. 192, 18:27–20:56, Shenron offers two wishes; he cannot revive Goku again, while the group identifies Namek's Porunga as another possible route. Goku then asks not to be revived (21:07–22:24). The earlier “100-day recharge” statement was wrong. Do not silently generalise these rules to every later version.
- **Deaths:** Goku does **not** die on Namek. He sacrifices himself against Cell in the Cell Games, and only afterwards chooses not to be revived. Both events and the ability to make wishes may diverge in the serial.
- **Chronology is contested:** the standard guidebook-derived table places Raditz in Age 761, Trunks's warning in Age 764 and the Androids/Cell in Age 767, with retrospective dating and acknowledged contradictions [5](https://www.kanzenshuu.com/wiki/Dragon_World_Timeline). **Three years run from Trunks's warning, not directly from Raditz.** The earlier ages 8 and 9 were oversimplified. In the anime captions of ep. 171, 05:33–05:51, Chi-Chi says Gohan turned **11 while in the Room of Spirit and Time** and the current day is not his actual birthday. He later attends school from home: ep. 200, 16:44–16:54, he explicitly says he **commutes from home**, not that he moved to Satan City. The guidebook ages, the time chamber, and later age statements do not mesh neatly; preserve the conflict instead of selecting a spurious exact age for Goshin. The author directs a physical chamber year for Goshin before the Saiyan arrival (`O155`); the full lived year is shown in unaccepted Chapter 22, and unaccepted Chapter 23 stages the landing later the same morning as the exit. This early stay does not decide his participation in Gohan's later Cell-era visit.
- **Room ratio, source and boundary:** Dragon Ball Official's descriptive discussion says one year inside equals one day outside and mentions tenfold Earth gravity (`W-79`, `C217`). This article is not a review of every episode-specific room rule. The physical chamber is distinct from the selected Chapter 18's mind-travel exercise; entry, provisions and authored training outcomes must be established on page.
- **Other World tournament:** Goku is initially announced the winner, but the Grand Kai then disqualifies both Goku and Pikkon (ep. 199, 20:16–22:22). The old summary claiming an unqualified tournament win was incomplete.
