# Chapter 44 — Where the Wave Turns

*Anime episode reference: Dragon Ball Z, Episodes 83–84 (Freeza's final change and the danger to Dende, adapted to the changed branch), with the separate Earth-side rescue motive from Episode 88.*

## The Hand and the Tail

Freeza twists his captured wrist toward the trees.

Goshin pulls it back. The red light of the Kaiō-ken streams around his shoulders, and the water below them rises in a long ridge. He can feel Gohan moving away beneath the branches. Piccolo is on Freeza's other side, his torn sleeve dark against his arm.

"You won't reach him," Goshin says.

"I have another hand."

Freeza raises it. Piccolo fires before the finger can point at the shore. The shot strikes Freeza's forearm and turns it outward; a narrow beam cuts across the open lake instead of the trees.

Freeza's tail lashes toward Piccolo. Goshin catches it with his ki, but the tail jerks him sideways as Freeza tears it free. His grip on the wrist loosens. Freeza drives his knee into Goshin's stomach and pulls his hand away.

Goshin folds around the blow. Before Freeza can follow it, Piccolo stretches his good arm across the gap and catches Goshin by the back of his shirt. He pulls the boy out of the path of the next shot and lets him go at once.

"I had him," Goshin says, taking a breath.

"He still has both hands."

"And a tail."

Piccolo looks at the tail coiled around Goshin's waist. "I noticed."

Goshin grins despite himself. Then Freeza comes through the spray, and the grin vanishes.

Goshin meets his first punch on both palms. The force carries him backward over the water. He plants one foot against Freeza's middle, pushes away and swings around him. His heel strikes the cracked plate on Freeza's shoulder. A white fragment breaks off, and Freeza turns sharply after him.

Goshin is already moving again. He strikes Freeza across the face, catches the answering blow on his forearm and drives him farther from the shore. His hurt shoulder protests, but the arm holds. The strength that has been growing through the fight answers him there as well.

Freeza stops above the broken island. His eyes flick toward the ridge, then return to Goshin.

"You were very impressive a moment ago," he says. "Will you do that for me again?"

"I'm not doing it for you."

"No? Then perhaps I should find someone who will persuade you."

Two lights leave Freeza's hands. One streaks toward Piccolo; the other climbs toward the ridge.

Piccolo drops beneath the first and strikes it upward with a short blast. Goshin catches the second light from below. It pushes at his palms, hot and hard. He turns his body and sends it across the lake, where it bursts against an empty stretch of water.

The instant his hands are occupied, Freeza pulls away. A brighter pressure gathers around him, flattening the spray beneath his feet.

"He's changing again!" Piccolo calls.

Goshin remembers the last change. Freeza had taken his blows and kept going. Goshin is not going to stand here and watch him do it.

He draws the red rush higher through his body.

"Kaiō-ken, times thirty!"

The greater force tears a shout from him. Water leaps around his boots as he drives through the light. Freeza brings his arms up, but Goshin's fist breaks through the guard and strikes his chest. Freeza is thrown across the island, gouging a fresh trench through its broken surface.

Goshin follows. His second blow knocks Freeza into the lake beyond it.

For an instant the water closes over the taller fighter. Then a column erupts. Goshin turns his face away from the spray and reaches through it with his ki. He finds Freeza moving downward, not toward the village.

Piccolo flies to the island's edge. "Did you stop him?"

"I don't know."

The pressure below them rises. Goshin dives before it can build any farther.

He catches Freeza beneath the surface. The long head is still there when Goshin strikes him, but the body under his hand is already changing. Freeza's tail coils around Goshin's leg; Goshin seizes it and pulls him away from the lakebed. Freeza wrenches loose and sends a burst between them.

The water drives them apart. Goshin shields his face, then pushes back through the churning blue. He cannot see Freeza in the cloud, but he can feel the enormous ki gathering inside it.

He lifts both hands and fires a short blast into the cloud, angling it away from the shore. Stone breaks beneath the water. A pale figure shoots past the edge of the light and rises toward the surface.

Goshin follows.

## The Lower Spring

Gohan takes the nearest trail through the grove. The bound soldier's shout fades behind him, swallowed by another distant impact.

The path divides around a low bank. Gohan starts left, then stops. A broad footprint has slid in the damp earth beside the other path, and a smaller one has stepped into it. He crouches to look. The marks lead downhill, toward the sound of water.

He follows them beneath the branches. His brother's ki burns fiercely beyond the ridge, and another huge presence presses against it. Gohan tries to find the smaller lives ahead without losing himself in the fight behind him.

A twig snaps.

He turns. A green hand reaches around the trunk of a tree, followed by Kado's companion.

"Gohan?"

"Where are the others?"

"This way. Keep below the bank."

She leads him to a narrow spring running between two rises. The older villager stands where the water bends. Cargo is sitting on a flat stone with his feet in the stream, while Dende kneels beside him and watches the path. Muri holds the wrapped ball against his chest.

Cargo scrambles up. "You found us!"

Gohan lands beside him. "I followed your footprints."

Cargo looks down at his wet feet. "I thought I was being quiet."

"You were. You just left your feet behind."

Cargo examines the prints, then looks at Gohan. The smallest part of a smile appears on his face.

Dende is watching the ridge. "Is Goshin all right?"

"He was fighting Freeza when I left. Piccolo is with him." Gohan lowers his voice. "Freeza heard me say that you can heal. He asked where you were."

Dende's hands close around the edge of his tunic. "Did you tell him?"

"No. He doesn't know where you are, and I didn't tell him what you look like. I don't know whether he saw you near the house. He might look for you. I'm sorry, Dende. I was so happy you came back that I didn't think."

Dende looks at Cargo, then at the water running past the stone. "I was happy too."

Muri rests the ball on the bank. "Freeza chose to threaten a child. That choice belongs to him, not to you."

Gohan swallows. Another tremor reaches the spring, rippling the surface around Cargo's ankles.

"We shouldn't stay where someone can follow the path," he says.

"There is a channel beyond the bend," Kado's companion says. "It runs between the banks. We can keep out of sight there."

The older villager lifts the bundle for Muri while he helps Cargo climb out of the water. Dende offers his hand to his brother, who takes it.

"Kado went to the ship with Bulma," the woman tells Gohan. "He was going to meet us here."

Gohan looks in the direction of the inlet. "I'll find them."

"And bring them to the channel?" Muri asks.

"If it's safe. I won't lead anyone after me."

The older villager settles the bundle in his arms while Muri puts a hand on Gohan's shoulder. "We can move ourselves. You do not have to carry all of us at once."

"I know. I want to find the people who aren't here too."

"So do I," the woman says. "Some were working beyond the houses. If they saw the soldiers, they may have hidden where we cannot see them."

Cargo tugs at her sleeve. "Kado said he would come back."

"He did." She kneels beside him. "We'll leave a mark where the channel turns. If he comes to the spring after we go, he can follow it."

She takes a pale stone from the water and places it on the bank, then sets a second beside it, pointing toward the channel. Cargo chooses a third, smaller one and adds it to the line.

"Now there are three," he says.

"Then Kado will know you helped."

Gohan watches the little marker. It will not protect the group if a soldier finds the trail, but it gives Kado a direction without putting anyone back in the open. He looks at Dende. "Stay with them."

Dende's face tightens. "I will. But if someone is hurt, I want to help."

"All right. Just don't go to Freeza."

"I won't." Dende looks at Muri. "If he comes here, should we hide the ball?"

"Keep each other in sight," Muri says. "The ball can be moved. I don't want anyone going back alone for it."

Cargo slips his hand into Dende's. "I'll stay with you."

"I'll stay with you too," Dende says.

Gohan rises only as high as the lower branches. He sees the group begin along the channel before he turns toward the inlet. He does not look above the trees.

## A Message Carried by Hand

Puar reaches Capsule Corporation with the folded paper held against his chest. The laboratory door is closed, and no one answers when he calls through it.

He flies around to the front entrance. A woman at the reception desk looks up as he comes in.

"I need to find Dr. Brief and Chi-Chi," Puar says. "Bulma called from Namek. I promised to tell them."

The woman reaches for the telephone. "They went with the police to look at the second pod. I don't know which street, but I can ask."

Puar waits, holding the paper so tightly that one corner crumples. The woman speaks to the police desk, gives the doctor's name and listens while someone checks. At last she writes an address and turns the pad toward him.

"They have an officer there. This is the cordoned site."

"Thank you!"

Puar is out of the door before she can put the telephone down.

At the pod, Chi-Chi watches the small light beside Dr. Brief's scanner. It goes out when he moves the instrument away from the hatch and comes on again when he brings it back.

"It changed at the same place," she says.

"It did." He makes a mark on his own sheet. "That tells us where the reading changes. It doesn't tell us whether the hatch is safe."

Yamcha is checking the barrier with the officer when a small shape appears between the buildings.

"Puar!"

Puar flies straight into his arms. Yamcha catches him, nearly losing the paper in the process.

"Bulma called!" Puar says. "She's on Namek. She said the ship was intact."

Chi-Chi is beside them before he can unfold the note. "The boys?"

Puar smooths the paper against Yamcha's arm. "She said Gohan and Piccolo went to help Goshin fight Freeza. They were on their feet when she last saw them. She couldn't see the fight from the ship."

"She didn't see Goshin herself?"

"Not then. She told me not to make it sound as if she knew more."

Chi-Chi closes her eyes for a moment. She had wanted to hear both boys' voices. Bulma's careful words are less than that, but they are words from someone who was there.

"Thank you for writing it down," she says. "Did she say she was hurt?"

"She said no. She was with a Namekian named Kado. Freeza's men had found the ship, so she didn't know how long they could stay."

Yamcha reads the note again. "She's all right," he says quietly. Then he looks toward the pod. "We still have to get them a way back."

Dr. Brief holds out his hand. Puar gives him the paper, and the doctor reads the line about Bulma's saved route.

"If I find a craft we can use, that could help us reach them. I can't promise the pod is one yet."

"Then keep testing it," Chi-Chi says. "Puar, will you go back to the house? She may call again."

"I'll listen for her."

Krillin touches the pocket where he has kept the Senzu. "I'll take this news to Goku. He ought to hear what Bulma actually said."

"Tell him that she couldn't see the fight," Chi-Chi says. "And tell him we're still trying."

Krillin nods. Puar takes his note back, folds it along the same crease and sets off toward the house. Krillin rises over the barrier and flies toward the quarry. Yamcha stays with Chi-Chi and the doctor as the scanner's light flickers again.

## The Branch That Moved

The ship's landing feet scrape against the inlet floor. Bulma stops the craft before its aerial can touch the shelf.

"Was that you or the rock?" she asks.

"The rock," Kado says. He has one hand on the edge of the window cover, holding it just far enough open to see outside. "There. The branches are moving again."

A soldier steps from the trees. His scouter is missing, and he keeps one arm close to his side. Kado recognizes him as the man they passed lying beneath the branches.

The soldier sees the ship. He raises his hand and fires.

"Down!" Kado cries.

Bulma drops behind the console. The shot strikes the bank beside the hatch, throwing earth across the window. The ship shudders on its landing feet.

"He can see us," Kado says.

"I noticed!" Bulma reaches for the controls. "Hold the cover so I can see the shelf. If I lift too fast, the aerial goes straight into it."

The soldier moves along the bank for a clearer shot. Kado pulls the cover wider. Bulma eases the ship sideways, putting the thickest part of the rock between them and the raised hand.

Another shot strikes the shelf. Dust spills over the window.

Kado leans toward the hatch. "I can try to draw him away."

"And leave you outside with him? Wait."

In the trees, Gohan hears the shot strike the shelf. He changes direction toward the sound and drops behind the soldier.

"Gohan!" Kado calls.

The soldier swings around. Gohan catches his wrist before he can fire. The man's other fist strikes Gohan's shoulder; Gohan barely moves. He turns the raised hand toward the empty bank and pulls the soldier away from the ship.

"Stop shooting!"

The man struggles. Gohan ducks his next swing and strikes him once at the side of the head with the edge of his hand. The soldier goes limp. Gohan catches him before his head hits the ground and lowers him carefully. The man's chest rises with a breath.

Bulma opens the hatch. "Are you hurt?"

"No. Is the ship all right?"

"It is if he didn't knock anything loose. I haven't checked the outside yet."

Kado comes down beside them. He brings a spare tie-down strap from inside the cabin, and together he and Gohan fasten the soldier's arms around a tree. Gohan tests the strap. The man's scouter is still broken farther up the path.

"He might get out when he wakes," Gohan says. "We shouldn't leave anyone close to him."

"Muri and the children?" Kado asks.

"They're by the lower spring, moving along a channel. They sent me to find you."

Kado lets out a breath. "My friend is with them?"

"Your companion from the grove is. She showed me the way."

He nods, then looks back toward the village. "Another friend was working beyond the houses. I haven't found her."

"We can look," Gohan says.

Bulma has come down from the hatch. "I called Earth. Puar answered. He was going to tell Chi-Chi and Dad what I knew."

"Did you speak to Mother?"

"No. And I told Puar I hadn't seen the fight. He knows the difference."

Gohan wants to hear his mother's voice. He looks at the aerial under the shelf and at the tied soldier, then toward the ridge where Goshin's ki is rising again.

"Freeza heard where Zarbon found the ship," he says. "If he comes back here, he might look for it."

Bulma follows his gaze. "Then this shelf isn't enough."

"The channel is wider beyond the spring," Kado says. "The banks are high, and there are trees on both sides. I don't know if your ship will fit."

"Neither do I." Bulma looks at the damaged bank, then at the window. "Show me from the air. I can move slowly and stop if it won't."

Gohan starts toward the hatch with them, then pauses. Freeza's presence has changed. It swells so suddenly that he braces a hand against the ship's side.

"Goshin?" Bulma asks.

"He's still there. Piccolo too." Gohan can feel them both, but the new pressure is different from the one he left at the lake. "Something happened to Freeza."

Kado looks toward the trees. "We should warn Muri."

"We will." Gohan climbs into the ship. "I'll watch for anyone following us."

Kado follows him up the steps and looks around the curved cabin. The beds and instruments are fastened close to the walls; a loose cloth has fallen across one of the seats.

"You came all the way from another world in this?"

"And we were going to go home in it," Bulma says. She picks up the cloth and wedges it under a strap. "I still intend to."

Gohan touches the back of the seat where Goshin had slept on the journey. He can feel his brother at the lake, but not what is happening to him. He wants to go straight back there. He also remembers the little line of stones beside the spring.

"If we find the others," he says, "could they come with us?"

"We can bring some people aboard and get them out of the open. I need to check the space and supplies before I promise how far we can take everyone." Bulma points to the window. "Kado, can you see the low bank from there?"

"Yes. There's a gap just beyond it."

Bulma closes the hatch. She keeps the covered window narrow while Kado shows her the first turn along the inlet. The ship rises a little, clearing its landing feet, and moves away from the damaged bank.

## A Smaller Shape

Goshin breaks the surface with a gasp. The red light rolls across the water as he rises. Piccolo is above the island, watching the widening ring of foam.

Freeza comes out of the lake a short distance away.

He is smaller than before. The long head and broken projecting plates are gone; his smooth white body and purple crown catch the light. Water runs from his tail. A dark scrape remains on one shoulder where Goshin struck him.

Piccolo's eyes narrow. "Don't judge him by the size."

"I wasn't going to," Goshin says.

The ki inside the smaller figure is vast. Goshin can feel more of it than he could in the last shape, though he has no way to know how much Freeza is keeping back.

Freeza looks down at his shoulder and touches the mark. "You have been troublesome."

"You could still leave."

"After all the effort you've made to entertain me?"

Goshin's hands rise. His breath is still rough, but the power he gathered answers when he calls it. He does not wait for another shot toward the shore.

He rushes Freeza and punches. Freeza catches his fist. The impact shoves both fighters through the air; their feet leave two furrows in the island before they rise clear of it.

Goshin drives his free hand toward Freeza's side. Freeza turns, and the blow clips his ribs instead of landing squarely. His tail comes around. Goshin blocks it, but Freeza pulls his fist free and strikes him across the jaw.

Goshin spins once and stops. The hit stings all the way to his ear. He goes back at Freeza with a yell, feints toward his face and drives a kick into his middle.

This time Freeza slides backward over the water. He catches himself and looks at the boy more carefully.

"You can still be hit," Goshin says.

Freeza smiles. "So can you."

He moves. Piccolo sees him leave one place, but the next motion is too quick for him to follow. He feels the collision instead: Goshin's red light jerks sideways as Freeza strikes his guard.

Goshin catches Freeza's wrist again, but not before a second blow lands against his shoulder. Pain runs down his arm. He refuses to let the hand go; he pulls Freeza close and head-butts him.

Freeza recoils. Goshin shakes his head, blinking water out of his eyes.

"That hurt," he mutters.

"You did it to yourself," Piccolo calls.

"I know!"

Freeza fires past him. The narrow beam is aimed at Piccolo, who has already started to move. Goshin brings his hands to his side. His father bent a wave around Raditz; Goshin has practised turning his own since then.

"Ka… me… ha… me… HA!"

The wave leaves his hands toward open water. Goshin pivots his palms, and the bright stream curves across Freeza's shot without crossing Piccolo's path. The two lights collide beyond the island. Goshin pushes the meeting point down and away from the ridge.

The lake erupts. Spray rises over Piccolo, and a rolling surge tears loose pieces from the island's edge. Goshin cuts off his wave before it can sweep farther toward the shore.

Freeza comes through the falling water. Piccolo sees the movement first this time: a pale shape angling toward Goshin's unguarded side. He fires at the water between them. The burst of spray does not hurt Freeza, but it gives Goshin an instant to turn.

Freeza's fist meets Goshin's palm. Goshin catches it and answers with a punch to the chest. Freeza blocks the next strike; Goshin turns his wrist, slips around the guard and lands another against the shoulder with the dark scrape.

Freeza's expression changes. He pulls back, and Goshin follows him across the water.

The red surge is fierce and demanding, but beneath it Goshin feels his body responding more readily than it did when Freeza first changed. His ki rises with that strength. He reaches Freeza sooner on the next pass and forces him to turn away from a third blow.

Freeza stops at the far edge of the broken island. For a moment he does not smile.

"You have become stronger again."

Goshin eases the Kaiō-ken to draw a fuller breath. The red light falls away for a moment, but the strength he found beneath it remains in his body, and his ki answers when he gathers it close. He wipes his mouth. He can taste blood, and his shoulder aches, but his legs are steady beneath him.

"I'm not finished."

Freeza glances beyond him, toward the eastern trees.

"Your ship was found near an inlet, wasn't it?"

Goshin's stomach tightens. He heard the soldier tell Freeza where Zarbon had found it. He does not know whether Bulma has moved it since.

Piccolo moves to his side. "He knows where it was."

"Perhaps your healer is there as well," Freeza says.

"You don't know that," Goshin replies.

"Then I should go and ask."

Freeza darts toward the ridge. Red light flares around Goshin again. He reaches Freeza and drives both hands into his side. They crash through the top of the broken island instead of crossing the shore. Piccolo fires at Freeza's trailing hand, forcing it away from the trees.

Freeza tears free and rises on the inlet side of them. His next shot rips through the ridge above an unoccupied stretch of water. Stone and soil rain into the lake.

Goshin turns the largest falling pieces aside. Piccolo watches Freeza rather than the debris.

"He's moving!"

Freeza shoots low along the shore. Goshin leaves the stones to fall into the water and races after him, with Piccolo close behind.

## What the Message Can Carry

At the quarry, Goku listens while Krillin repeats Bulma's words. Tien stands a few steps away, keeping Vegeta in view. Across the tracks, Chiaotzu remains beside Nappa.

"Bulma says they're on Namek," Krillin says. "She saw Gohan and Piccolo go to help Goshin, but she couldn't see the fight from the ship."

"And the ship?"

"It was intact when she called. She was with a Namekian named Kado, but Freeza's men had found them. That's what Puar wrote down."

Goku looks toward the open end of the quarry. He wants to be beside his sons. He wants to see the fighter Vegeta and King Kai have both warned him about. Neither wish puts a ship in front of him.

"Chi-Chi's still with Dr. Brief?"

"And Yamcha. They're testing the pod. The doctor doesn't know if it's safe to open."

Vegeta's tail stops against the stone. Namek. He had suspected that was where they meant to go, but now he knows where the ship went.

"If that planet has Dragon Balls," he says, "find one before Freeza does. If they have to be gathered like the ones on Earth, he can't make his wish without it."

Krillin studies him. "You don't know there are any."

"No. And I don't know how many he may already have. But if there is one left, get it away from him."

"You want it for yourself," Goku says.

"Of course I do. Give me a ship, and I'll help you stop him."

"You attacked people to get our Dragon Balls. I'm not handing you a ship or taking somebody else's ball for you."

Vegeta leans forward. "Then tell the people there to hide it themselves. Do you think Freeza will ask before he takes it?"

Goku's hands tighten. He remembers Goshin's palms against his on the ridge, and Gohan watching the dust as they practised. The boys had wanted to go to Namek, and he had wanted them to see it. He had not known Freeza would be there.

Vegeta watches him. Kakarot wants to reach his sons, and those sons are fighting Freeza. If they can hold him off, Vegeta might have time to find a ball that Freeza has missed. One ball could keep the tyrant from making his wish. Later, Vegeta would take the rest and make the wish himself. He does not know whether Namek's balls work like Earth's, or whether immortality would be enough to win—but he has no intention of leaving the chance to Freeza.

First, he needs a way off this rock.

Goku closes his eyes. *King Kai, Vegeta thinks Freeza might be after Dragon Balls. If you reach Piccolo again, tell him to warn the people there.*

*I'll try,* King Kai answers. *I can't promise when I'll reach him.*

Tien turns toward Chiaotzu. "Can you go to Kami's lookout? Ask him and Popo whether they can help us arrange a watch here."

Chiaotzu glances at Nappa. "What about him?"

"I'll come over. Goku and Krillin can stay with Vegeta."

Goku nods. "Tell Kami what King Kai relayed and what Bulma said. Dr. Brief is still testing the pod; we don't know whether we can use it."

"I will."

Tien crosses the tracks. Chiaotzu waits until he reaches Nappa, then rises above the quarry wall and heads for the lookout.

Krillin stands beside Goku. The prince watches them both, his hands free beneath the shade.

"Bulma's good at finding a way," Krillin says.

"She is." Goku looks toward the distant sky. "I want to find one too."

## Along the Bank

The ship moves between the trees at little more than walking speed. Kado points out a gap above the water, and Bulma angles the cabin through it. Gohan stands at the narrow window, looking only at the bank and the branches below it.

"The spring is past that bend," Kado says.

Gohan feels for the small presences ahead. Muri and the others are still moving together. Behind them, Goshin's ki is coming nearer, and Piccolo's is with it.

Then Freeza's presence moves toward the inlet.

"Bulma!" Gohan turns from the window. "He's coming this way!"

The water beside the ship begins to rise.
