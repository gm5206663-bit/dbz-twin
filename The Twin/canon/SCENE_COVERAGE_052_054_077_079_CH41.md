## Ordered source inventory prepared before drafting

### Z 52

1. In the source chronology Gohan and Bulma are separately concerned with the ball and hiding; Krillin accompanies Dende toward the Great Elder. Vegeta and Zarbon meet and begin their contest. Goku trains aboard a ship. None of those positions is automatically true of the branch.
2. The episode's search, travel and confrontation connect forward to 53–54. The branch has its own occupied ship, awake Vegeta on Earth, and Piccolo carrying Dende.

### Z 53

1. Pressed by Vegeta, Zarbon explains that his transformation increases his power but spoils an appearance he values. He chooses to transform rather than die. In the original confrontation he turns the fight around and leaves Vegeta beneath the water, without confirming his death.
2. Dende directs Krillin toward the Great Elder. The source conversation mentions Namek's three suns. They feel the distant battle, arrive at a conspicuous house high on a rock and meet its guardian Nail, who explains the elder's frailty. The elder welcomes his guests. Source Earth and afterlife intercuts involve Goku's training and dead fighters; those lives and positions have changed in this branch.

### Z 54

1. The Great Elder grieves his murdered children. Krillin seeks help and describes Earth's Dragon Balls; the elder recognizes the child of Katatz and asks to examine Krillin's past. His reading discloses the division associated with Earth's Kami and Piccolo in that source.
2. The elder offers his Dragon Ball, explains its dependence on his remaining life, and awakens Krillin's latent power; the boost has no universal multiplier. The source directly discusses awakening dormant power in another child. Krillin leaves; Dende remains. Later source events include another village, Zarbon's report and Vegeta's retrieval, and Freeza's order to summon the Ginyu Force. The order is not already issued in the present branch.

### Z 77–79, brought forward only where cause supports it

1. Z 77's original confrontation involves Vegeta, Krillin, Gohan and Freeza, with Dende and a separately arriving Piccolo; the changed branch has the living Piccolo traveling with Dende and no Vegeta or Krillin on Namek. A transformation threat is possible, not guaranteed by source order.
2. In Z 78 Freeza transforms to a much larger second form, boasts of power over one million and demonstrates a dangerous rise in power. The episode also recounts his attack on the Saiyans; that testimony was already selectively brought forward in Chapter 40. This is not a measurement of his output in another form or a fixed required number for the branch.
3. In Z 79 source Freeza severely wounds Krillin. Gohan's fury produces a dangerous independent attack; Freeza survives and raises his own ki. Dende's position and Krillin's source injury do not transfer to the branch. Z 80 was reread for context on what follows, but those later rescues and healing are not assumed here.

## Branch reconciliation after drafting

1. **Lake confrontation, rather than Z 52's source positions:** Chapter 41 begins at Chapter 40's raised hand. Both brothers act, and Goshin's established ×10 genuinely overpowers the first form in their immediate exchange. Gohan diverts attention; Goshin prevents Freeza's hand from following him when he goes to the village. The original Goku ship-training and dead-fighter intercuts do not describe this living, Earth-bound roster.
2. **Zarbon, Z 52–53:** Zarbon has been ordered to preserve the visitors' ship. After his soldier's partial report goes silent, he scans two far-off combatants, reading **1,100,000** and **480,000**; these are authored snapshots of ×10 Goshin and first-form Freeza, respectively, which Zarbon does not identify. He leaves the closed ship unattended and discovers the small evacuation group. He reads Gohan at **41,200**, confronts him and uses shots toward the civilians to buy time to transform. Muri and the stronger, protective Gohan act; Gohan's surge and technique knock the transformed Zarbon unconscious and breathing. His scouter breaks. There is no source Vegeta–Zarbon fight or assumed source numerical value here. Automatic telemetry is not established either way. Zarbon is not secured, and the ship is not yet recovered by the travellers.
3. **Great Elder and Nail, Z 53–54:** Piccolo, not Krillin, carries Dende to the high house; Nail questions them and remains to guard the elder on request. Piccolo actually tells the elder about Kami and Earth's Dragon Balls and consents to a reading. The elder recognizes Katas's child and describes the loss of Katas, while acknowledging he does not know whether Katas learned of the child's safe arrival. The branch's Kami and the present Piccolo are alive; the elder describes the division and Piccolo's subsequent relationship without treating him as the first expelled Piccolo. Piccolo chooses the elder's offer to awaken his own latent strength; an unnumbered genuine increase persists, but no formula or equality with Freeza follows. The elder states that his balls will disappear with his death. His own ball remains above his chair: he has not given it away, and no wish or complete rule set is established. Dende chooses to accompany Piccolo toward Muri; they remain at the high house as the chapter cuts away. No Nail assimilation, Gohan/Krillin awakening, Dende healing or invented death is imported.
4. **Freeza's change, Z 78:** His first-form attempts at targeted shots give him an opening; Goshin actively intercepts and attacks his developing shield and body, not passively waiting through an invulnerable transformation. Freeza completes a larger form and claims over one million. Its actual current value is not scanned; physical exchanges show that this form overcomes the previously advantageous ×10 output. Goshin chooses ×20 by doubling his ongoing tenfold rush, not by multiplying an already boosted state by twenty. His own body and ki genuinely strengthen beneath the temporary technique. He gains a clear advantage and chips a horn. Freeza deliberately aims a large projectile toward the village to draw him away, then escapes beneath the lake while Goshin diverts it. No planet, civilian or fighter is stated dead; no third form is yet shown.
5. **Gohan's protective urgency, Z 79:** The source Krillin casualty is impossible here because Krillin is on Earth. Gohan instead chooses to defend Muri's group against Zarbon; his independent power, anger, technique and retained unnumbered growth affect that encounter. He and the others then actively guide two more villagers toward the hollow. He plans to rejoin Goshin after helping; he has not abandoned the uncounted residents, and no complete evacuation is claimed. His output is not stated equal to Freeza's.
6. **Earth and remaining source threads:** Goku and Tien continue the actual custody discussion; Chiaotzu remains by Nappa and Vegeta is awake. Chi-Chi, Krillin, Yamcha and Dr. Brief prepare to inspect the second pod, but no inspection result or usable rescue craft is claimed. Puar remains by the home telephone. King Kai's requested message to the boys remains unshown. The original source Ginyu Force summons, Vegeta's retrieval, ball collection and later Z 80 rescue/healing are deferred possibilities or changed circumstances, not events already completed in this branch. The Raiti/Zākuro promise and refugee children's outcome remain open.

## Exact handoff and scale

Goshin, tailed and ordinary-sized, dives after the still-active transformed Freeza beneath the lake with ×20 active. Gohan, also tailed, is near Muri, Bulma and two newly found residents by the low wall; Cargo and the older resident are at the hollow. Zarbon is breathing but unbound and unconscious in the open; Dodoria is alive, unconscious and unbound where last seen, and the other downed soldiers are not securely held. The closed, intact visitors' ship is unattended at the inlet. Piccolo and Dende are at the Great Elder's house with Nail and the elder. The village and outlying workers are not all counted.

The distant scan's 1,100,000 corresponds, under the branch's stated ×10 arithmetic and same-scale assumptions, to a selected unboosted equivalent of 110,000 at that instant; it is not a demonstrated maximum, an independent direct unboosted scan, a measure of permanent growth, or a reading of Freeza. The later ×20 is not newly scanned and is not a ceiling. Freeza's 480,000 is a branch-authored snapshot, distinct from his earlier 530,000 claim; his transformed claim over one million is not an exact reading or proof of a fixed ceiling. Neither scouter capability nor the outcome is generalized beyond this device and scene.
