## COLD OPEN

A torn section of wall lies on the beach outside Kame House. Waves reach the broken boards and draw back.

**THE RESCUE**

---

## 1 · THE LAST WARNING

**NARRATOR:** Raditz has taken both boys and risen above Kame House. Before he leaves, he gives Goku one last warning.

Raditz hovers over the beach with Gohan under one arm and Goshin under the other. Goku lies in the sand below, with one hand pressed against his stomach. Krillin pulls himself out of the hole in the house wall. Bulma is in the surf, and Roshi gets to his knees beside Goku.

"Let them go," Goku says.

"Tomorrow," Raditz says. "Bring me a hundred bodies by this time tomorrow, and you can have your children back."

Gohan pulls at Raditz's arm. "Father!"

"You heard me. I want them here, where I can count them."

"He won't kill anyone for you," Krillin says. "You can't ask him to do that."

"Then the children die."

Gohan kicks at Raditz's armour. Goshin reaches across and catches Gohan's hand, but Raditz pulls them apart before the brothers can hold on to each other.

"Tell him, Kakarot," Raditz says. "Tell him which matters more to you."

Goku pushes both hands into the sand. "Leave them out of it. You want to fight, fight me."

"I've already done that. Look where it got you."

Bulma wades toward the beach. "They're four years old!"

"You should be pleased they have Saiyan blood. The rest of this planet won't live much longer anyway."

Roshi looks up at Raditz. "What do you mean by that?"

"Earth is our next target. Three Saiyans could clear this world in a month. Your friend can kill a hundred now, or wait until we kill the rest."

Goshin twists to see Goku. Raditz tightens his grip and lifts him against his armour.

"Gohan, are you all right?" Goku calls.

"I'm here!"

"Goshin?"

"I'm here too!"

Goku tries to get a knee under him, but his arms give way. Krillin runs across the beach and reaches him before he falls flat again.

"I'll come after you," Goku says.

"Without the hundred? I'll kill them before you get close."

"Don't listen to him, Father!" Gohan calls.

Raditz rises higher. The wind lifts sand around Roshi's feet and scatters it toward the house.

"Don't try to follow," Raditz says. "You already know what happens when you fight me."

"Put them down!"

Raditz flies out over the water. Bulma runs back into the surf, but he is already beyond the beach. Gohan stretches one hand toward Goku until Raditz turns and carries them farther out.

Goku pushes himself up and takes a step. His legs give way, and Krillin catches his shoulder before he hits the sand.

"Goku! Wait!"
---

## 2 · THE TAIL

Goku pushes himself onto one knee. He watches the sky above the sea, then moves away from Krillin's hand.

"I've got to get them."

"You'll get yourself killed," Bulma says. "He put you down with one hit."

"I know what he did."

"You can't help them if you rush in and die," Krillin says. "At least let us come with you."

Goku looks at the hole in the wall where Raditz threw Krillin. Broken boards cover the floor inside, and Krillin is favouring one leg as he walks back to the beach.

"He hurt you too."

"I'm all right. Tell me what you need."

"Then stop for a minute," Roshi says. "We need a way to beat him."

Goku presses a hand against his stomach. The water has reached Bulma's knees; she comes back onto the beach and kneels beside him.

"Can you stand?"

"Yeah. Give me a second."

Krillin looks toward the horizon. "He's your brother, and he still did that to you."

Goku turns toward him.

"His tail."

"What about it?"

"Get hold of it and squeeze hard. That might give us a chance."

Roshi nods. "The difficulty is getting close enough to take it."

"I can't do that on my own," Goku says. "He'll hit me before I get behind him."

"What if I keep him looking at me?" Krillin asks. "You could come at him from behind."

"You saw how hard he hits," Goku says.

"I did. I can still keep him busy for a little while."

Bulma picks up Roshi's staff from the sand and hands it back. Roshi brushes the grit off the end, then plants it beside his foot.

"One of us may not be enough," he says. "If we attack together, we can give Goku a chance."

Krillin looks at Roshi. Roshi sets his staff against the broken wall.

"Then you won't go alone," Roshi says. "The three of us can try."

"Right," Krillin says. "One of us keeps him busy, and another goes for the tail."

Goku looks at both of them. "You'd do that?"

"They're your kids," Krillin says. "Of course we will."

Krillin turns to Bulma. "If I don't make it, you can bring me back with the Dragon Balls, can't you?"

"We'll find them," Bulma says.

Goku's hand drops from his stomach.

"Krillin, not you. Not Master Roshi either. Remember what happened last time?"

Krillin's smile disappears. He looks at Roshi, who is standing very still beside the wall.

"Oh," Krillin says. "That's right."

"We'll still go," Roshi says.

"Yeah," Krillin says. "We said we would."

Goku puts a hand on Krillin's shoulder. Krillin looks down at it, then back at Goku.

"Tien and Yamcha aren't here," Krillin says. "You'd help me if it were my family."

"You don't even have a family yet," Bulma says.

"I could have one someday."

"You will," Goku says. "All of us are coming back."

Bulma folds her arms and looks down at the wet sand.

"What if we get all seven Dragon Balls first? We could ask Shenron to save the world."

"Could you find all seven before tomorrow?" Roshi asks.

Bulma looks up. "Not that quickly."

Goku gets to his feet. This time he stays up.

"Then we'll go after him now. He doesn't expect us to come to him."

"We don't know where he went," Krillin says.

Goku turns to Bulma. "The Dragon Radar. Have you got it?"

"Gohan's hat!"

Bulma runs inside through the damaged doorway. She returns with a small round device and presses its button. A light moves across the screen.

"There. That's the Dragon Ball on Gohan's hat."

Goku leans over her shoulder. The light travels across the display, then stops.

"At least they're still on Earth," Bulma says.

Krillin comes around to look at the screen. The light is inland, beyond a long stretch of coast, and it remains in the same place when Bulma changes the range.

"Can you tell how far away it is?" he asks.

"Far enough that we can't walk there before tomorrow."

"Let me see where he is."

Bulma turns the screen toward him. Goku studies the position and closes his hand around the edge of the device.

"All right. We're going."

"You won't beat him."

The voice comes from above the house. All four of them look up.

Piccolo hangs in the air beyond the broken roof. His cape moves in the sea wind, but he does not come down.

"Piccolo," Krillin says.

---

## 3 · AN OFFER

Goku steps away from the others and looks up at Piccolo.

"How did you get here?"

"I followed Raditz. I met him before he came to this island."

"Then you know how strong he is."

"I know that none of you can beat him alone. You and I may have a chance together."

"What happened when you met him?" Goku asks.

"I attacked him. He was still standing when I was finished, and then he went looking for you."

Goku looks at the torn beach, then back at Piccolo. "You couldn't stop him either."

"No. That's why I'm here."

Krillin moves beside Goku. "Why would you help us?"

Piccolo drops onto the sand. Krillin raises his hands, but Piccolo keeps his attention on Goku.

"Don't misunderstand. I want this world for myself. Raditz and the two Saiyans with him are in my way."

"And after they're gone?" Goku asks.

"I'll deal with you. Then I'll take the world."

"I won't let you do that."

"We'll see."

Goku looks at the radar in Bulma's hand. The light on the screen has not moved.

"For now, we both want Raditz," he says. "That's enough for me."

"Goku," Roshi says, "are you sure?"

"He's right about one thing. We need somebody strong enough to hold Raditz while the other one gets his tail."

Piccolo's eyes narrow. "A tail?"

"I'll explain on the way."

Bulma holds out the radar. "Take it. Keep the screen facing up so you can see if he moves."

Goku takes the device and tucks it into his belt. He looks once more at the line where the sea meets the sky.

"Wait for us, boys."

"Then stop talking," Piccolo says. "Let's go."

Goku rises from the sand. Piccolo lifts beside him, and the two of them turn toward the open sea.

Krillin steps forward. "Goku!"

"What?"

"Bring them back."

Goku nods. He and Piccolo accelerate over the water, leaving a wake of spray below them.

Bulma watches until they are beyond the island. The radar is gone with Goku, but she has seen where the light stopped.

"Do you really think Piccolo will help him?" she asks.

"He wants Raditz out of the way," Roshi says. "For now, that gives them the same enemy."

Krillin picks up a piece of the broken wall and sets it aside. "And after Raditz?"

"We can worry about that when the boys are safe," Roshi says. "Bulma, you remember the position?"

"Yes."

"Then get us a vehicle. We're going after them."

Bulma opens her case and takes out a capsule. She throws it onto the sand beyond the broken wall, and a compact aircraft appears in a burst of smoke.

Krillin opens the door while Roshi retrieves his staff. Bulma climbs into the pilot's seat and starts the engine; Krillin helps Roshi into the back before taking the seat beside her.

"If Piccolo changes his mind, Goku will need us," Krillin says.

"And if he doesn't, we'll still need to get the boys home," Bulma answers.

The aircraft lifts over the beach and turns toward the coast.

---

## 4 · OVER THE SEA

**NARRATOR:** Earlier, while Goku and his friends were making their plan, Raditz was still carrying the boys over the sea.

Raditz carries the boys across the water. The island is behind them, and the coast ahead is still distant. Gohan's hat has slipped over one eye; he pushes it up and looks toward Goshin.

"My hat's going to fall."

"Put it on tight," Goshin says. "Father gave it to you."

Gohan pushes the hat down with his free hand. Raditz shifts his hold, trapping that arm against his side.

"Stop moving."

"You're hurting him," Goshin says.

"He'll live. Whether he keeps living depends on Kakarot."

Goshin presses his feet against Raditz's armour and tests the space beneath his arm. Raditz pulls him in closer, but Goshin manages to turn one hand toward the joint at his elbow.

"Can you get out?" Gohan asks.

Goshin pulls at Raditz's wrist. Raditz's grip does not move.

"Help me."

Gohan twists and strikes Raditz's other hand with both fists. Raditz looks down at him, and Goshin presses his palm against the edge of Raditz's armour.

A burst of ki strikes the joint at Raditz's elbow. His arm jerks open. Goshin slips free and drops below him.

"Gohan!"

Gohan catches Raditz's wrist and pulls with both hands. Goshin rises again and takes hold of Gohan's arm, and the two boys wrench him loose.

Raditz reaches for Goshin, but Gohan kicks his hand away. Both boys drop. Goshin gets under Gohan, lifts him by the waist and turns back toward the island.

"Hold on!" Goshin says.

Gohan wraps his arms around Goshin's shoulders. They fly low over the water, with the surface racing beneath their feet.

Raditz comes down in front of them. Goshin swerves, carries Gohan past his shoulder and fires a ki blast back at him.

Raditz turns his palm and knocks the blast away. It strikes the sea, throwing up a column of water between him and the boys.

"You think you can get past me?"

He extends two fingers and sends a smaller burst toward them. Goshin turns his hand the way Raditz did. The burst strikes his palm, slides away and explodes above the water.

Goshin's hand burns. He pulls it close to his chest, but keeps flying with Gohan against his other shoulder.

Gohan looks back. Raditz is coming through the spray with his hand extended, and Gohan pulls Goshin's shoulder to turn him away.

"This side!"

Goshin banks toward the open water. Another blast strikes behind them, lifting a wall of spray that falls across both boys. Goshin shakes water from his eyes and keeps moving.

"Goshin," Gohan says, "your hand—"

"Hold on."

Raditz closes the distance. Gohan kicks at him, forcing his reaching hand aside, but Raditz catches Goshin's tail with the other hand and squeezes.

Goshin's legs drop. His grip on Gohan loosens, and both children fall toward the sea. Gohan grabs Goshin around the chest and holds him as they fall.

Raditz dives beneath them and catches both before they strike the water. He grips their shirts this time, keeping their arms pinned against their sides.

"Try that again, and I'll hurt him," Raditz tells Goshin.

Gohan struggles. Raditz tightens his hold, and Gohan stops pulling at the cloth around his throat.

Goshin looks at Gohan, then at Raditz's hand.

"Leave him alone."

"Then keep still."

Raditz turns toward the distant coast. Goshin holds his burned palm against his shirt while Gohan reaches across the space between them and catches his sleeve.

---

## 5 · THE BOOK

Chi-Chi sits on the step with a bowl on her lap and Gohan's schoolbook open in her hands. Two lines of sums are done on the first page. She turns it and finds two more finished, then several pages with no answers at all.

"Half," she says. "Half of it done, and gone off without a word to me."

A chicken comes around the corner and stops beside the bowl.

"No," Chi-Chi says. The chicken turns and goes.

She looks toward the empty road below the gate. "He's sitting in that house with his feet under a table, being fed. Goshin too."

Chi-Chi closes the book around its ribbon and takes it inside. She returns with a stool and places the book on it, squared with the edge of the boards.

"They can finish this when they get home."

---

**NARRATOR:** At Raditz's landing place, the boys have not stopped trying to get away.
---

## 6 · THE POD

Raditz lands in a broad crater surrounded by broken hills. His round space pod rests at the bottom, its hatch set into one side. He puts Gohan down beside it and keeps hold of Goshin's shirt.

"Get inside."

"I want my father," Gohan says.

"You'll see him tomorrow if he does as he's told."

Gohan backs away from the open hatch. Raditz catches his arm and pushes him through the doorway. Goshin plants his feet, but Raditz lifts him and puts him inside beside his brother.

The hatch closes. A lock clicks outside.

Gohan pounds on it. "Let us out!"

Raditz's voice comes through the metal. "Stop that noise. You're Saiyans, not babies."

Goshin looks around the cramped cabin. A padded seat fills most of the space, with a narrow panel beside it. He presses a button on the panel; lights come on, but the hatch stays shut.

Gohan tries the handle. It moves partway and stops.

"Hold it while I pull," he says.

Goshin puts his burned hand against the seam. The skin is red where he turned Raditz's blast; he shifts his weight and uses his other hand.

"Pull when I tell you."

Gohan takes the handle. Goshin concentrates a narrow stream of ki against the edge of the hatch. The metal glows, then splits along a short section of the seal.

"Now."

Gohan pulls. Goshin pushes against the opening, and the hatch gives a little. A line of daylight appears between the door and the frame.

Gohan gets his fingers into the gap. "Again!"

They push together. The gap widens enough for Gohan to put one shoulder through.

A hand comes down on the outside of the hatch. Raditz forces it shut. Gohan snatches his fingers back as the metal strikes the frame.

"I said stay inside."

Goshin hits the door with his palm. The damaged seal bends, but Raditz holds it closed.

"Open it!" Gohan shouts.

Raditz presses a control beside the hatch. A metal bar slides across the outside, and the light at the seam disappears.

Goshin pushes again. The hatch no longer moves.

Gohan puts his back against the door and braces his feet against the seat. He pushes until the metal creaks, while Goshin runs his fingers along the damaged seam.

"It's still split here," Goshin says. "If I can open it more, we can reach the lock."

"Do it. I'll keep pushing."

Goshin places his palm against the metal again. He sends a narrow stream of ki into the crack, drawing it slowly upward as the edge begins to separate. Hot metal curls back from the seam.

"Keep your fingers away," Goshin says.

Gohan shifts his hands and pushes with his shoulder. The hatch moves a fraction, but the bar outside keeps it from opening.

Outside, Raditz straightens beside the pod. "Now I need something to eat."

His scouter gives a sharp signal. He turns his head toward the pod and touches the control at his temple.

"Seven hundred and ten?"

The signal stops. Raditz looks across the crater, then back at the pod.

"That can't be one of the children. It must be damaged."

Raditz keeps one hand on the bar while he checks the scouter with the other. Inside, Gohan presses his ear against the hatch. Goshin examines the panel again, feeling along its edges for a place to pull.

"Can you hear him?" Gohan asks.

"He's just outside."

Gohan crouches beside him. One corner of the panel lifts when they pull together, exposing a bundle of wires beneath it. Goshin reaches inside, but the lock does not move.

"We need the bar outside," he says.

Gohan looks at the narrow split they have made. "Maybe we can break it from here."

"When he moves away. Not while his hand is on the door."

Gohan stays beside the hatch, listening.

The scouter sounds again. Raditz checks the display: **710**. He taps the side of the device, and the number vanishes.

"It is damaged."

Two new signals appear, coming toward the crater. Raditz leaves the hatch and turns toward the sky.

---

## 7 · TWO SIGNALS

Goku and Piccolo fly above the hills. Goku takes the radar from his belt and checks the light.

"We're close. We should land and go the rest of the way on foot."

"It won't help," Piccolo says. "He's already tracking us with that machine. He'll know we're coming."

"Then he already knows where we are."

"Yes."

Goku puts the radar away. "We'll go straight at him."

They cross the next ridge together. Below them, the land changes from green hills to bare stone. Goku takes the radar out once more and holds it where Piccolo can see the light.

"He's just past that ridge," Goku says.

"Raditz will be waiting. Once we land, watch him. Don't rush straight at him. You'll lose. Each one of us needs the other. Stay close."

"I'm going for the boys."

"You won't reach them until we can get past him."

Goku returns the radar to his belt, and the two of them descend toward the crater.

---

Raditz studies his scouter. Two numbers settle on its display: **322** and **334**. He looks toward the approaching signals, then down at the pod.

"One of them reads the same as Kakarot. But he doesn't know where I am."

A shadow crosses the crater. Goku and Piccolo land on the rim, with the pod below and Raditz between them and it.

"So it's you," Raditz says to Piccolo. "How did you find me?"

"I'm not telling you," Goku says. "Give my sons back."

Inside the pod, Gohan lifts his head.

"Father!"

Goshin moves to the narrow window beside the seat. He can see Goku on the rim and Raditz standing below him. Piccolo is a few paces to Goku's left.

"Keep working," Goshin tells Gohan. "We can open it for him."

Raditz looks over his shoulder at the pod, then back at Goku.

"You still won't join us?"

"I told you. I'm not going anywhere with you."

"You came all this way to die over that?"

"I came for my boys."

"Then you should have brought what I asked for. There is still time to change your mind."

"There was never going to be a hundred people. Let them out."

"You have until tomorrow. After that, I won't ask again."

Goku starts down the slope. Raditz moves to meet him, and Piccolo puts an arm across Goku's path.

"Wait until we're ready," Piccolo says.

Goku stops. His eyes remain on the hatch below them.

Piccolo pulls his cape from his shoulders and lets it fall. The weighted cloth hits the ground and cracks the stone underneath. He removes his turban and drops it beside the cape.

Goku looks at him. "You've been training with weights too?"

"Yes."

Raditz's scouter sounds. The display changes to **408** as Piccolo steps away from the discarded clothes.

Goku removes his own weighted gear. It hits the ground beside him, and dust rises around his boots.

"Feels better," he says.

The scouter sounds again. **416.**

Raditz laughs.

"You took those off and thought it would be enough?"

"We have a plan," Goku says.

"I'm finished trying to recruit you, Kakarot. You'd be a burden to us, and you're a disgrace to our family. I'll kill you. Now try your plan."

Goku and Piccolo move apart, one toward each side of the crater. Raditz stays between them and the pod.

---

## 8 · THE FIRST EXCHANGE

Goku attacks from the right. Raditz catches his fist and drives a knee into his stomach. Goku bends around the blow, and Raditz throws him toward the crater wall.

Piccolo closes from the left and reaches for Raditz's tail. Raditz turns, knocking Piccolo's hand away with his forearm, then strikes him across the face.

Piccolo lands on one knee. Goku hits the rock, breaks through a slab and rolls back onto his feet.

Raditz flies straight at Piccolo. Piccolo raises both arms to block, but Raditz passes his shoulder and strikes him in the back. Piccolo stumbles forward as Raditz turns toward Goku.

Raditz follows Goku across the crater. Goku blocks a strike at his head, but the next blow lands in his ribs and drives him against the broken wall. He catches Raditz's wrist before another punch can reach him.

Piccolo fires a short blast from the side. Raditz releases Goku and turns; the blast hits the ground beside him and breaks the stone under his boots.

Goku uses the opening to leap over Raditz. His fingers brush the end of the tail, but Raditz pulls it away and strikes him across the back before he can grab it.

Goku lands hard. Piccolo rushes in as Raditz turns toward him, and their forearms meet above Goku. Piccolo drives his other fist forward; Raditz blocks it and pushes him back.

"Piccolo! Take the tail!"

Goku rushes in again. He punches at Raditz's chest, then drops under the return strike. Piccolo comes in behind Raditz and grabs for the tail with both hands.

Raditz drives his elbow backward into Piccolo's ribs, knocking him away before his hands reach the tail. Raditz kicks Goku across the crater and sends Piccolo after him with a blow to the shoulder.

Inside the pod, Gohan grips the edge of the window.

"Father! Get up!"

Goku gets to one knee. Piccolo stands beside him, wiping blood from his mouth.

"Try from behind again," Goku says.

"Keep him facing you longer."

Goku wipes his mouth with the back of his hand. "Come in from the left. I'll make him turn."

He charges straight at Raditz and throws two punches. Raditz blocks the first and catches the second, but Goku holds on to his wrist and swings him toward Piccolo.

Piccolo reaches past Raditz's shoulder. Raditz stamps down on Goku's foot and tears his wrist free, then catches Piccolo's hand and drives him into the ground.

The impact opens a shallow trench through the crater floor. Goku lands beside it and pulls Piccolo clear before Raditz's next blast strikes the same place.

"I'm all right," Piccolo says, pushing Goku's hand away.

Goshin presses his hand against the damaged hatch. Gohan takes the handle again, and they pull and push together. The bar outside holds.

A blow shakes the pod. Both boys turn to the window.

Raditz has driven Goku backward into the crater wall. Gohan pushes against the hatch again, then looks at the thin opening Goshin cut through the seal.

"Can you get a hand through?"

Goshin tries. The gap is too narrow, but he can see a strip of the crater through it. He presses his palm to the opening and starts cutting the edge again.

"Hold it steady."

Gohan braces the hatch with both hands. The cut widens, and Goshin turns his arm so he can aim through the gap.

Outside, Goku catches Raditz's wrist and pushes him away. Piccolo fires a blast at Raditz's side; Raditz twists clear, and the blast tears a deep channel into the empty hillside.

Raditz drives Piccolo back toward the rim, then turns and raises his hand at Goku, who is still getting up beside the crater wall.

Goshin fires through the gap. His ki blast strikes Raditz's forearm and knocks his hand off course. Raditz's blast hits the wall above Goku, showering him with broken stone.

Goku rolls clear and comes up behind Raditz. He drives his fist into Raditz's side, and Piccolo closes from the other direction.

Raditz catches Piccolo's arm and throws him into Goku. Both men fall, but Raditz turns toward the pod rather than following them.

"Stay inside!"

He tears a slab from the crater wall and drops it against the hatch. The slab covers the split and pins the bar in place, blocking Goshin's next shot.

Inside, Gohan shoves at the door. It does not move.

"We'll find another way," Goshin says.

He moves back to the window while Gohan checks the damaged panel. Outside, Piccolo has regained his feet and is moving toward Raditz.

Goku pushes himself up beside the wall. Piccolo fires again, forcing Raditz away from the pod, and the blast cuts a second groove through the rock beyond him.

Rock slides down behind the pod. Gohan moves back from the window as stones strike the shell.

Raditz flies through the dust and catches Piccolo by the front of his shirt. He swings Piccolo into Goku, knocking both of them across the ground.

Goku pushes Piccolo off him and stands. Piccolo gets up more slowly, with one hand pressed against his ribs.

Goku glances at the slab covering the hatch. Raditz follows his gaze and moves sideways, keeping himself between Goku and the children.

"Get away from them," Goku says.

"Make me."

Goku comes forward. Raditz meets him with a kick that Goku catches on both arms, then turns and strikes Piccolo before he can reach the tail. Piccolo blocks, but the impact pushes him back across the loose stone.

Goku attacks again from the other side. Raditz catches him by the shoulder and throws him into the same broken wall; Goku digs his hands into the rock and forces himself upright.

"You can defend yourselves a little," Raditz says. "It won't keep you alive. I'll hit you harder each time until you can't get up."

He advances toward them. Goku raises his hands. Piccolo moves to Goku's left, leaving enough room for either of them to reach the tail.

"You still think there's a way to win?" Raditz asks.

"I'm not leaving my sons with you," Goku says.

Raditz stops a few paces away.

"Before you die, there's something you should know. The two Saiyans with me are both stronger than I am."

Goku looks at Piccolo. Piccolo keeps his eyes on Raditz.

In the pod, Gohan takes Goshin's wrist. Goshin places his unhurt hand on the damaged seam.

Outside, Raditz raises his hands.

---

At Kame House, a wave reaches the broken boards again and recedes. The island is empty.
