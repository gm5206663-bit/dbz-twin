1. Show a review of Bubbles, Gregory and the begun Kaiō-ken; do not pretend a source stopwatch is an established branch result. Teach and test the Genki-dama on-page before the warning, including the potential danger to Earth. No copied technique is granted to Goshin merely because the reader sees Goku's lesson.
2. King Kai notices the Saiyans are close and acknowledges the omitted return journey. He helps living Goku communicate with Roshi and, by a shown separate contact, Kami. Keep their knowledge distinct; show how Chi-Chi, the boys and the others learn rather than giving them an off-page message or exact clock.
3. Piccolo independently reaches the Lookout. Gohan freely chooses a bounded exchange and uses the flight learned in Chapter 22. If Piccolo's instinctive eye shot violates the agreed condition, acknowledge it, stop the round and let the family respond; do not reinstate source captivity. Goshin then has agency of his own and carries the strength, ki, growth and control from the lived year into the action. Gohan retains agency and growth; neither has an invented reading or form.
4. Treat the dragon question causally: Goku does not need revival; seven Balls have not been gathered. A character who knows the dragon's actual limits may distinguish a wish from a guarantee, without asserting an unmade wish happened. The source summoning, revival and halo change do not happen simply because they did in the source.
5. Stage a farewell and departure after the lesson; Goku traverses the actual road toward Yemma's end, without an unearned reunion or arrival time. Resolve the clothing beat only if the branch gives it a reason; his prior weighted training bands and supply capsule have established origins.
6. Land both Saiyan pods shortly after the family exits the chamber. Treat the source's East City setting as secondary-guided and the exact clock, civilian details and physical consequences as authored where not directly established. Show people reacting and established fighters sensing the arrivals. Do not force all source casualties, destruction, a specific power reading, or a battle conclusion.
7. Keep the children’s room, school, C-6, machine, Baba, family promises and Goku's arrival open unless a scene actually resolves one. Include agency and practical danger rather than moving civilians or characters off-screen without explanation. After this episode-21 remainder, research episode 22 before writing its own battles.
