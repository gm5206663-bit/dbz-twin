## Authored action and continuity check

- **The boys change the outcome.** Gohan calls Nimbus, spots the trapped young dinosaur, proposes and operates the branch-and-stone lever, crawls under the slab and pulls the animal free. He steers Nimbus around the trees to evade Piccolo. Goshin flies, carries and protects Gohan, chooses to help the young animal, directs the larger one away, holds the slab, fires at the water to slow the charge and interrupts Piccolo's grab. Their rescue and escape involve both children before the arriving adults end the larger confrontation. Gohan suspects the slab *might* be from the cliff he broke; the story does not assert he witnessed it fall on the animal.
- **Piccolo.** He pursues both children, briefly diverts the attacking animal for his own stated reasons, resumes the attempted seizure, and withdraws when the healed Goku and others are present. He trains alone afterward. His action is not a pardon, a secure alliance, a completed custody arrangement or a fixed future death/survival.
- **Routes and knowledge.** Popo reaches the aircraft first, learns Goku and Roshi went up the river, and picks them up. Gohan rides Nimbus while Goshin flies beside him; they reach the carpet. Goshin tells Goku, within Popo's hearing, that Piccolo stopped the big dinosaur. Popo can therefore tell Kami that later. Krillin meets Chi-Chi and Ox-King on the road, admits what he does not know and carries Chi-Chi to the river. Popo brings the family and Roshi back to the clearing; Krillin flies alongside. Popo then returns to the Lookout. Ox-King is still following by road, not magically present at the reunion.
- **Senzu count and injuries.** Krillin started with three whole beans. Goku eats one and Gohan eats one; each is fully healed and restored, including Goku's arm/rib-area injuries and Gohan's shoulder. Krillin keeps one. Goshin refuses the bean whose preparation he did not watch; his sore head, burned palm and recently grabbed ankle are not healed by it. His hand remains usable, his ankle stings, and he can still fly and act. Chi-Chi adds a clean dressing, and she and Goku intend to have him examined **today**; no examination or diagnosis has happened yet. There is no partial Senzu recovery, invented instant medical clearance or permanent diagnosis.
- **Character rules.** Goshin remains directly effective at least alongside Gohan; neither boy is reduced to a power reading. Goshin repeats `Lift it on three`, `Show me your hand`, and Chi-Chi's request to stay within sight before he complies; he does not comply with Piccolo's coercive demand. He is invited before entering Goku's conversations, takes the downhill side beside Gohan on the paths, and puts his hands behind his back when Chi-Chi looks him over. He does not eat the apple or an unwatched bean. He can be playful and frightened as well as determined; no transformation, god ki, free observational power increase or new numerical scan is introduced. His expressed wish to practice aiming concerns an existing ability, not automatic mastery.
- **Family and moon.** Gohan retains his hat and scholar ambition. Roshi reports the tournament transformation he personally saw; he does not claim to know how Grandpa Gohan died. Chi-Chi takes the risk seriously without calling the boys monsters. No present full moon or forced outcome is established.
- **Objects and other cast.** Bulma removes the bent aircraft rod and stores it in her toolkit, then re-capsulates the damaged aircraft; its original capsule deployment was established in protected Chapter 4. It is not repaired. The scouter remains wrapped and unpowered, its cell separate; Bulma has not established that it is safe to transmit or found Yamcha and Tien. Roshi still has the medical case containing the Dragon Radar. Baba is on her way to find Roshi; Kami intends to speak to Goku; neither message has yet been delivered.

## Handoff for a possible Chapter 9

The family, Krillin, Roshi and Bulma are at the forest clearing, preparing to leave; they are not already home, and Chi-Chi is not shown riding Nimbus. Goku is carrying Goshin, Gohan is with Chi-Chi, and the cloud waits nearby. The parents intend an examination for Goshin today. Popo has returned to Kami, Piccolo is training elsewhere, Baba is traveling, and Ox-King is still on the road. Any continuation should resolve the necessary travel and medical follow-through credibly without letting it displace the adventure, and should preserve the actual location and knowledge of each character.

Kami's warning, Baba's message, Bulma's investigation and the conditional moon risk are open, not predetermined outcomes. The old Chapters 9 and 10 do not automatically attach to this branch. In particular, the rejected C-6 adventure still requires a later, separate repair that gives **both** boys direct, sustained agency and a developed relationship with the robot; neither the robot's survival nor death is fixed here.
