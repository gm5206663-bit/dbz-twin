## COLD OPEN

An open space pod lies in its crater, with its hatch bent outward and dust settled over the glass.

**THE WORDS HE CAME TO SAY**

---

## LAST TIME · THE CRATER

Earlier, the hatch is shut. Gohan presses both hands against the glass. Goshin stands beside him, holding the edge of the seat.

Outside, Raditz has driven Goku and Piccolo back from the crater. The boys have been shouting through the hatch, but Raditz stays between the pod and their father.

"Can you open it?" Gohan asks.

Goshin pulls the handle beside the seat; it moves a little, then stops, and he puts both hands on it to pull again.

"It won't go any further. Help me."

Gohan takes hold beside him. The handle bends, but the hatch stays shut, and a heavy blow against the pod knocks them both onto the floor.

Beyond the glass, Goku draws his hands to his side, and blue light gathers between his palms.

"Kamehameha!"

The beam crosses the crater, and Raditz catches it between his hands and forces it upward. The blast tears through a ridge behind him, and broken stone falls across the crater.

"Father!" Gohan calls.

Goku gets to his feet in torn clothes, putting one hand against his ribs. Across the crater, Piccolo stands with his left arm gone.

Raditz strikes Goku and drives him into the ground. Gohan hits the glass with his fist.

"Stop it!"

The hatch buckles as Goshin braces one foot against the seat and pushes beside him. Metal folds outward, and both boys scramble through the opening.

Raditz turns from Goku, and Gohan runs at him and drives his head into Raditz's chest. The armour cracks, and Raditz is thrown back across the crater.

Gohan lands beside Goku. Raditz gets up, holding his chest.

"Gohan, run!" Goku shouts.

Raditz lunges. Goshin rises from the ground and fires a burst of ki into Raditz's arm. The blow turns Raditz away from Gohan, and Raditz's hand strikes the rock instead.

Goshin flies down toward Goku, but Raditz catches his tail.

Goshin drops with his hands hitting the ground, and Raditz pulls him backward.

"Let him go!" Gohan runs at them.

Raditz kicks Gohan aside. Before Raditz can raise his hand again, Goku gets behind him and locks both arms around his chest.

"Now, Gohan! Take Goshin!"

Gohan grabs Goshin's shoulders. Goku pulls Raditz's arm back, and Raditz loses his grip on the tail. Gohan drags Goshin clear. Goshin gets to his knees and reaches for Goku.

"Father!"

"Get behind Piccolo! Both of you!"

Piccolo has two fingers at his forehead, and light gathers around them. Goshin looks from Piccolo to Goku, then pulls Gohan with him across the crater.

Raditz struggles against Goku's arms. Goku holds on.

"Piccolo! Do it!"

"Special Beam Cannon!"

Piccolo thrusts his hand forward. The beam pierces Raditz's chest and passes through Goku behind him, then cuts into the rock. The ridge splits, and dust rolls across the crater.

Gohan runs back first, then drops to his knees; Goshin catches him before he falls, but Gohan closes his eyes and does not answer. Goku lies a short distance away with blood spreading across his shirt.

Raditz is on his back, one hand over the hole in his armour. His scouter crackles beside his face.

"Everything went through this," Raditz says. "My two companions heard it. They'll come here."

Piccolo steps closer. "When?"

"One year. They're both stronger than I am. They'll kill everyone on this planet."

Raditz laughs, then coughs. Piccolo raises his hand and fires. The blast strikes Raditz, and he does not move again.

---

Krillin kneels beside Goku as Bulma and Roshi reach the crater. Gohan lies unconscious near the broken pod, and Goshin holds his hand.

"We'll bring you back," Krillin says. "I'll find the Dragon Balls."

Goku gives him a small nod.

"Take care of them."

"I will."

Goku closes his eyes, and Krillin takes his hand, but Goku does not answer him again.

The wind moves the dust around them, and then Goku's body disappears.

Goshin runs to the place where Goku was lying and reaches down into the dust, but finds nothing there.

"Where did he go?" Goshin asks.

"That was Kami," Piccolo says. "He is the only one who could do it."

Roshi puts an arm around Gohan. "We'll find out what happened."

A short distance away, Piccolo grits his teeth, and a new left arm pushes out from his shoulder. He flexes the hand once while Bulma steps back.

"You three look for the Dragon Balls," Piccolo says. "I'm taking the children."

Krillin gets up. "You're not taking them anywhere. Their mother will want them home."

"Their mother won't be able to protect them from the Saiyans. I need them trained before those two arrive."

Roshi moves in front of the boys. "You cannot make that decision without asking Chi-Chi."

"There isn't time to argue. Move, or I'll make you move."

Piccolo reaches past Roshi and lifts Gohan, but Goshin rises from the ground, catches Gohan's arm and pulls. Piccolo holds on, then takes Goshin by the back of his shirt with his other hand.

"Let him go!" Goshin strikes Piccolo's wrist.

Piccolo jerks his arm away and flies upward with both boys. Goshin kicks free of his hand and turns in the air, but Piccolo still has Gohan.

Gohan hangs motionless in Piccolo's grip, and Goshin flies after them with his hand out. Krillin rises from the crater too, but Piccolo is already above the ridge with Gohan under one arm.

"We'll get them back," Krillin calls.

Goshin looks over his shoulder at Roshi, Bulma and Krillin growing smaller below him, then follows Piccolo toward the mountains.

## 1 · SIX MONTHS

Gohan wakes beside a pool, with Goshin sitting next to him and Piccolo standing on a rock above them.

"Goshin?" Gohan says.

"I'm here."

Gohan sits up and looks around the valley, where cliffs stand on three sides and a stretch of dry ground lies on the fourth. No road crosses it.

"Where's Father?"

Piccolo steps down from the rock.

"Your father died fighting Raditz."

Gohan looks at Goshin, whose hands close on his trousers.

"Krillin said he would bring him back," Goshin says.

"His friends are looking for the Dragon Balls," Piccolo says. "But the two Saiyans coming here are stronger than Raditz. Goku and I won't be enough to stop them."

"Mother doesn't know," Gohan says.

"Someone will tell her."

"I want to go home."

"You're staying here."

Goshin gets up. "Which way is our mountain?"

"You won't find it by walking. There's desert beyond those cliffs."

Goshin catches Gohan's wrist and rises above the pool. They lift together, Gohan's feet trailing through the water as they head toward the nearest cliff.

Piccolo cuts across their path from above, catches Goshin's forearm and brings both boys down beside the pool.

As Gohan stumbles, Goshin plants his feet and swings his free hand at Piccolo. Piccolo turns sideways, guides the strike past his shoulder and lets go.

Goshin pulls Gohan behind him, raises his hands again and watches the position of Piccolo's feet.

"Get ready to run," he tells Gohan.

Goshin steps to the side, drawing Piccolo away from the pool. When Piccolo moves after him, Gohan runs toward the stream, and Goshin lifts into the air to follow.

Piccolo lands in front of Gohan, and Goshin drops between them and pushes at Piccolo with both hands. Piccolo catches his wrists, but Goshin turns his shoulder and slips one arm free.

The two of them strike the bank together. A section of earth breaks away under their feet, and water rushes into the gap. Piccolo releases Goshin and steps back.

Goshin lands beside Gohan, breathing hard but keeping his hands raised.

"You can try that until you run out of strength," Piccolo says. "I'll bring you back every time."

"Then why did you bring us here?" Gohan asks.

"To make you strong enough to fight."

"I don't want to fight. I want to be a scholar."

"Fine. Be a scholar after the Saiyans are beaten."

Gohan keeps hold of Goshin's sleeve and looks from the broken bank to Piccolo, who is standing between them and the open desert.

"If we stay here, can we see Mother?" Gohan asks.

"Not until I say so. You need to learn how to look after yourselves first."

"She'll come looking for us."

"Then she'd better stay away from me."

Goshin looks toward the cliffs. "How long?"

"One year before they arrive. For the first six months, you stay here."

"And then?"

"If you're alive, I'll teach you to fight."

Gohan holds on to Goshin's sleeve. "What are we supposed to eat?"

"Find something."

"Where do we sleep?"

"Find somewhere."

"What about a bath?" Gohan asks. "And my books?"

Piccolo folds his arms.

"There are animals here. There is water. You have each other. That's more than most fighters started with."

Goshin glances at the pool, where a narrow stream runs out through grass and between two boulders.

"You said you'd teach us," he says.

"After six months. First, stay alive."

As Piccolo lifts off the ground, Gohan runs underneath him.

"Please don't leave us!"

Piccolo stops above the trees.

"Learn to use the strength you have. You'll need it when the Saiyans come."

Piccolo flies over the cliff and disappears beyond it, while Gohan stands with his face turned up. Goshin rises a little above the ground, but he does not follow.

The stream runs past their feet.

## 2 · THE LOOKOUT

Popo finds Kami standing at the edge of the Lookout, with clouds covering the mountains far below them.

"You're worried about Piccolo," Popo says.

"He killed Raditz. Raditz's soul reached the other world."

Popo comes to stand beside him.

"Piccolo is not what he was," Kami says. "Now he has taken Goku's sons."

"Will he look after them?"

"He intends to make them fight. What happens before that is up to him."

Kami looks down through a break in the clouds, but the valley is hidden beneath them, and the wind carries the cloud across the Lookout.

"He threatened Goku's friends to take them," Popo says. "Does that mean he wants to protect the boys?"

"He wants their strength against the Saiyans. He believes he can make use of it."

"Then the boys must get stronger quickly."

"All of us must. Goku has a long journey before he can begin his training, and the Saiyans are already travelling toward Earth."

Popo looks back at the doorway. "I can prepare the Lookout. We can ask the others to come here."

"Yes. Send for them as soon as we can."

Kami rests one hand on the stone railing.

"We may have only one chance left to use the Dragon Balls. I hope the boys are ready when the Saiyans come."

## 3 · THE ROAD

Earlier, at the entrance to Snake Way, Goku stands beside the guide and looks out at the long road while the guide holds a small cart by its handles.

"Do you know Fortuneteller Baba?" Goku asks.

"She visits now and then. Is there a message you would like me to give her?"

"Ask her to tell Master Roshi not to bring me back for a year. I've got to find King Kai and get some training first."

The guide takes a notebook from his pocket. "For a year, you said? And the message is for Master Roshi?"

"That's right. Two more Saiyans are coming, and I want to be ready when they get here."

The guide writes it down, repeats the request and puts the notebook away; Goku thanks him and steps onto the road.

Now Goku runs along Snake Way, which curves through clouds in both directions. He stops, puts his hands on his knees and looks ahead.

"A million kilometres," he says. "That's a long way."

He straightens and looks back at the empty road.

"I didn't know Gohan had that much power. I'll ask the boys about it when I get home."

He smiles, then presses a hand against his ribs. The wound is gone, but his fingers stay there for a moment.

"Krillin'll take care of them until I get back."

Goku starts running again.

Two bends of the road come close together, and Goku slows to look across the gap between them.

"I don't have to follow every turn."

He jumps. His feet leave the road, and he sails toward the next bend. The far edge slips beneath him. Goku reaches out and catches it with both hands.

With his legs swinging above the clouds, he hauls himself up, rolls onto the road and sits there breathing hard.

"Right. No more shortcuts."

He looks at the long curve ahead, gets up and begins to run.

## 4 · THE GATE

Krillin stands outside Chi-Chi's gate, taking a breath and putting his hands together in front of him.

"Goku was killed."

He winces.

"No. That's too sudden."

He tries again.

"Goku isn't coming home. But he will come home. Just not yet."

Krillin rubs his forehead.

"Chi-Chi, please don't worry. We're going to bring him back."

The gate opens, and Chi-Chi stands on the other side with a folded cloth in her hands.

"Krillin? What are you doing out here?"

"Hello, Chi-Chi."

"Where's Goku?"

"I came to tell you—"

Chi-Chi notices the dirt on Krillin's clothes and reaches toward a tear at his shoulder. He moves his arm back before her fingers touch it.

"What happened to your shirt? Were you fighting?"

"I got it caught. It's nothing to worry about."

"Is Goku hurt? Are the boys all right?"

Krillin's hands close at his sides as he looks toward the house, where Gohan's test paper is still pinned beside the door.

"The boys are alive," he says. "I saw them."

"Alive? What kind of answer is that?"

The Ox-King comes down the path behind her.

"Krillin! Good to see you. Are Gohan and Goshin with you?"

"No. They're not."

Chi-Chi steps through the gate. "Did Goku send you?"

"Yes. Goku is..." Krillin stops. "Goku is d—"

"What?"

"Don't worry," Krillin says. "He said not to worry."

Chi-Chi looks past him, down the road.

"So they're late again. I called Kame House and got the machine."

"Everyone must have gone out," Krillin says.

"Did they go out with the boys?"

"They could have. I mean, Goku was with them."

"And where is he now?"

Krillin opens his mouth. The Ox-King puts a hand on his shoulder.

"Come inside and tell us properly. We've got plenty to eat."

"I should go back."

"You've only just got here," Chi-Chi says. "Come on."

She holds the gate open, and Krillin walks through it.

## 5 · THE STREAM

Gohan crouches at the edge of the pool, reaches for a fish beneath the water and comes up with an empty hand.

"They go under the rock," he says.

Goshin lands beside him after looking across the valley from above the trees.

"Did you see our house?" Gohan asks.

"No. There are more cliffs beyond these."

"Can you see the sea?"

"Not from here."

Goshin points over the trees toward a break between two cliffs. From above, he could see a wide stretch of bare ground through that gap, with no road or houses on it.

"There might be another way past the desert," he says. "I want to look when we can see farther."

"Piccolo might come back."

"If he does, we'll stay together."

Gohan looks at the water again. "We can follow the stream. It has to go somewhere."

"Let's eat first."

Goshin picks up a fallen branch, breaks it across his knee and holds the shorter piece out to Gohan.

"Put it in front of the rock. I'll drive them toward you."

Gohan takes the branch and wades into the pool. Goshin circles to the other side, his feet just above the water, and lowers one hand into it.

The fish dart away, and Gohan brings the branch down to pin one against the bottom.

"I got one!"

Gohan lifts it out, and Goshin catches another against the bank. They carry both fish to a flat rock and gather dry sticks.

Gohan cleans the fish with a sharp stone while Goshin puts the sticks together and holds his palm above them. A small flare of ki catches the wood, and Gohan sets the fish over the fire.

When the skin starts to blacken, Gohan moves the fish farther from the flame and props the branch between two stones, then breaks another stick to turn it.

"We'll need more than this," Goshin says.

"I know. We can catch more when we've eaten these."

"And we need a place where nothing can get at us while we're asleep."

Gohan looks up at the cliffs. Several shelves run along the rock above the trees, but most of them are narrow and exposed to the wind.

"Mother uses a pan," he says.

"We don't have one."

"I know. I'm making sure it's cooked."

They wait with Gohan turning the fish on the branch and Goshin watching the trees across the stream.

A heavy step shakes the water, and Gohan looks up.

A dinosaur pushes through the trees, its head above the lower branches, and lowers its snout toward the fire.

Gohan picks up the branch.

"Can we keep the fish?"

"Take one and move back," Goshin says.

Gohan takes one fish off the fire and Goshin takes the other, and they back toward the pool as the dinosaur comes down the bank.

The dinosaur snaps at Gohan, and Goshin flies between them and shoves Gohan clear. The jaws close on Goshin's sleeve, tearing the cloth as he pulls away.

Gohan throws the branch at the dinosaur's face; it strikes the snout and falls, and the dinosaur turns after him.

Goshin brings both hands to his side, but the dinosaur swings its tail through the fire. Burning sticks scatter across the bank, and Goshin jumps back.

"Gohan, get behind the rock!"

Gohan runs. The dinosaur charges after him, knocking the flat rock into the pool. Goshin flies over the water and strikes the dinosaur's shoulder with a ki blast. The impact twists it away from Gohan.

The dinosaur roars and whips its tail across the bank. Goshin turns sideways, catches it on an open palm and guides it past his shoulder, just as Piccolo turned his strike. The tail smashes a tree behind him.

The dinosaur twists and swings again. This time the tail catches Goshin across the back and throws him into the stream.

Goshin comes up in the shallows as the dinosaur lunges toward him.

Gohan plants both feet, lifts the rock from the pool and throws it into the dinosaur's jaw. The impact knocks its head sideways, and its teeth close on the bank instead of Goshin.

Goshin gets to his feet and sets his hands at his side again, with blue light growing between his palms.

"Ka... me... ha... me..."

The dinosaur pulls free of the broken bank and charges.

## 6 · THE OTHER BANK

"Ha!"

Goshin thrusts both hands forward, and the Kamehameha strikes the dinosaur in the chest and carries it across the stream. It crashes through the trees on the opposite bank.

The beam cuts into the earth beyond it, bursting rock outward and opening a long furrow through the slope. Goshin lowers his hands. Water pours into the broken bank.

The dinosaur gets up among the fallen trees, looks toward the boys, then turns and runs into the forest.

After it has gone, the damaged slope keeps shifting, and a slab of rock slides into the furrow, followed by smaller stones that splash into the stream.

Goshin lowers his arms slowly, his palms red, and takes several breaths before he turns toward Gohan.

"Did it get you?" he asks.

"No. Sit down."

Gohan runs to Goshin and takes his wrist, looking at the torn sleeve and the scrape underneath. Blood runs down to Goshin's elbow.

"Let me wash it."

He dips the end of his own sleeve in the stream and wipes the scrape while Goshin holds still.

"It hurts," Goshin says.

"I know. I'm nearly done."

Gohan ties a strip from the torn sleeve around the scrape, then pulls Goshin's shirt back to check where the tail struck him. A dark bruise is spreading across his back.

"Can you walk?" Gohan asks.

Goshin takes a few steps along the bank and turns around. "Yes."

The water carries ash and broken twigs past their feet.

"The fish," Gohan says.

One lies in the grass where Gohan dropped it, while the other has fallen into the stream. Goshin lifts it out and puts it back, and they divide the remaining fish between them.

Gohan takes a bite. "It's cooked."

Goshin eats his half while looking across the stream at the damaged slope.

"We should find somewhere high," he says.

Gohan looks at the cliff above the pool, where a wide shelf juts out halfway up, with a hollow behind it.

"There. Can you take me up?"

"Hold on to my shoulders."

Gohan climbs onto his back, and Goshin lifts them above the water. They pass the broken bank and the trees before Goshin lands on the shelf and lets Gohan down.

The hollow is dry, and Gohan goes inside and comes back with a handful of dead leaves.

"We can put these on the ground."

Goshin looks out over the valley. From here he can see the stream bending through the trees and the desert beyond the cliffs. There is no house on the horizon.

"We'll look again tomorrow," he says.

Gohan spreads the leaves in the hollow.

"Tomorrow we'll find a way home."

"Yes."

## 7 · DINNER

Chi-Chi puts another plate on the table; Krillin reaches for it, but she sets it in front of the empty chair beside him.

"That's Goku's. The boys sit over there."

The Ox-King brings in a pot and sets it down, and Chi-Chi fills Krillin's bowl, then her father's.

"Eat while it's hot," she says.

Krillin picks up his chopsticks, takes a mouthful and puts them down again.

"Chi-Chi, I should tell you something."

She turns from the stove. "Go on."

"Goku isn't..." He looks at the empty chairs. "He isn't coming back right away."

"How late is right away?"

"I don't know."

"You said he told you not to worry. Did he say where they were going?"

"No."

Chi-Chi sets the ladle down.

"Gohan has school. Goshin has work to finish too. Goku knows that."

"He does," Krillin says.

Chi-Chi takes the test paper from the doorframe, brings it to the table and lays it beside Krillin's bowl.

"Gohan did well. I was going to show Goku as soon as they came home."

Krillin looks at the red mark at the top of the page, with Gohan's name written carefully in the corner beneath it.

"Goshin's work is in his bag," Chi-Chi says. "He finishes his sums, but then he starts taking the pencils apart. Gohan puts them back together for him."

The Ox-King laughs. "I brought him a new schoolbag. He'll have that one open in an afternoon."

"He can open it after he finishes the work inside."

Krillin touches the edge of the paper, and Chi-Chi picks it up and puts it back on the doorframe, where the first person through the door will see it.

"Then why didn't he bring them back?"

The Ox-King puts another piece of food in Krillin's bowl.

"Goku probably lost track of time. The boys will be hungry when they get here."

"They were meant to be here yesterday," Chi-Chi says. "I don't mind them visiting, but he could have sent word."

Krillin looks at her with his chopsticks resting across the bowl.

"I should have said something sooner."

"Then say it now."

Krillin's hand closes around the chopsticks, and he looks down at the table.

"I'm sorry."

"What for?"

"For making you wait."

"Is that all? You came all this way, and you still haven't said where they are."

Krillin looks toward the gate. The Ox-King gets up and draws the curtain across the window, shutting out the dark road.

"I saw Goku," Krillin says. "And I saw both boys."

"Then you can tell me what he said."

"He... he wants them to be safe."

"Of course he does. I want to know when they're coming home."

Chi-Chi waits, but Krillin does not say anything else.

The Ox-King clears his throat. "Have some more, Krillin."

"No, thank you."

"You've hardly touched it."

"I'm not very hungry."

Chi-Chi takes the pot from the table and puts it back on the stove. She moves Goku's empty plate out of the way, then stops with her hand on it.

"I'll keep his dinner warm," she says. "The boys' too."

She sets the plate beside the others, and Krillin picks up his bowl again.

## 8 · THE MESSAGE

A bell rings over the door of a busy restaurant as the guide from the other world comes in and looks across the tables. Fortuneteller Baba is sitting near the window.

"Madam Baba?"

"Yes?"

"A message for Master Roshi. It's from Son Goku."

Baba puts down her cup.

"Go on."

"He asks that they don't bring him back for one year. He wants that time to train."

"I'll tell Roshi."

"Please don't forget."

"I won't."

The guide bows and leaves, and Baba picks up her cup again.

---

Goku runs on Snake Way until his stomach rumbles, and he stops to press a hand against it.

"The guide said I wouldn't starve. He didn't say I wouldn't get hungry."

He looks along the road. There is no food on it.

"King Kai had better have something to eat."

Goku starts running again.

## 9 · THE SHELF

As darkness settles over the valley, Gohan sits at the mouth of the hollow with his arms around his knees. Beside him, Goshin arranges the leaves in two piles.

"Do you think Mother's angry?" Gohan asks.

"Probably."

"At us?"

"At Father first. Then at us."

Gohan gives a small laugh, which stops when he looks down at the valley.

"Piccolo said Father's friends would bring him back."

"Krillin said it too."

"Did you hear him?"

"At the crater. He told Father."

Gohan pulls his knees closer. "I want to see him."

Goshin sits beside him.

"We'll see him."

Gohan looks up at the sky above the cliff. "When we get home, I want to sleep in my own bed for a whole day."

"Mother won't let you. She'll want the schoolwork finished."

"Then I'll finish it first."

Goshin draws his knees up and rests his chin on them. From the shelf they can hear water below, but the trees block the view of the stream.

"We'll go back to the pool in the morning," he says. "After that, we can follow the water and look for a road."

"Even if Piccolo says not to?"

"We can decide when morning comes."

Something taps against the stone outside, and Gohan jumps as an apple rolls across the shelf and stops against his foot.

He picks it up and looks over the edge.

"Where did that come from?"

Goshin comes to the edge beside him and looks down at the dark trees.

"I don't know."

Gohan wipes the apple on his shirt and bites it, and his face tightens.

"It's sour."

He takes another bite, then holds the apple out.

Goshin looks at it. "You have it."

"There's enough for both of us."

"I don't know where it came from."

Gohan examines the apple and looks down into the trees again, then breaks it in half and sets one piece on the stone between them.

"I'll leave it here."

"All right."

Gohan eats his half while Goshin picks up the strip of cloth on his arm, tightens it and ties it again.

Below the shelf, Piccolo stands on a branch with his hand empty. He looks up once, then turns toward the distant cliffs.

"You'd better survive," he says quietly.

He flies away through the trees.

Gohan lies down on the leaves. Goshin sits near the entrance for a while, facing the valley. When Gohan begins to breathe steadily, Goshin lies down beside him.

The half apple remains on the stone. The stream runs below the shelf.

---

## 10 · THE GUEST

At the house, Chi-Chi covers the food on the stove while the Ox-King carries a blanket into the front room.

"You can stay here tonight, Krillin. It is too dark to set off now."

"Thank you."

Chi-Chi brings a pillow and puts it beside the blanket.

"If you hear them on the road, wake me."

"I will."

She pauses at the door.

"You'd tell me if something was wrong, wouldn't you?"

Krillin looks up at her.

"Chi-Chi..."

She waits, and he grips the edge of the blanket.

"I'll wake you if they come."

Chi-Chi looks at the closed door for a moment, then pulls the curtain aside to check the road herself. The Ox-King is shutting the kitchen window behind her.

"There is food on the stove if you're hungry later," she says. "I can warm it again when they get back."

"Thank you, Chi-Chi."

She leaves the room. Krillin sits down without unfolding the blanket, while the sound of dishes being washed carries through the doorway.

Outside, the stool is still in the yard. Beyond the gate, the road is empty.
