# Scouter and power correction — Chapters 38–40

**Owns:** the author's recurring direction about scouter readings, the source-bounded comparison, the actual corrections to the three unaccepted chapter drafts, their information paths and the preserved predecessor.

The author objected that scouters were being used as if they were for Goshin alone, and again asked that the writing learn from the original. The earlier drafts did not wholly omit other characters—Chapter 38 already gave four suppressed readings, and Chapter 39 gave Gohan 27,600—but that does **not** excuse an active Gohan scan left as “more than three hundred,” two later scans without readings, or fights insufficiently connected to the known power differences. The prose, not merely the explanation, has been changed.

## What the checked sources actually show

- In the retained original-Z 45 caption cues (18:19–18:59), Zarbon's **old model** malfunctions around an increase past 22,000; Dodoria describes his own as a **new model** and reads Vegeta at 24,000. The separate official [Dodoria feature](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_3406.html) describes the same model distinction. One old unit's failure is not proof that every scouter explodes at 22,000 or that the newer model has a known upper limit (`C310`).
- Z 47's retained cues (21:35–22:11) report all three Namekian warriors rising from about 1,000 to 3,000, and Dodoria realizes that they can control their battle power. Z 65 (06:03–06:09) reports Gohan's attack momentarily exceeding 20,000. Z 67 (20:24–20:53) has Vegeta explain that an extremely brief increase can evade a device's pickup. These are distinct situations, not grounds to hide a figure that this branch actually shows a device measuring (`C310`).
- In Z 71 (10:27–11:14), Freeza reads Nail at **42,000**, states **530,000** for himself and says he does not intend to fight Nail at full power (`C311`). The branch now has Freeza repeat his self-statement in a different encounter; it is not a new scouter measurement, a measurement of his current suppressed output or the limit of later transformations.
- [Kanzenshuu's source-separated battle-power list](https://www.kanzenshuu.com/battle-power/list/) attributes **Dodoria 22,000** and **Zarbon approximately 23,000** to *Daizenshuu 7*. Those are guidebook figures, not spoken readings invented for Z 47. The branch stages one actual scan of Dodoria at 22,000 (`C312`).

For this correction the specified caption passages were checked directly in the retained Japanese tracks; complete-caption readings previously documented for the cited episodes remain earlier work, not a new count. Caption origin and cut are unverified; neither short guides nor selected stills amount to complete original video/audio review.

## Subsequent dialogue and continuity review

The subsequent author-requested pass also corrected Chapter 37: Freeza sees unidentified settlement signals **24, 58, 113**, and the approaching scout reads Gohan **430** and Goshin **520** before suppression. In Chapter 38 that same scout notices the later **310/360** decrease; in Chapter 39 he reports the earlier figures and his own **14,600** to Zarbon, not his companion’s **36,800**. These added figures are branch-authored snapshots, not source values or maxima.
