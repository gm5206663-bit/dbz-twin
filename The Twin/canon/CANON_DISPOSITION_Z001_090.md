# Original Dragon Ball Z 1–90: source-to-branch disposition through Chapter 46

**Owns:** the bounded episode-by-episode source-to-branch disposition and evidence limits for original Z 1–90 through the selected Chapter 46 frontier.

**Later sequential review (`O354`):** The selected story bodies 33–46 were subsequently read in full, and three local corrections clarify radar state, Zarbon's knowledge and Chapter 46 wording without changing the frontier. [Actual sources, scope and predecessors](SEQUENTIAL_CORRECTION_CH33_46.md). This later pass does not upgrade the historical audit below to full audiovisual coverage.

**Purpose:** Answer which *identified source movements* have counterparts in the selected manuscript, which have changed, and which remain unshown. This is a working crosswalk, **not a certification that every scene, line, visual, musical cue or performance in 90 episodes has been checked or reproduced**. The chapter links are anchors for the described branch facts or conditions; a link is not a claim that the chapter includes everything from that source episode. The selected Chapter 46 and corrections are **unaccepted**. The separately preserved original Chapter 20 is accepted and is not replaced by the selected correction.

### Legend

- **A** — the named source function or beat is adapted, sometimes elsewhere or for different participants; it does not mean the whole episode is reproduced.
- **Δ** — the source conditions, participants, order or outcome are materially changed; a similar result must arise from the branch, not be assumed.
- **N** — a specified original event's prerequisites do not hold at the current branch checkpoint. This is not a ban on a different future event.
- **U** — not shown or not established through the audited Chapter 46 frontier. This includes a possible future thread and an absence that cannot be decided from available evidence; it does **not** mean the author must reproduce it or that it cannot occur off page.
- **†** — episode number absent from all 46 visible chapter episode-reference headers. Header absence is not itself an omission: a beat can have a counterpart, a changed cause or no present branch prerequisite. An episode may receive several dispositions because its different beats are not interchangeable.

The current headers identify **63 distinct Z episodes**: 1–24, 27, 30–54, 71, 77–85, 87–88 and 90. The **27** gaps are 25–26, 28–29, 55–70, 72–76, 86 and 89. Later source elements are sometimes deliberately brought forward, so this list is not a chronological requirement to replay the intervening arc.

## Current consequential gaps and decisions

1. **Keep the two absence questions distinct.** The source has a Ginyu/Body Change chain in 54, 58, 62–75 and 89–90; the selected branch has not shown the summons, the team's arrival, the swap that could produce a frog or a Bulma encounter. Existing scouter telemetry is uncertain. Treat the whole chain as unshown and causally open, not as a secret event already completed and not as forbidden solely because the source timeline was bypassed.
2. **Do not manufacture a source wish.** Muri's covered ball is with the older resident; the Great Elder's own ball was last with him. No complete set, wish rules, dragon, source password handoff or wish is established in this branch. Piccolo and Kami already live. Source knowledge belongs to those who actually learned it, not automatically to the branch cast.
3. **Keep current threats and rescue live.** Freeza is unconscious and watched, not securely contained. Zarbon has freed the grove soldier and follows an old path; his current intelligence, the separate inlet soldier, Dodoria, wider village and Sela's two fellow workers remain uncertain. Bulma's aerial carried a call, not proof that the whole ship can safely return. The Earth pod and provisional Saiyan watch remain unresolved. Dende chooses to help, but has not yet reached the fighters again.
4. **Preserve knowledge and causality.** Freeza saw two children and heard a name and healing report; whether he connected that name to one child or could recognize Dende is not established. His claim about the Saiyan world's destruction reached the boys directly; do not give other people that knowledge without a shown route. Earth–Namek intercuts do not prove exact simultaneity.
5. **Maintain the author's branch rules.** Goshin's Goku-like child-sized appearance and Chi-Chi's eyes, both tails, whole Saiyan–Earthling inheritance and real cumulative whole-being growth remain. Temporary Kaiō-ken is distinct from retained growth; ×30 is a chosen action, not a maximum, fixed permanent increase or invented cap. Goshin is never less capable than Gohan, who acts and develops independently and must not be erased to reproduce a source outcome. No early divine/golden form or compulsory source death is warranted.
6. **Further source work remains possible.** If lawful complete episodes and suitable playback/review access become available, examine each continuous audiovisual episode and compare silent action, voice, timing, score and cut against the relevant source-order cards and selected prose; update dispositions only from observed material. The present crosswalk identifies major movements and review priorities, not every source scene or a promise of perfect fidelity.
