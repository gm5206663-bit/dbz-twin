## Provisional handoff and constraints

The separate Chapters 6–9 remain unaccepted. It is night after the bridge: Goku, Chi-Chi, Gohan, Goshin and Ox-King have accepted a room at the village doctor's, with the curtains closed. The moon is intact; neither boy looked directly at it or transformed and both tails remain. Gohan has the Four-Star Ball on his hat and a machinery book loaned by Mina, the child the brothers helped rescue; he would like to help find the books lost in the river when it is safe. The Three- and Six-Star Balls remain last known at home, unchecked. Piccolo heard Goku's moon warning, saw the boys' tails and went toward his training ridge; he has neither boy in custody.

Goku and Gohan each ate a whole Senzu in candidate 8 and were fully restored. Goshin refused an unwatched bean: his head, burned palm and ankle remain relevant. The doctor examined him, asked for quiet and monitoring, ordered no training or fighting until she sees him again, and requested a follow-up **tomorrow even if he feels better**. After the bridge she checked him again; the head still hurt, the burned palm was pressed by the slipping board, and the dressing was damp. He ate rice and eggs whose preparation he watched. Do not treat a better morning as automatic full recovery, invent a diagnosis, erase the follow-up, make him a spectator, or let him eat unobserved food.

Krillin has the last whole Senzu and is flying with Bulma toward her lab; the scouter is unpowered with its cell separate. Yajirobe gave the summons to meet at Korin Tower; Roshi has the medical case and Dragon Radar on the island. Baba delivered only the report that Yemma would ask King Kai; the question of access for a living Goku and King Kai's answer remain unresolved. Neither Yamcha nor Tien has been found on the page. Kami informed the family that his and Piccolo's lives are linked and that his one-year feeling and possible last wish are uncertain.

The old adult-led Chapter 10 and `O124`–`O129` are rejected and cannot supply continuity. A useful book motif has an independent, earned origin in the new Chapter 9; the family has not encountered C-6. Give **both twins direct, sustained action and a developed relationship with the robot before the decisive danger**. Parents may contribute in ways that follow their abilities, but must not conduct the primary robot rescue while the boys remain remote. The robot's survival or death is not a target to force.

## Proposed story shape and tests before drafting

1. Open at the doctor's room, keep the following-morning examination on the page, and let a clinician give specific cautious advice grounded in the symptoms actually reported. No instant Senzu or automatic blanket clearance. The family can reasonably help search a reachable river bend for Mina's lost books under supervision; any accident must grow out of that task rather than foolish parenting.
2. A bank or ancient opening can fail beneath both boys. Goshin should use his flight to protect them, and Gohan should use Nimbus if it can reach him. Establish why a sandfall or stone obstruction prevents immediate escape even with those abilities. If parents cannot simply blast through, show the occupied, unstable support and what Goku actually does; no arbitrary anti-ki barrier or adult incompetence.
3. Spend substantial story time with C-6 and **both** children: introductions, repeated sleep requests, differing reactions, scorpion, a genuine food interaction, history, trust, danger and choices. Give each brother independent ideas and physical contributions. C-6 decides for himself about his power, sensor and rescue. The children—not a radio-linked adult team—do the decisive work at the robot.
4. Consider letting a child's well-intended action worsen the structure, followed by an apology and a safer plan. If outside family help becomes possible, specify the route, knowledge and division of work; it may support the boys but not replace their rescue. The result for C-6 must emerge from the actual staged action and costs.
5. Maintain the standing repeat-back of applicable instructions before compliance, invitations into Goku's conversations, Goshin's downhill position with Gohan, hands behind his back when Chi-Chi looks him over, and watched food preparation. Goshin may react instinctively in danger; no free numerical growth, new mastery, early golden form or god ki. Do not make him less capable than Gohan or take away Gohan's compassion and scholar interests.
6. Check the emotional result, symptoms, doctor follow-up, family's locations, Nimbus, both tails, three known Dragon Balls, the separated scouter/cell, Krillin's one bean, Piccolo's knowledge, summons and unanswered King Kai inquiry. Preview the next source episode without claiming Yamcha or Tien has already been recruited.
