## COLD OPEN

The sea reaches the beach at Kame House. A broken board lies at the water's edge, and another wave lifts it.

**THE RESCUE**

---

## 1 · THE ISLAND

**NARRATOR:** Raditz has come for Goku. Goku refused him, and Raditz has turned toward Gohan and Goshin.

Goku steps between Raditz and the boys. Raditz drives a knee into his stomach and sends him down into the sand.

"Goku!" Krillin shouts from the broken wall of the house.

Gohan runs toward his father. Bulma catches his arm, but Goshin pulls free of her other hand and moves in front of Goku.

Raditz reaches past him for Gohan. Goshin catches Raditz's wrist in both hands and pushes it away.

"Don't touch him."

Raditz turns his hand and throws Goshin backward. Goshin lifts before he hits the sand, stops in the air and rushes back. His fist strikes Raditz's jaw and turns his head.

Raditz steps back. Goshin lands between him and Gohan.

"Gohan, go to Father."

Gohan pulls free of Bulma and runs to Goku. He drops to his knees beside him and takes his hand.

"Father! Get up!"

Goku tries to rise. His arm shakes and he falls back onto his side.

Raditz moves toward them. Roshi kneels beside Goku and pulls him farther from the space between Raditz and the house. Krillin moves Gohan behind his father, while Bulma keeps the doorway clear for them.

Goshin goes still as Gohan calls for Goku. Ki flares around him, lifting sand from the beach.

"Stay away from my father," he says.

Goshin fires from his palm. The blast hits Raditz's armour and drives him across the beach, away from Goku and Gohan. Sand sprays into the water behind him.

Raditz plants a foot and stops. His scouter beeps. The number rises past the four hundred it showed before and keeps climbing.

Goshin sends two quick blasts after the first. Raditz bats one away, but the other strikes his shoulder and turns him toward the sea. He catches himself above the water and flies back over the beach.

Goku tries to stand again. Gohan puts both hands against his father's arm, and Krillin helps him to his feet. Goku takes one step before the pain in his stomach forces him down again.

"Get the boys away," Goku tells Krillin.

"I'm trying," Krillin says.

"Broc," Raditz says. "Come here."

"My name's Goshin."

He flies at Raditz again. Raditz catches his first punch, but Goshin twists, kicks his arm aside and strikes him twice in the chest. Raditz blocks the third blow and sends him down the beach with a backhand.

Goshin's feet drag through the sand. He steadies himself and fires another blast. Raditz turns his palm and knocks it toward the sea. It bursts against the water, throwing spray over the beach.

Roshi steps in front of Bulma. "Get back inside!"

"Goku's still out here!"

Krillin reaches Goku and helps him sit up. Gohan stays on the other side, holding Goku's arm. Goku looks toward Goshin.

"Goshin! Stay away from him!"

Raditz raises one hand. A blast leaves his palm and crosses the beach toward Goshin. Goshin turns his own hand the way Raditz did. The blast glances away and strikes the sea, but the contact burns his palm.

He holds the hand close for a moment, then flies forward again. Raditz blocks a punch at his face. Goshin drops below the return strike and drives his shoulder into Raditz's stomach, forcing him back another step.

Gohan gets to his feet. "Leave my brother alone!"

Raditz looks toward Gohan. Goshin moves between them and raises both hands.

"Don't go near him."

Raditz comes at him from the side. Goshin turns to meet him, strikes his forearm away and kicks at his chest. Raditz catches the kick and swings him toward the water. Goshin twists free, rises and comes back before Raditz can reach Gohan.

Goshin sends a rapid line of ki blasts across Raditz's path. Raditz blocks the first two and moves around the third. Goshin follows the change in direction, turns in the air and strikes him in the side.

Raditz grabs for Goshin's tail. Goshin pulls it clear, catches Raditz's reaching arm and drives another blast into his armour. Raditz pulls his arm free and lands near the broken wall.

"Gohan, run," Goshin says.

Gohan takes Goku's hand instead. "Father, come with us!"

Raditz fires at the ground beside Gohan. Goshin flies across the beach and pushes his brother behind him. The blast tears through the sand in front of them.

Gohan catches Goshin's sleeve. "Come on!"

"Get behind the house," Goshin says.

Gohan pulls at him. "You too!"

Raditz raises his hand again, aiming at both boys. Goshin pushes Gohan toward Krillin and turns his burned palm outward. The blast slides off his hand and explodes above the sea.

Raditz follows his own attack through the spray. Goshin turns and brings his injured hand up to block, but Raditz knocks it aside and strikes the side of his head. Goshin drops onto the beach and does not get up.

"Goshin!" Gohan runs to him and tries to lift his shoulders. Goshin's eyes stay closed.

Goku gets one foot under him. "Get away from my sons!"

Raditz catches Gohan around the waist and pulls him away. Gohan kicks and reaches for Goshin, but Raditz holds him with one arm. Only then does Raditz bend and pick up Goshin with the other.

Krillin steps forward. Raditz turns toward him, and Krillin stops beside Goku.

"Goshin!" Gohan calls. He touches his brother's sleeve and waits for an answer. Goshin does not respond.

---

## 2 · THE WARNING

Raditz rises above the beach with Gohan struggling under one arm and Goshin unconscious under the other. Goku pushes himself up, with Krillin supporting his shoulder.

"Put them down."

"You have one day," Raditz says. "Kill a hundred people by this time tomorrow. Pile their bodies here on the island, and you can have your children back."

"He won't kill anyone for you," Krillin says. "You're using his children because he won't come with you."

"Then the children die."

Roshi steps beside Goku and looks up at Raditz. "Goku has spent his life protecting people. He won't do what you're asking."

"He can decide that when he has had time to think."

Bulma stands at the water's edge. "You hurt him!"

Raditz looks at Goshin. "They're my brother's children. I'd rather not kill them. Kakarot can make sure I don't have to."

Gohan twists to look at Goshin. His brother's chest rises against Raditz's arm. Gohan catches his sleeve and keeps hold of it.

"Gohan, is he breathing?" Goku calls.

"Yes, but he won't wake up!"

Goku reaches for them, but Raditz rises farther from the beach before Krillin can help him stand.

"After we take the planet we're working on, Earth is our next target," Raditz says. "Three Saiyans could clear this world in a month. Your friend can kill a hundred now, or wait until we kill the rest."

Goku looks from Gohan to Goshin. Raditz lifts both boys higher, keeping them away from the hands reaching toward them from the beach.

"Don't listen to him, Father!" Gohan calls.

Goku tries to pull free of Krillin. "I'm coming after you!"

"Without the hundred? I'll kill them before you get close."

Raditz turns toward the sea. Bulma runs into the surf as he flies away, but he passes beyond the beach. Gohan reaches toward Goku until Raditz carries them out over the water.

Goku takes a step after them. His legs give way, and Krillin catches him before he falls.

"Goku! Wait!"

---

## 3 · THE TAIL

Goku pushes himself onto one knee. He watches the sky above the sea, then moves away from Krillin's hand.

"Goshin needs help. I've got to get them. Nimbus!"

A yellow cloud comes over the water and stops beside the beach. Goku gets one foot under him, but his arm buckles when he tries to rise.

"You'll get yourself killed," Bulma says. "He put you down with one hit."

"I know what he did."

"You can't help them if you rush in and die," Krillin says. "At least let us come with you."

Goku looks at the hole in the wall where Raditz threw Krillin. Broken boards cover the floor inside, and Krillin is favouring one leg as he walks back to the beach.

"He hurt you too."

"I'm all right. Tell me what you need."

"Then stop for a minute," Roshi says. "We need a way to beat him."

Goku presses a hand against his stomach. The water has reached Bulma's knees; she comes back onto the beach and kneels beside him.

"Can you stand?"

"Yeah. Give me a second."

Bulma looks out over the water. "You find out you have a brother, and he turns out to be someone like that."

Krillin follows her gaze. "He's stronger than any of us."

Goku turns toward him.

"His tail."

"What about it?"

"Get hold of it and squeeze hard. That might give us a chance."

Roshi nods. "The difficulty is getting close enough to take it."

"I can't do that on my own," Goku says. "He'll hit me before I get behind him."

"What if I keep him looking at me?" Krillin asks. "You could come at him from behind."

"You saw how hard he hits," Goku says.

"I did. I can still keep him busy for a little while."

Bulma picks up Roshi's staff from the sand and hands it back. Roshi brushes the grit off the end, then plants it beside his foot.

"One of us may not be enough," he says. "If we attack together, we can give Goku a chance."

Krillin looks at Roshi. Roshi sets his staff against the broken wall.

"Then you won't go alone," Roshi says. "The three of us can try."

"Right," Krillin says. "One of us keeps him busy, and another goes for the tail."

Goku looks at both of them. "You'd do that?"

"They're your kids," Krillin says. "Of course we will."

Krillin turns to Bulma. "If I don't make it, you can bring me back with the Dragon Balls, can't you?"

"We'll find them," Bulma says.

Goku's hand drops from his stomach.

"Krillin, you've already been brought back. So has Master Roshi."

Krillin's smile disappears. He looks at Roshi, who is standing very still beside the wall.

"Oh," Krillin says. "That's right."

"We'll still go," Roshi says.

"Yeah," Krillin says. "We said we would."

Goku puts a hand on Krillin's shoulder. Krillin looks down at it, then back at Goku.

"Tien and Yamcha aren't here," Krillin says. "You'd help me if it were my family."

"You don't even have a family yet," Bulma says.

"I could have one someday."

"You will," Goku says. "All of us are coming back."

Bulma folds her arms and looks down at the wet sand.

"What if we get all seven Dragon Balls first? We could ask Shenron to save the world."

"Could you find all seven before tomorrow?" Roshi asks.

Bulma looks up. "Not that quickly."

Goku gets to his feet. This time he stays up.

"Then we'll go after him now. He doesn't expect us to come to him."

"We don't know where he went," Krillin says.

Goku turns to Bulma. "The Dragon Radar. Have you got it?"

"Gohan's hat!"

Bulma runs inside through the damaged doorway. She returns with a small round device and presses its button. A light moves across the screen.

"There. That's the Dragon Ball on Gohan's hat."

Goku leans over her shoulder. The light travels across the display, then stops.

"At least they're still on Earth," Bulma says.

Krillin comes around to look at the screen. The light is inland, beyond a long stretch of coast, and it remains in the same place when Bulma changes the range.

"Can you tell how far away it is?" he asks.

"Far enough that we can't walk there before tomorrow."

"Let me see where he is."

Bulma turns the screen toward him. Goku studies the position and closes his hand around the edge of the device.

"All right. We're going."

"You won't beat him."

The voice comes from above the house. All four of them look up.

Piccolo hangs in the air beyond the broken roof. His cape moves in the sea wind, but he does not come down.

"Piccolo," Krillin says.

---

## 4 · AN OFFER

Goku steps away from the others and looks up at Piccolo.

"How did you get here?"

"I followed Raditz. I met him before he came to this island."

"Then you know how strong he is."

"I know that none of you can beat him alone. You and I may have a chance together."

"What happened when you met him?" Goku asks.

"I attacked him. He was still standing when I was finished, and then he went looking for you."

Goku looks at the torn beach, then back at Piccolo. "You couldn't stop him either."

"No. That's why I'm here."

Krillin moves beside Goku. "Why would you help us?"

Piccolo drops onto the sand. Krillin raises his hands, but Piccolo keeps his attention on Goku.

"Don't misunderstand. I don't care about your sons, and I'm not doing this for peace. I want this world for myself. Raditz and the two Saiyans with him are in my way."

Goku's hands close at his sides.

"And after they're gone?" he asks.

"I'll deal with you. Then I'll take the world."

"I won't let you do that."

"We'll see."

Goku looks at the radar in Bulma's hand. The light on the screen has not moved.

"For now, we both want Raditz," he says. "That's enough for me."

"I don't like working with you any more than you like working with me," Piccolo says. "Put up with it."

"Goku," Roshi says, "are you sure?"

"He's right about one thing. We need somebody strong enough to hold Raditz while the other one gets his tail."

Piccolo's eyes narrow. "What about his tail?"

"If we grab it, he'll weaken," Goku says. "I can't get to it alone."

"Then one of us will have to hold him off."

Bulma holds out the radar. "Take it. Keep the screen facing up so you can see if he moves."

Goku takes the device and tucks it into his belt. He looks once more at the line where the sea meets the sky.

"Wait for us, boys."

He looks at Piccolo. "Can you keep up with Nimbus?"

"Don't compare my flying with yours," Piccolo says. "Let's go."

Goku steps onto Nimbus. Piccolo lifts beside him, and they turn toward the open sea.

Krillin steps forward. "Goku!"

"What?"

"Bring them back."

Goku nods. Nimbus carries him over the water, with Piccolo flying beside him.

Bulma watches until they are beyond the island. The radar is gone with Goku, but she has seen where the light stopped.

"Do you really think Piccolo will help him?" she asks.

"Goku and Piccolo are the strongest pair we could have sent," Roshi says. "Together, they might be able to win."

"But can we trust Piccolo?"

"He wants Raditz out of the way. For now, that gives them the same enemy."

Krillin picks up a piece of the broken wall and sets it aside. "And after Raditz?"

"We can worry about that when the boys are safe," Roshi says. "Bulma, you remember the position?"

"Yes."

"Then get us a vehicle. We're going after them."

Bulma opens her case and takes out a capsule. She throws it onto the sand beyond the broken wall, and a compact aircraft appears in a burst of smoke.

Krillin opens the door while Roshi retrieves his staff. Bulma climbs into the pilot's seat and starts the engine; Krillin helps Roshi into the back before taking the seat beside her.

"If Piccolo changes his mind, Goku will need us," Krillin says.

"And if he doesn't, we'll still need to get the boys home," Bulma answers.

The aircraft lifts over the beach and turns toward the coast.

## 5 · OVER THE SEA

**NARRATOR:** Earlier, while the people on the island were making their plan, Raditz was carrying the brothers across the sea.

Gohan reaches across Raditz's armour and takes Goshin's hand. Goshin's fingers do not close around his.

"Goshin. Wake up."

Raditz adjusts his grip and keeps flying. The island is far behind them now.

"You hit him," Gohan says. "You hurt my father and you hit Goshin."

"Your brother attacked me."

"You attacked us first!"

Raditz looks down at Gohan. "If your father does as he's told, neither of you needs to be hurt again."

Gohan pulls at the arm holding him. Raditz tightens his grip, and Gohan stops struggling long enough to reach Goshin's hand again.

"Goshin, it's me. Please wake up."

Raditz carries them past a steep section of coast. Gohan twists to see Goshin's face and brushes his hair away from the place where Raditz struck him. His brother's breathing is steady, but he still does not open his eyes.

"Hold on," Gohan whispers. "Father will come."

The coast grows larger ahead. Goshin remains still as Raditz carries both boys inland.

## 6 · THE BOOK

Chi-Chi sits on the step with a bowl on her lap and Gohan's schoolbook open in her hands. Two lines of sums are done on the first page. She turns it and finds two more finished, then several pages with no answers at all.

"Half," she says. "Half of it done, and gone off without a word to me."

A chicken comes around the corner and stops beside the bowl.

"No," Chi-Chi says. The chicken turns and goes.

She looks toward the empty road below the gate. "He's sitting in that house with his feet under a table, being fed. Goshin too."

Chi-Chi closes the book around its ribbon and takes it inside. She returns with a stool and places the book on it, squared with the edge of the boards.

"They can finish this when they get home."

## 7 · THE TRUCK

Raditz lands in a crater surrounded by bare hills. His round space pod rests at the bottom. A blue pickup stands on the gravel beside it, with its hood raised.

Raditz puts both boys down. Goshin lies on his side, and Gohan kneels beside him.

"Stay there," Raditz says. He walks toward the pod.

Gohan shakes Goshin's shoulder. "Come on. Please wake up."

Goshin's eyes remain closed. Gohan slips his arms beneath his brother's shoulders and pulls him toward the truck. Goshin's shoes scrape across the gravel.

Raditz bends over the pod's hatch control. Gohan pulls again. He gets Goshin behind the front wheel and crawls under the truck with him.

"We'll wait here," Gohan whispers. "Father will find us."

He holds Goshin against him and watches Raditz through the gap beside the wheel.

Raditz turns from the pod. He looks at the marks on the ground, then at the pickup. Gohan draws his feet beneath the truck.

"Come out," Raditz says.

Gohan presses closer to Goshin. Raditz walks around the truck and stops beside the open hood.

Gohan covers his brother's head. "Go away!"

Raditz drives his fist down into the engine. Metal folds around his arm. He pulls upward, lifting the truck clear of the boys.

Gohan stares up at him. Goshin lies across his lap, still unconscious.

"Leave my brother alone!"

Raditz throws the truck high over the far rim of the crater. He raises his hand and fires. Light surrounds the pickup, and burning pieces fall beyond the ridge.

Gohan pulls Goshin closer as the sound rolls back across the crater.

"I want my father!"

"Stop crying," Raditz says. "You're a Saiyan too."

He bends and reaches for Goshin. Gohan holds on to his brother's sleeve, but Raditz pulls Goshin free and carries him toward the pod. Gohan scrambles after them.

Raditz opens the hatch.

"Get inside."

"I want to go home!"

"You'll see your father tomorrow if he does as he's told."

Raditz places Goshin in the pod. Gohan climbs through the open hatch to reach his brother. Raditz shuts the door behind them.

Gohan puts a hand on Goshin's shoulder. "Goshin?"

Goshin's head rests against the seat. He is breathing, but his eyes remain closed. Gohan pulls him away from the edge of the seat and holds him upright.

"Please wake up."

He looks at the closed hatch, then pounds on it.

"Let us out! My brother's hurt!"

"Keep quiet in there!" Raditz calls from outside.

"I want my father!"

Gohan tries the hatch handle. It moves partway and stops. He pulls again, planting both feet against the seat, but it will not open.

He presses the buttons beside the hatch. Lights appear on the panel, yet the door stays shut. He takes his hat off and strikes the handle with the hard ball on top; the handle bends without releasing the lock.

Goshin slips sideways against the seat. Gohan puts down his hat and catches him before his head hits the panel. He lays him back carefully and keeps one hand on his shoulder.

"Father is going to find us," Gohan says. "He has to."

Outside, Raditz steps away from the pod. "Now I need something to eat."

His scouter gives a warning signal. He looks toward the surrounding hills and touches the control at his temple.

"Seven hundred and ten? It's close."

The signal stops. Raditz looks across the crater, then at the pod.

"It must be damaged."

Inside, Gohan uses his sleeve to wipe the sand from Goshin's face. He takes his brother's hand again.

"I'm here."

## 8 · APPROACHING

Nimbus carries Goku over the hills, with Piccolo flying beside him. Goku takes the Dragon Radar from his belt and checks the light.

"We're close. We should land and go the rest of the way on foot."

"It won't help," Piccolo says. "That machine finds people and measures their strength. He'll know we're coming."

"Then we'll go straight at him."

Goku holds the radar where both of them can see it. The light remains beyond the next ridge, so he puts the device away and increases his speed.

"When we get there, I'll go for my boys," he says. "Can you keep Raditz busy?"

"If he comes at me first. Otherwise you'll have to hold him while I get behind him."

"All right. Whichever one of us has an opening goes for his tail."

They cross another ridge. Goku checks the radar again and points toward the bare hills ahead.

"Past there."

"Watch Raditz when we land," Piccolo says. "You won't reach the boys until we get past him."

Goku puts the radar away. The two of them descend toward the crater.

"Still broken."

Two new signals appear in the distance. Raditz checks the readings: **322** and **334**.

"One of them reads the same as Kakarot. But he doesn't know where I am."

Behind him, something strikes the inside of the pod.

## 9 · THE HATCH

Goshin opens his eyes. Gohan is beside him, still holding his hand.

"Goshin!"

Goshin touches the side of his head. He winces, then sits up and looks around the cramped cabin.

"Where's Father?"

"On the island. That man brought us here. He said Father has until tomorrow."

Goshin grips the edge of the seat. "He hurt Father."

"Krillin and Bulma were there. They'll help him."

Goshin sees the closed hatch. He gets to his feet, with one hand against the seat while the cabin tilts around him. He takes a breath and steadies himself.

"Is he outside?"

Gohan nods. "He wouldn't let us go."

"Move back."

Gohan pulls his hat onto his head and stands against the seat. Goshin plants his feet in front of the hatch and raises his good hand.

Ki gathers around his palm. He fires at the edge of the door. The lock breaks; the hatch bursts outward and strikes the crater floor.

Goshin takes Gohan's hand. They climb out together, stepping over the bent hatch onto the crater floor. Goshin starts toward the open side of the crater, but a shadow crosses the ground in front of them.

Raditz turns from his scouter. "Get back in there."

"No," Goshin says.

Raditz reaches for Gohan. Goshin strikes his arm aside, pulls Gohan behind him and fires at Raditz's chest. Raditz blocks the blast, but it pushes him away from the pod.

"Gohan, hold on to me."

Gohan wraps his arms around Goshin's shoulders. Goshin lifts and flies toward the rim. Raditz comes after them and fires across their path. Goshin turns the blast aside with his palm and carries Gohan past it.

They reach the top of the crater. Goshin lands behind a broad rock and puts Gohan down.

"Stay here while I keep him away."

"Don't let him hit you again," Gohan says.

Raditz rises above the rim. Goshin meets him before he reaches the rock. Their fists collide; Raditz pushes forward, and Goshin twists out of his reach and kicks him in the side.

Raditz's scouter beeps rapidly. Goshin attacks again, striking the arm Raditz raises to defend himself. The force drives Raditz back across the rim.

Raditz fires. Goshin slips below the blast and sends his own toward Raditz's face. Raditz turns it away, then moves behind Goshin and reaches for his tail.

Goshin turns and catches his wrist. He fires at Raditz's shoulder from close range, forcing Raditz to pull back.

Raditz sweeps his other hand toward Goshin's head. Goshin blocks with his forearm, pivots and drives both feet against Raditz's chest. The push separates them long enough for Goshin to move back in front of Gohan.

Gohan picks up a loose stone. Raditz dives toward Goshin; Gohan throws the stone into his path. Raditz knocks it away, and Goshin uses the opening to drive a fist into his jaw.

Raditz lands several paces back. He wipes his mouth and looks at the blood on his hand.

"You'll pay for that."

Goshin stands in front of Gohan, with his burned hand raised. Raditz advances, but Nimbus comes over the ridge. Goku jumps down between him and the boys, and Piccolo lands beside Goku.

"Gohan! Goshin!" Goku calls.

"Father!" Gohan runs to him. Goku drops to one knee and catches him. Goshin lands beside them, and Goku puts a hand on the side of his head, away from the place Raditz struck.

"How's your head?"

"It hurts, but I'm all right."

Goku looks at the broken hatch below, then at Raditz.

"Get Gohan behind that ridge," Goku tells Goshin. "If you can, take him back to the island."

"Raditz came after us when I tried."

"Piccolo and I will give you a chance."

Goshin takes Gohan's hand and leads him behind the broad rock. He looks back toward Raditz, ready to move when Goku and Piccolo can draw him away. Gohan stays beside him, watching their father.

## 10 · THE FIGHT BEGINS

Raditz looks from Goku to Piccolo.

"So the other one was you. How did you find me?"

"I'm not telling you," Goku says.

"You came here to fight your own brother?"

"You're no brother of mine. I came for my sons. Leave them alone."

"You still refuse to join us?"

"I told you. I'm not going anywhere with you."

Piccolo pulls his cape from his shoulders and drops it. The weighted cloth strikes the ground. He removes his turban and sets it beside the cape.

Goku looks at him. "You've been training with weights too?"

"Yes."

Raditz's scouter sounds. **408** appears as Piccolo steps away from the discarded clothes.

"You've been working hard," Goku says. "I'm glad you're on my side for now."

Goku removes his own weighted gear and lets it fall. Dust rises around his boots. The scouter sounds again: **416**.

Raditz laughs. "You think that's enough?"

"We have a plan," Goku says.

"I'm finished trying to recruit you, Kakarot. You'd be a burden to us, and you're a disgrace to our family. I'll kill you."

Goku and Piccolo move apart. Raditz watches both of them, with the broken pod below and Gohan and Goshin behind the rock on the rim.

Goku attacks from the right. Raditz catches his fist and drives a knee into his stomach. Goku bends around the blow, and Raditz throws him toward the crater wall.

Piccolo closes from the left and reaches for Raditz's tail. Raditz knocks his hand away and strikes him across the face.

Piccolo lands on one knee. Goku hits the rock, breaks through a slab and rolls back onto his feet.

Raditz flies at Piccolo. Piccolo raises both arms, but Raditz passes his shoulder and strikes him in the back. Before Piccolo can turn, Raditz moves toward Goku.

Goshin rises from behind the rock. "Gohan, stay down."

He flies toward Raditz as Goku blocks a blow at his head. The next punch lands in Goku's ribs and drives him against the wall. Goshin fires at Raditz's arm, turning it away from his father.

Goku catches Raditz's other wrist and swings him toward Piccolo. Piccolo reaches for the tail, but Raditz stamps down on Goku's foot, tears his wrist free and strikes Piccolo in the ribs.

Goshin comes in from the side. Raditz blocks his punch and answers with a kick; Goshin catches it on both forearms and is pushed back through the air. He stops above the crater floor and attacks again.

Goku moves away from the wall and fires a short burst toward Raditz's right side. Raditz raises one arm to stop it, leaving his left side open. Goshin closes the distance and strikes him twice before Raditz can turn.

Piccolo comes in behind them. Raditz catches Goshin's next punch, uses the arm to pull him across Piccolo's path and kicks Piccolo away. Goshin twists free and follows with another blast, forcing Raditz back toward the middle of the crater.
