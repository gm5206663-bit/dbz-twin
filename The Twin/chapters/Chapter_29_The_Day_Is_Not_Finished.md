# Chapter Twenty-Nine — The Day Is Not Finished

*New local review draft; not yet accepted.*

Goku presses the button on the capsule and sets it on a clear patch of stone. A puff of smoke rises between him and Goshin. When it clears, a travel box sits open at their feet.

Yajirobe leans over Goku's shoulder. "What's in it?"

"Let's see."

There are sealed packets of crackers and dried fruit, several bottles of water and a folded cloth tucked into one corner. Goku lifts the cloth and looks beneath it.

"That's all?" Yajirobe says.

"I ate some on the way to King Kai."

"And some on the way back?" Gohan asks.

Goku grins. "Some."

Goshin reaches into the box. Yajirobe reaches at the same time. Goshin draws out two packets and slips one behind his back before Yajirobe can take it.

"I saw that," Yajirobe says.

"Good. This one's yours."

Yajirobe opens the packet Goshin gives him, then eyes the fruit in the other one.

"I'll trade you," Goshin says.

"I'll take the bigger half."

"We'll see about that."

They divide the packets between them, arguing over which half is bigger.

Goku looks into the box once more. He had kept checking the capsule on the long road back, saving what he could for a journey whose end he could not yet see. Now his sons are eating from it beside him.

Gohan laughs and takes a bottle of water to Chi-Chi. She has the two bags beside her and the folded blanket on the car seat. She drinks, then passes the bottle to him.

Goku opens another packet and offers it around. Goshin takes a handful and sits beside his mother. Gohan settles on her other side. They eat with the box between them while the wind rattles the broken rails.

A short distance away, Krillin watches Vegeta. Tien and Chiaotzu remain near the gully with Piccolo, where Nappa is resting against the wall. Launch stays close to Tien. Yajirobe puts Upa's flask beside him while he eats.

Goku sits with his family, but his eyes return to the prince whenever Vegeta moves. The Saiyan shifts against the stone and stares at them all.

"Does your side still hurt?" Chi-Chi asks Goshin.

"A little when I turn."

"Tell me if it gets worse."

"I will." He takes another piece of fruit. "I can still eat this way."

"That's a relief," Gohan says.

Goshin offers him a piece. When Gohan reaches for it, Goshin pops it into his own mouth. Gohan grabs the packet, and Goshin catches his wrist before he can pull it away.

For a moment Goshin forgets the broken quarry around them. This is the kind of argument they had at the little table in the room, except that both their parents can hear it now.

"I was going to share!"

"You just ate the one you offered me."

Goshin considers this. He holds out the packet. Gohan takes two pieces and hands it back.

Goku watches them, smiling. The last time he had eaten with them at home, their arms had not reached so far across the space between them. He knew they had lived a year together. He keeps finding small places where that year shows.

"What was it like?" he asks Goshin. "Getting so big."

Goshin sits up. "I could see over everything. Vegeta looked big too, but I could hold him. My hands were enormous!"

He spreads his fingers as wide as he can. Goku spreads his own hands beside them.

"Bigger than mine?"

"Much bigger. I heard Gohan and Mother, and I could still think. I knew it was me."

"That's amazing."

"I liked it," Goshin says. "Except when he tried to hurt Mother."

Chi-Chi's gaze goes to the torn wall. She remembers the light gathering in Vegeta's mouth, then the blue wave from Goshin's enormous hands turning it into the sky. Her fingers close on the edge of her skirt.

Beside Goku, Goshin has almost his father's face and the same unruly black hair. When he turns to her, his eyes are hers. She had known those eyes even when she had to look up past the ridge to find them.

"You turned that away from us," she says.

"I wasn't going to let it hit you."

She puts her arm around him, careful of his shoulder. "I know. I'm glad you could stop him."

Goshin leans against her. After a moment, he takes the hand gripping her skirt and puts it around himself too. She holds him there while he goes on telling his father how big he was.

Gohan looks at the footprints cut deep into the quarry floor. "He could hear me too. I asked him to keep Vegeta away from the light."

Gohan had wanted his father to see the shot. He points past the broken ridge instead, toward the place where he stood with Piccolo.

"I kept my face down so I wouldn't see the light. Piccolo moved my arm until it was pointing the right way."

Goku shifts closer. "You fired without looking?"

"I could see my hands. And the rock beside me." Gohan lifts his arm to show him. "I remembered the gap between the blocks in the room."

"Vegeta tried to turn back," Goshin says. "I held his shoulders away."

Goku follows Gohan's pointing arm up into the empty sky. "You both had to get that right."

Goku leans to follow the line of Gohan's raised hand. Gohan lowers his chin as he had beside Piccolo and points out the edge of the spur. His shoulder aches, but he wants his father to see where he stood.

Gohan lowers his hand. He had not been sure it would work, right up to the moment the light broke apart. His father is still looking where the wave went, and Gohan starts telling him what Vegeta had done to his first shot.

Yajirobe reaches for another cracker while they talk. Goshin moves the packet out of his reach without looking. Yajirobe takes one from the other side, and even Chi-Chi laughs.

## Taking a Turn

A scrape comes from the gully. Nappa has planted a hand beside him and is trying to move his leg. Tien steps where he can see the Saiyan's other hand. Chiaotzu floats up beside the wall.

Gohan starts to rise, but Goku is already on his feet.

"I'll take a turn," he says.

Goshin stands too. "I can stay with you."

"You can. You can finish eating first too."

Goku walks to Krillin. Together they face Vegeta, who has been watching Nappa's effort.

"Your children are doing your work for you, Kakarot," Vegeta says.

"They did a lot while I was away. I'm proud of them. And they didn't learn everything from me."

Vegeta's expression hardens. "Give me my scouter. I'll call my ship and leave."

Krillin glances toward the two devices on the stone. "You tried to kill Nappa after you woke up. We're not handing you a way to reach a ship just because you say you'll go."

"You'd rather keep me here?"

"I'd rather nobody else got hurt," Goku says. "We're going to work out how to get you help. Until then, you stay where we can see you."

Vegeta looks from Goku to Krillin. He makes no move toward the devices.

Goku can feel the prince's ki. He wants to see for himself how Vegeta fights, and his sons' account has only made him more curious. Vegeta is injured now, though, and there are people Goku has promised to protect.

At the gully, Nappa lets his leg settle again. Piccolo keeps his place near the opening. Gohan watches until Tien gives him a small nod.

"Bulma said she'd ask about the people upstairs," Gohan says to Chi-Chi. "She told them I could help later. Can I go and see what they need?"

Chi-Chi looks toward the city. She had left the unchecked rooms to the rescue crew, but she has been waiting to hear what they found. Gohan had wanted to go before and had stayed to watch the Saiyans instead.

"I'll come with you," she says, reaching for the bags.

Goshin folds the empty fruit wrapper. "Me too. If there's something to move, I want to help."

"The two of them are staying here?" Yajirobe asks, looking at the Saiyans.

"For now," Tien says. "Chiaotzu and I can watch Nappa."

"And I'm not leaving him alone with Vegeta," Piccolo adds.

Krillin touches the pouch inside his clothes, making sure it is still secure. "I've got the bean. Goku and I will watch the prince."

Goku comes back long enough to hear the plan. He had only just got his arms around his sons; he would like to go wherever they go. Krillin is standing alone by the prince now, though, and Tien and Chiaotzu are still at the gully.

He touches Gohan's shoulder. "I'd like to see you helping. But somebody has to stay here too."

Goshin puts his arms around him again. "You can see me when I show you my wave."

"I'm looking forward to it."

Gohan steps close enough for Goku to put an arm around him as well. "We'll come back and tell you what happens."

"You'd better. I missed a whole year of stories."

Popo lifts the edge of his carpet. "I can take Chi-Chi and the bags."

Chi-Chi closes the bag with the letter inside. The loose pages are still safe with it. She settles both bags on the carpet and looks toward Goku.

"We'll ask Bulma about the doctor too."

"I'll be here," he says.

Gohan and Goshin rise beside the carpet. Goshin glances down at his bare feet.

"I can fly over the glass."

"And land somewhere clear," Chi-Chi says.

"I know."

Popo takes them over the ridge. Goku stays until they pass out of sight, then turns back to the quarry and his place beside Krillin.

## The Building with the Broken Stairs

East City is still noisy. Sirens move along the clear roads, and people call to one another around the damaged buildings. The aircar route toward the school gym remains open.

Goshin sees a row of windows with boards across them and a bicycle lying on its side outside a shop. Their fight moved away from the city, but the people below are still dealing with what happened before it did. He stays close to the carpet as they pass.

As they pass the landing site, Gohan sees the two round objects through the dust. Officers are keeping the barrier in place. Farther back, a group with equipment cases is speaking to one of them; nobody is touching either object.

Popo lowers the carpet outside the gym. Bulma comes through the doorway with Yamcha and Puar as Chi-Chi steps down.

"Goku's back," Chi-Chi tells her.

Bulma stops. "He's here?"

"At the quarry. He and Krillin are watching Vegeta."

Bulma catches Chi-Chi's hands. "Oh, thank goodness. I told Roshi he hadn't arrived. Wait till he hears this."

"How's your arm?" Gohan asks Yamcha.

Yamcha lifts the dressed forearm. "Clean and still attached. Bulma's checked it twice."

"Three times," Puar says.

"Twice," Bulma and Yamcha say together.

Goshin laughs. "That sounds like Mother."

"I heard you," Chi-Chi says, and he grins at her.

The woman with the notebook meets them inside. Before Chi-Chi can ask about the building, a volunteer comes from the office with a message from the crew.

"They've reached one of the upstairs rooms," she says. "It was empty. They heard someone farther back, but the stairway isn't safe. They're looking for another way up."

"We can fly," Gohan says. "Can we go and ask them where they need us?"

The woman studies him, then looks at Chi-Chi. "The crew leader can tell you what they've found. Please don't move anything without speaking to her."

"We won't," Chi-Chi says.

Bulma points toward a woman carrying a medical bag at the next table. "A doctor is willing to talk about the Saiyans. I explained that they're injured and that they've attacked people."

The doctor comes over. She is older than Bulma, with her hair tied back and a pair of glasses pushed high on her nose.

"I'm Dr. Mori," she says. "Your friend said someone asked for help."

"I did," Gohan says. "Nappa was hurt badly in the fight. Vegeta is hurt too."

"Can they move?"

"Nappa tried to stand, but he couldn't," Gohan says. "Vegeta woke up and tried to shoot him. We stopped him."

Dr. Mori looks at the adults. "I need to know if I can examine either of them without being left alone."

"You won't be," Chi-Chi says. "Our friends are watching both. But I won't tell you there's no danger."

"I wouldn't believe you if you did." Dr. Mori adjusts the strap of her bag. "I'll speak to your friends before I go close. If either patient refuses or I think it's unsafe, I may have to stop."

"That's fair," Bulma says. "Let me make the calls here, and I'll take you there."

Gohan looks from the doctor toward the door. "Will you tell them we're coming back?"

"I'll tell them when we arrive. You go and talk to that crew."

Popo carries Chi-Chi and the bags toward the damaged building, with the boys flying alongside. The crew has marked the street beneath the broken stairs and moved the onlookers back. A woman in a hard hat stands near a truck, studying the upper wall.

Chi-Chi introduces them and points to the fractured stairwell. "We brought people out of the lower rooms. Bulma said you were checking upstairs."

"I'm Ren," the crew leader says. "We heard someone in the rear room. There's a window on that side, but the front stair has dropped against the passage. If that section shifts again, it could pull more of the wall with it."

Gohan circles upward until he can see the back of the building. A narrow window stands above a lower roof. He comes down beside Ren.

"I can reach the window without using the stairs. I can ask whoever's there if they can come out that way."

"Can you carry someone?"

"Yes."

Ren looks at the damaged front section again. A broad slab of stair and masonry rests crookedly across the entrance, with one end caught beneath the wall. "We'll keep everyone away from here while you look."

The collapsed flight includes a thick concrete landing and a long piece of the outer wall. Goshin follows the crack above it to the corner of the building; each small movement sends grit into the street.

At the quarry, he had caught the falling rock before it reached Piccolo and Krillin. This is a different weight, tangled with a building that still has people inside.

"I could hold that," he says.

Ren turns to him. "If you pull it straight out, it might take the wall with it."

"I won't pull it out. I can lift the broken part and hold the wall there too."

She looks at the small boy, then at Chi-Chi. Chi-Chi has seen what both her sons can lift. She has also heard the wall creak.

"Show her what you mean before anyone goes underneath," she says.

Goshin walks to the clear side of the entrance. He points to the place where the slab catches beneath the wall, then to the fractured edge above it. "I can raise this end just enough. I'll keep the rest from moving."

Ren studies the gap. "We have supports in the truck. If you can hold it, we can place them without standing beneath the slab. Wait until my people are ready."

"All right."

Gohan is already looking back toward the window. "I'll go around the other side."

"Call down if you find someone," Ren says. "I'll have a medic ready."

Gohan nods and rises. Goshin crouches beneath the projecting edge. Ren clears the entrance and signals her workers back. When she raises her hand, he puts both palms under the broken stair and lifts.

The stair, its landing and the masonry across it rise together. The load bears down on his hands, broader and heavier than the rock he had carried out of the quarry. He feels the pull in his sore side, but his arms raise it easily. He shifts one hand beneath the central steel beam and settles his shoulder under it. With the weight balanced there, he can hold it on one arm.

Goshin had felt that new strength while fighting Vegeta after they changed back. It answers him now, in his own small body. He grins at the great section of building hanging above him—then the cracked wall shifts against its caught end.

He reaches out with his free hand. His ki spreads along the fracture and holds the upper section in place. Bricks tremble; one drops free, and he catches that too before it reaches the street. There is far more power in the current than before his first change, and he can hold it while the weight rests on his arm.

"Is this high enough?" he calls.

Ren has stopped with one hand raised to her crew. She looks from Goshin to the cleared gap beneath the stairs. "Yes. Hold it there."

Two workers bring the supports. They keep to the side as Ren directs them, settling each brace where it can take weight without forcing the broken section farther into the wall.

On the far side of the building, Gohan reaches the rear window. Papers are caught against the dusty glass, with a handprint on the inside. Whoever made it may still be there. He taps on the frame.

"Hello? Is someone in there?"

A woman answers from inside. "The door won't open."

Gohan rises above the roof until he can see Ren on the other side.

"Someone's here!" he calls down. "I'm talking to her!"

Ren sends the waiting medic toward the clear side of the street, where Gohan will have room to land.

Gohan drops back to the window. "My name's Gohan. There are people outside to help. Can you get to the window?"

"I can walk. I can't climb over all this."

He eases the window higher and looks in. A bookcase has fallen across the doorway. The woman stands beyond it, one hand pressed to her dusty sleeve. Papers cover the floor around her.

"There's space beside the bookcase," Gohan says. "Can you get around it?"

She looks down, then picks her way around the end and comes to the sill. She looks past him at the drop.

"I can fly," Gohan says. "I can carry you down."

"Are you sure?"

"Yes. You don't have to jump. I'll hold you."

Gohan offers both hands and waits. When she takes them, he steadies her as she climbs through, lifts her clear of the window and flies down to the open street. She grips his sleeves as the roof drops away beneath them. He keeps his descent slow and steers clear of the broken front wall, where Ren and the crew are working.

"We're nearly down," he tells her.

Gohan lowers her to the pavement. Her hands loosen when her feet find the ground. A volunteer brings a chair, and the woman sits and takes a long breath.

Goshin watches their hands as well as the stone. He could keep holding it, but he wants the supports in the places Ren has chosen. When one worker changes her grip, he waits for her to set her feet before easing the weight down.

"Ready?" Goshin asks.

"A little lower. Slowly."

He keeps his ki against the wall while he brings his other hand back under the stair. He lowers the slab until the first support takes it, then eases the other end onto the second. Only when Ren checks both braces and tells him to let go does he release the wall.

Goshin steps back. A line of dust lies across his palm. He rubs it against his trousers, then looks up through the space beneath the braced stairs.

"It stayed," he says.

Ren tests the nearer support with the end of her tool. "Because you held it where we could brace it. Thank you."

He smiles at her. He wants to tell his father about the weight and how readily it had lifted. Across the cleared street, Gohan is beside the woman he brought down.

"My neighbour was in the room near the stairs," she says. "Did anyone find her?"

Ren kneels beside her. "We brought a woman from downstairs to the gym earlier. What does your neighbour look like?"

The woman describes her coat. Ren asks a volunteer to check the names at the gym.

"She's probably—" Gohan begins, then stops. He has not seen her neighbour. The woman keeps watching the street the volunteer takes, so he stays beside her until the medic comes over with water.

Gohan walks over to Goshin. "There was someone in the back room. She got out."

"I saw you with her," Goshin says. "I held up the stairs!"

"I saw that too."

Chi-Chi draws them both close, then lets go so they can look toward Ren. The crew leader is speaking to her workers about the next section of the building.

"We've checked those two rooms," Ren tells Chi-Chi. "There are still other places to look. I can't say we're finished."

"Do you need us here?" Gohan asks.

"My crew can handle the next part. You've made the entrance safer for us. If we find something we can't move, I'll ask the gym to get word to you."

Popo offers to take the message if they need him. Chi-Chi thanks Ren. As the family heads back toward the gym, the crew moves a ladder around to the next section of the building.

## The Telephone

Bulma is at the office door when they return. "I got through to Roshi. He nearly dropped the receiver when I said Goku was back."

"Did you tell him Dad ran all the way?" Goshin asks.

"I told him he made it. You can give him the details yourself."

Dr. Mori is checking the contents of her bag. Yamcha has found a small tool case for Bulma, and Puar is keeping the car door open for them.

Gohan looks toward the telephone. "Can we call Hatch before we go? You said he was asking about us."

"I did. Come on."

The director answers. After Bulma explains, there is a muffled sound as the receiver is passed along. Gohan hears someone calling Hatch's name.

"Hello?" Hatch says.

His voice sounds the way it did before the long year. Gohan remembers the drawing beside their table, the days he and Goshin counted and the evenings when they wished they could show Hatch what they were making. There is suddenly so much to tell him.

"It's Gohan. Goshin's here too."

"Are you all right?"

"Yes. We helped some people, and we're with Mother. Father came back too."

"Good. Chiko keeps asking if you've seen the strangers."

Gohan looks at his brother. "We have. The fighting stopped, and our friends are watching them. We don't know everything yet."

There is a pause on the line, followed by a voice farther away asking whether the boat drawing is safe.

"That's Chiko," Hatch says.

"Your drawing is with us," Gohan tells him. "We kept it safe."

Goshin leans close to the receiver. "It was beside our table for a whole year!"

"A year?" Hatch asks.

"A very long one," Goshin says. "We made lots of pictures. I want to show you."

"Did you finish the roof?"

"We made more ideas," Gohan says. "Your drawing is still yours. We didn't change it."

Hatch seems satisfied by that. He tells them the others are still with him in the hall and that the director is busy with the younger children. Chiko's school question has not been answered yet.

"We'll talk again," Gohan says. "I want to show you what we drew."

"And I want to see the next door you add," Goshin says.

"I haven't added another one," Hatch says.

"You could."

Hatch laughs. Gohan passes the receiver to Chi-Chi so she can speak to the director before they hang up.

Outside, Dr. Mori waits beside Bulma's car. "I've told the coordinator where I'm going. She knows the situation may change."

"I'll take you straight to our friends," Bulma says. "Yamcha and Puar are coming too."

Chi-Chi takes her place on the carpet with the bags. Gohan and Goshin lift beside it, and the two vehicles leave the gym together.

## Two Small Devices

At the quarry, Goku and Krillin are still near Vegeta. Tien, Chiaotzu and Piccolo remain by Nappa. Launch comes to meet the arriving car, with Yajirobe a few steps behind her. He has fastened Upa's flask at his side.

Goku looks up as the boys land. "Did you find the people?"

"One woman in the back room," Gohan says. "The crew is checking the rest."

"And Goshin held the broken stairs while the crew braced them," Chi-Chi says.

Goshin spreads his arms. "They were bigger than the rock at the quarry. Ren knew where to put the supports, and I held the wall too."

Goku's face lights up. "I wanted to see that!"

"Next time you can hold the other end."

"I'd like that."

Bulma brings Dr. Mori forward and introduces her. The doctor listens while Tien explains where the two Saiyans are and what happened when Vegeta woke. Krillin tells her he will stay close, and Piccolo says he will be beside Nappa.

"If either of them moves to attack, we'll stop him," Goku says. "I can't promise you they won't try."

Dr. Mori looks at the Saiyans from a distance. "Thank you for telling me. I'll ask before I touch either patient. I haven't treated anyone from their world, so I can't assume every medicine will be safe."

She takes a moment to arrange the things she may need at the top of her bag. The fighters have promised to stay near her. She still looks for a clear path back to the car before walking toward the gully.

Bulma opens the tool case. "Before we bring you close, I want to deal with those."

The scouters are still together on the stone. Neither Saiyan can reach them from where he lies. Bulma approaches the stone while Goku and Krillin keep watch.

"You're going to break them," Nappa says.

"I'm going to look at the power source. Raditz's scouter had a removable cell. These might be built the same way."

"Give me mine and I'll call the ships."

"You offered before. The answer's still no."

She turns the first device by its frame, keeping her fingers away from the controls. The lens faces the stone; she does not put it on or aim it at anyone. A narrow cover opens under the tip of her tool. She removes a small cell and sets it on a folded piece of cloth.

The second cover takes longer. Bulma works carefully around a bent edge until it slides free. She removes that cell too, then wraps each device and each cell separately.

"Are they off now?" Gohan asks.

"I've removed the cells I found. I haven't checked every circuit, and I don't know whether they have another power source. I also can't tell what they may already have sent."

Vegeta watches her close the tool case. "You think taking those apart makes you safe?"

"No," Bulma says. "I think it gives me something to examine without giving either of you a control."

She keeps the wrapped devices and cells apart in the case. Nappa follows it with his eyes as she carries it away.

## A Doctor's Visit

Dr. Mori approaches the gully with Piccolo on one side and Tien on the other. Chiaotzu remains where he can see Nappa's hands. Gohan comes a little behind them; Goshin and Chi-Chi stop farther back, within sight of Goku and Vegeta.

Nappa looks at the medical bag. "What's in that?"

"Things to clean and dress injuries. A stethoscope. Some medicines, but I'm not offering those until I know more about you."

"I'm not taking a needle."

"I haven't asked you to." Dr. Mori crouches where he can see her face. "May I listen to your breathing and look at the places that hurt? You can tell me to stop."

Nappa glances toward Vegeta. The prince is watching, his mouth set hard.

"I need my ship," Nappa says.

"We aren't giving you a way to call it," Tien says. "You can still let her look."

Nappa's hand tightens on the ground. At last he nods once. Gohan stays where Nappa can see him; the doctor keeps her movements slow enough for the injured man to follow.

Dr. Mori asks where he hurts. He indicates his side and lower back, then answers her questions about breathing with a scowl. She listens through the stethoscope where his damaged armour leaves room and asks him not to try standing while she examines him.

Gohan watches her hands. He remembers the doctor in the village asking Goshin before she touched him. Nappa is not like Goshin, and Gohan has seen what he meant to do in the city. He is glad the doctor asks him anyway.

"I can't tell exactly what's injured without more examination and equipment," Dr. Mori says when she finishes. "Moving him carelessly could make things worse. I would normally want him at a hospital, but we need a safe way to do that first."

"Your hospitals can't hold me," Nappa says.

"Then we'll have to consider that," she replies. "For now, can you breathe more easily sitting as you are?"

"Yes."

She checks whether he wants help cleaning a scrape on his arm. He lets her use water and cloth, then pulls away when she reaches for a dressing.

"Leave it."

She stops. "Tell me if your breathing becomes more difficult."

Vegeta gives a sharp laugh. "You let them fuss over you?"

Nappa turns his head. "You were going to kill me."

He had seen Vegeta destroy a world and had followed him afterward. Now the prince's raised hand is part of his own memory too. He does not look away from Vegeta while he waits for an answer.

"You failed."

"I was still going to take you out of here."

Vegeta does not answer. Nappa looks back at the doctor and keeps his arm where she can see it. He does not ask her to put on the dressing.

Dr. Mori rises. "May I speak to the other patient?"

Goku walks with her toward Vegeta. Krillin remains close enough to see the prince's hands.

"I'm a doctor," she says. "I'd like to examine you."

"No."

"Can you tell me where you're hurt?"

"I said no."

She waits a moment, then steps back. "If you change your mind, tell one of them."

Vegeta looks up at Goku. "You won't keep me on this rock forever."

"We're not going to pretend this is settled," Goku says. "Right now you're hurt, and there are people nearby. I'm staying."

Dr. Mori returns to her bag. She speaks with Bulma and Chi-Chi away from the Saiyans, keeping her voice low enough that she does not have to shout across the quarry.

"Nappa accepted a limited examination," she says. "He needs more than I can do here, but I don't know his anatomy well enough to promise an outcome. If you arrange a transfer, someone needs to protect the medical team and keep him from hurting anyone."

"And Vegeta refused," Bulma says.

"Yes. I can't tell you the extent of his injuries by looking from here."

Chi-Chi looks toward the city. "The gym can't take two people who might attack the patients."

"It shouldn't have to," Dr. Mori says. "I'll speak to the coordinator about what equipment and places might be available away from the shelter. Don't move Nappa without telling the team first."

"Before you go," Chi-Chi says, "could you look at my sons too? Their shoulders and Goshin's side are sore."

Dr. Mori turns to the boys. "May I?"

Gohan nods and tells her where Vegeta hit him. He lifts his arm when she asks, stopping at the point where the movement becomes uncomfortable. Goshin shows her the mark on his shoulder and explains about his side. She listens to both boys' breathing.

"I can still lift things," Goshin tells her.

"I heard," Dr. Mori says. "Does your side hurt when you breathe?"

He tries a deeper breath and shakes his head. "Only when I turn this way."

"Any dizziness?"

"No," Gohan says. Goshin shakes his head too.

"You're breathing comfortably sitting here," she tells them. "I can't rule out an injury from this short examination. If the pain gets worse or you have trouble breathing, tell an adult and get medical help. I'd like you both properly assessed when that's possible."

"We will," Chi-Chi says. She touches Gohan's hand, then Goshin's.

Bulma takes the tool case to the car with the four wrapped pieces still separate. She writes down a way to reach the doctor, who accepts a ride back. No ambulance has been arranged for the Saiyans. Yamcha and Puar go with Bulma and Dr. Mori. Before they leave, Goku thanks the doctor for coming.

"You told me the truth before I got here," she says. "That helped."

## A Name and a Place

The sun has moved across the quarry. Launch shares the last of the crackers with Chiaotzu, and Yajirobe pours some water from Goku's supplies into Upa's flask.

"I'm bringing it back full," he says when Goshin notices.

"Upa will like that."

"He'd like it better if I didn't lose the flask."

"You didn't."

"I know. I just said so."

Goshin smiles and goes to sit beside Gohan. Their father is close enough to speak to, though he keeps looking toward Vegeta. Chi-Chi has the bags at her feet. The letter remains folded inside.

Piccolo stands with Popo beyond the gully. Gohan sees them talking and goes over. Goshin follows him.

"Nappa called me a Namekian," Piccolo is saying. "He says Namek is a planet, but he can't tell me where it is."

Popo considers this. "Popo has not heard that name before."

"Kami hasn't said anything to you?"

"Not about Namek. A long time ago, Kami told Popo about a place where he lived when he was a child. Yunzabit. He waited there for his family, but they did not come."

Piccolo's eyes narrow. "Why was he there?"

"Popo doesn't know. Kami did not tell me why."

Popo remembers Kami speaking of that long wait. At the time, he had not known why a child would live there alone. The name Nappa has given Piccolo may mean something, but Popo cannot supply the missing answer himself.

Gohan looks from one to the other. "Could it have something to do with Namek?"

"It might," Popo says. "Or it might not. Kami can tell you what he remembers."

Piccolo looks toward the open sky. He has spent years knowing that his life and Kami's are bound together. That has never meant Kami would answer every question for him.

"I'll ask him myself," he says.

Gohan shifts closer. "Would you like me to come?"

Piccolo looks at him. "You don't have to."

"I know. I want to."

For a moment Piccolo says nothing. "When we can leave here, then."

Gohan nods. They have no answer yet, only a name Nappa gave them and a place Popo remembers Kami describing. It is enough for Piccolo to know what he wants to ask next.

Goku calls to the boys from the tracks. "You two promised me stories."

Goshin runs back first. "Gohan found the woman at the window all by himself. And the stairs were this big."

He stretches his arms wide. Gohan comes beside him and stretches his own arms wider.

"Bigger," he says.

"I know how big they were! I was holding them."

"Then show me properly," Goku says.

Goshin looks around for something to use. There are no crackers left. He picks up two small stones instead and places them on the rail: one for the wall, one for the broken stairs. Gohan adds a third for the window and begins explaining the way he flew around the back.

Chi-Chi sits beside them. Goku listens, asking questions whenever the boys leave something out. Across the quarry, Krillin and Tien keep their watch; Piccolo returns to the gully when Nappa shifts.

Chi-Chi's hands rest on the bags. She has not opened the letter, and there are still people whose care needs arranging. For a little while she can listen to both boys talking over each other while their father tries to make sense of the stones.

Goku picks up the stone that represents the stairs and balances it on his fingers. "Like this?"

"Only bigger," Goshin says.

Gohan moves the window stone clear of his father's hand and starts the story again.
