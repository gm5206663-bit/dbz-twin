# STATUS — the boy, designed

**Owns: the design, the standing, and the era.** Who he is at four: figure, face, speech, movement, appetites, faults, habits — then the biology, the scale, the traits canon certifies, the traits we declare, and what none of it is allowed to mean.

**The rule of this file:** **a resemblance is drawn, never fractioned.** "99% Goku" is **retired** — a casting note, not a design: it tells nobody what he does with his hands, what he asks, or what he is afraid of. Everything below is a thing a camera can see or an ear can hear.

**THE ERA LOCK — read this before anything else.** **As of the Chapter Nine draft he is four years old and he is nothing but a boy.** Mortal. One tail. No early golden form, god ki or divine promotion — **the ladder in §13 is documented for a much later era and is locked.** Natural Saiyan moon-and-tail biology is not itself prohibited; no ape event has occurred in this branch. In the Z-era he eats, trains, flies, learns techniques, plays, cries and grows; being mortal does not mean being helpless. **Sections 12–14 describe the FUTURE; §§15–17 include current constraints and open slots.** (`O045`)

**Where this file and a chapter disagree, this file is right and the chapter is rewritten.** Canon that has already explained something is **recorded** here, never restaged in prose.

## 2 · The face, and the half-second

**He watches mouths, not eyes.** When somebody talks to him he looks at how the words are made. It is not rudeness and it is not affection; it is a boy copying a machine he intends to run.

**The lag.** His face arrives **half a second after the room.** A joke lands, everyone laughs, and *then* his face does it — because he was still reading whether it was a joke. It is the single most useful thing about him on a page: it lets the narrator show him thinking without ever entering his head.

**When he decides something,** he does not nod. He looks away first, and then back, and then he does it.

## 3 · Speech — the repeat-it-back

- **He asks exact questions.** Not *when?* but *how many days?* Not *where?* but *which ridge?* A four-year-old's vocabulary doing an adult's filing.
- **He says an instruction back, word for word, before he does it.** He got this from his mother, who does it to his father in the kitchen — *"Say it back to me."* He has never been told to do it. He watched it done, and it works, so it is his.
- **Then he does it once.** Exactly once, exactly as said, and if it was said wrong he does it wrong and does not argue.
- **He never reports what he can do.** The deed is on the page or it did not happen. He does not narrate himself, and he does not say *watch this.*
- **Vocabulary of four.** The exception, always, is **timing** — the pause before an answer, the answer arriving late and complete.
- **He does not lie.** He also does not volunteer. Asked a question he does not want, he says something true about something else.
- **Never in his mouth:** *pure* · a boast · an explanation of a feeling · **"Broc"** · any word he has no reason to know.

## 4 · Movement and nerves

- **He copies posture without meaning to.** Sit like a person and he will be sitting like them in a minute. Stand like one and he will stand like that. It is not mockery and he cannot switch it off. **This is the human half of the Talent** — the same machinery that lets him master a technique after seeing it has him walking like a stranger because the stranger walked interestingly.
- **He stands still to think.** He never paces. The stillness is the working.
- **The louder the room, the quieter he gets.** Not sullen — *concentrating*. In noise he is at his most dangerous and looks at his calmest.
- **Foundations are serious work.** When he trains, he returns to what he can already do: stance, balance, footwork, guarding, breath, conditioning and ki control as they become relevant. He works on the basics of flight and techniques too. Show the repetitions, corrections and eventual results; do not treat the basics as a boring prelude he immediately abandons (`O105`).

## 6 · The three habits — discipline with no caption

- **with his mother:** when she looks him over, **his hands go behind his back.** He does not know he does it. She has never asked him to.
- **with his brother:** on any path, **he takes the downhill side.** Every time, without being asked, and if the path turns he crosses behind him to get it back.
- **with his father:** **he waits to be invited to speak.** He will stand at the edge of a conversation for a quarter of an hour, entirely comfortable, and answer the moment he is asked — never before.

## 7 · The fault, and the fear

**The rage is cold.** Canon's Gohan screams and erupts; this one **goes quiet.** The noise drains out of him, the fidgeting stops, and then it is done and it is over. **The 1,500 belongs to a silent boy** — which is why nobody sees it coming twice. Chapter Six uses it as one authored surge, not a lifetime cap (`O089`).

**What he is actually afraid of.** Not pain — he is a Saiyan child and pain is weather. Not the dark. **He is afraid of being the second to know.** Being told *after* is the thing that makes him small: after the decision, after the visitors, after the bad news. It is the fault that will generate scenes for a hundred chapters, and it is the one his mother hands down to him without either of them noticing.

## 12 · The gate — the one thing he cannot have

**He cannot achieve Super Saiyan God as a form. There is no form for him.**

Secondary sources describe a **ritual** involving five righteous Saiyans and a recipient, and a legend with a time limit (C117). The film and Super versions and the precise participant requirements remain to be checked directly. For this serial, the idea of a mortal temporarily borrowing godhood is an **authored analogy**, not a universal canon rule about all later god-ki users.

**His Talent does not give it back.** Godly ki is not a bigger version of what he already has — it is a different substrate — and the Talent's one talent is that it **makes native what it adapts to**. So the ritual would not hand him a transformation. It would adapt, become a **divine** talent, and promote his **baseline**. **His base form would become Super Saiyan God** — permanently, at rest, with nothing to switch off.

| | ordinary ki | godly ki |
|---|---|---|
| what it is | an amplification of his base | a different substrate |
| what he can do with it | **enter and leave it** — the Talent makes it cheap and quick | **nothing. He can only become it** |
| the result of the ritual | — | **no transformation, no time limit, no way down** |

**It is a door, and it opens once, late.** In this serial his god state would be a *promotion*, and that authored promotion cannot be undone. So the gate is a **timing rule**, not a ban: **no god ki in the arcs we are writing** — and when the serial finally reaches that era, the crossing is **one event, one direction, and the end of his mortality.** **It is not the end of him** (§14). After it, **Super Saiyan God is not a form he takes. It is his base** (§13). **Write the refusal for a hundred chapters. Write the crossing once, and never write it as a power-up** — and never write it as a loss of self (§14).

**Why it bites, specifically:** the ritual wants the **righteous**, and he is the most certified heart on the planet (C115, §10). He is *exactly* who they would ask. **The pure-hearted child is the one who must say no** — and his fault is that he cannot bear to be left out of anything (§7). Being left out of this is the mercy, and he will not be able to see it that way.

**What it does not touch:** before that crossing, his own line — Super Saiyan and whatever he earns above it — uses ordinary ki. His later perfected-rung presentation is separately ruled in §13. **Ultra Instinct is a different mechanic** (a state earned by the body, not ki borrowed from gods) and is **not** ruled on here. Slots stay slots.

## 13 · The ladder — what he uses, and what he never will

**Two roads, and he walks both — because he builds the second one himself.** Canon includes golden transformations, god forms and **Blue**. This serial excludes Blue for Goshin under its own authored rationale about an internal perfected first form and an adapted divine base; it does not claim that all canon Blue users literally borrow their ki (`O036`). From a divine base and an ape's body he **makes his own rungs**, and the top is an authored form.

| rung | what it is | what it costs him | era |
|---|---|---|---|
| **the ordinary body** | a four-year-old with a tail | everything, for a hundred chapters | **NOW — this is all he is** |
| **Super Saiyan 1 · 2 · 3** | **finished.** Perfected, internal, never announced (O033) | nothing — engine, not gear | **locked.** He has not transformed once on the page |
| **Super Saiyan Rage** | his signature. Anger made cold (O034) | he can be cruel in it, and remembers it | **locked.** It arrives the first time he is hurt badly enough |
| **Super Saiyan 4** | his **physical apex** — fur, red, the wild body. Earned on the ape road (O035) | fear, paid in advance, at the moon | **locked.** Far. Years of story away |
| **── the crossing ──** | the ritual, once, late (`§12`) | the **mortal** — not the boy | **locked** |
| **Super Saiyan God** | **his base.** Not a form — what he *is* (O037) | nothing. It is only a floor | **locked** |
| **God 1 · God 2 · God 3** | **the same three rungs, converted** — reach for gold, arrive divine (O038) | **each rung leaks.** He gets *louder* | **locked** |
| **Super Saiyan 4 over god** | the ape body wearing the divine base | — | **locked** |
| **GOD 4 — the ultimate** | **the fusion:** god worn inside the SS 4 body. His Ultra class — **instinct when calm, ego when not** (O039) | **silent, and slow to arrive** | **locked** |
| **Super Saiyan Blue** | **never** (O036) | — | **never** |

**Why 1, 2 and 3 are not in his mouth.** A form you have finished is not a **gear you shift**; it is part of the engine. Perfected, they cost him nothing and announce nothing. **No hair, no lightning, no counting out loud, no "and this… is to go further."** The power is simply present, and the page proves it by what he breaks.

**Why Rage is his.** Canon's vocabulary, our future mechanism: the anger is **cold** (`§7`). The age-four 1,500 is an ordinary-ki anger surge, not the locked future Super Saiyan Rage transformation. The later form can express the same cold trait without having occurred already (`O034`, `O089`).

**Why Super Saiyan 4 is his and nobody else's.** Canon now has two roads to it: the old one — **tail, full moon, mastery of the Golden Ape** — and *Daima*'s, which is **a gift of magic from Neva and needs no tail at all** (C118). **This serial takes the ape road, and says so:** his tail is kept (**`OPEN-3` closed**), and *comfortable as a Great Ape* has been his declared trait since R10. **The gift costs nothing. The ape costs him fear, and that is why the form is his.**

**The god rungs are conversions, not inventions.** He does not design anything. **He reaches for a golden rung, and the reach comes back divine.** Stand on a Super Saiyan God base, pull the lever marked *Super Saiyan 2* — and the Talent folds the base into the reaching, so what arrives is **God 2**: the same degree, the same shape, the same three-step feel in his hands, on a substrate that is not gold. **God 1 is what Super Saiyan 1 makes of him now. God 2 is what 2 makes of him. God 3 is what 3 makes of him.** He still has exactly three gears. They simply do not run on the old fuel.

**And that is the retention law showing its face.** The Talent **never lets a thing be lost** (O041). It refused to give godhood back, which is why his base is what it is (`§12`); and it refuses to let a golden rung *revert* him to a lesser state, which is why the gold cannot come out any more. **Nothing he has acquired is ever dropped** — not a form, not a technique, not a grudge. He can **hold back**; he cannot **un-have**.

**The scene this writes for free.** Every enemy who has ever fought a Saiyan waits for the hair. He reaches for the second rung, and the hair does not come, and something much worse does. **The expectation is the weapon.**

**And why the rungs get louder.** God ki **leaks** — canon names leakage as the flaw in the higher golden forms, and it is worse for a boy working a substrate he is inventing. Every rung up his own ladder **bleeds power into the world**: he can be sensed at God 1, tracked at God 2, and heard coming at God 3. **For a child whose whole method is silence, the climb is a confession.** He uses them as little as he can and hates them.

**GOD 4 — the crown, and the silence coming back.** Take the body that is not afraid of the ape and put the divine base *inside* it, and the leakage stops: **fur is a vessel.** The paradox of the top of his ladder is that it is **quieter than the rung below it** — a god state nobody can feel, arriving in a body built to hold one. Its character is the fusion of the two states he already owns: **when he is calm it is instinct** — the body answering before he decides, which is what a boy who copies postures has been practising since he was three; **when he is not calm it is ego** — his cold rage, feeding on itself, taking more damage and becoming more. **He does not choose which one comes up. The mood chooses.**

**It is in canon's Ultra class and it is not canon's.** Ultra Instinct and Ultra Ego are **other people's** states, and this serial does not lend them out. His is his own, reached by his own road, with the same two faces.

**Order matters.** The physical apex comes **first**, while he is still a boy with a tail. The crossing comes **late**. **God 4 is the last thing in the ladder and the only form in this serial that nobody else has a road to.**

**And none of it is happening yet.** Nine of those ten rungs are **locked**. The only rung in play is the first one, and it is a four-year-old eating rice. **A chapter that reaches up this ladder has broken the era, not the ladder.**

## 14 · After the crossing — everything, promoted

**The Talent itself becomes divine, and it works on everything.** That is the whole content of the crossing: not a form gained, but a **substrate changed**, permanently, in every part of him at once.

| what it touches | what happens |
|---|---|
| his ki | divine at rest. There is no ordinary setting any more |
| his forms | every rung — Rage, Super Saiyan 4, God 1·2·3, God 4 — now runs on divine qi (O042) |
| his techniques | whatever he learns from that day is learned **divine**, first time, because the substrate is what he learns on |
| **his children** | **born divine, not mortal.** His line does not start at the ritual; it starts at him (O044) |

**And nothing is subtracted. This is the ruling that matters most on the page.**

Canon's Super Saiyan God **cools a Saiyan** — the form is described as bringing a calm mind, and it is one of its listed benefits (C119). **His promotion does not do that, because the retention law does not take things away** (O041). What godhood costs other people is the thing it does not cost him:

- **He keeps his temper.** All of it. The cold rage is still cold, and it still arrives.
- **He keeps his appetite, his jokes, his grudges, his grief.** He eats like a Saiyan at a divine substrate. He cries at the same things.
- **He keeps being wrong.** A god with a four-year-old's filing system and a mother's rules, still guessing.
- **He keeps loving people who can die.** That is the one that will hurt, and it is not a power.

**The line to write by:** *the crossing makes him more, and takes nothing.* Every scene after it must be able to prove that sentence — **and any scene where godhood makes him serene, distant, or above a joke is a scene to cut.** (O043)

**What it does not do.** No Blue (`§13`). No borrowed Ultra Instinct, no Ultra Ego — his Ultra class is God 4 and it is his own. No new rungs beyond the ones already named. **And no ceiling removed:** divine substrate is a floor and a fuel, not a guarantee.

## 15 · What none of it may mean

- **No free numeric boost from observation.** Training and adaptation can support growth; a demonstrated technique does not grant an arbitrary instant reading (O004).
- **Purity is not judgement.** The cloud certifies his heart, not his aim. He can be wrong all afternoon with a clean heart — **the cloud and the scouter disagree, and both are telling the truth.**
- **No meta-knowledge** (R6). He knows what that mountain knows.
- **Mastery is attainable.** The former claim that nothing he has can be finished is withdrawn: he works to master or perfect existing abilities first and retains what he has achieved (`O104`, `R-26`). His Talent supports rapid learning and mastery; fatigue or injury may affect a performance without erasing what he knows.
- **No god ki early.** Not a taste of it, not a spark, not "for a second" — until the crossing, which happens once (§12), and after which it is not a form but a floor (§13).
- **No Blue, ever.** This is Goshin’s authored structural rule: his perfected first rung is internal and his godly base is adapted permanently (§13). It is not a universal statement about every canonical Blue user.
- **He retains what he has learned** (O041), while injury, fatigue, suppression and distraction can interrupt performance; this does not erase the skill. Future divine-promotion rules remain as separately ruled.
- **He never announces a rung, and the narrator never numbers one.** No "God 3". The page shows the cost — the leaking, the silence, the mood — and lets the reader count.
- **Nothing here is recited in prose.** This is a ledger. A chapter shows one line of it and says nothing about the rest.

## 16 · The brothers — capability and agency

**Standing authored direction:** Goshin is never less capable than Gohan (`O056`). Make this visible through effective action, learning, technique and growth—not a fixed hierarchy borrowed from a single number. Temporary fatigue, injury, suppression or a tactical mistake can affect what either child does in a scene without erasing learned capability.

**Gohan grows too.** His **1** after the Raditz outburst is a momentary low reading, not his permanent resting state. His later Masenko reaches **2,800** in ep. 28 and **over 20,000 for an instant** in ep. 65. Dende’s healing and Vegeta’s explicit observation of a recovery-linked increase are in ep. 80 (`C144`, `C146`). He learns, trains, chooses and contributes.

**Goshin grows too.** The ~400 island scan is a snapshot. He can fly and use techniques; he learns from demonstrated movement and retains skills, with continued development from training and experience (`O004`, `O058`). Do not write him as a static reading, a child with no techniques, or invulnerable to every method he has encountered.

This comparison is the user's serial rule, **not** a canonical law ranking him above Goten, Trunks, Piccolo, Nappa or all opponents (`O060`). Both brothers matter to the family, and neither is merely the other's prop. Let changed choices determine changed outcomes.
