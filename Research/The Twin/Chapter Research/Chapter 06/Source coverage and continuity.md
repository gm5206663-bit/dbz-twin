## Readings, capabilities and injury

- **1,307**, **1**, and **1,480** are spoken in the local episode-5 captions. **1,500** for Goshin is authored for this candidate's angry surge. His **1,450** Kamehameha scan and comparison with Goku's wave were already established in the protected Chapter 5. Neither number is a permanent cap, a damage equation or a free increase earned by merely watching someone.
- Goshin flies, fights, turns a blast, protects Gohan and physically helps his father. He begins to gather his existing wave but stops because Raditz has put Goku behind him; he does not forget the technique or perfect it on the spot. He does not learn a new technique or transform.
- Gohan's headbutt and his later choice to throw dirt both matter. His burst subsides, but that does not remove his initiative. Both boys remain conscious; Goshin is not depicted as less capable than Gohan.
- Goku remains seriously hurt around his ribs from Raditz's attack, with an additional graze from the beam's spiral; no exact diagnosis is established. Gohan's shoulder hurts. Goshin's earlier head injury and burned palm have not been healed; the palm is bandaged. No Senzu is consumed or partially effective, and Goshin's food rule is not bypassed.

## Manual character checks

- Goshin repeats **“Get Gohan behind the wall”** before he takes Gohan there, and **“Show me your hand”** before showing Bulma his burn. Raditz's “Use your father's attack” is a taunt Goshin rejects, not an instruction he carries out. This targeted check is not proof that every possible semantic instruction has been caught.
- The standing Chapter 5 instruction to call if the brothers lose sight of each other is observed when Raditz throws Goshin behind the wall: Gohan calls, Goshin answers, and they see each other again. Goshin approaches the hold from Raditz's side; Raditz then pulls him across Piccolo's aim, and Goshin breaks free. He takes the downhill side beside Gohan on the slope and while helping with the stretcher.
- Goshin responds after Goku addresses him in their emotionally important exchange; he does not interrupt his father's conversation with Piccolo. His anger is quiet in the fight, but he also shows fear, relief and delayed amusement. He does not boast, nod when deciding, eat unseen food or speak his Saiyan name.
- Chi-Chi does not examine Goshin in this chapter, so the hands-behind-the-back habit is not triggered. Gohan's school ambition has already been established and is not gratuitously inserted into the fight.

## Handoff and consequences for later chapters

At the end, Goku is alive, awake and injured in Bulma's aircraft with both sons and Roshi. Gohan has his hat; Bulma has the Dragon Radar and the scouter, with its cell separated. Krillin has departed for Korin's tower. Piccolo has flown away; Vegeta and Nappa have departed for Earth. Roshi intends to call Chi-Chi once the aircraft reaches Kame House. The family has not yet reunited, no bean has yet been obtained, and the children's custody and training remain open.

**Important dependency:** in the existing provisional Chapter 7, Krillin is still with the group during Piccolo's later confrontation before seeking beans. He has already left in this candidate. The old Chapter 7 therefore cannot simply be appended unchanged. If this Chapter 6 is accepted, that later sequence must be reviewed and resolved in a separate candidate rather than silently changed.

Chapter 3's unanswered telephone call already occurred during the same visit. A later draft must not stage it as a second call; Roshi's intended truthful call is a different event. The rejected Chapter 10 and `O124`–`O129` remain unaccepted.
