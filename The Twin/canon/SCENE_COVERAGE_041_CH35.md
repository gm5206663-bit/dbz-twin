## Exact handoff

- The four travellers are together beside their intact ship on an unidentified world. Raiti stands by the door of his little vehicle; Zākuro is away from the ship's hatch. Piccolo asks Raiti to state the village's location, and Gohan asks what happened at the marsh. Both hosts are present and have not answered the final question. Apparent grass and distant trees are moving oddly; cause and physical extent are not yet established.
- Bulma has her voice-command recorder in her pocket, the radar with its power cell removed in her hand, and two apparent orange, starred spheres in her bag. She alone reports a beep after removing the cell. Their reality, ownership, wish-granting power and the radar's compatibility with actual Namekian balls are not established. The third signal yielded no sphere.
- Goshin chose to guard the ship. An access catch moved twice while Zākuro was beside him; Goshin stopped it and signaled with two prearranged ki pulses. Zākuro offers an innocent explanation. No one has confessed to sabotage or mind-reading, and no host has been physically attacked.
- Goshin's ship hold uses substantial prior strength, while his power and fine spatial sense genuinely develop further during the work and remain afterward. He remains in breathable sampled atmosphere, not unaided space. No new maximum, numerical reading, fixed duration, automatic psychic immunity or inability to evolve follows. Gohan searches and tests independently. Both boys retain their tails; Piccolo checks observed skies and they avoid unchecked views.
- Goku and Krillin continue to watch awake, hostile Vegeta; Tien and Chiaotzu remain by awake Nappa. Chi-Chi visits and brings a repaired shirt, food and water; Vegeta takes the food but not the water in this scene. No release, full care, secure custody, new Freeza information for the Earth defenders, or use/transfer of Krillin's whole Senzu is shown; Vegeta's thoughts remain private. The children's later refuge and the true Namek route remain open.
