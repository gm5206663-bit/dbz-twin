## Authored chamber and continuity check

- `O155` is the author's direction: Goshin trains for one full year **inside** the physical chamber before the Saiyans arrive, close to their arrival, with about a day outside. In unaccepted Chapter 22 he remains inside continuously for the full counted year; the Saiyans have not landed at that endpoint. Unaccepted Chapter 23 stages their arrival later the same morning as the boys' exit, without assigning an exact clock.
- Gohan and Goshin choose to enter together and remain inside for the full lived year; Chi-Chi prepares them and waits outside. At most two people occupy the room. The earlier seven-day changeover survives in the pre-correction backup as a superseded review candidate; the two-brother full-year treatment is an authored revision, not an author-accepted outcome or an extra user direction. The family reunites roughly one outside day after entry (`O163`, `O164`).
- Popo explains the room's per-person cumulative limit and the disappearing exit before the family decides; Kami recalls Goku's earlier visit. The closed door is shown, and Gohan cannot feel Chi-Chi from inside. Piccolo recognizes the boys when they come out, not from an asserted view into the closed room. Each boy's first physical-room year leaves approximately one inside year of the stated allowance; any later use must be accounted for rather than assumed (`C234`–`C236`, `O163`).
- The chapter shows more than a reference claim: initial training, cooking, reading and recording days; Gohan's homesickness and autonomous decision to stay; several progressively more demanding training scenes; and the final day. No child is forced to remain against a decision to leave. The brothers often cook together and sometimes divide other meal tasks; Goshin watches some preparations, without an every-meal ritual. Both boys carry the physical consequences of their lived time; no new precise birthday, eye colour, scouter reading or form is invented.
- Earlier Chapter 21 growth and Kamehameha mastery carry forward. After the authored outside interval Goshin moves six rocks, including one wider than his shoulders, while firing, and catches the board piece at speed. In the chamber he threads his wave between moving blocks to hit a target Gohan moves. Under the same heavy gravity he flies with one large block in the first week and later physically supports eight same-sized blocks in two stacks. A much stronger sustained wave splits the empty floor before he turns and stops it; both boys clash waves and his ki rises in a spar. These gains are not a numerical maximum, mandatory Zenkai or immunity (`O165`, `O177`, `O179`).
- Gohan uses his own ki to fly out of the fissure in selected Chapter 13, flies with Chi-Chi watching in Chapter 14 and uses flight in Chapter 17's ball game. In the chamber he adapts flight to the greater gravity and fires a stronger controlled wave through three spare blocks and into the empty floor while airborne. He chooses whether to stay, helps cook and studies; he and Goshin clash Kamehamehas (`O166`, `O176`). Neither boy transforms; both tails and the natural moon remain. The demonstrated wave is not a maximum.
- Following the authored several-month gap, Goku demonstrates a controlled moving Kaiō-ken and gathers a practised Genki-dama; King Kai warns of strain and has him release the gathered energy. The final review and target test, message to Earth and departure are not shown at the Chapter 22 checkpoint (`O167`, `O179`). Earth has not learned of their meeting. Krillin and Yamcha train on the coast with Puar, and Tien and Chiaotzu train in the mountains. At the exit Piccolo immediately recognizes both enormously increased familiar signatures, senses each is now far stronger than he is, and goes toward the Lookout; Kami and the four named fighters also sense the change. Sensing alone does not tell them the chamber's duration or conditions.
- The school forms Bulma brings are **blank copies**, not confirmed places. After the outside interval, Chi-Chi reports the director has asked the children again and renewed their shared room temporarily while seeking a lasting arrangement; long-term placement and school remain open. Gohan carries Hatch's original drawing into the chamber and back. His roof plan was last at the mountain house. Mina's book and C-6's arm, the covered machine and Baba's new request remain unresolved.

The selected source plan, automated checks, backup and delivery do not establish original video provenance, literary quality or author acceptance.
