# Franchise, film and continuity research map

**Subsequent study, in workshop append order:** The later [world-and-anime study](07_DRAGON_BALL_WORLD_AND_ANIME_STUDY.md) adds 21 complete retained Z spoken-caption reads (146 distinct Z episodes total), all 64 GT and 130 available individual Super short Toei summaries, the 172 available Z 118–291 summaries, and 24 individually inspected official page stills. Its people, craft and branch applications are current working guidance; its source limits remain explicit. The older counts and date labels below describe their historical passes, not the current total or an independently established chronology.

**Scope:** the user selected the whole project and franchise for research. This is a map of sources and continuity questions, **not** a claim that every installment has been watched or that all stories can coexist unchanged. The story starts at original **Dragon Ball Z episode 1**; earlier *Dragon Ball* is essential background. The project records “DB + Z + Super + all films,” with each film’s eventual continuity treatment open. GT, DAIMA, specials and later material must not be silently excluded, declared compulsory in unchanged form, or merged without a conflict decision.

## 2. Arc-level causality map

The following is navigation and dependency analysis, not a promise that the original events must happen. Exact episode inventories and participant-level effects remain future work beyond the first Saiyan arc.

| Phase | Source focus | Dependency changed by the twins |
|---|---|---|
| **Pre-Z background** | Original DB, films’ reimaginings, Bardock accounts. | Goshin has no knowledge of events he did not witness or learn. Existing relationships, Dragon Balls and Goku/Chi-Chi’s personalities must predate the twins consistently. |
| **Raditz → Saiyan arrival** | Z 1–35, plus episode-specific anime-original adventures. | The abduction, Goku’s survival/death, Piccolo’s access to the boys, the moon/tails, Senzu and transmitted Dragon Ball information determine almost every subsequent route. They are causes, not predetermined checkpoints. |
| **After Saiyans → Namek/Frieza** | Original Z 36 onward, the Namek Dragon Balls and Frieza’s independent operations. | Whether Piccolo/Kami or other fighters die changes the reason to travel; Bulma’s ship, Earth’s Dragon Balls, who survives and Vegeta’s knowledge all matter. Frieza’s separate plans do not require that Earth’s exact canon tragedy repeat. |
| **Garlic Jr. / return / Androids and Cell** | Z’s anime-original Garlic Jr. material, Future Trunks, Gero’s creations, Androids and Cell. | *Dead Zone*’s prior encounter conflicts with Z’s first meeting, yet the later anime uses Garlic Jr.; plan a causal treatment rather than erase either source. Goku’s medical state, Android warnings and time travel require their own chain. Gohan’s choices and the twin’s growth both matter. |
| **Buu and end-of-Z period** | School, Great Saiyaman, tournament, Babidi/Buu, fusion and later family. | Goku’s presence or absence changes family timing; Goten would be a third son if he is born. Do not guarantee a birth date, death, fusion, revival or unchanged tournament outcome. |
| **DAIMA / Super / GT** | Separate published continuations and overlapping depictions, to be compared when reached. | Relevant prior wishes, deaths, family relationships and available transformations must follow the approved branch. Do not stack separate retellings of one event as three successive events. |
| **Later cosmic and divine material** | Beerus, Universe Survival, modern Broly/SUPER HERO; Super manga, enhanced anime and announced Galactic Patrol; GT/DAIMA. | Goshin’s future divine-base and SS4 rulings are project-specific; canon’s version-specific mechanics are source facts only after checking the relevant work. ### Why *Dead Zone* matters earlier than the old project note assumed

The old `OPEN-6` says opening arcs touch no film. That is too broad as a research-planning claim. *Dead Zone* uses the early family/Piccolo situation, while Z’s later Garlic Jr. storyline follows the character. The [official Ginger profile](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_2893.html) identifies Gohan’s kidnapping in the film; the [official Garlic Jr. note](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_1778.html) notes his film debut and later TV appearance. [Kanzenshuu’s continuity analysis](https://www.kanzenshuu.com/episode/filler-plothole/) identifies conflicting introductions and prior knowledge. These are research anchors, not proof of the precise footage or a unilateral decision to insert the movie before Raditz. The serial can preserve one continuous narrative while openly adapting an encounter when the reader approves the solution.

## 5. Four source and version issues that need explicit comparison

1. **Episode-18 pod versus episode-46 ship — possible salvage, not a proved contradiction:** the [secondary continuity analysis](https://www.kanzenshuu.com/episode/filler-plothole/) says Goku's pod is destroyed in episode 18 and found intact for the later ship-building account. The retained episode-18 captions identify the pod as the apparent moon's source; a selected guide still shows a beam striking it and the [episode guide](https://www.kanzenshuu.com/episode/dbz/eps-018/) reports its destruction. The retained episode-46 captions initially have Goku say his old ship was safe (12:06–12:34), but later have Brief say it was found **broken in pieces**, crucial parts remained and almost all of it was rebuilt; Goku is surprised (18:04–18:28). The later dialogue therefore permits salvage as a possible reconciliation. The exact episode-18 visual aftermath and retrieval are not verified by the caption, selected frame or synopsis, so neither an intact discovery nor a definite contradiction should be asserted. In the selected unaccepted Chapter 19 branch the family covers and preserves the machine for Bulma instead; she has not yet identified ownership. See `C207`, `C245` and `SCENE_COVERAGE_018.md` in the living project.
2. **Dead Zone / Z introductions / Garlic Jr. television return:** first meeting and prior knowledge cannot both be exactly unchanged. Track who actually met whom and what they know. Bring a concrete solution before that history is needed.
3. **Classic versus Super Broly:** distinguish them in the source register. A single serial may creatively adapt material, but the narrative/identity decision belongs to the user when the conflict becomes consequential.
4. **Battle of Gods film versus Super TV versus Super: Beerus:** all depict overlapping material with version-specific staging. Toei's [Super episode-9 synopsis](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragon_super/episode/9/) says five righteous Saiyans pour their hearts into a sixth, and Videl's unborn child supplies the needed Saiyan. This official TV synopsis is not full footage, a comparison of the film, or Goshin's separately authored divine-base rule (`C117`, `W-100`). Toei's Super episode-4 synopsis places Bulma's party on a passenger ship; Kanzenshuu's secondary comparison says the theatrical film uses Capsule Corporation instead. A Dragon Ball Official Whis retrospective labelled as the film also says cruise ship, so its venue claim conflicts with that film comparison and may conflate versions. Neither complete version was viewed here (`C258`, `W-105`, `W-107`). Check direct versions before resolving differences; do not average incompatibilities or repeat the event as new material.

**Resolved author direction:** the earlier comfortable-as-ape versus compulsory-fear ambiguity is superseded. Goshin's very first Great Ape is aware and controlled; fear of his form and loss of control are not required. His actual trigger and the future scene remain unwritten. Preserve historical versions without reopening that settled rule (`O035`, `O046`, `OPEN-3`).
