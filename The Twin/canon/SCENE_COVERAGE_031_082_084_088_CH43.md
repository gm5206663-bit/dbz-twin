## Actual branch sequence

1. **Exact seam and first exchange:** Chapter 43 opens with Goshin's charge at Freeza's newly completed form, ×20 already active. Goshin can hurt and move Freeza, but the changed opponent can stop and drive him back in a direct contest and can attack several people or directions. Goshin's shoulder takes an elbow; his breath grows shorter across the long, continuous fight. No new scouter is used or reading invented.
2. **Villagers' choices:** The previously awake, scouterless soldier follows disturbed ground to the grove, sees the bundle and attacks. Muri, Kado, Kado's companion and the older resident use the soldier's injury, numbers, a thrown-off footing and bindings to secure him to a tree for the moment; it is not proof he can never escape. Bulma protects Cargo and lends her case strap; Kado adds his belt. Dende chooses not to heal the hostile soldier while he might hurt them. Muri keeps the covered ball and takes Dende, Cargo, the older resident and Kado's companion toward a lower spring. Outlying people remain uncounted.
3. **Gohan and Piccolo:** Freeza fires on Piccolo and hits his shoulder. Gohan's own gathered Kamehameha catches the firing hand from the side and interrupts the attack; Freeza resists and turns the wave, which Gohan ends. Piccolo stretches an arm to spoil a shot at Gohan; Gohan helps release him. Their contributions matter through timing, position and concentration, not an invented claim that either equals Freeza. Freeza knows the name and that Dende healed two people from Gohan's Chapter 42 report. He saw the two children cross in Chapter 39, but the prose does not establish whether he linked the name to either child or could recognize Dende; he does not know the exact grove.
4. **Earth and ship:** Dr. Brief conducts further external, non-contact scanner observations at the cordoned pod, helped by Chi-Chi, Krillin and Yamcha. The changing instrument reading is not identified as a safe hatch, a flight system or a usable rescue craft. Kado carries Bulma under the trees to the inlet; they avoid a still soldier, see the closed ship and no visible guard, and use the established recorder/piloting controls. Bulma carefully moves the ship sideways until the aerial clears the shelf, connects her compact communicator and reaches Puar at the Son house. Her information is time-bounded: Gohan and Piccolo were on their feet when they left to help Goshin; she has not seen the fight since. She reports the ship intact and her own safety. Puar says Chi-Chi and Krillin went to the laboratory and there is no rescue ship ready; he promises to pass the information. The laboratory does not answer his next call, so he leaves the phone to fly toward Capsule Corporation. **No one is shown yet delivering Bulma's message to Chi-Chi, Dr. Brief, Yamcha or Goku.** Bulma disconnects the communicator; Kado boards after seeing an unexplained movement in the trees. She begins moving the craft back toward the shelf; a completed re-hide, a safe return to the group or departure is not established.
5. **A specific limit and retained growth:** Having sustained ×20 across the second-form pursuit, underwater exchange and new fight, Goshin is short of breath and shaking. He deliberately raises the existing technique to **×30 of selected unboosted power**, not ×30 of ×20. The additional force clearly overpowers Freeza in the direct exchange: Freeza's guard collapses, he is struck and driven through the island. Goshin simultaneously directs beams away from shore and adapts to the greater rush. Freeza's dispersed stones and Goshin's prolonged exertion complicate pursuit. Goshin chooses to release the ×30 while Piccolo and Gohan cover him; his genuine added body-and-ki strength remains without the temporary factor, but it does not erase his present fatigue. This is a present circumstance, not an author-specified permanent ceiling, compulsory injury, universal duration or measured maximum. His whole-hybrid inheritance and developing Talent remain applicable without an invented genetic calculation or requirement that every expression be newly named.
6. **Exact frontier:** Freeza rises, battered but active, and flies toward the village side he saw the others approach from; he does not have the exact location of Dende or Muri's moved group. Gohan independently chooses to warn them. Piccolo interrupts Freeza's shot, and Goshin renews **×20** and catches the wrist; their contest remains active. Gohan reaches the now-empty grove, sees the temporarily bound soldier and begins following a trail beneath the branches. He has not yet found Muri's group, Bulma or Kado. The soldier is alive, secured only as shown, and still a potential risk.
