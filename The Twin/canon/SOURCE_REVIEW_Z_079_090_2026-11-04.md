**Owns: the retained caption evidence, source comparisons and branch implications for this historical checkpoint.** The subsequent [91–107 review](SOURCE_REVIEW_Z_091_107_2026-11-04.md) adds later evidence and raises combined coverage to 115.

**Scope.** I independently read every retained cue, including recaps, speech, previews and trailing fragments, in the 12 local Japanese SRT members listed below. This rereads episodes 79–83, which earlier records had already counted, and adds 84–90. At this checkpoint, together with the earlier records, full spoken-caption coverage was **1–90, 183–184, 205–208 and 256–257: 98 distinct Z episodes**. The 4 November continuation had independently read **37–90** end to end at this point; the earlier 1–36 and separate later full-read records were not all reread in this continuation. The redistributed archive's provenance and release/cut remain unverified, and no complete original episode video or audio has been examined. Dialogue is translated/paraphrased here; captions do not prove silent staging. Official Toei synopses `W-109` corroborate selected TV beats, not full footage or the manga.

## Episode sequence and branch implications

- **79:** Freeza overwhelms the fighters and wounds Krillin; Gohan attacks Freeza in anger. Vegeta comments on the boy's rising energy and on anger drawing out his power, but Freeza continues and Gohan is in danger (11:20–17:41, 19:11–25:22). Dende reports Krillin is still breathing; Goku and Piccolo sense danger from separate locations. This is a momentary, observed angry release and Vegeta's interpretation—not a measured stable Gohan base, a required transformation or an excuse to remove his agency.
- **80:** Krillin re-enters and draws Freeza away from Gohan, including with Solar Flare; Dende heals the living Gohan (08:24–17:35). Vegeta says Gohan's fighting power has greatly increased after recovery and attributes this to Saiyan blood (18:07–18:18; `C144`). Piccolo arrives and tells the others not to interfere. Goku, still elsewhere, initially cannot confidently identify the enormous ki as Piccolo's (21:58–22:13; `C261`). Dende's rescue is an action with a cost and context, not a reusable resurrection machine.
- **81:** Gohan defends his teacher's judgment, Krillin reassesses Piccolo's strength, and Piccolo refuses offers of help (05:39–06:07, 11:58–12:09, 17:26–18:03). At 19:07–20:34 the captions label a voice addressing Freeza as Nail, including “I assimilated with Piccolo,” followed by Piccolo's explanation of the increase. This is important **caption speaker attribution** beyond episode 77's assurance about personality (`C255`); without audio/video it does not settle the staging or persistence of a separate consciousness. The narrator and characters say “several times,” not an exact universal factor.
- **82:** Freeza initially pushes Piccolo back; Piccolo removes his heavy turban and cape and regains the initiative (06:22–14:20). Freeza says two transformations remain and undertakes the next one; he then overtakes Piccolo in speed (15:08–19:59). Vegeta asks Krillin to wound him so he can be healed, believing another near-death recovery will make him a Super Saiyan (23:46–24:14). His prediction is not an established result. Piccolo's weighted equipment is situational; it does not undo the author's settled direction that both chamber-trained boys comfortably outclass their Piccolo in their ordinary spars.
- **83:** Gohan intervenes for Piccolo with a full-power attack, which Freeza repels; Piccolo praises the growth and Gohan says the attack did not work (04:17–09:26; Toei `W-109`). Vegeta says self-inflicted injury will not help, asks Krillin to attack while he suppresses his power, and Krillin reluctantly does so (09:41–10:41, 16:07–16:39). Dende refuses to heal the killer of his people, heals Piccolo first and ultimately heals Vegeta after appeals (17:58–23:33; `C260`). The final transformation and Vegeta's claim that he is now a Super Saiyan close the episode. Neither the claim nor Vegeta's proposed method is a universal numerical law (`C144`).
- **84:** The group identifies Dende as killed by Freeza, and Piccolo infers Freeza observed the healing (06:40–08:30; `C260`). Vegeta's recovery has greatly increased his ki, but his self-identification as Super Saiyan is challenged as Freeza outpaces him (16:23–17:39, 23:31–24:30). Krillin says recovery from near death can multiply a Saiyan's power severalfold (18:25–18:37); record this as his statement in this confrontation, not a guaranteed factor every time. These source deaths and reversals are not mandated for the divergent branch.
- **85:** Freeza outclasses Vegeta, who privately questions both his own claimed transformation and whether the legend would be enough (04:11–07:28). Goku's treatment finishes and he senses he has become much stronger (22:33–24:01). The captions do not give a measured new base, and no branch character receives one from this.
- **86:** Goku reaches the fight and recognizes Piccolo after all; Krillin says Goku's own ki feels different after recovery (08:09–09:05; `C261`). Freeza's private recollection connects Goku's appearance to the Saiyan who resisted the planet's destruction; the dying Vegeta explicitly tells Goku that Freeza destroyed Planet Vegeta and killed their fathers, then asks Goku to defeat him (09:52–10:53, 20:14–22:00; `C043`). Goku responds as an Earth-raised Saiyan and speaks of both Saiyans and Namekians (22:29–24:35). This is a **later source knowledge handoff**, not information the early branch family automatically had. Toei's short synopsis separately confirms Vegeta's death and the confrontation (`W-109`).
- **87:** Piccolo asks Gohan and Krillin to leave the immediate fight; Gohan asks his father to survive and win (08:22–08:39). Goku judges that Freeza cannot locate others by sensing ki (10:19), developed again in episode 88; retain it as a situational inference in this version. King Kai starts to generalize about Saiyan growth and immediately wonders whether it is Goku in particular (16:12–16:20): not a demonstrated universal infinity rule.
- **88:** Chi-Chi still wants to reach Gohan, despite others' warnings; Brief reports the ship repaired, and Roshi proposes a group journey (04:58–07:15; `C250`). Goku exploits his inference that Freeza uses sight rather than sensing ki (10:16–11:58). Later Piccolo infers that Freeza is still playing and says the planet could be destroyed if he chose (22:29–22:45). Those are observations/claims, not proof of a precise power number or permission to assume a branch journey.
- **89:** Brief explains the craft's conversion from one seat to five and that filling its energy takes time (09:01–10:45). Bulma befriends the frog, builds a translation device, and the device voices “Change”; Ginyu occupies her body and she ends up in the frog (17:27–21:46; `C252`, `C262`). Toei's episode synopsis confirms the TV swap (`W-109`). The local TV caption is not a direct comparison with every manga or later version, and its character voice tags do not independently establish the audio track.
- **90:** Freeza offers Goku a job and claims half his maximum would defeat him; later he claims he is using less than one-third (04:35–06:07, 20:54–21:01). King Kai says Goku is already using tenfold Kaiō-ken, and the narration repeats it (23:06–23:14, 24:24–24:31; `C263`). Do not back-calculate an unmeasured base from those contextual claims. On Earth the group's attempted launch fails (17:41–20:18; Toei `W-109`); Ginyu in Bulma's body reaches the Namek group while Bulma is the frog, and the ending raises a threat against Piccolo. This review does **not** read episode 91 or decide the threat's or exchange's resolution.

## Working consequences

- The branch keeps Gohan's independent choices and accomplishments. Canon character estimates, rage episodes and injury-recovery claims do not make Goshin's cumulative whole-being Talent a fixed Zenkai multiplier or erase either boy's independently earned progress (`C144`, `O181`).
- Knowledge must move through an actual branch-specific source. The Z-86 explanation to Goku occurs after the current selected Chapter 23, and a divergent Namek sequence is not guaranteed (`C043`). The episode-90 name “Bardock” is spoken by Freeza but that moment does not itself identify the named man to Goku as his father.
- `C250`, `C252`, `C255` and `C144` retain their earlier values alongside new information. `C260`–`C263` register Dende's choice, energy-signature observations, the bounded TV body swap, and the source Kaiō-ken/percentage context. `canon/techniques.json` reflects the latter findings without promoting them to branch facts. No selected chapter prose was changed.
- **Remaining work at this checkpoint:** Original-Z episodes from 91 onward, original Dragon Ball and manga, the films, later versions, and archive origin and direct visuals/audio remained to be checked. The subsequent [91–107 review](SOURCE_REVIEW_Z_091_107_2026-11-04.md) covers part of that debt; the historical 98-caption count is not a complete canon check or a claim of a complete viewing.
