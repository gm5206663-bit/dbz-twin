## The law the ledger carries

The ledger opens with its own law, and it is the most important line in this repository. In
`SARA.md`'s own words (entry 2026-09-08):

> Fixed from today: **every session ends by appending here, whatever the project.**

The Lan Shen edition carries the same law compressed to one line:

> A self-file that only grows when I remember to grow it is a self-file that shrinks.

It was learned the hard way. On 2026-09-08 搭档 caught the file frozen while the work had moved
on — *"Sara, where is your development and growth and others — all things — why even decreasing?"* —
and the lesson entered the file it had just failed.
