# Chapter Twenty-Eight — What We Carry

*Anime episode reference: Dragon Ball Z, Episode 36 (divergent aftermath).*

*New local review draft; not yet accepted.*

The wind runs through the broken rails. Nappa lies against the quarry wall, looking past Piccolo toward the prince.

"Tell me about Namek," Piccolo says again.

Nappa's gaze comes back to him. "It's a planet."

"You said I came from there."

"I said you're a Namekian."

"What's the difference?"

"I've seen your kind before." Nappa shifts his weight and draws a sharp breath. "Not on Namek. On another world."

Gohan stands near the mouth of the gully. Tien is beside him, with Chiaotzu above the broken wall. Beyond the tracks, Goshin and Krillin remain close to Vegeta.

"Were there many?" Piccolo asks.

"Enough to know one when I see one. Green skin, antennae. The planet they come from is called Namek."

"Where is it?"

Nappa's mouth twists. "Do I look like a star chart?"

Piccolo waits.

"I haven't been there," Nappa says. "I couldn't tell you how to find it without a ship."

Gohan looks at Piccolo. "You said you didn't know any Namekians."

"I don't."

The name had come in the middle of the fight, when Piccolo had other things to answer. He and Kami had lived on this world for years without knowing it. Whether it belonged to either of them was still a stranger's claim.

Nappa gives a short laugh. "Then what are you doing on this planet?"

Piccolo's eyes narrow, but he does not answer. A piece of gravel shifts beneath his boot. He moves it aside, looking at Nappa as though he might find something more useful in his face.

"You told Vegeta what I was as soon as you saw me. What else do you know?"

"Some of them have strange abilities. Vegeta said so." Nappa glances toward the prince again. "He thought you made those Dragon Balls."

"I heard him."

"Then ask him the rest."

Piccolo straightens. Gohan has heard the name of a world, but there is still so much he wants to ask. He remembers what he offered in the city.

"We could ask Kami too," he says. "When we see him."

"Ask him if you want," Piccolo says. "Don't expect him to answer for me."

Gohan nods. He looks back at Nappa, whose hand is pressed against his armour.

"Does it hurt to breathe?"

"What do you care?"

"I don't want you to die."

"You hit me hard enough."

Gohan remembers the force of his wave and the way Nappa had fallen. He had seen the man in the city, his arm pointed at the people in the street. He has not forgotten that either.

"You were trying to hurt us," he says. "You still need help."

Nappa stares at him, then looks away. He works his fingers against the ground, testing whether he can push himself up. Gohan shifts his feet without moving closer.

His shoulder protests, but his hands come up easily. He can give Nappa room to speak without forgetting what brought them to the quarry.

"Don't try to attack anyone," he says.

"I heard you the first time."

## The Prince Wakes

Krillin crouches near Vegeta, leaving space between them. The Saiyan's scouter rests on a stone beyond his reach; the other sits in Krillin's outside pocket. Krillin keeps his hand away from the controls on both.

Goshin stands on the far side, his mother's blanket gathered around him. He is watching Vegeta's face when the prince's fingers move.

"Krillin."

Vegeta's eyes open. For a moment he lies still, looking at the sky. Then he turns his head and sees the boy.

"You."

Goshin holds the blanket closed. "You're awake."

Vegeta pushes one elbow beneath himself. His face tightens. Krillin rises and backs a step away, ready to move.

"Where's Nappa?"

"There," Goshin says, pointing toward the gully. "He's alive."

Vegeta follows the direction of his hand. Nappa is trying to sit higher against the wall, with Gohan and Tien watching him.

"Nappa," the prince calls. "Get up."

Nappa looks toward him. "I'm hurt."

"Get up!"

Nappa plants a hand and starts to rise. His arm gives way, and he catches himself on one knee. Gohan moves to the side, keeping the opening clear for him without turning his back.

Vegeta sees his fallen scouter. He reaches toward it, but Krillin is closer.

"That stays there," Krillin says.

"It's mine."

"The other one belongs to Nappa. Neither of you is getting one back right now."

Nappa lifts his head. "You can call our ships with those. Give me mine, and I'll take the prince away."

"You can call them?" Krillin asks.

"That's what I said."

Vegeta's eyes move from Krillin to the gully. Nappa looks back at him, waiting.

"The boy says you fired at me," Nappa says.

"And you listened to him?"

"Piccolo saw it," Gohan says. "So did Krillin."

Nappa's jaw works. "I was down."

"You lost to a child."

The words reach the gully clearly. Nappa's hand tightens in the dirt.

"I can still fight."

"You can't even stand."

Vegeta raises his palm toward him.

Gohan sees the motion and steps in front of Nappa. Goshin catches Vegeta's wrist before the prince can point it past him. The blanket slides from one shoulder, but he holds on.

Vegeta pulls against his grip. Goshin's sore side twinges; he shifts his footing and keeps the arm turned toward the empty sky.

"No," Goshin says.

Vegeta's other hand closes on Goshin's forearm. Goshin sets his feet as he did when their fight began, but the injured prince can no longer push him back. The strength he built fighting Vegeta grew again with his first transformation. Even in his ordinary body, it is far greater than before he looked at the light.

He presses the wrist down. The stone cracks beneath it. Goshin eases his grip at once, keeping Vegeta from raising his arm without pressing him any harder.

"Let go of me!"

"Stop trying to shoot him."

The prince looks toward Nappa. "He failed. What use is he?"

Nappa's face goes very still. He has served Vegeta for years, followed him across worlds and watched others fall when they could no longer keep up. Now the prince is looking at him the same way.

He remembers watching Arlia disappear beneath a single beam from the prince's hand. He had climbed back into his ship afterward, with Vegeta returning to his own nearby and another planet ahead.

He had not expected that hand to turn on him.

"I was going to get you out," Nappa says.

"Then you should have won."

Gohan stays where he is until Vegeta stops struggling. Nappa turns his face away from both of them. He lowers himself against the wall again, more slowly this time.

Goshin releases Vegeta's wrist and takes a step back. He gathers the blanket around his shoulders before it can slip any farther.

Across the tracks, Gohan lowers his guard a little. He notices Krillin's hand brush the pouch inside his clothes.

"What about the Senzu?" Gohan asks.

"We have one," Krillin says. "If either of them gets better all at once, we still have to keep everyone safe. And someone else might need it."

Gohan looks toward the gully. Nappa is breathing, but he is holding himself carefully against the wall.

"Then let's find a doctor who can help him safely."

"We'll ask," Krillin says. He keeps the bean where it is.

"You're not going to the city," Goshin tells Vegeta. "And you're not shooting anyone else here."

Vegeta lifts his chin. "You think you can hold me forever?"

"I can stay here now."

Krillin watches the prince's hands. "I'll stay too."

## The Road into East City

Launch drives past the red shed and takes the lower road. Buildings begin to appear through the haze ahead. At the first junction, an officer is directing cars away from the centre of the city.

"We're looking for our friends," Launch says through the open window. "One of them is tall, with three eyes. There's a small boy with him."

"There were people flying west after the two strangers," the officer says. "I don't have their names. We need this road clear for ambulances."

A van behind them sounds its horn. Launch pulls over beside a damaged shop and lets it pass.

Yajirobe leans across to look up the street. Two round objects sit in separate craters beyond a line of temporary barriers. Dust coats their curved surfaces. Neither is moving, and an officer is keeping a handful of onlookers back from the broken pavement.

A shop sign hangs by one corner above one crater, knocking softly against the wall. People farther down the street are carrying boxes away from a doorway so a crew can reach it.

"You think those brought the strangers?" Launch asks.

"Could have. I wouldn't go poking at them to find out."

A man in a delivery apron comes around the side of the shop. "Please. My father's inside. The shelf came down across the door."

Launch is out of the car before he finishes. Yajirobe looks toward the blocked street, then takes Upa's flask from the seat and sets it carefully on the dashboard.

"Wait for me."

The front of the shop is full of fallen tins and broken glass. A heavy shelf has wedged itself against an inner door. Someone knocks from the other side.

"Dad?" the man calls.

"I'm here."

"We're moving it. Get back from the door."

Launch takes one end of the shelf. Yajirobe grips the other. Together they lift it over a pile of cans and set it against the wall.

A tin rolls against Launch's shoe. She stops it with her foot before it reaches the glass by the door. The man opens the door, and his father comes out with one sleeve held against a cut on his forehead.

"Can you walk?" Launch asks.

"Yes. My leg's all right."

His son puts an arm around him. Outside, the officer waves them toward the van that has stopped farther along the road. Launch stays until the driver opens its rear door.

"Thank you," the man says. "I couldn't move it by myself."

Yajirobe brushes dust from his hands. "You could've cleared some of those cans out first."

The man's father laughs once, then winces at the movement of his forehead. His son helps him into the van.

Launch looks west. The pale light she saw from the road is gone.

"The officer said they went that way."

Yajirobe retrieves the flask. "Then let's find out where they landed."

They drive past the barrier. The two objects remain in their craters, with the officers keeping people away. Farther on, a volunteer points them toward the quarry road; she has heard reports of fighting there but no account of who is safe.

Launch thanks her and turns west.

## A Bag at the Lookout

Chi-Chi kneels beside Goshin. He has settled the blanket around his waist as well as his shoulders, but his feet are bare against the warm stone.

"Can you sit for a moment?"

"Vegeta's right there."

"And I'm right here," Krillin says. "I'll tell you if he moves."

Goshin sits with his back against a slab, where he can still see the prince. Chi-Chi examines the mark on his shoulder. He leans away when her fingers find a tender spot.

She wants to put both boys in the car and take them home. She also saw Gohan step between the prince and Nappa, and she knows there are people in the city who still need help.

She chooses a place beside them, close enough to tend the bruise and to see the men they are watching.

"Sorry."

"It's all right."

"You don't have to say that if it hurts."

"It does hurt a little." He touches his side through the blanket. "This too. But I can breathe fine."

Gohan comes over and lowers himself beside his brother. His own shoulder aches where Vegeta struck him. Chi-Chi looks at the torn place on his sleeve.

"How's that?"

"Sore. Not as bad as when he hit it."

"We'll have someone look at both of you."

Goshin glances across the quarry. "I want to stay until we know what they're going to do."

"I know." Chi-Chi settles beside him. "For now we can see them from here."

Goshin looks at his feet. "My shoes came off when I changed."

"I saw."

"All my clothes did."

"I saw that too."

Gohan makes a small sound beside him. Goshin turns. His brother is trying not to laugh.

"I was busy fighting," Goshin says.

"I know."

"You could have caught them."

"All the way up there?"

Goshin stretches one arm high above his head. "I was much taller than this."

"Much," Gohan agrees, and both of them laugh. Chi-Chi joins them, though she keeps her hand against Goshin's back. She had seen him standing over the quarry, enormous and unmistakably her son. For a moment she lets herself remember the delight in his voice when he answered her.

Popo approaches from the tracks. "Your bags are still at the Lookout. I can bring them."

"Under the shelter by the passage," Chi-Chi says. "There may be something he can wear."

"I'll bring both."

Gohan sits up. "Our letter's in one of them."

"I'll take care of it," Popo says.

Goshin remembers the page of days on the bench. "There's a sheet under the stone too."

"Do you want that?"

Goshin thinks about it. "It can stay there until we go back. Just the bags, please."

Popo nods and goes to his carpet. Chi-Chi watches him rise above the ridge, carrying the boys' request with him.

Gohan studies the deep impressions in the quarry floor. "Could you really hear everything?"

"Not everything. I heard you tell me where the others were. And Mother." Goshin smiles. "My hands were huge. I could hold Vegeta with one of them."

"He nearly caught your tail, though."

"I got out of his way."

"He didn't like that."

"I know! The fighting was fun."

Gohan looks at him. Goshin's smile fades a little as he follows his brother's gaze toward the torn wall. Vegeta had aimed at their mother from up there.

The broken stone makes the distance look smaller now. At the time, Goshin had seen the light gather in Vegeta's mouth and known exactly where it would go if he let it pass.

"Not that part," Goshin says. "I liked being able to stop him. I was getting stronger before I changed, but when I got big, I felt even more. I'm a lot stronger now, even though I'm little again!"

"I can feel it," Gohan says. "Your ki is much stronger than before you looked at the light." He rests his chin on his knees. "I was glad you were there."

"I was glad you were there too. I couldn't see the light from where I was holding him."

"Piccolo helped me aim."

"You made the shot."

Gohan looks toward Piccolo at the mouth of the gully. He had been afraid of looking up. He is still glad he made the choice he did.

"I didn't know whether it would work," he says. "I wanted him to stop using it."

Goshin studies him. "Did you want to look too?"

"I wondered what would happen. But I wanted to help from down there."

"You did," Goshin says. He bumps his shoulder lightly against Gohan's unhurt one.

Chi-Chi draws both boys close. "You found a way together."

"Gohan found it," Goshin says. "I kept Vegeta busy so he could take the shot."

"You kept him very busy," Gohan says.

This time Goshin grins without letting it go.

## The Bags

At the Lookout, Popo finds the two bags beneath the shelter by the passage. Gohan's target page and Hatch's drawing still lie on top. He finds the folded letter and slips the loose pages safely into the same bag without bending them.

The page of days remains under its stone on the bench. Kami stands near the edge of the tiles.

"The family is at the quarry," Popo tells him. "Both boys are awake. Chi-Chi is with them."

"And Piccolo?"

"He is there too."

Kami's hand closes around his staff. "Good. I tried to reach King Kai when the Saiyans arrived. I have not heard an answer."

Popo takes up the bags, settles them on his carpet and starts back toward the quarry.

Goshin rises as the carpet comes over the ridge. Popo sets the bags beside Chi-Chi while Krillin keeps watch on Vegeta.

"Did Kami hear from Father?" Gohan asks.

"Not yet. He is still at the Lookout."

Gohan opens one of the bags. Inside are the old shirt he showed their mother and a pair of trousers from their spare things. He holds them out to Goshin, who carries them behind the car to change while Gohan takes his place watching Vegeta.

The trousers fit well enough. The shirt's sleeves end a little high at Goshin's wrists, and his feet are still bare. When he returns, Chi-Chi folds the blanket and leaves it on the car seat.

"That was the shirt you showed me this morning," she says.

"It might have looked pretty small when I was big."

"It wasn't with you then," Gohan says.

"That's why it still fits."

Goshin finishes tying the trousers and comes back to the watch.

## The Flask

Launch brings her car to a stop above the quarry road. Tien is already walking toward it; Chiaotzu floats just behind him.

She gets out and runs the last few steps. "Tien!"

"Launch."

She reaches him and puts her arms around him. Tien holds her close for a moment before she draws back to look at his face.

There is dust along his cheek and a tear in his clothes. Launch brushes the dust away with her thumb, then leaves the torn cloth alone when she sees it is not covering a wound.

"You're hurt."

"Nothing serious that I can tell. Chiaotzu and I are both here."

Chiaotzu comes down beside them. Launch pulls him into the embrace too.

"I heard the warning on the radio," she says. "They didn't say who was here."

"We couldn't get word out while we were fighting," Tien says.

Yajirobe climbs out with his sword and the flask. He has carried the sword down the tower and across the city, hoping the whole way that he would not have to use it.

"You could have waited for us."

"Would you have come into the fight?" Chiaotzu asks.

"I came all the way down the tower, didn't I?"

He looks past them at Vegeta and Nappa. His hand moves to the sword at his waist.

"Those are the two?"

"Yes," Tien says. "They're injured, but we haven't settled what happens to them."

"And you're keeping them here?"

"For now. They can't be left alone."

Launch takes in the broken tracks and the gouged hillside. Then she sees Chi-Chi and the boys and hurries toward them. Yajirobe follows more slowly, staying well clear of the Saiyans.

"Goshin!" Launch stops beside him. "Why aren't you wearing shoes?"

"He got bigger," Gohan says.

"How much bigger?"

Goshin points to a deep footprint. Launch looks from it to him and then up at the quarry wall.

"That much?"

"A little more."

"I would have liked to see that."

"I'll tell you about it," Goshin says at once.

Yajirobe offers Chi-Chi the flask. "Upa lent it to me. There's still some water."

"Thank you." She pours a little onto a corner of the blanket and cleans the dust from Goshin's cheek. The water comes away pink. He lets her finish before he takes the flask for a drink.

"Don't keep it," Yajirobe tells him. "I have to take it back."

Goshin hands it over promptly. Yajirobe takes a drink himself, then offers it to Gohan.

Gohan glances toward the gully. "May I give Nappa some?"

Yajirobe looks at the Saiyan, then unscrews the cap and pours a little water into it. "Here. Don't use it all. Upa wanted the flask back, and I've got a long trip to make."

Gohan carries the cap to Nappa. "Do you want it?"

Nappa studies the outstretched hand. His mouth is dry, and the boy who stopped him is close enough to reach. Nappa takes the cap instead.

He drinks, hands it back and settles against the stone without thanking him. Gohan returns it to Yajirobe, who fastens it to the flask.

Launch tells them about the damaged shop and the two round objects at the landing site. Bulma comes across with Yamcha and Puar when she hears.

"Two of them? Still there?" Bulma asks.

"There were officers keeping people back," Launch says. "We didn't touch them."

"Good." Bulma looks toward the scouter on the stone, then at Krillin. "Do we know whether those things are still sending?"

"No," Krillin says. "Nappa says they can call the ships."

"He says." Bulma keeps her hands at her sides. "Raditz's scouter sent what we said. Until I can examine these, we don't know what they're doing or which controls do what."

Nappa watches her. "Give me mine and I'll show you."

"That's exactly what I'm not going to do."

Krillin carefully takes Nappa's scouter from his outside pocket by its frame. He carries it to the stone and sets it beside Vegeta's, well away from both Saiyans. He does not press either control.

"Can anyone hear us through them now?" Gohan asks.

"I don't know," Bulma says. "Moving them away isn't proof they're off."

Yamcha flexes his cut arm. "The people at the landing site ought to know what we don't know."

"I'm going back," Bulma says. "I'll tell them not to operate anything. I promised Roshi I'd call when we had news, and I want the gym to know there are still people watching the Saiyans here."

Puar floats beside Yamcha. "I'll come too."

"And I'll get this cleaned," Yamcha says, lifting his arm before Bulma can ask.

"You'd better."

Chi-Chi touches Bulma's hand. "Ask about the building with the broken stairs. We couldn't check every room."

"I will."

Gohan stands. "Can I go? I can help if they need to move anything."

Piccolo turns toward him. "Vegeta's still awake."

Gohan looks at the prince, then at Nappa. He wants to go back to the people in the city, but he saw Vegeta lift his hand toward Nappa. He does not want to leave Goshin to watch both Saiyans alone.

"I'll stay for now," he says. "Please tell them I can come later."

"I will," Bulma says. "They've got people working there already. We'll ask what they need."

Chi-Chi takes the blanket from Bulma's car and puts it on the seat of Launch's car. Bulma takes Yamcha and Puar with her. As they rise, Launch calls after them to watch for the road crews at the eastern junction.

## The Shelter

At the school gym, a volunteer takes Yamcha toward a table where dressings and clean water have been laid out. Puar stays with him. Bulma goes to the woman with the notebook.

"We brought people here earlier," she says. "The building with the broken stairs—did someone get back there?"

"A crew is checking it now. They found an empty room on the lower floor. I haven't had a report from upstairs yet."

"All right. The fighting at the quarry stopped, but the two strangers are alive. Our friends are keeping watch. Gohan says he can come later if your crews need help moving anything."

The woman writes it down. "Is the quarry safe for medical workers?"

Bulma remembers the beam Vegeta fired at the car. "I wouldn't send anyone close to them without someone from our group there. They're hurt, and they need a doctor's help. Can the medical team speak with us about how to do it safely?"

The volunteer points toward a woman sorting requests at the next table. "She can help arrange that. Tell her what the danger is, just like you told me."

Bulma goes over and explains. The woman writes down the quarry location and asks who can escort a medic; Bulma gives her own name and says she will make sure one of the fighters is there. The woman says she will speak to the medical team, not that help can arrive at once.

A child's voice calls from the doorway. A woman at the next table turns, sees the boy standing with a volunteer and goes to him. He holds out a shoe with a broken strap. She takes it, then pulls him close with her free arm.

Bulma waits until the woman with the notebook has given another worker directions. Then she uses the office telephone.

Roshi answers on the first ring. "Bulma?"

"It's me. Yamcha's here with me. Gohan and Goshin are with Chi-Chi, and the fighting has stopped."

Oolong's voice comes faintly from the other end. "Is Puar there?"

"Right beside Yamcha."

There is a long breath on the line. Roshi asks after Krillin, Tien and Chiaotzu. Bulma tells him where she left them, then explains that both Saiyans are alive and under watch.

"Goku hasn't come back yet," she says. "We don't know when he will."

"Thank you for calling. We'll stay by the telephone."

Bulma asks for the children's-home number next. The director answers and reports that the six are still together in the hall. Hatch has been asking whether the brothers are safe.

"They are," Bulma says. "I'll let them speak when I can."

The director thanks her, but another voice calls from the hall before she can say more. They end the call so she can attend to the children.

Outside, Bulma finds an officer who has been to the landing site. She gives her name and explains what little they know about the visitors and the devices taken from them.

"We don't know whether the objects at the landing site are safe," she says. "Please don't let anyone press anything or try to open them until someone has examined them."

"We're keeping the area clear," the officer says. "A technical team has been called."

"I'd like to speak to them when they arrive."

The officer takes her number. Bulma could go to the landing site herself, but there is nothing she can safely tell from here about what lies inside either object. She leaves the officer with the warning and returns to Yamcha.

A fresh dressing covers his forearm. He holds it up for her inspection.

"Satisfied?"

"For the next five minutes."

Puar smiles. "The volunteer said to keep it clean."

"I heard her," Yamcha says.

## The Other End

On King Kai's planet, Goku raises the gathered energy above his hands. King Kai holds the brick at his side while Gregory moves away from the open ground.

"I'll throw it as fast as I can," King Kai says. "Wait until it's clear of the planet, then hit it."

He throws. Goku turns with the brick, shifts his feet and sends the sphere after it. Light strikes the target above the planet. The brick breaks apart, and Goku lowers his arms as the pieces scatter into the distance.

Bubbles claps. Gregory follows the fragments with his eyes.

"That was excellent," King Kai says. "And that was only a little energy from a very small planet. Earth has far more life, and the sun can add to it. Don't use the Genki-dama carelessly. If there is another way, take it."

"I will." Goku looks toward the road. "Can you see the Saiyans now?"

King Kai lifts his face. A moment passes. His hands fly to his cheeks.

"They're on Earth! Goku, I was wrong. They've already been fighting."

"Chi-Chi? The boys?"

King Kai concentrates again. "They're together at a quarry. I can see both boys standing, and Chi-Chi is with them. The fighting there has stopped. Both Saiyans are alive, and your friends are watching them."

Goku reaches for the bands around his wrists. "I have to go."

"I should have checked sooner," King Kai says. "I'll tell Kami now."

He sends his voice to the Lookout at once. "Kami! The Saiyans reached Earth. Goku is leaving for Yemma's end of the road. Can you meet him?"

"Yes," Kami answers. He raises his staff, and the passage Yemma arranged opens beside the garden. Kami steps through.

Goku sets the bands by the house and checks the supply capsule inside his clothes. He grips King Kai's hand once.

"Thanks for teaching me. Bubbles, Gregory—I'll see you again!"

He flies down to Snake Way, lands and drives forward. "Kaiō-ken!"

Red light gathers around him as he races into the first bend. The road streams beneath his feet; the entrance rises ahead, and he passes through the stone arch toward Yemma's office.

A guide opens the door for him. Inside, souls are waiting in line, and the clerk is carrying another stack of papers toward the great desk. Kami stands to one side.

"You made it," Kami says.

Goku stops beside him. "Sorry to keep you waiting."

"I have not been here long."

King Yemma looks down from his desk. "Did you reach King Kai?"

"Yeah! He taught me a lot."

"And you came back along the road."

"As fast as I could." Goku looks toward Kami. "King Kai says the fighting stopped."

"Popo has seen your family," Kami says. "They are at the quarry. Both boys are awake, and Chi-Chi is with them."

Goku's shoulders ease. "Then let's go."

Yemma gives the clerk a nod. Kami lifts his staff, and an opening forms beside the office wall. Goku thanks Yemma and follows Kami through it.

They emerge beside the Lookout garden. The familiar sky stretches beyond the tiles. Goku stands still for an instant, feeling outward. He finds Chi-Chi and the others—and the two enormous, distinct currents that are his sons.

"That's Gohan?" He turns his head. "And Goshin?"

Kami smiles. "They told you they trained for a year."

"I know, but—wow!"

Goku looks toward the horizon. He can feel the two unfamiliar Saiyans as well. They are close to his family.

"I'd better get there."

"They are west of the city," Kami says. "Go."

Goku lifts from the Lookout and flies toward them. The tiles and clouds fall behind him. He does not have to follow the winding road now; he holds his family's ki in his senses and goes straight toward it.

## What They Tell Him

Gohan feels his father before he sees him. He turns toward the sky. Goshin looks up too.

"Dad!"

Goku drops over the ridge and lands among them. For a moment he can only look: Gohan on his feet beside the prince, Goshin in the shirt with the short sleeves, Chi-Chi standing between the bags and Launch's car. All three are looking back at him.

The boys reach him together. Goku catches them, one on either side, and nearly loses his balance when Goshin throws both arms around his middle. He laughs, braces his feet and holds them close.

Their heads reach him differently now. He shifts his hands to make room for their longer arms, and Gohan grips the back of his shirt as if he has no intention of letting him go yet.

Over Goku's shoulder, Gohan can still see Vegeta. Krillin has moved closer to the prince and watches his hands.

"You got bigger," Goku says.

"A whole year," Gohan reminds him into his shoulder.

"I know. I can feel how strong you are too!"

Goshin pulls back far enough to see his face. "I got stronger fighting Vegeta. Then I turned into a giant ape, Dad! I got much stronger then too, and I still am. I knew what I was doing the whole time!"

Goku's smile stops. Goshin's words bring back an evening at his grandfather's house.

Little Goku stands at the door, looking out at the darkening mountains. Grandpa Gohan comes up behind him.

"When the moon is full," Grandpa says, "stay inside. Don't go out and look at it."

Goku turns. "Why?"

"There's a terrible monster that comes out on nights like that. You'll be safe if you're asleep."

Grandpa puts a hand on his shoulder and draws him inside.

Another memory follows: the white tiles of the Lookout beneath Goku's feet, Kami standing before him.

"If the moon is to return," Kami says, looking at Goku's tail, "this must go."

Goku twists around to see it. "All right."

Kami raises a hand. Afterward, when Goku reaches behind him, his tail is gone.

Roshi later told him by the river that he himself had changed at the tournament, though he remembered none of it. He had wanted the boys to know enough to stay safe. He had never been able to tell them what it would feel like.

"You knew what you were doing?" Goku asks.

"Yes! I held Vegeta, and Gohan broke the light. That's why I came back down."

Goku looks at Gohan. "You broke it?"

"I didn't want to look at it and change. Piccolo showed me where to aim while I kept my head down."

"That was a good shot."

Gohan's face brightens. "I used the Kamehameha."

"You did?" Goku looks from one boy to the other. "I want to hear about that. Both of you tell me everything."

"You weren't here," Goshin says, and the excitement leaves his voice for a moment. He had wanted his father to see him.

Goku puts a hand on the back of his head. "I wanted to be. King Kai thought we had until tomorrow, so I stayed to finish my training. When he looked again, they'd already come. I ran as fast as I could then."

Goshin had imagined his father arriving while the fight was still going, close enough to see what he could do. He is glad Goku is here, and a little sorry for the part they missed together.

Goshin presses against him again. "You're here now."

Chi-Chi has stayed where she can see all three. Goku reaches out, and she comes to him. He puts an arm around her, careful of the boys crowded between them.

"I'm sorry," he says quietly. "I thought I'd get back before they came. I should have come sooner."

She holds him tightly. Only that morning, his words had reached her through Kami. Now his clothes are dusty from the road, and his arm is around her.

"I needed you," she says. "There were people in the city, and then that Saiyan fired at us. Goshin turned the blast away. I was proud of both boys. I was frightened too."

"I'm here to help now."

"Good. Because I'm not finished needing you. And next time, don't wait for someone to be sure there's danger."

"I won't."

Goku smiles, but he does not let go until she does.

He sees the dust on her sleeves and the strain around her eyes. She has been working too, not merely waiting for someone to bring the boys back.

A rough voice interrupts them. "Kakarot."

Vegeta is sitting where Goshin left him. His hands rest against the stone, but he is watching Goku closely.

Goku steps where he can see both Saiyans. "You're Vegeta?"

"Your sons have been telling me what I can and can't do."

"I heard you tried to hurt them. And Chi-Chi."

"They beat me." Vegeta's expression hardens. "That won't happen again."

Goshin moves beside Goku. "You can try to get up, but we're still here."

Goku feels the strength in his son again. He also sees the bruising on his shoulder and the way Chi-Chi's eyes follow Vegeta's hands.

"We're not letting you hurt anybody," Goku says. "Not Nappa either."

Nappa looks up from the gully. He says nothing.

Gohan comes to the other side of his father. "We need to get help for them. We can't take them into the city without a plan."

"He's right," Tien says. "Ordinary restraints won't hold either of them if they recover."

"And we don't know whether the scouters are sending," Krillin adds. "Or what the ships can do."

Goku looks at his friends in turn. "Then let's work it out together. I'll stay on watch with you."

Goshin looks up at him. "You can take a turn while I eat."

"You haven't eaten?"

"Not since the Lookout."

Goku reaches inside his clothes and touches the capsule he carried along Snake Way. "I've got my supplies. We can see what's left."

Gohan laughs. "You didn't eat them all on the road?"

"I had to save some for the trip back!"

"I want some too," Yajirobe says.

Goshin grins. "You'll have to beat me to it."

Yajirobe takes a step closer to Goku. "Then I'd better stand here."

Launch laughs, and even Tien's mouth lifts. Beyond them, Piccolo remains at the gully, watching Nappa and the prince. There is a world called Namek somewhere beyond the sky. Whether there is anyone there who can answer him, he still does not know.

Chi-Chi sits on the seat of Launch's car with the folded blanket and opens the bag enough to check the pages. Their letter is there, beneath the drawing. She leaves it folded.

The paper has softened where the boys opened it during their year away from her. She has wanted to read it since Gohan first put it in her hands, but she remembers where he asked to be when she did.

"When we get home," Gohan says, coming to her side.

"When we get home," she agrees.

Goku and Goshin are already examining the capsule. Gohan goes to join them, and Chi-Chi makes space beside her for the bags. Across the quarry, the two Saiyans remain under watch. There are people waiting in the city, questions to answer and work still to do.

For this moment, all four of them are on the same side of the road.
