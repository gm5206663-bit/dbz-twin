## What changed

- **6 — charged piercing attack.** The Makankosappo still passes through Raditz after Goshin frees Goku. It now punches through the ridge, continues toward the horizon and breaks rock on both slopes. Its narrow path and visible consequences suggest reach and penetration, not a stipulated maximum yield or a Moon strike. Goku lives; the boys' roles, the 1,500 reading here and the earlier 1,450 snapshot are untouched.
- **7 — chase.** Piccolo's shot at Nimbus misses the boys and cuts a trench in the empty far bank, scattering rock and a tree into the river. Gohan's decision to jump and the brothers' escape remain intact. Piccolo is not declared to be using his maximum output.
- **9 — bridge.** Goku says pulling the trapped fork would bring the occupied bridge with it. The obstacle is protecting Mina and the structure, not a limit on lifting a log. Gohan identifies the dangerous line of fire; Goshin moves and severs the fork precisely; Goku lifts the trunk away. Mina, her father, Chi-Chi and Ox-King keep their roles. Neither boy looks at the full Moon.
- **10 — C-6 and the roof.** Goshin says he can hold the beam, while warning that the roof continues to shift. Gohan still finds and sets the support; C-6 chooses to use his reserve despite the leak; the boys and their parents carry out the rescue. It is a coordination and structural-integrity problem, not a declared cap on Goshin's strength or Talent.
- **13 — ring and earthquake.** Goku and Goshin agree to limit and direct their waves for a family match with Chi-Chi and Gohan nearby. Controlled exchanges still have impact: a missed burst cuts the empty wall, and the beam collision scars the ring and strips rock. Goku flies forward under his own ki, the beam contest remains two-sided, and both redirect and stop their waves when the crack advances toward the ledge. Goshin still wins the genuine ring-out; no lifetime or full-power ranking is implied. Goku immediately experiments with the technique and commits to further growth. Gohan gets his own turn. In Piccolo's subsequent separate quake, Chi-Chi still grabs Goku's gi as he jumps with her clear of the collapsing edge; Gohan gets himself out and Goshin protects Chi-Chi from the slab.
- **14 — Snake Way.** Goku covers bends in sustained flight, his punches crack the air, and he alternates repeated fingertip sets with sprints and movement practice. No travel time, speed tier, new form or power multiplier is invented. He is still alive, travelling with permission, carrying provisions, seeking King Kai's training and not yet at King Kai's planet. His friends and family keep their own training and destinations.

## Audited but unchanged

- **8:** The dinosaur and slab scenes are precise rescues around living creatures. Goshin's retained abilities and choices, Gohan's agency, Goku's and Gohan's whole Senzu, and Goshin's refusal of the unwatched bean are already established. Small motions do not imply weak maximum output.
- **11:** The leaf and everyday actions can be deliberate fine control. Goshin observes Chi-Chi preparing food. Goku actively obtains C-6's consent and seeks Bulma's help. Characters do not have to use destructive attacks to solve a problem.
- **12:** Sparring and food gathering are bounded family activities, not a measurement of a maximum. Vegeta destroys Arlia in the separate Saiyan thread; Earth has no knowledge of that event. A later benchmark from the original franchise is not made into an event in this divergent branch.

## Rules preserved

- Goshin is never less capable than Gohan; Gohan has independent agency and growth. Goshin's exceptional Talent is broad and works with his Saiyan heritage, including adaptation, recovery, abilities and strength—not just copying. Existing flight, ki, Kamehameha and unusually quick healing remain.
- Goku actively seeks instruction and grows. His spar is a bounded contest, not an artificial cap or a ban on training with his sons. King Kai has not yet agreed to train him on-page or been met.
- The 400, 1,450 and 1,500 readings remain authored snapshots, not permanent levels, destructive-radius formulas or speed measurements. No invented multiplier or unestablished near-fatal Zenkai has been added.
- Both four-year-old boys keep their tails. No Great Ape transformation occurs here; Goshin's eventual first Great Ape is controlled from the start. There is no early golden form, god ki, Kaioken, Instant Transmission or new scouter measurement.
- Goku and Gohan each ate one whole Senzu. Goshin refused the bean whose preparation he had not watched; he watches the food he eats being prepared. Krillin retains the remaining whole bean. Gohan's hat carries the Four-Star Ball; the Three- and Six-Star Balls remain at home. The scouter and its cell are separate.
- By the end of Chapter 14 Goku is on Snake Way; Gohan, Goshin, Chi-Chi and the other gathered fighters are at Kami's Lookout; C-6 and Dr. Brief are at the lab, while Bulma heads back there. Vegeta and Nappa are travelling toward Earth; the family and friends do not know about Arlia. Sato, Mina and her father remain in the village. These positions have not been reset.

## Evidence and limits
