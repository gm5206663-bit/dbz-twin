## Standing instructions for future work

Read the actual relevant chapters and the author’s latest corrections. Goshin repeats applicable direct instructions before obeying and eats only food whose preparation he watched; preserve his other family habits and a child’s voice. He is never less capable than Gohan, while Gohan retains agency and growth. His Saiyan bloodline becomes purer and deeper as he grows, without assuming his human heritage disappears. Goshin’s first eventual Great Ape is aware and controlled from the start; the form and its future applications develop with him. Do not force canon outcomes, invent numerical ratings or mark drafts accepted without the author’s decision.

After a chapter or revision, update the foundation, timeline, relationships, status or status delta, world/knowledge and source notes that actually changed. Keep one clearly identified working version and preserve necessary earlier versions in the designated Research backup locations; existing older Drive copies are separate, not automatically synchronized. Check before deleting, replacing or syncing anything. Do not claim checks prove literary quality or full video review.
