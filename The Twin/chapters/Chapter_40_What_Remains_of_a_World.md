# Chapter 40 — What Remains of a World

*Anime episode reference: Dragon Ball Z, Episodes 49–52, with selected, brought-forward elements from Episodes 71, 77 and 88, adapted to the changed branch.*

## On Either Side

Freeza moves toward Gohan.

Gohan brings his arms up. The first blow strikes his guard and drives him backward along the path. He catches himself before he reaches the house, twists and sends a short burst of ki toward Freeza's chest.

Freeza turns aside. Goshin is already coming from his left.

Goshin's fist reaches the edge of Freeza's armour. Freeza catches his wrist and swings him across the path toward Gohan. Gohan ducks beneath his brother, then rises with an open palm. His strike catches Freeza under the arm and turns him just enough for Goshin to wrench his hand free.

For an instant the brothers are on the same side of him.

"Apart," Gohan says.

He drops low while Goshin goes over Freeza's shoulder. Freeza's tail whips around. Goshin blocks it with both forearms, but the blow sends him past the corner of the house. He plants a boot against the wall and pushes off before he can break through it.

The older villager flattens himself beside Muri. Piccolo stands in front of them, one foot holding down the soldier he knocked over earlier.

Goshin comes back across the path. Freeza meets him with a punch. Their fists strike; the ground jumps, and the soldier by the empty chair stumbles backward. Goshin holds the contact and drives forward. Freeza's feet slide a short distance through the dirt.

His smile changes.

"Well, well. Both of you are stronger than Dodoria, it seems," he says.

Dodoria, still on one knee in the open patch, glares at them. "Lord Freeza, let me—"

"You have had your turn." Freeza looks back at the boys. "My battle power is five hundred and thirty thousand. I do not intend to use all of it on you."

Gohan remembers the number the soldier called for him. He does not know whether Freeza is telling the truth, but the first blow drove him across the path, and Freeza still looks amused. Muri and the others are behind them; Gohan cannot simply leave.

Freeza pulls his fist away and raises one finger toward the house. Gohan sees the line before the light appears. He dives across it, striking Freeza's hand with the side of his own. The shot cuts past the roof and disappears into the bare slope beyond.

Goshin catches Freeza around the middle. He lifts and flies toward the open ground, carrying him away from the people at the corner.

Freeza's tail coils around Goshin's ankle. He pulls, turns and throws the boy down. Goshin lands on both hands and springs up before Freeza can follow, keeping his eyes on his opponent rather than the strip of sky above the roofs.

Gohan lands beside him. "Are you all right?"

"Yes. He caught my ankle."

"I saw."

Goshin grins despite himself. "Next time I'll grab his tail."

Freeza glances down at his tail, then at the two brown ones tucked around the boys' waists. He takes a step toward them, and the air presses against Goshin's shoulders.

Goshin tries to move. His arms stop halfway up. His feet lift off the ground without his permission.

Gohan reaches for him. Freeza flicks two fingers, and Goshin is pulled sideways, out of his brother's reach. The pressure tightens around his body.

"Put him down!" Gohan shouts.

"I was just thinking where."

Freeza turns toward the house.

Goshin thrusts his ki downward. It strikes the earth beneath him, tilting his body away from the wall. At the same time Gohan fires a bright burst across Freeza's face. Freeza turns his head from the light; the pressure shifts, and Goshin forces an arm through it.

He catches the ground with one hand. The rest of the invisible grip slips, and he lands beside Gohan.

A crack runs through the path beneath his palm. Freeza looks at it, then back at the boys.

"You noticed," he says to Gohan. "And you moved before I finished."

"He wasn't going to wait," Goshin says.

Gohan takes a breath. "Neither was I."

At the house, the soldier beneath Piccolo's foot reaches for his leg. Piccolo strikes him across the side of the head and leaves him lying still. Beside the chair, the last working village scouter beeps as its wearer looks at Gohan.

"Thirty-one thousand two hundred!" The soldier turns the lens toward Goshin. "Sixty-eight thousand six hundred!"

He touches the transmitter. "Lord Zarbon, Lord Freeza is—"

A thin beam from Piccolo's hand cracks the lens. The soldier cries out and pulls the broken device from his face. Before he can raise his other hand, Muri sends a second shot into the earth at his feet. He falls onto his back beside the chair.

"That was the last one here," Muri says.

"There are more at the ship," Piccolo answers.

The soldier Gohan sent sliding earlier gets up near the front wall. He sees Muri and runs for the door. The older villager steps in his path and catches his arm; Piccolo crosses the space between them and knocks the soldier down with a blow to the chest. The older villager releases him and hurries back to Muri.

Freeza watches the broken scouter fall. His eyes go to the elder.

"Dodoria," he says, "search that house. I want the elder alive."

Dodoria pushes himself upright. His damaged armour creaks when he breathes, but the house is closer to him than it is to the brothers.

Gohan turns toward it. Freeza steps across his path.

Goshin strikes Freeza's arm from the side. "Go, Gohan!"

Gohan flies low toward Dodoria. Behind him, Freeza's tail snaps toward Goshin again. This time Goshin catches it with both hands and pulls.

Freeza is dragged half a step off balance. His other hand closes around Goshin's shoulder, and the two of them shoot across the open ground beyond the houses.

## The Other Door

Dodoria reaches the corner first. The older villager pulls Muri back against the wall as Dodoria's hand tears through the place where the elder was standing.

Gohan hits him from behind.

Dodoria crashes onto the path and rolls. Gohan lands between him and the two Namekians. "Get away from them!"

Dodoria spits dirt. "You again."

He charges. Gohan shifts to the side and catches him with a kick, but Dodoria keeps coming, one hand reaching past him toward Muri. Gohan grabs the arm and turns with it. The larger fighter's weight pulls them both across the path; Gohan lets go only when they are clear of the wall.

Light gathers in Dodoria's mouth. Muri and the older villager are behind Gohan, close to the line of fire. Gohan catches Dodoria's jaw and forces it toward the empty ground beyond the path before the shot leaves his mouth.

The blast cuts into empty ground beyond the path. Stone erupts. Gohan throws up an arm to shield his face and sends a push of ki through the nearest fragments, knocking them away from Muri and the villager.

Dodoria comes through the dust with both fists raised. He catches Gohan across the shoulder and follows with another strike. Gohan blocks the second, drops beneath the third and drives his own fist into the dent Goshin left in the armour.

Dodoria doubles over. Gohan backs away, breathing hard. Dodoria is slower now, favouring the damaged armour. Gohan has the strength to stop him, but a blast aimed carelessly could strike the house behind him.

"You can stop."

Dodoria glances toward the distant clash, then bares his teeth. "Get out of my way, brat!"

He lunges again. Gohan sees Muri at the corner and the path running behind the house toward Bulma and the children. He has promised Dende he will come back. He will not let Dodoria reach them.

His anger comes with the thought, hot and sudden, and his ki swells with it. Gohan draws it together instead of letting it burst toward the house. He shifts until only the empty rise is behind Dodoria, then brings his hands to his side.

"Kamehameha!"

The wave strikes Dodoria squarely in the chest. It carries him backward across the empty patch and into the stony rise beyond it. Gohan stops the wave as soon as Dodoria hits. The broken earth settles around the large figure.

Gohan goes after him. Dodoria is unconscious, but his chest still moves. Gohan looks toward the house before he returns; he has not tied the fighter up, and he cannot stay there to guard him.

Muri is at the rear corner with Piccolo. The elder's sleeve is torn where the wall broke, but he is standing.

"Are there people still inside?" Gohan asks.

"Two came out while you were fighting. Others may be in the far houses, and some were working away from the village. I have not found everyone."

A series of blows cracks across the open ground. Gohan feels Goshin's ki, then the great pressure of Freeza's. He turns toward them, but Muri puts a hand on the door at the back of his house.

"My Dragon Ball is inside. If I leave it there, his men may find it."

Gohan remembers the cloth on the shelf. "I'll watch the corner."

Piccolo moves with Muri through the rear door. The room is dim. Muri lifts the covered sphere from its recess and gathers the cloth securely around it. Piccolo takes the other end when the fabric catches on the shelf; neither of them uncovers the ball.

A voice reaches Piccolo while Muri ties the bundle.

*Piccolo. Can you hear me?*

*King Kai?*

*Goku asked me to try you again. Are you still with the others?*

The house shakes at another distant impact. Piccolo looks at Muri's hands on the cloth.

*Freeza is here. He attacked both boys. Goshin is fighting him now; Gohan stopped another fighter and is with me. Freeza's men found our ship, but Bulma is with two children. We are trying to move the village elder and his people away.*

There is a sharp intake of breath in his thoughts.

*Get those boys away from him!*

*We're trying to keep him away from the village.* Piccolo steps to the door. *Tell Goku what I said. I have to go.*

*I'll tell him. Piccolo—*

The voice stops as Piccolo goes outside. Muri carries the wrapped ball against his chest. Gohan looks at it but does not reach for it.

"Can we get them to Bulma?" he asks.

"The wall still gives us cover," Piccolo says. "Go first."

Gohan runs along the narrow passage between the buildings. The older villager follows Muri, looking back toward the other houses. Piccolo comes last. At the gap by the bank, Gohan checks the path, then waves them across to the rock.

Dende is the first to see them. He scrambles out from beside Bulma.

"Elder Muri!"

Muri lowers the bundle long enough to hold him. Cargo comes close on his other side, still pressing Bulma's cloth to his knee. The older villager crouches beside the children and touches Cargo's shoulder.

Bulma looks past them at the bundle. "Is that what I think it is?"

"It belongs to our people," Muri says.

"Then you keep it. I was going to ask whether we could study it, but I think that can wait."

Muri looks at her, and despite the noise beyond the bank his mouth lifts slightly.

## The Quiet Line

At the inlet, Zarbon presses the control on his scouter again.

"Report."

No one answers. The closed ship sits beneath the shelf, its aerial almost touching the rock above it. Zarbon walks around the spur and looks toward the ridge, but the village is hidden from here.

The soldier beside him tries his own device. Static answers.

"Perhaps the equipment has failed," Zarbon says.

"Or they're fighting."

"That is also possible." Zarbon considers the ship. Lord Freeza ordered it kept intact; he did not order Zarbon to smash the hatch in frustration.

He points toward the ridge. "Go to the village. Find the soldier who reported to me and tell me what has happened. If there is trouble, report before you interfere."

"Yes, sir."

The soldier rises over the inlet and heads inland. Zarbon stays beside the ship. His scouter picks up the departing soldier at one thousand two hundred; the signal moves toward the ridge. Zarbon leaves the receiver open and checks the hatch once more without touching its controls.

## The Watering Place

Behind the bank rock, Cargo peers around his elder at the wrapped ball.

"Are the trees all broken?" he asks.

Muri looks toward the far side of the village. Through the gap between the buildings, a row of young trees is visible beyond the damaged path. Several have been knocked over. Others are still upright.

"Some are standing," he says. "We will have to see to the rest."

Dende wipes his face with his sleeve. "I watered those this morning."

"What kind are they?" Bulma asks.

"Ajissa. We grow them so there will be forests again. A long time ago, terrible weather destroyed almost all the trees and most of our people." Dende looks at Piccolo. "The Great Elder lived through it. He helped our world grow again."

Piccolo's eyes narrow. King Kai had told them about the old disaster. Kami's letter and the lonely house at Yunzabit return to him, but Dende has not said anything about the child who left.

"Does your Great Elder know the people who lived here before?" he asks.

"He knows more than anyone."

Muri shifts the wrapped ball into the older villager's hands for a moment. "He should be warned about Freeza. If the other villages have been found, he may know what we can do."

"I can take him," Dende says. "I know the way."

Cargo catches Dende's sleeve. Dende covers his brother's hand with his own.

"I don't want to leave you."

"I'll stay with Elder Muri," Cargo says, though his voice is small.

Another impact sounds from beyond the houses. Gohan feels Freeza's ki move farther across the open ground. Goshin is with him. Gohan starts to rise, then stops when Piccolo catches his eye.

"Freeza isn't the only one here," Piccolo says. "If I take Dende, Bulma and the others will still need cover."

"I'll help them," Muri says. "And our people are not all children."

The older villager returns the ball and looks toward the buildings. "I'll bring those I can find along the low wall. There is a hollow beyond the trees."

"I'll go with you," Bulma says. "Cargo, can you walk?"

He nods. Bulma offers her hand, and he takes it.

Gohan looks toward the open ground. "Goshin and I will keep Freeza away from here. Piccolo, if Dende can show you where the Great Elder is, he needs to know what's happening."

Piccolo studies him. "Don't let that thing draw you too far from your brother."

"I won't."

Muri bends to Dende. "Tell the Great Elder what you saw. Don't promise him a Dragon Ball or anything else on our behalf. Ask him to warn the others if he can."

Dende nods. He lets go of Cargo's hand and moves beside Piccolo.

"Can you fly?" Piccolo asks.

"Yes."

"I'll carry you. We can move faster, and you can show me the way."

Dende puts both arms around Piccolo's neck. Piccolo lifts him and turns toward the trees, away from the open fight. He pauses only long enough to look back at Gohan.

"Come back," Gohan says.

"You too."

Piccolo flies low beneath the crowns. Dende points between two trunks, and they pass out of sight of the bank.

Gohan gives Cargo a quick touch on the shoulder, then runs around the house toward the fight. Bulma follows Muri and the older villager along the wall, keeping Cargo close. They have a direction to move in, not proof that everyone in the village has reached it.

## News at Home

Krillin finds Chi-Chi on the path below the quarry. She turns at the sound of his landing, one hand already raised.

"What happened?"

"King Kai spoke to Piccolo. When he reached him, the boys and Bulma were with him. They'd hidden the ship and were going to help some people. King Kai didn't see what happened afterward."

Chi-Chi lets her hand fall. "Goku sent you?"

"He's still watching Vegeta. He asked whether you wanted to go to Capsule Corporation with me and talk to Dr. Brief."

"I do." She looks down the path toward home. "But Bulma said she'd call again."

"Let's use the telephone first. Maybe we can get somebody to wait here."

They reach the house together. Chi-Chi picks up the receiver before she has taken off her shoes and calls Capsule Corporation. Dr. Brief answers from the laboratory.

"Chi-Chi! I've spoken to the officers about the other pod. They'll let me examine the site."

"Is it safe?"

"I don't know yet. It wasn't in the picture when the first one exploded. I want to see what condition it's in before anyone turns anything on."

"Can it take us to Namek?"

"I can't tell you that until I've seen it, either. If it can fly, we'll still need to work out the controls and a route."

Chi-Chi grips the receiver. She had wanted an answer she could use at once, but he has given her the truth.

"We're coming to you. I can't leave the telephone with no one here. Is Puar there?"

"Just a moment."

Puar's voice comes down the line. "I'm here. Yamcha is helping Dr. Brief get things ready."

"Could you come to the house? If Bulma calls, I need somebody to hear her. Ask her where they are and whether the boys and Piccolo are with her. Don't tell her we have a ship ready, because we don't."

"I'll come as fast as I can," Puar says. "I'll write everything down."

Chi-Chi puts the receiver back carefully. Krillin stands by the window, looking out toward the quarry.

"I'll carry you to the laboratory when Puar gets here," he says. "Then we can go with Dr. Brief to the site if he wants us."

"I'm not sitting at home while everyone else looks for a way."

"I know."

The house is quiet enough for her to hear the receiver settle in its cradle. She looks at the table where Gohan and Goshin had unfolded their letter before leaving, and pulls a sheet of paper and a pencil close to the telephone for Puar.

## Across the Open Ground

Goshin slides backward through the grass as Freeza presses a palm against his guard. He turns the force aside and kicks. Freeza catches the kick, but Goshin twists in the air, frees his leg and strikes him across the cheek with the back of his fist.

Freeza's head turns. He brings it slowly back.

"Much better," he says.

He disappears from in front of Goshin. Goshin feels him move and turns in time to catch an elbow against his forearms. The blow drives him down. He stops just above the ground, sends a wave of ki along the grass to lift himself clear and shoots after Freeza.

The two meet again beyond the bare slope. Their collision folds the grass outward in a wide circle. Farther ahead, the ground falls toward a broad lake. Goshin has pulled Freeza away from the houses, but he can still feel the people behind him.

Freeza reaches for him with that strange pressure again. Goshin braces, but this time Freeza's attention snaps toward the village. Gohan is coming fast from behind the houses.

"Where is Dodoria?" Freeza asks.

"He won't be taking anything from them."

Gohan comes in on Freeza's right, keeping his eyes low over the ground. Goshin moves to the left as they had agreed. Freeza's tail flashes between them, but Gohan ducks it and drives both hands forward. His burst strikes Freeza's side; Goshin hits the opposite shoulder an instant later.

Freeza is thrown across the lake. He stops above the water, and a sheet of spray rises beneath him.

Gohan reaches Goshin. "Muri has his ball. Piccolo's taking Dende to warn another elder. Bulma and the others are getting away from the houses."

Goshin looks back. He cannot see them through the rise, but he can feel Piccolo moving with a smaller presence. "They're going the other way?"

"Yes."

"Then let's keep him here."

Freeza lowers one hand. The water swells around the rocky island beneath him. A wide shelf of stone tears free from the lakebed and rises, water pouring from its underside. Freeza pushes his hand forward.

The mass comes at the boys, with the village somewhere behind them.

Gohan spreads his hands. "I'll break it!"

His Kamehameha cuts through the front of the oncoming stone. The shelf splits, but the pieces continue toward them. Goshin reaches out with his ki, catches the largest two and turns them sideways. Gohan redirects a third with a sharp burst; it lands far along the empty bank, beyond the route their friends took.

Freeza moves through the falling spray.

Goshin is still holding the two pieces when Freeza strikes. His first punch slips past Goshin's guard and catches him in the ribs. Goshin bends with it rather than letting go of the stone. His other hand comes up, and he catches Freeza's wrist.

A smaller fragment drops between them toward Gohan. Goshin feels its weight separately from the pieces he is holding. Without looking away from Freeza, he turns the fragment down into the water. Then he drives the two larger pieces past both fighters and lowers them onto the far shore.

His grip on Freeza tightens. His body responds more quickly than it did on the path; strength runs through his arm and into his shoulders, and his ki deepens with it. He shoves Freeza back across the water.

Gohan is already there. He comes up from below and kicks Freeza in the chest. Freeza catches his ankle, but Gohan fires from his free hand. The flash bursts between them. Gohan pulls loose and drops beneath the surface before Freeza can seize him again.

Goshin feels his brother under the water. Freeza looks at the spreading rings, then turns toward a small burst of ki Goshin sends skimming across the surface. A second burst curves past Freeza's other side.

Gohan rises behind him and strikes the back of his armour. Freeza is driven forward; Goshin meets him with both hands and pushes him down into the lake.

For a moment the brothers hover above the water, listening to the rush of it against the broken island.

"Did it work?" Goshin asks.

"I don't know."

Freeza rises through the water without hurry. He wipes a drop from his cheek and looks first at Gohan, then at Goshin.

"You keep finding one another. How convenient."

He lifts one hand. Gohan moves left; Goshin moves right. Freeza's fingers close, and the air around Goshin tightens again. The boy is pulled toward him.

Gohan rushes in. Freeza catches him with the end of his tail and sends him skidding across the water.

"Gohan!"

Goshin lunges against the invisible pressure. It clamps around him, but across the lake his brother plants a hand on the surface, rises and comes back without waiting for him to call again.

Relief hits Goshin so hard it makes him angry. Freeza has aimed at the children, at Bulma, and now at his brother. Goshin gathers his ki. The strength that grew during the struggle with Dodoria is still there, and so is the greater strength now answering him over the lake. He could make a light like the one Vegeta used. But Gohan has a tail too; Goshin would warn him before putting that light above them. Freeza is already turning toward the village.

Goshin chooses the technique his father taught him instead. He brings far more of his own power forward than he showed when the soldier measured him at the village. His arms shake against Freeza's hold as he gathers it close.

He thinks of Goku pressing his palms against his at the quarry. *Let it rise. Keep hold of it.*

"You're not hurting them!"

Goshin cries out, drawing the rush through his shoulders, his chest and his legs. He pushes the Kaiō-ken far beyond the three times he used at the quarry.

"KAIŌ-KEN, TIMES TEN!"

His gathered power surges tenfold. Red light explodes around him; the lake bursts outward, and Freeza's grip shatters. Goshin feels the force driving through his body, his muscles answering and adjusting as he takes hold of it. With a fierce yell he surges forward and strikes Freeza across the jaw.

Freeza throws an arm up against the next blow, but Goshin drives it back against his chest. He follows before Freeza can recover, sending him skidding across the water.

Freeza's tail whips toward Gohan. Gohan drops beneath it and fires into the lake. Spray rises across Freeza's face; he sweeps it aside with one hand. Goshin catches his other arm. Freeza pulls against the grip, but Goshin turns and hurls him farther across the lake, away from the village.

Freeza stops above the broken island and touches his cheek. A thin cut marks the pale skin beneath his fingers. Goshin stays beside Gohan, the red light still close around his body. He had felt his own body and ki grow stronger before he called the technique; that strength is his, beneath the temporary surge.

For the first time, Freeza does not smile. His ki surges. The water hollows beneath him and rises in a wall of spray. Gohan is driven backward; Goshin catches his hand. The pressure reaches Goshin too, but this time it does not push him back.

Freeza stops advancing and draws his power closer again. The spray falls. Gohan feels greater pressure from him than before, but Goshin is still holding their place above the water. Freeza watches the red around him.

## The Road Beneath the Trees

Piccolo carries Dende low over the uneven ground. The child's hand points beyond a line of trees toward a distant ridge.

"After that," Dende says, "there is a taller place. The Great Elder lives there."

Piccolo slows. An unfamiliar presence is approaching from the direction of the inlet. He lands beneath the trees and sets Dende behind a thick trunk.

"Stay here."

A soldier passes above the branches, his scouter sweeping the ground. Through a gap in the leaves he spots the two figures, and the lens registers each in turn.

He touches the transmitter. "Lord Zarbon, a Namekian at two hundred and forty, and a child at eighteen—"

Piccolo rises through the leaves. The Namekian's reading jumps to two thousand six hundred. The soldier gasps, but Piccolo strikes the scouter off his face before he can report the new number. Piccolo catches the man's raised arm, forcing it upward before the shot in his palm can fire. The burst goes into the open air. Piccolo drives a knee into the soldier's middle and carries him down to the ground.

The soldier struggles once. Piccolo knocks him unconscious and leaves him breathing beneath the trees, his broken scouter several steps away. He returns to Dende.

"Did he tell the others where we're going?" Dende asks.

"Not while I heard him. We should keep moving."

Dende holds on again. Piccolo flies beneath the next trees, taking the direction the child gives him. The soldier's interrupted voice report identified a Namekian and a child and gave their first readings, but not their destination. Neither Piccolo nor Dende knows what else the scouter may have sent or whether another device is searching for them.

## A Voice at the Quarry

Goku hears King Kai while he is standing in front of Vegeta.

*Goku. I reached Piccolo.*

Goku's hands close. *How are they?*

*Freeza is there. Piccolo says he attacked both boys. Goshin was fighting him when Piccolo spoke to me; Gohan had stopped another fighter and was with Piccolo. Freeza's men found the ship. Bulma was with two children, and Piccolo was trying to move the village elder and his people away.*

Goku takes a step toward the open end of the quarry. Vegeta's eyes follow him.

*Are the boys hurt?*

*Piccolo did not say. He had to go before I could ask more.*

Goku stops. Across the tracks, Tien and Chiaotzu are still watching Nappa. Vegeta's tail is moving beneath the overhang, and his hands are no longer resting on his knees.

*Tell Chi-Chi exactly that,* Goku says. *Please. Krillin went to find her.*

*I will try her now.*

Goku looks at Vegeta. "Freeza attacked my sons."

Vegeta's hand closes on the edge of the stone. "Then you had better hope they can run."

"Goshin's fighting him."

The boy who beat him. Vegeta remembers the strength behind Goshin's small hands and the enormous body he had controlled. Freeza may be stronger still; Vegeta has no way to know how much strength he keeps in reserve. But if the boy can keep him busy, there may be an opening.

"Get me to my ship," Vegeta says. "I'm not staying here while somebody else decides what happens to Freeza."

"You tried to get past me once already. You're not taking a ship."

Goku wants to put his fist through the wall beside the prince. He lowers his hands instead.

"Tien!" he calls across the quarry. "Can you hear me?"

Tien turns. "What is it?"

"I need to talk about the watch. King Kai just reached Piccolo again."

Chiaotzu moves closer to Nappa while Tien comes along the tracks. Vegeta watches both of them. Whatever arrangement they make, Goku will not leave him unattended while he is still able to hurt people.

At the house, Chi-Chi hears King Kai's voice beside the telephone. Krillin hears it too.

*Piccolo says Freeza attacked both boys. When he spoke to me, Goshin was fighting Freeza and Gohan was with Piccolo after stopping another fighter. Bulma was with two children. Their ship has been found, and Piccolo was trying to move the elder and his people away. He had to go before I could ask whether the boys were hurt.*

Chi-Chi's fingers tighten around the pencil. "You haven't heard them yourself?"

*No. I heard Piccolo.*

"If you reach him again, tell them we're trying to come. Tell them to come home if they can."

*I will.*

Krillin takes the pencil from her before it breaks. Puar appears beyond the window and taps on the glass. Chi-Chi opens the door for him and repeats the message she has just heard, making sure he knows it came through Piccolo and King Kai, not from Bulma's telephone.

Puar sits beside the receiver with the paper ready. Chi-Chi picks up her bag. A moment later Krillin carries her toward Capsule Corporation; behind them, Puar watches the telephone.

## What Freeza Tells Them

The cut on Freeza's cheek is small. He touches it once more, then looks at the mark on his fingers. The arm he raised against Goshin's blow trembles; he closes his hand and holds it still.

"I haven't had cause to pay much attention to Saiyans for a very long time," he says. "Perhaps I should have asked more questions about the two of you."

Gohan comes level with Goshin. "We're from Earth."

"Are you? Your tails suggest another connection."

"Our father is a Saiyan," Goshin says. "Our mother is from Earth."

Freeza's gaze rests on him. "And he sent his children here?"

"We wanted to come," Gohan says. "Piccolo wanted to meet his people. We didn't know you were going to hurt them."

"You know now."

Goshin remembers the children in the great mirrored ship: Bun holding his gun, Miri speaking about the people who had come to their world, Zeshin trying to keep the others together. He had wanted to find a place where they could be safe. He still does not know whether they reached it.

"You hurt those other children too," he says.

"I have taken many worlds. Am I supposed to remember every child on them?"

"Those children didn't do anything to you!" Goshin shouts. Bun had tried so hard to look old enough to protect the others.

His fists close so tightly his nails bite his palms. Beside him, Gohan's ki rises too.

Freeza watches them both. "Your father's people became troublesome. They grew stronger and began to think that made them important."

"What did you do to them?" Gohan asks.

"I destroyed their planet."

The water moves around the broken island. Gohan hears it against the stone before he finds his voice.

"Raditz said a meteor hit it."

"A useful story. It kept the Saiyans who were away from home doing as they were told."

Gohan remembers the beach, his father facing Raditz, and the stranger saying that his parents had died on a world Goku could not remember. Gohan had never seen that world either. He had a name for one grandfather and stories about another; suddenly he is thinking of two people no one at home could tell him about.

"Were Father's parents there?" he asks.

"If they were on the planet, they died with it."

Goshin looks at Gohan. His brother's face has gone pale, but he is still holding his place above the water. Goshin has never met those grandparents; he had never known their names. His father never had the chance to know them either.

"Why?" The word comes out rough.

Freeza's tail curves behind him. "Because I chose to."

"You just CHOSE?"

Goshin's voice cracks into a roar. Red fire leaps from his shoulders, and the lake heaves beneath him. He sees the frightened children at the village doorway, remembers the children on the other ship, and feels his own ki surge beneath the Kaiō-ken. He turns the rising force away from the shore, but he cannot make himself stop glaring at Freeza.

Gohan's hands shake. He draws a breath and steadies them. The village is behind the rise; Dende is on his way to warn someone; Cargo is with Bulma. Freeza is looking at both boys as if those people are already his to take.

"You don't get to do that here," Gohan says.

Goshin moves beside him. The strength in his body is his own; the red light of the Kaiō-ken still surrounds him. He can feel Gohan's ki at his side, and farther away the other presences moving beneath the trees.

"We won't let you," he says.

Freeza's smile returns, thin and sharp. He raises his hand.

The brothers move before the light leaves his finger.
