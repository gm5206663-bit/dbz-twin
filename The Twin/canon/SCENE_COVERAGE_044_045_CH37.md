## Audit and exact handoff

- The party examined a real damaged wreck but did not fully diagnose or repair it. A transmitter worked in a local test; range beyond the current and response are unknown. Raiti and Zākuro remain at that wreck with two food packs, water and the device; their survival, honesty and ability to leave are not guaranteed. Gohan and Goshin promised to ask for help, Bulma kept route readings, and she warned them that listeners could be hostile. They have not been rescued (`O266`, `O267`).
- The strong four-person crew left through a piloted opening. From within the ship Gohan and Goshin independently moved exterior rocks, building on shown ki use. Goshin's body and ki genuinely gained additional unnumbered retained strength; this is neither a maximum nor a claim of automatic immunity, transformation or unaided-space survival. Both brothers and Vegeta still have tails (`O268`).
- On Earth Goku and Krillin remain with Vegeta; Tien and Chiaotzu remain with Nappa. The shade is open and removable, not secure custody or a prevention of Vegeta's own artificial moon. The whole Senzu was not used or transferred. Chi-Chi has directly heard the travellers and their limited account, and says she will tell Goku; no further relay is shown (`O269`).
- In the separate offworld scene Cui found an old survey and Freeza reached Namek with Zarbon and Dodoria. A soldier's scouter displays unidentified settlement signals 24, 58 and 113. Freeza sent scouts, not yet shown obtaining a ball or receiving their report. The narrator identifies the travellers' current world as the same Namek, but they have not independently authenticated its name or any Dragon Ball. The exact final position is Bulma and Piccolo at the open hatch, Gohan and Goshin flanking it, the intact ship behind them and two armoured scouts descending; neither side has attacked. The scout first reads Gohan 430 and Goshin 520, then the boys lower ki without knowing those figures; any later detection and the scouts' choices remain open (`O270`, `O271`).
- The refugee children's later destination and safety remain unknown. The 430/520 and settlement readings are authored scene snapshots, not source readings or maxima. No rescue of the stranded pair, full natural-sky survey, source casualty, further Earth relay or future outcome is implied. The chapter is a review draft, not author-accepted.
