## What was done

All 40 selected chapter files were searched for the quoted wording, related terms, forms of address and scouter mentions. Dialogue extracts were generated across the manuscript and reviewed in bounded chapter groups; flagged shorter lines were also sampled, and full scenes were read around the identified character, scouter, timing and knowledge issues. This was a substantial targeted review, **not a verified line-by-line literary reading of every sentence or proof that no errors remain**. In particular, shorter dialogue and unflagged narration were not all individually examined.

## Source and voice boundaries

The [official Freeza leadership interview](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_1486.html) discusses his refined, courteous way of speaking; it does not prescribe a single English translation. The [official Dodoria profile](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_3406.html) describes his hot-headed conduct. The [official Zarbon profile](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_1616.html) helps distinguish his composed presentation. These inform characterization rather than mechanically dictating every line (`C313`).

The retained Japanese subtitle collection includes polite forms such as `ドドリアさん`; [one secondary recollection](https://dragonball.fandom.com/wiki/User_blog:KillRoy231/Classic_original_DBZ_lines) reports an English version using “Mr. Dodoria,” but also cautions that its remembered quotations may not be exact. The subtitle collection's origin and cut remain unverified, attribution from unlabelled cues is contextual, and no original dub audio was checked. The author's objection governs this story's wording; it is not evidence that the address is absent from every source version (`C314`). No complete source video or audio was reviewed for this pass.

“Mother” and “Father” are already established forms of address in the branch; they were not globally replaced. The supported changes do not force a source death, alter the current fight's outcome, change the Kaiō-ken multiplier, add a ceiling or take Gohan's decisions away.
