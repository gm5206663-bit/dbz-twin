# CANON — how we know it

**Owns: the evidence.** The rows themselves live in `canon/facts.json`, one row per fact, each naming its witnesses and its grade. This file owns what a witness is, which ones we have, what is contested, what is ours, and what has been withdrawn.

---

## 1 · Witness classes

| Class | What it is | Can prove |
|---|---|---|
| **P** | the episode itself, on the track | truth. **We hold none.** |
| **P-C** | redistributed Japanese caption files, read directly; broadcaster origin not verified | provisional support for captioned words and their order; not silent pictures, authenticity or intent |
| **P-R** | production record — airdate, director, animation supervisor, the anime-only verdict | how an episode was made and what is filler. Not dialogue. |
| **M** | manga chapter | manga staging and manga dialogue. Anime does not inherit it. |
| **T** | a person's translation of dialogue | the shape of a line. Not the words. |
| **S** | plot record / synopsis | beat order and who is present. **Never** dialogue, never motive. |
| **E** | encyclopaedic record | dates, ages, objects, equipment readings. |
| **D** | distributor blurb | that the episode exists. |

**A summary is not a witness.** Absence from a summary is not evidence of absence. **Independence is by publisher:** two pages copying one text are one witness.

Earlier chapter cards’ phrase “broadcast captions” was too strong; their evidence cards now distinguish provisional dialogue from visual staging.

## 3 · Grades

| Grade | Rule |
|---|---|
| **A** | directly read and unambiguous caption wording, provisionally, or independently corroborated primary evidence; not proof of broadcaster provenance |
| **B** | two or more independent groups agree |
| **C** | one group only — may be true, must not be leaned on |
| **D** | nothing attests it. **Ours. Must be declared in the chapter it appears in.** |

**Tier I, provisional:** a directly read `W-30` caption statement, with local timecode, pending independent source and video verification. A character's claim is recorded as a claim. A grade A on that row means direct, unambiguous text under this project convention—not verified broadcast provenance or guaranteed visual staging. **Tier II** covers secondary evidence and unverified visuals. **Tier III** is authored. The audit checks labels and references, not factual truth.

**Episode 6 road correction.** The guide presents about one million kilometres as tradition and recalls Yemma as the only one to reach the end **in the past 100 million years**, not necessarily ever (`C114`). Yemma says he restrained Raditz and sent him to Hell (`C170`). The local captions do not establish the silent mountain-throw or telekinetic choreography; `C159` and `C171` use secondary reports with their limits.

- **21:52** the guide passes the message to Uranai Baba: **do not revive Goku for a year.**

**Ep. 8 — the telling, and the moon.**
- **05:10–05:38** still nothing. She assumes they are being kept up late, and that nobody at Kame House is a fit example for the boy.

## 5 · Contested, and how it was ruled

**The scouter.** Two records put 322 / 334 / 408 / 416 at the **fight**; one translation thread puts them in **Kai's** second episode, which re-cuts Z 3–4. **No witness puts a numeric reading of either child in Z ep. 2.**
**Ruling:** the island reading stands as **ours** — `O005`, the panel's 400 — declared in the chapter card, never quoted as canon's. If the tape shows a scan, the row is re-graded, not silently upgraded.

**Further contests, recorded and not averaged:**
1. The second Special Beam Cannon is **1,480** in the local Japanese ep. 5 caption. A secondary page gave a conflicting number; that page is not the authority for the spoken line.
2. **Piccolo's Special Beam Cannon.** The captions say **1,330** for the first shot in ep. 4 and **1,480** for the final shot in ep. 5 (C062, C097, C102). The episode page's **1,485** is struck (**R-20**).
2b. **Raditz's finishing blow on Goku.** The captions say only that it is one blow that Krillin cannot follow (W-30). Summaries say **a knee to the stomach** (W-03) or **a kick to the ribs**. The chapter uses the knee, and now says it is the chapter's word, not the episode's (R-19).
3. **The date on the calendar.** Daizenshuu 7 (via W-23) assigns Raditz's arrival to **12 October, Age 761**, working backwards from later anchors. W-12 reports **28 November, Age 761** instead (`C047`). These are competing secondary chronology accounts, not spoken dialogue or verified primary calendar scenes. The project selects the October guidebook date with this caveat. The prior Chapter Three body used 4 December; its approved revision no longer asserts an on-page calendar date.
4. **The Makankōsappō reading is anime-only** (W-19). The manga never prints it; the local anime caption says 1,480; a conflicting secondary page is not averaged into it. Manga and anime remain separately sourced.
5. **The wiki's appearance list is not exhaustive.** Ep. 3 gives Chi-Chi an at-home scene *and* omits her from its own Appearances list (W-17). So an appearance list is **corroboration, never proof** — which weakens the *reasoning* of C030 without touching its conclusion.
