## Added authored consequences and current continuity

- Goku and Gohan each ate a Senzu in Chapter Seven and are fully healed and restored; they have no residual battle injuries or fatigue. The four-year-old Goshin is still injured only because he refused his bean. At the doctor's permitted non-strenuous level he voluntarily makes a few seated foot placements in §9, corrects one and rests; he wants to make his existing Kamehameha reliable. Full practice awaits recovery. This applies mastery-first and serious foundations without denying the user-approved emergency copying exception or declaring a perfected wave (`O088`, `O103`–`O105`, `O108`). Gohan has independent courage, choice and a continuing scholarly ambition. Neither child's capability is reduced to a number.
- Gohan's Four-Star Ball stays on his hat at Kame House; the family's two other balls remain at their mountain home. The family discusses finding and protecting them but has not completed a search. Ox-King, Gohan and Goku temporarily board the wall damaged in the earlier island encounter, and Chi-Chi covers its gaps. After Roshi's earlier disclosure, Goku wonders whether he caused Grandpa Gohan's death, but Roshi does not claim to know (`O112`, `O113`). The Ox-King's truck remains at the ninth marker.
- At the end Bulma takes the unpowered scouter and removed cell separately toward her laboratory; it is neither decoded nor proven safe to transmit. She takes the doctor back to the village first. Roshi has the unused third Senzu. Goku, Chi-Chi, both children, the Ox-King, Roshi and Krillin are at Kame House for the night; the doctor's follow-up and travel home remain ahead (`O107`, `O111`, `O112`).
- Kami intends to send word to Korin. Baba intends to tell Roshi of Kami's inquiry, not of permission. Piccolo trains alone. No new training custody, moon event, revival instruction or King Kai access is established (`O109`, `O110`).
