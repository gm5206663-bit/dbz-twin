# Chapter Fifteen — The House Beyond the Bend

*Anime episode reference: Dragon Ball Z, Episode 14.*

*Revised story draft; not yet accepted.*

A hat rests on a book in the shade. The Four-Star Ball on its crown holds a square of white sky.

## The Road

The scraping comes before Goku sees the cart.

It stops when he comes round the bend. A little man in a work cap stands in the middle of the road with both hands on a long broom. Behind him is a wheeled cart with a row of stiff brushes underneath it.

Goku lands and slows to a run. His boots strike the stone three more times before he stops.

"Hello! Does this road go all the way to King Kai?"

The cleaner points along the curve. "Only one road up here."

"That's good. I was going to ask which way."

Goku looks back along the stretch he has covered. His tracks cross a clean patch where the broom has just been.

"Sorry about that."

"I'd have to sweep it again anyway."

The cleaner pushes the broom toward his cart. One brush catches against a raised seam in the road. He bends to free it, and the cart rolls sideways toward the edge.

Goku catches the handle before the wheel goes over. The cleaner pulls the brush loose, and together they bring the cart back onto the road.

"Do you clean all of it?" Goku asks.

"That's the job."

Goku looks round the bend. There is road beyond that bend and another bend beyond that.

"That's a lot of road."

"I've noticed."

Goku lets go of the cart. The cleaner tests its wheel, then sets the brushes turning again.

"Well, thanks!" Goku says. "I'd better get going."

He starts at a run, placing his feet between the seams. At the next rise he jumps from one joint to the next, turns in the air and lands facing back the way he came. The cleaner is still pushing his cart.

Goku waves, turns and runs on.

He tries the jump again at the following seam. This time he lands ready to keep moving, and his next step carries him up the curve without losing speed.

## Wind in the Wasteland

Sand lifts from the dry ground around Piccolo. Three slabs of broken pyramid rise with it, turning slowly above the empty desert.

Piccolo spreads his hands. Wind strikes the slabs from below and drives them higher. The first cracks against the second; the third turns edge-on, catches the gust and shoots toward the ridge.

Piccolo thrusts out one hand. The slab stops short of the rock face. He draws it back into the wind and sends the other two against it.

Stone bursts apart.

The gust carries fragments across the desert. Piccolo raises both arms and gathers them again before they reach the ridge. Dust streams between his fingers while the pieces hang in the air.

Beyond the wasteland, a familiar ki rises and settles. Piccolo turns toward it. The fragments begin to fall, and he brings them back up with a sharp movement of his hand.

"Not enough."

He drives them together again.

## The Book in the Shade

Gohan has put his hat on top of Mina's borrowed bridge book so the wind will not take the wrapping cloth. One end of the cloth has escaped anyway. It flaps against the bench while Gohan tries to tuck it back in.

"The hat's too light," he says.

"It's got a Dragon Ball on it," Krillin says. "How heavy do you want it?"

Gohan looks at the hat. "I could put a stone on it."

"Then you'd need another hat to keep the stone down," Goshin says.

Krillin laughs, and both boys laugh with him. Goshin moves the book farther beneath the bench, where the wind cannot reach it. Gohan puts his hat back on and checks the Four-Star Ball with one hand.

Across the tiles, Tien and Chiaotzu are practising with two stones. Chiaotzu holds one above the ground while Tien tries to touch it without letting the other fall. Yamcha waits for Krillin with his arms folded; Puar is keeping a towel for him in the shade. Yajirobe sits farther back with his sword across his knees.

"Are we waiting for you or the hat?" Yamcha calls.

"I was helping," Krillin says. He goes over to Yamcha, who sends a small burst toward the open sky. Krillin meets it with his palm and turns it away from the tiles.

Popo gathers four flat stones from the circle where Goshin left them. He sets them down again, one at each side.

"Keep two stones moving while the others cross the circle," Popo tells Goshin.

Eager to begin, Goshin raises one hand. Two stones lift and travel around the edge of the circle. The other two rise between them. One begins to turn; he steadies it and sends both across, while the first pair continue their different paths.

Goshin sends the first pair looping around Gohan's outstretched hand. Gohan swipes at one; it slips between his fingers, and both boys laugh.

Gohan follows one with his finger until it passes behind the other. Then he loses it.

"Which one was that?"

Goshin brings all four to the ground. He picks up the one nearest Gohan and turns it over. There is a dark mark underneath.

"That one."

Gohan takes the stone and turns it back and forth. "I want to try something with it."

"Go ahead," Popo says.

Gohan carries it to the centre of the circle. He sets it on the tiles, backs away and holds out his hand. The stone shifts a little, then scrapes toward him.

"I was trying to make it go the other way."

Goshin crouches beside the stone. "Is the other way harder?"

Gohan looks at the mark. "I don't know yet. I want to send it past Krillin without him catching it."

"Past me?" Krillin says. "All right, but I get to try to catch it."

Yamcha comes over to watch. Krillin takes his place at one side of the circle, and Gohan puts the stone back where it started.

Chi-Chi stands near the bench with Kami. She looks up at the sky above the Lookout.

"How long will the road take Goku?"

"I cannot say," Kami tells her. "Even King Yemma could not give him an exact time."

Goshin leaves the stones and comes to stand beside Chi-Chi.

"How many days until we know he got there?"

"I don't know that either."

Goshin takes hold of the loose end of the wrapping cloth. He winds it once around his finger and lets it go.

"Will you tell us as soon as you hear?" Chi-Chi asks.

"I will."

Goshin looks up at her. She takes his hand.

"I wish he'd hurry," he says.

"So do I."

A stone skids across the tiles and stops against Krillin's foot. Gohan runs after it.

"That time it went too far down."

"Try again," Krillin says. "I haven't caught it yet."

## The House

Goku slows and presses a hand against his side. He flies over the next bend, lands and runs again, breathing hard. Then a roof appears beyond the curve.

A great house stands across the road. Its walls rise above the clouds, and red lanterns hang beneath its eaves. Goku stops outside the gate.

"King Kai's place?"

The gate opens. A rush of air pulls at Goku's clothes, drawing him across the threshold. He plants one boot on the courtyard stones and catches himself before he reaches the door.

"Hey!"

An attendant in a bright robe bows beside him. "Our mistress has been expecting you. Please come in."

"Is your mistress King Kai?"

"Come in," the attendant says again, stepping aside.

Goku follows her across a courtyard. Water runs through a stone channel beside the path, and little bridges arch over it. He looks back at the gate before the attendant leads him through another door.

Princess Snake sits at the far end of a polished room, attended by kneeling servants. Her skin is bluish, her features sharp, and bright orange hair frames her pointed ears. A pale fur boa lies over her patterned dress; earrings swing as she rises to greet Goku.

"Welcome. You must be tired after such a journey."

"Are you King Kai?" Goku asks. "I've come to train!"

Princess Snake takes his hand. "First, you must tell me your name."

"Goku. Can you train me?"

"There is time for that. Stay with us a little while, and you won't have to think about anything at all."

"But I'm trying to think about training!"

Princess Snake's smile wavers. An attendant leans toward her.

"Princess, this is the first time you've looked at a visitor like that since King Yemma."

"King Yemma came here?" Goku asks. "He sent me along this road!"

"He visited five hundred years ago," Princess Snake says. She turns away from the attendant and smiles at Goku. "I don't often meet someone who interests me so much."

Music starts. Princess Snake draws him across the room, one hand still holding his. Goku follows her feet, then steps the wrong way and has to hop aside before he treads on her robe.

"Sorry! Is this how you do it?"

"A little closer," she says.

Goku takes another step. Princess Snake turns; Goku turns with her, catches her other hand and moves beneath her arm.

"Hey! This is like a fighting move!"

He turns his hips and throws her. Princess Snake lands among the cushions at the end of the room.

"Ow!"

Goku hurries over while the attendants help her sit up. "Sorry! I thought you were teaching me a move."

"Princess Snake is not King Kai!" an attendant says.

Goku stops. "You aren't?"

"I am Princess Snake," she says.

"Oh. I thought I'd got there already."

He looks toward the doorway. The music comes to a stop behind him.

"How much farther is it?"

"A very long way," Princess Snake says. "You should eat before you go."

Goku rubs his stomach. "I'm still full, but thanks. I can ask King Kai to train me when I get there."

Princess Snake keeps one hand on his sleeve. "Then you must be especially careful to arrive in good condition. Come and sit down."

Goku looks at the tables being carried into the room, then at the door. One attendant sets down a platter almost as wide as he is.

"What a shame," he says to his stomach. "You had to be full today."

Princess Snake leads him to a cushion before he can get to the door.

## At the Laboratory

A small black cat sits on Dr. Brief's shoulder while he holds a listening instrument against C-6's casing. C-6's bent arm rests on its padded support.

"There," Dr. Brief says. "I heard it again."

"So did I," C-6 replies. "You don't have to put that thing in my ear."

"I thought it might help me find which side the sound came from."

"It came from inside."

Bulma comes in with her bag still over one shoulder. The cat raises its head as she sets the bag down.

"Father, I'm back."

"Welcome home."

She peers at C-6. "How's the arm?"

"Bent."

"Good. You're in a talking mood."

Bulma takes a chair beside the table. On the next bench, Raditz's scouter lies beside a closed box; its power cell is in the box, separate from the device.

"Gohan says Mina lent you a book about bridges," Bulma tells C-6. "He'll bring it when he visits."

C-6 turns his glass eye toward her. "Did she say I could draw in that one?"

"I didn't ask."

"Then I won't."

Dr. Brief sets down the listening instrument. "I'd like to look under the plate near your shoulder. It may let me see where the leak is coming from."

"What will you touch?"

"The cover first. I won't disconnect anything. If I need to do more, I'll ask."

C-6 keeps his eye on Dr. Brief's hands. "All right. Leave the support under the arm."

Bulma brings a light closer while Dr. Brief loosens the plate. Beneath it, several narrow lines disappear into the casing. He points without touching them.

"There may be a mark there. I can't tell yet."

"Can I move the other arm?" C-6 asks.

"If you like."

C-6 lifts his working arm a little. A click comes from inside, and the light in his eye flickers. He lowers the arm again.

"That's enough moving."

Dr. Brief leaves the support where it is and replaces the cover. Bulma switches off the light and moves it away.

"We'll work out how to see that mark," she says. "You can tell us whether you want us to."

The cat climbs down from Dr. Brief's shoulder and settles beside the pencil on the table. C-6 turns his eye toward it.

"Not on the bridge book when it gets here."

"I'll tell Gohan," Bulma says.

## The Feast

Dishes cover the table from one end to the other. Steam rises from bowls of rice and soup; fruit is piled beside a whole roasted fish. An attendant lifts a lid for Goku and waits with a serving spoon.

Goku leans forward. "That smells great."

Princess Snake smiles and holds out a plate. Goku looks at the fish, then presses a hand to his stomach.

"I ate a Senzu before I left. I don't think I could fit any more in."

The attendant's spoon remains over the bowl.

"Not even a little?" Princess Snake asks.

"Maybe when I come back. Will you be here then?"

Princess Snake sets the plate down. "Of course. I'm sure we could find something for you."

Goku gets up. "Thanks for showing me the house. I've got a long way to go."

An attendant moves to the doorway before him. Princess Snake speaks quickly.

"You have been travelling for so long. King Kai will expect you to be clean when you arrive."

Goku looks down at the dust on his gi and the dirt around his boots.

"He will?"

"Of course. Let us prepare a bath."

"A quick one, then."

The attendant leads him out. Princess Snake waits until the door closes, then takes a small packet from her sleeve and gives it to another attendant.

"Put this in his drink."

The attendant looks at the untouched food. "Will he drink it?"

"He has been running. He must be thirsty."

## Mother's Turn

Gohan's stone reaches Krillin at last. Krillin closes his hands around it before it can pass him.

"Got it!"

Gohan runs over. "Can you stand there again?"

He takes the stone back to the circle and holds out his left hand. Krillin watches it; Gohan steps right and sends a small burst from his other palm. The stone lifts and darts past Krillin's shoulder. Yamcha catches it before it can reach the edge of the platform.

"You looked the wrong way," Gohan says.

Krillin grins. "You made me. Let's see if it works twice."

Yamcha tosses the stone back, and Gohan runs after it.

On the clear tiles, Chi-Chi faces Tien. "Would you spar with me? First to two touches?"

"Gladly," Tien says. He restrains his ki for the match, and they take their places well away from the boys and the bench.

Tien comes forward with an open hand. Chi-Chi turns it aside and kicks at his ribs. He blocks; she changes her footing and reaches his shoulder before he can close the distance again.

"One," she says.

Tien touches his shoulder. "Quick."

"I'm not finished!"

He comes in again, and this time he watches her feet. Chi-Chi ducks beneath his arm and turns, but Tien pivots with her and taps her shoulder as she raises her guard.

"One each!" Gohan calls. He has stopped beside Goshin, the stone held against his stomach.

"Have you seen Mother fight like this before?" Goshin asks.

"Not like this."

Tien reaches again. Chi-Chi starts the same kick as before; when he shifts to block, she plants her foot instead. She slips inside his arm, pushes his wrist past her and touches his shoulder.

"Two for Mother!" Goshin shouts.

Puar claps from the shade. Even Yajirobe lifts his head from his sword.

Chi-Chi steps back, breathing hard and smiling. Tien bows his head. "Again later?"

"You can count on it."

The boys hurry over. "You nearly got him with that kick," Goshin says.

"Next time I will. Did you see where he moved his feet?"

Goshin points to a tile, and Chi-Chi shows both boys how she might turn around the same move. Gohan holds up the marked stone.

"I got it past Krillin."

"I saw. How are you going to do it next time?"

Gohan looks at Krillin, who is waiting by the circle. "I haven't decided yet."

"Then you'd better go find out," Chi-Chi says.

## An Extra Bowl

Popo shows Chi-Chi a small kitchen inside the Lookout. She rinses rice in a bowl, and Goshin stands on a stool to measure the water for her. His tail curls around the stool's leg while he pours. Chi-Chi puts the pot on the stove and turns the heat down when the lid starts to rattle.

At the table, Gohan works through a page of sums Chi-Chi brought with her. His tail moves against the side of his seat as he counts. One sum asks about ten apples.

"Can I draw them?" he asks.

"After you've written the answer."

Gohan counts on his fingers. Goshin looks from the pot to the page.

"Are they all for one person?"

"It doesn't say," Gohan says. "Three have been eaten. I have to say how many are left."

"Are the cores left?"

Gohan studies the page. "It doesn't say that either."

Chi-Chi cracks eggs into a pan. Goshin carries three bowls to the table while she stirs the eggs. When the rice is ready, she fills the bowls and adds the eggs. Gohan brings her his paper, and she checks the answer before setting it aside.

They eat at the table. Goshin finishes his bowl, looks into the pot and holds the bowl out again. Chi-Chi divides the remaining rice between him and Gohan.

"How much food was in Father's capsule?" Goshin asks.

"Dr. Brief didn't say how much. He gave him water too."

"Will Father know how to open it?"

"He's used capsules before. He'll know."

Goshin takes the last mouthful from his bowl. Then he slides off his stool and brings a fourth bowl from the shelf. He puts it on the end of the table.

"That's for Father," he says.

Chi-Chi looks at the empty bowl. "It could be a while before he comes back."

"It can wait."

Gohan puts his chopsticks down. "He promised we'd have another match."

"And I'm going to show him my Kamehameha," Goshin says. He looks at the empty bowl, then at Chi-Chi. "Do you think he'll be surprised?"

"I think he'll be sorry he missed your mother's match," she says.

Gohan grins. Chi-Chi moves the extra bowl away from the edge of the table and leaves it there.

## A Bath and a Night

The bath is wider than the floor of Goku's kitchen. Hot water fills it almost to the rim.

"That's a big bath," Goku says.

The attendant closes the door behind him. Outside, another attendant takes the end of a rope and waits for a signal from Princess Snake.

Goku takes Dr. Brief's capsule from his gi and leaves it in a folded sleeve beside the bath. Then he steps into the water. He washes the dust from his arms and shoulders, then ducks beneath the surface. Bubbles rise. Outside the bath, the attendant leans close to the door.

Goku stays under.

The attendant opens the door a little. Goku's hands are planted on the bottom of the bath; he lowers himself toward them, then pushes up again.

"Ninety-eight," he says when he surfaces for air.

He goes down once more. The attendant pulls the door shut, then jumps when his voice comes through it.

"One hundred!"

Goku dresses and tucks the capsule back inside his gi. By the time he comes out, the sky beyond the windows is dark. A round moon hangs over the courtyard.

"It wasn't night when I went in."

"Time passes pleasantly here," Princess Snake says.

Goku walks to the window. The moon sways. Somewhere beyond the wall a rope creaks, and the stars shift together.

He leans out. Above the false sky, a strip of light remains.

"That's not the real sky."

Princess Snake comes up behind him. "We wanted you to stay and rest."

"I've rested enough. King Kai's still waiting at the other end, isn't he?"

"You haven't met him yet."

"Then I don't want to keep him waiting!"

An attendant enters carrying a small tray. On it is a cup of juice, bright as the fruit that stood on the table.

"For your journey," she says.

Goku reaches for the cup. Another attendant calls from the doorway.

"Princess, everything is ready."

Princess Snake takes the cup from Goku before he can drink. "One last entertainment. Then you may go."

She places the cup back on the tray and leads him into the next room.

## The Game

An attendant stands in the centre of the room with a pistol in her hand. Princess Snake and Goku sit across from her. Another attendant waits near the door with the tray.

"It's a game of chance," Princess Snake says. "She will show you."

The attendant raises the pistol toward her own head. Goku is beside her before she can pull the trigger. He catches her wrist and points it at the floor.

"Hey! That could hurt you."

Princess Snake lifts one hand, and the attendant lowers the pistol.

"Don't you like games?" Princess Snake asks.

"Sure. But I don't want anybody getting shot."

The attendant takes the pistol away. Two others enter with curved swords and begin a display in the open space. Their blades cross above their heads; they turn apart, then come together again. Goku follows the movement of their feet.

"That's good," he says. "Do you practise every day?"

The attendants stop and look at him. "Every day," one says.

Princess Snake laughs, a little too loudly.

"You really do think about training all the time."

"King Kai's supposed to be even stronger than Kami. I want to see what he can do."

The swords are put away. The attendant brings the tray to Goku again, and this time he takes the cup. He drinks, thanks her and puts it down.

"Now I've got to go."

He reaches the doorway. The floor tilts beneath his feet.

Goku catches the frame. Princess Snake crosses to him, but he moves his arm away from her hand.

"Something's wrong."

He tries to take another step. The lights in the room run together. His knees give way, and the attendants lower him onto a cushion.

"King Yemma got away," an attendant says.

"This one won't," Princess Snake says. "Bring the dream mirror."

## In the Mirror

The mirror stands beside Goku's cushion. Its surface clouds when Princess Snake passes her hand over it.

"Show me his dream."

On a small mountain, Chi-Chi sets bowls on a table. Gohan is beside her with a schoolbook open. Goshin stands at the doorway, holding a piece of wood.

"Father?" Gohan says.

Goku is there. The yard is under his feet, and the open road is gone.

"Hey! What are you doing here?"

"This is our house," Chi-Chi says. "Where else would we be?"

Goku laughs. Goshin runs over and holds up the wood.

"Look, Father! It was too big for the fire, so I split it."

Goku takes one half and examines the clean break. "All by yourself?"

"I was going to make a really big fire."

"Not in my kitchen," Chi-Chi says.

Gohan laughs, and Goshin sets both halves beside the door.

Chi-Chi puts a fourth bowl on the table. A shadow falls across it.

Two shapes stand beyond the gate. Their faces will not stay clear in the mirror. Gohan closes his book and pulls Chi-Chi away from the window as the gate breaks inward. Goshin turns and fires a burst of ki toward the first shape, driving it behind the wall.

The other reaches the doorway. Chi-Chi kicks the door against its arm and pushes it back. A shelf tilts above her, and Gohan flies up and catches it with both hands before it can fall.

Goku rushes for the step. The yard stretches under his feet, keeping the doorway just out of reach. Goshin fires again; Gohan and Chi-Chi turn toward Goku's voice.

A blast strikes the yard, and the house disappears behind white light.

"Chi-Chi! Gohan! Goshin!"

He sits up on the cushion, breath catching in his throat. The mirror is dark again; there is no broken gate, no kitchen, only the unfamiliar room and Princess Snake beside him.

"It was a dream."

His gaze goes to the doorway, then the window. He presses a hand against the cushion until the room steadies.

Princess Snake stands above him. "You have a wife?"

"And two sons. Where's the door?"

"You were calling for them in your sleep."

Goku gets to his feet, one hand against the wall. "They're on Earth. If those Saiyans get there before I come back..."

He looks at the dark mirror. "That's why I have to get stronger."

Princess Snake takes a step away from him. "After everything I offered you, you would leave me for them?"

"I told you I was going to King Kai. My family's waiting for me too."

The floor shudders. The attendants back toward the walls. Goku steadies himself as the polished boards bend beneath his boots.

## The House Opens Its Mouth

The walls draw together. The window vanishes; the doorway stretches into a narrow passage, then closes. Goku jumps clear of a folding beam as the room twists around him.

A voice rolls through the house.

"I have lived on this road for fifteen hundred years! You won't leave me!"

The ceiling sinks. Goku runs down the passage, but the floor rises under him and throws him against a soft, ridged wall. The lanterns disappear. Light from an opening above reveals rows of teeth.

He is inside Princess Snake.

The opening closes. Goku braces his boots against the wall. A thin stream runs down beside him and hisses where it touches the floor; a drop lands on his sleeve and leaves a dark spot.

"Hey! What are you doing?"

"You will stay inside me until there is nothing left of you!"

Princess Snake's body tightens around him. Goku pushes away from the hissing stream and flies up toward the opening. He catches one of her teeth with his hand, braces both boots against the opposite jaw and pushes.

The opening widens. Goku lets go and flies through it, with the teeth closing behind his boots. He comes out above the road. Princess Snake rears where the house stood, her body a vast serpent coiling across the pale stone. Her head rises above him, and the house he entered is nowhere to be seen.

Her head turns after him. Fire pours from her mouth.

Goku flies along the road. Ahead, the cleaner is pushing his cart around the bend.

"Look out!"

The cleaner drops the broom. Goku lands between the cart and the fire and thrusts both palms upward. His ki turns the flames into the open air. Heat washes over the road, blackening the stone beside the cart.

"Get back!" Goku calls.

The cleaner drags his cart away as Princess Snake's head strikes the road. The impact breaks a raised piece from the edge. It rolls toward the cart, and Goku knocks it aside before it reaches the wheel.

Princess Snake rises again, her long body crossing the road behind her. Goku cups his hands, and light gathers between them.

"Ka..."

"If you want to pass," Princess Snake says, "you'll have to defeat me!"

Goku looks at the light, then at her. He closes his palms and lets it go.

"You gave me a bath! I don't want to blow you up. I just want to get past."

He flies past her head and back over one of her coils. She turns after him. He cuts through the gap between two loops, and she follows, pulling the outer coil across the inner one.

He climbs. Her head comes after him, but the crossed loops pull tight around her body. She twists once, then stops with her coils tangled across the far side of the road.

Goku lands near the cleaner. The cart has lost one brush, and a loose block of stone lies in its path.

"Are you all right?"

The cleaner picks up his broom. "I've had quieter days."

Goku lifts the block out of the path and sets it on the solid part of the damaged edge. The cleaner retrieves the brush and fits it back beneath the cart. Beyond them, Princess Snake pulls at her coils.

"Will you be able to get past?" Goku asks.

The cleaner tests the wheel against the rough stone. "I can manage from here."

The cart rolls through the gap without touching the coils.

"Sorry about the mess," Goku says.

"I'll put it on the list."

Goku waves and runs ahead. Behind him, the broom begins to scrape again.

## Another Try

The light has shifted across the Lookout. Gohan puts his stone in the circle and walks to one side. Krillin waits opposite him, with Yamcha and Puar behind the line of tiles.

Gohan holds out his left hand again. Krillin watches it, then glances at Gohan's right.

This time Gohan moves neither hand. He kicks the stone into the air, runs after it and gives it a short push with his ki. It changes direction above Krillin's shoulder.

Krillin jumps. His fingers touch the edge, but the stone drops past him and lands inside the far side of the circle.

"I nearly had it," he says.

"You touched it," Gohan says.

"That's not catching it."

Gohan picks it up. The dark mark has chipped at one corner. He turns it toward Goshin, who has been watching from beside Popo.

"Could you send one for me next? I want to see if I can turn it while it's already moving."

"Yes."

Goshin lifts the marked stone. Gohan stands beyond the circle, and Goshin sends it toward him. Gohan reaches out with his ki; the stone veers, drops and strikes the ground before it reaches the line.

Gohan retrieves it. "Again."

"I'll keep it the same speed," Goshin says.

He sends it again. Gohan moves beside it, gives it another push and guides it over the line. It lands against his boot.

"Now faster," Gohan says.

Goshin smiles. "This time you won't catch it."

He sends the stone faster. Gohan turns it before it passes him, and it crosses the line again.

While they reset, Popo lays the other stones in a row. Goshin looks at them, then at the open sky beyond the platform.

"May I use my Kamehameha?"

"Yes," Popo says. "Take the stones beyond the edge and aim into the open sky."

Goshin looks over the edge. "There's space out there."

He lifts the three stones. Gohan gives him the fourth and moves back beside Chi-Chi. Krillin and Yamcha leave the clear space in front of him.

Goshin flies beyond the edge of the Lookout. Below him, clouds cover the world. He raises the stones above his head, sends two around each other and guides the other pair across their path.

The four stones keep their separate paths as Goshin brings his hands to his side. Light gathers between his palms.

"Kamehameha!"

His wave strikes the nearest stone. It breaks apart in the open air. Goshin cuts off the wave exactly where he wants it and catches the falling pieces with his ki; the other stones continue along their paths.

He brings the fragments back to the tiles and lowers them beside Popo. Then he flies out again.

The remaining stones travel in three directions. Goshin turns his shoulders, follows one with his hands and fires. This time he catches the pieces before they drop below the other two stones. He sends one fragment looping between them before bringing the pieces back as well.

"That one got dizzy," Goshin says.

Gohan leans toward the edge. "Can I try turning one of the pieces?"

"Yes."

Goshin sends him a fragment. Gohan meets it with his ki and guides it across the line to Krillin, who catches it in both hands.

"This one I caught," Krillin says.

Gohan smiles. "I was sending it to you."

"I know. Let me enjoy it."

Goshin flies back onto the tiles, still grinning. "Next time I'll send them past both of you!"

"I'll catch one before Krillin does," Gohan says.

"You're both making this harder for me," Krillin says.

Chi-Chi gathers the wrapping cloth from beneath the bench and ties it securely around the bridge book. Gohan comes to take it from her.

"I still want C-6 to have this."

"Then we'll take it when we visit," she says. "Mina asked for it back afterwards."

"I remember. And I'll ask C-6 about the picture on the cover."

Goshin comes down beside them, carrying the last two stones. He looks at the wrapped book.

"How many bridges are in it?"

"I haven't counted," Gohan says.

They sit together on the bench. Gohan opens the wrapping and turns the first page. Goshin keeps one hand on the edge of the cloth while they count the pictures, and Chi-Chi sits close enough to see.

---

## Far Beyond the Road

Two round pods move through the dark. Their windows show no ground, only distant points of light.

Far away, Goku runs along the curve of Snake Way. At the next seam he jumps, turns and lands facing the road behind him. There is no house in sight.

He turns back toward King Kai.

"All right. Let's see how far I can get."

He runs, then lifts into the air and follows the bend out of sight.

---

## The Book Again

On the Lookout, the bridge book lies open in the shade. Gohan has put his hat beside it, and the Four-Star Ball holds a square of white sky.

Goshin turns another page. Gohan points to a bridge that crosses a deep valley.

"That one makes six."

"Does the small one behind it count?" Goshin asks.

Gohan looks closer. "It goes across too. Seven."

Chi-Chi rests her hand on the page so the wind cannot turn it before they have finished looking.
