## Realized coverage and current handoff

[Chapter 32 — The Road Beyond Earth](../chapters/Chapter_32_The_Road_Beyond_Earth.md) is a new **unaccepted** local continuation. The source-order table above is the pre-draft plan; the following records what the prose actually does, not a claim that the source episode was fully reproduced.

| Source movement | Realized branch treatment |
|---|---|
| Preparation, language and translation. | Kami expressly permits the described beds, monitors and use, with permission required for further changes. Bulma and Dr. Brief work over several unspecified days; Popo teaches commands and stays on Earth, Piccolo participates and the boys learn distinct words. An air-monitor connection is repaired, a cabin test and several short return-command tests are described, and an opaque latched window cover is installed. These do not certify an interstellar return or every hazard. |
| Family training and separation. | The brothers return home for supper, and all four discuss the trip at the quarry. Goku wants to go and fight strong opponents but elects to stay on guard; Chi-Chi examines the ship, asks pointed questions and consents only after the work and discussion. After both boys report that soreness is gone, she **explicitly permits** fighting with conditions. Gohan wins an independently devised shoulder-touch match, then Goku takes a second round with Kaiō-ken; Goshin shows his controlled Kamehameha and trains with his father. The source's exact mother–son spar and canon injury/relationship staging are not simply copied. |
| Training and growth. | In the corrected unaccepted scene Goku explains and demonstrates Kaiō-ken **times two**. After instruction and practice over more than one visit, Goshin performs **times two and times three**; each multiplies the same unboosted starting output rather than stacking three on the doubled state. He holds back in the family spar, and his active decision redirects their movement after his doubled force carries Goku backward. His substantial fight/first-ape gain and Earth-destruction capability remain; the training also brings unnumbered ordinary-body and ki growth that remains when the temporary bursts end. These chosen factors are not a current scouter reading, complete mastery, a full-power ranking or a ceiling. Gohan wins his own touch round and asks to learn (`C308`, `C309`, `O240`, `O249`). |
| Preparation montage and departure. | Over several days Dr. Mori and a colleague confer with the guards and assess Nappa with his consent, without a definitive diagnosis or transfer; Vegeta continues to decline. Goku, Krillin, Tien and Chiaotzu remain on temporary watch. Their precautions, including a removable cover near Vegeta, are **not secure custody** and do not prevent his artificial moon. Bulma, Piccolo, Gohan and Goshin choose to go; Kami and both parents consent, and they depart from Yunzabit. No ten-day deadline or fixed crew is imported from the source. |
| First shipboard exchanges and future episode. | At launch the window is covered and both sons remain tailed; they fasten their seats and hold hands. The source's particular outfits and later shipboard banter are not forced into this branch. No complete Goshin-made Power Ball, independent space-survival demonstration, confirmed Namekian Dragon Balls, Episode 39 encounter or fixed travel-day count is shown. |

At the Chapter 32 endpoint, the ship is following Bulma's best conversion of King Kai's tentative direction. Its Earth return command and air reserves have bounded local tests, not an interstellar guarantee. Bulma, Piccolo, Gohan and Goshin are aboard; the boys' letter and bags travel with them. Chi-Chi, Kami, Popo and Dr. Brief remain on Earth. At the quarry Goku and Krillin guard Vegeta, while Tien and Chiaotzu guard Nappa; Krillin retains the whole Senzu. Both captives remain injured and awake, and Vegeta may have inferred something from audible conversation; the coordinates were not deliberately disclosed to him. The dates, full route and eventual fates remain open (`O239`–`O242`).

## Historical handoff to protect at the starting line

At the end of Chapter 31, the brothers have brought their letter and a portion of supper to Goku and the guards at the quarry and promised to return to Chi-Chi at home. Goku and Krillin watch Vegeta; Tien and Chiaotzu watch Nappa. Vegeta has refused food, water and an examination; Nappa accepted food, water and limited care. Chi-Chi has asked the family to discuss the trip together, and a medical team is only looking into equipment for Nappa. The exact phase of the natural moon is not known; both moon risks matter. Krillin retains the one whole Senzu. Popo has offered instruction, but ship modifications, the complete route, safe care, roster, Chi-Chi's consent and departure are not yet established. Neither brother may fight until no longer sore and Chi-Chi says so. Goshin has retained substantial first-ape growth and can make a Power Ball, but has not made a full one on-page. Gohan acts independently; Piccolo and Kami are both alive.

At the time of this historical plan the return-from-Snake-Way chronology remained open; the later [coordinated revision](VEGETA_AND_SNAKE_WAY_CORRECTION.md) places the warning before an immediate return without a speed cap, delay or time dilation. This plan does not accept any draft or authorize a GitHub update.
