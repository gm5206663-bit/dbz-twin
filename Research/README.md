# Research library

**Public snapshot:** This is the existing source and research library, not a second working manuscript. Older chapter copies and backup archives were removed from the public working tree; some historical links below refer to local-only material and may not resolve here. Current chapters are in [The Twin](../The%20Twin/chapters/).

These are working references, not replacement story drafts. One verified pre-repair archive is retained locally because Chapters 6–15 were explicitly revised without a remote sync. The immediately previous Chapter 19 and Chapter 20 drafts, and the first repaired Chapter 6 before its continuity correction, are also preserved in their designated chapter-research folders. The selected manuscript and latest author corrections control the current story. Research, proposals and handoffs retain their original date and may describe a different branch.

When a note disagrees with the selected text, use the current [timeline](../The%20Twin/foundation/TIMELINE.md), [status](../The%20Twin/foundation/GOSHIN_STATUS_PANEL.md) and manuscript.
- **[Checker-repair safety snapshot](The%20Twin/Chapter%20Research/Checker%20corrections/Pre-correction%20chapters%2006%E2%80%9315%20%282026-11-03%29.zip)** — exact pre-correction Chapters 6–15 and prior checker/fixtures; see the [repair report](../The%20Twin/canon/CHECKER_REPAIR_2026-11-03.md).
