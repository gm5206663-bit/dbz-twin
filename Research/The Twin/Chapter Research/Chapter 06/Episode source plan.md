Gohan and Goshin are already out of the pod, awake, and rushing from left and right; Goshin's earlier shot has moved Raditz's hand away from Gohan. Goshin's head was struck at Kame House and his palm was burned. He can still fly and fight. The tail cannot be gripped again and the pod breakout cannot happen twice.

## Character and craft checks to perform manually afterward

- Both boys act, choose, and react as four-year-olds. Gohan retains agency and his own capacity for growth; Goshin remains no less capable than Gohan.
- Goshin is emotional and capable of play, but his anger in the fight is quiet/cold. No boast, invented foreknowledge, early golden form, divine ki or observation-triggered numerical boost.
- In deliberate training he masters what he has and works on foundations; no such training is required to interrupt this emergency. Instinctive copying is allowed but must be shown, not an automatic free power increase.
- Manually inspect each instruction directed at Goshin that he obeys: word-for-word repeat before compliance. Do not manufacture extra orders. He waits for an invitation into his father's conversation. On a path with Gohan he takes the downhill side. His hands go behind his back when Chi-Chi looks him over; he only eats food he watched being prepared. He looks away and back when deciding, rather than nodding.
- Keep the protective food and medical consequences honest. Do not use the rejected Chapter 10 or `O124`–`O129` as continuity. No replacement, remote edit or publication is authorized by the candidate request.

After the story is written, make a separate beat-by-beat coverage and authored-change note. Mechanical checks are aids, not a certificate of narrative quality, character compliance or complete visual coverage.
