**Scope and limit.** This is a visual aid, not a substitute for watching the original series. During the 7 November research pass I inspected the **one published still on each Toei episode page for original Z 35–107**: 73 stills in total, 71 of them from the journey/Namek block 37–107. I also read the available short synopses; the earlier full retained-caption reads are a separate evidence stream. A still is one selected, low-resolution picture, sometimes from a flashback, composite or moment outside the advertised central event. Its source cut, colour treatment and broadcast fidelity have not been verified. It does not establish animation, the complete setting, shot order, timing, music, voice, dialogue or a whole performance. No complete episode video or audio was examined.

The links below open Toei’s episode pages and their individual published stills; the observations are limited to what is visible in those samples. **Do not make the source characters’ clothes or appearance automatic facts in *The Twin*.** The selected branch’s manuscripts govern, including its different travelling party and intact tails.

## Namek, buildings and objects

- **Landscape:** [Z 47](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/47/), [65](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/65/) and [84](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/84/) show combinations of blue ground or water, pale rocky shapes and a green sky; [Z 70](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/70/) includes round blue trees. These are selected shots, not a topographic map, a scientific ecology or proof that every region looks identical.
- **Elder’s place:** [Z 54](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/54/) shows the huge seated Elder, his pale garment and pink mantle, a blue-domed interior and much smaller visitors. Use the scale and protective arrangements without attributing dialogue or unseen rooms to the image.
- **Dragon Balls and custody:** [Z 50](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/50/) shows several large orange star-marked balls around Freeza; [Z 75](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/75/) shows a group around seven on the ground. Which individual ball changed hands when must still be established by scenes/captions, not guessed from these two frames.
- **Vessels:** [Z 39](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/39/) offers a crowded, dark larger-ship interior. [Z 60](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/60/) instead shows Bulma in a small, arm-equipped submersible holding a Dragon Ball; the episode synopsis supplies the underwater context. The earlier description of a child in a spacecraft was wrong. Do not transfer equipment between vessels without evidence.
- **False Namek imagery:** [Z 41](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/41/) presents Bulma and a skeletal figure; [Z 42](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/42/) shows Gohan and Krillin from behind facing a large blue, horned figure. The earlier description of two Namekian-looking figures and children was wrong. The later false-planet explanation matters, but this still does not by itself establish that figure's identity or the full scene. The unaccepted branch reaches a changed version of this detour in Chapters 35–36; the still does not establish the authored scene or mandate its outcome.

## Presentation and writing cautions

- Selected images show contrasting scales: tiny Dende near adults, the Elder in his dwelling, armour-clad soldiers around villagers, and wide exterior spaces alternating with cramped cabins. Use a concrete physical arrangement before relying on a list of power readings.
- A held moment can combine danger and care: Goku holding Gohan ([66](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/66/)), Dende threatened by Vegeta ([76](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/76/)), Gohan beside Piccolo ([95](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/95/)). Describing a frame is not proof of its full dialogue, intentions or animation.
- Swirling lines, glowing auras and motion emphasis in stills such as [Z 91](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/91/) are visual presentation; their appearance alone does not certify the Kaiō-ken factor. The retained Japanese cue in that episode explicitly names ×20; see the [power ledger](04_RULES_WISHES_AND_POWER.md).
- **Sound remains a real gap.** The retained captions preserve words and some musical/sound labels, not actual music, pauses, vocal performance, effects, mixing or the relationship between a sound and an uncaptioned movement. Do not claim those elements have been reviewed.
