## Chamber and spar: selected review treatment

In the revised, unaccepted Chapter 22, Gohan and Goshin choose to enter the physical Room of Spirit and Time **together for the full lived year**. Chi-Chi prepares them and waits outside; they can leave if needed before exceeding the cumulative time allowance. About one day passes outside. This joint participation is an authored review choice, not an extra user-mandated rule or acceptance. Gohan already flies independently with Chi-Chi present in Chapter 14 and in the Chapter 17 game. In Chapter 16 he chooses Nimbus as a working platform beside the tiger's net; he develops his existing flight during the chamber year (`O176`).

The room's tenfold gravity, thin air and severe environmental variation are supported as franchise reference by `W-79`; the choice of participants and early visit are authored. In the later selected-caption correction (`C234`–`C236`), Popo explains the cumulative lifetime allowance and the risk of the exit disappearing if exceeded; Kami remembers Goku's childhood visit, and Gohan cannot feel Chi-Chi after the door closes. Chapter 23 lets Goku recognize the room. The twins' first full year uses about half their individual allowance; later participation remains open and the time limit is not a power ceiling. Goshin flies with one large block during the first week and later supports eight same-sized blocks in two physical stacks under the same gravity. Separately he controls moving blocks and targets while using his already-mastered Kamehameha; a stronger sustained wave splits the empty floor before he turns and stops it. Both brothers clash waves. These are displayed gains, not a measured maximum, scouter number or automatic new form (`O165`, `O177`).

At their Chapter 22 exit Piccolo immediately recognizes both familiar, separate ki signatures and senses that each is far stronger than he is; Kami, Krillin, Yamcha, Tien and Chiaotzu also sense the increase without acquiring unshown chamber knowledge. In the author-corrected Chapter 23 he removes his weights and uses his speed, but both boys comfortably outclass him in their chosen ordinary aerial spars and hold back to avoid hurting him. Gohan shows his own stronger wave; Goshin uses chamber-developed speed and previously held-back ki, and steers his mastered wave while moving eight blocks. The easy exchange is not a fresh permanent power increase (`O188`). The brothers then spar each other under control and land breathing easily. Chi-Chi supports and coaches. The assistant-authored eye-blast breach is superseded; the previously winded Goshin portrayal is also rejected (`O170`, `O181`). Gohan tells Goku the year through Kami; the interval before the chamber includes unspecified outside time. Vegeta and Nappa land later the same morning; Chi-Chi, Popo and Bulma plan evacuation away from the fighting, but no evacuation or battle outcome is written.
