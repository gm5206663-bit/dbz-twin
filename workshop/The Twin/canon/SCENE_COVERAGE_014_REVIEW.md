# Chapter 15 — independent review and pre-draft plan

Recorded before the new story draft, 2 November 2026. This is a separate, unaccepted candidate, not a replacement for any protected chapter or prior delivery. The selected continuation is the power-scale-reviewed Chapter 14, itself provisional. No remote copy will be changed.

## What was reviewed

Read the full selected prose of Chapters 1–14, the protected Chapters 1–2 and their preserved pre-targeted-revision bodies, the restored Chapter 2 scenes, the prior Chapter 15 candidate, the prose and cast rules, and the later character, Goku-training, power-scale and continuity handoffs. Earlier versions are useful for voice, not permission to restore their superseded actions. In particular, the current early text has Goshin obey Chi-Chi's instruction to stay and watch the food he eats being prepared. The corrected abduction and the later broad Talent/controlled-ape rulings take precedence over conflicting old language. Do not use the rejected adult-led Chapter 10 branch as current continuity.

Chapter 1 gives the child a concrete thing to want before it explains danger: a bowl, a book, his brother's return. Its pauses are not evenly spaced. Chi-Chi's rules are a way of loving, and Goshin's answers can be exact and funny without making him a miniature teacher. Chapter 2 makes room for sand, a crab, the family resemblance, the friends' interruptions and then Raditz; the ordinary enjoyment makes the interruption matter. Goku's affection is visible in what he does, and the children are young people, not training reports.

Chapters 3–14 were also read as stories, not only for their end states. They contain effective moments worth retaining, including Chi-Chi's waiting, Gohan's animal rescue and book, C-6's independent choices, Goku learning from his loss, and both brothers choosing to train. They also become increasingly reliant on clipped instructions followed by dutiful actions, repeated explanation of actions and similarly sized beats. The issue is not that every later scene is bad or that short sentences are forbidden. It is that variation, child viewpoint and room for unspoken relationships too often disappear. Chapter 14 itself contains an example to keep in view: Popo tells Goshin to try the moving stones again, and Goshin answers "All right" rather than repeating the instruction. Its preceding "Keep up with our studies too" also changes Chi-Chi's "your" to "our." The earlier draft of Chapter 15 repeated this failure. This review is not an authorization to rewrite Chapter 14 or any other earlier chapter.

The new chapter will make Goshin a four-year-old first while showing his exceptional, broad Talent through consequential choices, growing control and quick adjustment. Gohan's own idea and action will matter. Preserve Goshin's verbatim repeat-back of applicable direct instructions before doing them, his downhill position beside Gohan on a sloping path, hands behind his back when Chi-Chi looks him over, and waiting for Goku's conversational invitation. These are standing habits, not decorative tokens; avoid contriving a scene simply to exhibit all of them. Goshin does not eat food unless he watches it being prepared, does not boast or say his rejected Saiyan name, and does not report his own accomplishments as a substitute for action. Goku is already a capable, eager martial artist: his pursuit of King Kai, protection of others, and effort to grow must not become idle waiting or a mechanical checklist.

Respect the external camera for canon characters: their dialogue, movements and visible reactions carry their feelings. Close attention can follow Goshin. Vary rhythm and scene length, use sensory detail where it matters, and keep fights clear without explaining every successful movement twice. Use the episode form, but do not mistake its devices or a numerical score for a successful chapter.

## Goshin and family: review of applicable cues

- Direct instructions addressed to Goshin or both boys are repeated before action: Popo's two-stone instruction; Chi-Chi's “Hold still”; Chi-Chi's dream instruction “Stay behind me, both of you”; Gohan's “Again” and “Now faster”; and Popo's instruction to take the stones beyond the edge. Gohan's “Can you/May I” questions are answered as questions. Other commands in the episode are directed to other characters. This is a manual reading of this candidate, not a proof that every instruction in earlier chapters is compliant.
- Chi-Chi looks Goshin over and his hands go behind his back. Goku's dream Goshin waits for his father's question before speaking. No sloping path has both brothers walking together in this chapter, so the downhill habit is not artificially staged. He watches Chi-Chi rinse and cook the rice and prepare the eggs before he eats them. He does not eat a Senzu.
- Both boys retain their tails. Gohan has the Four-Star Ball on his hat; the hat is beside the book at the end. The Three- and Six-Star Balls remain at home, and Krillin still has the last whole bean. The new ordinary meal does not alter that inventory.
- Gohan chooses his tactic against Krillin and initiates the moving-stone exercise. Goshin controls moving stones and uses the Kamehameha he already knows, including a quick improvement in handling fragments. His Talent is broader than copying; this exercise is a chosen task, not a number, cap, form or ranking. No early golden transformation, god ki, forced ape event, Zenkai or scouter reading is introduced.
- The food, homework, absent father's bowl, uncertainty about news and bridge book are intended to let the family live around training, not just report their abilities. Whether the rewrite achieves the desired voice remains for the reader to judge.
