## Authored action and continuity check

- **Authored rather than source footage:** the village doctor, Mina and her father, the covered bridge, Piccolo's dislodged trees, the trunk and wagon, the rescue, blanket shelter, machinery book and closing visit are new branch staging. Source captions and synopses do not certify their geography or silent actions. Piccolo's training causes the timber to move; he follows it and the family powers downriver rather than appearing from nowhere.
- **Both boys directly change the rescue.** Gohan chooses Nimbus, circles a branch, frees Mina's caught bag, lifts her, and holds a swinging rail away from her. Goshin flies alongside, braces the board, catches it with his injured palm when it slips, and leads Mina out. At the later trunk problem Gohan spots the unsafe firing line; Goshin changes position and uses a narrow shot with his good hand to free the fork. This is an existing ability used under pressure, not an observational power increase or new technique. Neither child is made less capable merely to preserve a source scene.
- **Adults and immediate consequences.** Goku physically holds the trapped trunk and later removes it; Ox-King supports the fallen entrance beam; Chi-Chi tries the unsafe decking, keeps Mina's father back and helps anchor the wagon. They are not absent or made useless, and they do not replace the boys' rescue. The wagon tips and some parcels are lost; the bridge remains standing but is unsafe for the truck. Goku detects Piccolo nearby but stays with the family.
- **Examination and injury.** The family actually reaches a village doctor the same day. She examines Goshin's head, burn and ankle, changes the hand dressing, notes the limits of a single examination, advises monitoring and a return if symptoms worsen, orders no training or fighting until re-examination, and wants to see him again tomorrow even if better. The emergency is an exception to quiet rest, not a declaration of recovery; Chi-Chi checks him afterward and returns him to the doctor. His head still hurts, his burned hand was pressed by the board, and the damp dressing is checked. No Senzu is given to him, no permanent diagnosis is invented, and tomorrow's examination is not yet complete.
- **Moon, tails and knowledge.** The doctor identifies tonight's full moon; a second log levers branches into the bridge roof, opening the boys' sightline to the sky. Goku sees the moon and warns them. The boys say they did not see it, Chi-Chi covers both heads before they leave, and the doctor provides a room with closed curtains. Piccolo is explicitly placed where he hears the warning; the tails are visible below the blanket, not through it. His inference about other Saiyans is conditional. Goku has picked Goshin up before Piccolo observes him carrying the boy. The moon remains intact and both tails remain; no transformation has begun or been reversed.
- **Kami and the other-world inquiry.** Popo reports the dinosaur intervention he actually heard about in candidate 8. Kami comes to the family, explains the two-way Piccolo link and the dependence of the Dragon Balls on his life, and describes his roughly one-year feeling and one-use concern as uncertain. Yemma has only agreed to ask; King Kai has not answered, and access for a living Goku is not granted. Goku wants to learn, but will plan with Chi-Chi before leaving. No death, resurrection, road access, Piccolo fate or guaranteed last wish is fixed.
- **Applicable character rules.** Goshin repeats the doctor's `Follow my finger` and `Don't train or fight until I see you again`, Chi-Chi's `Stay together` and conditional return instruction, Goku's `Don't look up`, Chi-Chi's `Come toward my voice`, and Goku's `Hit the branch without touching the bridge` before complying. The father invites his questions at the clinic, by Mina's book and during Kami's visit. Goshin takes the downhill side beside Gohan on the sloped road, and puts his hands behind his back both when Chi-Chi looks him over at the clinic and when she checks him after the bridge. He watches Chi-Chi wash and cook the rice and eggs before eating them. Gohan has independent initiative, reads and keeps his interest in study; Goshin can be playful and precise as well as hurt. No golden form, god ki, automatic new mastery or numerical power increase appears.
- **Objects and independent cast.** The Four-Star Ball stays on Gohan's hat; the Three- and Six-Star Balls are still last known at home and have not been checked. Raditz's device remains unpowered with its cell separate. Bulma examines it without certifying any component, repacks it, and flies with Krillin toward her lab; neither has yet been shown arriving or finding Yamcha. Krillin still has the remaining whole Senzu. Roshi keeps the medical case containing the Dragon Radar on the island; Turtle, Yajirobe and Baba are also last seen there. Popo leaves the island on the carpet; his arrival elsewhere is not shown. Kami returns toward the Lookout.

## Handoff for a possible Chapter 10

It is night. Goku, Chi-Chi, Gohan, Goshin and Ox-King have accepted the doctor's back room; the curtains are closed, the moon is intact and both boys retain their tails. Goshin has eaten food whose preparation he watched, remains under medical advice, and is due another examination tomorrow. Gohan has borrowed Mina's machinery book and hopes to help recover the lost books when the river is safe. Piccolo is heading toward his ridge. The bridge has been damaged, and the road/truck and medical follow-up are real constraints, not reasons to keep the boys inactive for an entire future chapter.

The preview is only a preview. It does not establish where C-6 is, how the twins meet him, whether an adult accompanies them, or whether the robot survives. Before any next story, inventory the full episode-9 evidence separately. The replacement for the rejected robot adventure must give **both boys direct, sustained agency and a developed relationship with C-6**; the adults must not perform the main rescue while the boys remain remote. Avoid forcing either survival or death.
