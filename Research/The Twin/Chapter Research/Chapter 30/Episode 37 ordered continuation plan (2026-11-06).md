## Source movement in order; branch treatment

1. **37, medical aftermath (04:55–07:01):** Source characters are hospitalized and discuss the future Senzu crop. The branch instead has living Goku and friends, two sore boys who have received a limited check and two injured, hostile Saiyans with a doctor seeking safe care. Continue the actual consent, guard and medical choices; do not create a secure prison, diagnosis, healing or automatic recovery.
2. **37, Saiyan pod and apparent remote accident (07:45–09:31):** In source dialogue a reporter says the pod exploded after Bulma operates a control, and she wonders whether it was self-destruct. In the branch, two round objects remain in separate cordoned city craters and two captured scouters and their separately wrapped cells are in Bulma's case. Do not duplicate the accident or assume the removed cells settled past transmissions or hidden power. City people and the earlier neighbour inquiry can progress through an actual path.
3. **37, Popo's offer and journey (10:21–17:37):** Popo proposes that Kami's old home may be a spaceship and takes Bulma to the highlands. In the branch Popo has only remembered Kami waiting for family and has heard Piccolo relay Nappa's limited claim. Piccolo actually intends to ask living Kami; Gohan has chosen to go with him. Let the brothers, adults and guards make their own choices and show the journey; do not give them the source group's unspoken knowledge.
4. **37, Kami's childhood account and the old house (18:00–20:43):** In Popo's source recollection Kami says he does not remember how he came there, suspects an earlier injury, recalls a letter saying to wait for his family, and describes a round home whose door opened to the word “Piccolo.” He estimates, uncertainly, 20 or 30 years of waiting. The altered branch can have Kami tell Piccolo and the boys himself, distinguish his memory from an independently confirmed origin, and allow a visit if earned. The spoken door word and its meaning are source character claims, not proof of a route, flight, language mastery or a Namekian Dragon Ball set.
5. **37, voice and flight test (20:43–25:21):** Source Bulma and Popo consider the house a possible ship, use voice commands and reach Jupiter. Neither the living-Goku branch's roster nor that flight must be reproduced. Discovery of a working door or light is not proof that a vessel can fly, that its origin or destination is known, or that a voyage has been chosen. Source episode 38's ten-day preparation and Gohan's wish to revive dead Piccolo are contingent on different circumstances.

## Actual starting conditions and design

The family is reunited at the quarry. Krillin and Goku watch awake injured Vegeta; Tien, Chiaotzu and Piccolo watch Nappa. Dr. Mori, Bulma, Yamcha and Puar have departed by car toward the city, with no arrival yet shown. Popo, Launch and Yajirobe remain; Yajirobe has Upa's refilled flask. The two bags and the family's still-folded letter are with Chi-Chi; the separate day-count sheet is at the Lookout. Goshin is ordinary-sized and tailed, in spare trousers and his old shirt, barefoot; both brothers are sore but mobile, neither diagnosed or declared healed. Goshin has substantial actual body-and-ki growth retained after his first conscious Great Ape experience and the fight, and Gohan has independent agency. The promised wave display, fuller assessments, Saiyan care and the city neighbour inquiry are open.

Let Goshin show his father what his stronger ordinary body and ki can actually do, at a chosen safe place while the guard remains visible. A demonstration is not a new scouter measurement or an automatic permanent gain; there is no ceiling or mandatory growth trigger. Let Piccolo ask Kami the question on page, give Gohan and Goshin separate contributions, and let a visit to Yunzabit and the old house follow from the information Kami actually gives them. Keep the history of the two Namekians emotionally specific, not a lecture. The living Goku and his friends remain a real, non-guaranteed temporary guard; the doctor and the city keep their agency. No unexplained ship, route, diagnosis, transformation, release, countdown, automatic source death or voyage.
