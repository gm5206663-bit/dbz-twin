# Goku voice correction — scope and plan

**Written before the new prose edits, 1 November 2026.** The reader correctly objected to Goku saying that he will not go anywhere until he and Chi-Chi know what it means for the family. That line gives him a cautious, procedural motivation instead of his direct eagerness to meet a stronger teacher. This is a writing error, not a necessary consequence of Goku surviving Raditz.

These are **new, separate review copies** of the corrected Chapter 9 and the Chapter 11 candidate. The previous files, protected project, earlier candidates and remotes are not replaced. Chapter 9 is included because its earlier King Kai scene contains the same problem and otherwise would contradict an improved Chapter 11. Chapters 6–8 and 10 were searched for the same explicit family-plan wording, but this targeted pass is not an exhaustive re-evaluation of every character or scene.

## What Goku should sound and act like here

- He becomes openly interested when he hears that King Kai is stronger than Kami. His interest in training and testing himself is real, not merely a grudging duty on behalf of his family. He speaks plainly, reacts in the moment, and can be impulsive, overconfident or tactless.
- He is not a cold stranger to his sons. He has just fought for them against Raditz, and Piccolo recently tried to take them. He can respond directly to that immediate danger without turning into a careful household planner, conditioning his training on Chi-Chi's approval, or promising that he will never leave home.
- Chi-Chi gets to object and worry in her own voice. Her concerns do not become Goku's internal rule or prevent him from wanting to go. Gohan and Goshin keep their established abilities and choices; C-6's informed agreement to transport and inspection remains intact.
- In Chapter 9, replace the earlier overly procedural Dragon Ball and King Kai exchanges with a more natural response, preserving the dragon-ball positions, the unresolved living-Goku inquiry and the family's knowledge of Piccolo. Do not make King Yemma or King Kai grant an answer offscreen. Avoid implying the boys are guaranteed to travel to the Other World.
- In Chapter 11, revise the flagged line, the nearby Piccolo and family-decision dialogue, and the over-formal food/training exchanges where needed. Keep the book-search handoff, robot consent, baseball thread and dinosaur rescue causally intact. Goku can be enthusiastic about training without becoming a one-note caricature who does not care about anyone.

## Evidence considered, not a substitute for character judgement

The local Japanese episode-6 captions at about 10:20–10:30 show Goku immediately interested in training with Yemma after learning his strength; at about 11:40–11:47 he eagerly says he will meet King Kai. The captions' provenance and exact cut remain unverified, and this branch differs because Goku survived. The new dialogue is authored, not presented as a translation of that episode.

A [Kanzenshuu translation of a Toriyama interview](https://www.kanzenshuu.com/translations/wired-japan-1997-akira-toriyama-interview/) distinguishes Goku's desire to face strong opponents from an uncomplicated righteous-hero portrayal; that is the creator's discussion of his work, not a claim that anime Goku lacks affection or never protects anyone. The official site's [martial-artist interview](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_1305.html) also discusses Goku's enjoyment of testing himself. Its [retrospective](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_3850.html) notes a later occasion when Goku spends time with Chi-Chi and Gohan; that does not dictate how he must react in this earlier scene. No complete episode video was watched for this targeted correction.
