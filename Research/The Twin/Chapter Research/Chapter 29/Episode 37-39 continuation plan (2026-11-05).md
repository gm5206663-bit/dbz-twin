## Order and branch choices

1. **Source 37, medical treatment:** Source Goku, Gohan and Krillin are hospital patients; Korin mentions a later bean crop. The selected branch has a living, returned Goku, two wounded and hostile Saiyans, sore boys and the single unused bean. Gohan already asked for ordinary care; Bulma asked a coordinator for help without promising arrival. Continue that thread with an informed, voluntary medic and guarded, partial assessment, not an imposed diagnosis, magically secure prison or a bean used merely to reproduce source staging.
2. **Source 37, TV/pod accident:** Bulma presses a remote and the reporter says a remaining Saiyan craft explodes; Bulma wonders about self-destruct. Two objects instead remain at the branch's cordoned city landing crater; no one has operated them. Do not require an explosion or prove either device safe. The two captured scouters can be examined cautiously after the city response, with any power isolation and uncertainty shown explicitly rather than silently solving past or future transmissions. A scouter visibly measuring someone requires its actual scene-specific reading; do not stage such a scan just to assign a number.
3. **Source 37, Popo and Kami's old home:** Popo connects a remembered childhood account to information that Kami was Namekian and shows Bulma a possible old craft, then helps test it. The branch has only Nappa's limited claims; Kami has not yet been shown hearing them. Popo and Piccolo may discuss what they actually know, but no coordinates, discovery of a functioning ship, other Dragon Balls, disaster or mission follows automatically. Any old memory newly introduced must be clearly Popo's account and not a claim that Piccolo knew it before.
4. **Source 38, agency and voyage:** Source Bulma's first five-day departure suggestion is revised to ten days to prepare the language; Piccolo's death motivates Gohan's independent insistence. Here Piccolo is alive, the world has different needs, and nobody has acquired a route, a confirmed craft or a resurrection objective. Preserve Gohan's independent choices and Chi-Chi's agency; do not force the source party, consent, countdown or launch.
5. **Source 39, space encounter:** Source travelers are on their seventh day and encounter an unknown ship. No branch travelers or comparable encounter exist yet; leave it contingent. Source scouter and Saiyan statements are character- and situation-specific, not branch power ceilings.

## Immediate authored starting point

Continue at the quarry exactly after Goku opens the question of what remains in Dr. Brief's supply capsule. Goku, Chi-Chi and the boys are reunited; both boys are ordinary-sized and tailed, Goshin remains barefoot in the spare clothes, his side/shoulder and Gohan's shoulder are sore without a diagnosis, and his substantial growth in ordinary body and ki from the fight and first controlled Great Ape experience is retained. Goku offered to share the watch. Vegeta and Nappa are awake, injured, hostile and guarded; the two scouters lie on one stone without controls being used; Krillin has the whole bean. Bulma, Yamcha and Puar were last at the gym. Popo has returned with the bags; Yajirobe has Upa's flask. The letter stays folded for the requested home reading, and the day-count sheet remains at the Lookout.

## Intended shape, subject to prose review

Let the reunited family eat and make choices without dropping the guard. Resolve the earlier promise to pass Gohan's offer to the city by actually asking what the crew needs; show the boys and Chi-Chi responding to a specific unfinished upstairs search, with Gohan's own planning and choices and Goshin's retained real strength made meaningful rather than merely named or made into a number. Give the rescue personnel and those helped agency and keep citywide casualty totals unknown. Give the children at the hall the news they were promised if it can occur naturally.

Have Bulma and the medical coordinator make a voluntary, informed plan for a guarded quarry assessment; let treatment be as limited as the actual risk, cooperation, information and equipment permit. Respect refusal while keeping a credible guard. Let Bulma's technical choices be grounded in her earlier handling of Raditz's scouter, with no claim that a removed cell proves nothing has already been transmitted or that unknown craft are safe. Let Piccolo pursue the Namek question with appropriate uncertainty and support; introduce an old-house memory only if it follows naturally. Do not force a death, release, pod accident, journey, new transformation, numeric scale, permanent prison, home letter reading away from home, or acceptance.
