| Question | Earth's set under Kami, in this source period | Namek's set under the Great Elder, in this source period |

For the first Namekian encounter, the official manga-based Porunga feature separately confirms three wishes, the Namekian language, 130 days and the initial individual-revival rule, then describes a **later** upgrade [8](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_2809.html). It is not a direct audiovisual check of the TV episode. A rejected or interrupted request is not automatically a successfully consumed wish; episode 107 explicitly proceeds to a third successful wish after the failed Goku transport.

### Invocation and language

The official feature gives a transliterated summoning phrase, **“Takkarapto Popporunga Pupiritto Paro”** [8](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_2809.html). The local episode-107 caption writes it in Japanese phonetics; spelling in Latin letters varies by translation. Having the seven balls, knowing that a dragon exists and knowing how to summon it are separate prerequisites. Frieza has the balls but not the required method; Krillin and Gohan likewise cannot use the Earth phrase. Dende's participation is essential in the demonstrated source chain.

### Creator, death and succession

In episode 76 the Great Elder dies after two wishes, so Porunga vanishes before the third is completed. King Kai later proposes a rescue that depends on the Earth dragon restoring people killed by Freeza's group. Kami is initially uncertain whether the Elder's life, shortened by the violence, will return; it does briefly return in the source. Porunga reappears and grants the remaining wish. Later the Elder appoints Mūri and dies naturally again; the subsequent 130-day use occurs under the new situation. Avoid stating that **any** dead creator can always be recovered, or that mere succession proves an unrelated set's precise rules.

## 2. The seven balls — chain of custody

This is an aggregate inventory, not a claim that every star marking has been independently checked in footage.

| Step | Original source possession and stakes |
|---|---|
| Village raids | Freeza's party obtains five balls by force, using scouters and questioning villagers. The elder's destruction of scouters changes their search. |
| Vegeta's raid | Vegeta kills a separate village, takes its ball and hides it in water. Gohan later locates it independently; the source identifies his ball as the **four-star**. |
| The Great Elder | The Elder judges Krillin's purpose and entrusts him with the ball he is guarding. |
| Theft and recapture | Vegeta escapes medical treatment and takes Freeza's **five**. He also pressures Krillin into handing over the Elder's ball. Gohan keeps the lake ball hidden temporarily. |
| Ginyu arrival | The temporary Vegeta/Krillin/Gohan alliance brings the five and remaining two near each other. The Ginyu Force intercepts and collects all seven; Ginyu delivers them to Freeza. |
| Secret use | Gohan and Krillin find the assembled set, but the Earth invocation fails. With Dende and the Namekian words, they summon Porunga while Vegeta rests and Freeza is delayed. |
| Interruption and evacuation | The Elder's death interrupts the third wish; his temporary return restores it. The last request moves the Namek population and others, except Goku and Freeza, to Earth. The balls also come to Earth in the source aftermath. |
| Later cycles | On Earth, the recharged balls grant the two further sets of requests listed below, including the Namekians' relocation. |

**Why the count matters:** possession of seven is necessary but not sufficient; the right language, a living dragon/set, the speaker, the remaining wishes, timing, and who can physically intervene all affect the result. Individual star numbers other than the four-star are not asserted here as independently verified from the television visuals.

### Specific branch arithmetic — not a new reading

In selected Chapter 32, Goku uses **×2**, while Goshin uses **×2** and **×3** at separate moments. Each factor multiplies the user's **chosen unboosted output**; the ×3 is not applied to the already doubled state. For illustration only, if the chosen unboosted output were 19,400, then ×2 would be 38,800 and ×3 would be 58,200. The actual 19,400 reading in Chapter 25 preceded further battle and first-Great-Ape growth; it is **not** the current base or maximum. Goshin's real retained growth continues separately from temporary Kaiō-ken; no exact current scouter value is established. Chapter 33 shows a restrained ×2 use and separate further growth. See the [specific audit](../../../The%20Twin/canon/KAIOKEN_POWER_AUDIT_2026-11-07.md).

The author's developing Talent applies to Goshin's **whole** Saiyan–Earthling hybrid bloodline and complete existence. It can interact with life, training, combat, recovery and other factors, producing real retained development. Do not turn source Saiyan recovery claims into a fixed growth rate, arbitrary cap, mandatory injury, compulsory weakness or delay, and do not present an unshown possibility as an already completed feat. Gohan retains his own development and agency.
