## The author's complaint was justified

The selected Chapters 38–41 were read as scenes, with the original Chapter 32 instruction checked as well. Goshin did rescue people and develop strength, but in the three most recent confrontations his speech and internal response were frequently flattened into short instructions and a statement of power. In Chapter 40 he heard that Freeza destroyed his father's world and was contemptuous of children; his anger barely changed what the reader could hear or feel. In Chapter 41 he jumped from ×10 to ×20 with an efficient declaration, but the physical and emotional intensity of the decision scarcely appeared. Gohan's protective anger was more explicit. Counting the word “roar” alone does not establish the quality of characterization; the problem was the surrounding lack of lived expression and consequence.

These are flaws in this branch's prose, not evidence that an original character must imitate a source character or always shout. Goshin's hybrid potential, joy, fear, anger, love, deliberate choices, own voice and actual gains can all coexist. Emotion may produce a major release without requiring loss of control, a fixed multiplier, injury or a new transformation. Gohan deserves his own choices and growth.

## What the reviewed sources actually support

1. **The source's emotional cause is specific.** The [Toei Z 79 report](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/79/) describes Freeza impaling Krillin, Gohan's furious attack, and Freeza subsequently increasing his power. In the retained Z 79 captions, the cry to get out of the way, the repeated fighting sounds, Vegeta's reaction to the rising power, Gohan's concern for Krillin afterward, and Freeza's return occur in a distinct sequence. Some voices are unlabelled and long visual stretches have only music cues: captions cannot verify the exact shot, sound performance or attribution. The [official manga-based Gohan feature](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_1201.html) separately describes Gohan's protection of Namekians and his anger when Krillin appears mortally wounded. Krillin is on Earth in *The Twin*; no identical injury or beat is imported.
2. **The technique is action, not just a number.** The [official Kaiō-ken feature](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_277.html) describes concentrated control and temporary amplification of the body's ki, affecting power, speed, attack and defense; in its source account Goku is its first successful user. In selected retained Z 29–31 cues, Goku first uses it against Nappa, tries ×2 against Vegeta, reaches for ×3 when ×2 is insufficient, and pushes to ×4 during the Kamehameha struggle. The [Toei Z 30](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/30/) and [31](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/31/) reports separately establish the fight's changing advantage and that Goku's body struggles in that particular encounter. The captions include extended vocal effort, observers' reactions, aggressive movement and a subsequent physical price. Not every activation needs the same duration, cry, damage, aura presentation or staging; no new bodily danger is imposed on Goshin merely to copy Goku.
3. **A multiplier is not a permanent gain.** On one selected unboosted output B, ×10 gives 10B and ×20 gives 20B, not 20 times 10B. If the underlying chosen output grows or is raised during the fight, the boosted result can grow too; the temporary multiplier itself is not retained growth. Goshin's own Talent can genuinely improve his body, ki, senses and inherited whole-hybrid qualities, rapidly where supported, without a fixed progression or maximum. The source overview's “all of the ki” wording is not independent proof of every possible held-back application: *The Twin* specifically establishes selected-output practice in Chapter 32.
4. **×10 and ×20 in Z are specific Goku examples, not ceilings.** The retained Z 90–92 captions and [official Z 91](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/91/) and [92](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/92/) summaries show his struggle against a different Freeza, his memory of loved ones, his ×20 Kamehameha and its painful, insufficient result in that fight. The current branch has a different Goshin, different underlying strength, a different Freeza form and different partners. Copying Goku's exact exhaustion, wounds or failure would not be faithful to those changed causes. Use felt pressure, directed movement, force, breath, a genuine opponent's reaction and aftermath when they matter, rather than compulsory suffering.
5. **Connected techniques and later uses remain versioned.** Kaiō-ken can accompany physical movement or a Kamehameha, as those Z sources show. The separate [official Ginyu feature](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_2068.html) describes Goku's amplified scouter rise and Ginyu's Body Change in a different encounter; it does not transfer that event or number here. A [secondary source investigation](https://www.kanzenshuu.com/rumor/goku-kaio-ken-x100/) distinguishes the Slug film's unnumbered original dialogue from ×100 promotional/later-digest claims. The unverified-origin local Z 199 caption has a call of “Super Kaiō-ken” during the separate Other World tournament; a [secondary Z 199 guide](https://www.kanzenshuu.com/episode/dbz/eps-199/) identifies that tournament episode but does not itself specify a multiplier. Do not turn a fan explanation of why the combination works into established mechanics. The official [Super 40 synopsis](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragon_super/episode/40/) and [official game feature](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_970.html) separately describe later Blue/Kaiō-ken combinations; neither creates an early divine form in this branch. ×100, ×20, ×3, a particular transformation combination or a medical price is not a universal ceiling or mandatory ladder.
6. **Other characters react and act.** [Toei Z 80](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/80/) describes Krillin distracting Freeza and Dende helping Gohan; Z 81–82 describe Piccolo's fight after a very different source route, including Nail. [Z 83](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/83/) describes Gohan intervening when Piccolo is attacked, Freeza's further change and Vegeta's separate plan. These are source-specific causes: branch Piccolo is alive and has the elder's awakening, not Nail's assimilation; branch Vegeta is on Earth. Dende has visited the elder but was not shown receiving his own awakening in Chapter 41. Any new aid, healing, changed form, rescue or attack needs its own on-page cause and consequences. Gohan can intervene without making an unearned claim that he equals Freeza.
7. **Freeza has more than one mode of pressure.** The source reports for [Z 87](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/87/) and [88](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/88/) describe a later battle, manipulated surroundings, and Goku discovering an exploitable sensory limitation in that scene. The branch characters have not automatically made Goku's later discovery. Freeza can use his shown movement, intimidation, manipulation, transformation and intelligence, but cannot be handed new knowledge or suddenly erase a demonstrated power advantage to force canon.

## Changes actually made

- **Chapter 39:** Goshin reacts audibly and physically to the threatened villager, Dodoria's shot and Freeza's shot toward Bulma and the children. He chooses to protect the house instead of continuing to hit Dodoria. His retained growth, readings, relative advantage, Gohan's decisions and outcome are preserved.
- **Chapter 40:** the threat to Gohan and the children motivates an embodied ×10 release, including breath, force and control; Goshin also reacts openly to Freeza's contempt and the claimed destruction of his father's world. The earlier 68,600 scan, its greater-than-686,000 same-scale implication, real growth, Gohan's agency and raised-hand endpoint remain.
- **Chapter 41:** Goshin's anger carries into the first-form exchange; ×20 is a full-bodied, vocal choice with an actual reversal and Freeza's reaction. The village-directed attack frightens and enrages him. Piccolo's certainty about how long the boys can hold Freeza was tightened to the danger of the fight reaching the village. The 1,100,000 / 480,000 / 41,200 branch readings, arithmetic, retained gain and exact underwater endpoint remain.

These are selected unaccepted corrections, not a total re-edit or author approval. The exact prior versions of Chapters 39–41 and the separately accepted original Chapter 20 are in the owner-only [Chapter 41 project/research snapshot](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1YAz39qpR6abAN4IgGORSmGBoW_YdJoWE/view). ## Pre-Chapter-42 ordered source plan

- **Z 80:** Freeza threatens Gohan; Krillin diverts him and Dende helps the injured. The source's Krillin/Gohan injuries, Freeza's tail cut, rescue order and later reunion are not automatic here.
- **Z 81:** Piccolo arrives and takes a major role after his source-specific combination with Nail. Here Piccolo has awakened his own potential but has not combined with Nail; source confidence is not a numerical equality.
- **Z 82:** Piccolo and Freeza continue, both raise their commitment, and Freeza makes another change; the source provides possible villain capability, not invulnerable transformation time or an inevitable outcome.
- **Z 83:** Gohan intervenes to protect Piccolo, Freeza counters and begins another change, while Vegeta and Dende face a separate decision. The branch can dramatize protective agency and the changing enemy only where actual positions and choices permit; it does not copy absent characters or the source injuries.
- **Selected Z 87–88:** a later fight uses the landscape and Freeza's abilities. An underwater continuation may draw on those options without presenting their exact later exchange or an unearned sensory discovery as already achieved.
