- **Gohan:** let him rescue, think, ask, fight, play, be afraid and make mistakes for his own reasons. He is not an echo of Goshin, and his independent growth is not erased by his brother's Talent.
- **Goshin:** carry the actual substantial integrated gains and developing whole hybrid bloodline into movement, restraint, problem-solving, battle and reactions. Do not make a threat credible by forgetting those gains, nor invent a completed new ability simply to make a scene easy. He can develop and adapt in ways the author permits.
- **Piccolo:** his earlier connection to Gohan, the new encounter with his people's possible homeland, his link to Kami, and his own strategic choices make him more than a substitute for source Krillin.
- **Bulma:** maintain engineering, navigation, improvisation, fear and agency. Strong companions protect her but do not solve every tool, trust, route or moral problem for her.
- **The children:** they control a vessel, carry fear and can disagree with one another. Their weapons are dangerous to vulnerable people and a ship without necessarily overpowering the fighters. Their future survival and trust are not decided merely by their source identities.
- **Goku and Chi-Chi:** Goku's wish to test strong opponents coexists with family and guard responsibilities. Chi-Chi's protective voice and prior permission conditions are lived history, not a generic ban on adventure.
- **Vegeta and Nappa:** both are alive, injured, proud and under provisional watch. Their prior violence, individual choices, needs, loyalty, information and possible independent schemes have consequences.
- **Dende, Mūri, the Great Elder, Nail and villagers:** if met, let them be people with information and stakes, not an inventory of wishes, healing and power-ups.
- **Freeza and his force:** the original techniques, organization, resources and cruelty are useful source material; a changed branch can alter what they know, when they arrive, which actions they take and who survives. Experienced opponents can react intelligently to demonstrated power.

## 5. Working method for any later chapter

1. Start from the last **actual** scene and selected facts, not this collection's source ending.
2. Check the relevant episode's prior caption-review times and Toei synopsis, noting any missing audio, visual or version evidence.
3. Track positions, possession, injuries, living/dead status, supplies, knowledge, time and personal motives. Do not assume a source death or resurrection.
4. Carry both boys' growth and each person's agency into the action; let an observed gap matter without making source numbers or a temporary boost into the branch's cap.
5. Let a source beat recur, change, or not happen for a cause in the story. Preserve a person's distinctive goal even when their original scene has disappeared.
6. After writing, record what actually happened versus what was merely planned, preserve prior versions and author acceptance boundaries, and review prose independently of the mechanical checks.
