## The correction

The recent drafts put repeated emphasis on Goshin's injury, doctor's restrictions and follow-up, but did not make his unusual recovery and adaptation comparably visible. They give him plenty of action, but the handoff and audit make the head and hand sound like ordinary human injuries progressing on an ordinary human schedule. I also summarized the Talent too narrowly as quick copying and skill retention. **Both are errors of emphasis relative to the user's design.** A four-year-old who is mortal and has not unlocked later forms can still be an extraordinarily resilient, adaptive Saiyan hybrid with a supernatural Talent.

The user's present clarification is broader: the Talent is exceptional rather than ordinary learning; its expression depends on its user and the applicable world; it affects Goshin as a whole, not just technique copying. The user's further statement that his Saiyan bloodline becomes **purer and deeper** is an authored character rule to account for. It is not a source-verified rule that every Saiyan's ancestry changes after a Zenkai. Exactly how this purification affects his human inheritance is not yet specified in the record and should not be guessed.

An early historical entry in the protected `HISTORY.md` records a narrower formulation, including that the Talent never raises battle power. It is a historical record, not a reason to override the user's present clarification. Later `STORY.md` and `STATUS.md` already allow adaptation to support growth, but their present skill-centered summaries still understate the broader scope the user now describes. Preserve historical wording as history; record any correction in a separate candidate or with the user's authorization, not by silently rewriting old evidence.

## What the checked sources actually support

### 1. Recovery, power increase and adaptation are related but distinct

- **Recovery/healing:** the body returns toward health after injury. Saiyans show considerable durability and survival, and some accounts describe rapid recovery, but the sources checked do **not** give a universal natural healing timetable for every wound. Some dramatic recoveries involve an external medical machine, Senzu or Dende. Separating those means does not prohibit Goshin's own Talent from enhancing his recovery beyond an ordinary Saiyan's. For example, the official Gohan profile identifies the Senzu that saves him after Recoome and Dende's separate later healing. [1](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_1201.html)
- **Zenkai / recovery-linked power increase:** a Saiyan can return stronger after recovering from serious injury, classically a near-death encounter. This is an increase associated with recovery, not the name for the healing process itself. The official retrospective says Vegeta powered up after recovering from the battle on Earth. [4](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_2528.html)
- **Hybrid example:** in the local Japanese episode-80 caption file, approximately 18:07–18:15, Vegeta remarks that Gohan's fighting power has greatly increased after recovery and connects it to his Saiyan blood. The official Gohan profile also says Dende heals him and he returns stronger. This is especially relevant: **Gohan's human heritage does not disqualify him from Saiyan-linked growth.** The exact caption provenance and cut remain unverified; the full episode video was not watched for this note. [1](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_1201.html)
- **Growth during fighting:** in the local episode-49 captions at approximately 20:25–20:47, Vegeta says Saiyans grow stronger through fighting and specifically cites nearly dying on Earth. Fighting experience and recovery-linked growth need not be collapsed into a single mechanism. The caption file's provenance and exact cut are unverified; the full episode video was not watched.
- **Individual variation:** official material describes Broly's exceptional innate power and further development during combat. Broly is not a formula for Goshin and his later transformations are not imported into the four-year-old's present era, but the example cautions against treating all Saiyans as having the same development rate. [2](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_941.html)
- **Later manga example:** the official Goku Black profile says Zamasu's restorative power repeatedly heals Black and triggers major Saiyan-cell power increases. This concerns *Dragon Ball Super* manga continuity and external healing; it is comparison evidence, not proof that Goshin now has divine ki or an identical cycle. [4](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_1877.html)

These are selected relevant sources, **not a comprehensive review of every Dragon Ball episode, film or continuity**. Synopses and attributed commentary are not substitutes for viewing complete scenes. Fan sources disagree about how quickly an ordinary Saiyan heals naturally, and estimates of a universal rate should not be presented as settled fact. For this story, the author's supernatural Talent rule can supply stronger recovery without being mislabeled as a universal canon statistic.

### 2. Bloodline and inheritance

Toriyama's 2017 Saiyan Q&A describes S-Cells, inheritance and environmental factors relevant to later Super Saiyan transformations. It does not establish that every Zenkai makes a Saiyan genetically purer. The Q&A concerns a different issue from defining Goshin's bespoke Talent and does not authorize an early transformation in this story. [1](https://www.kanzenshuu.com/translations/saikyo-jump-january-2018-we-asked-akira-toriyama-sensei-saiyan-special-qa/)

The distinction to maintain is:

1. **Canon evidence:** Saiyan and hybrid characters have inherited potential and can gain strength after recovery.
2. **User-authored Goshin rule:** his supernatural Talent can deepen and purify his Saiyan bloodline and affect the rest of him as directed by the user.
3. **Still to clarify:** whether that means a literal change in biological ancestry, a fuller expression of the inheritance he already has, refinement of both heritages, or another mechanism. Do not assume that Chi-Chi's contribution disappears, that a percentage changes, or that one interpretation is the only possible one.

The user's rule may be stronger and more unusual than what is independently verified about ordinary Saiyans. It should be labeled as his design, **not dismissed because canon does not give it to everyone**.

## How the mechanisms interact for Goshin

The following is the user's broad direction, applied without assigning unsupported exact numbers:

- **Saiyan/hybrid constitution:** his inherent endurance, recovery, potential and ability to grow through fighting and serious recovery matter from the beginning. Gohan also retains his own hybrid potential, choices and development.
- **Adaptation Talent:** native to Goshin since conception, exceptional and supernatural, not merely an unusually attentive child's mimicry. It can affect his body, healing, senses, instincts, energy, techniques, learning, inherited qualities and responses to environments or other settings, according to the user's design. It can interact with Saiyan biology rather than acting as a separate passive label. The user has not specified an exact trigger, rate or universal limit for every case; do not invent one, and do not insist adaptation is confined to what he has watched someone demonstrate.
- **Growth and Zenkai:** the Talent and Saiyan development can reinforce each other. A significant recovery-linked increase is possible and may be substantial. The 100-at-birth and later scouter values are historical snapshots, not ceilings. No new exact multiplier or battle-power reading is justified merely because the Talent exists.
- **Skill and retained gains:** exceptionally fast learning, including rapid mastery when appropriate, is possible. Under the established retention rule, what the Talent genuinely makes native is retained; this principle is not confined to copied techniques. Fatigue or injury can affect present performance without erasing an acquired quality. The prior first Kamehameha was ruled to have more raw ki than Goku's in that moment but less practiced control. The mastery-first training preference and the emergency-copying exception both still apply; do not fabricate mastery of a particular unshown technique or force a long remedial stage merely to slow him down.
- **Purification/deepening:** his Saiyan bloodline and its relevant qualities develop as the user directs; this is part of the broader Talent, not a claim that all hybrids are deficient or that every Saiyan automatically changes ancestry.
- **Era and person:** he remains Goku and Chi-Chi's native son, Gohan's twin and mortal at four, with his tail, individual personality and family bonds. No early golden transformation or god ki is introduced by this correction. Mortal does **not** mean biologically ordinary.

No claim here makes him automatically invulnerable or requires him to be hurt to learn. Conversely, absence of a prescribed numerical boost or a bean must not be used as a reason to make him heal and grow like an ordinary human.

## Concrete continuity diagnosis

1. **Raditz, protected Chapters 4–5:** Raditz knocks Goshin unconscious with a blow to the head and burns his palm. Goshin later wakes and acts in the same confrontation, then uses a first Kamehameha after seeing Goku's. This already shows rapid capability and recovery, although the blow's clinical severity and a near-death state are **not explicitly established** in the text. Unconsciousness alone does not prove a completed Zenkai. The Talent can still contribute to growth and adaptation without one. Do not fabricate a completed boost as an already shown fact; a future separate revision can decide how recovery and any qualifying increase are actually shown.
2. **Chapter 6 candidate:** he remains active against Raditz and his hand hurts. That immediate pain is not inherently incompatible with accelerated recovery, but no meaningful comparison shows how quickly the injury is changing or how the whole-body Talent responds.
3. **Chapter 7 candidate:** he flies, uses ki, acts against Piccolo and catches Gohan. The separate candidate does **not** show the particular non-contact hold copied in an older protected draft, so that old event must not be silently imported as something he has already learned here. His recurrent head/hand symptoms are emphasized; recovery or progress in the same interval is not comparably clear.
4. **Chapter 8 candidate:** the battle and rescue continue largely on the same day. Goku and Gohan eat Senzu and are restored; Goshin refuses an unwatched bean and remains capable, but his continuing injuries are presented without a clear visible sign of accelerated self-recovery. A genuine improvement can be shown without inventing bean consumption.
5. **Chapter 9 candidate:** the doctor examines the head, burn and ankle and restricts training/fighting. Goshin still acts decisively at the bridge. The medically cautious framing is not itself impossible, but ordinary-human presumptions would be wrong; a doctor can reassess observable improvement rather than treating a standard timetable as binding on his biology.
6. **Chapter 10 candidate:** it is the following morning. The doctor says the ache has lessened but persists; the hand remains dressed, the ankle stings, and exertion during the C-6 rescue worsens his reported head pain. This is the clearest place where repeated restrictions and follow-up crowd out visible supernatural progress. Revisiting it should consider rapid healing and adaptation, whether exertion truly worsens the original injury, and a medically sensible reaction to his actual condition. It need not remove the child-led robot adventure, C-6's choice, Gohan's agency or capable parents.

A hospital visit or some remaining pain is not logically impossible for a Saiyan. The problem is **using human-like persistence as the unquestioned default and failing to show the extraordinary recovery and growth the user designed**. Nor is it justified to say a normal bruise guarantees a Zenkai or to secretly decide that the Raditz knockout was near fatal.

## Proposed treatment in a separate revision

- Show **observable and consequential recovery** rather than only repeated complaints: compare the hand and head with their condition after Raditz, and let healing proceed at a rate appropriate to Goshin's Saiyan biology and exceptional Talent. The actual pace depends on the injuries and the author's rule, not on a human default or an invented universal clock.
- Show **adaptation as more than a copied attack**: let changes in body, precision, instinct, stamina, senses or his response to the environment emerge through action and subsequent consequences, on top of the skills he already has. The Talent's scope is broad, but do not invent an unrelated ability or claim an unshown event occurred in an existing candidate.
- Treat post-combat growth—including a possible recovery-linked increase—as a real design consideration for Goshin and Gohan, without inventing exact power numbers or declaring a near-death event already proven. Goshin's ability must not be written as static or less capable than Gohan's.
- Let the doctor and parents react competently to what they can observe and know. If symptoms resolve quickly, their advice can change based on a real re-examination; if an actual dangerous injury remains, they can protect him. Neither automatic illness nor unexplained instant clearance should exist merely to serve the plot.
- Preserve the known food rule: Goshin has not eaten the unwatched Senzu. Keep early-era, family, character and source-order constraints, and leave the first five chapters and delivered candidates intact.

These are proposed corrections, **not changes already made**. A separate revision can be prepared once the scope and bloodline interpretation are clear. The full episode-video gap in the Chapter 10 source review remains; this note does not resolve it.
