## Scene-level story decisions before prose

1. **Handoff and consent:** start at the doctor's yard immediately after the previous reading scene. Goku asks C-6 for permission to call Bulma and then to carry him. Bulma and Dr. Brief explain what they propose; C-6 decides whether to permit inspection. Do not imply he has been repaired, that the leak has stopped, or that anybody opened the panel without asking. Chi-Chi and the boys stay in the village and tell Goku where they are going.
2. **Yamcha:** Krillin was last seen leaving Bulma's lab; he finds Yamcha at the stadium. Yamcha, Puar, Bulma and Krillin all have distinct responses and knowledge; Goku's survival, Piccolo's hostility to the boys, Kami's offer, the Saiyans and the former couple's disagreement must not be confused. No Tien/Chiaotzu discovery is invented.
3. **Family and the dinosaur:** Mina's second parcel was last seen near the fallen tree. Chi-Chi, the twins and Mina's father search from a safe high path, not through the unstable bank. A storm drives them to a stable shelter. The wounded herbivore is not declared the same animal as the small dinosaur freed in Chapter 8. Gohan takes initiative, Goshin acts without reporting or boasting about capabilities, and Chi-Chi remains competent and aware of their location.
4. **Action and continuity:** establish the shelter, injured animal, approach and safe line of fire; give Gohan and Goshin separate decisions and effects. Goshin's known non-contact ki and narrowing aim are retained, as is the rapid healing shown previously. Do not turn an ordinary predator into a Raditz-level enemy, invent another blackout, relapse the old injuries, or remove Gohan's ability to act. Goku's arrival follows the flight and lab sequence and supports rather than replaces their choices.
5. **Finish:** the animal is alive but wounded and animal care is sought; no automatic miracle recovery. C-6 is awake and at the lab under a consent-based plan, with no repair promised. The family remains together. The last whole Senzu stays with Krillin, the scouter and its cell remain separate, the moon and both tails remain, and the next Saiyan arrival is still approximately a year from the original warning.

## Character priorities

Goshin's supernatural Adaptation Talent is broad and native; show its retained precision, instincts and developing Saiyan body as appropriate rather than reducing it to imitation. His first Great Ape transformation, whenever it occurs, is controlled, but no such event is introduced in this chapter. He remains no less capable than Gohan without making Gohan dependent. Four-year-old vocabulary, food-watching, downhill positioning, repeat-back of applicable instructions and paternal invitation remain established habits. Gohan's own interests, feelings and choices matter. No early golden form, god ki, invented genetic percentage, guaranteed Zenkai or fresh numerical reading.

## Branch and character review

- **C-6:** the story starts at the doctor's yard. C-6 agrees to the call and to Goku carrying him. At the lab, Bulma asks before padding or examining his arm; Dr. Brief asks before adding support. They explicitly discuss external inspection before any opening. C-6 chooses to stay and wants to know what is being done. The bent arm, uncertain power and possible leak are not declared repaired or cured. The eye has not been repaired, and C-6 does not promise he will wake if he sleeps.
- **Positions and timing:** Goku leaves the village with C-6 after Chi-Chi names the safe path. Chi-Chi, both boys and Mina's father travel together, recover the parcel from above the river and shelter in solid rock during rain. The father goes for Sato; returning Goku meets him near the village and later reaches the shelter. The dinosaur confrontation is already underway when Goku arrives. This is an authored route; it is not the source's solitary wilderness chronology.
- **Gohan:** he finds a safe way to recover Mina's parcel, catches a fish, notices the injury, works with Chi-Chi and Goshin to free the shallow snag, holds food for the animal, physically opens the carnivore's jaws and diverts it. He then chooses to practise, hits his target and keeps his schoolbook in the plan. He has independent choices, growth and the capacity for fear or mistakes.
- **Goshin:** he steadies the branch near the snake with his already shown non-contact ki, notices the approaching animal, protects Chi-Chi from its tail and redirects the predator with a narrow shot. At Goku's invitation he practises existing aim and succeeds without a mandatory period of failure. His old wound and headache are not reset. These are applications of the broad native Talent and Saiyan constitution, not an assertion that his whole Talent consists of copying or that a specific Zenkai occurred.
- **Standing habits:** Goshin watches the rice and apples prepared before eating the rice. He repeats the instructions to stay with Chi-Chi, leave the snake, retrieve a leaf, wait before touching the dinosaur's branch, hold it, keep food visible, aim away from the shelter and practise on the leaf. He takes the downhill side of the path and waits for Goku to address him in their conversations. These passages were checked in context; a phrase count is not a complete semantic proof.
- **Dinosaur identity and care:** the herbivore is not asserted to be the youngster freed in Chapter 8. Goshin says the carnivore *looks like* the one from the river, rather than proving an identity from insufficient marks. It is driven away, not declared evil or guaranteed never to return. Sato examines and dresses the herbivore; it follows Mina's father to a sheltered field, still favoring one side. Sato promises a follow-up, not an instant cure. The field care is fictional and should not be treated as veterinary guidance for a deeply embedded object.
- **Practice and forms:** both boys practise with Goku. Goshin immediately places two controlled ki shots; Gohan succeeds with a pebble at a moving leaf. These are not scouter tests or a ranking. Both tails remain and the moon is intact. Goshin's first Great Ape transformation must be controlled when it happens, but none happens here. No early golden form, god ki, new reading or invented Zenkai is introduced.
