# Chapter 43 — The Breath Between Blows

*Anime episode reference: Dragon Ball Z, Episodes 82–84 (Freeza's changed form, Gohan's intervention and the danger to a healer, adapted to the changed branch), with selected Kaiō-ken and Earth-side elements from Episodes 31 and 88.*

## The New Shape

Goshin's fist comes straight for Freeza's face.

Freeza catches it against his forearm. Goshin twists before the other hand can close around him and drives his knee into Freeza's middle. The blow lands hard enough to bend the taller fighter. Goshin pulls his fist free and strikes him across the jaw.

Freeza's head snaps sideways. A piece of white plate breaks from his cheek and drops into the water.

Goshin's own tail is tight around his waist. He feels a fierce rush of satisfaction: the new shape can be hurt too.

"I hit you," he says.

"So you did."

Freeza's tail snaps around his ankle. Goshin kicks loose, but Freeza is already inside his guard. An elbow catches Goshin across the shoulder, and a second blow drives him down toward the lake.

Goshin stops just above the surface. Water piles up beneath his feet. He pushes off through the spray and comes back with the red light streaming close around him.

Freeza meets him with both hands. Their palms strike, and the broken island shudders beneath them.

Goshin pushes. Freeza slides backward in the air, then braces and pushes in return. Goshin's arms bend toward his chest. He cries out and turns sideways, letting the greater pressure pass him instead of taking it straight on. His shoulder catches Freeza under the ribs.

They shoot across the island together. Freeza digs his fingers into Goshin's arm and flings him away before he can strike again.

Goshin catches himself. His shoulder stings where the elbow landed. The Kaiō-ken is still fierce in his body, and he can feel the difference between the power Freeza is using now and the power he met underwater.

Freeza touches his broken cheek. The dark mark Goshin gave him during the change is still visible on his chest.

"You changed because you couldn't stop me," Goshin says.

"And now?"

"I can still hit you."

Freeza smiles through his sharp teeth. "Then try it again."

Goshin goes in low. Freeza turns faster than he did in the larger form and catches the punch on his palm. Goshin drives his other fist into Freeza's side. This time Freeza releases the fist; he takes the blow, turns with it and lashes out with his tail.

Goshin blocks. The tail hurls him sideways, and a finger points past him toward the shore.

"Gohan!"

Gohan is already moving. A thin beam flashes through the place where he was hovering and strikes the water behind him. Piccolo flies above the second shot and sends a bolt down at Freeza's hand. Freeza turns it aside, but Goshin reaches him before he can aim again.

The boy's fist strikes his chest. Freeza skids backward over the island, his feet cutting two grooves through the broken stone. He catches himself at the edge and looks at all three of them.

Goshin follows, breathing harder. He has been driving the Kaiō-ken through his body since Freeza's previous change. It answers him when he moves, but each breath seems shorter than the one before.

"You said one of the Namekians can heal," Freeza says to Gohan.

Gohan's stomach tightens. He remembers his own voice, excited to tell Goshin that Dende was back.

"I would like to meet this Dende," Freeza continues. "Where is he?"

"I'm not telling you."

"You have told me rather a lot already."

Goshin darts forward. Freeza lifts both hands, and a fan of narrow lights flashes past him toward Gohan and Piccolo.

Goshin turns after the nearest beam. There are too many, and the two fighters are on different sides of the island.

"Down!" Piccolo calls.

Gohan drops toward the lake and fires into the side of a beam. It shifts just enough to miss him, cutting a long line through the water instead. Piccolo knocks another shot upward with his forearm and twists clear of the third.

Goshin catches the last light between both hands. It pushes against him; he shouts and turns it away from the shore. The flash vanishes across the open water.

Freeza comes through the spray behind it. His punch strikes Goshin's guard and drives him back. Goshin's boots skim the surface before he can lift them again.

"You keep looking after everyone else," Freeza says. "How long can you do that?"

Goshin wipes the water from his face. He does not answer. He brings his hands up again.

## Beneath the Branches

In the grove, Cargo sits with his back against the thickest trunk. Dende kneels beside him, watching the branches move whenever another shock reaches the trees.

"Can you heal the houses too?" Cargo asks.

Dende looks toward the village. From here he can see only the upper edge of one roof.

"I don't know how."

"That's all right." Cargo picks at a piece of bark. "Elder Muri knows how to fix them."

"I know how to ask for help," Muri says. He settles the covered Dragon Ball against his knees. "That is how the houses were built in the first place."

The man whose arm Dende healed sits near the path. His companion checks the other approach, pushing aside a low branch just enough to see beneath it. The older resident stays with the children.

Bulma has her case open. She checks the compact communicator, though she already knows what she will find.

"The ship's aerial is still at the inlet," she says. "Without it, I can't call Earth from here."

"Your family is there?" Dende asks.

"Yes. Gohan and Goshin's family too. I promised their mother I'd call when I could."

Another deep impact rolls over the ridge. Cargo presses closer to Dende. Bulma closes the case, keeping the strap in her hand.

"Are they winning?" Cargo asks.

Bulma looks at the trees. She cannot see the lake from the dip.

"I don't know. Gohan and Piccolo went to help Goshin. That's the last I saw of them."

The healed villager looks at his arm. The scrape has closed, leaving a faint line along it.

"I'm Kado," he tells Bulma. "If you need to reach the inlet, I know the shore."

His companion turns from the path. "We don't know who's still there."

"I know."

A branch moves behind her. She starts to raise a hand, but an armoured soldier steps through the leaves first. One hand is pressed to his side, and the strap of his broken scouter hangs against his cheek.

Muri rises with the bundle. The soldier's eyes fix on it.

"I knew you couldn't have gone far."

He has followed the disturbed ground around the village and the marks along the sheltered path. Now that he can see the bundle, he lifts his free hand toward Muri.

"Put it down."

Kado steps between them. The soldier fires at the ground beside his feet. Soil bursts upward, and Kado stumbles back against a tree.

Bulma pulls Cargo behind the trunk. Dende follows, but he keeps his eyes on Muri. The older resident reaches for a fallen branch.

"You can't carry it and fight," the soldier tells Muri.

"I don't have to do both alone."

Muri sets the wrapped ball behind the tree and raises his hand. The soldier turns toward him. Kado's companion rushes in from the side and catches the raised wrist with both hands.

The soldier wrenches against her grip. Kado drives his shoulder into the man's injured side. The soldier gasps and swings his other arm, knocking Kado to one knee.

Muri fires into the ground beneath the soldier's next step. Earth gives way around his boot. Kado's companion pulls the trapped arm sideways, and the soldier falls against the root of the tree.

The older resident brings the branch down across his forearm. The soldier's fingers open. Kado catches that wrist, and Muri holds a bright point of ki close to the man's chest.

"No more," Muri says.

The soldier struggles once, then stops when the light brightens. Bulma comes out from behind the trunk and pulls the strap from her case.

"Use this. It won't hold forever, but it's better than leaving him free."

With the two villagers keeping hold of his arms, Muri winds the strap around his wrists behind the trunk. Kado adds his own belt. The soldier tries to pull his hands apart, but the straps stay tight for the moment.

"He can still shout," Bulma says.

"Then we should move," Muri replies. He looks to Kado and his companion. "Can you take the children to the lower spring? The bank gives better cover than this grove."

"I can," the woman says. "What about you?"

"I'll come with the ball."

Dende has been watching the soldier. The man's breathing is rough, and he curls around his injured side.

"He is hurt," Dende says quietly.

The soldier looks at him. Dende remembers the soldiers at the house and Cargo crying out when the fighting began. His hands stay at his sides.

"I don't want him to die," he says. "But I don't want to heal him while he might hurt us again."

Muri rests a hand on his shoulder. "Then you don't have to."

Cargo catches Dende's sleeve. "You're coming with us?"

"Yes."

Kado looks toward Bulma. "I can fly. I could take you along the trees to the inlet and come back to the spring afterward."

His companion studies the bound soldier, then the path. "We can watch this one until we're clear. Go with her."

Bulma hesitates. The people in the grove have already had one man find them without a scouter, and she has no way to tell whether another is nearby.

"If the ship's gone, we come straight back," she says. "And if anyone's watching it, we don't walk up and introduce ourselves."

"Agreed."

Muri lifts the covered ball. "We'll take the low path to the spring. Watch the village on your way, Kado. Some of our people may still be out there."

"I will."

Cargo hugs Dende around the middle before letting him go. Kado supports Bulma under her arms while she holds on around his shoulders. They rise only as high as the branches and move between the trees, away from the lake.

Behind them, Muri and the others wait until the soldier is no longer pulling at the straps. Then they start toward the lower spring, keeping the children between the adults.

## The Hand in the Spray

Freeza comes at Goshin over the island. Goshin keeps the broken stone behind him in view rather than the open sky. He meets Freeza with a punch that breaks another piece from the plate at his shoulder. Freeza recoils, and Goshin follows with a second strike.

Freeza catches that one. He turns Goshin's arm across his body and brings a knee up toward his stomach. Goshin blocks with his free hand, but the impact jars him all the way through his back.

He pushes away and tries to close the distance again. A pressure catches him around the waist. It is not as tight as the grip Freeza used in the village, but it holds him for the instant Freeza needs.

Freeza shoots past him toward Piccolo.

Piccolo sees the movement and flies across the water. Freeza comes after him with one hand extended, firing a rapid line of beams. Piccolo dodges the first two. The third tears across his sleeve and strikes his shoulder, spinning him toward the surface.

"Piccolo!" Gohan cries.

Piccolo catches himself. Another beam reaches him before he can rise; he knocks it away, and the force drives his heels into the lake. Water erupts around his legs.

Freeza descends, firing again. Piccolo throws up an arm. The shots keep coming, cutting through the spray on either side of him.

Gohan brings his hands together. His fear for Piccolo rushes through him with the strength he found against Zarbon, and more ki gathers between his palms.

"Kamehameha!"

The wave strikes Freeza's firing hand from the side. It does not hurl him across the lake. His arm shifts, however, and the next shots go wide of Piccolo. Freeza turns toward the light.

Gohan pushes harder. For a moment the wave presses against Freeza's hand and shoulder. The larger fighter's fingers close; the bright stream buckles under them.

"Get clear!" Gohan shouts.

Piccolo pulls his legs from the water and flies sideways. Freeza forces the wave away from himself. Gohan sees it bending toward the island and cuts it off before the light reaches the far shore.

Freeza points at him.

Piccolo's arm stretches across the gap. His hand catches Freeza's wrist and jerks it down. The beam tears through the water instead of Gohan.

Freeza clamps his other hand around the stretched arm and pulls. Piccolo is dragged off balance. Gohan rushes forward and strikes Freeza's fingers. They loosen for an instant; Piccolo pulls free and shortens his arm as he flies back.

"You could have stayed down," Piccolo says, landing beside Gohan.

"You were still in the water."

Piccolo looks at him. "You gave me an opening. Next time, take one for yourself too."

Gohan nods, though his hands are trembling. Freeza is facing them again, and Goshin is still forcing his way through the pressure around his waist.

"I shouldn't have told him about Dende," Gohan says.

"He heard a name. That doesn't tell him where Dende is."

The grip breaks. Goshin shoots out of it and strikes Freeza in the back. Freeza pitches forward; Goshin catches his arm and pulls him away from Gohan and Piccolo.

"I'm glad you told me Dende came back," Goshin calls to his brother. "I wanted to know."

Freeza tears his arm free. "How touching. Perhaps the Namekian will tell me where your little friend is."

"No," Piccolo says.

"You haven't heard the question."

"I know enough."

Freeza's tail flicks. A piece of the broken island lifts behind Piccolo and Gohan. Gohan feels it move and pulls Piccolo aside as the stone shoots through the place where they were standing.

Goshin catches the next piece before it reaches them. His fingers close around it. Freeza raises a hand toward the shore, and Goshin throws the stone at him.

Freeza blasts it apart. Goshin comes through the fragments with a yell and punches him across the face. The force carries Freeza backward, but he strikes Goshin's shoulder as he goes. The boy's arm goes numb for an instant.

Goshin shakes it once. His hand opens and closes. It answers him again, though not as easily as before.

Freeza notices.

"I wonder which of us will tire first."

Goshin thinks of Cargo at the house and of Dende coming back to him. He thinks of the children aboard the other ship, and of Freeza saying he had taken too many worlds to remember them.

"You could have stopped," Goshin says. His voice rises. "You could have left every one of them alone!"

He attacks again. Freeza blocks the first blow and takes the second on his shoulder. Goshin tries to follow, but Freeza drives both hands against his chest and sends him flying across the lake.

Goshin stops above the water, his breath coming in short pulls. His red aura flares, then draws close again. Freeza remains over the island, waiting for him.

## The Other End of the Line

On Earth, Dr. Brief sets the scanner farther from the pod. The long cable connects it to a second instrument on the pavement; neither touches the hatch.

"I'm going to take readings from here," he says. "If the field changes when I move the scanner, I want to know before we bring anything closer."

Chi-Chi kneels beside the second instrument. "What am I looking for?"

"The needle and this little light. Tell me if either changes while I'm at the far side."

She nods. Dr. Brief walks around the pod, staying clear of the seam. Krillin watches him from beside the barrier, ready to pull him back if the craft shifts. Yamcha speaks to the officer about the buildings farther down the street.

The needle moves. Chi-Chi calls out the number beside it.

"It dropped two marks. Now it's going up again."

"Good. Keep watching."

She wants to ask whether that means the hatch can be opened. Dr. Brief is still moving the scanner, and the light has begun to flicker. She tells him instead.

"It's flashing now."

He comes back to look. "That could be the field, or it could be the instrument responding to the metal. I need another test."

"How long?" Krillin asks.

"I don't know yet."

Chi-Chi puts a finger on the paper beside the marks she has written. She thinks of Gohan copying the ship's words before the journey, and Goshin drawing a little bed beside the command that opened it. Both boys had been so eager to go.

"Then tell me where to put the next mark," she says.

Kado carries Bulma along the line of trees toward the inlet. From one gap he can see the edge of Muri's village. A door stands open between two damaged houses, but he sees no one coming out of it.

"My friend was working beyond those houses," he says. "I don't know if she got away."

Bulma follows his gaze. "Can we check on the way back?"

"We should. If she's there, she needs to know where the others went."

Farther along, Kado stops beneath a spreading crown. An armoured soldier lies still under the next trees. His scouter is broken and lies several steps away. The two of them watch from cover, but the soldier does not move.

"Can you go around him?" Bulma whispers.

Kado nods. He carries her in a wide curve, staying below the branches until they reach the inlet. The white ship is still beneath the stone shelf. They see no one beside it, though the spur hides part of the shore.

Kado sets her down and checks around the spur. When he signals that he cannot see anyone there either, Bulma hurries to the hatch and uses the recorder to open it.

Inside, the covered window is still latched. She opens it enough to see the shelf and checks the panel. The aerial barely clears the rock overhead; Goshin had put the ship there carefully, and she will have to be just as careful moving it out.

"Keep watching the top," she calls to Kado.

He stands where she can see him through the window. Bulma lifts the landing feet a little and eases the ship sideways toward the water. Kado raises one hand when the aerial comes close to the rock. She stops, adjusts the angle and tries again.

"It's clear!" he calls.

Bulma stops the ship with its aerial beyond the edge of the shelf. She takes the compact communicator from her case, connects it to the aerial lead and presses the call control.

At the Son house, Puar waits beside the telephone. He has checked the pencil twice and moved the paper close enough to the receiver that he can reach both at once.

The telephone rings.

He nearly knocks it off the table picking it up.

"Hello?"

Static crackles. Then a familiar voice comes through.

"Puar? It's Bulma!"

Puar grips the pencil. "Bulma! Where are you? Are the boys with you?"

"I'm at our ship, on Namek. Gohan and Piccolo went to help Goshin fight Freeza. They were on their feet when I last saw them, but I can't see the fight from here."

Puar writes as quickly as he can. "Are you hurt?"

"No. I'm with a Namekian named Kado. The ship's intact, but Freeza's men found it, so I don't know how long we can stay here."

There is a pause while the pencil scratches across the paper.

"Chi-Chi and Krillin went to Dr. Brief's laboratory," Puar says. "She asked me to tell you we don't have a ship ready to come for you."

"All right. Tell my father I saved what I could of our route. If he does find a way here, I'll try to give it to him."

"I will. Chi-Chi will want to know about Gohan and Goshin."

Bulma looks through the ship's window toward the inlet. Kado is standing beside the rock shelf, watching the trees.

"Tell her exactly what I said. I haven't seen them since Gohan and Piccolo went to the lake. I don't want her thinking I know more than I do."

"I understand."

"And tell Yamcha I'm all right."

Puar's voice brightens. "He'll be very glad to hear that."

"Don't make it sound too dramatic."

"I'll tell him you said it just like that."

Despite herself, Bulma smiles. Then she looks at Kado again. He has raised a hand, warning her to hurry. She cannot see what caught his attention.

"I have to go. Call the laboratory, Puar. I'll try to call again when I can."

"Please be careful."

"You too."

She ends the connection and disconnects the communicator from the aerial lead.

Kado is pointing toward the inland trees. A branch has moved there, though neither of them can see what moved it.

"Someone may be coming," he says when she opens the hatch.

"Then get in."

He climbs aboard. Bulma keeps the covered window open just enough to watch the edge of the rock. She begins to move the ship back toward its hiding place, slowly enough that Kado can help her watch the aerial.

At the Son house, Puar sets the receiver down and dials the laboratory. The telephone rings and rings. No one answers.

He looks at the note he has written, then at the receiver. If Bulma calls again while he is gone, no one will hear it. But Chi-Chi is waiting for news, and the laboratory is where he last knew to find her.

Puar folds the paper carefully and flies out of the house toward Capsule Corporation.

## A Little More

Freeza sends three pieces of the island at once.

Goshin knocks the first two apart. The third passes beneath him toward Gohan, who catches it with his ki and turns it down into the lake. Piccolo moves with Gohan, keeping the shore behind them in sight.

Freeza is already in front of Goshin.

Their fists collide. Goshin pushes, but Freeza's other hand catches him across the side of the head. He slips away from the next strike and lands a kick against Freeza's chest. The taller fighter is driven back, yet Goshin does not reach him before he stops.

Goshin's chest hurts with the effort of drawing air. He has followed Freeza beneath the lake, fought him back to the surface and held the Kaiō-ken through another change. His body is still answering, growing surer under the pressure, but holding the red rush together takes more of his attention now.

Freeza glances toward the ridge.

"You said there was a grove. I wonder how many Namekians I'll have to ask before I find your healer."

Goshin surges toward him. Freeza is ready. He catches the boy's arm, twists and throws him toward the broken island.

Goshin strikes the stone and rolls. Before he can rise, Freeza fires past him toward the ridge.

"No!"

Goshin thrusts both hands forward. His ki catches the shot from below and forces it upward, away from the land. The effort tears a cry from his throat. The beam breaks above the water in a burst of light.

Freeza descends through the falling spray. Goshin pushes to his feet, but his legs shake. He can feel Gohan and Piccolo coming closer.

"I can't get to both of you if you stand still!" he calls.

"We're moving!" Gohan answers.

Freeza's hand lifts toward Gohan. Piccolo fires at it; Freeza turns the small shot aside and fires back. Piccolo moves, and Gohan pulls a loose slab of stone between them and the second beam. It breaks apart, but the deflection gives both of them room to fly clear.

Goshin sees Freeza turn to follow them. He remembers his father on the ridge, holding out both palms.

*Feel what your ki is doing before you try to make it rise.*

He reaches beneath the red for the strength he has been using. Even with his breath coming hard, there is more of it than when he first faced Freeza. He gathers it together.

Freeza raises his other hand. Goshin can see the line it will take toward his brother.

"You don't get to choose who dies!"

The shout breaks out of him. He plants his feet against the fractured stone and drives that gathered strength higher through his body.

"KAIŌ-KEN, TIMES THIRTY!"

Red light erupts around him. The island splits farther under his boots, and the water leaps away from its edges. Goshin feels the greater force rush through his chest and down both arms. For a moment it is almost too much to hold together.

He cries out and takes hold of it anyway.

Goshin reaches Freeza before the shot leaves his hand. He strikes the wrist aside and drives his fist into Freeza's middle. The taller fighter folds around the blow. Goshin follows with a punch to his jaw, and Freeza flies across the island, tearing through the ridge of broken stone.

He stops himself above the lake. Goshin is already there.

Freeza brings both forearms up. Goshin's next blow drives the guard back against his face. His second strikes Freeza's shoulder, and the damaged plate cracks open. Freeza swings his tail; Goshin catches it, pulls him off balance and sends him down into the water.

The lake bursts upward. Freeza shoots out of it with both hands raised, firing a scatter of beams. Goshin turns the nearest ones aside as he advances. His body is learning the greater rush as he moves; his hands find the next light more surely than they did the first.

One beam slips past his left shoulder toward the ridge. Goshin reaches after it with his ki while he strikes another aside. The first light bends downward, scoring the water instead of the land. He is still moving when he catches Freeza again.

Freeza tries to block him again. Goshin seizes the front of the broken armour and drives him backward. Freeza's feet scrape a trench across the island before he can tear himself loose.

For the first time in this shape, Freeza looks startled.

"You—"

Goshin's fist catches him in the chest. Freeza crashes through the stone at the far edge, and the broken rock closes over him.

Goshin hangs above the crater. He can still feel Freeza beneath it. His own heart is pounding so hard that it seems to fill his ears.

A dozen stones lift from the crater. They shoot outward, some toward the lake and some toward the ridge.

"Gohan, the right!" Piccolo calls.

Gohan flies across the nearest stone and sends it down into the water. Piccolo strikes another away from the shore. Goshin reaches for the largest pieces and holds them suspended, his arms trembling as he turns them back toward the empty side of the island.

One stone slips. Goshin catches it again before it can pass the others. His fingers shake around the pressure, but the stone stops and settles into the lake.

The red light flickers. Goshin draws a breath that does not feel deep enough. He wants to dive into the crater and end the fight before Freeza can get up. His legs will move, but keeping the greater surge gathered while he holds the stones is taking everything he can give it at this moment.

Piccolo moves between him and the crater. Gohan comes to Goshin's side. Goshin looks at them both, settles the remaining stones on the empty side of the island and lets the Kaiō-ken go.

The red vanishes. He sinks a short distance before he steadies himself in the air. His arms are still shaking. The strength he found in the fight is still his, even without the red light. It hasn't given him back the breath he just spent.

Under the broken stone, Freeza pushes a slab off his leg. Through a gap, he sees the red light disappear. He stays where he is, listening for movement above.

Goshin lands on the broken island. Gohan comes down beside him, and Piccolo takes his other side, watching the crater.

"Are you hurt?" Gohan asks.

"My shoulder got hit." Goshin touches it. "And my lungs think I'm still underwater."

"You aren't."

"I know. The water tasted terrible."

Gohan's smile trembles. He puts a hand on his arm. "Take a breath."

Goshin tries. The first breath catches halfway in. He draws another, slower this time.

"I thought I could keep him away until you got back," he says.

"You did."

"I wanted to make him stop before he could hurt anyone else. I kept hitting him and he kept getting up."

Gohan looks into the crater. "He's still there."

"I know." Goshin swallows. "When he caught my hand, after he changed, I was scared. I didn't want him to see."

"I was scared when he went after Piccolo."

Piccolo keeps his eyes on the stone. "I noticed."

Gohan gives him a startled look. Piccolo's damaged sleeve hangs loose around his shoulder.

"You moved anyway," Piccolo says.

Goshin looks at his brother's hand on his arm. His own fingers are beginning to steady. He reaches up and covers Gohan's hand with his own.

"I'm glad you came back," he says.

"I wasn't going to leave you here."

The rock over the crater shifts.

Freeza rises through it. Water runs from the cracks in his armour. He brushes a piece of stone from his shoulder and looks at Goshin, then at the two fighters beside him.

"You were stronger for a moment," he says. "I see you couldn't hold it."

Goshin's hands clench. He wants to tell Freeza he can do it again. His breathing is still uneven, and Freeza is watching all three of them.

"You'll find out what I can do," he says instead.

Freeza smiles. He turns suddenly toward the ridge and flies low over the water.

"He's going back to the village!" Gohan says.

Freeza does not know where the grove is, but he knows which way Gohan and Piccolo came. The rise is between him and the people who have already had to flee once.

Gohan looks toward the trees. "I'll warn them."

"Gohan—" Goshin begins.

"You and Piccolo can keep him here longer than I can. I can get to Muri and the others."

Piccolo rises. "Go below the ridge. Don't lead him to them."

Gohan nods and drops toward the shore.

Freeza sees him move. His finger turns toward the boy, but Piccolo fires across his hand. Freeza bats the shot away. In that instant Goshin gathers his ki again.

"Kaiō-ken, times twenty!"

Red light returns around him. He catches Freeza's wrist before the next beam leaves it. The force between them throws spray across the lake; Freeza twists, trying to point his hand past Goshin, and Goshin strains to hold it away.

"Go, Gohan!"

Gohan flies beneath the rise without looking back, his tail still wound around his waist. His brother's ki is fierce behind him, and Piccolo's is close beside it. Ahead, the trees hide the village path.

He reaches the grove and drops beneath the branches. The hollow is empty except for the soldier bound to the tree. The man's scouter is broken, and his wrists are tied behind the trunk.

"Where are they?" Gohan demands.

The soldier jerks against the straps. "How should I know? They left me here!"

Gohan looks at the ground. There are fresh marks leading away between the trees, and more than one path beyond them. He can feel small presences somewhere ahead, though the fight behind him makes them difficult to separate.

He starts along the nearest trail, keeping low beneath the branches.
