# Chapter 38 — The People Below

*Anime episode reference: Dragon Ball Z, Episode 45 (the scouts and their report, changed) and Episode 46 (the search for shelter, Earth response and approach to the village, adapted in part), and Episode 47 (the village confrontation, changed).*

## Two Scouts

The two men descend onto the pale rock in front of the white ship. Each wears a newer-model scouter, its green lens over his left eye. One keeps a finger against the device at his temple.

Gohan stands to the left of the hatch. Goshin is on the right, with Bulma between him and Piccolo. Behind them, the ship rests on all four feet.

"A vessel on the eastern shore," the scout with his finger at his temple says. "Four occupants. One looks like a Namekian. Two small ones have tails."

His voice is quiet, but Gohan hears it. The scout releases the control and looks at Piccolo.

"Who are you?"

"We could ask you the same thing," Piccolo says.

The second scout's lens blinks. He looks at Bulma first, then shifts his gaze to Piccolo and the boys.

"Seven," he says. "Two hundred and forty. Three hundred and ten. Three hundred and sixty." He looks at the boys again. "Those two were higher when we first saw them."

Goshin feels his own ki held close, the way he practised with Gohan aboard the ship. Piccolo has lowered his as well. The numbers tell the scouts only what the four are giving off now.

"Which of those was me?" Bulma asks.

"The seven."

"Seven? Your machine doesn't measure brains, then."

The scout smirks. "You're not from this planet."

"Are you?" she asks.

"It belongs to Lord Freeza now. That makes it ours."

Gohan's hand closes at his side. He remembers Bun and the other children aboard the mirrored ship.

"If you do meet anyone who says they work for Freeza," Bun had told him, "don't wait for them to tell you what they want."

Gohan watches the scouts' hands. He will not let them reach Bulma.

The scout takes a step forward. "Open your ship."

"It's already open," Bulma says. "That doesn't mean you're going inside."

"We're looking for information."

"Then you can ask from there."

The other scout looks toward the ship. "The ship isn't one of ours. Did you steal it?"

"No," Piccolo says.

"Then where did you get it?"

"From someone we know."

The scout's gaze travels from Piccolo to the boys' tails. "Are they Saiyans?"

Goshin feels Gohan move a little closer to the hatch. Neither boy looks up past the men to the open sky. Their tails remain wrapped around their waists.

"Who wants to know?" Goshin asks.

"Someone who would like an answer."

"You haven't given us yours," Gohan says.

For a moment the scouts look at one another. The one with the blinking lens spreads his hands.

"We're looking for people, as I said. Lord Freeza wants to talk to the inhabitants. If you're visitors, you can come and explain what you're doing here."

Bulma folds her arms. "And if we don't?"

"We'll make sure you can't leave before he gets his answer."

His companion glances at the ship. Piccolo sees it too.

"Bulma," he says.

She moves one foot onto the bottom step. The scout's hand comes up, palm toward the hull.

Goshin moves before the light leaves it.

The blast strikes his open hand beside the landing foot. He turns his wrist and sends it down into the rock, away from the hatch. Stone bursts upward around his boots. He catches the larger pieces with his ki before they can strike Bulma or the ship, but the impact still shakes the ground.

"Get inside!" Piccolo calls.

Bulma grabs the rail and pulls herself through the doorway. She reaches for the door command, then stops with her hand beside it: Gohan is still outside, and the second scout is rushing toward him.

Gohan ducks beneath the scout's outstretched arm and catches his wrist. The man twists and tries to pull him off his feet. Gohan turns with him, plants one foot and sends him sliding across the stone instead. The scout's free hand shoots toward his scouter; the lens beeps.

"Fourteen thousand six hundred!" he shouts. "Lord Freeza—"

Gohan reaches him before he can finish. He pulls the device free and crushes it in his fist. Its broken lens falls between them.

"You already sent something," Gohan says. "What did you tell him?"

The scout answers with a kick. Gohan catches his ankle, releases it and pushes him back. The man hits the ground hard enough to lose his breath, but he scrambles onto one elbow, still watching Gohan.

Across the landing place, the other scout stares at Goshin through his lens.

"The number's going up. Twelve thousand—"

Goshin keeps the falling stones suspended while he turns toward him. The pressure of the blast is still working through his hand and arm. His body answers it; strength gathers through his shoulders and back, and his ki holds the scattered pieces more steadily than it did a moment ago.

"Thirty-six thousand eight hundred!" the scout says.

The number is still climbing when the scout reaches for the scouter's control.

A pebble strikes the device. The lens cracks and falls away from his eye. Piccolo stands near the hatch with two fingers extended.

"Don't let him aim at the ship again," he tells Goshin.

The scout fires at Piccolo instead. Goshin turns the shot aside with his free hand. This time he directs it into the ground farther from the hull. Piccolo crosses the space between them, catches the scout by the front of his armour and drives him down onto the rock. He lets go only when the man stops struggling.

Goshin lowers the stones into the fresh crater. The strength in his arm does not fade when the last one settles. He closes his hand and opens it again, feeling how easily his fingers move.

"Gohan?" he calls.

"I'm all right."

Gohan is standing over the other scout, whose arm is held against his chest. The man is awake. His companion lies where Piccolo put him, breathing but not moving.

Bulma comes back down the steps, holding the rail tightly. She looks at the crater, then at the nearest landing foot.

"Did it hit the ship?"

"No," Goshin says. "I got in front of it."

She crouches to inspect the foot anyway. Dust coats the lower bracket, but the pin is still in place. Goshin watches her check the hull above it. There is no hole where the blast would have struck.

Gohan picks up the pieces of the crushed scouter and puts them on the ground away from the injured man's hand. The other device lies cracked beside Piccolo's boot.

"Can they still call someone?" he asks.

"I don't know," Bulma says. "I don't want either of them touching those things while I find out."

The conscious scout bares his teeth. "You think breaking a scouter will make Lord Freeza forget you?"

"No," Piccolo says. "That's why we're leaving."

"He'll find you."

"Where are the people who live here?" Gohan asks.

The scout's eyes narrow. "Why?"

"We came to meet them."

"You came at a bad time."

Goshin looks toward the distant hills. He can feel the scattered presences Piccolo noticed earlier. They are still there, but he does not know which ones belong to the people who live on this world.

"Are you going to hurt them?" he asks.

"Ask Lord Freeza."

The man tries to stand. Gohan shifts in front of him, and he stops. Piccolo moves the unconscious scout into the shade of a low rock, out of the disturbed ground around the ship. He does not tie either man up.

"They'll be able to move again," Gohan says quietly.

"Yes," Piccolo replies. "We don't have a place to hold them, and we can't stay here waiting for the others."

Bulma has returned to the hatch. "I can lift us off this rock and get behind that ridge. The display showed a narrow inlet with high sides. It won't hide us from everything, but we won't still be standing where they reported us."

The scout gives a short laugh. "You'll be found."

"Maybe," Bulma says. "I'm still moving."

Gohan steps backward to the hatch, keeping the man in sight. Goshin follows, then pauses to look at the scout beneath the rock.

"He's breathing," Piccolo tells him.

Goshin nods and climbs aboard. Piccolo enters last. Bulma closes the hatch and checks that the opaque window cover is latched before the boys take their seats.

## What Reached Freeza

Freeza studies scouter readings reported from a cluster of settlements: twenty-four, fifty-eight, one hundred and thirteen, and more besides. A voice comes through Zarbon's scouter. Zarbon listens, then turns toward his master.

"A vessel on the eastern shore, Lord Freeza. Four occupants. One resembles a Namekian, and two are small and tailed."

"Tails?" Dodoria asks.

"That is what the scout reported."

Freeza rests a finger against his cheek. "Vegeta did go to Earth. I wonder what he found there."

The scouter crackles again. A voice begins, louder than before, but breaks off before Zarbon can make out the whole message.

"The connection has stopped," Zarbon says. He touches his control. "Report."

Nothing answers.

"Should I go?" Dodoria asks.

"You may stay with me." Freeza looks toward Zarbon. "Take two men and inspect the landing place. If there are visitors, I would like to know who they are and what they want."

"And if they resist?"

"Then use your judgment. I would still prefer someone who can answer questions."

Zarbon bows his head and selects two soldiers. They rise above the shore and fly toward the reported location.

Another soldier approaches Freeza from the opposite direction. His scouter has found several readings close together near a group of round buildings.

"A settlement," he reports. "The readings are between twelve and one hundred and forty. There are people inside."

"Good," Freeza says. "Perhaps these people will be more forthcoming than an empty field."

He turns his chair toward the distant buildings. Dodoria follows at his side, with the remaining soldiers behind them.

## The Inlet

The ship settles beneath a shelf of stone. Bulma keeps one hand on the controls until the vibration stops.

"The feet are down," Goshin says from beside her.

"I can see the indicators too."

"I know. I was checking."

"Good. Keep doing that."

On the panel, the inlet is a narrow curve between pale cliffs. The shelf above the ship hides much of the hull from the air, but the opening faces the water. A person who came close enough could still see it.

Piccolo checks through the doorway while the boys remain behind the covered window. The rock overhead blocks the sky from the foot of the steps. He looks farther along the shore before letting them come down.

"Stay under the shelf," he says. "I haven't checked beyond it."

Gohan looks at the narrow strip of water and the dark spaces between the blue trees. "Will those men tell the others where we went?"

"The first one sent a report before we stopped him," Bulma says. "I don't know what got through. And we didn't see everything their scouters did."

She takes the radar from her bag and turns it on. Several points appear. She switches it off again before putting it away.

"I still don't know what those are. A signal isn't enough."

"But there are people," Piccolo says.

"Yes." Bulma looks toward the opening. "And those two work for someone who's looking for them."

Goshin remembers the children on the mirrored ship and the noise of the guns they had aimed at Bulma. The men at the landing place had worn armour like the people Bun described. He rubs the dust from his hand.

"We should find the people before Freeza does."

"We don't know where he is," Gohan says. "Or how many men he has."

"We can look," Piccolo says.

Bulma glances at the ship. "First I'm calling home. I told Chi-Chi I would."

She returns to the cabin and connects her communicator to the aerial lead. Gohan and Goshin stand beside her. Piccolo remains at the hatch, looking out toward the inlet.

The line rings twice.

"Hello?" Chi-Chi says.

"It's Bulma again. We're together, and the ship's still in one piece."

"What happened? Who were those people?"

"Two men wearing scouters and armour like the Saiyans'. They said they worked for someone called Freeza. One of them tried to hit the ship."

Chi-Chi is silent for a beat. "Gohan? Goshin?"

"We're here," Gohan says. "They didn't get Bulma or the ship."

"We stopped them," Goshin adds. "Piccolo's with us."

"I'm here," Piccolo says from the doorway.

"Did either of you get hurt?"

"No, Mother," Gohan says.

"One of the men sent a message before we broke his scouter," Bulma says. "We moved the ship, but others may come looking."

"Then come home."

Bulma looks at the covered window. "We can try. But there are people here, and those men are looking for them. We don't know what Freeza plans to do."

"You know what his men tried to do to you."

"I do."

Gohan touches the edge of the table. "Mother, we met children whose home was attacked by people who said they worked for Freeza. If there are families here, we have to warn them."

"You have to stay alive too."

"We will be careful," he says. He knows how small the promise sounds after the blast at the ship, but he means it.

Goshin leans toward the communicator. "Tell Dad the ship's all right. And tell him we can feel a lot of people. We don't know which ones belong here yet."

"I will. I'll tell him everything you told me."

"Can you call Dad?" Gohan asks.

"Not from this room. He's still at the quarry. I'll go to him."

"Call my father too," Bulma says. "Ask whether he can think of a way to reach us if we need help. Don't have him touch the scouters to do it."

"I'll tell him. Bulma—"

"I know. I'll keep the boys with us."

Chi-Chi draws a breath. "Call again when you can. All four of you."

"We will."

Bulma ends the call. For a moment she keeps her hand on the communicator. Then she disconnects it from the aerial and puts it back in her case.

"If I want to call that far again, I'll need the ship's aerial," she says. "I'm taking this with me anyway. It may work over a shorter distance without it."

A heavy presence brushes the edge of Piccolo's awareness.

He turns toward the opening. Gohan feels it a moment later. Goshin comes to the hatch beside them and stops.

There are several powers moving together. One is so much larger than the scouts' that the others are hard to separate from it at first.

"Is that Freeza?" Gohan whispers.

"I don't know," Piccolo says. "Whoever it is, keep your ki down."

The three of them let their power settle. Bulma cannot feel what they feel, but she sees their faces. She does not need to ask twice.

"Inside?" she says.

"Under the rock. Away from the opening."

They move back into the shadow beside the ship. Bulma presses herself against the hull. Goshin stands beside her, close enough to feel the vibration when she shifts her weight. Gohan and Piccolo watch the narrow opening from behind the landing foot.

Shapes pass above the water beyond the inlet: armoured figures and, among them, a small figure riding in a chair. They continue toward the hills.

No one at the ship speaks until the last figure is gone.

Gohan lets out a breath. "That one in the chair."

"I felt him," Goshin says. The enormous presence is moving away, but he can still tell where it is. His fingers curl around the edge of the landing foot. He remembers Vegeta's power at the quarry and the way the ground shook when they fought. This feels different.

Piccolo's eyes remain on the opening. "There are smaller presences in that direction too. They were there before the group arrived."

"The people who live here?" Bulma asks.

"Maybe."

Gohan looks up at Piccolo, careful to keep the rock ceiling in sight behind him. "They're going toward them."

"I know."

"We can't leave them with those men."

Piccolo looks at Bulma. "You could stay in the ship. I'll stay with you while the boys look."

"And if more of them find us here?" She points toward the inlet. "The ship's a much bigger target than I am. You said they were going toward people. I want to see what they're doing."

"It could be dangerous."

"I noticed the blast at the landing place."

She takes her small case and slings the strap across her shoulder. "If we're going, we'll go together. Then nobody has to come back and tell me what happened while I sit here imagining it."

Goshin smiles, just a little. From the side, his child-sized face and black spiked hair make her think of Goku as a boy, though his eyes are Chi-Chi's and his shoulders are a little broader than Gohan's. The smile disappears when he looks toward the distant presences again.

"We can move the ship farther under the shelf," he says. "Not into the wall. Just behind that rock."

"Can you lift it without scraping the aerial?" Bulma asks.

"I can try. You watch the top."

He places both hands on the hull. The ship rises clear of its feet. Its weight comes to him through his arms, familiar from the repair on the previous planet. He finds more room for it in himself now; the effort that filled his shoulders at the landing place leaves him space to adjust.

"A little to the left," Bulma says.

Goshin carries it sideways. Piccolo watches the rear legs while Gohan moves a loose stone out of their path. When Bulma tells him to stop, Goshin lowers the ship behind the thickest part of the shelf. The aerial clears the rock above it.

"There," Bulma says. "We'll have to come out again to make a long call, but anyone flying straight past the inlet shouldn't see the whole ship."

"Shouldn't," Gohan repeats.

"I heard you. No, I'm not promising."

She closes the hatch and puts the recorder in her pocket. Piccolo looks at the boys.

"The sky changes as we move. Don't look up unless I tell you I've checked the view from where you are."

Both nod. Their tails are still tucked close.

Piccolo steps out from beneath the shelf first and examines the visible route. Blue trees rise along the slope, their round crowns spreading over a band of shadow. He takes Bulma securely under one arm and flies low toward them. Gohan follows on his left, Goshin on his right. They keep their eyes on the ground and the rocks ahead, using the broken cover of the trees rather than climbing into the open sky.

They know that flying may make them easier to find. They also know where the larger group is going. Twice Piccolo lands beneath the trees when unfamiliar presences move across their path; each time the four wait under cover until those presences have gone farther away.

## The Name at the Quarry

Chi-Chi reaches the quarry. Goku and Krillin are where she left them, watching Vegeta. Across the broken tracks, Tien and Chiaotzu remain with Nappa.

Goku rises when he sees her face.

"They called again," she says. "Gohan and Goshin are all right. Piccolo and Bulma are with them."

His shoulders loosen. Then she tells him about the two scouts, the armour Bulma described, the attack on the ship and the name they gave.

Vegeta opens his eyes.

"What name?"

Chi-Chi looks at him. "Freeza."

For the first time since she arrived, the prince's tail stops moving.

"Where?" he demands.

"They don't know for certain what planet they're on," she says. "They do know his men were there."

"And the ship?" Krillin asks.

"Intact. They moved it. Bulma said one of the men had already sent a message."

Goku crouches in front of Vegeta, outside the reach of his hands. "You know Freeza."

Vegeta's expression is tight. "Of course I know him."

"Were those men yours too? Their armour looks like yours."

"We served him." Vegeta looks past Goku at Chi-Chi. "If Freeza has gone there, he isn't looking for a friendly conversation."

"What does he want?" Goku asks.

"Your green friend is a Namekian. One of his kind may have made Earth's Dragon Balls." Vegeta's eyes flick toward the distant gully, where Nappa is sitting under guard. "If there are more of them out there, Freeza will want them."

"You wanted them," Krillin says.

"I still do. I wanted eternal life on Earth, and I would have had it if your friends hadn't interfered."

Chi-Chi steps closer to Goku. "If there are balls where they are, has Freeza taken them?"

"How should I know? I'm here, aren't I?"

Goku studies him. "Is Freeza stronger than you?"

The prince's mouth twists. "Yes. For now. If the wish works, I could survive long enough to find a way to destroy him."

"You served him," Krillin says.

"I didn't say I liked it." Vegeta leans forward, ignoring the pain in his side. "I want him gone. If he has heard about the Dragon Balls, he may want the same wish. Do you want him to have it first?"

Nappa lifts his head from the gully. "What's got him so worked up?" he calls.

"Freeza's men attacked our friends' ship," Chi-Chi calls back. Tien moves closer to Nappa while Chiaotzu watches from the other side.

Nappa looks toward Vegeta. "If Freeza finds out how you failed here, he won't need those children to finish you!"

"Worry about yourself!" Vegeta calls across the tracks. "He'd kill you for less."

Nappa settles against the rock again. His eyes remain on the prince, not on the sky.

Goku's hands tighten. He wants to meet someone Vegeta fears. He wants to know how that power moves, how it feels across a fight. He also sees Chi-Chi standing beside him and hears Gohan's voice in what she has just told him.

"I want to go," he says. "But I won't leave you here with him."

Krillin looks toward Nappa. "Tien and Chiaotzu can't leave their watch to take over ours. Not unless somebody else watches Nappa."

"I called Dr. Brief before I came," Chi-Chi says. "I told him what Bulma asked. He's looking at what he has, but he didn't say he had a ship ready."

"What about the Saiyans' ships?" Goku asks.

"I asked him. Bulma's test destroyed one of the pods, and he doesn't know whether the other is safe to use."

Vegeta shifts against the wall. Krillin steps forward, and the prince stops moving.

"Bring me my ship," Vegeta says. "I know his soldiers and how they search. I'll go after him myself."

"You tried to kill people here," Goku says. "I'm not handing you a ship. Tell me what you know."

Vegeta bares his teeth. "His men use scouters to find power and report it. If one sent a message before it was broken, more can follow. They'll look for the ship too."

"They moved it," Chi-Chi says.

"Then tell them not to assume it will stay hidden. Freeza won't send only two scouts if he thinks something is worth taking."

Goku looks toward the sky. Chi-Chi takes his hand, and he holds it for a moment.

"You want me to tell them more?" Vegeta asks. "Get me to my ship."

"No. Not after what you did," Goku says.

"Then stand here while Freeza finds your sons."

Goku turns toward Krillin.

"Stay with him. I want to ask King Kai something."

Goku closes his eyes without moving away from the wall.

*King Kai? Can you hear me?*

On his small planet, King Kai is sitting beside the road with Bubbles. He puts down the cup in his hand.

"Goku? What's happened?"

*Chi-Chi spoke to Bulma again and came to tell me. Two men attacked their ship. They said they work for Freeza.*

King Kai goes still. "Freeza?"

*Vegeta knows him. He says Freeza is stronger than he is.*

"Vegeta should know. Freeza has taken many worlds, Goku. If he's near your sons, don't assume the men at the ship are the worst they will meet."

*I want to go to them, but I'm still watching Vegeta. Chi-Chi asked Dr. Brief about a ship, and he hasn't said one is ready.*

*Can you reach Piccolo? Tell him what you know?*

"I'll try." King Kai puts a hand to his back and concentrates. "Give me a moment."

Goku opens his eyes. Vegeta is watching him.

"Talking to that friend of yours?" the prince asks.

"Yes."

"Ask him whether he has a spaceship."

"I will if you keep interrupting."

Krillin almost laughs. Chi-Chi does not, but her grip on Goku's hand eases.

"I'm going back to the telephone," she says. "If Bulma calls, I want to hear it."

"I'll come as soon as we can arrange the watch," Goku says. "And I'll talk to Dr. Brief about the ship."

"You'd better talk to me too."

"I will."

She looks once more toward Nappa and his two guards, then leaves the quarry. Goku returns to his place beside Krillin. Vegeta's eyes are open now, fixed on the gap beneath the rock overhang.

## A Place That Is Real

Piccolo hears King Kai's voice as he and the others pass beneath the blue trees.

*Piccolo. Goku asked me to reach you.*

Piccolo slows and looks toward the brothers. They have not reacted.

*I can hear you,* he replies.

*Chi-Chi told Goku about the scouts. Is everyone with you?*

*Yes. The ship is hidden for the moment. We felt a group moving toward people, and we're following them.*

*If Freeza is with them, be careful. He has a reputation for taking worlds and destroying anyone who stands in his way.*

Piccolo looks at the ground racing below them. *We met children who lost their world to his soldiers.*

*Goku wants to come. He is still watching Vegeta, and there isn't a second ship ready that he knows of. He asked me to warn you, not to tell you what you must do.*

*Tell him we heard you. Tell Chi-Chi the boys are beside me.*

*I will.*

The voice fades. Piccolo tells Bulma and the brothers what King Kai said, including Goku's wish to come and the fact that no ship is ready.

"Dad knows?" Goshin asks.

"He does now."

Gohan looks toward the distant ridge. "Then Mother told him."

"Of course she did," Bulma says.

They land beneath a broad rock ledge. Beyond it, the ground rises toward the rounded top of the ridge. The great presence is on the other side, no longer moving as quickly.

Piccolo puts Bulma down. "We'll go the rest of the way on foot."

Gohan starts up the slope, then waits while Piccolo checks the sky visible from the next patch of cover. Goshin stays beside Bulma. They cross a strip of pale ground to an overhanging bank, where the rock blocks the view above them.

Below the ridge, round white buildings stand among the trees. A narrow path runs between them. Several armoured soldiers are on the ground near one of the doors.

And at the door stands a green-skinned man.

His ears are pointed. Antennae rise from his forehead. He wears a dark outer garment over light clothes and holds one hand against the door behind him.

Piccolo stops so abruptly that Gohan takes another step before noticing.

"Piccolo?"

Piccolo does not answer at once. He has looked at his own face and Kami's all his life. He had looked at the two supposed Namekians on the last world and watched their faces change.

The man below is standing between the soldiers and his home. A child peers around his side. The man moves his arm to keep the child behind him.

"He looks like you," Goshin says quietly.

"I can see that."

Piccolo's voice is rougher than he intended. Gohan puts a hand on the rock beside him, close enough to touch his sleeve.

"They're real people," Gohan says.

"I know." Piccolo watches the man turn toward the soldiers. "I want to hear what they have to say."

Freeza's chair is near the center of the path. The large pink attendant stands beside it. The soldiers who came with them spread out among the buildings.

Bulma presses close to the bank, with the boys on either side of her and Piccolo at the edge of the cover. She watches one soldier peer through a doorway while another motions toward the people outside.

"There are children," she whispers.

Gohan has already seen them. A second small figure appears at the window behind the green-skinned man. The man speaks without turning around, and the child ducks back inside.

Freeza's voice carries up the slope.

"I apologize for the disturbance. We are looking for something, and I hoped you could help us."

The man at the door does not move. "This is our home. You were not invited."

"Even so, here we are."

"What do you want?"

"I've heard that there are objects on this world called Dragon Balls. I would be interested to know whether you have one."

Bulma's fingers close around the strap of her case. Gohan looks at her, then back at the man below. Until now, the marks on her radar have been marks on a screen.

"Who told you that?" the man asks.

"Someone who knew another person from your world." Freeza smiles. "I should introduce myself. I am Freeza."

The man keeps his hand against the door. "You are on Namek. Leave it."

Piccolo hears the name. It is not an impostor offering an elder in exchange for a ship. It comes from a man standing in front of his own home, with a child at his back.

Freeza turns slightly toward Dodoria. "Would you explain to him that I'm asking politely?"

Dodoria takes a step toward the door. One of the soldiers catches the arm of an older villager who tries to move between them.

Gohan rises from behind the bank.

"Gohan," Piccolo says.

"He's going to hurt them."

Goshin is already beside his brother. He can feel Freeza's enormous power, the nearer soldiers and the smaller lives at the doorway. He can also feel Gohan's ki beginning to rise.

"I see them," Goshin says. "I'll get the soldier away from that man."

Piccolo looks once at Bulma. She has taken the recorder from her pocket, but she is watching the people below rather than the screen.

"I'm staying behind the rock," she says. "Go."

Piccolo steps past the boys. "Gohan, the children. Goshin, with me until we're clear of the door."

Gohan does not wait for the soldier to raise his hand again. He pushes off the slope toward the village. Goshin follows, and Piccolo moves with him.

Below them, the elder turns at the sound of their approach.
