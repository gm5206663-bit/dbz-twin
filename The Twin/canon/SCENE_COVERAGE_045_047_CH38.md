## Audit and exact handoff

- The first two scouts remain alive at the original landing, one injured and conscious, the other unconscious but breathing. The first report describing the ship, four occupants, a Namekian-looking person and two tails reached Freeza's group; a later call broke up. The 14,600 Gohan display and 36,800 Goshin display are seen locally; do not assume Freeza received either or that the damaged devices can never transmit. In the subsequently corrected Chapter 39 the conscious scout tells Zarbon the earlier 430/520, their lower recheck and the 14,600 figure, but not the other device's 36,800. Zarbon and two soldiers were dispatched to inspect the original landing; their arrival and report are unshown (`O272`, `O273`).
- Bulma piloted the intact ship to an inlet. Goshin moved it farther beneath the shelf while Bulma watched the aerial and Piccolo watched the rear. No magical invisibility or absolute security is claimed; the ship and long-distance aerial are unattended while all four go toward the village. Bulma has the portable communicator and recorder; its long-range use needs the ship aerial, and shorter use is only a possibility (`O273`, `O274`).
- Goshin’s body and ki genuinely gain strength during the defense and retain it afterward; his ship lift applies the change. The 360 suppressed reading and 12,000/36,800 rising snapshots are authored, local and not maxima. Earlier major first-ape, whole-being and whole-hybrid development remain. Gohan disarms and acts independently, with his own 14,600 output measured in that encounter; Piccolo protects, investigates and chooses; Bulma inspects, pilots, calls and chooses to follow. No Kaiō-ken, new form, psychic immunity, full self-made moon or unaided-space survival is shown (`O272`, `O274`).
- The brothers remain ordinary-sized with tails, and Piccolo checks available sky views as the group travels. The unnamed resident identifies the world as Namek; none of the travellers has yet authenticated a radar point, an actual local Dragon Ball, its number, custody or rules. The party has not fulfilled its promise to seek help for Raiti and Zākuro, who remain at the damaged wreck on the previous world with food, water and a locally tested transmitter.
