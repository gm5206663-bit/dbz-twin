## Exact provisional handoff

Chapters 6 and 7 remain separate candidates, not accepted replacements. Gohan and Goshin are awake, together on the wooded side of a stream after escaping Piccolo's grasp; he is pursuing them. Gohan has the four-star hat and a hurt shoulder. Goshin can fly and fight; his palm is burned and bandaged, his head still hurts after being knocked out by Raditz, and Piccolo has gripped his ankle. Both have acted repeatedly. Goku is alive but seriously hurt; he and Roshi are following the river, with the Dragon Radar in Roshi's medical case. Nimbus is returning to Gohan on Goku's instruction. Bulma is at the aircraft, which landed in a clearing with damaged rear control; the wrapped scouter and its separate cell are in her different pockets, unpowered and unverified. Krillin has three Senzu from Korin and is flying toward the sensed powers. None has been consumed. Popo has left the Lookout on his flying carpet to help; he has not arrived. Kami asked Yemma about King Kai, and Yemma said he would ask without promising an answer. Chi-Chi has learned of Raditz's kidnapping from Turtle, but not of the later rescue or Piccolo's attack; she and Ox-King are driving toward the coast. Turtle only knows what he witnessed at the island.

## Before drafting: agency, consequences and anti-drift checks

- Start in the woods at the Chapter 7 cliffhanger and **resolve or materially advance the pursuit**; do not spend another chapter restating the demand and refusal. Let the boys' choices alter the outcome. If another part of this episode is needed, continue sequentially rather than silently discard source scenes.
- Make a physical danger clear in space and cause. Goshin remains at least as capable as Gohan and uses his retained flight/ki; Gohan's courage, observation and independent choices matter. Danger cannot depend on everyone forgetting Goshin can fly. No free numerical boost from observing, no early golden transformation or god ki. If Piccolo intervenes, his own self-interest and recent cruelty still count.
- The injured father and capable friends act, but do not replace the children's direct experience with an adult-led rescue. Popo, Krillin, Chi-Chi/Ox-King, Bulma and the unpowered scouter each have specific ongoing threads; any meeting must have an intelligible route and no character may know unobserved events without a source.
- Senzu completely heal and restore. If a bean is eaten, no leftover wound or fatigue. Goshin has not watched these beans' preparation and will not eat one. An unwatched bean cannot be snuck into him. A prior head injury and a burned palm need honest consequences, not an invented hospital or a chapter of medical logistics.
- Check Goshin's word-for-word applicable instruction repeat before complying, waiting to be invited into his father's conversation, taking the downhill side beside Gohan on paths, hands behind his back when Chi-Chi looks him over, and eating only food whose preparation he watched. These are behavior, not exposition.
- Preserve Gohan's wish to be a scholar and both children's playfulness and fear. Goshin's rage goes quiet/cold, but he is a four-year-old with feelings. His first Kamehameha had greater raw ki than Goku's at that moment with less control; momentary scans are not power ceilings or damage equations.
- The robot C-6 episode remains future work. Both children must eventually have direct, sustained agency and relationship with C-6, but neither its result nor any fabricated isolation or parent incapacity is predetermined now.
