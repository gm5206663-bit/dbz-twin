# Chapter 14 — Goku training correction: plan

**Prepared before modifying the copied Chapter 14, 2 November 2026.** The user objects that Goku is not going for training. The earlier candidate repeatedly left King Kai's answer pending while Goku stayed on Earth doing limited exercises. That was an authored delay and a characterization mistake; it is not excused by the existence of those exercises. The correction must change what Goku actually does, not just add dialogue about his intentions.

## Evidence and branch choices

- [Toei's Japanese episode-6 synopsis](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragonz/episode/6/) describes Kami taking Goku to King Yemma, permission to go to King Kai, and Goku setting out along Snake Way. The local episode-6 Japanese captions around 09:15–18:50 convey the Yemma conversation, guide, traditional road length and Goku's departure. Their provenance/cut are not verified; no complete video or new complete manga reread was performed.
- In that source Goku has **died** and retained a body. Here he survived. In the corrected branch Yemma authorizes Kami to bring the **living** Goku to the Other World and lets him take the road. That is an explicit authored adaptation, not a claim that canon universally requires or forbids a living person to enter. The existing Chapter 9 inquiry was pending, so its positive answer must be shown rather than retroactively presented as already received.
- Goku is the one who decides that waiting is wasting time: he goes to Kami and asks, accepts the opportunity immediately, reaches Yemma's office and **starts the journey on-page**. Snake Way is itself strenuous movement and training, not a replacement that indefinitely postpones the goal. He remains alive. King Kai's personal decision about teaching him and the journey's eventual duration remain future events; do not award Kaioken or prematurely put Goku at the destination.
- The original guide says a dead traveler cannot starve; that cannot be transferred to a living Goku. He takes ordinary provisions for the long road, without inventing another Senzu. He and Gohan recently ate a whole Senzu and are not suddenly hungry. No Instant Transmission, guaranteed instantaneous return, numerical boost or fixed power ceiling is introduced.
- The user already emphasized that Goku's strength and continuing growth come substantially from his choices, persistence, training and ingenuity, while Goshin has exceptionally broad innate Talent. Show Goku seeking a stronger master and working hard in transit. Do not make him wait for a family-negotiation pledge or rely on a winner/loser comparison as a measure of permanent potential.
