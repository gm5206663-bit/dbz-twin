# Goku voice review and provisional continuity handoff

## What changed

**Chapter 9.** When the Dragon Balls are discussed, Goku says they will stop the Saiyans and shows interest in how strong they are. Chi-Chi objects rather than adopting his enthusiasm. Hearing about someone stronger than Kami, he wants to meet King Kai and asks whether they can go immediately. Kami says Yemma has not answered the question of travel for a living Goku and that King Kai has not agreed to teach him. Chi-Chi raises the immediate threat from Piccolo; Goku answers that they will be ready if he comes back, then invites the boys to train. Gohan's studies are still part of the discussion. Goku no longer asks for a duration or pledges to settle a family plan before leaving.

**Chapter 11.** When Gohan asks to accompany him to see C-6's repair, Goku says yes immediately. Gohan then remembers their commitment to Mina's books, so Goku takes C-6 and the others follow the previously established safe path. Goku's C-6 dialogue is more casual, but he still asks about the call and transport, passes on C-6's conditions, carries him as requested and observes the limits on inspection. At the lab he speaks plainly about Piccolo and shows excited interest in training with King Kai, including an impulsive air-punch. He does not claim permission, an answer, a departure date or a route. In the dinosaur scene he adopts a ready fighting stance while Gohan and Goshin retain their own decisive actions. In the final exercise he eagerly includes both boys; his praise refers to the target actually hit instead of claiming that Goshin changed where he stood.

Goku's delight in food remains, but he does **not** complain of hunger or have a growling stomach: Chi-Chi reminds him that he is still full from the whole Senzu he ate in Chapter 8. He likes the taste of her rice and proposes catching fish for later. This distinction avoids importing the source episode's hungry, dead Goku into a branch where the living Goku recently ate a bean. Goshin watches preparation before his own rice; Gohan and Goshin keep their existing choices and capacities.

## Continuity if these candidates are chosen

- Read the revised Chapter 9 instead of the Chapter 9 inside the older combined Chapters 6–10 reading file; that combined file remains unchanged. The revised Chapter 11 follows the previously corrected Chapter 10. These changes do not imply that any version has been accepted.
- Kami's enquiry remains open: a living person's access has not been approved, King Kai has not agreed to teach Goku, and no one has started down Snake Way. Goku wants to go if the opportunity arises; he has not made the earlier pledge to wait until a family plan is settled. Chi-Chi has independently objected to the Piccolo danger.
- After Chapter 11, Goku, Chi-Chi, Gohan and Goshin are together near the village. They plan to visit C-6 with the borrowed book and to catch fish on the way home. The Three- and Six-Star Balls were last known at home; the Four-Star Ball stays on Gohan's hat.
- C-6 remains awake at Bulma's lab with Dr. Brief. External inspection and support were agreed to; no panel opening or completed repair is shown. His energy and bent arm remain unresolved. Bulma, Krillin, Yamcha and Puar are travelling toward Korin Tower; Krillin still has the last Senzu. Tien and Chiaotzu have not been located on the page.
- Gohan and Goshin retain their tails and independent actions. Neither has transformed; the moon remains intact. Goshin's eventual first Great Ape transformation is to be controlled from the start. His family habits, mastery-first practice and wide Talent continue under the existing rules. Piccolo knows the moon warning and remains at large.
- Mina's father has the recovered parcel. The injured herbivore is resting in a sheltered field under Sato's care; the carnivore has left with the fish, without a guaranteed future outcome.
