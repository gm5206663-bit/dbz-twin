# Chapter 41 — The Shape of Power

*Anime episode reference: Dragon Ball Z, Episodes 52–54, with selected, brought-forward elements from Episodes 78–79, adapted to the changed branch.*

## Above the Water

The brothers move before the light leaves Freeza's finger.

Gohan drops toward the water. Goshin darts across Freeza's raised hand and strikes it from the side. The thin beam cuts through the air where Gohan was and vanishes over the empty shore.

Freeza turns his wrist. A second flash forms at his fingertip, this one aimed down.

"Don't touch my brother!"

Goshin closes his hand around Freeza's arm and wrenches it up before the shot can leave. His red aura lashes over Freeza's white armour. Freeza tries to tear his arm free, but Goshin cries out and drives a fist into his middle.

The blow folds Freeza over. Goshin lifts him by the trapped arm and throws him across the lake.

Freeza stops himself above the broken island. His feet gouge a furrow in the stone, and he looks up with his teeth showing.

Gohan rises from the water behind him. "Goshin!"

He fires a short burst at Freeza's back. Freeza twists away from it, straight toward Goshin, who meets him with both hands. The impact drives Freeza back into the island. Water leaps around its edges.

"Keep him away from the village!" Gohan calls.

"I will!"

Freeza's answer about the destroyed world is still ringing in Goshin's ears. He can hear how angry he sounds, and he does not try to hide it.

Freeza comes out of the spray with a snap of his tail. Goshin catches it, swings him away from Gohan and releases him over the middle of the lake.

Freeza steadies himself. The cut on his cheek has opened again. He wipes it with one finger and stares at the red mark.

"You find this amusing?"

Goshin's face hardens. "No."

He remembers Freeza's answer about the Saiyans' world. He has never seen that world, but he has seen the people in the village and the soldiers hunting them. He moves between Freeza and the shore.

"Leave them alone."

Freeza raises a hand. The water beneath Goshin swells into a column and closes around his legs. Gohan sends a burst into its side; the column breaks, and Goshin shoots clear before it can pull him under.

"He keeps making us look away," Gohan says.

"Then don't look away from him. I'll get the water."

Freeza flicks two fingers toward Gohan. Gohan veers low, and Goshin is in front of Freeza before the invisible pressure can close around his brother. Goshin's shoulder strikes Freeza's chest. They travel together over the water until Freeza twists and drives an elbow at the side of his head.

Goshin blocks it, catches Freeza's wrist and hits him again. Freeza is forced back. This time he does not immediately return.

The lake rolls between them. Goshin's heart is hammering. The Kaiō-ken rushes through his arms and legs, making every movement feel fiercely alive. He holds the multiplied ki together, and his growing body answers the force. There is still more of his own power to draw on; for now, he can keep Freeza away from the village.

Gohan hovers a little behind and to his right, watching their opponent rather than the sky. His tail is wrapped close to his waist.

"You can still fly?" Goshin asks.

"Yes. Are you all right?"

"Yes. Don't let him catch you."

"I wasn't planning to."

Freeza smiles again, but his eyes remain on Goshin.

## The Search

Beneath the rock shelf, Zarbon touches his scouter.

"Report."

The last message from his soldier had named a Namekian and a child. Zarbon had heard two low readings, then a startled sound and static. No location or destination followed. The device has not answered him since.

The visitors' white ship remains closed on its landing feet. Zarbon looks from its hatch toward the ridge. Far beyond it, a low rumble reaches the inlet.

He directs the scouter inland. The lens clicks, searching through distant signals. Two readings rise clear of the others.

One is at four hundred and eighty thousand. The other settles for an instant at one million, one hundred thousand.

Zarbon reads it twice. He has no clear view of the fighters and cannot tell which reading belongs to whom. His hand goes to the transmitter.

"Lord Freeza?"

Static answers. Zarbon waits, then looks at the ship again. Freeza ordered him to keep it intact, and the hatch has not moved. But a soldier has stopped reporting, and something inland is registering beyond the number Zarbon associates with Freeza's usual form.

"If you're in trouble, my lord, I had better find out why."

He lifts above the shelf and flies toward the village. The ship stays where it is, closed and unattended.

On the way, his scouter finds a small cluster of readings moving beneath the trees. Zarbon changes direction. Through the branches he sees a green elder carrying a broad bundle, a woman with blue hair, a child and another Namekian.

The woman fits the scout's description of the visitors. The elder holds the bundle close while the others move around him.

Zarbon descends between the trees and the hollow.

## The Wall Ends

Gohan feels the unfamiliar ki coming from the inlet. It is moving toward the smaller presences behind the village.

"Someone's near Bulma," he says.

Goshin feels it too. "Go. I'll keep him here."

Freeza turns at once. Gohan accelerates toward the shore, and Freeza reaches after him. Goshin catches Freeza's arm, pulls him back and drives a knee into his side. Freeza's shot breaks harmlessly across the water.

"You'll have to get past me," Goshin says.

Gohan does not look back again. He crosses the rise and follows the new presence toward the trees.

Below him, Muri has stopped beside the low wall. The older villager stands in front of Bulma and Cargo, while Zarbon faces them from the path beyond.

"Put the bundle down," Zarbon says.

"No," Muri answers.

Zarbon's gaze moves over the cloth. "My lord is looking for something on this world. I think he would appreciate a closer look at whatever you're carrying."

Bulma pulls Cargo behind her. "You can ask him after he's finished getting hit."

"Bulma!" Gohan calls.

Zarbon looks up. Gohan lands between him and the wall, his boots scattering loose earth. Behind him, Muri shifts the ball against his chest.

"Are you hurt?" Gohan asks.

"We're all here," Bulma says. "This one just arrived."

Zarbon's scouter clicks as he looks at Gohan. The figures rise and stop.

"Forty-one thousand, two hundred." He studies the boy's tail. "One of the children from the ship."

"You found our ship?"

"I did. It is still intact, if that concerns you."

Gohan takes in the direction Zarbon came from. He wants to ask whether anyone else is there, but Zarbon is already moving.

Zarbon's hand reaches past him for Muri. Gohan catches his wrist. Zarbon pulls, expecting the child to come with it; instead Gohan turns and sends him skidding across the path.

Zarbon stops against a tree trunk. Leaves shake around him.

"Go to the hollow," Gohan tells the others. "I'll follow."

The older villager takes Cargo's hand. Muri starts after them, but looks back toward the houses.

"We haven't found everyone."

"Then we'll come back for them," Gohan says. "First get out of his way."

Zarbon flicks his fingers. Two bright shots streak past Gohan toward the wall. Gohan spreads both hands and knocks them upward. The bursts vanish above the trees; he keeps his eyes below their branches.

By the time he looks back, Zarbon has retreated behind a stone outcrop. A harsh light spills around its edge.

"You should have stayed with the others," Zarbon says.

His voice is deeper. The ground trembles, and he steps out again with a broader body and a heavy, changed face. His armour stretches over the new shape. He looks down at his hands with unmistakable distaste.

"I dislike doing that."

Gohan feels the increase in his ki. "Then you shouldn't have come after them."

Zarbon charges. Gohan meets his first punch, but the force pushes his guard toward his chest. He slips aside from the second and strikes Zarbon's shoulder. The larger fighter seizes his sleeve and hurls him toward the wall.

Gohan turns in the air. His feet touch the stones for an instant, and he springs away before they break. Zarbon follows, driving him toward the narrow space where Muri and the others are retreating.

Gohan lifts above him instead. "Over here!"

He fires at the empty ground beyond the path. Zarbon turns toward the flash, and Gohan drops onto his back, pulling him away from the wall. They hit the open earth together. Zarbon rolls, catches Gohan by the arm and throws him off.

Cargo cries out. Zarbon looks toward the sound and raises a hand.

Muri plants himself beside the wall and fires a thin bolt at Zarbon's face. Zarbon throws an arm up to cover his eyes. The bolt strikes his armour without stopping him, but Gohan reaches him before he can aim again.

Gohan knocks Zarbon's hand toward the ground. A blast tears a strip out of the path.

"Keep going!" Gohan calls.

"Come on!" Bulma pulls Cargo around the end of the wall. Muri follows with the covered ball, and the older villager takes the rear. They disappear into the hollow, where the rock slopes up between them and the fight.

Zarbon starts after them. Gohan catches him across the chest with both palms and sends him back three steps.

"I said leave them alone!"

His anger rises with his ki. This time he gathers the rush as he did against Dodoria, but more of it comes to his hands. Zarbon swings at him; Gohan turns inside the blow and strikes his wrist. The scouter slips from Zarbon's face, hits a stone and breaks.

Zarbon stares at the pieces. "You little—"

He lunges. Gohan ducks beneath his arm and drives a fist into the front of his armour. Zarbon's breath leaves him. Gohan follows with a blow to the side of his head, then catches him before he can fall against the wall.

Zarbon struggles once. Gohan takes him farther into the open and sets him down firmly. The changed fighter lies still, breathing.

Gohan stands over him for a moment. The strength he has drawn out settles, but it does not all leave him. His body and ki feel stronger than they did when he came back from Dodoria, and the steadier power is still there when he lowers his hands.

He looks at the broken scouter, then toward the hollow. Zarbon is unconscious, not secured. Dodoria and the soldiers are still somewhere near the houses, and Muri was right: there are people they have not found.

Gohan goes to the hollow.

"He's down," he tells Muri. "But we shouldn't wait for him to wake up."

Muri sets the ball against the rock. "The others need warning."

"I'll go with you."

Bulma kneels beside Cargo. "Can you stay here with him? I'll come back with the others."

Cargo looks toward the village, then nods. The older villager stays beside him. Muri takes the ball again, and Gohan leads him and Bulma back along the wall, watching the place where Zarbon fell.

## The High House

The distant ki changes while Piccolo is carrying Dende over the next ridge.

He stops in midair. Behind them, Goshin's familiar presence burns fiercely beside Freeza's. Gohan has moved away from the lake, toward the village.

Dende grips Piccolo's shoulder. "Is it them?"

"Yes."

Piccolo turns halfway back. He could reach the boys sooner than he could return with an answer, but Freeza's strength is far beyond what he felt in the village. Dende came to warn the Great Elder, and Muri's people need whatever help the elder can offer.

"How much farther?"

Dende points. "That rock, beyond the trees. The house is on top."

"Hold on."

Piccolo flies faster. The narrow pillar rises ahead of them, pale against the land. A round house sits on its summit. Piccolo lands at the door and sets Dende on his feet.

The door opens before they knock.

A tall Namekian stands inside, his arms folded. Piccolo feels the warrior's ki and squares his shoulders. The man's face and antennae are much like his own, though his clothes are different.

"Nail!" Dende says. "We came to warn the Great Elder."

Nail looks from the child to Piccolo. "You brought a stranger."

"He helped us. The others did too. Freeza and his men came to our village."

"Is Elder Muri alive?"

"Yes. Cargo is with him. Piccolo carried me here because we needed to come quickly."

Nail steps aside, but keeps his eyes on Piccolo. "The Great Elder knows that our people are in danger. He will hear what you have to tell him."

They climb into the upper room. The Great Elder sits in a large chair beneath a starred orange sphere. His body is immense, his breathing slow. Dende goes to him at once and bows.

"Great Elder, the strangers are attacking our villages. They want the Dragon Balls. Elder Muri told me to ask you to warn the others if you can."

"You have done well to come, Dende." The old Namekian's voice is quiet. "I have felt many of my children disappear."

Dende lowers his head. "Some of us are still alive."

"I know. I can feel them too."

The elder turns toward Piccolo. "And you are not one of the children who grew up here."

"No. I came from Earth."

"Earth."

Piccolo glances at the sphere above the chair. "We have Dragon Balls there. A Namekian made them. His name is Kami."

The Great Elder's eyes open wider. "A child was sent away before the great storms. His father was called Katas. I did not know whether the child had reached another world."

Piccolo thinks of the empty house at Yunzabit and Kami standing beside its door.

"He reached it. He waited for his family."

For a long moment the elder says nothing. Nail lowers his gaze.

"The storms took almost all our people," the elder says. "Katas was among them. I cannot tell you whether he knew his son had arrived safely."

Piccolo folds his arms. He had imagined an answer during the journey, but not the small room at Yunzabit growing quiet around it. Kami has waited a long time to hear this.

"There's something else," he says. "Kami and I are connected. If one of us dies, the other dies too."

The elder studies him. "May I see what you know? It would help me understand."

Piccolo hesitates. He has never met this man before. Yet Kami asked him to find the people who remembered the child, and the old Namekian is asking rather than reaching.

"All right."

He steps close. The elder rests one hand on his head. Piccolo feels a light pressure and holds still while memories rise: Kami's face at the old ship, the two of them speaking across the tournament ring, the knowledge that their lives are joined.

The hand lifts.

"The child of Katas divided himself," the elder says. "You came after the part he cast away, yet your life and Kami's are joined."

"My father was the one he cast away. I know what he did on Earth."

"And you?"

Piccolo glances toward the door. "I came here with the people he would have fought."

The elder's gaze follows his. "Then I am glad they brought you."

Piccolo looks at Dende, who is listening with both hands clasped in front of him.

"Can you tell Kami?" the child asks.

"When I get back."

Dende looks up at the elder. "He won't have to wait to know anymore."

"No," the elder says. "He has waited long enough."

A great pressure rolls across the land outside. The window trembles. Piccolo turns toward it, his expression sharpening.

"Freeza's still fighting."

Nail steps to the doorway. "Then I will go."

"Wait," the Great Elder says. "If he comes here, someone must remain to protect this house."

Nail stops. "My place is wherever you need me."

"For now, that is here."

Piccolo feels the strength in Nail and then the distant pressure again. Nail is powerful, but Piccolo cannot mistake the difference between that and Freeza.

"The boys are keeping him away from Muri's people," he says. "If that fight reaches the village, the people behind them need somewhere to go."

The elder studies Piccolo. "There is power in you that you have not yet brought forth. I can help awaken it, if you wish."

"Your power?"

"Your own. I would only help you reach it."

Piccolo looks toward the window. Gohan and Goshin had flown away from the houses because he asked them to protect the others. He can still feel them both.

"Do it."

The elder places his hand on Piccolo's head again. Warmth spreads through Piccolo's shoulders and down his arms. His ki rises, drawing on something deeper than the strength he had shown the soldier among the trees. The room shudders; Nail braces a hand against the wall.

Piccolo takes a sharp breath. When the elder removes his hand, the greater strength does not vanish. Piccolo closes his fists, feeling how readily it answers him.

"Thank you."

"Use it well," the elder says. "And understand that my life is nearly over. When I die, the Dragon Balls I made will disappear."

Piccolo looks up at the sphere, then back at him. "Then we'll keep Freeza away from you."

Dende moves to his side. "I want to help Cargo and Elder Muri."

"I know the way back," Piccolo says. "Do you want to come with me?"

Dende nods. Nail opens the door for them, but another tremor passes through the house before they can leave.

Piccolo feels Freeza's ki swell far beyond the pressure that reached them a moment ago.

## The Other Shape

Freeza's fist strikes Goshin's crossed arms. Goshin holds the blow, turns it aside and punches him in the chest. Freeza is driven across the lake again, carving a long trough in the water beneath him.

"Your brother has abandoned you," Freeza says.

"He went to help the village."

"And you think you can protect him from here?"

Freeza lifts both hands. A fan of narrow blasts spreads toward the shore behind Goshin. Goshin throws himself across their paths, catching the nearest with his ki and striking the others upward. The last shot bends around him toward the rise.

He reaches for it without taking his eyes off Freeza. The light turns sharply and bursts above an unoccupied stretch of water.

By then Freeza has pulled away. He lands on the broken island and gathers power around himself. The air tightens; the island cracks under his feet.

Goshin flies after him. A bright shell forms around Freeza, and Goshin's fist drives into it. The shell buckles. He strikes again, and it breaks in a burst of spray and stone.

Freeza comes through the opening with one hand raised. Goshin knocks it aside and lands a blow across his jaw. Freeza's head snaps back, but the pressure around him continues to grow. His armour splits at the shoulder. His body expands beneath the broken plates, and his horns stretch upward and curve.

Goshin hits him again while the change is happening. Freeza catches the next blow on a much larger forearm and shoves him away.

The boy stops above the water. Freeza is taller now, his limbs heavy and his face altered beneath the long horns. Pieces of armour fall around him into the lake.

"You made me do that," he says. His voice has lost some of its smoothness.

"You could have left."

"And let a Saiyan child strike me?"

Freeza lifts a hand. The water beneath him erupts. The force of it reaches Goshin and pushes him back across the surface; he digs his feet into the air and stops before the shore.

"My battle power is over one million now," Freeza says. "Would you like to see what that means?"

Goshin looks at him, not at the sky. Freeza's ki has risen sharply. It bears down harder than it did a moment ago, and the larger fighter is already moving.

Goshin brings his guard up. Freeza's punch drives him backward; the next reaches him before he has fully stopped. Goshin twists, catches it on one arm and sends his own fist into Freeza's ribs. Freeza takes the hit, but his tail lashes across Goshin's legs and throws him down toward the water.

Goshin stops himself and rises under Freeza's reach. His shoulder catches the larger fighter beneath the arm. Freeza turns with him, grabs for his tail and closes on empty air as Goshin twists away.

"You're quicker than I expected," Freeza says.

"You keep saying things like that."

Goshin punches again. Freeza blocks, and their hands remain pressed together above the lake. The water hollows beneath them.

Goshin can feel the difference. His tenfold rush gave him the advantage before Freeza changed; now Freeza is forcing his hands slowly apart. The larger fingers dig against his palms. Beyond Freeza, the shore rises toward the village.

Goshin remembers Goku's hands at the quarry and the first time the red light answered him. His father had laughed with him afterward. Freeza has laughed about children losing their homes.

"I won't let you do it again!"

The words tear out of Goshin. He draws breath, holds the strength already gathered in his body and calls for twice the tenfold surge he is using now. His whole body tightens around the rising force. The water sinks beneath him; spray rushes up around Freeza's legs.

"KAIŌ-KEN... TIMES TWENTY!"

The red aura erupts. Goshin roars, the anger in his chest breaking free with the rising ki. Freeza's hands stop moving. Goshin feels the greater rush hammer through his limbs; his body answers, stronger, and he pulls his arms together through Freeza's grip.

With both palms he drives the larger fighter away.

Freeza shoots backward and crashes into the far edge of the island. Stone breaks beneath him. He comes up at once, but Goshin is already there. A punch catches him under the jaw, and a second sends him across the water before he can bring his arm around.

Goshin follows, controlling the greater rush as he moves. Beneath it, his own body and ki are growing stronger again. He can feel that strength holding when he shifts direction; it is his, not just the red light around him.

Freeza stops above the lake and glares at him. One horn is chipped. He touches the broken edge and stares at the fragment on his fingers.

"You insolent little—"

Goshin flies at him again. Freeza twists aside, catches the boy's next punch on his forearm and is driven backward despite it. His gaze flicks toward the village.

"You cannot be in two places."

## At Home

Tien stands beside Goku at the quarry. Across the tracks, Chiaotzu keeps watch over Nappa. Vegeta is awake beneath the overhang, his tail moving slowly against the stone.

"If I leave, can the two of you hold him?" Goku asks.

Tien does not answer at once. "We can watch him. After that attempt to get away, I can't promise we can stop him if he gets more of his strength back, not without putting everyone nearby at risk."

"I could bring him back down," Goku says. "But I'd have to know he was moving."

"And if you're on another planet?"

Goku looks at Vegeta. The prince has heard every word.

"You have enough trouble without me," Vegeta says. "Go to Freeza."

"You'd like that, wouldn't you?"

"I'd like to see which of you survives."

Goku steps closer. "My sons are there. So is Piccolo. If I can find a way to reach them, I will. But I'm not leaving you free to hurt anyone here."

Vegeta's smile thins. He turns his face toward the open end of the quarry.

"I'll ask Kami and Popo if they can help with a watch," Goku tells Tien. "I won't go until we have an arrangement we can trust."

Tien nods. "I'll stay with you while Chiaotzu watches Nappa. We can change places if we need to."

At Capsule Corporation, Krillin sets Chi-Chi down outside the laboratory. Yamcha opens the door before they reach it. A portable scanner, cables and two heavy cases lie on a workbench behind him.

"Dr. Brief is getting the last of his equipment," Yamcha says. "The officers gave him directions to the other pod."

"Has he seen it yet?" Chi-Chi asks.

"Not yet." Yamcha lowers his voice. "Did King Kai hear anything more about Bulma?"

"Piccolo said she was with two children while they were moving people away. He had to stop talking before King Kai could ask whether anyone was hurt."

Yamcha grips the handle of one case. "She'd better call as soon as she can."

"Puar is waiting at our house for her."

"Good."

Dr. Brief comes around the bench with a small instrument in his hand. "I don't want anyone opening or powering it before we check for damage. The first pod's explosion may have affected it."

"How soon can we go?" Krillin asks.

"As soon as these are loaded. Yamcha, would you take the larger case?"

"Got it."

Chi-Chi picks up the cables. "Tell me where you want these."

Dr. Brief points her toward the waiting vehicle. She carries them out herself, then returns for the scanner. She cannot make the pod safe by wanting it, but she can help them reach it sooner.

Krillin stops beside her. "Puar knows to call here if Bulma gets through."

"I know."

She thinks of the message King Kai passed on: Piccolo had seen both boys, and Freeza had attacked them. It was already old when she heard it. She puts the scanner carefully into the vehicle and goes back for the remaining case.

"Let's find out what we have," she says.

## The Line He Chooses

A bright sphere forms above Freeza's palm. Goshin moves at once, but Freeza sends it past him, low over the water toward the rise and the village beyond.

Goshin shoots after it. He catches the sphere with both hands before it reaches the shore. Its pressure drives him backward, furrowing the water below. Freeza is still behind him; Goshin can feel him, but the people are ahead.

"NO! GOHAN!"

Goshin roars and forces his hands beneath the sphere. His arms tremble as its pressure drives him back. Gohan is somewhere beyond that rise, helping people who cannot outrun this.

He turns the ball upward. The light climbs steeply away from the land. Goshin keeps his eyes on the water while he guides it, then releases it high overhead. A distant flash brightens the lake without drawing his gaze to the sky.

Freeza has used the opening. His ki plunges beneath the surface, and the water closes over him.

Goshin follows to the edge of the disturbed water. The red aura stays close around him. He can feel Gohan near the village, and farther away the smaller presences moving together.

"I'm not going to let you go after them."

Near the low wall, Gohan hears the high report and looks toward the lake without lifting his eyes above the ridge. The ground is still shaking. Muri stands beside him with the ball, while Bulma waves two more villagers out of a house.

"This way!" she calls. "Stay close to the wall!"

One villager helps the other over a fallen beam. Gohan carries the beam aside and makes room for them. He can sense Zarbon where he left him, but the fighter has not risen. Dodoria and the other soldiers remain a danger; the people coming from the houses need to know where to go.

"The hollow is behind those trees," Muri tells them. "Cargo and another of our people are waiting there."

Gohan feels Goshin's ki at the lake and pauses. Then he looks at the two villagers, at Muri and Bulma, and toward the remaining houses.

"I'll help you get everyone I can find," he says. "Then I'll go back to him."

At the water, Goshin draws a breath and dives. The surface closes above his red light. Below, Freeza's presence moves through the dark water, away from the shore.

Goshin goes after him.
