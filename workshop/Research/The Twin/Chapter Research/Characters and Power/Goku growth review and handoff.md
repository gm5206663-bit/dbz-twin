# Chapter 13 — Goku's growth: review and continuity handoff

## Status

This is a **separate, unaccepted review candidate** of the previously delivered Chapter 13, continuing the provisional Chapter 12. Its starting text was copied from that candidate. It does not authorize replacement of Chapters 1–5, any other candidate, the protected project or remote copies. The exact story edits are in `diffs/Chapter_13_Before_And_After.patch`.

## What changed

- During the match, Goku anticipates Goshin's use of a loose stone, draws it toward one fist and comes in under it. Goshin counters immediately. The exchange illustrates Goku's tactical ingenuity without taking away Goshin's established ki use or ability to respond.
- Goshin still wins the ring-out. Goku has not deliberately thrown the match. Afterward, he asks Goshin to help him repeat the wrist turn, changes his step, and wants to try again when Goshin responds. He enjoys the challenge and acts on what he has learned.
- Goshin asks how his father became strong. Goku mentions Grandpa Gohan, Master Roshi, Krillin, the loss to Tao and the pursuit of Korin's ordinary first water. Goshin recognizes that Goku's persistence made him stronger and that he intends to keep growing; he chooses to continue training himself. The dialogue is authored, while the underlying prior experiences are supported in `00_CANON_RESEARCH_AND_REVISION_PLAN.md`.
- Goshin's greater, exceptionally broad innate Talent and potential remain the story's premise. The scene does not assert that Goku lacks innate aptitude, received no help, can no longer grow, or must forever be weaker than Goshin. Nor does it deny Goshin's own work or give him a new limitation. The current result is a sparring result, not a lifetime ranking.

## Unchanged coverage and ending

Outside the two targeted spar passages, the story text remains identical to the previous Chapter 13 candidate. The earlier episode-12 source order and coverage reviews are preserved in `reference/` as historical records. Their underlying coverage still applies: independent Tien/Chiaotzu and Launch training and recruitment; Bulma and Krillin's search; Roshi and Turtle's concern; Piccolo's separate accident and quake; Gohan's own match and escape; and the causally adapted living-Goku thread. Gohan's turn is not resolved by Goshin or written out of the chapter.

Goshin still uses flight, ki and the already-retained Kamehameha; his scrape visibly heals. His first Great Ape remains in the future and no new transformation or fixed power reading appears. He continues watching his food, repeating applicable instructions, taking the downhill side, waiting for Goku's invitation when appropriate and putting his hands behind his back for Chi-Chi's check. Both boys' tails remain. The whole-Senzu fullness and the remaining bean are unchanged.

The family ends at Capsule Corporation. C-6 remains awake with a bent arm and unresolved leak; he permits external listening, retains control of any panel opening and has not been silently repaired. The Four-Star Ball stays on Gohan's hat and the Three- and Six-Star Balls remain at home. Yamcha and Puar are at Korin Tower; Bulma, Krillin, Tien, Chiaotzu and Launch are at the waterfall; the scouter and cell remain separate. Piccolo returns toward the wasteland, the Saiyans are en route, Earth has not learned of Arlia's destruction, and King Kai's answer is pending.

## Source and verification limits

Later Dragon Ball material is retrospective context, not an event or technique available to this chapter's characters. The first, ordinary Super Holy Water is distinct from the later potential-releasing Super Water of the Gods.

No complete episode-12 video was watched for the original or this revision. The full local captions, available stills and summaries have the provenance and silent-action limits described in the historical source plan.
