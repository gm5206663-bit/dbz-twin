A next-episode preview, recap or unrelated trailing material is not automatically an event that occurs in the episode's main narrative. Speaker tags and narrator statements are still context-specific.

### B. Toei Animation official original-Z episode pages — brief synopses

The existing local corpus, `Research/The Twin/Original Episode and Franchise Research/sources/toei_franchise_official_synopses.jsonl`, contains 604 Toei synopsis records across several series, including Z 36–291. The records for **every Z 37–107** were reviewed for this collection. Each official Z 36–108 page is linked directly in the [chronicle](01_EPISODE_CHRONICLE.md); the [inventory](08_EPISODE_SOURCE_INVENTORY.md) provides all 71 Japanese titles and episode links.

A synopsis is an official **short description**, not a complete episode script, an authenticated recording or proof that every depicted moment was checked. Website text can be incomplete or corrupted. The live official pages for Z 106, 107 and 117 were fetched on 7 November to check the defects described below.

In addition, one published Toei episode-page still for each Z 35–107 was inspected: 73 individual images, including all 71 in the 37–107 block. In the subsequent understanding pass, the stills specifically cited in the visual guide were rechecked and the Z 42 and Z 60 descriptions corrected; the actual Z 38 crop was also clarified. These support only what is visible in the selected frames, not continuous action, sequence, audio, a complete episode or a verified broadcast cut. ## 3. Original TV, manga, Kai, films and later continuity

- The original-Z source includes the mirrored refugee ship, the deceptive apparent Namek, Bulma's frog-body exchange and the King Kai planet Ginyu confrontation. These are part of the chosen **television** research baseline, regardless of whether another edition follows them.
- Manga-based official articles are separately identified above. A full panel-by-panel original manga comparison has not been conducted in this task. An article's source label does not guarantee that every sentence is error-free.
- Kai is a separately edited/adapted version. A full shot-by-shot Kai comparison was not performed. Do not state that every anime-original scene was removed or that all Kai episodes/dubs use one uniform numbering and music track.
- The original-Z Bardock television special and the related flashbacks provide another versioned backstory; later *Super*, *Daima*, manga and films may present different material. The special, films and later works were not completely screened for this task. A filmmaker's feature or a game move name is not automatically a television-episode fact.
- Z 108 starts Garlic Jr.'s subsequent story. Z 119–123 include the source's later Freeza return and Goku's Yardrat account; their short Toei synopses were consulted as **later consequences**, not promoted to complete audiovisual research or current branch facts. The exact integration of movies into *The Twin* remains an author-facing continuity decision.
- No attempt was made to settle every release's dubbing, soundtrack, cropping, edit, subtitle, broadcast order or merchandise terminology. The Japanese Z number and direct page link are the safest navigation keys used here.

## 4. What still requires work before any claim of literal completeness

1. Obtain an identified, legitimately accessible original-Z video/audio release; watch and listen to all relevant episodes and record release, language, edit and timecodes. Verify especially silent trap mechanics, planet and creature appearance, scouter targets, assimilated voices, the specific five-minute sequence and unattributed cues.
2. Authenticate the SRT source and compare its timing and text with that release. Review questionable recap/preview/ending and appended material boundaries. Do not fix its text based on an unrelated dub.
3. Read the relevant manga chapters in an identified edition; compare the TV-only refugee, false-planet, Bulma/Ginyu and afterlife threads without assuming a short retrospective covers every panel. Compare Kai scene by scene only if needed, recording its particular cut and language.
4. Check the 1990 Bardock special, relevant films and any chosen later continuity directly before integrating them. Record conflicts and obtain a version decision where the project's rules do not settle one.
5. Verify a complete inventory of minor soldiers, villagers, vehicles, wildlife, production credits, music cues, regional names and star markings if those details become important. This collection deliberately avoids filling missing names and visuals from memory.
6. Recheck the actual manuscript and latest author decisions before using these references to write. The future is not determined by the source and mechanical checks do not certify literary quality or acceptance.

## 5. Accessing the underlying cues

The [query tool](tools/query_namek_captions.py) reads the already-present ZIP directly and prints matching episode numbers, local times and caption text; it does not create a second transcript or archive. Example:

```text
python3 -B 'Research/The Twin/Namek Arc/tools/query_namek_captions.py' --episodes 63-65 --query 戦闘力 --context 1 --limit 15
```

Use surrounding cues, the prior episode review and a correctly identified audiovisual edition before treating a hit as a scene fact. Search cannot prove that a word, visual action or idea is absent elsewhere.
