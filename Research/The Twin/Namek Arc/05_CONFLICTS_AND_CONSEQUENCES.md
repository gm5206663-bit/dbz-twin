# Conflict, strategy and consequences reference

This sheet reorganizes the [episode chronicle](01_EPISODE_CHRONICLE.md) around encounters. **Original Z only unless explicitly marked branch.** “Wins,” “dies,” and “escapes” describe these source events, not obligations on the author. Captioned dialogue does not establish every silent movement; Toei synopses and later official profiles are separate, often shorter witnesses.

## 1. The contest before the major battles

| Encounter / situation | What each side wants | Decisive source sequence | Immediate consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Earth recovery and expedition | Friends want the dead back; Bulma and Popo need a working route. | Kami's old ship is located and modified; the original crew sets out. | Popo remains at the Lookout; the party and home are separated. |
| Mirrored ship, Z 39–40 | Frightened refugee children want to stop supposed enemies; travellers want to get through. | Traps and capture precede a meteor emergency; the visitors help children and ship. | The children explain their history, trust changes and route information is offered. This is not free trust at first sight. |
| False Namek, Z 41–44 | The strangers want the visitors' spaceship; the visitors think they are finding Namek's Dragon Balls. | An apparent series of rescues and ball finds is exposed as deception. | The travellers regain their vehicle and reach the actual planet; fake finds do not count. |
| Vegeta vs Cui, Z 45 | Vegeta wants the balls and independence; Cui expects to eliminate a rival. | Vegeta reveals increased output and kills Cui. | A Freeza subordinate is removed, but the greater organization remains. |
| Ship attack, Z 45 | Freeza's scouts investigate outsiders; Bulma's group needs mobility and secrecy. | The original Earth ship is destroyed. | The party is stranded unless another vehicle or wish becomes available. |
| Mūri's village, Z 47–48 | Freeza wants a ball and information; the Namekians want to protect their community. | Warriors resist, the elder destroys scouters, and Dodoria kills villagers. Gohan breaks cover and Krillin helps rescue Dende. | One child reaches safety, but the raiders and Vegeta learn that opponents are present. |
| Escape from Dodoria, Z 48–49 | Gohan and Krillin want Dende safe; Dodoria wants them stopped. | Krillin uses Solar Flare and the group evades the chase; Dodoria attacks the area. | Dodoria mistakenly believes he has disposed of them and encounters Vegeta. |
| Vegeta vs Dodoria, Z 49 | Vegeta wants to reduce Freeza's forces; Dodoria wants to survive. | Dodoria discloses Freeza's destruction of Planet Vegeta in a plea; Vegeta kills him. | The prince gains important knowledge from this source event. It is not automatically shared with Goku or Earth. |
| Vegeta's village raid, Z 50–51 | Vegeta wants another ball and no witnesses. | He kills the village and hides the ball in water. | Gohan can later find it, and the village's killer matters to the later wish. |

| The Elder and Krillin, Z 53–54 | Krillin seeks help and the means to restore friends; the Elder protects Namek and evaluates the visitor. | The Elder gives Krillin his ball and draws out dormant strength. | Trust creates a new possible ally and stronger fighter; it does not solve the entire invasion. |

| Bulma's ball and soldiers, Z 59–60 | Bulma wants to keep the ball and escape; the soldiers become interested in using the balls themselves. | She searches underwater, faces local danger, and misdirects Blueberry and Raspberry. | A vulnerable non-fighter's independent action affects the search; danger is not only a contest of ki numbers. |

## 2. Ginyu Force: coordinated powers and disrupted plans

| Encounter | Source sequence and tactical insight | Outcome and limits |
|---|---|---|
| Race for the last wish, Z 61–62 | Gohan's potential is released. Vegeta, Gohan and Krillin form an uneasy alliance, with an immortality wish considered a desperate answer to the approaching squad. | The force arrives and seizes seven balls before they can act. Cooperation is contingent on a greater threat, not evidence that past violence has been forgiven. |
| Guldo vs Gohan and Krillin, Z 62–63 | The two pressure the physically slower member, but his brief time stoppage and separate immobilization turn the fight. | Vegeta attacks and kills Guldo. An unusual ability creates danger without establishing that every opponent must be helpless. |
| Recoome vs Vegeta, Krillin and Gohan, Z 64–65 | Vegeta's attack fails to finish the far stronger opponent; the other two intervene, and Gohan fights despite fear after Krillin falls. | All three are critically endangered. Goku arrives; their agency does not require them to win. |
| Goku vs Recoome, Z 66 | Goku uses the remaining Senzu on the three and strikes the squad member. | Recoome is disabled; Goku later wants him spared, while Vegeta kills him. |
| Goku vs Jeice and Burter, Z 67–68 | The pair combine their speed and attacks. Goku controls his output and avoids their attempts. | Burter is incapacitated, Jeice leaves, and Vegeta kills Burter. The 5,000 scouter figure is contextual suppression, not Goku's total strength. |
| Goku vs Captain Ginyu, Z 69–71 | Goku reveals a much higher reading after Kaiō-ken. | Nail vs Freeza, Z 71–75 | Nail knows he cannot defeat Freeza but delays him while Dende goes to the others. His regeneration and continued resistance buy time. | Nail is left dying, yet his delay allows the summoning plan to proceed. His goal was time, not a fair contest for victory. |

| Final exchange, Z 105–106 | Freeza attacks after being helped; Goku retaliates and searches for an escape. | Namek explodes. King Kai concludes both fighters have died; episode 107 corrects the Goku conclusion, and episode 119 later reveals Freeza's survival. |

## 4. Concurrent, easily missed threads

1. **Earth's rescue efforts:** Chi-Chi's concern leads to action; Brief's additional craft and the failed launch have their own sequence. The original source does not reduce her to homework, and the branch already gives her a different household and permission history.
2. **King Kai's planet:** Yamcha, Tien and Chiaotzu train while Piccolo is temporarily there; the original TV later stages their confrontation with the deceased Ginyu Force. Training partners, bodies, deaths and the later result differ from the living *Twin* cast.
3. **Bulma and the frog:** Bulma's compassion and engineering enable Ginyu's second body swap. Gohan helps thwart Ginyu's later attempt and Bulma returns to her body. It is an anime-TV thread, not a required manga/Kai event.
4. **Namek's victims:** the attack on Mūri's village and Vegeta's separate village killing have different perpetrators, which matters when the Earth wish is phrased. The survival of the Elder, the villagers, Dende and the dragon must each be tracked independently.
5. **Guardians and linked lives:** source Piccolo dies and returns; the linked Kami and Earth balls respond. Later Gohan removes the **living** but badly hurt Piccolo, keeping that connection meaningful.
6. **Knowledge and message routes:** Dodoria tells Vegeta; Goku hears from Vegeta much later; King Kai can convey specific messages; Dende translates; devices can be damaged or disabled. A fact known to the reader need not be known to a character.
7. **Comedy and play:** Gohan likes the battle clothes, the Ginyu Force poses and plays to choose matchups, Bulma's practical life continues amid peril, and Goku enjoys challenge. Humour and children being children can coexist with loss without canceling consequences.
8. **The false-ending problem:** Freeza's survival after the Genki-dama, Goku's apparent death after Namek's destruction, and Freeza's later survival are three different cases. Label who believes what and when.
