# THE TWIN — timeline and scene order

**Owns: chronology and relative scene order for the selected review branch.**

**Scope:** Chapters 1–19 through the current unaccepted review revision, plus the later unaccepted Chapter 20 draft on hold. This is a reading aid, not acceptance. Events absent from a chapter remain unknown. The selected guidebook date of 12 October, Age 761 for Raditz's arrival is contested; neither is a verified on-page date. The later Chapter 20 draft shows three weeks of Goku's Bubbles training, not an exact number of days since Raditz or a Saiyan arrival countdown.

**Checker-repair revision, 3 November:** The author requested correction of the seven reported checker findings. Chapters 6–9 have targeted scene extensions and sentence revisions; their established sequence and outcomes below remain. Chapters 10–14 add source footers only, and Chapter 15 adds a witness line. The [repair report](../canon/CHECKER_REPAIR_2026-11-03.md) and verified prior-version archive document the change; none of it is reader acceptance.

## Chapters 16–17: time, place and travel locks

1. **Chapter 16, before the island:** the family is at the Lookout; Chi-Chi, Gohan and Goshin travel by borrowed aircar to Capsule Corporation. C-6 receives Mina's book with an agreement to return it when finished. Bulma needs to inspect an island wind gauge; Chi-Chi agrees to go with her and the boys before visiting Ox-King.
2. **Island and storm:** Bulma works at the station. The boys' sand craft becomes a small raft; Gohan calls Nimbus and cannot swim. They try to help a tiger caught in a net. Goshin lifts Gohan out of the water; Chi-Chi and Bulma then arrive and help free the tiger. The raft drifts away. Piccolo senses the boys, travels to the island and speaks with the family. He does not take or touch them in this encounter.
3. **Chapter 16 evening:** the aircar takes Chi-Chi, the boys and Bulma to the mountain house, where Ox-King prepares another pot while Goshin watches. Gohan draws the raft. Goku continues on Snake Way.
4. **Chapter 17, following visit:** rainwater remains on the Lookout; Chi-Chi tells Kami about Piccolo. The family plays/trains with Krillin. Looking for their raft on the way to Bulma's, they find it near the old town and meet Rom, Chiko, Pigero, Ena, Jink, Hatch and Yordon. The boards can help mend the children's roof.
5. **First visit, later:** workers try to take Ena; the family intervenes. At the market Chi-Chi pays for the fruit taken by the children. She and the children prepare supper. She offers the whole group a place at her home; Pigero chooses to remain at the old house for that night. The family and Bulma leave the children there and visit C-6 at the lab; they do **not** take the seven children to the lab.
6. **Next morning, explicitly stated:** Chi-Chi collects Bulma and returns with the twins. Workers and police arrive. In the corrected review candidate Goshin removes Pigero's cuffs as requested; the officer receives the bent cuffs. Rom, Chiko, Pigero and Bulma visit the home and return to report what they saw.
7. **Chapter 17 ending:** the six younger children decide to try staying together in the common room for one night. Pigero can stay for supper; later placement is undecided. Chi-Chi promises to return after training. After taking Bulma home, Chi-Chi brings the twins to the mountain house. Gohan's hat, raft drawing and Hatch's school-boat drawing are there. Goku is still on Snake Way.

## Chapter 18: sequence and new handoff

1. **The following morning:** Ox-King and Chi-Chi prepare breakfast in Goshin’s view. Chi-Chi obtains permission to keep the borrowed aircar until after the children's visit and later return it. Gohan draws a larger school-boat picture while keeping Hatch's original for C-6.
2. **Training and the room:** at the Lookout the fighters, family, Kami and Popo train. Gohan practises noticing Krillin; Goshin directs four stones. Popo offers a voluntary mind-travel exercise: Tien, Chiaotzu, Krillin, Yamcha, Chi-Chi and both boys enter; Puar and Yajirobe stay out. Popo says he can bring the group back upon request. Their seven bodies stay at the Lookout.
3. **The experience:** in an apparent ruined street they encounter two unidentified fighters. Goshin protects Chiaotzu and directs four large pieces of wall while firing his existing Kamehameha. Gohan, Chi-Chi and all four fighters contribute. With both opponents down or attempting to rise, the group requests return; no opponent's later condition, physical location or certain identity is established. This is a mental exercise, not a physical measurement or proof of an actual death.
4. **The promised visit:** Pigero says he slept at the old house after supper and returned. The six younger children had their shared night and are offered the common room for a second night. Pigero may visit the following afternoon; later room and placement decisions remain open. The family leaves the bigger boat drawing with the children.
5. **C-6 and home:** Gohan shows C-6 Hatch's original drawing. Mina's bridge book remains on loan and its onward lending still requires asking her. Dr. Brief's car is returned; Bulma takes Chi-Chi and the boys home. Gohan keeps Hatch's original and a newly begun drawing. C-6's click remains; Goku has not reached King Kai.

## Chapter 19: sequence and new handoff

1. **Night and following breakfast:** Gohan dreams of Raditz and asks Goshin to teach him; Chi-Chi prepares breakfast while Goshin watches. Ox-King is present and plans to mend the gate; Chi-Chi accompanies the boys to a clearing beyond the ridge.
2. **Training:** Goshin demonstrates footwork, defence, stone control and his retained Kamehameha. Gohan's shoe feint makes Goshin look the wrong way; Goshin asks him to show it. Gohan fires his own wave, repeats it and practises gathering and releasing light without shooting.
3. **Machine:** an unidentified round object calls for Kakarot and projects a local image. Gohan recalls Raditz's use of the name and his round ship, but the resemblance does not prove ownership. Following Goku's earlier full-moon warning, Chi-Chi directs the boys to keep their eyes down; Piccolo also warns her to keep them sheltered and tests the projection against bare rock. Goshin holds four falling pieces while his wave diverts a separate slab; Chi-Chi identifies the support, Gohan hits it and the cover blocks the image. No transformation or damage to the natural moon occurs.
4. **Bounded exchange and departure:** with the boys' choice and Chi-Chi's limit, Piccolo tests them in one round. Bulma covers and loads the machine and departs with it, intending to study it; neither safe diagnosis nor lab arrival is shown.
5. **Telephone, home and Goku:** Pigero's permitted visit is confirmed by phone, without a longer placement decision. That evening Ox-King sees the boys display their practice at the gate; the family goes inside. Goku reaches the small planet, encounters Bubbles, and asks a newly arrived blue man whether he is King Kai. The family has no shown communication of Goku's arrival.

## Chapter 20: later draft sequence (on hold)

1. King Kai names himself and Bubbles, accepts Goku's adapted joke and agrees to teach martial arts. He explains approximately tenfold gravity, tests a jump, senses two approaching Saiyans and warns that victory is not guaranteed; he declines a certain arrival date.
2. Goku begins the Bubbles chase, eats, receives King Kai's weighted bands and says he is a Saiyan. The first attempts are physically difficult, and rest and food are shown.
3. On Earth, Bulma reports the covered machine has reached a separate lab room. The boys complete schoolwork, practise and each elect a short Piccolo round under Chi-Chi's conditions. Gohan's sore shoulder receives a cool cloth; Piccolo departs independently.
4. The director offers the shared common room for the week. Bulma takes the family to visit the children the next afternoon and brings them home. They work on the boat picture; the school inquiry and longer arrangement remain unresolved.
5. Training, rest and family practice continue. Three weeks after Goku starts, he catches Bubbles without hurting him. King Kai privately names the Kaiō-ken, which Goku is not shown hearing or learning.
6. In the later home call the director says the six have remained together since the prior call and may use the room another week. Pigero's visiting times are agreed for that week. Another family visit with Bulma is planned for tomorrow, not yet completed.
