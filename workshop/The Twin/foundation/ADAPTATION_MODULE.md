The universal doctrine describes the Talent as innate, non-sentient and operating from conception. Birth and island scouter values are authored snapshots, not a persistent progression table. The **mechanism and scope of each effect** must not be inflated beyond its evidence.

Age limits his life experience and vocabulary; it is not an ordinary-human growth cap or a reason to remove demonstrated Saiyan ability.

## 6. Progression law, not a plot promise

Deliberate training gives priority to mastery of existing abilities and serious foundations. Instinctive emergency copying is permitted. His broad Talent can refine the body, recovery, senses, instincts, ki, skills and inherited qualities and can contribute to new compatible abilities; it is not restricted to copying. The user's specific Saiyan direction includes a deepening bloodline and an evolving Great Ape. As mastery develops, accessing Great Ape power in a normal-sized body and combining it with anger or later Super Saiyan are planned possibilities, not present feats. Do not erase his human inheritance or import Broly's identity, forms or loss of control. Recovery-linked Saiyan growth can be significant, but the Raditz knockout alone does not prove a near-death event or a completed Zenkai. Injury is not required for growth. Show causal development without inventing a fixed multiplier, compulsory struggle, automatic immunity or forced defeat.
