## The author's clarification

Goshin became **very substantially stronger** through his Saiyan growth, innate Adaptation Talent and the other real factors already present. His **first Great Ape transformation is itself a new lived experience for his Talent**, not merely a temporary increase in size or a lesson in control. The whole-body Talent, his own Saiyan nature and the sustained Vegeta fight interact; the result includes a major real increase in both bodily strength and ki, and the new strength remains part of him after he returns to ordinary size (`O214`).

The Talent has been present since conception; the first transformation does not create it or switch it on. It is broad and non-sentient, not a separate voice, system or mere copying ability. Earlier development and a Saiyan's ordinary transformed power are not to be confused with the substantial further gain produced through this lived event. This is not just a release of reserves he already possessed before the experience. Goshin remains conscious and in control in the Great Ape state, with Gohan's independent capability and agency intact.

The user did not supply a new numerical reading, a fixed increase, or the percentage or exact mechanics of transformed power incorporated in his ordinary body. Do not turn that missing measurement into a small or insignificant boost. Do not require a near-death event, Senzu, mandatory delay, fixed ceiling, fear or loss of control to make this directed growth possible. Continued Saiyan and Talent growth, and further evolution of his separate Great Ape form, remain open. The selected scenes do not introduce a separate named ordinary-sized ape mode; they do not prohibit one or impose a waiting period. No early golden or divine form is added.
