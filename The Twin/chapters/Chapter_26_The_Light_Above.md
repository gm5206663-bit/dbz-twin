# Chapter Twenty-Six — The Light Above

*Anime episode reference: Dragon Ball Z, Episodes 27 and 30–32 (selected elements; divergent battle).*

*Review draft; not yet accepted.*

Vegeta twists between the brothers and drives his free elbow toward Gohan. Gohan ducks beneath it. Goshin still has hold of the prince's other arm; he pulls Vegeta around, away from his brother, and strikes him across the chest.

The cracked armour takes the blow. Vegeta is thrown back over the broken tracks, but he catches himself before he reaches the western wall.

**THE LIGHT ABOVE**

---

## Above the Tracks

Gohan comes at Vegeta from below. The Saiyan kicks down, and Gohan turns sideways in the air. The boot passes his shoulder. Gohan catches Vegeta's ankle with both hands and pulls, spoiling the prince's balance for the instant Goshin needs.

Goshin's fist lands against Vegeta's side. The prince gasps and spins away, one arm pressed against his armour.

"Leave everyone alone," Goshin says. "We'll let you take your ship."

Vegeta looks toward the ground. At the western wall, Piccolo and Krillin are crouched beside Nappa. Farther out, Tien, Chiaotzu and Yamcha are moving away from the tracks.

"You think you've won?"

"You tried to destroy the planet," Gohan says. "We stopped you."

"Together."

"Yes," Gohan says. "Together."

Vegeta darts at him. Gohan lifts both arms, expecting the punch, but the prince veers past him and drives a knee into Goshin's guard instead. Goshin slides backward through the air. Vegeta follows with a short blast toward his face.

Goshin turns his head and bats the light aside. It streaks over the broken ridge. Before Vegeta can follow it, Goshin reaches him and catches both of his wrists.

The prince strains against the grip. Goshin holds him, his father's black hair lifting in the wind of their ki, Chi-Chi's eyes fixed steadily on the man in his hands. Vegeta stares at the boy who will not let him move.

"I told you," Goshin says, "we're not letting you attack them."

Gohan comes around Vegeta's right side. He places one hand against the damaged breastplate and pushes. The armour creaks; Vegeta tears one wrist free and blocks Gohan's hand, but Goshin is still holding the other.

Vegeta snaps his head forward. Goshin shifts aside before the blow can reach him, and the prince pulls loose. He drops toward the tracks. Gohan follows on one side and Goshin on the other.

Vegeta lands between two bent rails and sweeps his arm through the gravel. A spray of sharp stones flies toward Gohan. Gohan raises a hand and stops the nearest pieces with his ki, turning them aside before they reach his face.

Vegeta is already moving through the gap. He drives a punch into Gohan's shoulder, then another toward his ribs. Gohan turns the second blow and catches the prince's sleeve. Goshin reaches them, and Vegeta springs back before either boy can close on him.

The prince's boots scrape along a rail. His breathing is still hard from the Galick Gun, and his fingers open and close at his sides.

Gohan rubs his shoulder once. "You should go while you can."

Vegeta's eyes move from one boy to the other. "Kakarot left you here to fight for him?"

"Father's coming back," Gohan says.

"He's a long way away," Goshin adds. "We're here now."

Vegeta's mouth curls. "Then I'll finish with you before he gets here."

He thrusts a hand past them. Three quick blasts shoot across the quarry toward the western wall.

Gohan turns at once. Goshin goes after Vegeta.

---

## The Man at the Wall

"Down!" Gohan calls.

Piccolo throws himself across Nappa as Krillin drops beside them. The first blast strikes the rock above their heads. Gohan catches the second in both hands and throws it toward the empty slope. The third tears through a section of old platform, scattering timber over the rails.

A broken beam falls toward Piccolo. He stretches one arm, catches it and shoves it clear before it lands on Nappa. Dust spills down the wall around them.

Gohan lands beside the three men. "Are you hurt?"

"We're all right," Krillin says. He looks at Nappa. "He still hasn't moved."

Nappa's chest rises beneath the broken armour. His eyes remain shut.

"Help me get him behind the shelf," Piccolo says. "I want him alive long enough to answer my questions."

Krillin takes Nappa under one arm. Piccolo lifts the other side, and they carry him along the foot of the wall toward a projecting shelf of rock.

Above them, Goshin drives Vegeta away from the quarry. A blow lands against the prince's jaw, turning his head. Vegeta answers with two fast strikes; Goshin blocks the first and takes the second across his forearm. The Saiyan tries to move around him, but Goshin follows and punches him back toward the bare ridge.

Vegeta looks down. He has heard Piccolo.

"Questions for Nappa?"

Piccolo does not answer. He and Krillin keep moving.

"He couldn't beat a child," Vegeta says. "What makes you think I'll let you keep him?"

A narrow bolt leaps from Vegeta's fingers toward the two men carrying Nappa.

Gohan flies up from the wall. He gets beneath the bolt and meets it with his palms. The force pushes him down, his shoes scoring the rock, but he turns the shot and sends it into the empty ground beyond the tracks.

The explosion shakes loose another shower of stone. Piccolo and Krillin reach the shelter and lower Nappa behind it.

Goshin hits Vegeta before he can fire again. The prince crashes into the ridge; a long crack runs through the stone behind him.

"You were going to kill him," Gohan says.

Vegeta pulls himself out of the rock. "He's finished. Why should I care what happens to him?"

Gohan looks back at Nappa. The huge man who tried to reach their friends lies motionless, one hand open in the dust.

"He can't fight you now," Gohan says. "That's why."

Vegeta's lip curls. "Fool."

Vegeta fires another burst, this one straight at Gohan. Goshin cuts across its path and knocks it upward. Gohan rises beside him, and the brothers face the prince again.

Behind the shelf, Krillin checks the little pouch inside his clothes. The last Senzu is still there. He closes it and looks up.

"Do you think Vegeta meant to hit Nappa?" he asks.

"Yes," Piccolo says.

"Some friend."

"Keep him covered."

Piccolo steps out from behind the shelf. Tien, Chiaotzu and Yamcha have reached the wall on the other side of the broken platform.

"The ridge won't hold if another blast hits it," Tien calls. "There's a gully beyond it. We can move him there."

"Do it," Piccolo says. "I'll watch Vegeta."

## The Next Car

Bulma brings her car back to the bus and opens the rear door. Two more passengers climb in, an elderly man first and then a woman carrying a small child.

"The gym has water and blankets," Bulma tells them. "Keep your heads below the windows if the glass starts rattling again."

The woman settles the child on her lap. "Is there room for my sister? She's helping someone inside the bus."

"There's one seat left. Tell her to come now."

Puar floats through the bus door and calls toward the back. A moment later the woman's sister emerges, supporting a man whose leg is stiff. Chi-Chi takes his other arm and helps him down the steps.

"Can you put weight on it?" Chi-Chi asks.

"A little."

"Then lean on me."

Popo brings the carpet low beside the kerb. Chi-Chi helps the man onto it while Puar makes room for him to stretch his leg. The sister climbs into Bulma's car.

A distant crack rolls across the city. The bus windows tremble in their frames. Chi-Chi turns toward the western roofs, but the quarry is hidden by smoke and buildings.

"Gohan and Goshin are with Piccolo," Bulma says quietly. "They've got the others too."

"I know. I just wish I could see them."

Chi-Chi keeps looking west for another moment. Then the child in the car begins to cry, and she bends beside the open door.

"It's all right to hold on tight," she tells him. "Your mother is right there."

He buries his face against the woman's coat. Chi-Chi closes the door gently.

"I'll take them to the gym," Bulma says. "Come back for you?"

"There are still people down this street. Take whoever's waiting when you return."

Bulma nods and starts the car. Popo's carpet rises after her, carrying the injured man and Puar. Chi-Chi waits until they have cleared the broken window before she heads toward the next doorway.

## Below Korin Tower

A car stops on the road beneath the tall white tower. Launch gets out, leaving its radio playing. A hurried voice is reporting damage in East City and asking drivers to keep the main roads open.

Bora comes out of his house with Upa behind him. Launch waves to them.

"I'm sorry to turn up like this," she says. "I heard the report and came to ask if you'd seen Tien."

"Not since he went to train," Bora says. "We don't know where he is now."

Launch looks up the tower. Its top is hidden by cloud. "I was hoping he'd come back this way."

A voice calls from above them.

"Hey! Down there!"

Yajirobe is coming down the pole, his sword strapped at his waist. He slides the last stretch, catches himself with his feet and drops onto the grass.

"You made it," Upa says.

"Of course I made it. I just climbed down the whole thing."

Yajirobe bends over with his hands on his knees. A low rumble reaches them, and he straightens sharply.

"Korin felt two strangers land," he says. "The others went after them."

Launch takes a step toward him. "Was Tien with them?"

"I think so. He and Chiaotzu were training together."

The radio repeats the warning about East City. Launch opens the driver's door again.

"I'm going to find out."

Yajirobe looks toward the road, then at the tower behind him. "I was going to see where they are too."

"Get in, then."

"You don't have to tell me twice."

He climbs into the passenger seat, making room for his sword. Upa hands him a water flask through the window.

"For the trip. Bring it back when you can."

"Thanks, kid."

"Don't drink it all at once," Upa says.

Yajirobe looks at the flask. "You gave it to me, didn't you?"

"I want the flask back. You can keep the water."

Bora steps away from the car. "Be careful, both of you."

Launch pulls onto the road. Yajirobe takes a long drink, then holds the flask between his knees as the tower recedes behind them.

"The report didn't say anything about Tien," she says.

"They might not know who's there."

"I hope he and Chiaotzu are together."

"They usually are."

Launch turns up the radio. Another warning comes through, but no names. She keeps driving, her hands tight on the wheel.

"If we find them fighting," Yajirobe says, "don't drive straight into the middle of it."

"I wasn't going to."

"Good. Neither was I."

She glances at the sword across his lap. He pulls it closer and looks out of the window.

## What Vegeta Sees

Goshin reaches Vegeta at the ridge. The prince swings at his head, and Goshin slips inside the punch. He drives his palm against Vegeta's chest, sending him across the broken crest.

Vegeta catches the next blow, but the force moves him back again. Goshin feels the strength he gained during their fight still there in his arms. When Vegeta tries to turn him, Goshin keeps his footing and pushes the prince against the stone.

Gohan lands beside them. "It's over."

Vegeta's gaze flicks to Gohan's tail, then to Goshin's. Both move behind the boys as they hold their guard.

"Does Kakarot know what you've got?" he asks.

"We know about our tails," Gohan says.

"Do you?"

Vegeta throws his weight forward. Goshin releases him before the prince can drag him off balance. Vegeta drives an elbow into the ridge and tears free a slab. He hurls it across the quarry toward Tien and the others.

"Move!" Gohan shouts.

Tien and Yamcha lift Nappa between them. Chiaotzu reaches out with both hands, slowing the falling stone just enough for them to clear it.

Goshin raises his palm. The slab stops in midair, breaks into three pieces under the pressure of his ki and swings away from the fighters. He lowers the pieces beyond the tracks while flying after Vegeta.

The prince is moving upward, one fist closed. Goshin sees light forming in Vegeta's other hand and feels the ki gathering there. He catches the prince's shoulder and pulls him around. Vegeta twists, fires a flash into the space between them and throws the glowing object high over the quarry.

Goshin shields his eyes from the flash and reaches for Vegeta again. The prince knocks his arm aside and follows the rising light with his gaze.

"A Power Ball," he says. "It takes ki to make one, but you're worth the trouble."

The light bursts wider overhead. A pale circle hangs against the sky, bright enough to cast a second shadow behind the broken rail.

Piccolo looks up. He remembers the circle over the opened machine and the boys sheltering beneath the bank.

"Gohan! Goshin! Don't look at it!"

Goshin turns his face down at once. Gohan drops behind the ridge with him, keeping his eyes on the stone.

"Like the light from that machine," Gohan says.

"Yes. Stay below it."

Vegeta laughs. The boys can hear him above them, though neither looks up.

"So you've been warned. Good. Keep your eyes on the ground while I take care of you."

"You have a tail too," Goshin says.

"I know exactly what I'm doing. You two would be animals. Could you protect your friends then?"

Gohan feels the prince's ki change. For a moment it falls, as if the light overhead has cost him strength. Then it begins to rise.

The rock beneath the brothers shakes.

## Keeping It Together

Gregory darts around King Kai's house. Goku follows his flight with the sphere above his palms, shifting his feet as King Kai showed him. This time the light stays together through the turn.

Bubbles sits on the car roof with both hands over his eyes. Goku laughs.

"I can see the car, Bubbles."

"He can see it," King Kai agrees. "Now let's see whether he can keep seeing it while Gregory tries to fool him."

Gregory changes direction. Goku turns after him without letting the sphere drift. He thinks of Gohan and Goshin in the room together, and of Chi-Chi waiting to hear that he is coming home.

"One more," he says. "Then I want to take the test."

"One more," King Kai agrees.

## A Shadow Over the Quarry

A roar breaks over the ridge. Gohan feels the force of Vegeta's rising ki before he sees anything at all. The stones around him shudder and tumble from the ledge.

Piccolo stares across the quarry. Vegeta's tail has uncoiled from his waist. His body swells beneath his armour, rising higher and higher as dark fur spreads across his arms and face. His teeth lengthen, and his hands close into enormous fists. The scouter clipped to his armour swings against the expanding plate.

The ground shakes when Vegeta lands. He stands above the ridge, a giant figure in damaged Saiyan armour, his tail lashing behind him.

"Now," he says, his voice carrying across the quarry, "look at the strength of a Saiyan prince!"

Krillin backs against the shelf. "He can talk?"

"Get Nappa into the gully," Piccolo orders.

Tien and Yamcha carry Nappa around the end of the wall. Chiaotzu flies ahead to check the narrow opening. Krillin follows them, glancing back toward Vegeta.

The giant Saiyan swings an arm down toward Goshin. Goshin senses the movement and flies sideways with his face still turned from the light. The fist strikes the ridge. Stone erupts where he stood.

Gohan rises along the other side, keeping his back to the bright circle. "Goshin, to your right!"

Vegeta's tail sweeps toward his brother. Goshin ducks under it, then flies close to Vegeta's side and drives a punch into the armour beneath his arm.

The blow turns Vegeta's shoulder. He snarls and backhands Goshin across the quarry. Goshin catches himself above the tracks before he can hit the wall.

Gohan cups his hands and fires at Vegeta's wrist. The blue wave knocks the reaching hand aside, opening a path between it and Goshin. Vegeta jerks his arm back and stamps toward Gohan.

Gohan darts low, feeling where his brother is. He pulls Goshin clear as Vegeta's foot crushes a length of rail.

"I can feel where he is," Gohan says. "Like Popo showed me."

"I'll stay between him and the others," Goshin says.

Goshin rises again. Even without seeing the circle, he can feel the prince's power filling the quarry. He meets Vegeta's next punch with both hands. The impact drives him back, but he turns the huge fist away from the western wall before releasing it.

Vegeta looks toward the gully. "Still trying to protect that useless fool?"

Light gathers in his mouth. Goshin feels it and moves in front of him.

The beam fires. Goshin catches its edge, turning it upward. It tears past the ridge and bursts high above the empty slope. Heat washes over his arms; he drops beneath the fading light, still between Vegeta and the others.

"You missed them," Goshin says.

Vegeta bares his teeth. "I won't miss again."

## The Opening

Nappa lies in the gully, sheltered by stone on three sides. Yamcha sets him down and flexes his cut arm. The sleeve has stuck to the blood, but his hand still closes.

Chiaotzu hovers over the opening. "The rocks are holding."

"Stay with him," Tien says.

Krillin looks out toward Vegeta. "His tail's right there. If I can get a clear shot—"

"I'll give you one," Tien says.

Krillin leaves the gully and runs along the wall to the shelf. Tien waits until he reaches it, then rises above the gully.

"Everyone, close your eyes!"

Piccolo turns his head and covers his face. Gohan and Goshin close their eyes too, without lifting their faces. Krillin ducks behind the shelf and shuts his eyes; Yamcha and Chiaotzu turn their faces toward the gully wall beside Nappa. Tien spreads his hands beside his face.

"Solar Flare!"

White light floods the quarry. Vegeta throws one arm across his eyes and roars. His next swing tears through the rock instead of finding the boys.

As the flash fades, Krillin opens his eyes and comes out from behind the shelf. A bright, flat disc spins above his raised hand.

"Destructo Disc!"

He sends it toward Vegeta's tail. The prince hears the shout and twists, pulling his tail tight against his body. The disc passes behind him, slices through the ridge and continues into the empty ground beyond.

A great slab separates from the cut. Piccolo lowers his hand from his face and stretches an arm across the slab's path, but the falling stone is too broad for him to hold alone.

Gohan opens his eyes toward the ground and catches it with his ki. He turns the slab away from the gully and lowers it across the broken tracks with a crash.

"Thanks," Piccolo says.

"The light!" Gohan calls. "Can we hit it?"

Piccolo looks up. "Above the prince. A little to your left."

Gohan keeps his head down and aims where Piccolo directs him. Blue light gathers at his side.

"Kamehameha!"

The wave climbs toward the artificial moon. Vegeta drops his arm from his eyes in time to see it. He fires from his mouth; the blasts collide above his shoulder, and Gohan's wave breaks apart before reaching the circle.

"You won't take it from me!" Vegeta roars.

He sends another blast toward the gully. Gohan moves first, but Goshin reaches the line of fire beside him. Together they push the attack away from the opening. It strikes the empty slope, shaking loose more stone.

The brothers land behind a spur of rock. Goshin opens his eyes toward the ground. From there they can see the quarry floor without seeing the light overhead.

Gohan's hands are trembling. He presses them against his legs for a moment, then raises his head just enough to look at Goshin.

"He's watching the light. He won't let us reach it."

"We'll find another way," Piccolo says, landing near them.

Goshin looks at his brother. "The light changed him because he looked at it."

Gohan touches his own tail. "It might change us too."

They both remember the bridge: the pale light on the planks, their father's warning, and their mother waiting with the blanket.

Vegeta's shadow moves across the ground beyond their shelter.

"I could look," Goshin says.

Piccolo turns to him. "We don't know what it'll do to you."

"Roshi said Father didn't remember what he did," Gohan says.

"I know," Goshin says. He looks up at his brother, not at the light. "I don't want to hurt anybody here either."

Goshin watches the shadow stretch toward the gully. He can feel Chiaotzu there, and Yamcha and Tien, with Nappa between them. Krillin is moving along the wall, looking for another opening.

"If I get big too, I can keep him away from them."

Gohan takes his arm. "What if you can't tell where we are?"

"I don't know what it'll feel like," Goshin says. "But I can feel you now. I'll listen for you."

The ridge shakes as Vegeta steps closer. A stone falls past the opening and breaks against the ground. Goshin looks toward the gully again.

"He's going to keep shooting at them. I want to try."

"I can look too," Gohan says. He glances toward the gully. "But somebody has to help the others get clear."

Piccolo studies the ground between the spur and the broken tracks. "Move away from us before you do it. Whatever happens, you'll have space."

Goshin nods. "I'll keep him here. Gohan, you help them."

Gohan holds his arm a moment longer. "I'm not going away."

"I know."

Goshin squeezes his brother's hand and releases it. He rises above the spur, keeping his face down until he is clear of Gohan and Piccolo.

Then he looks up.

---

## A Mother's View

Chi-Chi comes out of the doorway with a woman beside her. Across the street, Bulma's car is returning. Popo's carpet follows it, empty now except for Puar.

"There are two more people upstairs," Chi-Chi tells them. "The staircase is blocked near the bottom."

Popo takes the carpet toward the upper window. Puar flies beside him, calling to the people inside.

Bulma steps out of the car and looks west. A pale circle hangs above the far buildings.

"What is that?"

Chi-Chi turns. She has seen that shape before, above an open machine in the hollow. Her hand closes around the car door.

"Bulma, it looks like the moon."

Bulma follows her gaze. "In the middle of the day?"

"The boys know not to look at a full moon." Chi-Chi watches the strange light. "But if they don't know what that is—"

A shout comes from the window. Popo has reached the two people upstairs, and Puar is helping one onto the carpet. Chi-Chi opens the car door for the woman beside her, and the woman climbs in.

"Once they're down," she says to Bulma, "we'll take them to the gym. Then I want to get closer to the quarry."

"I'll come with you."

"We can't fly into that fight blind. We'll stop where we can see."

Bulma nods. Neither of them takes her eyes from the light for long.

## Goshin's Answer

The bright circle fills Goshin's vision. His heart pounds once, then again, each beat stronger than the last.

His arms lengthen. Fur spreads over his hands as the seams of his clothes split. His shoes break apart, and pieces of cloth and leather scatter across the tracks. The air rushes around him; the ridge that rose over his head a moment ago sinks beneath his shoulders.

The ground is coming up beneath his feet. Gohan calls his name from beside the spur, and Goshin knows where he is. His brother's ki is familiar and steady. Piccolo is close to him; the others are farther left, in the gully.

Goshin plants one enormous foot on the empty side of the tracks, away from them. His tail swings behind him, and he brings it close before it can strike the wall. He bends his knees and spreads his new hands, feeling the weight of his body settle.

The rush of strength is unlike anything he has felt before. As he moves, his body answers it naturally, and the power inside him keeps building. Wind presses through the fur on his arms. He opens and closes one enormous hand, then looks past it at the ridge, which is lower than his shoulders.

An astonished laugh rumbles out of him. He can still feel Gohan right where he left him.

"Gohan! I can hear you! I can see right over the ridge!"

His voice is deeper, louder. Gohan looks toward him, keeping the moon out of his sight. His brother's ki has become huge, but he can still feel Goshin in it.

"I can feel you too!" Gohan calls.

Goshin turns his head toward the answer. The people below look very small. He lifts his heel from a broken rail and sets it on bare stone instead.

Vegeta stares.

"You looked?"

Goshin turns his great head toward him. The prince is still enormous, but the two now face each other above the ridge.

Vegeta lunges. Goshin catches his fist in both hands.

The ground breaks under their feet. Vegeta bears down, expecting the smaller fighter to give way. Instead, Goshin holds him, shifts his weight and drives him backward.

Vegeta's heel smashes through the cut ridge. Three heavy blocks tumble toward the gully. Goshin keeps hold of the prince with one hand and reaches for the falling stone with his ki. The blocks stop short of the gully, swing aside and settle beyond the tracks.

"Chiaotzu?" he calls.

"We're all right!" Chiaotzu answers from below.

Vegeta wrenches his fist free. "You should have lost your mind!"

Goshin steps between him and the gully. "I'm still here."

Gohan rises along the rock face, keeping the artificial moon behind him. He stops where he can see his brother's face without looking into the sky.

"Goshin?"

The enormous head turns toward his voice. Goshin lifts one hand, careful of the wall and the people beside it.

"I'm all right, Gohan."

Gohan lets out the breath he has been holding. "Good. Because I'm still helping."

Light gathers between Vegeta's teeth. Gohan flies beneath the giant's chin and sends a quick blast upward. It turns Vegeta's head just as he fires; the beam passes above the empty ridge.

Vegeta swipes at Gohan. Gohan drops past the hand, and Goshin catches Vegeta's arm before the prince can follow. Vegeta's tail lashes across the broken ridge as Goshin pushes him farther into the empty quarry. Their feet gouge deep trenches through the stone.

Gohan lands by Piccolo. "I'll keep him looking this way when I can."

"Don't let him catch you," Piccolo says.

"I won't."

Vegeta stops himself and raises both fists. Goshin sets his own hands in front of him, the same guard he used when he was small enough to stand beside Gohan.

The artificial moon burns overhead. Below it, the prince and the boy face each other across the shattered ground.
