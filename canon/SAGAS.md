# SAGAS — the Z-anime map
**Owns: the arc navigation, research scope and cautions for the serial.** The detailed evidence, corrections and limits live in `canon/RESEARCH.md`; citable rows live in `canon/facts.json`.

## Research scope

All **291 episodes were indexed and searched**, not read in full. Full spoken-caption reads: **1–36, 49, 55, 59, 61, 65, 79–83, 183–184, 205–208, 256–257**. Later episodes were otherwise read selectively. The Japanese SRT files were redistributed by Kitsunekko; their official/broadcast provenance has not been established. They attest their text provisionally, not silent frames. Treat all index boundaries as a search aid, not proof of complete episode content.

## Growth and the readings

Vegeta infers that Saiyan/Earthling hybrids can be powerful and fears their continued growth (ep. 5, 22:22–23:13; `C142`). Gohan then grows in multiple ways: training, latent ability, temporary surges, recovery after severe injury and later rituals. The **1** immediately after his Raditz outburst is not a lifelong baseline. His Masenko is **2,800** in ep. 28 and momentarily **over 20,000** in ep. 65. At ep. 80, 18:07–18:11, Vegeta explicitly links Gohan's increase after Dende's healing to Saiyan blood. `canon/RESEARCH.md §2` separates the readings and their conditions.

**Goshin's ~400** is our scouter snapshot at Kame House before Raditz takes both boys (`O005`), not his later permanent floor. He grows. His authored talent helps him learn and retain technique; a suppressed, tired or injured reading can fall. His standing capability is never beneath Gohan's by the user's direction, not by a canon equation. No Nappa ceiling, mandatory canon death, automatic ranking above all other hybrids, or fixed later number is imposed.

## Tail, flight and damage

- Piccolo considers taking Gohan's tail in ep. 8; it is missing in ep. 9, and in ep. 18 he says he pulled it. Goshin's kept tail is authored (`O046`). Vegeta and Nappa trained away their tail weakness (ep. 27), not by merely seeing a grip once.
- The natural moon's destruction does not end every ape route: ep. 18 has a pod-generated projection and ep. 31 has Vegeta's artificial moon. Natural restoration and future SS4 continuity need their own verification. The serial's ruled future route stands as authored.
- Piccolo flies. In ep. 6 it is **Goku**, not Piccolo, who says fatigue prevents further flight on Snake Way. Ordinary ki and flight are allowed now; **god ki is not**.
- Episode 22 reports East City's disappearance after Nappa's greeting. His guidebook value of 4,000 is not an on-screen measurement of the blast. Piccolo's 408 was a separate earlier reading, not a reading of the moon attack. No numerical formula converts battle power into collateral or guarantees victory.

## Later continuity and the clock

The Earth set is tied to Kami, not Namek's set. Piccolo's fusion with Kami removes Earth's balls until Dende revives them. In ep. 173, about **100 days** is the estimate to **make new balls**, whereas the old stones and dragon model permit immediate revival; the group selects a **two-wish** arrangement for group resurrection. Goku dies fighting Cell, not on Namek, and later chooses not to be revived in canon.

Raditz's opening date is a retrospective guidebook assignment, not episode dialogue. Trunks's three years begin with his warning, not Raditz's arrival. Ep. 171's anime dialogue says Gohan became **11 in the time chamber**; other time/age references conflict. Do not transfer that extra physical time to Goshin without writing him into the room. Exact later ages remain a source-and-divergence question (`C140`, `canon/RESEARCH.md §6`).

## Goten and the parents

Goten is canonically a prodigy, not an untrained replacement. In ep. 206 he says **Chi-Chi taught him sparring**, and he can transform before he can fly. Gohan teaches him flight in ep. 207. Gohan does not cease to grow or matter because Goshin is capable. Chi-Chi tells Roshi and the others that she has come to learn what happened; **Roshi tells her in ep. 8**, not Krillin in ep. 6 (`C120`, `C121`).
