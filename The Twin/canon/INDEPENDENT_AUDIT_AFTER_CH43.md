## The branch as it stands

**The heart of the story.** Goshin and Gohan are brothers with distinct desires and agency, not a single protagonist and an attendant. Goshin looks strikingly like Goku with Chi-Chi's eyes; his developing Adaptation Talent and his *whole* Saiyan–Earthling hybrid inheritance can affect his body, energy, senses, skills and more. Actual integrated growth is retained and can be substantial, but neither every action nor a particular emotion automatically grants a gain. Goshin is never less capable than Gohan in the author's standing comparison; temporary conditions and tactics still matter, and Gohan acts, feels and grows independently.

**A lived history, not a reset.** The brothers' full additional year in the physical chamber counts as lived time and development. Goshin's first Great Ape was conscious and controlled; his substantial resulting growth remains. Gohan, Goshin and Vegeta retain their tails and know the risk of a full or equivalent artificial moon. Caution about an unknown sky does not prohibit an informed choice. Goku is alive, curious about strong opponents, affectionate toward his family and still responsible for the actual watch on Earth.

**Power and context.** The author's working world-scale direction makes present ordinary-bodied Goshin capable of destroying Earth and places earlier Goku at light speed or above as an interpretation, not an exact measured speed or an obligation to move at maximum speed in every scene. A scouter reports a selected output at a particular moment; it does not by itself prove a maximum, a permanent gain or what another person heard. Kaiō-ken amplifies chosen unboosted power temporarily; it is not the same thing as lasting development. Chapter 43's ×30 means thirty times the chosen unboosted amount, not thirty times an already active ×20. Goshin releases that surge after a prolonged, costly fight, retains genuine growth and later renews ×20; neither this effort nor any source character's injuries fixes his future ceiling.

**People and information at the frontier.** Freeza knows the name Dende and that Gohan reported a healer; he has not been shown identifying Dende's face or learning the moved group's exact location. Freeza's active contest with Goshin and Piccolo is unresolved. Gohan's intervention saves Piccolo and his own choice takes him after Muri's group; he reaches the vacated grove, not the group. Muri retains the wrapped ball; the soldier is bound only for now, and uncounted residents, other soldiers, Zarbon and Dodoria remain open. Bulma and Kado reach the ship, clear the aerial and call Puar with news bounded by what Bulma last saw. Puar's subsequent note has not reached Chi-Chi or the others. The ship's re-hiding, the unopened Earth pod, safe custody and a rescue craft remain unresolved. The brothers' promise to seek help for Raiti and Zākuro and the refugee children's later safety also remain open. See the [precise Chapter 43 handoff](SCENE_COVERAGE_031_082_084_088_CH43.md).

**Voice and feeling.** Goku's enthusiasm for a strong opponent coexists with family concern and mistakes; Gohan's politeness can coexist with anger or fear and decisive action. Goshin can be delighted, frightened, angry, loud, affectionate or thoughtful in his own way. Chi-Chi, Piccolo, Bulma, Dende and the villagers need their own motives and choices. Freeza's courtesy can carry menace. A roar, injury, perfectly calm speech or one emotional sequence is not mandatory, and the particular original-anime deaths, transformations and winners are not compulsory outcomes in this changed branch.

## Findings requiring future literary attention

1. **Chapter 43, stone handling — local staging issue.** At lines 467–473 Gohan sends the nearest stone into the water while Piccolo and Goshin handle others. Line 477 then says Gohan lowers a stone he caught, without a separate catch or retained stone being shown. Clarify the object and action in a future authorized prose edit; this does not establish a larger plot failure.
2. **Chapter 43, immediate pronoun — local clarity issue.** Line 481 switches to Freeza, then line 483 says Gohan lands beside “him”; the following conversation indicates Goshin is intended. Naming the person and orienting the landing would make the handoff clearer. The current prose is unchanged.
3. **Goku's Snake Way return — existing unresolved chronology.** Chapters 7 and 14 give a traditional **approximate** length of one million kilometres. In Chapters 23–28 Goku promises to return as fast as he can, while the battle, civilian response and initial aftermath are intercut with his return. If the whole traditional distance were traversed at an average light speed, the ideal travel time would be about 3.3 seconds; neither assumption is established as fact. This is a genuine presentation tension against the author's speed interpretation, not a proved exact impossibility. A coherent repair may affect departure, battle, rescue and reunion across multiple chapters. Do not invent an afterlife speed cap, a convenient delay or unsupported time dilation. See OPEN-40 in [the open threads](../OPEN.md) and the [earlier full review](FULL_MANUSCRIPT_REVIEW_2026-11-06.md#chronology-and-scale-still-open).
4. **Repeated explanations can flatten the storytelling.** Across the travel chapters and battle, several successive scenes explicitly restate that Goshin's added strength remains when red light or effort ends; compare Chapter 32 around line 525, Chapter 33 around lines 127–129, Chapter 34 around lines 245–261, Chapter 36 around line 207, Chapter 37 around line 481 and Chapter 43 around line 479. Chapters 32–36 also repeatedly explain that Vegeta's shade is not confinement. These are valid continuity points, not errors in their underlying rules. The recurrence risks making different moments sound like the same verification; future prose can show gains and danger through varied action, character and consequence **without removing the growth or pretending the watch is secure**. The [previous style study](../../Research/The%20Twin/Original%20Episode%20and%20Franchise%20Research/08_ANIME_STYLE_EMOTION_ACTION_AND_VOICE_STUDY.md) had identified the same risk in Chapters 39–42. This is an editorial judgment for review, not a mechanical failure.
5. **Chapter 33, time-bounded reassurance — possible ambiguity.** About a week after departure, Gohan says Father and Krillin are there if Vegeta acts, and Goshin adds that Tien and Chiaotzu have Nappa (lines 271–273). This can be read as the boys' confidence in the last arrangement rather than a claim of live knowledge. Chapter 34 explicitly says the watch was in place *when they left* and Gohan does not know what happened since (lines 387–389). Consider whether the earlier line needs the same time qualification; do not treat this alone as proof of telepathy or an actual change in custody.

## Repairs to the living records and checker

- **Episode-splitting rule:** `RULES.md` previously failed a chapter merely for stopping short of a cited episode, contrary to the author's explicit direction (`O226`). It now permits an episode to span chapters and multiple episodes in one chapter while still requiring relevant source beats to be recorded as shown, altered, already shown or deferred.

Seven acceptance-range references in six foundation files now include Chapter 43, without accepting any selected draft or replacing the separately accepted original Chapter 20.
- **Prose boundary:** the checker formerly split only at the literal `## Footer` marker. Chapters 37–42 use `### Continuity and source note`, so unfenced explanatory notes were included in their word/sentence/dialogue metrics and in several story-only guards. One shared story-side extractor now handles both heading styles, fenced examples and hidden comments; the affected reveal, purity, era, names, flow, scene and narrow regression checks use it. Tests place the same forbidden terms in metadata and genuine prose, cover both footer styles, a hidden footer, false headings and nested-looking delimiters in comments and fences, unclosed comments, and self-test offsets. A comment containing a footer example must not hide later story. The new tests exposed the old boundary and comment-state failures and passed after the repair.
