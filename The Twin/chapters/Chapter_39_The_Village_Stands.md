# Chapter 39 — The Village Stands

*Anime episode reference: Dragon Ball Z, Episodes 47–48 (the village confrontation, scouters and children, adapted to the changed branch).*

## At the Door

Gohan reaches the doorway before Dodoria does.

The big pink fighter swings an open hand at him. Gohan brings both forearms up, catches the blow and slides backward across the path. His heel strikes the bottom step. Behind him, the elder pulls the two children inside.

Gohan plants his foot and pushes. Dodoria's arm shifts away from the doorway.

"Get away from them!" Gohan says.

Dodoria's small eyes widen. He puts his other hand against Gohan's guard, but Gohan turns under the pressure and drives a shoulder into his middle. Dodoria stumbles sideways, away from the door, and the wall trembles when his back hits it.

To the left of the house, the soldier jerks the older villager in front of him. The man's arm is already red where the soldier has been gripping it.

"Let him GO!"

Goshin lands hard enough to crack the path. The shout surprises even him. His ki jumps, hot and fierce, as he catches the soldier's wrist.

The soldier tightens his grip. Goshin pulls his arm away from the villager, then catches the soldier's other hand when a bright bead forms in the palm. His fingers close over the light before it can reach the man.

Piccolo lands at the villager's side. A second soldier fires from near the path. Piccolo strikes the shot aside with his forearm; it bursts against an empty stretch of stone, scattering chips across the ground. Before the soldier can fire again, Piccolo reaches him and knocks him down with a kick to the chest. The man reaches for his scouter, but Piccolo pulls it free and crushes it under his heel.

The older villager ducks behind Piccolo. He has a red mark around his arm, but he is on his feet.

"Can you move?" Piccolo asks.

"Yes." The man looks past him toward the house. "The children—"

"Gohan has them. Go around the wall."

The villager runs toward the side of the house. Goshin releases both of the soldier's wrists and pushes him back toward the center of the path, where Freeza's chair hovers above the ground. The soldier catches himself and reaches for his scouter.

Freeza has not moved. His gaze travels from Piccolo to the two boys and their tails.

"A Namekian and two small visitors with tails," he says. "I believe I have heard about you. Where is the fourth?"

Goshin's stomach tightens. Bulma is still behind the rock on the bank. He keeps his eyes on Freeza.

"Not with you," he says.

"Obviously. I do hope my men haven't frightened your friend away."

Dodoria pushes off the wall and lunges at Gohan. Gohan hops back from the doorway to meet him, keeping the elder and children behind him. Dodoria's fist cuts through the air where his head was; Gohan catches the next strike on one arm and hits Dodoria's chest with his free hand. The larger fighter skids across the path, digging two furrows with his boots.

The soldier Goshin released taps his newer-model scouter. Its lens flashes as he looks at Gohan.

"Twenty-seven thousand six hundred!"

"That's impossible." The soldier by the chair turns his own newer-model lens toward Dodoria. "Lord Dodoria is at twenty-two thousand! That child is even higher!"

"Twenty-seven thousand? That scouter's broken!" Dodoria snarls. He rubs the front of his armour and glares at Gohan. "I'll crush you, you little brat!"

Freeza's smile has narrowed. "I sent Zarbon to look for our visitors. How obliging of you to come to me instead."

Gohan glances back. The elder is in the doorway with one arm across the two children. The smaller child clings to the other's sleeve. The older one is watching Gohan as if he cannot decide whether the new arrivals are another kind of danger.

"We saw what your men were doing," Gohan says to Freeza. "Leave these people alone."

"And you have come to make me?"

"If we have to."

The words come out more firmly than Gohan expected. Freeza's power is still there, enormous and quiet. Gohan feels it every time he lets himself pay attention to the chair. But the children are close enough behind him that he can hear a child's quick breathing.

The elder steps onto the threshold. "Who are you?"

"I'm Gohan. We came from Earth."

The elder looks at Piccolo, then at Gohan's tail. He has no time to ask another question. The soldier who scanned Gohan turns his lens toward the far side of the village and presses the control again.

"Two hundred and ten, one hundred and eighty, ninety. There are more readings beyond those hills, Lord Freeza. A group—"

"Another village?" Freeza asks.

"I can't tell yet. I can find out."

The elder's gaze fixes on the small device. The soldier turns his head, and the lens turns with it. A few moments earlier he had found the approaching boys the same way.

The elder's hand lifts.

A thin bolt cracks the lens over the soldier's eye. The soldier cries out and pulls the broken scouter away from his face. Almost at the same instant, a second bolt snaps Dodoria's lens in two.

Dodoria stares at the fragments on the ground. "You old—"

"Those machines are how you find our people," the elder says.

"How observant," Freeza replies. "You might have told me you could fight."

A third scouter remains on the soldier by Freeza's chair. The elder raises his hand toward it, but Dodoria is already moving.

"Muri!" the older villager shouts from the side of the house.

Gohan turns toward the elder. Dodoria drives in between them, aiming both hands at the doorway.

Goshin crosses the path and catches Dodoria around the waist. The larger fighter's feet leave the ground. Goshin carries him past the house and sets him down hard in the open patch beyond it, facing away from the wall. The ground cracks beneath Dodoria's boots.

"Go!" Goshin calls.

Gohan looks back to the elder. "Is there another way out?"

Muri points into the house. "The rear door. Dende, take Cargo through it. Stay low by the wall."

"I can bring them to Bulma," Gohan says.

Muri studies him for an instant. Dodoria is struggling against Goshin's hold, and the soldier with the broken scouter has begun to get up. Muri puts a hand on the older child's shoulder.

"Go with him."

Dende takes Cargo's hand. Gohan follows them inside.

## A Ball in the House

The room is cool after the bright path. A low table has tipped against the wall, and a cloth has slipped from a recessed shelf. Something round and orange fills the hollow behind it.

Gohan stops.

The object is much larger than the Dragon Ball he remembers on his hat. Red stars show through its surface where the cloth has fallen away.

Dende sees where he is looking and rushes to the shelf. "Don't take it!"

"I wasn't going to."

Dende pulls the cloth up with both hands, but it catches beneath the sphere. Gohan lifts one edge of the heavy ball just enough for him to free it. The surface is smooth against Gohan's palm. Dende covers it again.

"Is it a Dragon Ball?" Gohan asks.

Dende nods. "It's Elder Muri's. Those men came to take it."

"We have them on Earth too," Gohan says. "One belonged to my grandfather, and my father gave it to me. It used to be on my hat."

Dende looks at the covered shelf, then at Gohan. "How big is your hat?"

Gohan holds his hands apart. "Ours are smaller. About this big."

For a moment Dende looks as if he might ask another question. Cargo tugs at his sleeve instead. Dende takes his hand again, and the question goes unasked.

On the last planet Gohan had watched apparent Dragon Balls become stones. He looks once more at the covered shelf. This time there is a village around it, and a child trying to keep its elder's possession out of someone else's hands. That does not tell him what the ball can do.

A crash shakes the house. Cargo flinches and catches Dende's arm.

"The back door," Gohan says. "I'll go first."

He opens it a crack. A low wall runs from the house toward the bank, with a narrow space between the wall and a second building. Beyond it he can see the rock where Bulma is hiding. Nobody is in the passage.

Gohan leads them along the wall. His tail stays tucked around his waist, and he keeps his eyes on the ground ahead rather than the open strip of sky above the roofs. At the end of the passage there is a short gap before the bank. Bulma leans out from behind the rock.

"Over here!"

Gohan guides the children across the gap. From his raised chair on the path, Freeza can see over the end of the low wall. He watches the three figures disappear behind the bank; he does not see Bulma.

Cargo runs to her. Dende follows, then stops and looks back toward the house.

"Elder Muri is still there."

"So is Piccolo," Gohan says. "I'm going back too."

Bulma crouches beside the children. "I'm Bulma. What's your name?"

"Dende. This is Cargo."

Cargo's knee is scraped. Bulma opens her case, finds a clean cloth and hands it to him. "Press that on it. Hard enough to keep the dirt out."

Cargo obeys, though he keeps watching the path. Dende moves close enough to see around the rock. Bulma catches the back of his clothing before he leans all the way out.

"You can look from here," she says, shifting aside to make room. "I don't want you in the open."

Gohan bends close to her. "There's a ball in the house. Dende says it's their Dragon Ball. I saw stars."

Bulma's eyes widen. "Did you touch it?"

"Yes. We covered it again."

She looks toward the doorway. "Don't tell that little monster in the chair where it is."

"I won't."

"And don't go charging at him just because you found it."

"I'm going to help the people outside."

She searches his face, then nods. "All right. We'll stay down."

Gohan gives Dende a quick smile. "I'll come back."

He runs along the wall until the house shields him from the bank, then rises just enough to clear its corner. He can feel Goshin and Piccolo nearby, and the much larger presence beyond them.

## The Old Landing

Zarbon lands beside the crater at the eastern shore. Two soldiers come down behind him.

One of the original scouts is sitting against a rock, holding his arm. The other lies in the shade a short distance away. Broken pieces of two scouters are scattered between the crater and the empty landing place.

"You're alive," Zarbon says.

"They left us here."

"I can see that. What happened?"

The scout jerks his chin toward the crater. "We found the ship and reported. The big green one and the two children fought us. The other one's a woman."

"And the ship?"

"It took off over that ridge." He points with his uninjured hand toward the water. "I couldn't see where it went after that."

Zarbon examines the broken device nearest his boot. "Your second message stopped in the middle. Did you identify the children?"

"They had tails. We asked if they were Saiyans. They didn't answer."

"Their strength?"

The scout looks toward his unconscious companion. "The little one caught a blast aimed at the ship. The numbers were still going up when the Namekian broke his scouter. I didn't see the final reading."

Zarbon's expression changes very slightly. "And yours?"

"The one who fought me was at four hundred and thirty when we first spotted them. The other was at five hundred and twenty. They were lower when I checked again. Then mine jumped to fourteen thousand six hundred. He broke the scouter before I could see any more."

"From four hundred and thirty to fourteen thousand six hundred?" Zarbon looks down at the broken lens. "And the other child's reading was still rising."

He motions one of his soldiers toward the unconscious scout. "See to him. If he wakes, ask what he saw. Don't leave them in the open."

The soldier kneels. Zarbon lifts off with the other and crosses the ridge toward the inlet.

They pass above the water once without finding the craft. Zarbon slows, circles back and follows the edge of the shore. At one bend, his companion points down to pale marks on the ground beneath a shelf of rock.

"Something stood there. Four feet, perhaps."

The shelf blocks their view from above. Zarbon descends to the waterline and looks under it. Behind a thick spur of stone, the white hull rests on its landing feet. The hatch is closed. A narrow aerial clears the ceiling by a small space.

"There you are," he says.

His companion raises a hand. "Shall I open it?"

"Not yet." Zarbon looks along the inlet and sees no one nearby. "Lord Freeza wants answers. A destroyed ship won't help us get them."

He touches his scouter. "Zarbon to Lord Freeza. I've found the visitors' vessel under a rock shelf near the eastern inlet. It appears intact. The occupants aren't here."

Static crackles for a moment. Then a soldier's voice answers.

"I'm with Lord Freeza. I'll tell him."

Zarbon keeps his eyes on the hatch. "Tell him we're watching it."

## The Answer at the Quarry

Goku sits on a flat stone across from Vegeta, close enough to reach him if he moves. Krillin stands beside the overhang Goku made for the prince. Across the broken ground, Tien and Chiaotzu remain with Nappa.

Chi-Chi has started back toward the telephone. Goku watches her until the rocks hide her from view. Then King Kai's voice reaches him.

*Goku. I found Piccolo.*

Goku straightens. *How are they?*

*When I spoke to him, he was with both boys and Bulma. Their ship was hidden, and they were moving toward people they thought might be in danger. I warned him about Freeza.*

*Did you see the fight?*

*No. Piccolo told me what had happened at the ship. I didn't see what happened after we spoke.*

Goku looks toward the hills as if he might see through them. "They're together," he says aloud.

Krillin turns. "King Kai?"

Goku nods. *Thanks. Piccolo said they were going to help someone?*

*That's what he told me. Goku, listen to me. I meant what I said about Freeza. Your sons have grown strong, but don't mistake that for knowing what they're facing.*

*I won't.*

He wants to feel that power for himself. Vegeta's reaction to the name, King Kai's warning and the thought of his boys somewhere near it pull at him all at once. Wanting to go does not put a ship in front of him.

*Can you reach them again?* Goku asks.

*I can try. I can't promise I'll catch Piccolo at a moment when he can answer.*

*Please try. Tell him we'll find a way to help.*

King Kai is quiet for a moment. *I'll do what I can.*

The connection fades. Krillin comes closer, keeping Vegeta in view.

"They were together when Piccolo talked to him," Goku says. "They hid the ship. They were going toward some people who might need help. King Kai doesn't know what happened after that."

Krillin lets out a breath. "We should tell Chi-Chi."

"Will you catch her? I can watch Vegeta while you're gone. Ask her if she wants to go to Capsule Corporation with you. Dr. Brief might need to know I'm ready to help with whatever ship he's checking."

Krillin looks at Vegeta. "You're sure?"

Goku rises. "He hasn't gone anywhere with both of us here. I'm not going to let him leave now."

Vegeta's tail shifts across the rock. "You'd rather send the little one to fetch messages than come after Freeza yourself?"

"I'd rather do both," Goku says. "But somebody has to stay with you."

Krillin gives Goku a brief nod and flies after Chi-Chi, following the route she took away from the quarry. Goku steps into the place he left and watches Vegeta's hands.

"Would Freeza kill those people for Dragon Balls, if they have any?" he asks.

Vegeta looks at him with an expression somewhere between anger and disbelief. "He'd kill them for less."

One guard instead of two. Vegeta presses his fingers into a crack in the stone. If he reaches the city and his pod, he can look for the place Freeza has found. If the pod is gone, he will find another way. He cannot do either from beneath Kakarot's feet.

He fires into the broken ground beside him. Grit and sharp chips leap between them. Goku shields his face, but he is already moving when Vegeta shoots low across the tracks toward the opening of the quarry.

"Tien!" Goku calls.

Tien turns, then sees Nappa push himself higher against the gully wall. Chiaotzu rises beside him, and Tien stays where he can block Nappa's path.

"He'd leave you here," Tien says.

"I can see that." Nappa watches Vegeta without moving to help him.

Vegeta clears the tracks. Red light flashes around Goku, and he reaches the prince before the open road. Vegeta twists and fires at him. Goku strikes the shot aside; it bursts against bare rock beyond the quarry wall.

Vegeta tries to slip past on the other side. Goku catches his wrist, and both of them hit the ground hard. The prince drives his free elbow into Goku's shoulder. Goku holds on, turns the trapped hand away from the gully and gets a knee under himself.

"Let go!"

"Not while you're going after your ship."

Vegeta strains against him. His side catches, and his breath goes ragged. Kakarot's red light is still there, and the other two guards are watching from the gully. Vegeta stops pulling before he gives Goku another opening.

Goku lets him get his feet under him but stays close enough to catch him again. Vegeta walks back to the overhang under his own power, furious that Kakarot saw him falter.

"Next time, you won't stop me."

"Then I'll be ready," Goku says. Across the tracks, Tien and Chiaotzu remain with Nappa. Goku takes his place facing Vegeta and keeps it while he waits for Krillin.

## Dodoria's Answer

Dodoria tears himself out of Goshin's hold and swings an elbow backward. Goshin catches it on his forearm. The blow drives one boot across the open ground, but he stays between Dodoria and the house.

"Get out of my way!"

"Stop trying to hurt them."

Dodoria drives forward with both hands. Goshin meets him, palms against the front of his armour. They slide several steps away from the village before Goshin digs his heels in. Dirt ridges around his boots. Dodoria bears down, his face close enough for Goshin to see the strain in it.

Goshin shoves him back.

Dodoria staggers, catches himself and looks past the boy toward Muri. Piccolo has moved the elder and the older villager behind the corner of the house. The soldier Piccolo knocked down is trying to rise; Piccolo puts a foot against his chest and makes him stay where he is.

Dodoria opens his mouth. Light gathers inside it.

Goshin sees the angle. The blast will pass him and strike the corner where Piccolo and the villagers are standing.

The thought of that blast reaching the house sends a hot rush through him.

"NO!"

Goshin bursts across the path with a roar and strikes upward beneath Dodoria's jaw. Dodoria's head snaps back before the light leaves his mouth. The blast goes into the bare ground beyond the houses, throwing up a high spray of stone and soil.

Goshin turns the nearest fragments aside with his ki. Smaller pieces rattle against the far wall, but Piccolo keeps the two villagers beneath the overhang until they stop falling.

Dodoria grabs Goshin's shoulders and tries to drive him into the fresh crater. Goshin catches both wrists. The pressure comes through his arms and into his back, stronger than the first push. He remembers holding Vegeta's weight in his giant body, the strain of the ship against the collapsing cliff, and the strength that remained afterward. All of it is with him now.

His arms answer. Even while he holds his ki at the level he has already brought forward, his shoulders and hands grow stronger under Dodoria's grip. His ki deepens with them. The pressure that drove him back begins to give way; Goshin straightens both elbows and forces Dodoria's arms apart.

Dodoria's boots leave the ground. Goshin throws him across the empty patch, away from the houses. He hits the earth, rolls and rises with dirt smeared across his face.

The remaining scouter beeps beside Freeza's chair. The soldier turns its lens toward Goshin.

"Sixty-two thousand four hundred," he says, his voice cracking.

Freeza watches the house. "Such a considerate child. How very useful."

Dodoria roars and charges again. This time he feints high, then drops low and reaches for Goshin's tail.

Goshin twists his hips away. His tail is wrapped close, and Dodoria's hand closes on empty air. Goshin catches the wrist before Dodoria can pull it back and strikes the front of his armour with his other fist.

The plate caves inward. Dodoria flies backward and cuts a trench through the ground before stopping on one knee. He presses a hand to the damaged armour and looks up, breathing hard.

Goshin opens and closes his fist. The greater strength is still there after the throw and the strike, settled into his ordinary body along with everything he had before. His ki answers just as readily. He does not need to keep pushing against Dodoria to hold on to it.

Goshin's chest heaves. He is still angry enough to go after Dodoria, and the dent in the armour shows what his next blow could do. He hears a child cry out behind the wall and turns his shoulder toward the house instead.

"Stay away from them!" he shouts.

Dodoria bares his teeth. "Lord Freeza!"

The soldier whose scouter Muri broke has made it to the house while everyone watches the fight. He raises a hand toward the older villager behind Piccolo.

Gohan comes around the corner and catches the soldier's arm. He pulls it away from the villager, turns and sends the soldier sliding on his back toward the path. The shot escapes into an empty patch of earth.

"There are people behind that wall," Gohan says. "Don't fire toward it!"

The soldier pushes himself up, looks at Gohan and stays where he is.

Piccolo steps alongside Muri. "Can the rest of your people get away from the buildings?"

"Some are inside. Others went to work before these men came." Muri looks at the fallen soldier and then toward Freeza. "If we fly away, that last machine will find us."

"Then stay under cover for now." Piccolo looks toward the bank. "The two children are with our friend."

Muri's shoulders ease a little. "You look like us. Yet you came with them."

"I grew up on Earth," Piccolo says. "I came here to find out about this world."

"What is your name?"

"Piccolo. There is another man on Earth who looks like us. He remembers living alone there as a child, with a letter telling him to wait for his family. They never came."

Muri's expression changes. "Sent away from Namek?"

"He didn't know where he'd come from. I hoped someone here might be able to tell us."

A voice calls from inside the house. Muri answers in words too quick for Piccolo to follow. Two residents appear at a side window, and the elder points them toward the wall leading away from the path. One helps the other climb through. They stay below the sill and hurry around the building.

"I cannot answer your question now," Muri says to Piccolo. "But there are others who know more of our past than I do. If we live through this, we will ask them."

Piccolo almost answers. A crackle from the scouter by Freeza's chair turns them both toward the path.

## The Last Scouter

The soldier beside the chair touches his earpiece. A voice crackles from it. He listens, then raises his voice to report over the noise in the village.

"Zarbon has found the visitors' ship, Lord Freeza. It's under a shelf near the eastern inlet. He says it is intact and he's watching it."

"Tell him to keep it that way," Freeza replies.

The soldier relays the order. Goshin looks toward the bank. He cannot see Bulma from where he stands, but he hopes she heard.

Behind the rock, Bulma has heard the report. She grips her case. Their bags are aboard the ship, along with the controls and the aerial she needs for a long call. She cannot send a warning to Earth from this hollow with the equipment in her hands.

Dende looks at her. "Can they take your ship?"

"They can try." Her voice comes out sharper than she intends. She puts a hand on the rock beside him. "Zarbon doesn't know how to work it just because he found it. But he can still damage it."

Cargo peers at the path. "Are they going to hurt Gohan?"

Bulma watches Freeza in his chair. "They're going to try that too."

Dende picks up the cloth Cargo has dropped and presses it back against his knee. Cargo holds it this time.

On the path, Freeza turns his chair toward Gohan. "Your ship is safe for the moment. You and your brother will come with me, and your Namekian friend will tell me what he knows about the Dragon Balls."

"He came here to find his people," Gohan says. "He's not one of your men."

"Then perhaps he would like to keep those people alive."

Goshin comes back across the open ground. Dodoria remains on one knee behind him, one hand against his dented armour. Goshin stops on Gohan's left. His child's face and black spiked hair are so like Goku's that, for an instant, Gohan remembers their father standing across the clearing with his hands raised for a match. The eyes looking toward Freeza are their mother's.

"We're not going with you," Goshin says.

"No," Gohan adds. "And you aren't taking anyone from here."

Freeza's smile returns. "Such confidence. You have no idea what you're up against, do you?"

"We met some children whose home was attacked," Gohan says. "They knew your name."

Freeza studies Gohan. "Did they? How unfortunate for them."

Muri steps out from behind Piccolo. "You asked about a Dragon Ball. I guard one for my people. You will not have it."

Piccolo glances at him. Until now Freeza had only said he had heard of such objects. Muri's own answer is different, though Piccolo has not seen the ball or learned its rules.

"I see," Freeza says. "You will risk your village for it."

"You brought soldiers to my door. You have already put my village at risk."

Freeza's finger lifts. A narrow line of light shoots toward Muri.

Gohan moves first. He reaches the elder, catches the shot on his palm and turns his hand outward. The beam cuts into the unoccupied ground beside the path. A deep groove opens in the earth; Gohan closes his fingers and breaks the remaining light apart before it can reach the next house.

Muri stares at the gouge, then at Gohan. "You came back."

"I promised Dende I would."

A second point of light appears at Freeza's fingertip. This one is aimed past Gohan, toward the bank.

"Bulma!"

Goshin reaches the shot before it crosses the path. The beam slams into his joined hands. Its pressure drives him half a step back, breaking the ground beneath his boots. He bares his teeth and shouts as he braces, holding the narrow light in front of him instead of letting it scatter toward the houses.

Freeza watches without leaving his chair. The pressure increases. Goshin's ki rises to meet it; the new strength in his body, still present after the fight with Dodoria, supports his arms as he shifts his footing. The light holds between them.

Then Freeza lowers his hand. The beam vanishes.

Goshin remains standing. Across the path, the edges of the broken earth slowly fall into the two impressions his boots have made.

"You may be worth more than answers," Freeza says.

His chair descends. Freeza steps out and sets both feet on the path, his pale tail curving behind him beneath the purple plates of his armour. He has shown no sign of strain from the exchange.

The pressure Gohan felt from him at the bank becomes clearer. Piccolo feels it too. He tells Muri and the older villager to move back along the house; Muri calls to the residents inside to keep away from the front wall.

Gohan comes to stand beside Goshin. Behind the rock, Bulma keeps the children low and the recorder in her pocket. At the inlet, Zarbon watches the closed ship. None of those places is safe yet.

Goshin looks at his brother. Gohan is frightened, but he does not step back.

"I'll watch his right," Gohan says.

"I'll take the left."

Freeza folds his hands behind his back and smiles at them.
