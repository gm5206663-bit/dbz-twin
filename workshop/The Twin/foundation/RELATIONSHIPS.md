**Checkpoint:** end of the revised Chapter 19 review draft. These are observable story relationships, not invented scores, diagnoses or accepted history. Chapter 20 is a later unaccepted draft on hold; earlier records remain historical.

## Chapter 19: current review changes and promises

- **Twins:** Goshin teaches Gohan stance, fighting, ki and the Kamehameha. Gohan makes real progress, feints Goshin, chooses his shots and later opts into the Piccolo exchange; Goshin asks him to show the feint. Goshin’s capability is not diminished, and their cooperation does not establish a numeric ranking.
- **Chi-Chi and the twins:** she accompanies their training, applies the earlier full-moon warning and limits the exchange; each boy chooses to participate. She listens to Gohan rather than granting Piccolo custody.
- **Piccolo and family:** Piccolo independently comes to the clearing, warns Chi-Chi to keep the boys sheltered and considers destroying the machine if necessary. He helps identify the image and accepts one bounded exchange. He leaves independently; future teaching, a truce or permanent partnership is not guaranteed.
- **Bulma and family:** she covers and transports the unidentified machine with the expressed intention of studying it. Its lab arrival, diagnosis and ownership remain unknown.
- **Children and C-6:** the telephone call confirms Pigero's permitted visit. Longer placement remains undecided, and Mina's book remains with C-6 until he finishes; no permission for onward lending or completed repair is shown.
- **Goku and family:** he has reached the heavy planet, but no news reaches home on-page. The promised rematch and wave display await his return. His question to the blue man is not yet answered.

- **Ox-King and family:** he is at breakfast, mends the gate and sees the boys display their practice that evening. No later travel or knowledge of the machine is shown.
