Goshin's established ×10 produces an actual advantage over first-form Freeza, and a ship-side scouter records the amplified output at **1,100,000**. On the same scale, that implies a selected unboosted equivalent of 110,000 at that instant, not a separate direct unboosted reading, a quantified permanent gain or a maximum. ## What the sources show

- The [official Kaiō-ken overview](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_277.html) describes a technique that controls and temporarily amplifies ki, increasing power, speed, destructive capability and defense. In that source account Goku is the first to use it successfully. The retained Japanese Z 29 captions likewise discuss control and amplification; King Kai warns **that Goku at that point** not to push beyond double. The same caption collection explicitly has Goku use ×2 and ×3 in Z 30 and ×4 in Z 31. This warning is not an eternal limit on every user (`C308`, `C309`).
- The later retained Z 90–92 cues identify Goku using ×10 against Freeza and attempting a ×20 Kaiō-ken Kamehameha. His body and remaining energy suffer in that particular confrontation. A separate [official manga-volume retrospective](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_3864.html) also describes ×10 and ×20 against Freeza, who withstands the attempt (`C263`, `C315`). The context, opponent and preparation differ from the branch. These examples prove that “×3 is the technique’s maximum” is wrong; they do not make ×20 a universal ceiling or a compulsory first step.
- [Kanzenshuu’s investigation of ×100 in the Slug film](https://www.kanzenshuu.com/rumor/goku-kaio-ken-x100/) identifies a contemporary promotional poster and a later digest narration, while reporting that the Japanese film dialogue itself names Kaiō-ken without saying ×100. Promotional/film material, the TV episodes, the manga and later continuities must not be collapsed into a single indisputable scene or one ceiling (`C316`).
- The [official Ginyu profile](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_2068.html) describes an on-scouter rise to 180,000 with Kaiō-ken and Ginyu’s tactical Body Change after Goku demonstrates superior power. A specific reading, the ability to control a body, and the consequences of an opponent’s special technique are distinct. The branch need not reproduce that encounter (`C256`).

The newly inspected SRT cues are selections from already-retained files, not new complete-episode readings. Their original source and cut are unverified; no full episode audio/video was reviewed here. Official features and the independent source investigation are separately identified, and summaries are not substitutes for the complete underlying media.

## Apply the technique to Goshin, not a generic copy of Goku

Goku teaches Goshin directly in Chapter 32; he uses ×2 and ×3 there and practices a restrained ×2 in Chapter 33. Those were **shown uses**, not a maximum, and there is no mandatory ×4 → ×5 → ×10 ladder. His rapidly developing whole-being Talent, real retained strength and ki, work on control, hybrid inheritance, experience and training all matter. They need not produce a fixed rate, a compulsory injury, an invented waiting period or a precise numerical ceiling. This does not mean every possible multiplier has already been achieved, that any particular use is automatically effortless, or that he is immune to every consequence.

The author asked that existing abilities be mastered or perfected before deliberate pursuit of new techniques; pushing and refining an already learned Kaiō-ken is application of that technique, not automatically an unrelated new ability. Emergency instinctive learning remains possible where the existing direction allows it. A temporary multiplier and Goshin’s independently retained development are different: the former is not permanent ×10 strength, and the latter must not disappear when a boost ends.

In the **revised unaccepted Chapter 40**, Goshin deliberately selects more unboosted output than the soldier’s earlier **68,600** measurement and uses **×10**, adapting to the rush while fighting first-form Freeza. The factor multiplies the chosen **unboosted** amount, not his previous ×3 state; ×10 is an authored use, not an author-prescribed limit. This branch’s explicit rule that he may choose a held-back output for the technique comes from its own training scene; the source overview’s broad description of controlling all the body’s ki is not by itself proof of every possible held-back application in every version.

The arithmetic has consequences. If the earlier 68,600 display and the tenfold stated factor use the same battle-power scale, and the selected unboosted output is greater than that displayed amount as the scene says, the boosted result is **greater than 686,000**. That is a conditional inference from the written branch, **not a new scouter measurement or an exact value**. It exceeds Freeza’s stated 530,000 in that first-form context if his claim is used as the comparison. The fight must show a real advantage, rather than use “unknown maximum” to erase the difference; it now does. Freeza resists, raises ki and reacts, but cannot push boosted Goshin back in that exchange. The claim is not a measured current output, and it must not be treated as the power of all Freeza’s forms or proof of every later result. No functioning village scouter is present for the tenfold use; do not invent a new display or automatic transmission.

## Connected abilities and people

- **Kamehameha and control:** Goshin has long used, aimed and steered his own wave, combines it with flight and can handle multiple pieces of matter with ki. Both brothers have their own established wave; Gohan acts independently. The [official early Goku technique feature](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_140.html) describes concentrating and releasing ki and Goku learning by observation in that source; it does not hand every later technique to either branch boy or establish a universal charge time.
- **Great Ape and moon:** the [official Vegeta profile](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_3929.html) describes a Power Ball/artificial moon and the source Saiyans’ transformations. In this branch Goshin has already consciously controlled his first Great Ape and can make an artificial moon, although his complete self-made moon has not yet been shown. His form and hybrid inheritance may develop. Gohan also has a tail and his own experience; Goshin’s choice to warn him before making a moon here is situational, not a permanent prohibition, prescribed fear arc or proof that future ordinary-sized ape-power applications already exist.
- **Recovery and adaptation:** real rapid recovery, lasting body-and-ki growth, inherited qualities and technique control can interact. A temporary output rise is not itself proof of a permanent gain; recovery is not automatically a new near-death power increase, and injury is not an obligatory condition for this Talent. Do not reduce his whole-being development to copying, waiting for a hit, or a fixed progression.
- **Gohan:** the [official Saiyan-arc profile](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_503.html) illustrates his own potential, Masenko and Great Ape in its source context. Branch Gohan has his own training, courage, choices and growth; he has not been shown activating Kaiō-ken. A large current gap should shape his tactics rather than erase his agency or make him equal to a stronger opponent without a cause. Goshin is not less capable than him under the author’s direction.
- **Freeza and other opponents:** the [first-form profile](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_576.html) associates that form with 530,000 and notes he can transform. A later source claim over one million belongs to a transformed state (`C256`), not the first-form measurement. The current Freeza may react intelligently and later use abilities or changes he actually has; neither an untouched first-form victory nor a mandatory transformation, death or source outcome is predetermined.
