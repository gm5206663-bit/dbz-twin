# Chapter Twenty-Seven — The Space to Stand

*Review draft; not yet accepted.*

Vegeta's tail lashes over the broken ridge. Goshin steps inside its reach and catches the prince's descending fist.

Before, he caught that fist with both hands. Now one closes around Vegeta's wrist and stops it.

Vegeta pulls. Goshin keeps hold.

**THE SPACE TO STAND**

---

## Beyond the Tracks

The two enormous figures stand on opposite sides of a split in the quarry floor. Behind Goshin lie the bent tracks and the western wall. Nappa is sheltered in a gully beyond that wall, with Tien, Chiaotzu and Yamcha beside him. Krillin waits near the rock shelf; Gohan and Piccolo are by the spur where Goshin chose to look at the light.

Vegeta drives forward. His free hand strikes Goshin across the cheek, snapping his head sideways. Before the prince can pull his trapped arm away, Goshin plants his feet and pushes him back.

Stone grinds beneath Vegeta's heels. His other fist swings toward Goshin's ribs. Goshin turns his body, takes the blow on his forearm and twists Vegeta's wrist outward.

"Let go!"

Vegeta's tail whips around behind Goshin. Goshin releases the wrist and rises, letting the tail pass under his feet. He comes down on the empty side of the split and drives his palm into the prince's armour.

The impact throws Vegeta across the quarry. He catches himself in the air, skidding through a bank of dust above the northern slope. Part of the cracked breastplate hangs away from his chest.

Goshin follows. The size of his body doesn't stop him flying; he crosses the distance before Vegeta can regain his footing and lands between the prince and the western wall.

"You keep going after them," Goshin says. His great voice rolls back from the rock. "Fight me over here."

Vegeta bares his teeth. "You think you can tell me where to fight?"

"I just did."

A spark leaps from Vegeta's hand. Goshin turns it aside, but the prince is behind the flash. He catches Goshin across the shoulder and drives him through the upper edge of the slope.

The rock gives way. Goshin feels the hit all the way down his arm, and for a moment the sky swings past him. He stops his fall in midair. Below, a long shelf of stone is breaking loose toward the tracks.

Gohan flies out from the spur. He keeps his face below the artificial moon and reaches the falling shelf before it hits the ground. Blue light flares around his hands as he lifts one end.

"Piccolo! The other side!"

Piccolo stretches both arms across the gap. His hands catch a projecting edge, and together they turn the shelf away from the gully. It crashes onto the empty quarry floor, sending grit over the rails.

"The wall behind Nappa's starting to crack," Krillin calls. "We need to move him again!"

"Take him north along the wall," Piccolo says. "The ground is lower there."

Vegeta raises his hand toward Krillin. Gohan fires first. His short burst strikes the back of the prince's hand and knocks it wide; Vegeta's shot tears into the vacant slope instead.

"You again!"

Vegeta reaches for Gohan. The boy drops beneath the huge fingers and sends a second burst into the damaged armour under the prince's arm. Vegeta twists after him, and Goshin catches him around the shoulders.

Both rise above the northern slope. Vegeta punches backward, but Goshin holds on and carries him farther from the tracks. When he lets go, he is already in front of the prince again.

Vegeta meets him with both hands. Their palms strike, and the air cracks around them.

Goshin feels the strength he built during the fight before the change. The immense power of his first transformation moves through him as his body responds to it, adding to what he has already gained. His strength and ki keep growing through the exchange. He closes his hands over Vegeta's hands and pushes. The prince strains against him, but Goshin forces him backward through the air and down onto the bare slope.

The hillside folds under Vegeta's weight. Dust billows between them.

"How are you doing that?" Vegeta demands.

Goshin wipes his cheek with the back of one enormous hand. It comes away with a little blood. The prince is strong enough to hurt him, even now. Goshin grins at him.

"I'm fighting you."

He steps forward before the prince can rise.

Vegeta braces one hand against the hillside. With the other he tears loose a slab and hurls it toward the spur where Gohan is standing. Goshin reaches with his ki and catches the stone above the tracks. For an instant it hangs there.

Vegeta comes out beneath it. His fingers hook the end of Goshin's tail, but Goshin twists before the prince can close his hand. He catches Vegeta's reaching wrist. The prince drives an elbow into his side; Goshin's breath bursts out, but his grip holds.

Gohan sends a quick shot past them. It strikes the underside of the slab, lifting it clear of Vegeta's head. Goshin guides it down onto empty ground. He plants a foot and shoves Vegeta away before the prince can seize his tail again.

"I can move the next one myself," Gohan calls.

Goshin nods and keeps his eyes on Vegeta.

---

## The Gully

Yamcha looks up at the cracked wall. A line of pebbles is trickling down beside Nappa's shoulder.

"We can't leave him under that."

Tien takes Nappa beneath the arms. Yamcha lifts his legs, wincing as his cut sleeve pulls loose from his forearm. Chiaotzu rises ahead of them and peers around the next bend in the gully.

"There's room here, Tien."

They carry Nappa along the wall and lower him into the shallower cut. His head lolls against Yamcha's hand, but he is still breathing.

Krillin runs in after them. "He's lucky Gohan stopped when he did."

"Vegeta wasn't going to," Yamcha says.

Tien points to Nappa's scouter, still clipped to his armour. "Take that before he wakes."

Krillin unclips it and slips it into an outside pocket, away from the Senzu. He leaves its controls alone.

Chiaotzu sets a fallen stone against a gap in the wall so it will not roll down on them. Another shock comes through the ground. He looks up sharply.

"They're moving away from us."

Tien watches the dust beyond the ridge. "For now. Krillin, keep a path open to the shelf. If they come back this way, we'll move him again."

Krillin nods. He glances at the pouch inside his clothes, then turns toward the opening. The Senzu is still there.

## The Last Trip to the Gym

Popo lowers the carpet outside the school. The two people he and Puar helped from the upper floor climb down with the woman Chi-Chi brought from the doorway. A volunteer takes them inside, where more blankets have been spread across the gym floor.

"There were still people on the next street," Chi-Chi tells her. "An injured man and the others from the bus came here on the earlier trips."

"The officers are sending another vehicle," the volunteer says. "We've got your description of the street."

She opens a notebook. "Were all the rooms in that building checked?"

"Not all of them," Chi-Chi says. "We couldn't get past the broken stairs. Popo brought down the two people who answered from the window, but someone should look again."

The volunteer writes it down and points out the building to another woman at the telephone.

Chi-Chi looks back toward the road. A police car is arriving at the junction, its lights turning against the walls. The officer inside stops to speak to another volunteer before driving on.

Bulma is already at the telephone in the gym office. The line rings twice before someone answers.

"It's Bulma. Are the children still with you?"

She listens, one hand braced against the desk. Chi-Chi comes to the door.

"All six? Good. Yes, I remember the hall. Keep them there until you hear otherwise." Bulma's shoulders ease a little. "Tell Hatch the boys haven't forgotten their visit. I'll call again when I can."

She puts down the receiver and faces Chi-Chi. "They're together with the director. She hasn't heard anything new about the fight."

"Thank you for calling."

Bulma calls Kame House next. Roshi answers, with Oolong close enough to ask whether Yamcha has come back.

"Not yet," Bulma says. "We've brought the people we found from this building, and the police are going to the next street. Chi-Chi and I are heading toward the quarry with Popo and Puar."

"What about that light?" Chi-Chi asks from the door.

"There's something above the quarry that looks like a full moon," Bulma tells Roshi. "We can't see the boys from here."

Roshi is quiet. "They know not to look at a full moon. Be careful getting close, Bulma."

"We will. I'll call you when we know more."

A volunteer approaches with a folded blanket. "Take this. If you find anyone hurt, keep them warm until help gets there."

Chi-Chi accepts it and carries it out to the car. Bulma follows, fastening the office door behind them so it doesn't swing into the passage.

Puar hovers beside the carpet, looking west. "Can we see Yamcha from there?"

"I hope so," Bulma says. She opens the driver's door. "Popo, can you tell where they are?"

"I can feel them near the quarry."

Chi-Chi gets in with the blanket across her lap. "We'll stop before we reach the fighting, like we said."

Popo nods. He and Puar rise on the carpet as Bulma takes the car above the roofs. From this height, the damaged street is visible behind them: the bus at the kerb, the open junction and the police car moving toward the people still waiting.

Farther west, a pale light hangs above a cloud of dust.

## The Road Below

Launch slows as a truck ahead pulls across the road. Its driver climbs out and waves at the cars behind him.

"The bridge is blocked! Take the lower road!"

A loose length of metal is wedged beneath one of his wheels. He pulls at it, but it won't come free. Launch stops behind him and gets out.

"Yajirobe, give me a hand."

"We're supposed to be going the other way."

"Then help me move it faster."

Yajirobe sets Upa's flask on the seat and follows her. The driver holds the wheel steady while Launch pulls at one end of the metal. Yajirobe lifts the other. It scrapes free and drops onto the verge.

The driver climbs back into his cab. "Thanks! The turn's just ahead, past the red shed."

Launch gets behind the wheel again. Yajirobe retrieves the flask before she starts.

The truck turns toward the lower road. Launch follows, leaving enough room for the cars behind her. The tower is no longer in sight, but the city has not appeared ahead yet.

Yajirobe looks through the windscreen at the distant light. "That's new."

"Did Korin say anything about it?"

"No. He felt the two strangers, that's all."

Launch tightens her hands on the wheel. "Then we'll ask Tien when we find him."

Yajirobe shifts his sword out of the way as the car takes the turn.

## A Voice on Snake Way

Goku runs along the inside of a long curve. The road ahead disappears into cloud; there is still no sign of Yemma's end.

"Goku!" King Kai's voice reaches him from the little planet.

Goku looks up without breaking stride. "Can you see them?"

"Yes. Both your boys are there. One of the Saiyans made a light in the sky. Your son Goshin looked at it."

Goku's foot catches the edge of the road. He rights himself and keeps running.

"Did he change?"

"He did. But listen to me: he's talking to Gohan, and he's protecting the others. He's fighting the Saiyan."

Goku breathes out. He remembers Roshi's account by the river, and the question he had asked about Grandpa. He had wanted the boys to know enough to stay safe; he had never been able to tell them what it would feel like.

"He's still himself?"

"I can hear him calling his brother by name. They're working together."

Relief gives Goku a brief, fierce grin. He wants to see them both for himself.

Goku's hand closes around the capsule inside his clothes. "And Chi-Chi?"

"I can see her and Bulma heading toward them. The fighting isn't over yet."

"All right. Keep watching, please."

He leans forward and runs harder. The clouds part around him, revealing another stretch of road, but not its end.

## At the Edge of the Quarry

Bulma slows the aircar above a low ridge east of the quarry. Popo's carpet draws alongside. The great cloud of dust ahead shifts, and a dark figure rises out of it.

Puar grips the edge of the carpet. "That's one of the Saiyans?"

The giant wears cracked armour. A second figure follows him out of the dust, catches his arm and turns him away from the western wall.

Chi-Chi leans forward. The second figure has a tail too. Its immense hand closes on the armoured Saiyan's wrist.

"Gohan!" the figure calls. "Are they clear?"

A much smaller voice answers from below. "Tien's moving them!"

Chi-Chi's hand closes on the blanket. "Goshin?"

The giant turns his head. For an instant she can see his eyes, and then he looks back at the prince.

"Mother! Stay back!"

He knows her. Chi-Chi's eyes sting. "All right, son," she whispers.

Bulma brings the car down behind the ridge. Popo lowers the carpet beside it, keeping Puar close. Chi-Chi gets out with the blanket, but she stays behind the rock and keeps the car between herself and the broken quarry floor.

Goshin is still holding Vegeta's wrist. The prince follows his glance toward the ridge.

"Your mother?" Vegeta says. "You brought her close enough to see this?"

Gohan, hovering below them, sees the light gather in Vegeta's open mouth. He flies toward the prince's face, but Vegeta sweeps his free arm across his path. Gohan checks his flight and drops under the hand.

"Bulma, move!" Chi-Chi calls.

Bulma throws the car into reverse. Popo lifts the carpet and takes Puar behind the ridge. Chi-Chi runs beside the car until Bulma can turn it away from the quarry.

"You drove my Galick Gun into the sky," Vegeta says. "Let's see you save her too."

He fires.

Goshin releases his wrist and brings both hands to his side. Blue light gathers between his palms.

"Kamehameha!"

His wave meets the prince's beam above the broken slope. The collision drives a hot wind over the ridge and rattles the car's windows. Goshin turns his hands upward, carrying both streams away from Chi-Chi and the others. Light tears into the sky and breaks apart above the empty ground.

Vegeta charges through the fading glare. He catches Goshin across the chest and drives him back toward the quarry floor. Goshin feels the edge of a broken wall under his heel and lifts his foot before it comes down on the car's side of the ridge.

Gohan flies between the two giants. He strikes Vegeta's bent elbow with both palms, knocking the reaching arm away from Goshin. Then he darts out beneath it as the prince turns on him.

Goshin catches Vegeta's shoulder and pulls him around. His next blow lands against the cracked armour. Vegeta is thrown back into the northern slope, where he lands on one knee.

Behind the ridge, Chi-Chi looks up at the enormous figure standing over him.

"He heard me," she says.

"And he heard Gohan," Popo says.

Puar lifts his head from the carpet. "Yamcha's still over there."

"We won't leave him," Bulma says. She keeps the engine running and looks toward the quarry again. "But we can't go through those two."

"Not yet," Chi-Chi agrees.

## Gohan's Shot

Vegeta pulls himself upright. He touches the broken edge of his armour, then looks from Goshin to Gohan hovering near the spur.

"Your brother can hide his face forever if he wants. I'll get to him eventually."

Gohan's tail curls tight around his waist. He keeps his eyes on the prince's feet, not the circle overhead.

"The light is still there," he says to Piccolo. "I can try again."

"He stopped your first shot."

"He was looking straight at me."

Gohan rises just enough to be heard across the quarry. "Goshin! If I hit the light, you might go back too!"

Goshin looks toward him. Vegeta lunges at once, and Goshin catches the prince's arm before answering.

"I don't know what will happen," Goshin calls. He likes the vast strength rushing through him, but he looks once toward the ridge where his mother is hiding. "I don't want him shooting at Mother again. Try it!"

He pulls Vegeta away from the spur. The prince drives a knee into his side; Goshin grunts, shifts his grip and carries him toward the open slope.

Gohan turns to Piccolo. "Can you see it from here?"

Piccolo steps beside him and looks up. "Yes. Keep your face down."

He puts a hand against Gohan's forearm and directs it past the edge of the spur. Gohan sets his feet, then brings his hands together at his side. The bright circle is above and behind his lowered face.

"A little left," Piccolo says. "There's a clear line."

Vegeta hears them. He tears one arm free and lifts it toward the sky, but Goshin seizes it again. Vegeta's tail sweeps across the stone toward Gohan. Goshin rises with him, dragging the tail clear of the spur before it reaches his brother.

"You'll give up that power just to help them?" Vegeta roars.

"I took it to help them!" Goshin says, and turns him toward the empty hillside.

Gohan gathers his ki. The blue light between his hands grows, bright against the stone at his feet. He remembers practising the moving shot in the room with Goshin, aiming through the gap between the blocks. This gap is wider, but he cannot look at the target. He listens to Piccolo and sets his hands for the shot.

"Now!" Piccolo says.

"Kamehameha!"

Gohan thrusts his hands forward and keeps his eyes down. The wave passes the edge of the spur and climbs.

Vegeta sees it. He opens his mouth to fire, but Goshin pulls his shoulders around. The prince's beam cuts across the empty slope while Gohan's wave reaches the pale circle.

The artificial moon brightens. Its round edge breaks into streaks. A white flash spreads across the sky, then the light thins and scatters until nothing remains above the quarry but daylight.

Goshin feels his grip change. Vegeta's arm is getting smaller in his hands. The prince twists free as both bodies begin to shrink.

"Gohan!" Goshin calls.

"I'm here!"

The ground rushes upward. Goshin catches himself in the air; his hands are small again, and the broken tracks lie below him. Pieces of his torn clothes are scattered across the stone where he first changed.

Vegeta descends on the other side of the slope. His armour has contracted with him, though the crack remains across its chest. His tail is still there.

Goshin's own tail moves behind him. He looks up. The artificial light is gone.

"You destroyed it," he says.

Gohan lifts his face carefully and sees only the ordinary sky. "Both of you changed back."

Vegeta lands hard. He looks at the empty sky, then at the brothers.

"You'll pay for that."

## One More Attack

Chi-Chi is already coming around the ridge with the blanket when Piccolo lifts a hand to stop her. Vegeta has not lowered his guard.

The prince shoots toward Gohan. Gohan meets him above the tracks, turning a punch away from his face and catching the next against his forearm. Vegeta drives a knee toward his stomach. Gohan twists aside, and the blow glances off his hip.

A burst of light forms in Vegeta's hand. Gohan catches his wrist before he can point it toward the ridge.

"Stop!"

Vegeta forces his arm around. Gohan holds it above them, away from Chi-Chi, but the prince's other hand comes up between them.

Goshin reaches them before the second shot fires. He catches Vegeta's free wrist and pulls him away from Gohan.

The prince kicks at him. Goshin lets the kick pass, turns with it and drives his palm against the damaged breastplate. The armour caves farther inward. Vegeta flies across the empty ground, strikes the slope and falls to one knee.

Goshin follows. He is small again, but his body has kept the strength it found in the fight and in that first change. His arms answer with far more power, and his ki rises with them. He is much stronger than he was before he looked at the light.

Piccolo feels the difference. Goshin has returned to his ordinary size, but his ki is far greater than it was before the transformation.

Goshin lands in front of Vegeta and raises his hands.

"You can stop now."

Vegeta looks up at him. Blood has reached the corner of his mouth. Beyond Goshin, Gohan lands beside the tracks, ready to move again.

"I am the prince of the Saiyans," Vegeta says. "You think I'll surrender to Kakarot's children?"

He fires at the ground between them. Rock bursts upward in a sheet. Goshin lifts clear, shielding his face, while Vegeta comes through the dust toward Gohan.

Gohan meets him. He catches the prince's fist and turns it aside, but Vegeta drives through the opening and reaches toward Gohan's tail. Gohan steps inside the grasp, just as he did against Nappa, and strikes Vegeta's wrist. The hand opens.

Goshin comes down beside them. He grips Vegeta's shoulder and pulls him away from his brother. When the prince swings again, Goshin moves inside the blow and strikes him once in the chest.

Vegeta's eyes widen. The force carries him backward across the quarry, but Goshin flies after him and catches him before he reaches the jagged wall. He lowers the Saiyan onto the bare ground.

The prince tries to lift one hand. It falls against the stone. His eyes close.

Goshin stays beside him, watching his chest. After a moment it rises.

"He's breathing," Goshin calls.

Gohan lands on the other side of Vegeta. He keeps his hands ready until Piccolo arrives.

Piccolo crouches and checks the prince for himself. The scouter remains clipped to the damaged armour. He removes it without touching its controls and sets it on the stone out of Vegeta's reach.

"Raditz's scouter sent what it heard," he says. "We don't know what this one has already sent."

Gohan looks at the silent device. "We should keep watching him."

Krillin comes from the gully and stops near the prince. "I'll keep an eye on him."

"Stay far enough back to move," Piccolo says. He rises, keeping Vegeta in sight. "Don't mistake this for the end of the danger."

---

## The Blanket

Chi-Chi comes across the tracks as soon as Piccolo moves aside. She unfolds the blanket and wraps it around Goshin, covering him where his clothes have torn away. His tail slips out beneath the edge.

"Are you hurt?" Goshin asks, looking toward the car.

"The windows rattled, but we got behind the ridge." She touches his cheek. "Let me see your face."

Goshin holds still while she brushes dust from his cheek. There is blood there, and another mark across his shoulder where Vegeta struck him.

"Does it hurt anywhere else?"

"My side a little. I can still move it."

He lifts his arm to show her. The blanket slips, and Chi-Chi catches it before it falls.

"Slowly. You don't have to prove it all at once."

Gohan comes close, rubbing his shoulder. Chi-Chi draws him under her other arm and feels along it. He winces when her fingers find the sore place.

"You were both fighting him."

"Goshin stopped the big blast," Gohan says. "And I got the light."

"I saw you together." Her voice shakes once. She takes a breath. "I saw you turn it away from us."

Goshin looks at the great trenches his feet left in the quarry floor. "I could hear Gohan even when I was that big. I could feel where everybody was."

"Could you hear me?"

"Yes. You said my name."

Chi-Chi puts her arms around him. Goshin presses his face against her shoulder, holding the blanket closed with one hand. Gohan stays close on her other side.

"I saw you protecting people," she says. "I was so proud of you both—and so scared."

Gohan leans against her for a moment. "We're here now," he says.

"You were taller than the house," she says.

Goshin lifts his head. "Taller than Grandpa?"

Gohan looks at the deep footprints. "Much taller."

For a moment Goshin grins. Then he glances back at Vegeta, and his smile fades. "Is everybody all right?"

"We're finding out," Chi-Chi says. "Your father is still on his way."

"Does he know?" Gohan asks.

"I haven't spoken to him since the Lookout."

Puar flies ahead over the ridge. Popo comes toward them from the carpet. "I felt Yamcha and the others by the western wall. Puar has gone to find them."

Bulma climbs out of the car and hurries after Puar. She stops when she sees Yamcha coming around the shelf. His sleeve is torn, and dust covers his clothes, but he is on his feet.

"Yamcha!"

Puar reaches him first. Yamcha catches the little blue figure against his chest, and Bulma throws her arms around both of them.

"You could have called," she says into his shoulder.

"I was a little busy."

She pulls back enough to look at his arm. "You're bleeding."

"It's a cut. Chiaotzu and Tien are all right too."

"I'll decide how bad it is when I've cleaned it."

"She gets to decide?" Yamcha asks Puar.

"Yes," Puar says at once.

Yamcha starts to protest, sees Bulma's expression and lets her take his arm.

---

## The Man Who Wakes

A low sound comes from the gully. Tien turns at once. Nappa's fingers have moved against the gravel.

"He's waking up," Chiaotzu says.

Tien looks toward the tracks. "Gohan!"

Gohan releases Chi-Chi's hand. "I'll go."

He flies over the fallen slab and lands beside Tien. Nappa's eyes open a little, then close against the daylight. His breathing catches; he shifts one arm across his cracked armour.

"Vegeta?"

"He's alive," Gohan says. "He's over there."

Nappa turns his head. He can see the prince lying beyond the tracks, with Piccolo and Goshin between him and the quarry wall. His expression hardens. He starts to push himself up.

Tien takes a step back, leaving Gohan a clear path. Gohan does not reach for Nappa, but he stands where the Saiyan can see him.

"Don't attack them," Gohan says.

Nappa's hand stops against the ground. "You."

"Yes."

"What did you do to the prince?"

"He kept fighting. We stopped him. And when you were unconscious, he shot at you. I turned the attack away."

Nappa stares at him. "The prince wouldn't—"

"I was carrying you when he fired," Piccolo says from the tracks. "Krillin was there too."

Nappa looks from Piccolo to Vegeta. He tries to rise farther, but his arm shakes and he sinks back against the stone. Gohan watches him, ready, while Chiaotzu moves out of reach beside Tien.

"In the city, you called me a Namekian," Piccolo says.

Nappa feels the empty place on his damaged armour. "My scouter?"

"Krillin took it while you were unconscious," Gohan says.

"It's mine."

"You can talk to us without it."

Nappa looks at the boy who defeated him, then toward the prince again.

Piccolo crouches at the mouth of the gully. "Tell me about Namek."

Nappa says nothing. Beyond the wall, the wind moves through the torn rails, and Vegeta remains where Goshin set him down.
