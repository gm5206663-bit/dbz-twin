# Chapter Twenty-Three — The Way Back

*Immersion-revised review draft; not yet accepted.*

*New review draft, continuing the unaccepted Chapter 22; not yet accepted.*

A page of pencilled days lies beneath a stone on the Lookout bench. A shirt with short sleeves rests beside it, folded over the strap of a bag.

**THE WAY BACK**

**NARRATOR:** Gohan and Goshin have spent a year together inside the Room of Spirit and Time. On Earth, roughly a day has passed. Their family is together again, but Goku is still far from home.

---

## A Visitor at the Lookout

Gohan slides the page of target marks beside Hatch's boat drawing. Chi-Chi leans over the bench while he points to the line that crosses the third square.

"I stopped the wave there," he says. "The target stayed standing."

"You stopped it before it reached the bottom," Chi-Chi says.

"Goshin brought a piece of the first target we used in the room."

Goshin takes the cut piece of stone from his bag. Chi-Chi turns it in her hands, feeling the smooth edge with her thumb.

"What made this cut?"

"My wave. On the other side, there's a mark from our first week," Goshin says. "Gohan turned the target around so we could keep using it."

"You kept a piece of that too."

Gohan picks up the shirt from the bag. He holds it against himself, with the sleeves ending above his wrists.

"We kept these so you could see."

Chi-Chi takes the shirt and lays it over her lap. Goshin stands beside Gohan, his black hair rising in the same wild spikes as Goku's. A year has lengthened both boys' arms and legs, but the likeness in Goshin's face remains, with Chi-Chi's eyes looking back at her.

"I'll have to check everything in your cupboard," she says. "I don't think those sleeves will reach either."

"There are bigger ones," Gohan says. "You packed them."

"For the room. We'll need some for home too."

Gohan reaches into the bag and takes out a folded letter. The paper has softened along the crease where he has opened it more than once.

"I wrote this when I was missing you."

Chi-Chi holds out her hand. "May I read it now?"

"When we get home," Gohan says. "I want to sit beside you."

She slips the letter into the bag. "We'll find a place for both of you."

Goshin leans against the bench. "I drew the board at the bottom. Gohan put you next to it."

"Then the letter is from both of you," Chi-Chi says. She touches the folded page before closing the bag.

A rush of air moves the loose corner of Hatch's drawing. Goshin catches it before the page can lift from the bench.

Piccolo lands on the far side of the tiles. His eyes go straight to Gohan, then Goshin.

"I felt you both the instant you came back. Your ki is enormous. Both of you."

Gohan looks up at him. "We were training in a room where time moves differently."

"For how long?"

"A whole year. Both of us."

"And about a day out here," Chi-Chi says, folding the shirt into the bag.

Piccolo looks from one boy to the other. "You were strong at the clearing. This is far beyond that."

Goshin grins. "We worked hard. I want to show you!"

Chi-Chi closes the bag, smiling at the boys' excitement. "You've been away for a year, and you're already planning another match? All right. I want to see what you've learned. Then we're having a proper meal together."

Gohan looks up at Piccolo. "There's something I want to show both of you."

Goshin puts the drawing beneath the stone. "And I want a turn after him."

Piccolo glances toward the open sky. "I won't fight either of you as though you were still at the clearing."

Chi-Chi looks at the boys, then at the edge of the Lookout. Mr. Popo picks up the second bag from beside the bench.

"There is room away from the edge," Popo says. "I will move the bench."

He places both bags on the bench, then lifts it beneath the trees, keeping the loose papers under the stone.

---

## The Old Exercises

On King Kai's planet, Goku tightens the weighted bands around his wrists. Bubbles sits beneath the tree, holding the end of a low branch.

"Ready?" King Kai asks.

Bubbles lets go and springs across the grass. Goku moves at the same instant and catches him around the middle before he can reach the tree.

"Got you!"

Bubbles kicks once, then settles against Goku's arm. Goku puts him down beside King Kai.

"That took less time than it used to," Gregory says from the roof.

"I should hope so," King Kai says. "Bubbles spent three weeks running from him."

Goku looks up. "Your turn?"

Gregory rises from the roof. "You still need the hammer."

Goku takes it from the hook. He lifts the head clear of the grass and waits for Gregory to fly beyond King Kai's car.

"I'm not standing on your car," Gregory says.

"Then where are you going?"

Gregory darts over the tree. Goku is already moving. He brings the heavy hammer around in one clean swing and taps Gregory on the back.

Gregory drops onto the grass. He sits up before Goku can reach him.

"You hit me already?"

"Are you all right?"

"Yes. I thought I'd have more time to get away."

King Kai examines the hammer. "And my car is still where I left it."

Goku hangs the hammer on its hook again. The bands pull at his arms as he rolls his shoulders.

"Is it time to test the Genki-dama?"

King Kai walks to the open ground beside the house.

"Let's see how much you can do with it. Come over here."

---

## Hands to the Sky

King Kai stands beside Goku and points upward.

"Tell me again where the Genki-dama gets its power."

"A little from everything that can share it," Goku says. "The grass, the tree, even the air."

"And people and animals. Don't drag the energy away. Ask, and let it gather."

Goku plants his feet and raises both hands. Bubbles comes to stand beside him and holds his arms up too. A light gathers above Goku's palms; Bubbles looks up at his own empty hands.

The sphere grows, casting light over Goku's face and the grass at his feet. Gregory lands on the fence while Goku waits for more energy. When he turns toward King Kai, the sphere stays together above his palms.

"What do I aim at?" he asks.

King Kai walks to the house and returns with a large brick. He holds it at his side while Gregory moves away from the open ground.

"I'll throw this as fast as I can. Wait until it's clear of the planet, then hit it."

"All right."

King Kai throws the brick upward. It climbs above the tree and keeps rising. Goku turns with it, holding the gathered light over his head.

"Now!" King Kai calls.

Goku sends the sphere after the brick. The light strikes it above the planet, and the brick breaks apart. Goku lowers his arms as the pieces scatter into the distance.

Bubbles claps. Gregory follows the fragments with his eyes.

"That was excellent," King Kai says. "I didn't expect you to use it so well."

"It took a lot of practice!"

King Kai looks up at the scattered pieces. "And one perfectly good brick."

King Kai comes to stand in front of him. "Remember what you saw. That was only a little energy from a very small planet. Earth has far more life, and the sun can add to it."

Goku's smile fades. "It could be a lot bigger."

"Big enough to harm the world you're trying to protect. Don't use it carelessly. If there is another way, take it."

"And if there isn't?"

"If there is no other way, you may use it once. Gather what you need, and be certain where you send it."

Goku looks at his hands. "I'll try to stop them with the Kaiō-ken first."

King Kai starts to answer, then turns his face toward the sky. His antennae rise.

---

## A Voice at Kame House

King Kai stays still for several moments. Goku waits beside him while Bubbles pulls at the grass.

"What's wrong?" Goku asks.

"The Saiyans are much closer to Earth than they were."

"How close?"

"Close enough that your friends need to know now."

Goku looks toward the edge of the planet. Snake Way lies below the clouds.

"Then I have to go."

King Kai's hands go to his cheeks. "The road."

"What about it?"

"I was watching their journey. I didn't allow enough for yours back along Snake Way."

Goku stares at him. "You forgot I had to go back?"

"I made a mistake. You can move much faster now, but it's still a long road."

Goku looks down at the pale curve winding away beneath the planet. The road he crossed so eagerly stretches back through the clouds; somewhere beyond its far end are Chi-Chi, the boys and everyone else waiting for him.

"Can you send me straight to Earth?"

"Not from here. You must reach Yemma's end of the road. Kami can help you through the passage when you get there."

Goku looks down again. "They might get there before I do."

"They might. Your friends must prepare without waiting for you."

"How do I tell them?"

King Kai turns his back. "Put one hand on me and think of someone on Earth. I'll carry your voice."

Goku rests his hand between King Kai's shoulders. "Master Roshi? It's Goku. Can you hear me?"

At Kame House, Roshi lifts his head from the table. The cup in his hand tips, and he catches it before tea reaches the edge.

"Goku?"

Oolong looks up from the floor. "Who are you talking to?"

"Be quiet a moment."

Goku's voice reaches Roshi again. "I'm with King Kai. He can help us talk."

"You found him! Chi-Chi will want to hear that."

"Tell her I'm all right. The two Saiyans are close, though. King Kai says I have to hurry back, but I don't know if I'll reach Earth before them."

Roshi sets down the cup. "How much time do we have?"

"He can't give us an exact time. Please tell everybody to be ready."

"I'll call Bulma. She may know where Chi-Chi is."

"And Kami. Ask him if he can meet me at Yemma's end of the road."

"We'll get word to him."

Oolong pulls himself upright. "Is that really Goku?"

"It is."

Goku's voice comes again. "I'm going as fast as I can, Master Roshi. Tell Chi-Chi and the boys I want to see them."

"I will. Take care on that road."

Roshi waits, but Goku does not answer. He reaches for the telephone and begins turning the dial.

---

## What Gohan Can Do

The eight training blocks Goshin set beside Popo after leaving the room remain on the tiles. Popo carries two out to mark the practice space. Chi-Chi helps him set them down, then stands where she can see the open sky beyond the Lookout.

"Show us that faster turn," she tells Gohan. "The one you made when you came out."

Gohan rises above the first marker and sweeps into the open air.

"That's much faster than it was at home," Chi-Chi says, following his turn with a smile.

Piccolo removes his turban and cape and sets them beside the blocks. The weighted cloth lands with a heavy thud. He looks up at Gohan.

"I can feel how much stronger you are. Let's go farther out, and don't slow down just to match me."

Gohan nods and flies past the edge of the Lookout. Piccolo follows until there is open sky behind both of them. His ki rises around him, and he rushes Gohan without holding his speed back.

Gohan stops the first punch with an open hand. The impact sends a ring through the clouds behind him, but he does not lose his position. Piccolo kicks from the side; Gohan slips past his foot and touches his shoulder before Piccolo can turn.

Piccolo draws back, eyes narrowed—not in anger, but in concentration.

"Again," he says.

He comes in low, then stretches his arm toward Gohan's wrist. Gohan turns inside the reaching hand and guides it past him. Piccolo sends two quick bursts after him and cuts across their path. Gohan knocks the first blast upward, avoids the second and appears beside Piccolo before the extended arm has drawn back.

His palm stops a finger's width from Piccolo's ribs.

Piccolo glances at it. "You didn't need to hit me."

"I didn't want to hurt you."

Piccolo looks at Gohan's face, then grins. "Good. You remembered the arm too."

"You reached for me with it when we were on Nimbus. Goshin and I kept practising what to do if somebody grabbed us."

They move higher, with the Lookout behind them. Gohan points toward a stretch of clear sky above the clouds.

"Can I show you my wave there?"

"Go ahead. I'll stay clear of it."

Gohan cups his hands at his side. His ki rises much higher than it did while he was sparring. He fires upward, and the broad Kamehameha opens a long passage through the clouds. Gohan turns the stream, holds it clear of Piccolo and the Lookout, and stops it with a movement of his hands.

The light fades. Gohan lowers his arms and flies back beside Piccolo as easily as he left.

"You kept all that out of our match," Piccolo says.

"I wanted you to see it without being in the way."

Piccolo folds his arms. Even at his fastest he had not forced the boy to struggle. "You've learned more than how to make a bigger wave."

Gohan smiles toward the Lookout. "Goshin, it's your turn!"

---

## Goshin's Turn

Goshin lifts off beside his brother. "That turn was even quicker than it was in the room!"

"I want to see yours," Gohan says, and returns to the Lookout.

Chi-Chi watches him land. "You didn't let Piccolo's reach send you straight backward."

"We practised that too."

Out above the clouds, Piccolo raises his hands. "Ready?"

Goshin grins and lifts his guard. Piccolo closes in at once, striking high and then low. Goshin turns both blows aside and slips behind him. Piccolo twists, firing a short burst to cover his turn; Goshin sends it up into the empty sky with his palm.

Piccolo comes after him again, faster than he moved at the clearing. He changes direction twice, trying to catch Goshin between the turns. Goshin follows without losing ground, then lets more of his ki flow and slips inside Piccolo's next turn.

Piccolo thrusts forward. Goshin is already behind him. He lays two fingers against Piccolo's shoulder and takes them away before Piccolo can finish turning.

"You got faster while we were moving," Piccolo says.

"We practised those turns in the room. Can I show you something else?"

The eight blocks rise from the Lookout and sweep into the open air below them. Goshin sends each along a different path, clear of Piccolo and the platform. He cups his hands, aims high into the empty sky and fires a broad Kamehameha.

The wave lights the clouds above them. Goshin turns it through a wide arc, then narrows and stops it. The blocks keep moving throughout; he brings them back to the Lookout, setting the two markers in their places and the other six beside Popo.

The light around Goshin settles, and he eases his ki back down as he returns beside Piccolo.

"You were holding even more back than I thought," Piccolo says.

"Gohan and I practised using it in the room. He helped me keep the blocks apart."

Piccolo looks toward Gohan. "Neither of you needed all of it against me. Let's see what happens when you practise with each other."

---

## Together

Gohan flies out to join them. Piccolo moves aside, giving the brothers the open space beyond the Lookout.

"One round?" Gohan asks.

"All right!"

Gohan darts in first. He feints toward Goshin's shoulder, drops beneath his guard and turns sharply upward. Goshin follows; their hands meet, and the impact rolls across the clouds. Gohan twists out of reach and comes back from the other side before Piccolo has finished turning to follow them.

Goshin catches his wrist. Gohan rolls with the hold, slips free and throws a quick punch toward his brother's chest. Goshin stops it with his palm, then moves again, faster than before. Gohan turns to meet him, but Goshin is already at his side, his hand resting lightly against Gohan's shoulder.

Gohan laughs. "You changed direction again!"

"You nearly got through when you turned. Let's try that one more time later."

They fly back to the Lookout and land beside Chi-Chi, both breathing easily. Piccolo follows and puts on his weighted cape and turban.

Chi-Chi puts an arm around each boy. "Then your father has a whole year of stories to catch up on."

Goshin laughs. "I want to see what Father learned too!"

Before Piccolo can answer, Kami lifts his head.

---

## What a Wish Can Do

At Kame House, Roshi holds the receiver against his ear. Oolong waits beside the table, listening to the sea through the open window.

"Bulma? Goku contacted me. He's alive and coming home, but the Saiyans are close."

At her laboratory, Bulma pushes a notebook away from the telephone. The machine brought from the coast remains covered on the other side of the room.

"How close?"

"He couldn't give me a time. He said we must be ready now."

"I left Chi-Chi at the Lookout. The boys should be out of the room by now. I'll go there."

"Tell her he's on his way. And tell Kami that Goku needs him at Yemma's end of Snake Way."

"I will."

After the call, Oolong pulls a chair beside Roshi.

"What about the Dragon Balls? Couldn't we ask Shenron to get rid of the Saiyans?"

"We don't have seven gathered," Roshi says. "And we can't spend the time we have pretending we do."

"Goku doesn't need a wish to come back, does he?"

"No. He's alive. What he needs is a clear road and people ready here."

Oolong looks at the telephone. "Can you call Krillin?"

"I don't know where he and Yamcha are training. I'll call the places I can reach."

Bulma picks up the receiver again and dials the children's home. The director comes to the telephone after the second ring.

"This is Bulma. I don't know where it will happen, but there may be danger soon. Can you get everyone away from the windows?"

"What kind of danger?"

"Two people are approaching Earth. That's all I can tell you for certain."

The director is quiet for a moment. "I'll speak to the children and bring them into the hall. Will you call if you learn more?"

"As soon as I can."

Bulma puts down the receiver and takes a capsule from the shelf. She leaves the covered machine where it is and runs outside.

---

## A Message for the Family

Kami stands with one hand on his staff. Chi-Chi comes toward him, with Gohan and Goshin close behind.

"What is it?" she asks.

"Someone is speaking to me."

Piccolo turns toward him. "Who?"

Kami raises one hand for silence. On the distant planet, Goku keeps his palm against King Kai's back.

"Kami?" Goku says. "Can you hear me?"

"I can."

"The Saiyans are close. I'm leaving now, but Snake Way is going to take time. Can you meet me at Yemma's office?"

"I can use the passage Yemma arranged. I cannot shorten the road for you."

"I know. I'll keep moving."

Kami looks at Chi-Chi. "Goku has reached King Kai. He's alive and preparing to leave. King Kai has told him the Saiyans are close."

Chi-Chi takes a step closer. "Is he hurt?"

Kami relays the question. After a pause, he looks back at her.

"He says he isn't. He asks where you and the boys are."

"Here, with me. Tell him we came out of the training room. Both boys are well, and they've grown."

Kami closes his eyes. Gohan and Goshin wait beside Chi-Chi while he passes the answer on.

"He asks which room," Kami says.

Gohan steps closer to Kami. "It's a room where time goes differently. Goshin and I trained together for a whole year. Tell him we have a lot to show him."

Kami passes that on too. His eyes widen, and a smile reaches his face.

"He says, 'That room? I couldn't stay a whole month when I was little! I can't wait to see how strong you are!' He remembers promising Gohan another match."

Gohan smiles. "I remember. Can you tell him about the boat?"

"He hasn't met Hatch," Chi-Chi says. "You'll have to tell him whose boat it is."

"It's a drawing Hatch made. We have some friends we want him to meet."

Kami carries the message. Gohan waits while Kami listens to the answer.

"He says he'll be glad to meet them," Kami says.

Goshin looks toward Kami. "Can he come here now?"

"He must travel back along Snake Way first. I will meet him when he reaches Yemma's office."

"How long?" Chi-Chi asks.

"I don't know. King Kai will tell me when Goku is near the end."

On King Kai's planet, Goku keeps his hand on the blue coat and looks toward the road.

"Tell Chi-Chi I love her. Tell Gohan and Goshin too. I'll get home as fast as I can."

Kami's hand tightens on his staff. "Goku says he loves you and will get home as quickly as he can."

Chi-Chi looks down at the boys. "Tell him we'll be waiting."

Kami relays the words. When he opens his eyes again, he turns toward the edge of the Lookout.

"The connection has ended."

Piccolo steps beside him. "Did King Kai say where the Saiyans will land?"

"No. He knows they are close, not where they will come down."

Goshin stands beside Gohan, watching the open sky.

---

## The Road Begins

Goku takes his hand from King Kai's back. Bubbles has brought the hammer from beside the house and is trying to lift it by the handle.

"I'll leave these with you," Goku says. He removes the weighted bands and sets them beside the hook.

"You'll move more easily without them," King Kai says.

Goku checks that his supply capsule is secure inside his clothes. He crouches beside Bubbles, takes the hammer before it can fall across his foot and lays it on the grass by the house.

"You can keep practising too."

Bubbles makes a noise and takes hold of Goku's hand. Gregory comes down from the roof and settles on the fence.

"Watch out for the edge of the road," Gregory says. "There's no point in going fast if you fall off."

"I won't."

King Kai walks with Goku toward the curve of the little planet. Snake Way stretches far below them.

"Those Saiyans are dangerous," King Kai says. "Don't assume you know what either one can do."

"I won't."

"And remember what I said about the Genki-dama."

"Once, and only if there's no other way."

King Kai nods. Goku looks back at the tree and the house.

"Thanks for teaching me. All of you."

Gregory lifts one hand. Bubbles waves both arms.

"Come back when you have time for another joke," King Kai says.

"I'll think of a better one on the road."

"It will have to be better."

Goku rises from the grass and flies down toward Snake Way. He lands on the broad path, bends his knees and starts running. The road curves away through the clouds, and his steps carry him around the first bend.

King Kai watches until Goku has gone from sight.

---

## The Hall

At the children's home, the director opens the door to the common room. Hatch and Ena are drawing on the large boat page. Chiko sits beside Rom while Jink finds a place for Yordon's cup on the table.

"Bulma just called," the director says. "There may be trouble outside. I want everyone in the hall, away from the windows."

Rom looks at Chiko. "Do we have to leave the room?"

"For now. Bring what you want to keep with you."

Hatch lifts the drawing by two corners. Ena holds the other side while he folds it along an existing crease.

"Gohan's page is underneath," Ena says.

Hatch puts both pages together. Yordon takes his cup, and Jink follows the others to the door.

Chiko waits for Rom before going into the hall. The director closes the common-room door behind them and leads them toward the inside wall.

---

## A Place to Meet

Bulma's aircar lands on the Lookout. Chi-Chi meets her before she has finished opening the door.

"Master Roshi told me," Bulma says. "Goku is on his way, but the Saiyans may get here first."

"Kami heard from him too. We still don't know where they'll land."

"I called the children's home before I left. The director said she'd take them into the hall. I'll call again when I can."

Gohan looks at her. "Did you speak to Hatch?"

"No. I spoke to the director."

He nods and looks toward his bag beneath the trees. "My roof page is at home. I told him I'd bring it."

"We'll call when we can," Chi-Chi says. "You can tell him then."

Bulma rests her hand on the open car door. "Could we use the Dragon Balls? Oolong asked Roshi about a wish."

Kami turns toward her. "Shenron cannot grant a wish beyond the power of the one who made him. I cannot promise that he could defeat these Saiyans."

"And we don't have all seven here," Bulma says.

"No."

Popo brings the boys' bags beneath the shelter by the passage. He sets Gohan's target page and Hatch's drawing on top so they will not be bent, leaving the page of days beneath the stone on the bench.

Piccolo looks out over the clouds. "When they land, we'll feel them."

Chi-Chi turns to her sons. "I know you both want to help. Stay together. And if there's fighting, don't lead it toward a crowd."

"We'll try to get people clear," Gohan says.

Piccolo looks toward the clouds. "They may not give you time. Don't count on them following you away."

Goshin's tail curls around his leg. "Where will you be, Mother?"

"Helping people get out. I can do that without bringing the carpet into the middle of a fight."

Popo nods. "The carpet can carry more than the two of us."

Bulma reaches into the car, switches on the radio and closes the door. "I'll listen for where they land and find somewhere to pick people up. The car can carry anyone who can't walk."

Chi-Chi takes her hand. "We'll work away from the fighting and bring people to you. If we get separated, we can meet back here when it's safe."

Bulma squeezes her hand. "All right."

Gohan moves to the edge of the tiles and peers down through the clouds. Goshin joins him and looks toward the distant city.

"Father will have to find us," Gohan says.

"Kami knows where we are," Goshin answers.

## Two Lights

Later that morning in East City, a father walks beside his daughter along a wide pavement. She tugs at his hand when they reach a crossing.

"I'm hungry."

"We can get lunch after we cross."

She looks up at the buildings beyond the road. A low sound passes overhead. The people waiting at the crossing turn toward the sky.

Two lights descend between the clouds.

The father pulls his daughter against him. One light passes over the avenue and strikes the road beyond the crossing. The second hits farther along the street.

The pavement bucks. A window breaks above them, and dust rolls between the buildings. The father carries his daughter into a doorway while people run past on both sides.

A car alarm sounds. Someone calls for a friend from the far side of the road. A woman helps another person to their feet and leads them away from the dust.

The girl's father keeps one arm around her while they look out from the doorway. Across the road, a delivery cart lies on its side, its wheels still turning. The driver crawls clear and pulls himself toward the pavement.

A man from the next doorway reaches him and helps him up. Together they move away from the nearest crater.

When the air begins to clear, two round vessels sit in separate craters. Pieces of paving have fallen across their curved sides.

A man at the edge of the crossing takes a step forward. "What are they?"

The first hatch opens.

Nappa climbs out, unfolding his massive frame from the round vessel, and stretches his arms above his bald head. Across the street, the other hatch lifts. Vegeta steps onto the broken pavement, shorter and spare beside him, his black hair rising sharply above his scouter. Neither looks at the damage as if it matters.

The man stops. A woman beside him catches his sleeve and pulls him back.

"There are people inside," she says.

Nappa turns his head toward the sound. Vegeta touches the device over one eye. It clicks as he looks across the city.

---

## The Direction of the City

On the empty coast, Krillin stops with one hand raised. Yamcha lands beside him, and Puar drops from above the water.

"You feel that?" Krillin asks.

"Two of them."

Krillin looks inland. "They've arrived."

Puar takes hold of Yamcha's shoulder. "Where are they?"

Yamcha points toward the distant city. "That way."

Krillin rises first. Yamcha follows, with Puar holding tight.

In the mountains, Chiaotzu turns toward Tien. The stones around them settle as Tien lowers his hands.

"Tien?"

"I feel them. Stay with me."

Chiaotzu moves beside him. They rise above the ridge and fly toward the same distant point.

On the Lookout, Gohan grips the edge of the stone rail. He and Goshin feel the two unfamiliar ki at once and turn toward the same distant point. Piccolo is already looking that way.

"They're here," Piccolo says.

Kami steps forward. "In the city. There are people near them."

A voice breaks through the radio in Bulma's car. The speaker reports two objects down in East City and asks people to keep away from the damaged street.

Bulma opens the car door again and turns up the sound. "East City. I know where that is."

"Can you find somewhere away from that street?" Chi-Chi asks.

"I'll look for a clear roof or a road people are using to get out."

Chi-Chi turns to Popo. "We'll help them reach Bulma. If the fighting comes our way, we move them farther back."

"I'll keep the carpet ready," he says.

Gohan looks toward the city. "If we can get the Saiyans away from the people, we'll try."

"Together," Goshin says.

Piccolo's cape lifts in the wind. "Watch what they do before you commit yourselves."

Popo spreads the carpet on the tiles. Chi-Chi steps onto it, and he takes his place beside her. Bulma climbs into the aircar and starts the engine.

Piccolo lifts from the floor. Gohan and Goshin fly after him toward the city. Popo steers the carpet toward its outskirts, with Bulma's car alongside, looking for people who need a way out.

At the bench, the page of days remains beneath the stone. The wind lifts one corner, then lets it fall.

**NARRATOR:** The Saiyans have reached Earth. Goku is still on Snake Way, and his family is moving toward the city.
