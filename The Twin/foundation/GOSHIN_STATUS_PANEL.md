# GOSHIN — STATUS PANEL

**Owns: Goshin’s working status across the accepted original Chapter 20 and later unaccepted review candidates.**

**Author clarification, 5 November:** His first Great Ape experience, the fight, Saiyan development and his innate whole-being Adaptation Talent interact to make him substantially stronger in body and ki. This genuine increase remains in his ordinary body after reversion; it is not merely form size, a release of earlier reserves or better technique (`O214`). The Talent predates the transformation. See the [correction and exact predecessor](../canon/FIRST_GREAT_APE_GROWTH_2026-11-05.md).

**Checkpoint:** home with Chi-Chi and Gohan after training and a children’s visit. Living Goku has caught Bubbles after three weeks with King Kai, but no news has reached Earth on-page. The author explicitly accepted the separately preserved pre-correction Chapter 20; its selected correction and Chapters 6–19 remain unaccepted review material. The later Chapters 21–30 are unaccepted continuations and do not alter this accepted checkpoint.

**Revised Chapter 21 candidate:** During his chosen Piccolo round Goshin's physical strength and ki rise, and he responds faster; after further practice his narrow wave cuts deeper at the previous bank mark while he controls four pebbles and a falling slab. Gohan independently chooses and improves his work. The revision addresses the previously identified presentation gap and remains unaccepted; no new measurement, form or chamber year is shown.

**Chapter 22 review candidate:** After an authored several-month outside interval, Goshin and Gohan live and train together for one uninterrupted physical chamber year while Chi-Chi waits outside; about one outside day passes. Goshin flies with a large block in the first week and later carries eight same-sized blocks physically in two stacks under the same gravity. Separately he combines moving blocks with a wave and fires a far stronger sustained wave that splits the empty floor before he turns and stops it. Both boys grow physically; Gohan improves pre-existing flight and fires a much stronger controlled wave through three spare blocks and into the empty floor. Piccolo senses each boy is far stronger than he is at their exit. No exact birthday, scan, form, cap or accepted event follows. The Saiyans land only in unaccepted Chapter 23, later that same morning.

**Chapter 23 review candidate:** Piccolo arrives already recognizing both boys' separate ki and that each now far exceeds his own. He removes his weights and uses his speed, but Gohan and Goshin comfortably outclass him in their individual ordinary spars while holding back. Gohan displays his stronger controlled wave; Goshin uses chamber-developed speed and previously held-back ki while applying his mastered wave with moving blocks. The brothers also spar each other under control and land breathing easily. Chi-Chi watches and coaches; the former eye-blast breach is superseded. The Saiyans land later that morning; the brothers head toward the city, not yet in combat. The comparison is local to this checkpoint. No scan occurs during the spar; later Vegeta reads two unidentified distant signals at 9,200 and 12,400, identified only in Chapter 24. No new form, fixed maximum or Saiyan outcome is implied. This candidate remains unaccepted.

**Chapter 30 corrected unaccepted frontier:** Goshin directs and stops a controlled wave for Goku. Piccolo recognizes that his ordinary body now carries Earth-destruction-capable ki, per the author, and the visible wave is not his maximum. Gohan makes independent choices and both brothers travel rapidly; Goshin helps Popo fetch Bulma. Both boys later obtain Chi-Chi’s conditional permission and reach Jupiter with Piccolo, Bulma and Popo aboard Kami’s craft. Kami stays at Yunzabit. Goshin remains tailed and ordinary-sized in borrowed boots and a wrap; previous soreness is not declared healed. No new scan, fixed multiplier, return to Earth or Namek route is shown (`O214`, `O221`–`O225`).

**Chapter 29 unaccepted frontier (earlier checkpoint):** Goshin applies the substantial ordinary-body and ki growth retained from his first controlled Great Ape experience and the fight: he proposes and lifts a stair, landing and masonry heavier than the quarry rock he carried before the transformation, balancing the load on one arm while separately holding the fractured wall with ki as the crew braces it. Gohan independently finds the rear-window route and carries a woman to safety. Goshin remains playful, ordinary-sized, tailed and barefoot in his spare clothes; his sore side and shoulder receive a limited examination but no diagnosis or declaration of healing. No new scan, fixed multiplier or fresh gain is asserted (`O214`, `O216`, `O218`).

**Chapter 28 unaccepted frontier (earlier checkpoint):** Goshin remains ordinary-sized, tailed, playful and alert. His substantial, real unnumbered growth from the fight and first Great Ape experience persists in his ordinary body as he restrains the injured, awake Vegeta's raised arm, then eases his pressure. He tells Goku about his controlled giant form and is pleased yet wishes his father had seen it. He wears an old shirt and spare trousers from the returned bags, remains barefoot and still has a sore side and cheek/shoulder marks; no diagnosis or new scan is shown. He and Gohan guard the Saiyans and reunite with Goku and Chi-Chi (`O210`, `O213`).

**Chapter 27 unaccepted frontier (earlier checkpoint):** Goshin remains conscious as a giant, fights Vegeta and directs his Kamehameha away from Chi-Chi. His body and ki grow substantially through the fight and first transformation, without a number, and that development remains after Gohan destroys the artificial light and both giants revert; Piccolo senses the much greater ordinary-sized ki (`O214`). Gohan independently makes the shot, Piccolo guides it and Goshin keeps Vegeta off the line. After another fight Goshin knocks Vegeta unconscious but alive. Goshin is ordinary-sized, tailed and wrapped in Chi-Chi's blanket; he reports a sore side and has marks on cheek and shoulder. Gohan has not transformed. The exact reversion is authored staging, with no fixed multiplier, new scan, separate named normal-sized ape mode or settled Saiyan custody (`O201`–`O204`).

**Chapter 26 unaccepted frontier (earlier checkpoint):** Goshin deliberately looks at Vegeta's artificial moon and becomes a giant ape with full awareness and control from the outset. He senses his brother and friends, controls his footing and tail, catches transformed Vegeta and moves falling blocks clear with ki while holding him. His clothes and shoes tear. Gohan does not transform and continues making independent choices. Both tails and the natural moon remain; no new scouter reading, fixed Goshin multiplier, separate ordinary-sized ape mode or final result is established at that earlier endpoint (`O197`–`O199`).

**Chapter 25 unaccepted frontier (earlier checkpoint):** Goshin defeats the pinned Saibaiman and faces Vegeta. Vegeta reads **16,300** and then **19,400** after Goshin releases more previously developed reserves. That reading is above the power Vegeta had been using; he clips away the scouter and raises his own ki, and Goshin's first punch pushes his guard back. No numerical Vegeta reading or maximum is established. In sustained combat Goshin then shows real further growth in strength and ki without a number. Gohan independently overpowers Nappa. Goshin intercepts Vegeta's full-commitment Galick Gun with his Kamehameha; Gohan joins him, and together they drive the clash skyward before continuing against the still-active Vegeta (`O190`–`O195`).

**Chapter 24 unaccepted frontier (earlier checkpoint):** Goshin decisively overpowers Nappa; Gohan independently restrains the Saiyan when he threatens civilians. The boys each quickly defeat a Saibaiman, then combine defensive Kamehamehas against Vegeta's attack. The resulting shock loosens the slab Goshin carries clear while Gohan moves debris; he keeps more previously held-back ki ready while watching Vegeta, with the scouter at 16,300 at that moment. Four Saibaimen remain alive, one pinned; Vegeta gathers light for another attack. Goku is still on Snake Way and Chi-Chi assists evacuation (`O182`–`O187`).

## Present first-form development and later paths

- **Great Ape:** his first transformation is staged in unaccepted Chapter 26 through his deliberate look at Vegeta's artificial moon, and is conscious and controlled from the beginning. The form and its power develop with Goshin and his deepening Saiyan bloodline, rather than remaining obsolete. He uses the form and shows further unnumbered growth in Chapter 27 before reverting. The older suggestion that the ape itself must frighten him does not override the later correction; further future development remains open.
- **Ape power in the ordinary body:** the first Great Ape experience already contributes to substantial, retained ordinary-body strength and ki (`O214`). The author also allows Goshin to access Great Ape power without the giant body and combine it with anger or, once earned, Super Saiyan. A separately activated mode, its exact mechanics and degree have not yet been shown; no mandatory delay is imposed. This is his own evolving power, not an automatic copy of Broly.
- **Golden line:** Super Saiyan 1, 2 and 3, and the distinctive Rage path, are later developments in the stored design. Their eventual perfected presentation is not an ability he possesses now. Their arrival must follow the author’s rulings and the story’s causes, not a defeat forced to preserve canon.
- **Ape road and Super Saiyan 4:** a later, earned route through the ape lineage; neither the golden ape nor SS4 has appeared in this branch.
- **Divine crossing:** the design reserves a late, one-way promotion to a divine baseline, followed by the authored God 1–3 and God 4 paths. Divinity must be genuinely reached through a valid path; the Talent does not conjure it from nothing. No god ki exists at the current checkpoint.
- **Excluded for Goshin:** Super Saiyan Blue. The future God 4 design is his own path, not an automatic copy of someone else’s Ultra state. The crossing must preserve his identity, emotions and relationships.

The detailed authored future design is in [`../STATUS.md`](../STATUS.md), especially §§11–16. Chapter 26 stages the first form; its substantial contribution to his ordinary-body baseline is now shown after reversion in the unaccepted continuation.
