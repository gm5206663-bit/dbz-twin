# THE TWIN — lessons, corrections and handoff

31 October 2026. This note records what went wrong, how it was corrected, and what the next writer or agent must still check. It is a handoff, not an approval of the current draft.

## Most important lesson

**A clean mechanical audit is not evidence that a story covers every source scene or respects the user's creative rules.** The user found omissions after the checker passed. Read and inventory scenes in source order, account for visual action as well as dialogue, and dramatize important beats rather than merely marking them present in a checklist. If coverage cannot fit, continue in another sequential chapter instead of skipping ahead.

## Mistakes and corrections

1. **I skipped ahead.** An earlier Chapter Four jumped to episode seven and compressed the intervening events. The user rejected it. The current rescue draft returns to the abduction and episode-three confrontation instead of treating that gap as a recap. It is still a draft for review.
2. **I mistook missing caption words for missing action.** The captions did not describe the stone throwing or the silent truck sequence. I incorrectly claimed that no stones were thrown, and missed Gohan hiding under the farmer's truck. The claim was withdrawn; secondary sources and selected fan-gallery stills were checked, and the reported stones, truck and Nimbus journey were restored in prose. Those secondary reports and stills are not a verified complete broadcast recording.
3. **I forced canon's kidnapping onto a changed character.** I let Raditz take Goshin while conscious without working through his abilities and anger-driven growth. The user's encounter-specific correction requires Raditz to knock him unconscious before taking him. The revised draft shows the fight, the knockout, Gohan protecting him, and changed consequences after he wakes. Goshin's roughly 400 island reading is one authored snapshot, not a cap or a complete power equation.
4. **I treated a green check as more reassuring than it was.** Structural tests now include a narrow truck-sequence marker, but markers only catch specific omissions. The last audit passed 20/20 and its focused self-test 10/10; neither proves prose quality, full source coverage or user acceptance.
5. **I initially chose private visibility when the user wanted public.** The GitHub repository was made public on request. Both `main` and the historical `archive` branch are public. Future visibility changes must be treated as deliberate publishing decisions.
6. **Credentials appeared in the conversation.** Never put credentials in story files, repository URLs, logs or public reports. Use a short-lived, narrowly scoped secure connection when available, and revoke exposed credentials. Two of the supplied tokens authenticated; their revocation remains the user's security action.

## Continuity and source boundaries

- Chapters One–Three are accepted historical bodies. Chapter Two's conscious-capture passage is explicitly superseded by its dated correction; five supplemental island passages are separately placed, not silently merged or accepted.
- Chapter Four and the five supplemental passages await the user's review. Do not claim they have been accepted or that every missing beat has been found.
- Chapter Two's source-order issue and Chapter Three's documented date/timing and anticipatory-preview debts remain open. Do not force later canon outcomes to erase those debts or the twins' consequences.
- The user prohibited a **new Nimbus purity demonstration**. Ordinary canon travel by Nimbus is not such a demonstration.
- All 291 caption files were indexed and searched; only the 54 episodes listed in `canon/RESEARCH.md` received full spoken-caption reads. The redistributed captions' original provenance is unverified. No complete original episode video was examined.
- Rejected drafts and old snapshots are historical references, not accepted continuity. They are separated in Drive's `archive/` and GitHub's `archive` branch.
