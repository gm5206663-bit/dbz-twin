# Episode 5 scene coverage — Chapter Six draft

**Owns:** the ordered episode-5 baseline and its comparison with the divergent Chapter Six draft. It is an inventory, not a completeness certificate. The entire local Japanese caption file was reread in order (`W-30`); its original broadcaster/official provenance is unverified, and caption text does not establish silent choreography. `W-18`, `W-46`, `W-47` and `W-48` are secondary episode summaries. `W-48` reports the distant planet and insect-like inhabitants; `W-49` search excerpts and `W-50` also report Vegeta eating an arm beside Nappa. The `W-49` page fetch was blocked by reCAPTCHA; `W-50` shares a publisher with the separately contaminated `W-13` and is used only on this corroborated point. No complete episode video or verified broadcast master was examined. All two-child blocking, altered dialogue and departures are authored.

## Existing overlap and remaining debt

- The authorized Chapter Three revision removes the earlier date, rigid countdown and incompatible future/overnight claims. Chapter Seven retains the same unanswered call and prompt later telling (`O101`).
- The earlier Chapter Two supplements and specified explanation-order correction are integrated with explicit authorization; the earlier separate file remains historical.
- After reader feedback, the unaccepted Chapter Six ending no longer proposes a doctor trip; it directs the family back to Kame House. Chapter Seven now has Piccolo confront them at the crater, Krillin seek Korin's beans, and Roshi tell Chi-Chi promptly from Kame House. The invented hospital and landing strip are withdrawn. The source-order comparison and Otherworld limits are in `canon/SCENE_COVERAGE_006.md`; later custody or training is not predetermined.
- Full visual provenance remains open. The story and the structured audit are drafts and mechanical checks, not proof that every silent anime frame has been covered or that the user has accepted the chapter.
