## Source and interpretation boundary

- The previously retrieved transcript of the linked Goku-scaling video was checked; its visuals and audio were not independently viewed. It is a fan scale, not an official table. Do not impose its dwarf-star, multi-solar-system, FTL, multiversal, outerversal or other speculative labels on these early scenes.
- The [official Jackie Chun profile](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_1502.html) reports that Goku matched a Jackie Chun Kamehameha during the 21st tournament and that Jackie later destroyed the Moon with a separate maximum-power Kamehameha. The comparison supports a substantial early-power inference, not a measured Goku feat or equivalence between all of their attacks.
- The [official Saiyan-arc Piccolo profile](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_1178.html) records Piccolo destroying the Moon during Gohan's canonical training. That original event is a benchmark, not something that happened in the divergent present story: Gohan is with his family, and the Moon remains intact.
- The [official Saiyan-arc Vegeta profile](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_3929.html) records Vegeta threatening Earth and Goku deflecting his Galick Gun with a Kaioken-times-four Kamehameha. This occurs later than these candidate chapters; do not give Goku Kaioken now or transfer the later feat wholesale to his current condition.
- Concentrated attack power, striking, environmental damage, speed, movement, endurance and precision are related but distinct. In a rescue a fighter can be strong enough to break an obstacle while choosing not to break its supports or hurt a bystander. A small target or gentle spar is not automatically a limit on a fighter's power. Conversely, do not excuse every escaped full-power attack as harmless without showing where it goes and what it affects. Never convert a scouter reading into a fixed damage radius or a speed.
- Goshin's 1,450 Kamehameha scan and 1,500 surge are separately authored, temporary moments, not constant output or ceilings. Preserve his broader native Talent, adaptation and recovery, not just copying; he is not less capable than Gohan. Keep Gohan's agency, growth and decision-making. Goku is talented in his own right, learns, grows, and is actively travelling and training for King Kai, without a permanent ranking or mandated separation from his sons.
- No first Great Ape transformation, new power scan, unearned Zenkai, Kaioken, Instant Transmission, god ki or early golden form is introduced. Goshin's first Great Ape remains controlled when it eventually occurs, in keeping with the later standing rule copied into `reference/`.
