# Chapter 46 — The Shore We Hold

*Anime episode reference: Dragon Ball Z, Episodes 88 and 90, with selected combat context from Episode 87, adapted to the changed branch.*

## Three Against One

Goshin's fist meets Freeza's raised forearm above the lake.

The impact drives Freeza backward. Before he can stop himself, Goshin follows with a second punch, turning his shoulders away from the shore. The red light of the Kaiō-ken streams behind the boy.

Gohan comes in from Freeza's left. He strikes the exposed side of Freeza's ribs with both hands. Freeza twists toward him, and Piccolo fires from the opposite side.

Freeza catches Piccolo's shot on his palm. The light bursts around his fingers, and Goshin hits him across the jaw.

"You forgot somebody," Goshin says.

Freeza's tail whips toward Gohan. Gohan drops beneath it, but the tail changes direction and clips his shoulder. He tumbles through the air. Piccolo reaches him before he falls to the water.

"Can you move it?" Piccolo asks.

Gohan rolls his arm. "Yes. Go!"

Piccolo releases him and rises. Freeza is already closing on Goshin. He catches the boy's next punch and drives an elbow into his sore shoulder. Goshin cries out; his fingers open, and Freeza's other fist strikes his stomach.

Goshin is hurled toward the broken island. He turns, puts a hand against the rock and pushes off before his body hits it. Red light flashes across the water as he comes back.

Freeza meets him halfway. Their hands lock. The water below them flattens under the pressure, then lifts in a ring around the island.

"You keep doing that," Goshin says through his teeth.

"Hurting you?"

"Going after the others when you can't get past me!"

Freeza's eyes narrow. He tries to force Goshin's arms apart, but the boy holds on. Goshin pulls one wrist down and drives his forehead into Freeza's face.

Freeza lets go. Goshin follows with a kick that sends him skimming across the lake. Water breaks around Freeza's feet as he catches himself.

Gohan draws level with his brother. "That shoulder?"

"Still there."

"I meant does it hurt."

"Yes!" Goshin looks at him. "Does yours?"

"A little."

"Then we're both still here."

Piccolo lands on the highest piece of the island, between them and the nearer bank. "Don't give him time to choose the next target."

Freeza touches his jaw. The smile he offers them looks carefully put back in place.

"I asked you to come with me before," he says to the brothers. "You've shown me more since then. I am prepared to be generous."

"Generous?" Goshin stares at him.

"Both of you could work for me. Your Namekian friend could be useful as well. Give me what I came for, and the people on this shore needn't suffer any more than they already have."

Gohan's hands close. He remembers Muri standing beside the covered ball, and Cargo asking whether the houses could be mended.

"You sent your men after them," he says. "You don't get to promise they'll be safe if we help you hurt somebody else."

"I destroyed your father's world," Freeza says. "Surely you understand that refusing me has consequences."

Goshin's breath catches. He had heard the words before; they have not become easier to bear.

"Those people had families," he says. "And you're doing it again here!"

He moves. Freeza raises one hand, and the island groans beneath Piccolo.

"Goshin!" Piccolo calls.

The rock splits across its middle. Piccolo leaps clear as the larger half rises from the lake. Water pours off it in sheets. Freeza sends it spinning toward the bank behind Piccolo.

Goshin changes course. He catches the slab with both hands, stopping its near edge a few body lengths from the shore. The force drives him backward through the air. His boots strike the bank and cut two furrows into the earth.

A second piece tears free of the island and follows the first.

"I've got it!" Gohan calls.

He puts his ki against the tumbling rock. It slows but keeps turning, its jagged edge aimed at the trees. Gohan flies with it, shoves from underneath and changes its angle. The slab strikes the water beside the bank instead of the trees. A wave rolls up the slope and falls back.

A third fragment comes after him. Piccolo breaks it with a sharp blast. Chips rattle across the bank around Gohan's feet.

Goshin holds the largest piece above him. It is broader than the landing feet of Bulma's ship, and water streams from its underside onto his face. Beyond it, he feels Freeza moving.

"Piccolo! He's coming around the right!"

Piccolo's arm stretches past the rock. His hand catches Freeza's wrist for an instant, pulling his raised finger away from Goshin. Freeza tears free and fires; the beam cuts through the edge of the slab instead of striking the boy beneath it.

Goshin grunts. A crack races across the stone. He thrusts upward and out, sending the whole piece back over the water. It crashes down beyond the remaining island.

"You didn't have to catch it," Freeza says.

Goshin pushes wet hair out of his eyes. "Yes, I did."

He launches himself from the bank. The torn ground drops away beneath him as he rushes back toward Freeza.

## The Road to the Spring

In the sheltered hollow, Sela ducks as the bank trembles. A shower of leaves falls across the ship's rounded roof. She waits until the sound passes before looking out between two branches.

"Did something hit the village?" Kado asks.

"I couldn't see that far. The water came up against the bank."

Bulma is crouched beside the aerial with her tool case open. She looks up at the moving branches, then switches off the small tester in her hand.

"Hold this for me, would you? Not the bare end."

Kado takes the insulated lead. The metal support above them is bent, and the covering around its base hangs open where the branch tore it.

"Can you make it stand straight?" he asks.

"Straight would be nice. Connected would be better." Bulma works the loose fitting free. "The wire has pulled out of this contact. I won't know what else broke until I test it."

Sela moves closer. "If you need both of us here, I can watch after she finishes."

Kado looks toward the trees. "Muri and the children will be waiting for me."

"And the two on the terrace," Sela says. Her hand tightens around the strap of her basket. "I left them up there."

"You got away from men who were shooting," Kado replies. "You didn't leave them because you wanted to."

Sela looks down at the small plants in her basket. "I still want to find them."

Bulma fits a new connector over the end of the lead. "I want to know where the others are too. But I can't see the spring from here, and I don't want to send you straight into whoever's out there."

"I'll go below the bank," Kado says. "If I see anyone following, I won't take them to Muri."

Sela studies the opening. "I'll stay with Bulma. If you get to the spring and they aren't there, look for a sign. Your companion knows you'd use that path."

Kado nods. He helps Bulma set the lead down without touching the exposed contact, then slips beneath the branches.

Bulma watches him go. "You two have more sense than some people I know."

"Does that include you?" Sela asks.

"On a good day, no."

Sela's mouth twitches. She returns to the opening while Bulma fastens the connector and reaches for the tester again.

Kado follows the bank until he can hear the spring. He stays under the trees and stops twice to listen. The sounds from the lake reach him through the ground as much as through the air, but he hears no footsteps behind him.

At the spring, three pale stones lie in a line on the bank. The smallest points toward the channel.

Kado crouches. Cargo had liked collecting the little stones near Muri's house. He cannot be certain who placed these, but the line points along the route his companion would have chosen for cover.

He follows it without calling out. At the bend he finds a scuff on the bank and a broken stem among the roots. Farther on, he hears a twig shift behind him.

Kado turns with his hand raised.

"It's me," says his companion.

She steps from behind a tree, keeping her own hand ready until she has looked past him. Kado lowers his arm.

"Did anyone follow you?"

"I didn't see anyone. Bulma and I moved the ship. Sela's with her."

His companion closes her eyes for a moment. "She's alive?"

"She was at the terrace. She doesn't know what happened to the other two."

"Then we'll have to look. Come on. Muri is just ahead."

Cargo runs to Kado as soon as he comes beneath the young trees. Kado catches him, lifts him off his feet and sets him down again.

"You found our stones!" Cargo says.

"I thought that small one might be yours."

"The other two were hers." Cargo points at Kado's companion. "I made sure you'd know where to go."

Dende comes forward more slowly. "Was Gohan with you?"

"He went back to the fight. He said he was going to help Goshin and Piccolo. I haven't seen what happened after that."

Muri listens without interrupting. The older resident still holds the covered ball, resting part of its weight against a tree.

"And the ship?" Muri asks.

"It's behind another bank. Bulma was working on something that lets her speak to Earth."

"Can it carry everyone?" Cargo asks.

"We don't know yet," Kado says. "We haven't asked Bulma to try."

Muri looks toward the terrace, though the trees hide it. "Sela knows where she last saw the others. Tell her we're here, and ask whether we can search without bringing anyone back to this path."

Dende steps closer. "If they're hurt, can you bring them to us? I want to help."

"I'll tell her," Kado says.

His companion points to a place beyond the next rise. "We'll stay under the trees for now. Don't call out until you've checked the path."

Cargo grips Kado's hand once before letting it go. Kado turns back toward the ship, keeping low along the channel.

## A Choice at the Quarry

Tien meets Kami and Chiaotzu beyond the quarry tracks. Nappa sits under the farther overhang, watching them. Goku and Krillin remain near Vegeta, who has shifted forward to hear what he can.

"I asked for help, not a replacement," Tien says. "Nappa can still move, and Vegeta has already tried to leave."

Kami looks at the two Saiyans in turn. "What do you need first?"

"Someone else to watch while we work out where they can be held. I don't want either of them alone with a guard who can't stop an attack."

"I'll stay with you at this side," Kami says. "Chiaotzu can help carry a message if the others need one."

Nappa snorts. "How many of you does it take to look at a man sitting down?"

"As many as it takes," Tien replies.

Chiaotzu floats down beside him. "I'm not going anywhere until we know who needs the message."

Across the tracks, Vegeta watches Kami. "Another Namekian. How many of them are hiding on this planet?"

"I'm here to help keep you from hurting anyone," Kami says.

"Then you're wasting your time. Freeza is the one you should be worried about."

Goku steps between Vegeta and the tracks. "We're worried about him too. That doesn't make it safe to let you go."

Vegeta's tail shifts across the stone. "When your children find out what he can really do, you may wish you'd listened."

Goku's hands tighten. He wants to be on Namek with the boys. He also remembers Vegeta rushing past him toward the open quarry. The prince has given him no reason to leave that path unwatched.

"If Dr. Brief finds a way there," Goku says, "I'm going. Until then, we keep working on this."

Vegeta leans back. "I intend to be gone before you finish discussing it."

"Then you'd better discuss it with us."

For an instant Krillin looks as if he might laugh. Vegeta does not.

## The Water and the Wave

Freeza meets Goshin above the scattered remains of the island. Their fists collide, and the water punches downward beneath them. Goshin turns his sore shoulder away from Freeza's next strike and drives his other fist into Freeza's chest.

Freeza catches his arm and swings him toward the bank. Goshin twists before they reach it, planting both feet against Freeza's middle. He pushes them apart over the lake.

The red light flares. His heart is pounding, but his arms answer him. The strength that has been building through the fight is there when he pulls his hands back into guard, as real as the ache in his shoulder.

Freeza darts in again. This time Goshin meets the blow without being driven toward the trees. He shouts and forces Freeza back across the water.

"Goshin!" Gohan calls from the broken rock. "If he turns, I'll make an opening!"

"I heard you!"

Freeza's gaze flicks toward Gohan. Goshin sees it and lunges, but Freeza drops beneath him. One hand opens toward the water.

The lake surges upward around Goshin's legs. He tears through the first sheet, only for another to fold over his shoulders. The pressure slams him against a floating slab of stone.

"Now," Freeza says.

The rock rolls over Goshin. Water and stone press down together. Red light escapes through the gaps, but Freeza has both hands raised, keeping the pieces in place.

Gohan sends a narrow blast into the water beside Freeza. Spray bursts between them. Freeza turns and fires through it, straight toward Gohan's position.

Piccolo catches Gohan around the waist and pulls him aside. The beam grazes Piccolo's forearm, burning through his sleeve before it vanishes over the lake.

"Mr. Piccolo!"

"I'm moving it, aren't I?" Piccolo releases him. "Watch Freeza!"

Inside the tumbling water, Goshin hears his brother's shout. He pushes against the rock with his arms and sends his ki around its broken edge. The pressure shifts. He drags one knee underneath himself, plants it against the stone and heaves.

The slab breaks through the surface. Goshin flies out beside it, breathing hard. He catches the falling rock with his ki before it can tumble toward Piccolo, then drops it into the lake.

Freeza is already coming. Goshin blocks a punch, misses his own return and takes a blow across the cheek. He answers with a kick that glances off Freeza's hip. The two of them separate above the water.

Piccolo lands beside Gohan. His burned forearm hangs for a moment at his side; then he flexes his fingers.

"Can you still use it?" Gohan asks.

"Enough."

Gohan looks from Freeza to the bank. "He keeps throwing things where we have to stop them."

"He knows what we'll protect."

"Then let's make him look at us instead."

Piccolo gives him a quick glance. Gohan points to the stretch of open water beyond Freeza, away from the village. He speaks fast, and Piccolo listens.

Goshin circles with Freeza, keeping his back away from the shore. He can feel Gohan and Piccolo moving behind him. Freeza's hands open and close, and his ki begins to rise again.

"You should have taken my offer," Freeza says.

"You should have left when I asked."

"I have never left a world because a child ordered me to."

Goshin thinks of the ship tilting beneath the wall of water. He thinks of Gohan holding it steady while Bulma and Kado found a way through. Freeza has been able to make them turn again and again. Goshin will not let him choose another house to aim at.

He draws his ki close and sets the Kaiō-ken at the force he chooses.

"Kaiō-ken! Times thirty!"

The red light tightens around him and bursts outward. Goshin cries out with it. His chest is tight, but the power answers, and beneath the rush his own body has grown stronger than it was when he first came to this lake.

Freeza's tail lashes. Goshin catches it, swings him away from the bank and releases him over the open water.

Gohan fires into the lake in front of Freeza. A wall of spray climbs between them. Gohan does not stay behind it; he flies sideways, low over the surface.

Freeza shoots through the falling water toward the movement. Piccolo's extended arm catches Freeza's tail from the other side and pulls it sideways. Freeza twists and yanks. Piccolo is dragged off the rock, and his burned arm snaps back toward him. Freeza raises a hand at Piccolo.

A small blast strikes that hand. It does not stop Freeza, but the shot spoils his aim. Gohan is already changing position again.

Goshin comes through the gap and punches Freeza in the ribs. Freeza folds around the blow. Goshin catches him under the chin with the next one and drives him back over the open lake.

Freeza answers with a strike to Goshin's stomach. The boy gasps, but his grip closes on Freeza's wrist. They spin together. Goshin wrenches the arm away from the bank, then releases it and drops below Freeza.

"Piccolo, Gohan! Clear!"

The two of them move to either side. Goshin brings his hands together at his hip. Blue light gathers between his palms, bright against the red around his body.

"Ka... me... ha... me..."

Freeza raises both hands. A hard white light swells between them. The water beneath the two fighters sinks into a hollow.

"You little fool!"

"HAA!"

Goshin thrusts his hands forward. His Kamehameha strikes Freeza's attack between them. Light spreads across the lake, and a deep roar rolls toward the banks.

For a moment neither wave advances. Goshin's boots slide backward through the air. His sore shoulder shakes. He sets his jaw and draws on the strength in his body, keeping the blue stream together instead of letting it scatter.

Freeza pushes with both hands. His own light thickens, forcing the clash toward Goshin. The boy's arms bend. He sees the shore beyond the edge of the glare and hears his brother call his name.

"I'm not letting you through!"

Goshin roars. Beneath the red rush, new strength builds through his body and ki together. His shaking arms straighten, and the blue wave surges forward. Freeza cries out and braces, but the meeting point passes his hands. The Kamehameha engulfs him and carries him away from the bank.

A spray of loose energy breaks toward the island. Gohan pushes it down into the water with a quick burst of his own. Piccolo knocks another fragment aside with his uninjured arm.

Goshin sees Freeza's arms fall. He turns his wrists upward without looking away from him, bending the wave above the lake. The blue light climbs away from Freeza and vanishes high overhead.

Freeza drops. He strikes the largest remaining piece of the island and lies still.

The roar fades. Water rushes into the hollow and breaks against the scattered stone.

Goshin hovers where he is, hands still extended. The red light dwindles around him. He takes one breath, then another, and lowers his arms.

"Goshin!"

Gohan reaches him first. He catches Goshin's forearm, ready to support him, but Goshin keeps himself in the air.

"I'm here," Goshin says. His voice is rough. "Did I hit the bank?"

"No. You sent it over the lake."

Piccolo lands beside Freeza and looks down at him. Freeza's chest rises, faintly but unmistakably. His eyes remain closed.

"He's alive," Piccolo says. "And unconscious."

Goshin joins him on the rock. He keeps his eyes on Freeza as the last red flicker leaves his hands. His shoulder still hurts. Under the tiredness, the strength he gained in the fight is still his; he can feel it gathering when he opens his hand.

Gohan lands on Freeza's other side. "Will he wake up soon?"

"I don't know."

Piccolo looks at the scattered island and the damaged bank. "A rope won't hold him if he does. We can't walk away and hope he stays like this."

Goshin steps closer to Freeza. "Then I'll stay."

"So will I," Piccolo says. He keeps his good hand raised. "If he moves, don't wait for him to attack."

Gohan looks toward the trees. "Bulma and Kado were hiding the ship. Muri's people are somewhere beyond the spring. I want to find them and tell them what happened."

Goshin looks up at him. "Don't go alone if you find any of his men."

"I won't chase them. I'll find the others first."

"And tell Bulma I asked about her."

Gohan smiles. "You can ask her yourself."

"I will. After this."

Piccolo's expression changes. He looks past them without taking his attention from Freeza.

A voice has entered his thoughts.

*Piccolo. Can you hear me?*

*King Kai.* Piccolo keeps his answer brief. *We've stopped Freeza. He's alive and unconscious. Goshin and I are watching him. Gohan is going to look for Bulma and the villagers.*

*I'll tell Goku. Kami asked me to thank you for the elder's answer. He wanted you to know it meant something to him.*

Piccolo is quiet for a moment. The wind moves over the broken rock beneath his feet.

*Tell him I heard.*

*There's something else. Chi-Chi asked me earlier to tell the boys that they're trying to come, and that the boys should come home if they can. I couldn't pass it on when you were fighting.*

*I'll tell them.*

Piccolo turns to the brothers. "Your mother sent a message through King Kai. She says they're trying to come for you. She wants you home if you can get there."

Goshin's face changes. For a moment he looks younger than he did in the fight.

"Did she say she was angry?"

"Not in the message I heard."

Gohan lets out a shaky laugh. "That doesn't mean she isn't."

"I know." Goshin rubs his cheek. "I want to hear her anyway."

"So do I," Gohan says.

He looks once more at Freeza, then at Piccolo. Piccolo nods. Gohan rises only as high as the nearer bank and flies toward the inlet, keeping the trees between himself and the open shore.

## The Call

Kado reaches the hollow before Gohan. He tells Sela and Bulma about the stones and the young trees, keeping his voice low.

"Muri says they're staying there for now. Dende asked us to bring the workers to them if they're hurt."

Sela takes a breath. "Then I can show you where I last saw them."

"We'll go carefully," Kado says. "Muri doesn't want a stranger following us back."

Bulma clips the tester onto the repaired lead. A small light comes on.

"The connection at this end is good," she says. "That doesn't tell me whether it will reach Earth."

A branch shifts at the hollow's entrance. Sela raises her hand. Kado steps beside her.

"It's Gohan," comes a voice from below the bank. "Is Bulma there?"

"Gohan!" Bulma scrambles down from the landing foot. "Come where I can see you."

He steps around the trunk. His sleeve is torn at one shoulder, and there is a smear of dirt across his face. Bulma catches both his arms and looks him over.

"Are you hurt? Where's Goshin?"

"My shoulder got hit, but I can move it. Goshin and Piccolo are at the lake. We stopped Freeza. He's alive, but he isn't awake."

Bulma stares at him. "You stopped him?"

"All three of us. Goshin hit him with a Kamehameha. Piccolo is staying with him, in case Freeza wakes up."

She pulls Gohan against her for a quick, fierce hug. He makes a surprised sound. Then she lets him go and turns to Kado and Sela.

"This is Gohan. Sela helped us hide the ship, and Kado found Muri's group."

"Did he find Dende and Cargo?" Gohan asks.

"They're with Muri," Kado says. "They asked about you."

Gohan's shoulders drop. "Good. I want to see them. There may still be soldiers near the village, though."

"And the two people from the terrace," Sela says. "We don't know where they are."

"We'll look for them," Gohan replies. "First I should tell Muri what happened."

Bulma points to the aerial. "First, help me see whether this works. If I can call home, Puar will be listening."

They connect the compact communicator to the repaired lead. Bulma checks the fitting twice, then presses the call control. Static fills the small cabin.

"Come on," she mutters. "You worked before the tree hit you."

The static breaks. A distant ringing sound follows.

At the Son house, Puar nearly drops the pencil when the telephone rings. He catches the receiver with both hands.

"Hello?"

"Puar, it's Bulma! Can you hear me?"

"Yes! Are you all right?"

"I'm all right. The ship got hit by a branch, and I've repaired enough of the aerial to make this call. I haven't checked the whole hull."

"I'll write it down."

"Gohan's here too. He has something to tell Chi-Chi."

She passes him the communicator. Gohan holds it close.

"Puar?"

"Gohan! Chi-Chi will be so glad to hear you called."

"We stopped Freeza. He's unconscious, but he's still alive. Goshin and Piccolo are watching him at the lake. They were standing when I left."

Puar's pencil scratches quickly. "And you?"

"I'm all right. My shoulder got hit, but I can move it. Mother sent us a message through King Kai. Please tell her we heard it."

He pauses. The line crackles in his ear.

"Tell her I want to come home. We still have people here to find first."

"I will. Dr. Brief and Chi-Chi are looking at the other pod, but they don't know if they can use it."

"Thank you. Tell Dad too, if you can."

Bulma leans toward the communicator. "Tell my father I can call again from here for now. I still have the route I saved. And tell Yamcha I'm all right."

"I'll call the laboratory as soon as we hang up," Puar says. "They can get word to them."

"Don't say the ship is ready to fly," Bulma adds. "I haven't checked."

"I won't."

The line crackles again. Gohan wants to keep listening, but Bulma is watching the aerial through the narrow window, and Sela is waiting beside the hatch.

"We'll call when we can," he says. "Please tell Mother I love her."

"I will," Puar answers softly.

Gohan gives the communicator back. Bulma ends the call and rests her forehead briefly against the panel.

"One thing works," she says. "Now I get to find out about the rest."

Puar places the receiver down, reads his note once to make sure he has not missed anything, and calls Capsule Corporation. He gives the receptionist the message and asks her to repeat it back. When she has, he thanks her and returns to watching the telephone.

## News Carried Two Ways

At the quarry, Goku hears King Kai while Vegeta is still talking.

*Goku. Piccolo says they've stopped Freeza. He is alive and unconscious. Goshin and Piccolo are watching him; Gohan is going to look for Bulma and the villagers.*

Goku lets out a breath so suddenly that Krillin turns toward him.

*The boys?*

*Piccolo said Goshin was with him and Gohan was leaving to look. I didn't ask for a full account of their injuries or the village.*

"They stopped him," Goku says aloud. "Piccolo and Goshin are watching him. Gohan's going to find the others."

Krillin grips his shoulder. "They did it?"

"Piccolo says Freeza's unconscious."

Vegeta gets to his feet. Goku moves with him at once.

"You left him alive?" Vegeta demands, as though the fighters on Namek can hear him. "If he wakes up, you may not get another chance!"

"They know he's dangerous. They're watching him."

"And the ball?"

"King Kai didn't say."

Vegeta studies his face. If Freeza is truly down, there is an opportunity on Namek that will not last. Kakarot is still standing between him and the quarry road.

"Get me a ship," he says. "I can finish what those children started."

"No. You're not taking a ship to go after their people."

"You don't know what I would do."

"I know you tried to get past me. Sit down, Vegeta."

For a moment the prince looks ready to refuse. Then he lowers himself against the stone, his eyes never leaving Goku.

Tien calls across the tracks, and Goku repeats the report for him and Kami. Kami's grip tightens on his staff. He looks toward the horizon, though Namek is far beyond anything he can see.

"Piccolo heard my thanks?" he asks quietly.

*He did,* King Kai tells him. *He asked me to tell you so.*

Kami closes his eyes. "Good."

At the pod site, Yamcha returns with the second instrument case. Chi-Chi comes toward him before his feet have settled on the ground.

"Did you hear from the house?"

"Puar called the laboratory. The receptionist gave me the note before I left." Yamcha unfolds it. "Gohan spoke to him. He said they stopped Freeza, and Goshin and Piccolo were watching him when Gohan left."

Chi-Chi takes hold of the paper. Her eyes move over the lines, then go back to the beginning.

"Gohan's shoulder was hit. He told Puar he could move it." Yamcha points to the next line. "He heard your message. He said he wants to come home, but they're still looking for people. And he said he loves you."

Chi-Chi presses the paper against her chest. She takes a breath that catches halfway, then laughs once through the tears in her eyes.

"Oh, Gohan."

She looks up. "Did he say Goshin was hurt?"

"He didn't give Puar an injury report for Goshin. He said Goshin and Piccolo were on their feet when he left."

"All right." She wipes her face with her sleeve. "That's what we know."

Dr. Brief reads the line about Bulma and the aerial. He smiles, then looks toward the closed pod. "She still has the route. That's good."

"And she says she hasn't checked the whole ship," Yamcha adds. "She didn't want us thinking it's ready."

The doctor opens the case Yamcha brought. "Then we'll both keep checking."

Chi-Chi picks up the long pole from beside the first scanner. "Tell me where you want this one."

He fastens the second instrument to the pole. They extend it slowly toward the marked place near the hatch while Yamcha watches from the side. The first scanner responds again; the second gives a different reading.

Dr. Brief studies both. "That tells me the instruments aren't reacting in the same way. It doesn't tell me yet what's causing the first response."

"Then what do we try next?" Chi-Chi asks.

He takes his pencil from behind his ear and turns to a clean page. She holds the pole steady while he begins to make a plan.

## Beneath the Young Trees

Gohan follows Kado beneath the bank. Sela comes with them as far as the spring, then waits while Kado checks the turn ahead.

"You don't have to take me straight to them," Gohan says. "I can wait here until you know the path is clear."

Kado disappears around the bend. He returns with his companion a little later; both look behind Gohan before they beckon him forward.

Cargo sees him first. "Gohan! Kado said you went back!"

Gohan kneels to meet him. "I did. We stopped Freeza. Goshin and Piccolo are watching him now."

Dende's hands rise to his mouth. "Is Goshin hurt?"

"His shoulder hurt while we were fighting, and he was breathing hard afterward. He could fly and stand when I left. Piccolo's arm was burned too, but he could move his fingers."

"I can help them," Dende says at once.

Muri rests a hand lightly on his shoulder. "You may choose to help. We should know the path is safe enough before you go to the lake."

Dende nods, though his eyes stay on Gohan. "Please tell them I want to."

"I will."

The older resident adjusts the covered ball against the tree. Gohan looks away from it and toward the terrace.

"Kado told me Sela saw two people there. I'll help look."

"So will I," Sela says. "I know where we were working."

Kado's companion looks toward the spring. "I'll stay with Muri and the children. If you find someone who can walk, take the long way around before you bring them here."

"And if they can't walk?" Sela asks.

"We'll carry them," Gohan says. "We won't leave them there."

Cargo reaches for Gohan's sleeve. "You're coming back?"

"Yes. If we find them, we'll bring them to Dende."

Cargo lets go. Sela leads Gohan and Kado back along the bank, toward the terrace where she last saw the two workers. Behind them, Muri and the others remain under the young trees.

## The Broken Scouter

Near the village, Zarbon opens his eyes.

The ground is hard against his back. He rolls onto one side and tries to draw a full breath; the dent in his armour presses painfully against his ribs. The child who struck him is nowhere in sight.

Zarbon pushes himself upright. His scouter lies in pieces against a stone. He picks up the broken lens, turns it in his hand and throws it down again.

A low sound reaches him from the direction of the lake, followed by silence. Before the child broke his scouter, Zarbon had read two enormous powers inland, but he never saw who they belonged to. He still doesn't know where Freeza is or how the fight ended.

The elder had been carrying something under a cloth. Zarbon remembers the size of the bundle and the path the elder took behind the wall.

He follows the disturbed ground around the damaged houses. At the grove he hears a man's voice before he sees the tree.

"Help! Over here!"

A soldier is bound by the wrists to the trunk. His own scouter hangs broken at his cheek. Zarbon recognizes the uniform, if not the man inside it.

"Where is the elder?" Zarbon asks.

"He went down that path with the bundle. The child came after them later. I couldn't stop him."

Zarbon hooks a finger under the straps and tears them apart. The soldier stumbles free, rubbing his wrists.

"The child is stronger than he looks," Zarbon says. "If you see him, do not attack. I want to know where the elder went, not hear how bravely you lost him."

The soldier swallows. "Yes, sir."

"Keep to the trees. If you find a working scouter, bring it to me. Don't transmit until I say so."

He looks once more toward the lake. His own device cannot tell him whether Freeza is there. The trail beneath the trees leads away from the houses; for now, it is the only direction the soldier can give him.

Zarbon steps onto the path, and the soldier follows.
