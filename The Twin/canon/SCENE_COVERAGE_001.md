## Provisional craft observations, not compulsory mannerisms

- **Gohan:** The caption and synopsis place fear, curiosity and pursuit in the same forest sequence; the selected stills show visibly different expressions. In prose, let wonder interrupt fear and let him make choices, while preserving real vulnerability. The duration and sound of the transitions are not known from these sources.
- **Goku and Chi-Chi:** The captioned hungry response sits beside Chi-Chi's practical concern, and the later rescue gives the father something urgent to do. Let ordinary humor, concern and direct action coexist instead of making either parent a single mood. Exact vocal delivery was not heard.
- **Piccolo and Raditz:** Sensing, challenge, failed attack, interrupted threat and Piccolo's admitted fear have distinct causes. Avoid treating Piccolo as either unafraid or frightened before he has assessed the stranger.
- **Goshin and the family:** The twin is authored, not a canon episode-one character. Give him his own feelings, initiative and established capability without copying Gohan's reactions or making canon events mandatory where his presence plausibly changes them.
