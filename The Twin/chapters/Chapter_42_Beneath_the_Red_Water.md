# Chapter 42 — Beneath the Red Water

*Anime episode reference: Dragon Ball Z, Episodes 80–83, with selected combat elements from Episodes 87–88, adapted to the changed branch.*

## Beneath the Surface

The lake closes over Goshin's head.

For an instant he can see nothing but the red light around his arms and a rush of bubbles. Then the water clears. Far below, Freeza's pale body cuts through the dark blue toward a shelf of stone.

Goshin kicks and follows. The Kaiō-ken drives through his legs, carrying him downward faster than the bubbles rising past his face. Freeza looks back. His chipped horn leaves a broken white line in the red glow.

A finger points at Goshin.

The thin beam flashes through the water. Goshin twists. It passes his cheek, strikes the lakebed and rips a trench through the rock. Mud billows between them.

Freeza vanishes inside it.

Goshin can still feel him. He changes direction before Freeza comes out of the cloud and catches the larger fighter's punch on his forearm. The blow pushes him through the water, but he grips Freeza's wrist, pulls himself close and drives his knee into Freeza's middle.

Freeza folds around it. Goshin hits him again and sends him crashing through the edge of the stone shelf.

The shelf breaks apart. Goshin starts after him, then feels a pressure close around his ankles. The loose rocks jerk upward. One catches his shoulder; another slams against his back.

Freeza spreads his hand. The stones press together around Goshin.

Red light bursts between them. Goshin throws his arms wide, and the stones shoot apart. His father's warning comes back to him: *Don't just throw it everywhere.*

He reaches with his ki and turns the largest piece before it can break the surface toward the village. It drops away to his left. By then Freeza is moving again.

Goshin catches his tail.

Freeza lashes around and strikes him across the face. The impact stings. Goshin's grip holds. He pulls Freeza away from the shore, swings him through the water and lets him go toward the broken island.

Freeza smashes through the island's submerged edge. A cloud of grit rolls outward. Goshin follows it with his eyes narrowed and his tail tucked close. The ×20 rush is still there, fierce in every limb. His heart beats hard, but when he calls on his body to move, it answers.

Freeza comes through the grit with both hands raised. Light blooms between his palms.

Goshin gets under the shot as it leaves them. The blast tears upward, carrying a pillar of water with it. Goshin catches Freeza around the waist and drives him sideways, clear of the pillar, before he can aim again.

They break the surface together.

Goshin draws a deep breath. Water streams off his hair and down his face. Freeza claws at his arms and plants a foot against his stomach. Goshin releases him, flips backward and stops above the lake.

The column of water falls between them with a tremendous crash. Beyond it, the green rise still stands between the fight and the village.

Freeza emerges from the spray. His armour is cracked across the middle, and a dark mark is spreading beneath it. He touches the chipped horn again.

"You dare put your hands on me?"

"You tried to kill my brother!" Goshin's voice shakes with anger. "And those people didn't even know you!"

"I don't need to know them."

Freeza lifts one finger toward the rise.

Goshin is in front of him before the light forms. He strikes the hand aside and punches Freeza in the jaw. Freeza spins; Goshin follows with a second blow to the ribs and a third to the stomach. The last one drives Freeza across the open water.

"You DON'T get to do that!"

The shout comes from somewhere deep in Goshin's chest. His ki surges with it, and the red light flares. He remembers the two children at the village doorway when Dodoria came for them. He remembers the children on the other ship and Freeza saying he could not be expected to remember them.

Freeza catches himself against the broken island. Goshin is already there. He seizes Freeza by the front of the armour and lifts him away from the rock.

"Leave them alone!"

He throws Freeza farther out over the lake. Freeza twists in the air and fires a scatter of narrow shots back at him. Goshin turns two aside with his hands and sends the others into an empty stretch of water. Steam bursts up around the impact points.

One more beam comes through the steam. Goshin ducks; it passes over his hair. Freeza is already dropping toward the far side of the island.

Goshin's body answers with a new rush of strength, deeper than the red light. He cuts across the distance before Freeza reaches the far side and kicks the next shot away from the shore. The strength remains as he stops and raises his hands again.

Freeza stops short. He looks at the place where his shot disappeared, then at Goshin.

"What are you?"

"Goshin."

"That is not what I asked."

"It's my name."

Goshin wipes water from his eyes with the back of his wrist. Freeza's gaze follows the movement, but the child does not lower his guard.

"You can go away," Goshin says. "Tell your men to leave this planet."

Freeza's mouth opens. For a moment no words come out. Then his lips pull back over his teeth.

"You think you can give me orders?"

He comes forward, fast enough to throw spray from the surface below. Goshin meets him. Their fists collide, and the island splits under the force passing between them.

## At the High House

At the open door, Dende takes hold of Piccolo's sleeve. Another tremor has passed through the pillar beneath the Great Elder's house.

"Goshin is still there?" he asks.

"Yes. So is Freeza."

Piccolo keeps his attention on the distant fight. Goshin's ki has risen far above the level Piccolo felt before he entered the house. It moves fiercely against Freeza's, and neither presence has disappeared.

Dende looks back at the Great Elder. "You helped Piccolo bring out more of his power."

"I did," the elder says.

"Could you help me too?"

Nail turns from the doorway. Dende's hands are clenched at his sides, but he does not move back from the elder's gaze.

"I want to help Cargo and Elder Muri," he says. "I don't want them to be hurt again."

The elder beckons him closer. "I can help awaken what is within you. I cannot promise what gift you will find there. Do you wish me to try?"

Dende nods and comes to the chair. The elder rests his broad hand gently on the child's head.

Dende feels a warmth spread through him. It is not like the heat of the sun on his shoulders. It reaches deeper, filling his chest and running out along his arms. He gasps. The room seems suddenly brighter.

When the elder lifts his hand, Dende holds his own hands in front of him. They look the same. His heart is beating quickly, and the warmth has not gone away.

"Thank you," he says.

"Listen to your own heart, Dende. Whatever you do with your strength must be your choice."

Dende bows. Piccolo waits until he reaches the door.

"I'll stay here," Nail says. "The Great Elder asked me to guard him."

Piccolo nods. "If Freeza comes this way, don't assume you know his full power."

"You think I would?"

"I nearly did."

Nail studies him, then looks toward the elder. "I won't leave him unprotected."

Piccolo steps outside. Dende climbs onto his back and holds on. The child looks once over his shoulder. Nail is closing the door behind them, and the old Namekian sits in his chair beyond him.

"Please be safe," Dende calls.

"You too," the elder says.

Piccolo pushes off. The tall pillar falls away beneath them, and the wind rushes around Dende's face as they fly toward Muri's village.

## The People Behind the Wall

Gohan keeps hold of the beam he has carried aside until the two villagers have cleared the gap. The second man is favouring one arm. His companion supports him, though he keeps trying to walk by himself.

"I can do it," he says.

"I know," she replies. "You can do it with my help."

Bulma leads them around the low wall. Muri follows with the wrapped ball held against his chest. Gohan lays the beam down across the broken path instead of dropping it; he has heard movement near the houses and wants a clear view before anything else falls.

The injured villager looks at his arm. A long scrape runs from elbow to wrist, with blood and dirt at its edges. Bulma opens her case again and offers him a clean piece of cloth.

"Press this against it. We'll find water when we're clear."

He takes it. "Thank you."

Cargo appears above the rim of the hollow. "Elder Muri!"

"Stay down," the older villager beside him says, pulling him gently back.

Muri raises a hand to show he has seen them. "We are coming."

Gohan feels Goshin's ki surge at the lake. The ground gives a small shiver under his boots. He turns toward the sound, then looks back at the villagers.

"I'll get them there," he says. "After that I want to go to Goshin."

"You should," Bulma says. "But don't leave them in the open first."

"I won't."

A stone clicks behind the beam. Gohan turns.

One of the soldiers who heard Muri name the Dragon Ball is on his knees beside the house. His scouter is broken, and he has one hand pressed to his side. He sees the round bundle in Muri's arms and reaches out with the other hand.

"The ball!"

A small bolt flashes from his palm. Gohan knocks it into the beam. The wood splinters but stays across the path. Muri pulls the injured villager behind him.

"Get down!" Bulma calls.

The soldier scrambles up. He tries to fire again, but Gohan reaches him first. He catches the raised wrist and turns it toward the ground.

"Stop!"

The man swings at his face. Gohan ducks, pulls him away from the houses and sets him down hard beyond the beam. The soldier's breath leaves him. Before he can recover, Muri steps forward and strikes the ground beside his hand with a narrow bolt. The soldier snatches his fingers back.

"You will not touch my people again," Muri says.

The man looks from the elder to Gohan. He rolls onto his side and stays there, breathing hard.

Gohan backs toward the others, watching him. "He can still get up."

"Then we keep moving," Muri says.

They guide the villagers into the hollow. Cargo hurries to make room for them beside the older resident. Bulma helps the injured man sit with his back against the rock, then peers over the edge toward the path. The soldier is still where Gohan left him. Farther away, Zarbon lies in the open and has not risen.

"We need someplace better than this," she says.

"There is a grove beyond the next ridge," Muri answers. "The ground dips there. We can reach it without crossing the open path."

"Do you know if anyone else went there?"

"Some may have. The workers were outside the village when Freeza arrived. I have not seen them come back."

Gohan looks at the houses. A door hangs open, rocking against its frame. He cannot feel every small presence clearly through the larger powers at the lake. If someone is hiding and afraid to answer, they might still be inside.

"I'll check the nearest houses on the way," he says.

A familiar ki approaches from above the trees. Gohan looks up only as far as the branches.

"Piccolo!"

Piccolo drops into the clearing with Dende on his back. Dende slides down before his feet have fully touched the ground.

"Cargo!"

His brother scrambles out of the hollow. They run to each other, and Dende catches him around the shoulders. Cargo buries his face against him.

"I thought you weren't coming back," he says.

"I'm here now."

Dende pulls back to look at him. "Your knee!"

"Bulma gave me a cloth."

"I can see that. Does it hurt?"

"A little."

Dende hugs him again, more carefully this time. Muri comes forward, still carrying the bundle.

"You reached the Great Elder?"

"Yes. I told him Freeza and his men came. Nail is guarding him."

Piccolo looks toward the lake. "He knew about Kami's family. I need to tell Kami when we can reach him."

Muri's expression softens. "I am glad you found an answer."

Gohan is watching Piccolo. The Namekian's ki is much greater than it was when he carried Dende away.

"You're stronger!"

Piccolo flexes one hand. "The elder brought out power I hadn't reached. Don't ask me how much."

"That's wonderful!" Gohan says. Then he looks toward the hollow. "One of the villagers is hurt."

Dende follows his gaze. At the Great Elder's house he asked for strength because he wanted to help his people, but he does not yet know what the warmth in his hands means.

"Can I help him?" he asks.

Bulma steps aside. "He's just here."

## A Small Light

The injured villager is still pressing the cloth to his arm. Dende kneels beside him.

"May I look?"

The man lifts the cloth. Bulma moves closer, ready to hold it again if he needs her. The scrape is not deep, but the dirt has worked into it, and his hand trembles when he turns his wrist.

Dende places his hands over the injury without quite touching it. When he was smaller, he had tried to help someone with a cut this way. Nothing had happened. He remembers how disappointed he was, and how the person had thanked him for trying anyway.

He closes his eyes. The warmth the elder woke in him gathers beneath his palms.

A soft light appears between his hands and the villager's arm. Dende opens his eyes wide. Bulma leans forward; the man looks from the light to Dende's face.

"Does it hurt?" Dende asks.

"No. It's warm."

The dirt loosens as the scrape closes beneath the light. Dende keeps his hands steady until the raw line has narrowed and the bleeding has stopped. Then the light fades. A faint mark remains, but the man can turn his wrist without flinching.

Dende sits back on his heels. "It worked."

Cargo crouches beside him. "You did that?"

Dende looks at his hands. "I think so."

The man flexes his fingers. "I can carry something now. Elder Muri, let me take the bundle."

Muri rests a hand on his shoulder. "Walk beside us first. You have only just got to your feet."

Dende turns to Cargo. "May I see your knee?"

Cargo uncovers it. The scrape is smaller than the one on the man's arm. Dende places his hands over it, and the little light returns. Cargo watches until the sore skin closes.

"It doesn't hurt anymore," he says, astonished.

Dende beams. "Good!"

A crash rolls across the ridge. The children turn toward it. Gohan feels Goshin's ki and Freeza's coming together again, closer to the surface now.

"I have to go," he says.

Piccolo has already turned toward the lake. "I'm coming too."

Bulma stands. "And who's staying with them? That soldier is awake, Zarbon could wake up, and we still don't know where Dodoria is."

Gohan stops. He had been about to leave the hollow without looking back at the path.

Muri shifts the wrapped ball against his chest. "We can move together. I can watch the path, and these two can help the others."

The villager whose arm Dende healed gets to his feet beside his companion. The older resident takes Cargo's hand. Dende stays close to his brother.

"I'll check the house by the ridge," Gohan says. "If there's anyone inside, I'll bring them here. Then we'll help you get to the grove."

"I'll take the other house," Piccolo says.

Bulma looks at them both. "Then go. We'll be ready."

They return quickly. Piccolo finds an empty room and a back door standing open. Gohan finds no one in the house by the ridge, though he calls twice and checks behind the fallen table. They cannot search every building before moving the people already with them.

Gohan and Piccolo guide the group along the sheltered side of the ridge. Muri leads them into a dip among the trees, where the branches screen the hollow from the village path. The two villagers choose a place from which they can watch both approaches. Bulma keeps her case; Muri keeps the ball.

"If you have to move again, don't wait for us," Piccolo tells them.

"We won't," Muri says.

Dende looks toward the lake. "Gohan, please bring your brother back."

Gohan gives him a quick nod. "I will."

He and Piccolo rise above the trees, keeping their eyes on the land ahead. Gohan's tail is wound close around his waist. He feels Goshin's fierce ki and flies toward it.

## The Other Pod

The remaining Saiyan pod sits in a broken street behind a line of barriers. A police officer moves one barrier aside for Dr. Brief's vehicle, then puts it back as soon as they have passed.

Chi-Chi gets out before the engine has stopped. The round craft is larger than it looked in the photographs. Dust coats one side; fragments of shattered pavement are piled against its base.

"That's the one?" she asks.

"That's the one we haven't touched," the officer says. "Everyone kept back after the other object exploded."

Krillin looks at the pod, then at the buildings on either side. Their windows are broken, but people have begun covering some of them with boards.

"Are the people out of those buildings?"

"The nearest ones, yes. We haven't finished checking every room farther down."

Yamcha lifts the larger case from the vehicle. "We'll keep our distance until the doctor says otherwise."

Dr. Brief sets the portable scanner on the pavement. He studies the pod without opening it, then passes the scanner slowly along the outside. Its needle flickers near the hatch.

"There's a field around the seam," he says. "I don't know yet whether it's from an active system or something left over from the explosion."

Chi-Chi watches the instrument while he watches the craft.

"Can it fly?" she asks.

"I don't know yet."

He crouches beside a damaged section of the outer shell. "This could be superficial. There may also be damage I can't see from here. And I haven't tested its controls or its air supply."

"Can you test them?"

"Perhaps. First I want to know whether it is safe to approach the hatch."

Chi-Chi looks at the seam running around the pod. On Namek, Gohan and Goshin have a ship. She knows that much from Piccolo's message. She does not know whether the people who found it have left it alone, or whether the boys can reach it.

"What do you need?" she asks.

Dr. Brief points to the smaller case. "The long cable, please. Don't connect it yet."

She brings it to him. Krillin sets the case beside the scanner, and Yamcha holds the loose end out of the dust.

"If this works," Krillin says, "could it take all of us?"

Dr. Brief glances at the round shell. "I wouldn't promise even one passenger until we understand it."

Chi-Chi grips the cable. She wants to tell him to hurry, but his hand is steady and his eyes are fixed on the readings. She lays the cable where he can reach it.

"Tell me what to do next."

Far from the city, Puar waits beside the telephone at the Son house. The paper Chi-Chi left lies beneath his paws. He looks at the silent receiver, then at the front door, and stays where he can hear either one.

## Three Against Freeza

Goshin drives Freeza down onto the broken island. Stone erupts around the larger fighter. Before it can fall across the lake toward the village, Goshin lifts a hand and turns the worst of it aside.

Freeza springs out of the crater and catches Goshin with an elbow. Goshin's head snaps to one side. He answers with a punch that drives Freeza back through the spray, then follows so quickly that Freeza barely gets his arms up in time.

The red light washes across Freeza's face. He blocks one blow, takes another against his shoulder and lashes out with his tail. Goshin ducks, catches it for an instant and pulls. Freeza's feet leave the air; he twists before Goshin can throw him again and tears free.

"You could have left when I told you to!" Goshin says.

Freeza's eyes narrow. "And leave your little friends behind? We were just getting acquainted."

"Stay away from them!"

Freeza glances past him. Two figures are coming over the rise: a green Namekian and another small boy with a tail. Freeza recognizes them from the village. His hand lifts toward Gohan.

"No!"

Goshin strikes his arm. The shot goes wide, hissing across the lake. Freeza uses his other hand at once, sending a second beam past Goshin toward the approaching figures.

Piccolo pulls Gohan aside. The beam cuts through the place where they were flying.

"He moved his hand before he looked," Piccolo says. "Don't wait for his eyes."

Gohan nods. Freeza's presence is enormous. Even after the strength Gohan found against Zarbon, it presses on him from across the water. Goshin is between them, his red aura burning fiercely, but Freeza keeps looking for a path around him.

Gohan remembers the water he used to open Freeza's guard before. He drops low and fires into the lake at an angle. A sheet of spray rises between Freeza and the approaching fighters.

Freeza fires through it. Piccolo swerves, extends an arm and catches Gohan around the waist, pulling him clear. The beam passes where Gohan was about to emerge. Piccolo lets him go as soon as they stop.

"I'm all right," Gohan says.

"Then watch his hands."

Goshin comes through the spray from the other side. His fist catches Freeza under the ribs and lifts him into the air. Freeza tries to close an invisible grip around him; Goshin cries out and tears through the pressure, then strikes him across the jaw.

Freeza crashes onto the island again. This time he stays down long enough to look at the three of them. Water runs from his armour into the cracks in the stone.

"The Namekian has come back," he says. "Did you bring me your Dragon Ball?"

"No," Piccolo says. "I came for you."

"You came for the people behind us," Gohan says.

Piccolo glances at him. "I can do both."

Goshin lands on the far side of the crater. "Gohan, is everyone all right?"

"We got some of them to a grove. Muri and Bulma are with them. Dende came back too!"

Goshin's face brightens. "Dende came back!"

"Piccolo brought him. And Dende healed two people!"

"He did?"

Freeza pushes himself upright. "A healer? How useful."

Gohan's pleasure turns cold. He had been so glad to tell his brother that he had not thought about who else could hear.

Goshin moves between them and Freeza. His relief does not make the anger go away.

"Stay away from Dende," he says.

Freeza looks from Goshin to Gohan. "The other child is far weaker. You must spend a great deal of time keeping him alive."

Gohan's hands tighten. "I'm right here."

He fires a quick burst at Freeza's raised hand. It does not drive Freeza back, but the flash catches his attention. Piccolo uses that instant to move across his line of sight.

Freeza flicks a shot at Piccolo. The Namekian twists clear, and Goshin is upon Freeza again, driving him away from both of them.

"I said leave them ALONE!"

Goshin roars and hits him twice. Freeza blocks the first blow; the second cracks the plate at his shoulder and sends him skidding over the island. Goshin feels stronger, quicker than he did beneath the water. He keeps the red rush close and follows.

Freeza stops himself at the island's edge. His tail whips water into the air. He stares at the broken armour, then at Goshin.

"Very well," he says, in a voice that has gone dangerously quiet. "Now you will see what I can do."

## The Shape He Chooses

Freeza raises both hands. A fan of beams flashes outward, not at Goshin but toward the shore and the two fighters approaching from that side.

Goshin moves into their paths. Piccolo calls, "Gohan, the left!"

Gohan answers with a burst of his own. His shots clip the nearest beam from the side and turn it into the lake. Piccolo fires across another, nudging it upward, away from the land. Goshin knocks the remaining lights together, breaking them above the water.

Freeza is already gathering power. The air around him tightens, and light spreads across the island.

"He's changing again!" Gohan says.

Goshin darts forward. He does not wait for Freeza to finish. His fist strikes the bright pressure around the larger body and drives through it; the light buckles, and his next punch lands against Freeza's chest.

Freeza snarls. His body is shifting beneath the broken armour. He catches Goshin's wrist, but Goshin twists free and strikes him across the face. A piece of white plate breaks away and tumbles into the lake.

The pressure grows suddenly greater. Goshin feels it batter against his red aura. He plants both feet in the air and pushes through, reaching for Freeza again.

Freeza sends a blast from his free hand. Goshin knocks it upward. In the same movement he drives his shoulder into Freeza, forcing him back across the stone. The changing fighter digs his fingers into Goshin's arm and throws him off.

Goshin rolls in midair and comes back. The red aura flares around him; he can hear Gohan calling his name, but he keeps his eyes on Freeza.

Freeza's head stretches into a long white dome. His shoulders hunch, and jagged plates rise behind them as the remaining armour splits. The power coming from him swells with the change.

Piccolo feels it and pulls Gohan farther from the island. "Keep moving."

"Goshin's still fighting him!"

"I know. Give him room to see us."

Freeza's hand closes around Goshin's next punch. For the first time since the underwater pursuit, his fingers stop it without being immediately driven back. Goshin pushes; Freeza bares his teeth and pushes in return. The island trembles below them.

Goshin yells and brings his other fist around. Freeza releases the first hand to block. The blow lands against his guard, and both fighters are thrown apart.

Goshin catches himself above the water. His chest rises and falls, and red light still burns around him. He can feel Gohan and Piccolo behind him, alive and moving. He holds his tail close and brings both hands up.

Freeza settles over the broken island in his new shape. A dark mark shows where Goshin struck him during the change. He touches it, looks at his fingers and smiles through sharp teeth.

"Shall we begin again?"

Goshin flies at him.
