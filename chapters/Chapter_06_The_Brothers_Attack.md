## COLD OPEN

The scouter's green display flickers as dust rises around Raditz's boot. Gohan comes in from one side, and Goshin closes from the other.

**THE BROTHERS' ATTACK**

## 1 · THE TWO READINGS

**NARRATOR:** Raditz has cut away his own tail. Goku is trapped beneath him, and Piccolo has one attack left.

Gohan runs past the broken slab while Goshin flies low on Raditz's other side. Piccolo watches from the far slope, two fingers against his forehead.

The wall behind Gohan has split where Raditz's last blast struck it. The ground beneath Raditz slopes toward the crater floor, and Piccolo's line of fire crosses the place where Goku is pinned.

Raditz turns his head toward Gohan. The scouter sounds again.

"A thousand?"

The numbers climb.

"One thousand three hundred and seven!"

Gohan drives forward. Raditz raises his hand, but the blast Goshin fired has already forced his arm aside.

Goku pushes against the boot on his chest. "Gohan, get back!"

Raditz looks toward Goshin. Another number flashes across the lens.

"Broc. Fifteen hundred?"

Goshin's feet leave the ground as the fingers of his burned hand close. He does not take his eyes off Raditz's raised arm.

"Both of you, run!" Goku calls.

Gohan shouts, "Get away from my father!"

He jumps.

His head strikes the middle of Raditz's chest. The armour cracks under him, and Raditz's boot lifts from Goku.

Raditz stumbles backward and tries to catch Gohan by the collar. Gohan pushes against the damaged armour and pulls away before Raditz's hand can close. Goshin reaches the injured shoulder from the other side.

Goshin hits him there. The blow turns Raditz away from Gohan, and both boys fall clear as Raditz crashes against a shelf of rock.

Goku rolls onto his side and gets one elbow beneath him, then stops with his hand pressed to his ribs.

Piccolo lowers his chin while light still gathers around his fingers.

"Gohan," Goku says.

Gohan has landed on his knees. He touches his forehead, then looks toward his father and crawls across the loose ground.

A piece of Raditz's broken armour slides down the rock beside him. Gohan stops when Raditz moves, but Goku reaches a hand out from where he lies.

"Father! Are you all right?"

"Get away from him. Take Goshin and go."

Gohan turns toward Raditz, who is pulling himself upright with one hand against the broken armour.

Goshin lands between Raditz and his brother. Raditz swings at him with his good arm, and Goshin meets the blow with both hands.

The impact drives Goshin back, and his burned palm opens. He catches Raditz's wrist with the other hand and fires at the ground beside his boot.

Raditz pulls against his grip and tries to bring his free arm across Goshin's face. Goshin lets go of the wrist and drops beneath the blow. The rock bursts upward, and Raditz jumps through the dust.

Gohan scrambles to his feet. "Goshin!"

Goshin rises after Raditz. A kick catches him across the side and sends him toward the crater wall. He turns in the air, plants his feet against the rock and pushes off again.

Raditz glances at the scouter, which has settled over Gohan.

"One? Now it's only one?"

Gohan backs toward his father and reaches down for Goku's hand.

"It changes with his feelings," Raditz says. "That little brat did this to me."

He presses his hand over the cracked centre of his armour. Goshin drops toward him, and Raditz sends a blast across the gap between them.

Goshin twists aside as the blast hits the wall behind him and breaks a section loose. He raises an arm over his head as stones fall around him.

Raditz lands in front of Gohan.

"I'll kill you before you can do it again."

Gohan picks up a stone and throws it at Raditz's raised hand, turning the palm a little to one side.

Raditz slaps him across the shoulder. Gohan falls beside Goku and rolls onto his back, with one hand against his shoulder.

"He's a child!" Goku says.

"That child hurt me worse than any of you. The other one's next."

Raditz raises his hand again. Goshin bursts out through the falling stones and comes toward him.

Goku gets to his feet behind Raditz.

## 2 · THE HOLD

Goku reaches under Raditz's arms and locks his hands behind his brother's neck. Raditz's blast flies over Gohan and strikes the empty slope.

Goku's feet slide on the loose ground, and Raditz drags him a step toward the crater. Goku sets his heel against a stone and pulls backward until Raditz stops.

"Piccolo!" Goku calls. "Use it now!"

Raditz twists against him, but Goku plants both feet and hangs on.

Goshin lands beside Gohan and lifts his brother by the arm, then steps in front of him as Raditz struggles.

"Are you hurt?" Goshin asks.

Gohan holds his shoulder. "I can stand. Father can't get away."

Across the crater, Piccolo's fingers shine against his forehead.

"I need a little longer," he says. "Keep him there."

Raditz drives an elbow backward. It catches Goku against the ribs, and Goku cries out, but his hands stay locked.

Raditz tries the other elbow. Goshin fires at the moving arm, and the blow glances off Goku's shoulder instead. Raditz kicks out at Goshin, who pulls back before the boot reaches him.

"Kakarot, let go!"

"Not this time."

"If he fires, he'll hit you too."

"I know."

Gohan takes a step toward them. Goshin catches his sleeve, but Gohan pulls it free.

"Father!"

"Stay back, Gohan."

Raditz pulls at Goku's arm. The cracked plates on his chest shift beneath his hand.

"A Saiyan warrior brought down by children," he says. "What are they, Kakarot?"

"They're my boys."

"Their mother is from this planet?"

"That's right. Leave them alone."

Raditz braces his boots and tries to lift Goku off the ground, but Goku tightens his grip. Raditz gets one foot beneath him, but his damaged shoulder gives and he drops back.

"Your son's blow has taken my strength," Raditz says. "Let me go. I'll leave this planet."

Goku shakes his head. "You said that already."

"I'm your brother!"

"You hurt Gohan. You hurt Goshin."

Raditz stamps against Goku's foot, and Goku gasps as he nearly loses the hold. Goshin fires at Raditz's boot; the blast knocks it aside before he can stamp again.

"Keep away from him!" Goku calls.

"Keep away from him!" Goshin repeats. He does not move closer, but stays on Raditz's right with his good hand raised. Gohan reaches the broken wall on the other side and looks back toward Goku.

Piccolo takes a step down the slope as the light at his fingers grows brighter, but Goku is directly behind Raditz.

"Goku, when it's ready, I'll fire. You know where the shot will go."

"He means it, Kakarot," Raditz says. "That creature wants you dead as much as I do."

"He's right about that," Piccolo says. "If you hold him, I can get rid of both of you at once."

"Do it," Goku says. "I'll hold him."

Raditz turns toward Piccolo. "He's going to kill you as well!"

"If that's what it takes."

Gohan grips the edge of the wall. "No."

The ground drops away behind the broken stone. Gohan crouches and looks through the gap toward his father, then clears a loose rock from the place where Goku might land.

Goku looks toward him. "Gohan, listen to me."

"Father, there's room here. The ground goes down behind the rock."

"You have to get out of the way."

"I can pull you down."

Goshin looks from Gohan to Raditz's feet. He starts toward Goku's arm, then stops when Piccolo shifts his aim and the light crosses the ground between them.

He moves back to Raditz's right, where he can see the nearer boot without standing behind him. Goshin cups both hands at his side as he did for the wave, and a little light gathers between them.

Raditz struggles again, dragging Goku across the place where the attack would go. Goshin closes his hands and lets the light fade; then he aims his good hand at the nearer boot.

"Goshin?" Goku says.

"When I hit his leg, let go."

Raditz tries to turn toward him, but Goku holds the shoulder that Goshin struck.

Piccolo's fingers brighten. "Decide quickly. I won't lose the shot for either of you."

## 3 · ONE CLEAR LINE

Goku looks at the broken wall, where Gohan is crouched with one hand out toward him.

"Goshin," Goku says. "Hit his leg, then get clear."

"Hit his leg, then get clear."

"Gohan, move back when I come."

Gohan shifts behind the rock but keeps his hand out.

Raditz pulls against Goku's grip. "You won't get away in time."

"Maybe not," Goku says.

The scouter sounds as Raditz looks toward Piccolo, and his face tightens.

"One thousand four hundred and eighty!"

Piccolo draws his hand away from his forehead, and the light follows his fingers.

"It's ready."

Raditz throws his weight backward. Goku takes the strain on his injured side and drops to one knee, but he keeps both arms around him.

Gohan picks up another stone and throws it at Raditz's face.

The stone strikes his cheek. Raditz turns toward Gohan, and Goshin fires at his leg.

Goku holds on until the flash reaches Raditz's leg. He has to let go of both arms at once, or Raditz will pull him back into Piccolo's path.

The blast hits the outside of Raditz's knee, and his boot slips across the loose ground.

"Now!" Gohan calls.

Goku releases him and falls toward the wall. Raditz catches at his sleeve, but Gohan takes Goku's hand and pulls while Goku rolls down behind the rock.

As Raditz lunges after them, Goshin flies in from the other side and strikes his injured shoulder again.

Raditz swings at him, but Goshin ducks and drives both hands against Raditz's arm. The burned palm slips; Raditz tears free and raises his good hand toward the boy.

Goshin fires into the ground at Raditz's feet and springs back toward the crater wall.

Raditz jumps away from the blast, but his damaged leg buckles when he lands.

Piccolo points across the crater, with Goku and Gohan below the broken wall and Goshin clear on the far side.

"Makankosappo!"

The beam leaves his fingers, its spiral cutting through the dust toward Raditz.

Raditz pushes off his good foot, but the injured leg drags for an instant.

The beam strikes his chest, passes through and cuts into the distant slope, then vanishes.

Stone breaks away where the beam entered the ridge. For a moment the crater fills with falling dust, and Gohan presses himself against Goku while the broken wall shakes around them.

Raditz stands with his arms apart. His raised hand falls, and the loose pieces of his armour settle at his feet.

Goshin lands beside the wall and looks down at his father and brother.

"Father?"

Goku lifts his head, keeping one arm over Gohan.

"I'm here."

Gohan puts both hands on Goku's sleeve. "It missed you."

Goku tries to rise and falls back against the rock. Goshin starts down toward him, but Goku points across the crater.

"Watch Raditz."

Raditz is still standing, but he takes one step and falls onto his back.

Piccolo lowers his hand. He stays where he is until Raditz stops moving, then walks down the slope.

## 4 · WHAT THE SCOUTER HEARD

Goshin comes down beside Goku, and Gohan moves over to make room. Goshin takes the downhill side of his brother.

Goku tries to put his weight on one hand, but Gohan catches his shoulder. A strip of torn orange cloth has folded beneath him, and Gohan pulls it straight so it will not press against his side.

"Can you breathe?" Gohan asks.

"A little. Don't pull on that arm."

Gohan lets go at once, then takes off his hat and presses it against his chest.

Goku turns toward Goshin. "Is your head all right?"

Goshin looks at Goku's torn shirt. "Your ribs?"

"I think they're broken."

"I'll get the cloud," Goshin says.

Goku catches his sleeve. "Wait. Stay with Gohan."

Piccolo reaches Raditz, whose scouter remains over one eye with its lens lit against his face.

Raditz's hand moves across the ground, and he tries to lift his head but cannot.

"Kakarot," he says. "You should have died with me."

Goku braces a hand against the wall. "It's over."

Raditz looks past Piccolo toward the boys. "You haven't won anything. The other two will come. They'll kill you and everyone on this planet."

Piccolo folds his remaining arm across his chest. "They'll have to find us first."

"They're stronger than I am. Both of them."

"Even if they kill Goku, he won't stay dead. This planet has the Dragon Balls."

Goku looks up. "Piccolo."

Piccolo keeps his eyes on Raditz. "Gather them, and a wish can be granted. They can bring the dead back."

Raditz's mouth opens, and he draws a breath before beginning to laugh.

"What's funny?" Piccolo asks.

"You fool."

The scouter gives a small tone.

"This thing doesn't just measure power," Raditz says. "Everything that's happened here has been sent to my comrades. They've heard you."

Piccolo bends toward him. "Sent where?"

"Into space. They'll come for your Dragon Balls. They'll wipe this planet clean, and then they'll bring me back."

Goku gets a foot beneath himself, but Goshin catches his arm before he can stand.

"How long?" Goku asks.

Raditz turns his head toward him. "One year."

"A year?"

"That's all you have. Enjoy it while you can."

Piccolo looks at the scouter. "Two stronger than you."

"You couldn't stop me without all of them. What will you do when there are two?"

Gohan puts his hat back on. The ball at its crown catches the light as he leans toward Goku.

"Father, don't get up."

Raditz's fingers curl, and a point of light gathers in his palm, low against the ground.

Gohan sees it. "His hand!"

Goshin moves between Raditz and his brother. Goku reaches for Goshin, but Piccolo has already raised his own hand.

"Piccolo, wait!" Goku says.

As Raditz lifts his arm toward the boys, Piccolo fires.

The blast strikes Raditz in the chest, and his hand falls open as its light goes out.

Goshin keeps his hand raised. Piccolo walks to Raditz and looks down at him.

The scouter continues to glow.

"He's dead," Piccolo says.

He removes the scouter from Raditz's ear and presses a small switch on its side. The display goes dark, and he puts it on a flat stone away from the body.

"Can they still hear us?" Goku asks.

"I don't know. Don't tell them anything else."

Goku stays against the wall. "You could have stopped his hand."

"He was going to shoot your sons."

"You didn't have to kill him."

"You told me to fire through him, and you couldn't stand to stop him now."

"I know what I asked you. I didn't want him killed like this."

"He wasn't going to stop."

Goku looks at Raditz while Gohan kneels beside him and Goshin lowers his hand.

"He was your brother, wasn't he?" Gohan asks.

"Yeah."

"He wanted to take us away."

Goku reaches for Gohan's hand and holds it against the rock. "He won't be able to do that again."

Gohan glances at the body, then back at his father. "He said the others would come for us. Will they hurt Mother?"

"We'll tell her what he said, and we'll find a way to keep her safe."

No one goes closer.

## 5 · THE TRUCK

At the mountain house, Chi-Chi sets a knife beside the cutting board. Two small shirts hang on the line outside, and the paper with the red circle is still fixed to the doorframe.

She looks toward the road.

"They'll be having a fine time at Master Roshi's," she says. "Gohan has good manners. He'll be all right."

She takes one of the shirts from the line and checks the stitching at the shoulder. The other shirt is still damp at the cuff, so she leaves it to dry.

"Goshin will listen too, if the old man has anything to tell him. I hope they've kept their hands clean before eating."

She straightens the paper with one finger.

"I should have gone with them. Next time Goku can give me a day's notice."

An engine changes gear beyond the ridge and comes up the road, growing louder at each turn.

Then a horn sounds twice, the second blast longer than the first.

"Gohan! Gohan, your grandfather's here! Is nobody going to come out and say hello to an old man?"

Chi-Chi comes out with a cloth in her hand. A large truck stops at the gate, its bed piled with sacks and a crate.

The Ox-King climbs down. "Chi-Chi!"

"Put it down there, Father, not in the doorway."

He sets down what he is carrying and folds her into his arms. She says something against his chest, and he laughs.

"So," he says. "Where's Gohan?"

"He's gone to Master Roshi's."

"Gone?"

"Gone. With his father."

The Ox-King looks past her at the house. The yard is empty, and the schoolbook is still lying on the stool.

"Ah," he says. He takes off his hat and holds it in both hands for a moment, then looks back toward the truck.

"Well! Then it's as well I brought the whole lot up. Get the gate open, girl. I've a crate of mikan in the back that won't last the day."

Chi-Chi opens the gate. He goes back for the crate, and she carries the plum-coloured cloth after him.

## 6 · THE ARRIVALS

The aircraft comes over the ridge and circles the crater. Bulma leans toward the window while Krillin looks down beside her.

The broken pod lies below them, with pieces of its hatch scattered across the crater floor. Farther up the slope, Piccolo stands beside Raditz's body.

"That's near where the radar light stopped," she says. "Do you see them?"

"There! By the wall."

Roshi grips the back of Krillin's seat. "Piccolo's standing."

"Goku's down," Krillin says. "Bulma, land!"

The aircraft touches down on the level ground beyond the broken pod. Krillin is out before the door has opened all the way.

"Goku!"

Gohan turns at the sound. "Krillin!"

Krillin drops beside Goku and reaches for his shoulder, then sees Goku's hand pressed against his ribs.

"Where are you hurt?"

"Here. Don't make me laugh."

"I wasn't going to."

Goku opens his eyes. "Good."

Bulma and Roshi come around the rock. Bulma drops to her knees beside the boys.

"Gohan, let me see you. Can you move your arm?"

He lifts it partway. "It hurts here."

"Don't make him lift it again," Goshin says.

"I won't. And you?"

Goshin opens his burned hand, and Bulma takes his wrist gently to turn the palm toward the light.

"You should have said something."

"Father was hurt."

"I can look at both of you. Keep that hand open."

Gohan stays close enough to touch his father's sleeve. When Bulma asks him to move aside, he gets up and makes room for Roshi without taking his eyes off Goku.

Roshi crouches on Goku's other side. Goku tries to sit up for him, but Roshi puts a hand against his shoulder.

"Stay where you are."

"Master Roshi, there are two more of them."

Krillin looks toward Piccolo. "Two more what?"

"Saiyans," Piccolo says. "Stronger than the one on the ground."

Bulma turns. "How do you know?"

"Raditz told us. He said his scouter sent everything to them."

Bulma looks at the scouter lying on the flat stone. "Sent everything? Is it still sending?"

"I turned the display off," Piccolo says. "I don't know whether that stopped the rest of it."

"Then stop talking."

She crosses to the stone and picks up the device while Krillin follows with a small torch from the aircraft's kit, keeping one eye on Piccolo as he goes.

"Hold the light this way," Bulma says.

Krillin angles the torch while Bulma turns the scouter over in her hands. The dark lens is still intact, and a narrow seam runs along the back of the earpiece.

"There's a catch here. No, the other side."

She presses it with a fingernail, and a narrow cover slides back to expose a small cell. A pin of light still glows inside the earpiece; when she pulls the cell out, it goes dark.

"The lens was off, but that light wasn't," she says. "We don't know whether it was still sending anything."

"That should stop it," she says. "I'll check it properly before I put the power back."

She sets the empty scouter on the stone while she wraps the cell. The lens stays dark, and Krillin lowers the torch.

Krillin looks toward the sky. "How much did they hear?"

"Enough," Piccolo says. "They're coming for the Dragon Balls."

Roshi straightens. "How long have we got?"

"Raditz said a year."

Bulma wraps the scouter and its cell separately in her handkerchief. "A year. Fine. First we're getting Goku out of here."

She looks at the ruined crater, then back at the aircraft.

"Krillin, bring the medical case."

## 7 · ANOTHER PLANET

The voices from the scouter stop. On a distant planet, two men sit above a broken settlement, with the bodies of insect-like inhabitants among the stone walls.

A small fire burns beside them. Vegeta lowers the severed arm he has been eating and touches the scouter over his ear. Nappa puts down his own food and looks toward him.

"Raditz is dead," Nappa says.

One of the settlement's walls gives way behind them, sending stones across the empty street. Nappa looks toward it, then back at his companion.

Vegeta lowers his hand from his own scouter. "He let himself be killed by fighters with powers only a little over a thousand."

"Do we leave this planet and go after them? We haven't finished here."

"This place can wait. Did you hear what the green one said?"

"About the Dragon Balls. He said they grant wishes."

Vegeta looks toward the two space pods beyond the ruined wall. He sets the arm down beside the fire and gets to his feet.

"Then we'll go to Earth."

"To bring Raditz back?"

"Don't be ridiculous. We don't need him."

Nappa folds his arms and looks toward Vegeta. "What would you wish for?"

"Eternal life. If we never grow old or die, we can fight as long as we like."

Nappa grins at him. "That would be worth the trip."

Vegeta starts toward the pods.

## 8 · THE GROUND BENEATH HIM

Krillin opens the medical case beside Goku. Bulma takes out a roll of bandage and gives it to Gohan.

"Hold this for me. Don't let it drag in the dirt."

Gohan holds the roll with both hands while she wraps Goshin's palm. Goshin watches her fingers turn the bandage around his hand.

"Not too tight," he says.

"Tell me if it is."

"It is."

Bulma unwinds one turn and does it again. Goshin bends his fingers, looks away, then back. "That's better."

Roshi and Krillin spread a folded stretcher beside Goku. The orange cloth of his gi is torn where Raditz's boot pressed against him.

"We need to move him together," Roshi says. "Goku, don't try to help us."

"I can get up."

He pushes against the rock. His arm gives, and he settles back.

"All right," he says. "Maybe I can't."

Krillin puts one hand behind Goku's shoulders. "We'll get you somewhere safe."

"You got here fast," Goku says.

"Not fast enough. We should have been here while you were fighting."

"You're here now. Help me get the boys home."

Krillin looks at Gohan and Goshin, then reaches for the blanket at the end of the stretcher.

"What about the boys?"

"They're coming too."

Gohan kneels beside the stretcher and smooths out a folded blanket. "Can I help?"

"Keep that end steady," Krillin says. "Don't lift until I tell you."

Gohan takes the two handles. Goshin moves to the opposite end and puts his good hand on the frame.

Goku looks from one boy to the other. "You two did well."

Gohan looks down at the handles. "Were you really going to let Piccolo shoot you?"

"If I had to."

Goshin's bandaged hand tightens against the frame. "You didn't."

"No," Goku says. "I didn't."

Gohan looks at the tear across Goku's shirt. He reaches out as if to touch it, then stops when Goku shifts against the rock.

"I'm sorry," Gohan says.

"For what?"

"You told us to run, and we didn't."

Goku lifts his hand and puts it on Gohan's head. "I know. You both came back for me."

Gohan leans against his arm. Goshin keeps hold of the stretcher until Bulma asks him to make room.

Goku turns his head toward Goshin. "You hit his leg just where you said you would."

Goshin looks at the bandage. "I thought he might hit you before you got away."

"You made him turn. That gave us a chance."

Gohan points to the torn end of Goku's sleeve. "I caught this, but he nearly pulled you back."

"You got me behind the rock," Goku says. "I saw your hand."

Gohan picks up the edge of the blanket and tucks it beneath Goku's arm, away from the place where Raditz struck him.

Piccolo stands beyond the stretcher. Goku turns his head toward him.

"What will you do about the other two?"

"They want this planet. I won't give it to them."

"Then we'll have to get stronger."

"Don't tell me what to do, Goku. Our agreement ended with Raditz."

Krillin rises between them. "He's hurt, Piccolo."

Piccolo looks at the stretcher, then at the two boys holding it. "I can see that."

"Are you going to attack him?" Krillin asks.

"Not while stronger Saiyans are coming. For now, he's useful."

Bulma looks at the torn cloth at Piccolo's left shoulder. "You need help too. Let me get another bandage."

"Keep it. I can take care of myself."

"Your arm is gone."

Piccolo takes a step away from her. "I said I don't need you."

He turns and walks toward the slope, holding the strip of cloth against his shoulder with his remaining hand. Gohan follows him with his eyes.

"Is he leaving?"

"Let him go," Goku says. "He isn't after you."

Goshin looks toward Raditz's body. "Are the other two coming in one year?"

"That's what he said."

"How many days is that?"

Goku looks toward Bulma. "How many days are in a year?"

"Three hundred and sixty-five, most years," Bulma says. She returns with water and a clean cloth, then sets them beside the medical case.

"But Raditz gave us a guess, not a date. It could be a little more or a little less, so don't count on an extra day."

Goshin looks at the handkerchief wrapped around the scouter. "Can that tell us?"

"Maybe. I'll find out what I can without turning it on again."

## 9 · A YEAR AWAY

Vegeta stands at the open hatch of his pod while Nappa pauses beside the other one.

"Those readings from Kakarot's children," Vegeta says. "They're high for Saiyan children."

"Could the scouter have been wrong?"

"You heard Raditz. One boy hurt him badly, and the other kept fighting him."

Nappa looks back at the broken settlement. "Their mother is an Earthling."

"Saiyan blood and Earthling blood. Perhaps that's what produces children like those."

Nappa touches his scouter again, though the transmission has ended. "Raditz said the other boy's attack was stronger than Kakarot's. The first boy's power fell after he hit him."

"Their power changed," Vegeta says. "It was real enough to hurt Raditz, and they are only children."

"Super Saiyans?"

"Perhaps."

Nappa laughs. "If we had more of them, we could make the Saiyans powerful again."

"And when they grew stronger than us?"

Nappa's laugh stops.

Vegeta rests a hand on the rim of his pod. "We'd be putting our own positions in danger."

"Then we kill them."

"Everyone on Earth. Once we have the Dragon Balls."

"And if these fighters try to hide them?"

Vegeta looks toward the empty pods. "Raditz heard enough to tell us the planet has them. We'll make the people there tell us the rest."

"Three days should be enough."

The hatches rise, and Nappa climbs into his pod and lowers himself into the seat.

"It's a long trip," he says.

Vegeta sits down in the other pod. "Then sleep. We have a year."

The hatches close, and two lights lift from the planet and travel into the dark.

## 10 · THE HANDKERCHIEF

At the crater, Roshi gives the count. Krillin and Roshi lift Goku while Gohan and Goshin hold the stretcher steady beneath him.

Goku catches his breath as they lower him, and Gohan's hands stay on the handles until Krillin tells him to let go.

Bulma checks the straps. "We'll take him in the aircraft. The boys need looking over too."

Goku reaches to his belt and takes out the Dragon Radar. "Here, Bulma. You'd better take this back."

She puts it in the medical case. "I'll keep it safe."

"We have to tell Mother," Gohan says.

"We will," Roshi says. "As soon as we get to a telephone. First we'll get your father back to Kame House."

"She'll want to know about Goshin's hand too," Gohan says.

"Then we'll tell her that as well. She should hear about all three of you from us."

Goku looks at Goshin's bandage. "Let Master Roshi look at that hand again."

"Let Master Roshi look at that hand again," Goshin repeats.

"And the bump on your head."

Goshin puts his good hand against his hair. "That too."

Gohan touches his own shoulder and looks toward Roshi. "I'll show him my shoulder, too."

"Good," Bulma says. "Nobody is going to forget anything just because another person got hurt worse."

Gohan picks up the medical case. Krillin takes one end of the stretcher and Roshi takes the other; together they carry Goku toward the aircraft.

Goshin walks beside his father, keeping pace with the handles. When one of the straps slips against Goku's arm, he lifts it clear with his good hand and holds it until Bulma can fasten it again.

Bulma follows them with the wrapped scouter and looks back toward the crater wall when she reaches the door.

Piccolo is gone.

She puts the handkerchief in the case. Inside it, the scouter's display remains dark.
