## Episode-by-episode findings

- **108 — return and altered ordinary life.** The narrator says more than ten months have passed since the fight with Freeza ended (04:30–04:38); later Gohan tells Marron he is five (07:50–07:57). These are individual original-TV statements, not a complete birthday or exact-calendar solution for the twins. Gohan fishes to help while Goku is away, and asks Chi-Chi's permission for the Kame House gathering; after she refuses and assigns study he decides to go anyway (06:05–09:07; 20:28–20:52; 23:45–24:05). Kami tells Piccolo he considers him a possible successor in strength and spirit; Piccolo refuses the post and warns about increased demon activity (10:27–11:30). Neither statement proves a pre-Saiyan appointment or future branch choice. Popo identifies Garlic Jr. as having been sealed in his own Dead Zone; Garlic claims eternal life from the Dragon Balls and describes a prior encounter with Goku and his son (14:21–16:43). His statements are part of the TV continuity; they do not by themselves verify every scene in the film or compel the altered family to have lived the film unchanged.
- **109 — the mist and mother/son.** Chi-Chi is already angry about Gohan leaving without permission, while the mist later changes people at the island. Gohan initially apologizes and promises study and help at home before realizing the others are behaving abnormally; he demands that the attackers restore his mother (12:11–13:51; 18:45–19:59; 22:13–23:08). Do not conflate her ordinary anger with actions after contamination. Krillin jokingly calls her the universe's strongest woman, frightening even Super Saiyan Goku (15:15–15:25): an in-context character remark is not an objective power measurement or a limit on the authored boys. Toei's synopsis places the Aqua Mist and antidote search here (`W-114`).
- **110 — stated conditions and danger.** Garlic's subordinate says the approaching Makyo Star, which he calls the demons' home and says returns every 5,000 years, awakened their power and enabled Garlic to escape (06:10–07:12). He calls the boost “tens of times”: a villain's contextual claim, not a measured universal multiplier. The same group says the mist becomes irreversible after **24 hours from inhalation**, even with the antidote (07:20–07:51); biting by a contaminated person is said to produce the same effect (17:26–17:37). Piccolo sends Gohan and Krillin for the antidote while he stays behind, and they stop first at Korin's (11:00–15:40; 20:29–23:05). Toei's episode-110 synopsis describes Yamcha biting Piccolo, but that is not proof he was permanently corrupted: the following episodes reveal a deception.
- **111–113 — apparent corruption and plan.** Garlic threatens Kami, whose death is said to kill Piccolo (111, 08:01–08:09). Krillin says his recent neglected training made his body fail to move as he wanted (111, 16:40–16:49): his self-assessment in these circumstances is not a quantified rule for every human. Episode 112 presents Piccolo and then Krillin as apparently turned; at 23:09–24:17 they reveal themselves and free Kami and Popo. Episode 113 explicitly recalls Piccolo explaining to Krillin his plan to hurt Gohan so Garlic would approach, while Gohan says the act fooled him too (05:51–07:06). The 110/112 Toei synopses preserve the apparent-corruption suspense; do not stop there and misstate the later reveal. These captions do not establish exactly why the bites failed to produce the expected effect, whether Gohan was informed earlier, complete contact choreography, or a general immunity rule.
- **113–115 — Kami, Piccolo and the antidote.** Popo proposes spreading the antidote through seven air currents at the Lookout's bottom, after warning that the sacred realm of former guardians is dangerous to Kami (113, 10:53–12:10). Kami accepts the risk; interference with him is associated in the dialogue with Piccolo's worsening condition (114, 20:16–23:18). Kami declares he loves Earth and entrusts the water to Popo, who is described by the next recap as pouring it into the air-current opening (115, 06:47–07:37 and 20:08–22:14; 116, 03:17–03:30). Piccolo and Krillin also try to protect each other, not merely measure damage (115, 11:22–11:56 and 16:55–17:36). This sequence is teamwork in particular conditions, not an automatic feature of every version's afterlife or immortal body.
- **116 — defense, opportunity and defeat.** Gohan holds a shield to protect Piccolo and Krillin from the Dead Zone, and resists dropping it because they would be endangered (08:00–08:40; 10:27–11:04). Toei's 116 synopsis says Piccolo and Krillin leave the shield and Gohan takes the opening to aim at the Makyo Star; the later caption dialogue says destroying the star made Garlic lose the support that kept him from falling into his own Dead Zone (17:35–19:36; 22:48–23:35). This is a **team-dependent outcome** with explicit care for others. Piccolo's later claim that Gohan should have been sufficient alone (23:44–24:03) is his judgment, not proof of an identical solo result or a mandatory numerical scale. Do not import this much-later Gohan's feats into the pre-Saiyan twins, or take the attack's complete visual choreography from captions alone.
- **117 — choices after the fight.** The group gathers for Turtle's 1,000th birthday. Although Chi-Chi initially objects to the false emergency, she softens when Turtle thanks his friends (06:46–07:47). Turtle presents the Mermaid's Tears' happiness as a **legend**, not a tested guarantee (10:02–10:38). When Gohan realizes the pearl is cherished and protected by the fish, he asks if Krillin could choose another gift; Krillin leaves it (19:39–20:55). Krillin then says goodbye to Marron because he doubts he can make her happy; she says she might have married him, and leaves (21:48–23:27). Do not turn his imagined wedding or his decision to seek a proposal into a completed proposal or marriage. Toei's relevant synopsis ends before an unrelated appended passage describing Namek's destruction; that stray text is not an episode-117 occurrence or proof Goku died (`C283`).

## Important source boundaries for this branch

1. **Original anime first.** The Garlic Jr. arc is genuine original-Z TV material even though its earlier Dead Zone backstory is associated with a film and the return is absent from the main manga comparison. Record the TV characters' stated history; do not require every filmed event, character meeting or movie timeline to have happened unchanged in the divergent branch. If it is eventually used, reconcile its prerequisites with the actual boys, training and prior knowledge.
2. **The two waters and a third similar name.** Toei's original-DB 61–62 synopsis describes `超聖水` as ordinary water whose chase was training. Its DB 115–117 synopsis describes a dangerous potential-revealing `超神水`; the Z 108–116 dialogue also calls the mist antidote `超神水`. The identical last name does not establish that the DB power-revealing water and Z antidote are the same substance, and the first term differs. Do not automatically transfer their effects between scenes. The original-DB accounts here remain synopsis-only (`W-113`).
3. **Claim versus observed event.** Garlic and his followers claim eternal life, a 5,000-year approach and tens-of-times empowerment. The team defeats his present threat through the stated star/Dead Zone sequence, not by proving that immortality guarantees invincibility in every circumstance. Do not present a boast as an author-neutral number.
4. **Agency is not a fixed outcome.** Baseline Gohan makes protective choices and can act without Goku, but the branch includes two boys, different relationships and a full lived chamber year. Both boys comfortably outclass this pre-Saiyan Piccolo in ordinary spars by author direction; this later-arc fight supplies no new transferable multiplier or required sacrifice. Goshin's Talent remains cumulative whole-being growth, and his first eventual Great Ape remains aware and controlled.
5. **Film continuity remains a separate, open decision.** The exact *Battle of Gods* film-party venue remains disputed across the existing official and secondary accounts; it is not needed to use the original Z TV arc. Film review was not substituted for this anime pass.
