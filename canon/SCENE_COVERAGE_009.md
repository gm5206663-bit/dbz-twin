# Episode 9 → Chapter Ten · scene-coverage inventory

**Owns: the source-order inventory and actual divergence decisions for the episode-9 adaptation.**

**Status:** The initial source inventory and treatment plan were written before Chapter Ten prose. A second caption-by-caption audit after the draft corrected several initial time ranges, a mistaken closing-narration attribution, and the placement of the hunger intercuts; the table below was then reconciled against the completed provisional draft. This is not a frame-by-frame video verification or reader acceptance.

## Evidence and limits

- The whole local Japanese caption file `Dragon_Ball_Z_009.srt` was read, including the ending, preview, closing music and the unrelated material after it (`W-30`; redistribution source and exact cut unverified). Timecodes below refer to that file, not an authenticated broadcast master. A caption records speech or an identified sound; it is not a visual witness. The indexed 29-minute boundary is a heuristic.
- The original Japanese episode title is 「ゴメンねロボットさん 砂漠に消えた涙」; the title and secondary episode synopsis are reported by `W-67`. `W-68` is a broadcaster programme synopsis. `W-69` and `W-70` are independent rewatch accounts, not primary footage. `C182`–`C190` distinguish the observed captions from reported images. W-69 and W-70 disagree on whether Gohan grabs the bird or is grabbed; the visual sequence remains unresolved.
- **Branch state before this chapter:** Goku is alive; he, Chi-Chi, Gohan, Goshin and Ox-King are at home. Goku and Gohan were completely restored; Goshin refused the third Senzu, which Roshi holds. Goshin is still injured, under restrictions, and not yet cleared for long travel; his next check is pending. Piccolo has neither boy. Both tails and the moon remain. Chapter Nine's preview is “The Book and the Road.”
- Episode scenes that depend on dead Goku, a solitary Gohan, or the missing moon and tail cannot be re-created unchanged. C-6's rescue and uncertain survival, adult participation, medical follow-up and changed route are authored alternatives, not claims about episode footage.

## Targeted post-draft audit, before sealing

- **Food:** §2 follows the raw whole fish, vegetables and rice through cleaning, cooking and packing before Goshin's meal. §9 shows him watch the previously prepared parcels opened and reheated and the whole lemon washed, cut, squeezed and added before his second taste. He eats no Senzu or unseen cave food.
- **Instructions:** checked spoken and narrated commands directed to him or both sons: “Hold still, please,” “Please stand,” “Travel only as a passenger,” the head-report, exercise and reading limits, the path and ride instructions, the moon precaution, no tunnel, the robot's noise/connection requests, and rest/report before return. The applicable words are repeated before the relevant compliance or continuation. The earlier instruction to stand was repaired during the draft audit. This manual list is a targeted review, not proof that every possible semantic instruction has been found.
- **Goku:** Goshin's substantive direct radio answer to Goku follows Goku's explicit invitation, “Goshin, what do you see in the picture?” His other statements are directed to the doctor, Chi-Chi, Gohan, Korin, C-6 or the radio, not uninvited conversation with Goku.
- **Position and manner:** Goshin is on Gohan's downhill side on both described sloping home paths. He puts his hands behind his back when Chi-Chi looks him over, including the cold open, the stops and the lookout. He does not boast. His contributions to the diagram are meaningful despite the medically restricted activity; Gohan initiates the inquiry, reasons from the book and makes choices as well.
- **Travel and continuity:** the doctor examines Goshin again, permits a monitored gentle passenger ride, and retains explicit no-running, independent-flight, heavy-lifting and training restrictions; the family stops to check him. They return home together before it gets late. The prior completely healed Goku and Gohan, rejected third Senzu with Roshi, both tails, untouched moon, no Baba reply and no Yemma permission remain unchanged. Kami explains the non-guaranteed Piccolo risk and his uncertain life warning. Goku temporarily goes to the desert only after the family explicitly weighs the separation.
- **Robot and ownership:** Gohan's inquiry leads to the bulletin; workers, radio and new tremor are authored. C-6 directs the rescue, agrees to help and chooses shutdown. The extra help gives a different, still uncertain outcome. No silent visual reconstruction is presented as verified episode footage.

A checklist and a green structural test do not establish a complete character, source or editorial review. Chapter Ten and Chapters Four–Nine remain provisional; Chapters One–Three await review of their authorized revisions.
