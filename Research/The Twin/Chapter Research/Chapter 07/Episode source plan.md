## Source episode, in spoken order, with branch decisions to test while writing

1. **After the fight, the scouter and Dragon Balls.** Bulma and Krillin discuss the device and whether to remove it; Bulma considers studying and fixing it, and the party recognizes that the Saiyans heard about the Dragon Balls. Goku's source death and Raditz's warning are the reason for the crisis. **Plan:** the candidate Chapter 6 already stages this discussion, deactivates the scouter by separating its cell and sends Krillin off; do not replay it or restore power automatically. The living Goku changes the consequences.
2. **Piccolo's regeneration.** His severed arm grows back; the people present react with disgust or surprise and he compares it to a lizard's tail. **Plan:** include regeneration and at least one honest reaction, with the energy cost and timing appropriate to his recent fight. Do not pretend other characters see it if they are not present.
3. **Piccolo seizes Gohan.** In the source Gohan is unconscious; Piccolo lifts and takes him, tells Krillin and the others that Gohan must be trained for the Saiyans, and rejects objections. Their fear, protest, and Piccolo's threat matter. **Plan:** Piccolo has seen both awake children, Goku survives, Krillin is absent, and the aircraft has left. Let confrontation, opposition, choices and physical outcome arise from those facts. Gohan cannot be made conveniently unconscious, Goshin cannot be carried away effortlessly, and Piccolo's custody or failure is not preordained.
4. **Kami and Goku in Yemma's realm.** The source has dead Goku accompany Kami. King Yemma identifies/judges Raditz, says Raditz struggled and he subdued him, and is surprised Goku beat him; they discuss the threat, whether the fighter can keep his body, and permission to train. **Plan:** living Goku has no reason to arrive as a dead man. The dead Raditz and the guardian's concern can have an independently motivated afterlife thread, but do not invent an automatic summons, a guaranteed living-person training permit, or a tour merely to reproduce the source.
5. **Chi-Chi and Ox-King.** Chi-Chi calls Kame House, gets the answering machine and worries that rough games, the sea, a shark, an island, a snake or poison might harm Gohan or interfere with study. Ox-King tries to reassure her. **Plan:** the unanswered call has already occurred in protected Chapter 3. If the worry continues, advance the existing scene without a second identical call or arrival; acknowledge both children and keep Chi-Chi's concern for their physical safety as well as schooling.
6. **King Kai and Snake Way.** Kami and Goku talk about King Kai, the long road (the approximately million-kilometre tradition), its danger and the possibility of Goku returning by the Dragon Balls; they set off. **Plan:** causally altered/deferred, not silently restaged. Otherworld training may be explored if characters have a reason and a viable route; do not assume it, an exact travel time or a resurrection.
7. **Piccolo tests Gohan.** Piccolo recalls the Saiyan attack, the Dragon Ball warning, the boys' future opponents and Gohan's enormous hidden strength; his cruel test sends the frightened boy toward a cliff. Gohan's crisis releases destructive power, and the child is then upset; he wants to be a scholar rather than a fighter. Piccolo is astonished and decides to make that power usable. **Plan:** preserve the collision between frightening potential and Gohan's ambition, but earn any test through the actual confrontation. Gohan and Goshin both take effective actions, not just serve as cargo or spectators. Piccolo is dangerous and self-interested, not automatically a tutor or a friend.
8. **Episode end and preview.** The source points ahead to harsh survival training. **Plan:** the candidate's own ending and preview must describe only an earned immediate situation. Do not predetermine long-term custody, moon, tail, robot, or a later victory.

## Craft and rule checks before prose

- Prioritize a readable adventure, physical action, jokes, emotion, and independent character threads over medical travel instructions. Keep danger credible; Goku, Gohan and Goshin are injured, not inert. Goku remains a parent making decisions.
- Goshin remains at least as capable as Gohan while Gohan's own decisions and courage change events. His burst scan was momentary, not a numerical ceiling or automatic damage scale; observation grants no free power increase. No new transformation or god ki. Emergency technique copying is possible but not mandatory.
- At age four Goshin has his tail, can fly, and uses learned skills. Check applicable verbatim repeats before obedience, waiting to be invited into his father's conversation, downhill position beside Gohan on paths, hands behind his back when Chi-Chi examines him, and eating only food whose preparation he watched.
- A Senzu fully heals and restores; do not stage partial healing. Do not have Goshin eat a bean prepared out of his view. No invented hospital detour.
- Give Piccolo's choice, the adults' reactions, both children's choices, Goku's living condition, the real flight geography and Krillin's independent route causal weight. Do not position anyone magically to preserve canon.
