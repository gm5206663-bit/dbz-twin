## Exact proposed endpoint

- Piccolo has Nappa's limited account of Namekians and a world called Namek, not independent confirmation, coordinates or a Dragon Ball handoff. Nappa knows Vegeta explicitly treated him as expendable and remains hostile and injured; Arlia is recalled only in Nappa's thoughts. The Earth group has not learned of its destruction through that thought.
- Vegeta is awake and hurt, and Goshin prevents his renewed attempt to aim at Nappa. He and Nappa remain at the quarry under temporary watch, not in a proven secure prison. Goshin's substantial unnumbered growth from the fight and first transformation remains in his ordinary body and ki after reversion; both boys are tailed, Gohan has not transformed and no new measurement or fixed form conversion is established.
- The two scouters are together on the same stone, out of both Saiyans' immediate reach; Krillin moved Nappa's by its frame without pressing controls. No one knows whether either is transmitting. Nappa's claim about calling ships has not been tested. Krillin still has the whole Senzu. Two dusty round pods were seen in separate city landing craters behind police barriers; no technical inspection or internal diagnosis is shown. A technical team has been called.
- Goku reached Yemma's end by travelling Snake Way; King Kai alerted Kami, who met him at the office and used Yemma's agreed passage to the Lookout. Goku flew from there to the quarry. He reunites with Chi-Chi and both children and offers to share the watch. His supply capsule is still with him; he tells Goshin they can see what remains, but it is not opened until Chapter 29. No reader acceptance or claim about his precise relative maximum follows.
- Launch and Yajirobe reach the city, help a trapped shopkeeper, then reach the quarry and reunite with Tien and Chiaotzu. Yajirobe retains Upa's flask after sharing some water, including a capful offered to Nappa with Gohan's help; returning it remains open. Chi-Chi cleans Goshin's cheek. Goshin uses an old shirt and spare trousers from the retrieved bags, but remains barefoot; Gohan's sore shoulder and Goshin's sore side and marks have not been diagnosed.
- Popo brings both bags and the two loose pages to the quarry; the page of days stays under the Lookout bench stone. Chi-Chi confirms the shared letter in a bag and leaves it folded for Gohan's requested reading at home. Bulma, Yamcha and Puar are last at the gym. Bulma calls Roshi and the children's-home director; the six are still together in the hall and Hatch wants news. A building crew is checking the previously unsearched upstairs; no final report has reached Bulma. She requests discussion with the medical team and advises an officer about the objects. Medical arrangements, technical inspection, complete evacuation and casualty count remain open.
- The shared letter, Gohan's promised rematch, Goshin's display for Goku, the children's long-term placement and schooling, C-6's loan and repair, the covered machine, Baba's request and the natural moon's present phase remain unresolved. No Namek ship or journey, departure or release of the Saiyans is shown.
