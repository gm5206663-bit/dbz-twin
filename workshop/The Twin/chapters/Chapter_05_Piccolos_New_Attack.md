## COLD OPEN

Dust drifts across the crater. The pod's broken hatch lies below the rim, with a stone resting on its bent edge. The stone rolls off and falls into the dust.

**PICCOLO'S NEW ATTACK**

---

## 1 · THREE DIRECTIONS

**NARRATOR:** Raditz gave Goku one day to bring him a hundred victims. Goku has come without them.

As Raditz raises his hands, Goku, Piccolo and Goshin move forward together.

Goku comes in from the middle of the ridge. Piccolo takes the slope on Raditz's left, while Goshin rises on his right. Gohan stays behind Goku, beside the broken rock where his brother brought him.

When Raditz turns toward Goku, Goku ducks his punch and drives a fist into his stomach. Piccolo reaches for the tail behind Raditz, but Raditz twists and kicks backward with both feet.

The kick catches Goku and Piccolo at once, sending them tumbling apart across the slope.

Goshin flies past Raditz's shoulder and fires at his scouter. Raditz raises a hand over the lens. The blast strikes his wrist, and Goshin follows it with a kick to his side.

Raditz drops toward the crater floor, stops above the broken pod and shoots upward after Goshin. Goku pushes off the slope to follow him.

Raditz reaches the ridge first, where a low wall of rock hides him from Goshin for a moment. Goshin rises above it and looks toward the pod.

Gohan calls, "Where did he go?"

"Behind you," Raditz says.

Goku shouts a warning as Raditz passes the end of the wall. Goshin wheels toward the sound, with the edge of the ridge behind him and open air in front.

Gohan calls, "Goshin, above you!"

As Goshin turns, Raditz's fist hits his raised forearms and pushes him back through the air. Goshin swings his legs up, plants both feet against Raditz's chest and shoves away. Raditz catches one ankle, but Goshin twists his foot out of the grip before Raditz can reach his tail.

Goku reaches them before Raditz can follow. He catches Raditz by the wrist and pulls him toward Piccolo, who has climbed back up the slope.

"Now!"

Piccolo springs from the slope. Raditz breaks Goku's grip and brings his other arm across Piccolo's face. Piccolo falls to one knee. Goku tries for the tail, but Raditz drives an elbow into his ribs and sends him into the rock beside Gohan.

Gohan catches his father's arm. "Father!"

"Get behind that rock," Goku says.

"Are you hurt?"

"Go on. I'll be right here."

Gohan moves to the far side of the rock, still watching him. Goku gets up and presses a hand against his ribs.

Piccolo wipes his mouth. "So you're the weakest of the three."

"Both the others are stronger," Raditz says. "You've been warned."

Piccolo looks at Goku. "Doesn't that excite you?"

Goku flexes his hand. "Not this time. I'm shaking."

Raditz laughs. "You should be."

"I could have done without hearing that," Goku says. He looks toward Gohan. "Are you all right?"

"Yes. Goshin got me out."

"Good. Stay where you can see me."

Raditz looks past Goku at the rock. "You got them out of my pod. You won't get them off this planet."

Goshin lands between Raditz and the ridge. "They're not going with you."

"Then all of you can die here."

Raditz rises above them, with Goku jumping after him and Piccolo flying in from the other side. Goshin follows below, keeping Raditz in sight.

Raditz brings both hands forward. Light gathers in each palm.

Goku shouts, "Move!"

Two blasts cross the crater. One goes toward Goku; the other goes toward Piccolo.

Goku throws himself aside as the blast tears into the ridge behind him. The rock beneath Gohan breaks loose, and he slips as the ledge tilts.

Goshin dives toward Gohan, who grabs the edge with both hands as a slab tips over him. Goshin strikes it with his shoulder and sends it past the ledge.

"Take my hand!"

Gohan lets go with one hand. Goshin catches it and pulls him clear. They fly down to a shelf below the ridge as the slab crashes into the crater.

As the second blast reaches Piccolo, he turns away, but it cuts across his left side and bursts against the slope behind him.

Piccolo drops out of the light, strikes the ground and rolls to a stop.

Goku calls, "Piccolo!"

Goshin looks up from the shelf. Piccolo pushes himself onto one knee with his right hand. His left arm is gone.

Raditz hovers above them. "Even together, you can't stop me."

## 2 · ONE ARM

Goku lands beside Piccolo. Dust slides from the torn edge of Piccolo's sleeve.

"Can you get up?"

"Don't fuss over me."

Piccolo plants his right hand and stands, pressing it against his left shoulder before he turns to face Raditz again.

"I can still fight with one arm."

Goshin brings Gohan up from the shelf. Gohan's hat has slipped over one eye. He pushes it back and looks at Piccolo's shoulder.

"Does he need help?"

"Keep your brother back," Piccolo says.

Goshin lands in front of Gohan. "Can you stand?"

"Yes. Your hand's bleeding."

Goshin looks down at his burned palm and turns it away from his brother. Gohan pulls a clean corner of his sleeve loose and offers it to him.

"We can tie it around. Mother does that when I cut my fingers."

Goshin starts to take it, but a pebble bounces between them. Raditz is gathering light in his palm again, high above the broken ledge.

Goku sees Raditz's hand and shouts, "Get apart!"

Goshin pushes Gohan behind the rock and leaps toward Raditz. He sends a blast across the raised hand, and Raditz's shot goes wide of Piccolo.

It strikes the ground beside Goku. Goku shields his face from the loose stones and runs back to the children when they stop falling.

Raditz looks down at Piccolo. "Shall I try the other arm?"

Goshin raises his good hand, and Raditz looks back at him.

Piccolo tears a strip from his sleeve with his teeth. He pulls it tight around his shoulder with his remaining hand and keeps his eyes on Raditz.

Gohan takes Goshin's hand for a moment, leaving the burned palm turned upward.

Goku looks up at Raditz. "Come after me. Leave them out of it."

"They've already joined in."

Raditz points at Goshin. Goshin lifts his good hand. Goku moves closer to both boys, but Raditz only smiles and lowers his arm.

"You're getting tired, Kakarot. How much longer can you protect them?"

Goku takes a breath. "Long enough."

## 3 · ON THE WAY

An engine sounds over the forest. Bulma's aircraft passes between two ridges toward the place where she last saw the radar light.

Krillin leans forward in the seat beside her. "Is that where Gohan's hat was?"

"That's where the light stopped," Bulma says. "Goku has the radar now. I can't tell if the ball has moved."

Roshi looks through the window, where trees cover the slope beneath them with no opening in the forest yet.

"Goku and Piccolo should be close by now."

"I hope they got there first," Krillin says. "Raditz hurt Goshin badly."

Bulma scans the hills ahead. "I don't like this. I've never felt quite like this before."

"The two of them are working together," Roshi says. "They won't be beaten easily."

"That's what worries me," Krillin says.

"What does?"

"Piccolo. What if he changes sides? What if he joins Raditz against Goku?"

Bulma looks across at him. "He wouldn't. Not after he came to the island."

Krillin rests a hand on the edge of the panel. "He came because Raditz was in his way. He said so himself."

"They're both in Raditz's way," Roshi says. "For now, that gives them a reason to fight together."

Bulma asks, "And if they can't beat him?"

Roshi keeps looking ahead. "If Goku and Piccolo fall, there may be nothing the rest of us can do."

Krillin sits back, then leans toward the window when a plume of dust rises beyond the next ridge. Bulma turns the aircraft toward it.

"Can you tell whether the boys are together?" he asks.

"Not from here. The radar showed the ball, but Goku has the radar now."

"If Raditz has left them somewhere, I can go in and get them while Goku keeps him busy."

"We'll have to find a place to land first," Bulma says. "I can't take this down in the middle of a fight."

Roshi rests a hand on the seat between them. "Wait until we can see what's happening. We won't help the boys by getting in Goku's way."

"Chi-Chi thinks they're visiting us," Bulma says. "She doesn't know any of this has happened."

Krillin looks past the window toward the empty hills. "Let's get them home before she has to find out."

The engine fills the cabin. Krillin grips the seat more tightly.

"Goku," he says.

The aircraft passes over the next ridge toward the rising dust.

## 4 · PICCOLO'S PLAN

Raditz watches from above while Goku and Piccolo stand below him. Goshin keeps one hand raised. Gohan takes two stones from the ground and holds them against his chest.

Raditz calls, "What's the matter? Finished already?"

Piccolo turns toward Goku. "Do you have a new technique? Something you've been saving?"

"A new one? Sorry. I don't."

"You've been wasting your time."

"Have you got one?"

"I developed it while I was training. It might finish him."

Raditz folds his arms. "Make whatever plan you like. It won't help."

Gohan watches the hand Piccolo presses against his shoulder. He moves a stone aside with his shoe, clearing a place to stand behind the broken rock.

Goku steps closer to Piccolo. "Can you use it with one arm?"

"Yes. But I need time to gather the power."

"How much?"

"Enough that he'll try to stop me. Keep his attention on you."

"Can you move while you're getting it ready?"

"Not enough to fight him at the same time. If he reaches me before I fire, we'll have lost our chance."

Goku looks from Piccolo to Raditz before asking, "Will it really work?"

"If it doesn't, I don't have another answer."

Goku turns to Goshin. "Can you help us?"

Goshin moves beside his father. "What do we do?"

"You and Gohan keep each other in sight," Goku says. "If Raditz comes for Piccolo, keep him away. Don't get between Piccolo and Raditz when the attack is ready."

"You and Gohan keep each other in sight. If Raditz comes for Piccolo, keep him away. Don't get between Piccolo and Raditz when the attack is ready," Goshin repeats.

Goshin looks at Gohan. Gohan holds up one of the stones.

"I'll help too."

"All right," Goku tells Gohan. "Keep some cover between you and him."

Gohan asks, "Can't we go home?"

Goku looks at the space above the rim. "If we try to leave now, he'll come after us. We have to stop him first."

"I'll go with Gohan," Goshin says.

"Stay where he can see you," Goku says. "If you have to go out of sight, call to each other."

"Stay where he can see you. If you have to go out of sight, call to each other," Goshin repeats.

Gohan nods and moves back toward the rock. Goshin takes the open side, where he can see both Piccolo and Raditz. He raises his good hand toward Goku, and Goku touches it with his own before turning away.

Piccolo steps away from them. "I made that technique to kill you, Son Goku."

Goku looks at him and asks, "You did?"

"If we get through this, you're next."

Goku laughs once and says, "Then I want to see it."

"This isn't the time to laugh."

"I'll hold him. You get ready."

Piccolo raises two fingers of his right hand to his forehead. While Goku flies toward Raditz, Piccolo closes his eyes and says, "Makankosappo."

A light forms at his fingertips.

## 5 · KEEP HIM LOOKING HERE

Goku rises above the crater and stops level with Raditz. Below them, Piccolo stands near the far slope. Goshin and Gohan remain by the broken ridge.

"Your friend is standing still," Raditz says. "Is he waiting for you to die?"

"You'll have to get past me to find out."

Goku rushes him. Raditz blocks a punch at his face and catches the next at his waist. Goku twists free, comes around behind him and kicks at his back.

Raditz turns. His forearm takes the kick, and his fist strikes Goku across the jaw. Goku falls several feet before stopping himself.

Raditz follows, forcing Goku back toward the crater wall with a flurry of blows. Goku catches one wrist and blocks another punch, but Raditz kicks him across the ribs.

Goku drops below the next strike and catches Raditz around the waist. For a moment Raditz's tail hangs behind him, but when Goku reaches for it, Raditz wrenches free.

Raditz says, "Still trying that? I know what you're after."

He turns toward Piccolo. Goku catches his shoulder and pulls him back before he can descend.

"I'm the one you're fighting."

Raditz drives his elbow into Goku's stomach and turns again. Goshin rises from the ridge. A blast leaves his good hand and crosses Raditz's path. Raditz knocks it down into the empty crater.

Goku calls, "Stay where Gohan can see you!"

"Stay where Gohan can see you!" Goshin repeats.

Gohan waves from behind the rock. "I'm here!"

Piccolo's light flickers as Raditz glances in his direction. Goshin sees the turn and fires a second blast, this one across the space in front of Piccolo.

Raditz catches it against his palm and sends it toward the crater floor. Goshin turns toward Piccolo, but Raditz drops toward Gohan instead.

Goku follows, and Goshin wheels around and reaches Raditz first. He catches Raditz's arm and drives a knee into his armour.

Raditz pulls free, but Goshin turns with him and lands a second blow against the same place. A plate shifts under Raditz's hair.

Raditz strikes Goshin in the side. Goshin is pushed toward the ridge, but he turns in the air and stops above Gohan. He touches the place on his head where Raditz struck him on the island.

Raditz comes after him, reaching again for his tail. Goshin folds his tail close, catches the reaching wrist with his good hand and kicks Raditz away from the rock.

"Stay behind the rock," Goshin says to Gohan.

Goku comes down between them. "Goshin, are you hurt?"

"Here." Goshin touches his side.

"Then get clear."

"Then get clear," Goshin repeats, moving back toward Gohan.

Goku raises both hands and fires a short burst. Raditz swerves around it and comes at him again. Their fists meet above the ridge. Goku blocks two strikes, but the third drives him down onto the slope.

Gohan runs out from the rock. Goshin catches his sleeve before he reaches the fight.

"Father said to keep cover."

"He's on the ground!"

Gohan pulls his sleeve free and throws one of his stones. It strikes Raditz's shoulder. Raditz turns his head.

Goku sweeps Raditz's feet from under him. Before Raditz can land, Goshin fires another blast across his path. Raditz lifts over it, and Goku follows him up.

"Good throw," Goku calls.

Gohan shifts the other stone into his throwing hand and goes back behind the rock to watch the air above him.

Piccolo's fingers remain against his forehead. The light around them grows brighter.

## 6 · THE WAVE

Raditz catches Goku's fist and drives him backward through the air. Goku plants both feet against the side of the crater and pushes off again.

"You won't keep this up," Raditz says.

"I don't have to keep it up forever."

Goku drops to the open ground below the ridge. He brings both hands together at his side. Light appears between his palms.

Goshin lands beside Gohan and watches Goku's hands.

Goku begins, "Ka—me—"

Raditz's scouter beeps, and he touches the control at his temple.

"Nine hundred and twenty-four. Nine hundred and twenty-five."

The light in Goku's hands grows. Raditz's smile fades.

"He's concentrating his power."

Goku keeps his hands together. "Ha—me—"

The scouter sounds again. Raditz turns toward Piccolo on the far slope, where light shines at his raised fingers.

"That one's at one thousand and twenty. It's still rising?"

Goku thrusts his hands forward and shouts, "Ha!"

Raditz turns his scouter back toward Goku. Its figures keep climbing as the wave leaves Goku's hands.

The light crosses the crater. Raditz flies up and to one side. Goku turns his hands, and the wave bends after him.

Goshin watches the turn as Raditz catches the wave against both palms. Light spreads around his arms and washes over the ridge.

Gohan shields his face. "Father!"

Goku pushes forward, and the wave presses Raditz back through the air. Raditz brings his arms together again, fighting to keep the light away from his face.

Goshin leans out from behind the rock. Goku shifts his wrists as Raditz moves, and the wave follows toward the far side of the crater in one steady stream.

Raditz holds his position in the air and forces his hands apart. The wave breaks around him, and its light fades over the far slope.

"He stopped it," Goku says.

Raditz lowers his arms and stays aloft, although his armour is scorched.

"Now let me give you something."

He fires toward Goku. Goku raises his hands, but the blast reaches him before he can move away. It strikes his guard and drives him against the crater wall.

Gohan runs down the slope with tears on his face. "Father!"

Goshin flies after him. Raditz raises his hand again, aiming at Goku and the boys.

Goshin lands to one side of his father. He brings his palms together at his hip, as Goku did. Light gathers between them, then flares around his fingers.

Raditz turns toward him. The scouter gives a rapid signal, and **1,450** flashes in the lens.

"The boy's attack? That's higher than Kakarot's!"

Goshin braces his feet as the light spreads against his palms. "Gohan, get down."

Gohan drops beside Goku and pulls at his arm. Goku tries to push himself up, but the wall behind him crumbles where the first blast struck.

"Father, come this way," Gohan says. "There's room behind the rock."

Gohan gets both arms around Goku's shoulders and pulls him out of the falling dust. Raditz aims at them before they can reach the rock.

"Then protect them," he says, and fires.

Goshin calls, "Kamehameha!"

Goshin thrusts his hands forward. His wave meets Raditz's blast from the side. Goshin's light spreads wide and tears into the empty rock beside him.

His arms jerk apart. He plants his feet, brings his palms back together and turns them toward the open slope. Both streams swing away from Gohan and Goku.

The blasts strike the slope and burst. Stone falls into the crater. The force pushes Goshin back, and he lands on one knee with his burned palm against his chest.

Gohan pulls Goku's arm across his own shoulders. "Come on."

Goku gets one foot under him. "Goshin?"

Goshin opens his hand. The skin is red around the old burn, but his fingers move. He rises and takes Goku's other arm.

"I saw it," Gohan says. "You did Father's attack."

Goku looks from the damaged slope to Goshin's hands. "You watched the whole thing?"

"Your hands went this way," Goshin says. He shifts his palms to show the turn, then winces and lets them fall.

"That was a good shot. But don't make that burn any worse if you can help it."

Goshin helps Goku stand. "This way."

Gohan ducks under Goku's other arm, and together they move him toward the broken wall. Goku takes his weight off them once his feet are steady.

Raditz descends toward them, looking at Goshin. "I won't let that child fire again."

Goshin raises his good hand. Raditz lunges toward him, but Goku pulls his arms free and strikes Raditz's arm aside.

"Piccolo!"

The light at Piccolo's fingers sharpens to a point.

## 7 · THE FIRST SHOT

Raditz turns toward Piccolo. His scouter begins to sound again.

"One thousand three hundred and thirty?"

Piccolo opens his eyes. "Get away from him, Goku."

"I can't get a clear path," Goku says. "He keeps coming with me."

"Then make him turn. I won't wait much longer."

Goku takes a step toward the boys. Raditz moves with him, keeping Goku between himself and Piccolo. Gohan looks from Raditz's back to the stone still held in his own hand.

Goshin rises beside the wall and fires at Raditz's left. Raditz turns to block it. Gohan pulls Goku down the slope, out of Piccolo's line.

Goku calls, "Now!"

Piccolo extends his two fingers and shouts, "Makankosappo!"

A narrow beam shoots across the crater, with a second strand of light winding around it. Raditz twists away. The beam tears through the edge of his shoulder armour and cuts the skin beneath it.

The beam continues beyond the crater and breaks a distant ridge, raising dust behind it.

Raditz lands on the slope, looks at the damaged plate and touches his shoulder. His fingers come away marked.

Piccolo lowers his hand. "He dodged it."

Raditz looks up at him. "That went straight through my armour. If it had hit me, I'd be dead."

Piccolo looks toward the broken ridge beyond him, then back at the wound. "I should have aimed farther to your left."

"It wouldn't have mattered. I saw it coming."

"And it still took part of your shoulder."

Raditz pulls a loose piece of armour away and drops it into the dirt. "You won't get another chance."

Raditz lifts his hand toward Piccolo. Goshin flies up from the wall and sends a blast at the raised arm. Raditz turns his wrist; the blast passes beside him.

Gohan throws his remaining stone, hitting the edge of Raditz's scouter and making him look toward the ridge.

Goku comes up behind him and closes both hands around his tail.

Raditz's arm drops as he falls to one knee, with Goku standing behind him.

Goku calls, "I've got it! Piccolo, use that attack again!"

Piccolo lifts his fingers to his forehead. "Hold on to him. I've only got enough left to fire it once more."

Goshin lands beside Gohan, clear of the line between Piccolo and Raditz. Gohan keeps his eyes on their father.

"Don't let go," Piccolo says.

Goku tightens his grip as Raditz braces one hand against the ground, but Raditz's legs do not straighten.

## 8 · THE PROMISE

"Kakarot," Raditz says. "Would you kill your own brother?"

"You tried to kill me. You took my sons."

"I didn't kill them."

"You said you would."

Raditz's fingers scrape through the dirt. "That was a mistake. I'll stop."

Piccolo's light begins to grow again. "Don't believe him."

"Listen to me, Kakarot. I'll leave this planet. I won't come back."

Goku looks at Piccolo and says, "Wait a minute."

"He's lying."

"Please," Raditz says. "I know what I've done. Give me a chance to make it right."

Goku's hands loosen a little around the tail, and Raditz lifts his head.

"Father," Gohan says.

Goku looks at him.

"He said you had to kill people before he'd let us go. He shut us in that thing when Goshin was hurt."

Goshin steps closer to his brother. "He hurt you too."

"I know," Goku says.

Gohan keeps looking at the hand Raditz has planted on the ground. Goshin moves a little closer, but Goku shakes his head and keeps both hands on the tail.

"Stay beside your brother. Piccolo needs a clear shot if we can't settle this. Tell me if Raditz tries to fire from that hand."

"Stay beside your brother. Tell me if Raditz tries to fire from that hand," Goshin repeats. He watches Raditz's fingers.

Raditz looks toward the pod, where its hatch is lying apart from the hull, then turns back to Goku.

"I gave you my word. You don't have to kill me."

Goku keeps hold of the tail. "If you mean it, stay there while we work out how to let you leave."

"Son Goku," Piccolo says. "Don't be a fool."

"I'm not letting him up."

"I can't hold this attack all day."

Goku looks at Raditz. "The other Saiyans you mentioned. Are they coming here?"

Raditz's hand moves across the dirt. "Let go, and I'll tell you what you want to know."

"Tell me first."

Gohan takes Goshin's hand. Piccolo's light brightens, throwing a sharp shadow from Raditz across the ground.

"You don't trust your own brother," Raditz says.

"I don't know you."

Raditz reaches behind himself with his free hand. Goku pulls the tail higher, and Raditz's arm drops again.

"Keep that hand where I can see it."

"You think this makes you a warrior? Holding on while someone else does the work?"

"I think it keeps you from hurting anyone."

A point of light appears between Raditz's fingers. Goshin calls, "Father, his hand!"

Raditz drags that hand back toward the base of his tail.

As Goku pulls, Raditz twists with the movement and presses the light against the fur. Goku tries to drag him away from his own hand, but Raditz catches the ground with his free fingers.

The tail parts.

Goku stumbles backward, still holding it. Raditz rolls away and pushes himself up, one hand clamped over the wound.

"You should have killed me," he says.

## 9 · THE OPENING

Goshin fires at Raditz's chest. Raditz knocks the blast aside and lunges at Goku. Goku drops the severed tail and raises his arms, but Raditz strikes him across the face.

Goku hits the ground, and Raditz follows him down with a knee to the stomach.

Goku calls, "Gohan, back!"

As Gohan runs toward Goku, Raditz swings an arm toward him, and Goshin catches it with both hands.

"Leave him alone."

Raditz tears his arm free and fires into the ridge above them. The rock breaks over Gohan. Gohan braces beneath a falling slab while Goshin blasts its edge apart.

The pieces fall around their feet. Gohan pushes the last one away and looks for Goku.

Raditz drags Goku across the slope and throws him onto his back. Before Goku can rise, Raditz plants a boot against his chest.

Goku catches the boot with both hands and pushes. Raditz leans on it until Goku's elbows bend, then looks toward Piccolo's gathering light.

"You believed I'd changed," Raditz says. "You even asked me to stay still."

Goku grips the boot. "I didn't let you go."

"You waited. That was enough. I'm a first-class warrior, Kakarot. If I have to kill my brother to win, I will."

Goshin flies around the fallen rock and fires again. Raditz turns, putting Goku between himself and the blast. Goshin shifts his hand, sending the shot into the ground beside them instead.

Raditz kicks a sheet of loose stone at him. Goshin breaks through it and keeps coming, but Raditz fires at Gohan. Gohan drops behind the slab he moved, and Goshin turns the blast upward before it reaches him.

Gohan rolls out from the other side of the slab and throws a broken piece of stone. It hits Raditz's boot; Goku pulls at the ankle, but Raditz stamps down before Goku can get clear. Gohan ducks behind the slab again as Raditz turns toward him.

Goshin sends two more blasts toward Raditz's raised hand. Raditz bats the first into the ground and moves his arm away from the second.

Gohan calls, "Father, try again!"

Goku pulls at the boot. Raditz raises his hand toward Gohan, and Goku keeps one hand on the boot while catching Raditz's wrist with the other.

Piccolo takes a step along the far slope, his fingers still at his forehead. Raditz watches him over one shoulder.

"Go on," Raditz says. "Try that attack again. I'll move, and you'll hit your friend."

Piccolo's hand remains raised. Goku is beneath Raditz, and the boys are closing in from the ridge.

"Hold still and I won't miss," Piccolo says.

Raditz jerks his wrist free and presses down. Goku's hand slips from the boot, and his cry carries across the crater.

Gohan comes out from cover. "Stop it!"

Goshin lands beside him. His palm shakes once; he closes his hand and raises the other.

## 10 · THE PAPER

At the mountain house, Chi-Chi is humming. The paper with the red circle hangs against the doorframe. The washing moves on the line beside her.

She takes the pegs off a boy's shirt, then puts them back on.

"Eight hours a day," she says. "Every day of the week. And there's the paper to show for it."

She looks at the circle. "Would you look at that."

"The paper came today, and he's not here to see it."

Chi-Chi lifts the basket onto her hip. She looks down the path beyond the gate, then back at the paper.

"Goku will be pleased when he sees it. They'd better come home soon."

She carries the basket inside. The paper stays on the frame.

## 11 · BOTH BROTHERS

Raditz bears down on Goku's chest. Goku reaches for his ankle, but Raditz drives his foot harder against him.

"You wanted me to leave," Raditz says. "I'll leave when this planet belongs to us."

Gohan shouts, "Get away from him!"

Goshin flies low across the ground, and Raditz fires at him. Goshin turns the blast aside with his good hand and sends it past the broken pod.

He closes the distance and strikes Raditz's arm. Raditz loses his balance for an instant. Goku gets a hand under the boot and pushes, but Raditz brings his weight down again.

Goshin lands another blow against the damaged shoulder. Raditz grabs for him; Goshin pulls back, fires at Raditz's wrist and comes in again from the other side.

The blow turns Raditz far enough for Goku to get one shoulder off the ground. Raditz catches Goku by the front of his clothes and forces him down again.

"Father, move when he lifts his foot," Gohan says.

"Get away from him," Goku answers. "Both of you."

Raditz sends a blast along the ground. Goshin rises over it, and the blast bursts against the crater wall. Gohan runs through the falling dust toward his father.

Goku calls, "I said get away!"

Gohan stops a few steps from Raditz. Goku's fingers are still against the boot. Raditz raises his free hand above him.

"You can watch, then."

Gohan's hands close, and the stones around his shoes begin to jump, then lift from the ground as he takes another step toward Raditz.

"Leave my father alone!"

Raditz's scouter beeps, and he looks from Gohan toward Goshin above him as the sound comes faster.

Goshin stops in the air. His burned hand hangs open, and light gathers around him, lifting dust from the ground below.

Raditz looks from one brother to the other and shifts his feet to face them both. Goshin lowers himself until he is level with Gohan.

"Gohan," he says.

Gohan looks up.

"Go left. I'll take the other side."

Gohan sets his feet. "Together."

Raditz turns his hand toward Gohan. Goshin fires first, forcing that hand away from his brother. Gohan runs in from the left as Goshin drives forward from the right.

Piccolo holds his aim. Goku looks up at both of his sons.

The scouter gives a long signal.

The broken hatch lies on the crater floor. A stone strikes its bent edge and falls into the dust.
