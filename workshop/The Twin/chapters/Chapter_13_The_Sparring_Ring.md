# Chapter Thirteen — The Sparring Ring

A leaf lies beneath a stone in the yard. Its tip lifts each time the wind reaches it.

**THE SPARRING RING**

**NARRATOR:** The two Saiyans are on their way to Earth. Goku has a family to protect, friends searching for new training, and a son who is ready to fight him.

---

## The Next Morning

Gohan wakes with both hands closed around his blanket. Light reaches the floor through a gap in the curtains. His hat rests beside the window, its Four-Star Ball glowing orange.

From the kitchen comes the scrape of a knife, then Goku's laugh.

Gohan sits up and looks at the empty place beside him, then finds Goshin's folded blanket at the foot of the bed.

"Father?"

"In here!"

Gohan carries his hat into the kitchen, where Goku is at the table with one of Chi-Chi's bowls in his hand. Goshin stands beside the stove, watching Chi-Chi crack eggs into a pan.

"Morning, Gohan," Goku says.

Gohan puts his hat on. "I dreamed Raditz was there again."

Goku puts down the bowl. "He's gone."

"I know. I looked for you anyway."

Goku reaches over and settles the hat on Gohan's head. "You found me."

Gohan slips two fingers beneath the brim and checks the ball where it is fastened to the hat.

"The other Saiyans know about this, don't they?"

"They heard Piccolo talk about the Dragon Balls. We'll have to be ready when they come."

"I'm going to get stronger too," Gohan says.

Goku smiles. "You helped us against Raditz. I want to see what you can do now."

Chi-Chi turns the eggs in the pan, then puts some on a plate. She warms the rice left from the previous night and sets it beside the egg while Goshin watches. When she places the plate in front of him, he sits and picks up his chopsticks.

"It's still hot," she says.

"It's still hot."

Goshin blows on the rice and tastes it. Goku takes a piece of egg from his own bowl.

"That's good!"

"You said you were still full," Chi-Chi says.

"I am. That doesn't mean I can't taste it."

Gohan opens the book he left on the shelf, with Mina's paper marker still sticking out between the pages.

"C-6 wanted to hear about the tunnel," he says. "We promised to bring this today."

"Then let's go see him," Goku says. "After we've had some practice."

Gohan looks up. "Can I practise with you?"

"Sure!"

Goshin puts down his chopsticks, and Goku catches his eye.

"What about you, Goshin? What do you want to do?"

"Will you fight me?"

Goku's eyebrows rise. Then he grins. "You mean a real match?"

"Yes."

"I want one too," Gohan says. "Can I go after Goshin?"

"Both of you? Now that's a good morning!"

Chi-Chi takes the pan from the stove. "And where are you planning to have these matches?"

Goku looks through the window at the yard.

"Not there," she says.

"I was just looking!"

"The stone basin beyond the ridge is empty. Keep your blasts away from the house."

"Keep your blasts away from the house," Goshin repeats.

"I'm coming with you," Chi-Chi adds. "Gohan, put the book away before we leave."

Gohan checks the paper marker before putting Mina's book back on the shelf and closing the door behind it.

## At the Tower

The previous evening, the aircar reaches Korin Tower before sunset. Yamcha steps onto the high platform, and Puar floats out behind him. Korin waits beside the doorway with his staff.

"You're after Tien and Chiaotzu?" Korin asks.

"That's right," Krillin says. "Have you heard where they are?"

"Somewhere in the western mountains. A traveller mentioned a waterfall, but there are plenty of those."

Bulma takes a map from her bag. "Then we'll have to narrow it down."

Yamcha looks up through the clouds. "I'm going to find out how to get to Kami's place."

"I'll come with you," Puar says.

"First, come inside," Korin tells them. "You can look at the tower in the morning."

Yamcha follows him indoors, still looking upward through the clouds that gather above the platform. Puar carries Yamcha's bag and sets it beside a low table.

"Krillin and I will bring the others as soon as we find them," Bulma says.

"Good," Yamcha replies. "Tell Tien not to expect me to wait around for him."

By first light, Bulma has marked three mountain valleys on the map, and Krillin climbs back into the aircar with the small bag still tucked inside his shirt.

"We've got one Senzu left," he says. "I'll keep it safe."

Bulma folds the map across her knees. "And the scouter stays at the lab until I know what it can send. We'll look for them ourselves."

"If we're close enough, I might be able to feel Tien's ki," Krillin says. "Especially if he's training hard."

"Then tell me which way to go when you do. Until then, we're checking every waterfall on this map."

The aircar turns west.

## The Waterfall

Water strikes the pool below the cliff. Tien stands beneath the fall with his feet planted against the rock. Chiaotzu hovers beside the bank, holding a smooth stone in each hand.

Tien raises both arms. His ki presses upward, and the falling water slows above his head. For a moment the stream hangs between the cliff and his palms.

"You've got it, Tien!" Chiaotzu calls.

Chiaotzu lifts one stone with his ki and sends it across the falling water. Tien shifts his left hand, pushing the current outward before the stone can enter it.

Chiaotzu catches the stone above the pool after it clears the stream, then brings it back to the bank.

Tien pushes higher. The water parts around him, then crashes down on both sides. He lowers his arms, breathing hard, and steps out onto the bank.

"The left side gave way first," he says. "Let's try that again."

Chiaotzu sets the stones on a flat rock. "I'll watch the left."

A burst of gunfire cracks through the trees.

"Hey!" Launch shouts. "Dinner's ready!"

Tien looks toward the clearing. "It's morning."

"Then call it breakfast! Get over here!"

Launch stands outside a small house with her gun in one hand. A long table has been set beneath the awning. Bowls, plates and covered dishes crowd its surface, and three cooks in white coats stand beside it.

Chiaotzu retrieves the stones from the flat rock and carries them to the table. He sets them beside a bowl as Launch lifts a lid, revealing a dish of steaming vegetables.

"I hired the best cooks I could find," she says. "And I told them to use the best ingredients."

One cook lifts a basket of utensils. "Is there anything else?"

"No. You've done your job."

As the cooks gather their things and leave along the path, Launch pulls out a chair for Tien.

"Sit down. There's plenty."

Chiaotzu peers into another bowl. "This must have cost a lot."

"I had a good job yesterday. Don't worry about it."

Tien stays standing. "We were training."

"You've been training every time I find you. How long are you going to keep this up?"

"Until he beats Goku," Chiaotzu says.

Launch throws up her hands. "Come on! You're strong enough already. Why not have some fun?"

Tien looks toward the waterfall. "Meeting Goku showed me how much stronger a fighter can become. There are others out there too. I want to find out how far I can go."

"I'll show you a better way to spend your time. You and me could rob a bank together. With all that money, we could eat like this every day."

"No."

"You haven't even thought about it!"

"I don't want to rob a bank."

Chiaotzu takes a small bite from a bowl. "The food is good."

"See? At least somebody appreciates it."

Tien picks up the two smooth stones from the table. "Chiaotzu, are you ready?"

Chiaotzu sets down the bowl. "Yes, Tien."

Watching them return to the water, Launch puts the lid back on the vegetables with a clatter.

## The Ring

The stone basin lies beyond the ridge, far from the house. Gohan drags a piece of white stone across the floor, drawing a circle around a wide patch of packed earth.

"If either of you touches the ground outside the circle, the other one wins," he says.

Goku steps inside and tests the ground with his heel. "Can we fly?"

"Yes. But if you come down outside, you're out."

"All right!"

Chi-Chi and Gohan stand on a ledge north of the ring. A thick shelf of rock rises behind them. The rest of the basin is open, with empty sky above it.

"No blasts toward this ledge," Chi-Chi says.

"No blasts toward this ledge," Goshin repeats.

"We'll hold back enough to keep the waves aimed," Goku says. "If one starts to get away from us, we turn them up and stop."

Goku walks to the opposite side of the circle. Goshin steps in, turns to face him, and lets his tail settle behind his legs.

"Ready?" Goku asks.

"Yes."

Gohan raises one hand. "Start!"

Goshin moves first. His fist drives toward Goku's chest. Goku turns his shoulder and catches the wrist, but Goshin's other hand is already coming up.

Goku ducks, but Goshin's palm strikes his shoulder and sends him skidding sideways, his boots cutting twin tracks in the packed earth.

"Good one!"

Goku springs back in. He sweeps at Goshin's feet, and Goshin jumps over his leg. Goku catches him in the air and turns, carrying him toward the white line.

Goshin fires a short burst at the ground. Dust rises between them, and the force twists him out of Goku's grip. He lands inside the circle.

"Still in!" Gohan calls.

Goku comes through the dust with both fists raised. Goshin blocks the first punch, but Goku changes hands and taps his ribs with the second. Goshin slides backward, digging two grooves in the earth.

He stops just short of the line.

"That was close," Chi-Chi says.

Goshin rubs his side once. A scrape on his forearm has turned red. He raises both hands again before Goku reaches him.

Goku kicks. Goshin catches the ankle with both hands and pulls. Goku lets himself turn, plants one hand on the ground and swings his free foot toward Goshin's shoulder.

Goshin lets go and drops beneath it, his tail striking the ground to steady him. He pushes upward and lands a punch against Goku's stomach.

The blow lifts Goku's feet from the earth and sends him backward, but he catches himself above the far edge of the circle.

"Oh, you're strong!"

Goshin rises after him. Goku meets him above the ring, and their arms strike together. Goku turns a punch aside; Goshin kicks at his chest. Goku catches the kick against his forearm and drives forward.

Both land inside the circle. Goshin's heels cut a long furrow, but he holds his ground.

Gohan leans over the ledge. "Goshin, the line's behind you!"

As Goshin glances back, Goku darts in before he can turn all the way around.

Goshin lifts one hand, and his ki raises a loose stone between them. Goku strikes it aside, and Goshin follows through the dust with a punch.

The blow catches Goku beneath the arm and sends him skidding toward the line, but he plants one foot and springs back into the centre.

"You moved that rock?"

Goshin nods.

Goku's grin returns. "Then I won't wait for the next one!"

Goku circles to his left, keeping the white line behind Goshin. A loose stone lies near Goshin's foot, and Goku starts toward it.

Goshin raises the stone with his ki. Goku swings one fist wide, and Goshin moves the stone across to meet it. Goku ducks beneath the stone instead and presses his other palm against Goshin's ribs.

Goshin slides back toward the line. He plants one foot, drops the stone and kicks into Goku's guard before Goku can follow.

Goku grins. "I thought you might try that again!"

Goshin steps forward, pushing against Goku's arm with his knee. Goku gives ground, then turns and sweeps at his feet. Goshin hops over his leg and reaches for both of his wrists.

Goku turns with him until they stand shoulder to shoulder, each holding the other's arm. Goshin pushes with his ki, and Goku's boots lift from the ground.

"Hey!"

Goku twists free, flips in the air and lands near the centre of the ring. Goshin is already flying toward him, with one hand open in front of his chest.

A burst flashes from Goshin's palm. Goku knocks it upward, then bends beneath a second burst. It passes over his shoulder and carves a deep channel along the empty east wall, away from the ledge. Rock breaks free and falls into the basin.

Goshin comes through the settling dust. Goku catches his fist, but Goshin brings his knee up against Goku's guard and pushes him backward.

Digging in his heels, Goku gives Goshin's wrist a sharp pull and tries to turn him toward the line. Goshin flies around the pull, keeping hold of Goku's hand until he has room to kick.

The kick strikes Goku's chest and sends him sliding across the ground; he stops inside the circle and looks down at the mark on his shirt.

"That one really landed!"

Gohan claps from the ledge. Chi-Chi keeps one hand on the rock shelf while the dust settles around the ring.

Goku raises both fists again. Goshin settles opposite him and brushes dirt from his palm. The red scrape on his forearm is already smaller than it was.

## The Wave

Goku runs straight at Goshin. Goshin braces for another sweep, but Goku jumps over him and lands behind him instead.

As Goshin twists, Goku puts a hand on his shoulder and pushes him toward the line.

Goshin flies up. Goku follows, but Goshin rolls over his head and comes down behind him. A quick kick strikes Goku's back and sends him toward the ground.

Goku catches himself on one hand, and the earth cracks beneath his palm. He pushes off again, climbing until he and Goshin face each other above the middle of the basin.

Goshin brings his hands together at his side.

Goku looks at the light forming between them. "You're going to use that?"

Goshin holds his position. The light spreads around his fingers, then draws in as he turns his wrists.

Goku cups his own hands. "All right!"

Below them, Gohan catches Chi-Chi's sleeve, and she moves him farther behind the rock shelf while keeping both fighters in sight.

The two lights brighten above the ring.

Goshin thrusts his hands forward. "Kamehameha!"

His wave crosses the open air. Goku fires from the other side, and the two streams meet above the centre of the basin.

The collision flashes white. Its pressure gouges two trenches into the empty centre of the ring and strips stone from the east wall. Dust rolls across the basin.

Goku holds his palms together. His boots slide backward through the air as Goshin's wave presses against his. Goku shifts one hand over the other, tightening his aim until the light stops advancing.

"That's it! Keep it coming!"

The edge of Goshin's wave begins to spread. He turns both palms inward, drawing it back into a narrow stream before it can reach the basin wall. The focused wave drives Goku farther across the basin.

Gohan shields his eyes from the light. Fragments skid across the ledge; Chi-Chi keeps him close behind the rock shelf.

Goku flares his ki and drives forward through the air. His wave gains ground, and the meeting point moves toward Goshin.

Goshin shifts his hands, his tail swinging once, and sends more ki through the centre of his wave. The meeting point stops, then pushes back toward Goku.

"You're really pushing me back!" Goku calls.

Goku's arms tremble. He braces in the air and pushes again, holding the meeting point between them. Goshin presses both palms forward, and a crack runs through the dirt far below.

As the light swells between them, the crack below reaches toward the ledge. Goku sees it and turns his hands toward the open sky.

"Goshin, point up!"

"Point up!"

When Goshin lifts his hands, both waves peel apart and shoot upward, clearing the rim of the basin. Goku cuts off his wave and Goshin closes his palms; the remaining light disperses high above the ridge.

Goku lowers his arms. Below him, two fresh trenches scar the middle of the empty ring, and broken stone lies at the foot of the east wall. Gohan steps out from behind the shelf and looks at the sky.

"Did either of them land outside?" Chi-Chi asks.

"No. They're still up there!"

Goku and Goshin drop toward the circle and land facing each other, both inside the white line.

The scrape on Goshin's forearm has already faded. He opens and closes his hand, then sets his feet again.

Goku rolls his shoulder. "You nearly pushed me all the way back."

Goshin looks at the line, then at Goku. "We haven't finished."

"We sure haven't!"

Goku sends three small blasts across the ring. Goshin catches the first against his open palm and pushes it upward. The second follows it into the sky, but the third slips past his fingers and bursts against the ground beside him.

Goku comes through the dust before Goshin can lower his hand. Goshin ducks the first punch and catches the second against his palm. Goku changes direction, stepping toward the open side of the ring.

Goshin turns with him and keeps hold of his wrist. Goku pulls back, then drives forward instead. Their shoulders meet, and both slide across the packed earth.

"Look at their feet," Gohan says. "They're nearly at the line."

Goshin releases Goku and sends a short burst into the ground beside Goku's foot. The earth breaks. Goku jumps clear and lands on a falling stone.

Goshin raises two fingers, and his ki tips the stone before Goku can push off it.

Goku twists in the air. Goshin reaches him with a kick to the ribs, follows with a punch and drives him across the circle.

Goku catches Goshin's wrist and pulls, trying to bring him past the line as well, just as he did at the start of the match.

Goshin turns with the pull. His ki presses against Goku's arm, holding it out for an instant while Goshin swings around to Goku's other side.

Goku reaches for him with his free hand. Goshin knocks it aside and strikes him in the chest, sending him across the last stretch of the ring.

Goku lands on his back beyond the white line, raising dust around his shoulders.

Gohan points. "Father's out! Goshin wins!"

For a moment Goku stays where he is. Then he sits up and looks at the line between them.

"You got me! I thought I had your arm."

Goshin lands inside the ring. "You did."

Goku laughs. "Next time I'll be ready for that turn!"

He gets to his feet and rubs his ribs. The white mark remains unbroken behind him.

"Goshin, will you do that again? The way you turned when I caught your arm?"

Goshin holds out his wrist. Goku takes it, and Goshin presses his arm aside with ki and starts to turn.

This time Goku lets go, steps inside the turn and reaches for Goshin's shoulder. Goshin ducks before his hand arrives.

"You saw that coming!" Goku says. "What if I move my foot like this?"

He tries the step again, slowly. Goshin watches his feet.

"Do you always practise after a fight?"

"If I see something I want to get better at!"

"Is that how you got so strong?"

"You bet! Grandpa Gohan taught me when I was little. Then I trained with Master Roshi and Krillin. We ran and carried things every day."

"Did you win every fight?"

"Nope! After I lost to a guy named Tao, I climbed Korin Tower. Korin had some water I thought would make me strong, but first I had to catch him and take it."

Goshin steps closer. "Did you catch him?"

"After a bunch of tries! Then I found out the water was just water. Chasing Korin was what made me stronger."

"You kept trying," Goshin says. "That's how you made yourself stronger."

"Yep! And I've trained a lot more since then. I want to see how much stronger I can get!"

Goshin looks from Goku's feet to his face. "You're going to keep getting stronger."

"That's the plan! What about you?"

"I'm going to keep training too. Next time I'll be ready for your step."

Goku grins. "Good! I'll be looking forward to it."

## Under the Water

Tien stands beneath the waterfall again. Chiaotzu floats near the bank and sends one of the smooth stones into the falling stream.

Tien parts the water with his ki and catches the stone. Chiaotzu guides the second one toward him from the other side. Tien shifts his weight and catches that one too, but water strikes his back before he can hold it away.

"I lost the left side again," he says.

Chiaotzu takes the stones and positions himself beneath the edge of the fall. He holds one in the stream with his ki, keeping it suspended while the water presses against it.

Chiaotzu catches the stone just above the pool when it drops, then brings it back to his hands.

"That was longer than before," Tien says. "Try it again."

Chiaotzu raises the stone. This time it stays where he puts it while Tien moves back beneath the centre of the waterfall.

## Three Pyramids

In the wasteland, Piccolo hovers above three stone pyramids. He spreads his fingers, and the structures rise from the desert floor. Sand pours from their bases.

He draws the pyramids higher. One turns, its pointed top passing close to another. Piccolo pulls them apart and holds all three above the empty ground.

He moves the first pyramid to the left and the second to the right, keeping their bases level. The third rises between them, shedding a curtain of sand across the desert.

Piccolo brings his hands closer, and all three structures begin to turn around him, their corners cutting through the drifting dust.

His face tightens. The nearest pyramid jerks sideways.

"No."

Piccolo tries to stop it. The other two tilt, and the first crashes into the desert. Its impact tears a crack through the ground.

The remaining pyramids drop. Stone breaks across the plain, and the crack races toward the distant hills.

Piccolo looks toward the ridge where he last sensed Goku and the boys.

"Damn it."

He flies after the tremor.

## Gohan's Turn

Chi-Chi climbs down from the ledge and looks Goshin over. His hands go behind his back.

"How is your arm?"

Goshin brings it forward. The scrape has almost vanished.

"It doesn't hurt."

Chi-Chi checks Goku's ribs next. "You too."

"I'm fine. That was a great match!"

Gohan steps into the circle. "Is it my turn?"

"You bet!"

Goku takes his place opposite him. Gohan sets his feet, his tail moving behind him as he watches Goku's shoulders. When Goku moves, Gohan drops low and reaches around his guard.

Goku turns aside, but Gohan changes direction and catches the edge of his gi. He pulls it toward himself and taps Goku's chest with his other hand.

"I got you!"

"You did! Now try—"

The ground shudders beneath them. A crack opens across the ring, cutting through the white line. Gohan jumps back, but the earth gives way under his feet.

"Gohan!" Chi-Chi shouts.

He drops into the fissure. His hands catch a shelf of stone below the rim, and his hat tips forward over his eyes. Gohan pushes it back and holds on.

Goku reaches the edge. A second crack runs toward Chi-Chi. She jumps aside as a slab breaks from the ledge above her.

Goshin raises both hands. His ki catches the falling slab before it reaches her. Stone grinds against stone as he holds it away from the path.

The edge beneath Goku crumbles. Chi-Chi catches the back of his gi and pulls as he jumps with her onto a firmer patch, clear of the falling stone.

"I'm clear," she says. "Gohan is below you!"

Goku kneels at the fissure. "Gohan, can you see a way up?"

A rock falls past Gohan's shoulder. Across the gap, another shelf juts from the wall. He looks down at the narrowing space beneath it, then presses one foot against the side of the fissure.

"I can reach that side!"

The walls begin to close.

## The Torrent

At the waterfall, the pool heaves against the bank. Launch stumbles out of the house as the dishes slide from the table.

"An earthquake? Tien!"

Launch grabs an awning post as the ground pitches beneath her. The post pulls free, and she drops it before it can strike Chiaotzu.

Water surges over the rocks toward her. Chiaotzu lifts from the bank and catches a falling branch with his ki before it strikes the path.

Tien runs to Launch, catches her around the waist and braces his feet as the torrent reaches them. Launch grips his shoulder while the water sweeps the loose post past them.

Water climbs to his shoulders. Tien raises one arm and drives his ki against the oncoming stream. The water divides around them and pounds the trees behind the house.

When the branch slips in Chiaotzu's grip, he catches it again before it can fall across Tien, then carries it above the water and drops it behind the house.

"Hold on!" Tien tells Launch.

Chiaotzu flies back beside them. Tien pushes through the water and brings Launch onto higher ground.

The torrent falls back into the pool. Launch coughs and pushes wet hair from her face.

"You took your time," she says.

Tien looks at the water still running from her clothes. "Can you stand?"

"Of course I can."

She gets up, takes one step and sneezes.

Her hair turns blue.

## The Fissure

Gohan pushes off the first shelf. He catches the opposite ledge with both hands, but its edge breaks under his fingers. He drives one foot against the wall and pushes himself upward before the stone can fall.

Goku reaches over the rim. "I've got you if you slip!"

Gohan finds another foothold and jumps again as the walls close below him. Broken rock strikes the place where he was hanging.

He reaches the rim. Goku holds out a hand, but Gohan lands on the ground beside him before he needs it.

"I made it!"

Goku catches him around the shoulders. "You sure did!"

Goshin guides the slab down onto an empty patch of ground. Chi-Chi comes to Gohan, checks his face and straightens his hat.

"Are you hurt?"

"I don't think so. My hands scraped the rock."

She turns his palms over, then looks toward the fissure. It has stopped moving, leaving a dark seam through the ring.

Goku raises his head. "That was Piccolo's ki."

"Is he coming here?" Chi-Chi asks.

"He's on the ridge."

High above the basin, Piccolo stands among the rocks. He can see Gohan beside Goku and Goshin beside Chi-Chi. Goshin lifts the edge of the slab again and sets it farther from the path.

Piccolo folds his arms. "Still standing."

He turns away from the family and flies toward the wasteland. Goku watches until he has gone.

"He's leaving."

Chi-Chi takes Gohan's hand. "We'll use another place next time."

Goku looks at the broken ring, then at Gohan. "We didn't get to finish your match."

"I still want to," Gohan says. "But I want to see C-6 first."

"Okay. We can fight again after that."

Chi-Chi leads them down the path toward the house. Goshin crosses behind Gohan when the path turns and takes the downhill side. At the gate, Gohan looks back toward the basin.

"Do you think Piccolo meant to shake the ground?"

"I don't know," Goku says. "He went away, but we'll keep an eye out."

Inside, Gohan takes Mina's book from the shelf and packs it with the paper marker still in place. Chi-Chi brings a clean cloth for his scraped hands, then closes the house behind them.

When Goku calls Nimbus, Gohan climbs onto the cloud with the book, and Goshin rises beside him. Goku carries Chi-Chi as they turn toward the city.

## A Change of Mind

Launch sits on a dry rock while Chiaotzu offers her a cloth. She dabs at her face and looks at the ruined table.

"Oh dear. Did I make all this trouble?"

"There was an earthquake," Tien says. "The water knocked the table over."

"I'm sorry I interrupted your training. I think I'd better return to Kame House."

Chiaotzu helps her find the path. Tien bows his head.

"Please give Master Roshi our regards."

"I will. Take care of yourselves."

Launch walks down the path and waves once before the trees hide her. Chiaotzu turns toward the waterfall.

"We can train again now."

"As soon as the ground settles," Tien says.

Farther down the mountain, Launch stops beside a patch of flowers. She bends to smell one, and pollen brushes her nose.

She sneezes. Her hair turns blond.

Launch looks down the path, then back toward the sound of the waterfall. "They thought they could get rid of me?"

She turns around.

## Found

The aircar passes over the mountains. Krillin leans forward, one hand against the side of the cockpit.

"There! That's Tien's ki."

Bulma follows his gaze. A plume of spray rises above the trees beside a high cliff.

"I see the waterfall."

They descend into the clearing. Tien and Chiaotzu stand near the damaged table. Launch has just returned and is pointing a finger at Tien.

"I'm not leaving this time!"

"Launch?" Bulma calls.

Launch turns. "Bulma? What are you doing here?"

Krillin jumps out beside her. "We've been looking for Tien and Chiaotzu."

Tien steps toward the aircar. "What's happened?"

"A Saiyan named Raditz came to Earth," Krillin says. "He was Goku's brother. He attacked Goku and his sons."

"Sons?" Chiaotzu asks.

"Two of them," Bulma says. "Gohan and Goshin."

Krillin nods. "Goku was badly hurt, but he survived. Raditz didn't. Before he died, he said two more Saiyans were coming, and they're stronger than he was."

Tien's hands close at his sides. "Where is Goku now?"

"With his family. Knowing Goku, he's probably training too."

Bulma takes the folded map from the car. "Korin sent for both of you. Kami is going to train you, Krillin and Yamcha to get ready."

Tien looks toward the waterfall, then back at Krillin. "Kami himself?"

"That's what we were told."

"I'll go. I want to see what his training can do."

Chiaotzu moves beside him. "Am I invited too?"

"Both of you," Krillin says.

"Then I'm going."

Launch puts her hands on her hips. "And I'm going with Tien. I don't care where this Kami lives."

"The tower is a long way up," Bulma says.

"Then I'll find a way up it."

Tien looks past her toward the sky. "When we meet again, Goku, I won't be the same fighter."

## The Book for C-6

The laboratory door opens, and Gohan carries Mina's book inside. Goku follows with Chi-Chi and Goshin. On the table, C-6 rests against the padded support beneath his bent arm.

His glass eye brightens when Gohan comes close.

"You brought it."

"We promised," Gohan says. "Do you want me to start at the tunnel?"

"The part after the map. The map was wrong."

Gohan finds the paper marker and turns one page back. Dr. Brief draws a chair over for him.

Goshin looks at the support beneath the arm. Goku notices him and turns.

"What is it?"

"Your arm is still bent," Goshin tells C-6.

"I noticed," C-6 replies.

Dr. Brief adjusts his glasses. "I've listened around the casing from the outside. I haven't opened anything. The support keeps the arm steady, but I still need to work out where the leak is."

"And you'll ask before you open a panel?" C-6 says.

"I will."

"Then you can listen again while he reads."

Dr. Brief places a listening instrument against the outside of the casing. C-6 watches him, then turns his eye toward the book.

"Can you hear the leak now?" Gohan asks.

"Not the way I did when I used my reserve. That doesn't tell us where it went."

Dr. Brief listens near the bent arm, then moves the instrument to the other side of the casing. He writes a note on a pad without reaching for a tool.

"I'll tell you what I find," he says. "You can decide what you want me to do."

"Good. And don't let the cat sit on the book."

The cat lifts its head from Dr. Brief's shoulder. Gohan pulls the book a little closer and begins to read about a tunnel under the old temple. He pauses at a drawing of a collapsed wall.

"Did you find this one?"

"No. We found the room on the other side. Your picture leaves out the stair."

Gohan studies the page. "Can I draw it where you say?"

"On a separate piece of paper. That book belongs to Mina."

Dr. Brief brings paper and a pencil. Gohan draws a line where C-6 directs him, then adds the stair beside it.

Goku leans over his shoulder. "Was there anything at the bottom?"

"Sand," C-6 says. "A great deal of it."

Goku laughs. Goshin stands beside him, looking at the drawing. Goku notices and turns.

"What do you think, Goshin?"

"Can we show Mina the stair when we return her book?"

"Yeah! We'll take the drawing too."

C-6's eye moves back to Gohan. "Now read the next page. I want to hear what else they got wrong."

Gohan turns the page.

## The Leaf

The wind lifts the leaf's tip. This time it pulls free and slides across the ground.
