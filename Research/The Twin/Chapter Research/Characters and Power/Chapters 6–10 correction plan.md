**Scope:** a targeted, sequential continuity correction, not a new shot-by-shot adaptation. Starting point: protected Chapter 5 and the five separate Chapter 6–10 candidates. The user delegated the starting point; the entire dependent sequence is revised together, in new files. No original or previous candidate is overwritten.

The earlier individual episode plans and coverage tables were read as the source baseline. They were made before their respective candidates, not before this new correction. This document is the before-prose plan for the correction only. ## One continuous condition, not five resets

The Raditz, aircraft, river and family-reunion events proceed on the same day; the bridge occurs at dusk/night, and the C-6 story starts the following morning. Exact hours are not imposed.

1. **Chapter 6 — source episode 5:** Preserve the 1,307 and 1,500 snapshot readings, Gohan's independent attack and dirt throw, Goku's choice, Goshin pulling him clear, Piccolo's beam and its graze, Raditz's death/warning, the scouter cell, Krillin leaving, and the Saiyan and family cutaways. Make the burn's bleeding stop and show early improvement, while immediate tenderness remains credible. Let Goshin refine the ki he already gathered without turning a momentary reading into a ceiling. Do not assert that the knockout was near death.
2. **Chapter 7 — source episode 6:** Preserve the aircraft, Piccolo's attempt, Gohan's flight and cliff burst, the parents' separate knowledge, Otherworld/Chi-Chi/Korin threads, and the shared escape. Show the previously bleeding palm beginning to close and the head pain receding; distinguish fresh marks from continuing old symptoms. In the emergency, Goshin may instinctively counter Piccolo's non-contact force. If introduced here, it must be on the page, not silently imported from the older protected draft, and can be retained later. Gohan's choices and burst still matter.
3. **Chapter 8 — source episode 7:** Preserve the dinosaur parent-and-young rescue, both boys' work, Piccolo's self-interested intervention, family reunion and independent threads. The head and burn continue to improve rapidly while Goshin acts; let the ankle recover too. Goku and Gohan eat and are fully restored by Senzu; Goshin refuses an unseen bean, and his own healing is visible without it. Chi-Chi can still responsibly seek an assessment after an earlier knockout. No bean or diagnosis is invented.
4. **Chapter 9 — source episode 8:** Keep the initial doctor's examination, the bridge rescue, parents' and twins' effective roles, Mina/book, full-moon precaution, Piccolo's knowledge route, Kami/Baba/scouter/Dragon Ball threads and overnight room. A real safety warning may be conditional on actual symptoms. Make his recovered palm useful in the rescue; maintain the active choices of both boys. Neither looks at the moon or transforms.
5. **Chapter 10 — source episode 9:** The following morning, make the improved condition and any brief reassessment fit the prior day's recovery, rather than restoring a sore head, bleeding hand and painful ankle. Keep the watched breakfast and apple, supervised search, unstable sand, Nimbus and flight, substantial interactions with C-6, the boys' wedge/support/wire/rescue, the robot's consent and energy cost, and the parents' geographically plausible supporting work. Let a previously shown application of Goshin's adapting ki be useful again, without stealing Gohan's or C-6's decision. Do not invent fresh symptoms or a routine post-rescue re-examination with no cause. C-6 remains awake, damaged and uncertain of his energy.
