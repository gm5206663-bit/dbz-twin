Muri guards a locally identified ball, Zarbon watches the intact ship, and Dodoria remains alive on one knee. **Historical branch note, Chapter 38:** Gohan, Goshin and Piccolo are moving toward a threatened Namekian settlement while Bulma remains behind a ridge rock. The profiles below describe **the original Z source situation** unless expressly marked *The Twin*. Episode links and chronology are in [the chronicle](01_EPISODE_CHRONICLE.md); specific media and evidence limits are in [sources](07_SOURCES_AND_GAPS.md). Spellings vary: Freeza/Frieza, Kuririn/Krillin, Tenshinhan/Tien, Chaozu/Chiaotzu, Recoome/Recoom, Burter/Butta, Jeice/Jiece, Saichōrō/Great Elder/Guru and Mūri/Moori can refer to the same figures in different releases.

## The travellers and their families

### Son Gohan

- **Source role:** a young Saiyan–Earthling child; son of Goku and Chi-Chi and Piccolo's student. He insists on going because he wants to help restore Piccolo and the other friends who died. With Krillin he watches Freeza's violence, chooses to rescue Dende, later retrieves and hides Vegeta's Dragon Ball, and risks himself against Guldo, Recoome and Freeza.
- **Character:** compassionate and frightened without being passive; attached to Piccolo and his parents; capable of play, loyalty, anger and independent decisions. His emotional outbursts are particular scenes, not his only source of agency. The Great Elder draws out latent power; later Vegeta comments on Gohan after healing. Neither establishes a constant formula or an exhaustion-free unlimited rage state.
- **Relationships:** Krillin is companion and protector as well as someone Gohan helps; Dende is a rescued child and later a crucial ally; Piccolo is a mentor for whom Gohan fights and whom he carries away; Goku is a father he believes in but also chooses to defend. In episode 100 Gohan returns to confront Freeza because he believes this will protect Earth.
- **Branch:** Gohan has already trained and grown extensively with Goshin and Piccolo; he has choices and abilities of his own. His source motive to revive a dead Piccolo does not apply when Piccolo is travelling alive. No Gohan Kaiō-ken activation has been shown in Chapter 33.

The official manga-based Gohan profile is a useful separate retrospective, not proof of every original-TV detail: [4](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_1201.html).

### Bulma

- **Source role:** engineer, pilot, communicator and Dragon Radar user. She helps locate and modify Kami's ship, makes the interstellar voyage possible and is repeatedly exposed to danger while the fighters leave to investigate. She survives independently through observation, bargaining, deception and improvised equipment, including the local underwater incident and her translator for the frog.
- **Personality and connections:** outspoken, impatient and funny, sometimes frightened or selfish in a way that coexists with real courage and practical intelligence. She worries about her friends and her family's support matters. The frog-translator decision has consequences even though she does not know Ginyu's identity.
- **Historical Chapter 33 branch checkpoint:** she has her tool case, uses the ship's command system, checks the mirrored vessel's machinery, decides with the others and is **not** successfully held hostage at that endpoint. Her independence must not disappear because three strong fighters are present.

### Krillin / Kuririn

- **Source role:** experienced Earth martial artist, Goku's close friend and Gohan's travelling companion and protector. He supports Gohan's rescue of Dende, uses Solar Flare against Dodoria, meets the Elder and receives a potential release, makes pragmatic decisions about the balls and fights alongside Vegeta when there is no better option.
- **Moral position:** practical and afraid when appropriate, yet repeatedly helps others, including a wounded Vegeta. He is gravely injured by Freeza and healed; later Freeza kills him, triggering Goku's first source Super Saiyan transformation. Porunga ultimately restores him after his soul is moved to Earth.
- **Branch:** Krillin stayed on Earth with Goku to watch Vegeta; he is not aboard the Namek-bound ship. His source scenes cannot be assigned to him on another planet without a new cause.

### Son Goku / Kakarot

- **Source role:** recovers from the Saiyan fight, follows in Dr. Brief's six-day ship, trains in increasing gravity and arrives during the Recoome crisis. He heals the three fighters with Senzu, opposes the squad, loses and regains his body to Ginyu, receives medical care, and fights Freeza.
- **Values and contradictions:** protects family and friends, enjoys testing strong opponents, gives defeated enemies chances and yet can become furious. He refuses to let Freeza leave unchallenged after the murders, but later offers the defeated tyrant energy and an opportunity to escape. His wish to remain on Namek is his choice, with consequences for everyone waiting.
- **Abilities and knowledge:** Kaiō-ken and Genki-dama are earlier King Kai teachings; Namek displays later uses, not an automatic lesson for others. He learns about Planet Vegeta from Vegeta in episode 86 in the source; his precise comprehension of the Bardock material requires the episode/version-specific evidence noted in [the source register](07_SOURCES_AND_GAPS.md).
- **Branch:** Goku is alive, unhospitalized and on Earth watching the injured Vegeta with Krillin. He has the author's established desire to test strong opponents. No source-style six-day Namek departure or medical-machine scene is yet established.

### Chi-Chi and the Earth household

Chi-Chi opposes Gohan's source trip out of concern for his life, not only concern for school. She tries to reach Namek and takes part in a separate rescue effort in the original TV account; a failed ship launch and later family reunion are not proof that she did not act. Roshi, Yajirobe, Oolong, Puar, Turtle, Bulma's parents and other Earth friends each participate in news, logistics, anxiety or comic relief in particular scenes. Dr. Brief and Capsule Corporation make the second ship and other equipment possible; Mr. Popo guards and operates the Lookout and gathers Earth's balls when Kami returns.

**Branch:** Chi-Chi explicitly permitted the boys' fighting after their soreness ended, and consented to the journey after family and ship discussions. She and Roshi visit the guard in Chapter 33. Tien and Chiaotzu watch Nappa; the guard is provisional. Their education, feelings and decisions still matter even when they are not on the voyage.

### Piccolo, Kami and Mr. Popo

- **Piccolo, source:** died protecting Gohan in the Saiyan conflict and trains in the afterlife. He requests revival partly because it also restores Kami and Earth's balls, then asks to be sent to Namek. His meeting with Nail is consequential and voluntary; as a stronger fighter he challenges Freeza. He is grievously injured but alive when Gohan carries him away.
- **Kami, source:** Earth's Guardian, linked to Piccolo through their divided origin. Piccolo's death means Kami's death; Piccolo's revival restores him and Earth's Dragon Balls in this situation. Kami provides information and works with King Kai and Popo on the later resurrection plan.
- **Mr. Popo, source:** helps locate and operate Kami's old ship, remains responsible for the Lookout and collects Earth's Dragon Balls. His knowledge of the ship and the old language matters.
- **Branch:** Piccolo and Kami are alive. Piccolo is aboard the ship with Bulma and the boys; the source's first and second Porunga wishes therefore do not have their original necessity. The life link remains important, but research does not make a future death inevitable.

### King Kai, Bubbles and Gregory

King Kai observes great powers from afar, warns against Freeza, supports afterlife training and transmits messages. Bubbles and Gregory serve in original-Z training material; the source Piccolo and dead humans follow their own sequence. King Kai proposes the double-dragon rescue and relays wishes, but he also makes imperfect judgments, including concluding after the explosion that Goku has died. Long-distance sensing and telepathy in particular scenes do not automatically define an unlimited range or infallible information network.

### Yamcha, Tien and Chiaotzu

In the original source all three have died in the Saiyan battle. They reach King Kai's planet and train; the TV has a later confrontation there with four deceased Ginyu Force members. At the wish on Earth, Tien chooses to stay for more training with Chiaotzu, allowing Yamcha to return first; they are revived in the next 130-day cycle. The afterlife fight is a specific anime episode sequence, not evidence that the present branch's living fighters died or that source rankings must override the author's established comparisons.

## Namek and its people

### The Great Elder / Saichōrō / Guru

The aged leader is connected to the Namekian Dragon Balls and grieves the loss of his people. He reads Krillin's intentions, gives him a ball, draws out latent power and enables Dende to help. He initially dies before the third wish; after the Earth dragon restores Freeza's victims he briefly returns, supports the evacuation and appoints Mūri before his final natural death. He cannot be reduced to a power-up station: his moral judgment, people, mortality and succession drive the plot.

A manga-based official profile describes the old environmental disaster, the Elder's eggs and later succession [1](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_2251.html). Its historical language should not be read as a full survey of every later Namekian origin story.

### Dende

A young Namekian survivor rescued by Gohan and Krillin after his village is attacked. He guides Krillin to the Elder, interprets and makes Namekian wishes, and heals living allies. He has reasons to hate Vegeta and initially refuses to heal him; later he acts after appeals. Freeza kills him after observing the effect of the healing, and the Earth wish brings him back. His language, knowledge, courage and own moral choices are as important as his healing.

### Nail / Nailu

The Elder's powerful protector meets Krillin and Dende, then delays Freeza in a fight he cannot win. He regenerates a damaged limb but Freeza observes a reduction in strength. Near death, he proposes assimilation with Piccolo so the combined fighter can oppose Freeza. An official **manga-based** character profile describes the transfer of body/personality [1](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_2250.html); another official editorial interprets possible later influence rather than proving a simple universal identity rule [2](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_1365.html). Original-Z captions separately tag a later Nail voice, with exact audiovisual staging unverified.

### Mūri, Cargo and the villagers

Mūri is the village elder who resists the raiders and later succeeds the Great Elder. Cargo is another Namekian child associated with Dende's village in the separate manga-based official overview [2](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_530.html). The village fighters risk their lives; elders protect the balls and the children. Other villages, including the one attacked by Vegeta, are not interchangeable anonymous crowds: who kills whom affects the later wording and outcome of revival.

**Warrior-type caution:** the episode-70 Japanese caption has three Namekians describe themselves as warrior-types like Nail; episode 77 calls Nail the last at that moment. The manga-based official Nail profile calls him the lone warrior-type. Different sources and times are being described; do not flatten the discrepancy into an invented single population statistic.

### Porunga

Namek's dragon is a wish-granter, not a Namekian villager. In the source situation he requires a Namekian summoning phrase and Namekian wishes, can grant three wishes within his power and initially revives only one individual per wish. His response to Goku's refusal and his restoration of Krillin's damaged body are observed later results. The official manga-based profile [8](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_2809.html) also discusses a **later** removal of the mass-revival limitation; do not retroactively give that upgrade to this first encounter.

## Vegeta and the Saiyan thread

### Vegeta

- **Source starting position:** the Saiyan prince survives the Earth battle, returns to Freeza's base for treatment and heads to Namek. His goal is immortality, which he expects will let him surpass Freeza and everyone else. He is ruthless, ambitious, proud, capable of tactical retreat and not initially a trusted ally.
- **Campaign:** he kills Cui, Dodoria, Zarbon and a Namekian village; steals balls; recognizes the danger of the Ginyu Force; cooperates with Krillin and Gohan when necessary; kills defeated squad members; uses Goku's medical machine as a practical asset; seeks repeated growth after injury; and challenges Freeza. He cries and asks Goku to defeat their common oppressor before dying.
- **After revival:** he returns to Earth with the evacuees, remains competitive and antagonistic, identifies the missing village and offers the soul-transfer solution. His actions do not erase the village killings, make him a settled friend or prove that every one of his claims about power is correct.
- **Knowledge problem:** the Dodoria disclosure in episode 49 and a retrospective in episode 104 do not sit neatly together. Preserve the conflict instead of asserting that the prince always knew or never knew in every version.
- **Branch:** he never escaped in his pod in the shown story. He is alive and injured, guarded on Earth with his tail intact. His next decisions, knowledge, medical care and possible route to Namek are unresolved.

### Nappa, Raditz, King Vegeta and Bardock

These figures provide the Saiyan background rather than a uniform set of present Namek combatants. In the original source Nappa and Raditz are dead by this point; King Vegeta and Bardock appear through backstory/retrospective material. The original-TV retrospective and the 1990 Bardock television special must be distinguished from later manga, *Super* and film revisions. Source characters do not automatically know every later revelation.

**Branch:** Raditz's earlier account and Vegeta's limited warnings are things characters may actually remember; do not inject Dodoria's later source disclosure into the family's knowledge. Nappa is alive and under a different guard. Goshin's Saiyan name and Goku's parentage have their own actual chapter history.

## Freeza's empire and the Ginyu Force

### Freeza / Frieza

The empire's ruler seeks the Namek Dragon Balls for eternal life. He commands Zarbon, Dodoria, soldiers and the special squad; treats planets and inhabitants as expendable; and is cruel, controlled or mocking before his rage and fear become more visible. He has several forms, significant psychic attacks and the capacity to damage Namek. During this source battle Goku infers that he does not sense ki as the Earth fighters do. Freeza is badly injured and believed dead after the fight; a **later** original-Z episode establishes that he survived. Neither a character's boast nor an episode-106 assumption is a final omniscient verdict.

The official first-form profile gives 530,000 and the immortality motive [3](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_576.html); the second-form profile reports over one million [2](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_1819.html). Both are separately labelled manga-based features.

### Zarbon, Dodoria and Cui

- **Zarbon:** Freeza's elegant, image-conscious aide. His powerful transformed form disgusts him aesthetically; he defeats Vegeta, retrieves him for interrogation, and is later killed by the recovered Vegeta. A separate manga-based profile gives his motivations and the recovery chain [5](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_1616.html).
- **Dodoria:** brutal subordinate involved in the village massacre and the chase after Dende. He tells Vegeta about Planet Vegeta's destruction to bargain for his life; Vegeta kills him.
- **Cui / Kyewi:** Freeza soldier and Vegeta's former peer who confronts him on Namek. Vegeta defeats and kills him. Claims of equivalent earlier strength should not be mistaken for equivalent strength at the new encounter.

### Other soldiers and machinery

Freeza's ordinary soldiers use ships, battle armour and scouters; some intimidate villagers and destroy the Earth travellers' ship. Blueberry and Raspberry are named in Toei's episode-59/60 synopsis during Bulma's underwater subplot. A medical staff and mechanical facilities on Planet Freeza No. 79 and Freeza's own ship treat specific fighters. The data available here is not an exhaustive name-and-death list of every background soldier; avoid assigning an unnamed soldier a fandom identity without a specific visual/source match.

## The two voyage detours

### Zeshin, Bun, Emi and the children

The inhabitants of the mirrored vessel are refugee children whose families were attacked by Freeza's forces in the **original-Z** account. Zeshin leads; Bun is distrustful and has a personal reason to hate the attackers; a younger girl's testimony also matters. The retained episode-40 caption explicitly names Zeshin and Bun. **Emi** is a secondary character-guide identification for a girl; without a source-specific audiovisual match do not assign that name to a particular unlabelled caption or to an individual child in the branch. Their fear has an intelligible cause, but their traps are still dangerous. After the meteor rescue Bun continues to distrust the visitors and points to their extraordinary strength; Zeshin reasons differently from their decision to help. Trust is a judgment made by distinct children, not an automatic reward for rescuing them. They hope a former trading partner will shelter them.

**Branch:** at the end of Chapter 33 the children have not yet provided names or their history. The later unaccepted Chapter 34 dramatizes their separate judgments, remembered attack, cooperative rescue and informed departure; see [its source comparison](../../../The%20Twin/canon/SCENE_COVERAGE_040_CH34.md). Their refuge and complete safety remain open, and Miri and Naro are authored branch names rather than verified source identifications.

### Raiti and Zākuro

The apparent Namekians on false Namek are impostors who deceive the original travellers in order to get a functioning spaceship. Their apparent Dragon Ball hunt and geography are a constructed deception, not a reliable guide to Namekian people or the real planet. The branch has not reached this place or established that its route will do so.

## Relationship map — useful tensions, not fixed future beats

- **Gohan ↔ Piccolo:** affection, respect and learned skills; both can decide independently.
- **Gohan/Krillin ↔ Dende:** a rescue creates trust, but Dende has his own grief, choices, language and loyalties.
- **Bulma ↔ fighters:** different skills and risks; even powerful companions still need engineering, navigation and moral judgment.
- **Vegeta ↔ Freeza:** former subordinate and oppressor, with revenge and domination mixed into Vegeta's ambitions.
- **Vegeta ↔ Earth group:** temporary shared danger, real prior violence and competing wishes; neither simple friendship nor a frozen eternal hostility.
- **Namekians ↔ Dragon Balls:** inherited guardianship and a community under attack; the balls are not unowned game pieces.
- **Freeza ↔ Ginyu Force:** obedience and elite competence, complicated by theatrical play, fear and ambition.
- **Goku ↔ Freeza:** concern for the murdered, appetite for a test, anger, choice, mercy and renewed violence interact.
