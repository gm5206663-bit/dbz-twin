2. Goku begins the Bubbles chase, eats, receives King Kai's weighted bands and says he is a Saiyan. The first attempts are physically difficult, and rest and food are shown.
3. On Earth, Bulma reports the covered machine has reached a separate lab room. The boys complete schoolwork, practise and each choose a Piccolo exchange. Chi-Chi offers footwork advice and tends Gohan's sore shoulder; Piccolo departs independently.
4. The director offers the shared common room for the week. Bulma takes the family to visit the children the next afternoon and brings them home. They work on the boat picture; the school inquiry and longer arrangement remain unresolved.
5. Training, rest and family practice continue. Three weeks after Goku starts, he catches Bubbles without hurting him. King Kai privately names the Kaiō-ken, which Goku is not shown hearing or learning.
6. In the later home call the director says the six have remained together since the prior call and may use the room another week. Pigero's visiting times are agreed for that week. Another family visit with Bulma is planned for tomorrow, not yet completed.

## Chapter 22: unaccepted review sequence

1. Months have passed since the Chapter 21 direct lesson, and nearly a year since Raditz's warning. The twins practise at the mountain clearing; Goshin moves six rocks while firing and catches the board piece at speed. Goku demonstrates his already-practised Kaiō-ken and gathers a Genki-dama. Gohan speaks with Hatch about a folding roof; the director has renewed the children's temporary shared room, and the proposed page remains separate from Hatch's original drawing.
2. Kami and Popo offer the physical room, explain its conditions, two-person capacity, outside/inside time and cumulative limit, and leave the family free to choose. Kami recalls Goku's childhood visit. Bulma supplies blank school forms and brings additional provisions, clothing and blocks. Chi-Chi teaches both boys kitchen safety at home.
3. Goshin and Gohan choose to enter together while Chi-Chi waits outside. The door closes, and Gohan cannot feel her from inside. They experience the increased weight, prepare food, train and record the days.
4. The brothers study, cook, sleep and play. Gohan expresses homesickness and independently chooses to continue; he adapts his existing flight to the greater gravity and later fires a stronger controlled wave through three blocks and into the empty floor. Both improve their fighting; Goshin's strength, ki and response grow, and he eventually flies while physically carrying eight blocks of the size he carried singly in the first week. The brothers clash waves. These feats are not maxima.
5. The brothers' full continuous year ends together. They bring their pages and Hatch's original drawing out and reunite with Chi-Chi at the Lookout after about one outside day. Piccolo recognizes both familiar ki signatures, senses that each is far stronger than he is and travels toward the Lookout, without a meeting shown.
6. Goku continues practising Kaiō-ken. Vegeta and Nappa are still in transit and consider Earth close; no exact date, landing, final Genki-dama test, message or departure is shown. Those remaining episode-21 beats are deferred to the immediately following chapter.
