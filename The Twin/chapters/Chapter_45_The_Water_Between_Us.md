# Chapter 45 — The Water Between Us

*Anime episode reference: Dragon Ball Z, Episodes 84–85 and selected combat elements from Episodes 87–88, adapted to the changed branch.*

## The Rising Water

Water climbs past the ship's window.

Kado catches the edge of the cover before it swings shut. Through the narrow opening, Gohan sees a white figure against the high bank. Freeza has one hand raised. The water beside the ship follows it, curling over the hull.

"Back away from the bend!" Gohan says. "Muri and the others are ahead."

Bulma grips the controls. "Kado, tell me where. I can't see through that!"

"To the left. There's a cut in the bank before the spring."

The ship begins to turn. The rising water strikes its side and shoves it toward the trees. Kado falls against the window; Bulma braces herself against the panel.

Gohan spreads his hands. He sends his ki beneath the floor and out around the hull, taking hold where the water is pushing hardest. The ship shudders. Its movement slows, but the branches are still coming closer.

"Bulma, now!"

She presses the control again. The landing feet swing clear of one trunk. A thicker branch scrapes across the roof with a shriek of metal.

"The aerial!" Bulma cries.

The branch snaps behind them. Gohan feels the ship roll against his hold and shifts his ki to the other side. The water is still rising. Freeza turns his hand, and the water begins to roll toward the bend.

"Don't go that way!" Kado points into the gap between two banks. "There!"

Bulma turns the nose of the ship toward it. Water pours across the window and hides the opening again.

A red streak hits Freeza from behind.

His hand drops. The water falls with it, crashing against both banks. Gohan holds the ship level as the wave rushes past. For a moment the cabin rocks so hard that Bulma has to catch the panel with both arms.

Through the clearing water, Gohan sees Goshin drive Freeza away from the bank. Freeza points a finger at the ship. Piccolo comes after them and fires at his wrist. The shot makes Freeza turn; the thin beam from his finger cuts into the empty water beside the ship instead of crossing its path.

"Gohan!" Goshin calls.

"We're here!"

Freeza twists in Goshin's grip and lashes his tail across the boy's back. Goshin grunts but holds on. He flies outward, pulling Freeza toward the open lake.

A broken length of branch falls toward the window. Gohan catches it with his ki and pushes it aside. It drops into the water behind them.

"The cut is still open," Kado says. "Just beyond that pale stone."

Bulma guides the ship around it. The cabin moves into the shelter of a low bank, and the landing feet touch a narrow shelf of ground. She keeps one hand on the controls until the rocking stops.

Gohan lets his hold on the hull ease. His arms are shaking. He looks toward the lake; Goshin's red light flashes between the trees, and Piccolo's ki is moving after him.

"Can you stay here?" he asks.

Bulma looks through the window at the piece of broken branch floating past. "For a little while. He saw the ship, though. I don't want him finding it here a second time."

"There's another hollow behind this bank," Kado says. "It doesn't lead to the spring. We can see whether the ship will fit."

"I'll have to check what hit the roof first."

Gohan puts his hand on the hatch. "I'm going after them. If Freeza turns back, I'll try to keep him away from you."

Bulma catches his sleeve before he opens it. "You don't have to do that alone."

"I won't be alone. Piccolo and Goshin are there."

She looks toward the gap in the bank. "Then watch each other. And don't tell your mother I let you go without saying that."

Gohan manages a smile. "I won't."

He opens the hatch and steps down. Kado follows him out, keeping close to the side of the ship while he checks the slope.

"The aerial is bent," Kado says. "Something around its base has come loose."

Bulma leans through the hatch. "Don't touch it yet. I want to see what pulled free."

Gohan looks toward the lake. Freeza's ki is still close to Goshin's, but the two are moving away from the bank. He draws a breath and rises between the trees, keeping his eyes on the water ahead of him.

## The Higher Bank

Farther along the channel, Cargo stops at the sound of the water striking the shore.

"Was that the spring?"

"I don't think so," Kado's companion says. She listens, but there is only the stream trickling around their feet now. The heavier sound came from farther back.

The older resident is carrying Muri's covered ball against his chest. Dende takes hold of the cloth when it begins to slip, and together they lift it over a root crossing the channel.

"I'll take it for a while," Muri says.

The older resident shakes his head. "You watch the path. I still have it."

Muri nods and moves ahead. The channel narrows between two banks, with a shelf of dry ground above the water on one side. He climbs onto it and offers Cargo a hand.

"Why are we going up?" Cargo asks.

"If more water comes, I want us somewhere it can pass beneath us."

Dende helps his brother onto the shelf. Kado's companion comes up last, then reaches down for the other end of the wrapped ball. The older resident passes it up carefully, and Muri takes its weight until he can climb beside them.

Dende looks back the way they came. Another distant impact reaches them through the trees.

"Do you think Gohan found Kado and Bulma?" he asks.

Muri follows his gaze. "I hope so. We haven't seen them since he went to look."

Cargo presses close to Dende. "Kado knows the way to the spring."

"He does," his companion says. "And we left the stones where he can see them."

"What if someone else sees them?"

She considers the question. "Someone might. That's why we won't wait in the open beside the marker."

The older resident settles the covered ball in his arms again. Beyond the shelf, a row of young trees grows along the bank. Muri points to the space beneath them.

"We'll go as far as those trees and watch from there. Kado will look for signs, but we should be able to see who comes before we call out."

Cargo picks up a fallen leaf and holds it above his head. It is too small to cover him.

"That won't work," Dende says.

"I know. It was for you."

Dende takes the leaf. For a moment he holds it over Cargo instead. Then they duck beneath the first low branch together.

## A Voice Among the Trees

Bulma climbs onto the ship's lowest landing foot and reaches toward the bent aerial. The metal support has pulled away from its fitting. A strip of outer covering hangs loose, exposing part of the lead beneath it.

"Can you straighten it?" Kado asks.

"Maybe. I don't know what's happened to the connection inside." She studies the scrape on the hull. "I need my tools, and I need us out of the way before I start taking things apart."

Kado peers around the bank. "The other hollow is close. There are trees over it, but the opening is wider than this one."

"Wider than the aerial?"

"I think so."

"That isn't quite the answer I wanted."

"I'll look from above the bank."

"Without going into the open?"

"I'll try."

Bulma climbs down. Kado moves along the water's edge and disappears behind a thick trunk. She goes back into the cabin for her case, glancing at the covered window before she pulls the hatch mostly shut behind her.

A voice calls from the trees.

"Kado?"

He stops. "Sela?"

A figure rises from a shallow ditch beyond the trunk. Her clothes are muddy, and a basket hangs from one shoulder. She stays below the bank, looking first at Kado and then at the white ship.

"I heard you," she says. "I thought it might be a trick."

Kado crosses the gap and takes her by both arms. "I've been looking for you."

"I was at the planting terrace. We saw the strangers over the houses, and I ran down the watercourse." She glances toward the lake. "I didn't know where to go after that."

"Muri and the children got away from the houses. They went toward the lower spring with the others."

"Did everyone get out?"

"I don't know."

Sela looks down at the basket. A few young plants are packed inside, their roots wrapped in wet cloth. She takes a breath and puts the basket on the ground.

"Two others were farther along the terrace when I left. I couldn't see them after the shooting started."

"We'll look for them," Kado says.

"When we can," Sela replies. "Who is at the ship?"

"Bulma. She came with the children and Piccolo. The men attacking us found their ship, so she's trying to move it."

Bulma comes out with her case. Kado introduces them, and Sela looks at the bent aerial.

"The hollow is past that tree," she says. "The opening is wide enough for the white part. I don't know about the pole."

"You're both being very reassuring."

Sela's mouth twitches. "I can stand on the bank and watch it."

"That would help. Stay below the top, though. If you see anyone coming, don't wait for me to finish moving."

Sela picks up her basket and follows Kado around the ship. Bulma climbs back inside. She opens the window cover only as far as she needs, waits for Kado's signal and lifts the landing feet from the shelf.

The ship moves a short distance at a time. Sela raises her hand when the aerial approaches the branches. Bulma stops, lowers the hull and changes the angle. This time the bent support clears.

Kado guides her into the hollow. It is not deep enough to hide the entire ship from every direction, but the bank screens it from the inlet. Bulma lowers the feet and switches off the movement controls.

She joins them outside. Sela gathers fallen branches and places them against the most exposed side of the hull. Kado helps her while Bulma opens her case beneath the aerial.

"Can you call your family?" Sela asks.

"I need this connection for a call that far." Bulma touches the loose covering, then withdraws her hand. "I haven't even checked the inside yet."

Kado looks toward the spring. "We should tell Muri we're here."

"We should," Sela says. "But if that fighter follows one of us, we might lead him straight to them."

The three of them listen. Another impact reaches them across the water, followed by a tremor through the bank.

"I'll fix what I can while there's cover," Bulma says. "Then we'll decide whether to move the ship or go on foot."

Kado nods. Sela sets her basket beside the trunk and takes a place where she can watch the opening.

## A Request at the Lookout

Chiaotzu lands at the edge of Kami's lookout. Popo is waiting near the doorway, and Kami comes out behind him with his staff.

"Tien sent me," Chiaotzu says. "We need help watching Vegeta and Nappa."

Kami's grip tightens. "Have they broken free?"

"No. Goku and Krillin are with Vegeta, and Tien is watching Nappa. But we need an arrangement we can trust."

"What has happened on Namek?" Popo asks.

Chiaotzu folds his hands. "Goku asked me to tell you what King Kai and Bulma reported. I was across the tracks with Nappa when Goku and Krillin talked, and I didn't hear all of it."

Kami grips his staff. "Did you hear anything about Piccolo?"

"Not enough to tell you properly. I'm sorry. Goku and Krillin can give you the reports."

"And the ship here?" Popo asks.

"Goku said Dr. Brief is still testing the pod. Nobody knows yet if it is safe to use."

Chiaotzu looks down. "I should have asked Goku to tell me the rest before I left."

"You came to ask for help," Kami says. "We'll hear the rest at the quarry."

Popo turns to Kami. "I can stay at the Lookout while you go."

"Tien asked both of you," Chiaotzu says. "He didn't tell me to promise what either of you could do."

"Then we won't make that promise for him," Kami says. "I will come and hear what the guards need. Popo, if I am away longer than I expect—"

"I will look after things here."

Kami rests a hand on Popo's shoulder. Piccolo left aboard the ship, and Kami's life is still joined to his. If Piccolo is killed, Kami will not return to the Lookout at all.

"Thank you," Kami says.

Chiaotzu waits for him at the edge. They rise together and leave the Lookout behind.

## Away from the Bank

Freeza tears loose from Goshin and spins above the water. Goshin stops in front of him, breathing hard. The red light streams from his shoulders, and his tail is wrapped tight around his waist.

"You found your ship," Freeza says. "Perhaps your healer is aboard. Or perhaps someone there can tell me where to look."

"You're not going back there."

"You've said that before."

Freeza rushes him. Goshin catches the first punch on his forearm, slips the second and strikes Freeza beneath the ribs. Freeza bends around the blow. Goshin seizes the chance to push him farther over the lake.

Freeza's tail catches Goshin's ankle. He pulls, bringing the boy close enough to strike his sore shoulder. Pain shoots down Goshin's arm. He cries out and drives his knee into Freeza's middle. The tail loosens, and Goshin twists free.

Piccolo reaches the water behind them. He stays to one side, watching Freeza and the shore rather than closing on the two fighters.

Freeza sees him. "Perhaps I should ask you again. Where is Dende?"

"No."

"Still not curious about the question?"

"Not that one."

Freeza's eyes narrow. The broken edge of the island lifts behind Piccolo. Piccolo feels the stone coming and darts aside; it passes him and breaks apart against the water. Smaller pieces continue toward the shore.

Gohan arrives beneath them. He catches the nearest fragment with his ki and sends it down into the lake. Piccolo knocks another away from the bank.

"The rest are mine!" Gohan calls.

He spreads his hands. Two pieces stop in midair; a third slips past them. Gohan turns and hits it with a short blast. It breaks into a spray of chips that fall harmlessly into the water. He lowers the pieces he is holding and turns back toward the fight.

Goshin has driven Freeza away from the island, but Freeza plants both feet against him and shoves. Goshin slides backward in the air. He catches himself above the broken stone as Freeza raises one finger toward Gohan.

Gohan moves before the shot comes. He dives across the island, keeping the rock between himself and Freeza. The beam cuts through the edge behind him.

"You missed!" Gohan shouts.

Freeza follows the voice. Gohan sends a burst into the water on the far side of the island. Spray leaps up, hiding him for an instant as he changes direction.

Freeza fires into the spray. Gohan is already below it. He comes up behind the broken ridge and sends another shot toward Freeza's side.

Freeza knocks it away. The small delay is enough for Goshin to reach him again. Goshin catches his arm and turns him away from Gohan.

"I saw that," Goshin says.

"Good," Gohan calls back. "I wanted him looking at me."

Freeza strikes Goshin across the cheek. Goshin's head snaps aside, but his hand remains on Freeza's wrist. He pulls the arm down and punches him in the chest.

Freeza flies backward. He stops above the open water and looks from one brother to the other. His expression has lost its smile.

"I was willing to keep you alive," he tells Goshin. "You've made that less appealing."

"I don't want anything from you. Just leave these people alone."

"They have something I came for. So do you, it seems." Freeza looks at Gohan. "A name, if nothing else."

Gohan's hands clench. "I'm not telling you where Dende is."

"Then I'll find him myself."

The pressure around Freeza swells. Water flattens beneath his feet, and the broken stones on the island tremble. Piccolo feels the increase and looks sharply at Goshin.

"He's raising it again!"

Goshin feels it too. He brings his hands up. Freeza moves, and Goshin meets him over the lake.

Their fists strike. Freeza forces Goshin's guard inward and drives him down toward the water. Goshin turns with the pressure, catches Freeza's arm under his own and swings him sideways. They hit the surface together. A sheet of water climbs around them.

Goshin comes out first. Freeza rises through the spray and catches him by the front of his shirt. The boy's feet lift clear of the water. Freeza draws back his other hand.

Goshin grabs the wrist. He can feel Freeza pressing against his grip, stronger than a moment ago. The red rush answers him, but something deeper is changing too. Strength builds through his shoulders and back, and his ki rises with it.

He shouts and forces Freeza's hand away from his face. His hurt shoulder aches, yet the arm holds. Goshin pulls the wrist across Freeza's body and strikes him with his free hand.

Freeza releases the shirt. Goshin follows him, landing another blow against the marked shoulder. Freeza blocks the next, but the impact carries him farther from shore.

Gohan feels the rise in his brother's ki. "Goshin's getting stronger."

Piccolo has felt it too. "Then don't let Freeza turn him back toward the bank."

Gohan looks at the island, at the shore beyond it and at the two fighters above the water. He chooses a place to the side of Freeza and moves there, leaving Goshin room to advance.

Piccolo starts after him. A voice enters his thoughts.

*Piccolo. Goku asked me to warn you. Vegeta thinks Freeza may be seeking Dragon Balls on Namek. If the people there have any, he says they should hide them.*

Piccolo keeps his eyes on Freeza. *Muri told him he guards one. Freeza already knows. Muri took it away from the house, but I don't know where it is now.*

*I'll tell Goku what you said.*

*There's something for Kami too. I met the Great Elder. He knew of the child sent away before the storms.*

Freeza slips past Goshin's next strike. Piccolo flies toward them while he answers.

*The child's father was called Katas. The elder said Katas died in the storms. He didn't know whether Katas learned the child reached Earth.*

*I'll tell Kami.*

*And tell Goku the boys are fighting beside me. I can't speak for everyone at the village.*

Freeza raises both hands. Piccolo fires between them. The burst does not stop Freeza, but it makes him turn, and Goshin hits him from the other side. The voice is gone when Piccolo reaches them.

## His Father's Name

King Kai finds Kami as he and Chiaotzu fly toward the quarry.

*Kami, Piccolo has an answer for you.*

Kami stops. Chiaotzu turns back and hovers beside him.

*He met the Great Elder on Namek,* King Kai says. *The elder remembered a child sent away before the storms. The child's father was called Katas.*

Kami's hand closes around his staff. For a moment he can hear the wind moving through the empty hollow at Yunzabit. He remembers sitting beside the little house and waiting for someone to come.

*Katas,* he repeats.

*The elder said Katas died in the storms. He did not know whether Katas ever learned you had reached Earth.*

Chiaotzu watches Kami's face. "What is it?"

Kami opens his eyes. "Piccolo found someone who remembered my father."

"Is that good?"

"Yes." Kami's voice catches. He looks down at his hand on the staff. "And it is sad."

He has a name now. He says it once more, aloud this time, so he can hear it in his own voice.

Chiaotzu waits beside him. After a while Kami looks toward the quarry again.

"Piccolo is still fighting?" he asks.

*He was when he spoke to me. Gohan and Goshin were with him. I can't tell you how the fight will end.*

"Then thank him for remembering to send this."

*I will, when I can reach him again.*

Kami turns to Chiaotzu. "We should go to Tien."

They continue toward the quarry, with Chiaotzu matching his pace.

At the guarded pod, Chi-Chi holds the end of a long pole while Dr. Brief checks the scanner. She reaches the bare pole toward the hatch; the instrument stays dark in the doctor's hand. She draws the pole back, and Yamcha passes the doctor a strip of tape to fasten the scanner to its end.

"Is that enough?" Chi-Chi asks.

"For a slow sweep, I think so. We won't try to open anything."

She extends the pole toward the hatch again. This time the scanner's light comes on near the same place it did before. Dr. Brief notes the position, then asks her to move it back.

"Again?" she asks.

"Please. And a little higher this time."

The light comes on again. Yamcha marks the position from the side while Chi-Chi holds the pole steady.

"Does that mean the hatch is dangerous?" he asks.

"It responds there even when we're standing back." Dr. Brief studies his notes. "I still don't know what is producing the response. We need a different measurement before we can decide what to touch."

Chi-Chi lowers the pole. She wants him to say the ship will be ready by morning. She looks at the cordon, the closed hatch and the marks they have made instead.

"What do you need?" she asks.

"Another instrument from the laboratory. One I can leave in place while we move this one."

"I'll get it," Yamcha says.

The doctor tears a page from his pad and writes the shelf and case number. Yamcha takes it and lifts into the air.

Chi-Chi rests the pole against the equipment case. "I'll stay here. If Bulma calls the house again, Puar will hear her."

"And if she calls the laboratory?"

"Yamcha can tell them to send the message here." She looks at the pod. "We'll keep working until we know what this thing can do."

At the Son house, Puar returns to the telephone. He unfolds the note beside it and lays the pencil across the top. The receiver stays silent.

## The Opening

Goku hears King Kai at the quarry while Krillin stands beside him. Vegeta is awake under the shade; across the tracks, Tien remains with Nappa.

*Piccolo heard your warning,* King Kai says. *Freeza already knows Muri guards a ball. Piccolo doesn't know where Muri has it now.*

Goku keeps his face turned toward Vegeta. *The boys?*

*Piccolo said they were fighting beside him when he spoke to me. He couldn't account for everyone at the village.*

Goku lets out a breath. He would like to ask a hundred more questions, but he can hear Piccolo's answer in King Kai's words. His sons are there, and the fight is still going.

"What did he say?" Krillin asks.

"Piccolo got the warning. He said the boys were with him, fighting Freeza. He didn't know about everybody else."

Vegeta leans forward. "And the Dragon Balls?"

Goku knows what Vegeta wants for himself. He will not give the prince Muri's name or tell him where the ball was last seen.

"You were right: there's a ball, and Freeza knows it," he says. "The people there are trying to protect what belongs to them."

"Protect it? Against him?" Vegeta's mouth twists. "They need to get it out of his reach."

"They know that."

Vegeta studies Goku, waiting for more. When none comes, he settles back against the stone. Kakarot has heard something and chosen not to say it. Vegeta will have to find out the rest another way.

Across the tracks, Tien raises a hand. Chiaotzu and Kami are descending beyond the quarry wall.

"They came back," Krillin says.

"Let's hear what they can do," Goku replies. He stays where he can see Vegeta while Tien goes to meet them.

On Namek, Gohan lands beside Goshin on the broken island. Freeza has stopped a short distance away, above the water. Piccolo comes down on their other side.

Goshin lets the red light fade while he draws a deeper breath. His shoulder hurts, and the scrape along his cheek stings. The strength that rose in his body while he held Freeza's wrist is still there. He opens and closes his hand and feels his ki gather readily around it.

"Bulma and Kado?" he asks.

"They got the ship behind a bank. Kado knew another place to hide it, but I left before they moved again. The aerial was bent."

Goshin looks toward the inlet. "Did he hurt them?"

"They were standing when I left. I don't know what happened to the ship underneath."

"All right." Goshin swallows. "I wanted to go after it when he did."

"I know. You got there."

Goshin turns to him. Water has plastered some of his black hair against his forehead, but the rest still stands out in damp spikes. Gohan sees their father in his face, and their mother's eyes fixed on the shore.

"You caught the ship," Goshin says.

"Bulma moved it. Kado showed her where."

"You helped."

"So did you."

Goshin touches his sore shoulder. "He keeps finding this one."

"Then keep it away from him."

"I tried!"

Piccolo's mouth shifts at one corner. "Try again."

Freeza lifts a hand. Three slabs of broken island rise from the water around him. The largest turns toward the shore.

Gohan sees the angle. "I'll take that one."

He flies toward it before either of the others answers. The slab shoots past him; he catches its lower edge with his ki and turns it out over the lake. Piccolo strikes the second piece away from the island.

The third comes straight at Goshin. He puts out one hand and stops it. Freeza darts after the stone, using it to hide his approach.

Goshin feels him coming. He shoves the slab aside and meets Freeza with both arms. The impact cracks the stone beneath Piccolo's feet. Freeza drives forward, trying to carry Goshin back toward the bank.

"No!"

Goshin's voice rings across the lake. He thinks of the ship rolling under the water and of the little houses behind the trees. He plants his feet against a broken rise in the island and pushes.

The red light flares around him again. He drives Freeza away from the shore, across the island and out over the water. Freeza catches his wrists, but Goshin twists one hand loose and strikes him across the face.

Freeza answers with a blow to Goshin's side. Goshin takes it, cries out and holds his place. His tail unwinds for balance as he turns in the air.

Below them, Gohan lets the last slab sink into the lake. Freeza's back is toward him. Gohan could fly at him, but Goshin is in the way of a straight shot. He shifts to the left instead, waiting until his brother turns Freeza's shoulders.

Piccolo sees what Gohan is doing. He rises on the other side, hands ready.

Freeza looks between them. "Do you think three of you will be enough?"

"We're going to find out," Goshin says.

Freeza's ki rises again. Goshin feels the weight of it and grins, though his chest is still heaving. Gohan comes level with him. Piccolo holds his position across the water.

"Don't let him past us," Gohan says.

Goshin nods. This time he does not look back at the shore. He can feel his brother beside him.

"Come on, then!" he shouts.

He rushes Freeza. Gohan moves a heartbeat later, angling toward the opening on Freeza's left, and Piccolo raises his hands as Goshin closes on Freeza above the lake.

### Continuity and source note

This is an unaccepted continuation of the selected Chapter 44. The rising water is Freeza's deliberate attack on the ship. Gohan braces the hull and independently rejoins the fight; Bulma, Kado and Sela move the ship into partial cover, with its aerial bent and its full condition untested. Sela is the worker Kado had been seeking; other residents remain unaccounted for. Muri's group moves separately, with the older resident retaining custody of the covered ball after a brief shared lift. The ship's concealment is not guaranteed.

Goshin's developing body and ki gain real, retained strength during the fight, separate from the temporary Kaiō-ken surge; the renewed factors in this chapter are not assigned numbers. Freeza raises his own power, but no measured maximum, exact factor or fight result is given. Both brothers and Vegeta retain their tails, and Gohan's own decisions and contributions remain distinct. Chiaotzu reaches Kami and Popo; Kami goes with him toward the quarry, but no secure custody or usable Earth craft has been established. King Kai conveys Goku's warning to Piccolo and, at Piccolo's request, carries the Great Elder's bounded answer about Katas to Kami. No one is granted unshown knowledge of the wider rescue or the fight's outcome.
