# GOSHIN — STATUS PANEL

**Owns: Goshin’s working status across the accepted original Chapter 20 and later unaccepted review candidates.**

**Checkpoint:** home with Chi-Chi and Gohan after training and a children’s visit. Living Goku has caught Bubbles after three weeks with King Kai, but no news has reached Earth on-page. The author explicitly accepted the separately preserved pre-correction Chapter 20; its selected correction and Chapters 6–19 remain unaccepted review material. The later Chapters 21–25 are unaccepted continuations and do not alter this accepted checkpoint.

**Revised Chapter 21 candidate:** During his chosen Piccolo round Goshin's physical strength and ki rise, and he responds faster; after further practice his narrow wave cuts deeper at the previous bank mark while he controls four pebbles and a falling slab. Gohan independently chooses and improves his work. The revision addresses the previously identified presentation gap and remains unaccepted; no new measurement, form or chamber year is shown.

**Chapter 22 review candidate:** After an authored several-month outside interval, Goshin and Gohan live and train together for one uninterrupted physical chamber year while Chi-Chi waits outside; about one outside day passes. Goshin flies with a large block in the first week and later carries eight same-sized blocks physically in two stacks under the same gravity. Separately he combines moving blocks with a wave and fires a far stronger sustained wave that splits the empty floor before he turns and stops it. Both boys grow physically; Gohan improves pre-existing flight and fires a much stronger controlled wave through three spare blocks and into the empty floor. Piccolo senses each boy is far stronger than he is at their exit. No exact birthday, scan, form, cap or accepted event follows. The Saiyans land only in unaccepted Chapter 23, later that same morning.

**Chapter 23 review candidate:** Piccolo arrives already recognizing both boys' separate ki and that each now far exceeds his own. He removes his weights and uses his speed, but Gohan and Goshin comfortably outclass him in their individual ordinary spars while holding back. Gohan displays his stronger controlled wave; Goshin uses chamber-developed speed and previously held-back ki while applying his mastered wave with moving blocks. The brothers also spar each other under control and land breathing easily. Chi-Chi watches and coaches; the former eye-blast breach is superseded. The Saiyans land later that morning; the brothers head toward the city, not yet in combat. The comparison is local to this checkpoint; no scan, new form, fixed maximum or Saiyan outcome is implied. This candidate remains unaccepted.

**Chapter 25 unaccepted frontier:** Goshin defeats the pinned Saibaiman and faces Vegeta. Vegeta reads **16,300** and then **19,400** after Goshin releases more previously developed reserves; he clips the scouter to his armour. In sustained combat Goshin then shows real further growth in strength and ki without a number. Gohan independently overpowers Nappa and later joins Goshin against the still-active Vegeta (`O190`–`O194`).

**Chapter 24 unaccepted frontier (earlier checkpoint):** Goshin decisively overpowers Nappa; Gohan independently restrains the Saiyan when he threatens civilians. The boys each quickly defeat a Saibaiman, then combine defensive Kamehamehas against Vegeta's attack. The resulting shock loosens the slab Goshin carries clear while Gohan moves debris; he keeps more previously held-back ki ready while watching Vegeta, with the scouter at 16,300 at that moment. Four Saibaimen remain alive, one pinned; Vegeta gathers light for another attack. Goku is still on Snake Way and Chi-Chi assists evacuation (`O182`–`O187`).

## Growth that must remain visible

1. **Chapter 7 — non-contact rescue:** In the selected unaccepted draft he bends Gohan’s path with ki and diverts a falling slab before catching him. The text does not explicitly describe the earlier root draft’s copied hand gesture or gauze lift (`O098`, `O099`).
2. **Chapter 8 — strength:** Goku says Goshin’s ki feels stronger than at the crater. This is an actual qualitative observation, not merely a possibility of future growth.
3. **Chapter 9 — precision and recovery:** Goku notices the rescue shot is narrower than the earlier crater wave; the doctor observes how quickly the burn has closed.
4. **Chapter 13 — combat integration:** Goshin uses flight, strikes and ki manipulation in a real ring match. During the wave contest he narrows his spreading Kamehameha, adjusts its pressure and redirects it upward when the family is endangered. He wins the ring-out; both fighters were directing and limiting their waves for safety.
5. **Chapter 15 — parallel control:** he keeps four stones on their paths, fires a Kamehameha at one, stops it precisely and catches its fragments while the others continue. This is retained control, not a temporary first-use state.
6. **Chapters 16–17 — applied control and strength:** he keeps the branch raised while lifting Gohan from the water, adjusts its position against the wind, uses ki during the ball exercise and bends both cuff locks at Pigero’s request. These are meaningful feats and applications; they are not automatically a new named power.
7. **Chapter 18 — practice and a distinct mental experience:** at the Lookout he moves four stones on different paths. In the room experience, he holds four larger wall pieces while firing the retained Kamehameha and protects Chiaotzu. That scene alone is not an independent physical measurement.
8. **Chapter 19 — teaching and physical application:** he teaches Gohan and holds four falling rocks while firing the retained wave, then lowers them away from the family. The event is physical in this draft, not a numeric reading or proof of a new form.
9. **Chapter 20 — selected correction, unaccepted:** he flies, controls two stones and reaches Piccolo's shoulder in his chosen exchange; he later practises four-pebble control. The separately preserved pre-correction original, not these new revisions, is accepted.
10. **Revised Chapter 21 — unaccepted:** in the Piccolo round, his strength and ki rise while he is already resisting, and his response becomes faster. After further practice, his narrow wave cuts a deeper channel at the old bank mark while he holds four pebbles in motion and lowers a slab. This is displayed development, not a quantified gain, limit or accepted chapter.
11. **Chapter 22 — unaccepted:** the full physical chamber year builds on prior gains. He flies with one large block in the first week and later physically supports eight same-sized blocks in two stacks under the same gravity; independently he combines moving blocks with controlled waves, and a much stronger sustained wave splits the empty floor before he turns and stops it. His ki also rises in a spar. The year counts in both brothers' lived age and life; no numerical maximum or transformation is supplied.
12. **Chapter 23 — unaccepted:** Goshin and Gohan each comfortably outclass Piccolo in their chosen ordinary rounds. Goshin uses chamber-developed speed and held-back ki and controls his mastered wave with eight moving blocks; the brothers' own short spar remains controlled (`O170`, `O181`).

13. **Chapter 24 — unaccepted:** Goshin decisively overpowers Nappa, and Gohan independently restrains him. The brothers separately defeat Saibaimen and combine waves to redirect Vegeta's attack. The 16,300 reading is held-back power he sustains while on guard after the slab rescue, not a permanent gain from the wave or lift; Gohan retains independent readings and decisions (`O182`, `O186`–`O188`).
14. **Chapter 25 — unaccepted:** Goshin releases more previously developed reserves against Vegeta, with an authored 19,400 reading, then feels genuinely greater strength and ki after the scouter is removed. He applies the development in their exchange; it has no numeric amount, compulsory trigger or demonstrated ceiling (`O192`).

## Later path — planned, not present

- **Great Ape:** the eventual first transformation is conscious and controlled from the beginning. The form and its power develop with Goshin and his deepening Saiyan bloodline, rather than remaining obsolete. The older suggestion that the ape itself must frighten him does not override the later correction. The first trigger is separate from control and has not been fixed beyond the known Saiyan possibilities.
- **Ape power in the ordinary body:** the author’s later direction allows Goshin to access Great Ape power without the giant body as his mastery develops, and to combine it with anger or, once earned, Super Saiyan. Its first appearance, exact mechanics and degree are not established. This is his own evolving power, not an automatic copy of Broly and not a current feat.
- **Golden line:** Super Saiyan 1, 2 and 3, and the distinctive Rage path, are later developments in the stored design. Their eventual perfected presentation is not an ability he possesses now. Their arrival must follow the author’s rulings and the story’s causes, not a defeat forced to preserve canon.
- **Ape road and Super Saiyan 4:** a later, earned route through the ape lineage; neither the golden ape nor SS4 has appeared in this branch.
- **Divine crossing:** the design reserves a late, one-way promotion to a divine baseline, followed by the authored God 1–3 and God 4 paths. Divinity must be genuinely reached through a valid path; the Talent does not conjure it from nothing. No god ki exists at the current checkpoint.
- **Excluded for Goshin:** Super Saiyan Blue. The future God 4 design is his own path, not an automatic copy of someone else’s Ultra state. The crossing must preserve his identity, emotions and relationships.

The detailed authored future design is in [`../STATUS.md`](../STATUS.md), especially §§11–16. Exact scenes and timing remain unwritten; do not treat a plan as a current feat.
