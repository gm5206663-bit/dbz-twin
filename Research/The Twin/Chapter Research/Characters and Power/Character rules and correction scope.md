## Goshin's extraordinary Talent

- Native to Goshin since conception; exceptional, supernatural, broad and responsive to him and his environment. It is **not just copying**, ordinary learning, or limited to what he watches. It can act on his body, recovery, resilience, instincts, senses, ki, fighting, techniques, learning, inherited traits and responses to other environments or worlds.
- His Saiyan heritage and the Talent interact. His Saiyan bloodline becomes **purer and deeper** as the user directed; its traits and Great Ape potential can develop along with the rest of him. Do not invent ancestry percentages, erase his human inheritance, assert an unchosen genetic mechanism, or make this a universal rule for Saiyans.
- Adaptation can contribute meaningfully and substantially to development, healing, control and strength, not merely retention. What it genuinely makes native is retained. Skill may develop or be mastered exceptionally quickly; do not invent mandatory slow remedial stages. Deliberate training prioritizes mastery of existing abilities and foundations; instinctive emergency use or copying remains possible.
- Mortal at four, with ordinary ki, means **not yet divine**, not an ordinary human body or ordinary human rate of growth. He can be injured and encounter real challenges, but his response must reflect Saiyan constitution and exceptional Talent.
- The stable 100 birth reading and later 400, 1,450 and 1,500 readings are authored snapshots, not static limits. Goshin is never less capable than Gohan under the user's story direction. Gohan retains agency, his own growth, independent choices and potential.

## Recovery and Zenkai

Goshin recovers unusually rapidly without a Senzu, with visible changes in condition and ability. Some immediate pain after a fresh blow remains possible; it must not become the unquestioned human-like default. His Talent can adapt during combat and recovery even when there is no demonstrated Zenkai.

A Saiyan/hybrid may become stronger following recovery from serious, classically near-death injury; this power increase is distinct from the physical healing itself. The protected Raditz blow knocked Goshin unconscious, but the prose does **not** establish that he was near death. Do not announce a specific completed Zenkai from that blow, deny that further adaptation occurs, add an invented multiplier, or confuse Gohan's Senzu with Goshin eating one. Goku and Gohan each consumed one whole Senzu; Goshin declined the unwatched bean, and Krillin kept the last one.

## Great Ape and later applications

When Goshin first becomes a Great Ape, he remains himself, aware and able to direct his actions. His bloodline and Talent both matter. He does **not** require an uncontrollable rampage, amnesia, or a fear-of-the-ape arc before he can use the form. Experience can refine a form he already controls; he may adapt remarkably quickly.

As his bloodline and Talent develop, his Great Ape and its traits can develop too. Later, Great Ape power can be used in his normal body and combined with other powers, including anger and Super Saiyan once the latter is available in the established timeline. This is Goshin's own development, inspired in part by the Broly analogy, not an identical copy or a mandatory loss of control. Other possibilities remain open; no new numerical multiplier, compulsory weakness or form name is declared here.

Control once transformed is separate from the trigger. No transformation has occurred in the earlier Chapter 6–10 candidate branch. Full-moon/tail conditions are the ordinary starting context; do not invent a shown moon-free activation or decree it forever impossible for this Talent. Both twins retain their tails and the moon remains intact. The family may take precautions before witnessing either boy transform. Gohan's experience is his own, not silently determined by Goshin's Talent.

The old 'fear at the moon' wording in protected `WRITING.md`, `STATUS.md` and `OPEN.md` must not be imposed as a required loss-of-control or fear-of-his-own-ape arc. Do not rewrite those protected files without explicit authorization. No early golden Super Saiyan, god ki, divine promotion or later SS4 form is added to the present chapters. A normal Great Ape is not itself prohibited by that era rule, but no Great Ape scene is forced into this correction.
