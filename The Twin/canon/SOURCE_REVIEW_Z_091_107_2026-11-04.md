# Continued canon source review · Z episodes 91–107

**Owns: the retained Japanese-caption evidence in this block, its source-specific conflicts and consequences for the selected branch.** Review date: 4 November 2026. This is research, not a chapter, reader acceptance, a remote publication or a complete-franchise check.

## Scope and evidence

Every retained cue in the local SRT members for **91–107** was individually read, including recaps, music labels, previews and trailing fragments. The 91–100 and 101–107 reads were made in successive continuations, with untruncated per-episode outputs. Times below are local SRT times; paraphrases are not a certified translation or verified Japanese audio. Some members append a different program after the episode; those cues were read to establish the boundary but are **not Z story evidence**. Preview dialogue is identified as such and is not treated as an event completed in the current episode. Unlabelled speech and silent staging require corroboration.

Combined with the preceding source reviews and earlier recorded full reads, coverage is **1–107, 183–184, 205–208 and 256–257: 115 distinct Z episodes**. The 4 November continuation independently read every retained cue in **37–107**; the 1–36 and separate later blocks remain earlier research records, not all independently repeated in this continuation. All 291 files were indexed/searched, not all semantically read. The archive's original source and release/cut remain unverified. No complete episode video or audio has been examined.

Short Toei synopses (`W-110`) corroborate selected TV beats; Dragon Ball Official's separate manga-based/official features (`W-111`) and the community rewatch (`W-112`) retain their own source boundaries. None is a complete manga comparison or a substitute for footage.

## Episode sequence and branch implications

- **91:** In the captioned original-Z TV sequence, Ginyu in Bulma's body attempts Change against Piccolo; Bulma's body is restored and Ginyu is again a frog (04:41–08:00). The physical interception is not fully described by the speech alone; Toei specifically credits Gohan with throwing the frog and foiling the attempted swap (`C252`, `C262`, `W-110`). Goku risks a **20-fold Kaiō-ken Kamehameha** against Freeza (15:25–15:39, 22:36–24:37). This is a later source confrontation, not a multiplier established in the selected early branch (`C263`).
- **92:** Freeza privately admits the twentyfold attack was dangerous; Toei's synopsis specifies that his hand was injured. King Kai judges the technique beyond Goku's limit (09:47–09:51). Marked subjective speech attributed to Vegeta includes “your father Bardock” (11:24–12:53); a community rewatch calls this a vision (`W-112`). The captions alone do not establish the exact visual presentation, whether it is memory/vision/communication, or how Goku came by the name; they do not justify saying he never hears it. Goku starts a Genki-dama and asks nearby celestial bodies for energy (21:09–22:46; `C043`, `C264`).
- **93:** Goku continues gathering; Krillin estimates the sphere's diameter and interprets its growth, rather than providing a measured universal requirement (09:12–09:58). Piccolo asks Gohan and Krillin for their remaining ki, tells them to retain some, and intervenes when Freeza discovers the attack (19:45–24:07). Their support is explicitly given **to Piccolo** in this sequence, not explicitly placed directly into Goku's bomb. The four dead Ginyu Force members arrive on King Kai's planet; their exchange with the humans is a separately bounded TV sequence (`C264`, `C265`).
- **94:** Piccolo buys time at personal risk while Goku says the Genki-dama still needs more gathering (04:54–06:38, 16:02–19:22). Goku releases it; King Kai and the others initially think Freeza has been defeated (19:22–23:26). The humans begin to turn their own afterlife fight around, describing improvement through King Kai's training (09:12–11:34). Do not turn this contingent team effort into guaranteed branch success, a fixed charging time or an unconditional hierarchy over the branch's chamber-trained children.
- **95:** Goku, Piccolo, Gohan and Krillin reunite after the blast; Freeza returns despite the earlier assumption and grievously injures Piccolo. Gohan wants to fight for Piccolo; Freeza kills Krillin, and Goku transforms. Goku says Piccolo is still barely alive and orders Gohan to take him away while some of his own reason remains (17:52–24:23; `C137`, `C266`). The official Krillin feature separately associates Krillin's death with Goku's first Super Saiyan transformation (`W-111`). On King Kai's planet the three humans defeat the four dead Ginyu Force members; King Kai says he requested them as training opponents (09:01–13:18; `C265`). That specific TV result neither supplies a reliable numeric ranking nor overrides the author's post-chamber comparison.
- **96:** Goku urges Gohan to take the living Piccolo to safety, citing Piccolo and Kami's linked lives; Gohan worries about his father's escape, thanks him and leaves (06:11–07:15). Goku remains angry at Freeza's killings but continues to recognize and protect others. A threatened loss of reason at first transformation is a character statement at that moment, not a universal requirement that every transformation or Goshin's first Great Ape entail fear or loss of control (`C137`, `C266`).
- **97:** Freeza damages Namek and declares approximately **five minutes** to destruction (21:58–23:16). His stated clock is part of this stretched TV sequence, not a reliable real-time stopwatch or a branch deadline. Chi-Chi, via the ship's monitor, urgently calls for Gohan and learns of Krillin's death (11:38–12:32). Kami's continued life is evidence within the scene that Piccolo has not died (19:12–19:39), not an independent medical assessment for all future injuries.
- **98:** Goku deliberately waits for Freeza's full power despite King Kai's objection; he wants to defeat him and avenge Krillin and believes Gohan can escape (10:29–13:12, 18:59–19:32). His motives include both attachment and a desire for the fight, not a single simplistic explanation. King Kai's description of the “warrior of rage” is his interpretation, not proof Goku has ceased making choices (`C268`).
- **99:** Kami states that the contemporary Earth dragon can revive a group sharing a condition, with the discussed group wish restricted to those dead within one year; natural deaths do not qualify. He tentatively suggests that a lifespan indirectly shortened by an evil act might be restored for the lost period, explicitly without certainty (08:17–09:44). King Kai proposes reviving Freeza's victims to bring back the Elder and recover the remaining Namek wish (09:56–10:04, 16:17–17:19; `C267`). Gohan insists on taking Piccolo and asks Bulma to wait briefly for Goku (19:08–19:56, 21:42–22:16). Separate the Earth and Namek sets, and the proposal from its later demonstrated result.
- **100:** Believing Goku dead, Gohan asks Bulma to take Piccolo home and returns to delay Freeza rather than lead him to Earth (08:14–10:13, 17:48–17:54, 22:29–23:14). Goku reappears and orders him away (24:47–25:06); Gohan's belief was not proof of his father's death (`C266`). Earth's Shenron announces the revival of victims of Freeza's group on Namek; the Elder and Dende return and Porunga reappears (11:47–12:28, 18:05–20:30; `C267`). Bulma's approximately three-minute estimate belongs to the same extended dramatic countdown, not a verified stopwatch (`C271`).
- **101:** Goku interrupts King Kai's proposed rescue and insists that **both Freeza and Goku** be excluded from the final transport wish so he can settle their fight; King Kai reluctantly agrees (06:22–10:02). Dende speaks the wish in Namekian despite Freeza's attempted immortality request in another language. Porunga confirms transport of everyone there except the two; Goku later states that result (16:47–20:22). The Elder says he will soon die naturally again (23:01–23:09). These source choices need not be reproduced in the divergent branch (`C253`, `C268`).
- **102:** On Earth, the Elder says that Earth's dragon cannot revive the same person a second time; Piccolo and Gohan worry for Goku. Vegeta claims that his death and revival should have increased his own power, which is a character claim, not a measured recovery formula (06:16–07:29, 10:42–12:12). He invites Gohan to join him; Gohan rejects him and identifies himself as Goku's Earthling son (12:55–13:11). The source again places the countdown below two minutes and later one minute while the fight continues (`C271`).
- **103:** Goku says Freeza's full-power ki is diminishing, declares himself satisfied and offers to leave rather than kill the frightened opponent; Freeza continues attacking (19:45–21:40). The scene adds agency and mercy to the transformation's angry beginning. It does not mean Goku has no anger, no competitive motive, or unlimited control in all contexts (`C268`).
- **104:** A caption-marked retrospective exchange has Nappa tell Vegeta and Raditz that Freeza caused Planet Vegeta's destruction; Raditz reacts and Vegeta says he already knew (15:42–17:20). This sits in tension with episode 49, where Vegeta responds to Dodoria's disclosure and says he had not known while being used since childhood (49, 21:46–24:32). A rewatch independently notes the problem (`W-112`), but the caption text and secondary analysis do not establish whether a visual framing or later version resolves it. Do **not** silently reconcile it or import that knowledge into the early family (`C043`, `C269`). Freeza is cut by his own attack, pleads for help, and Goku gives him some ki (20:30–24:17; `C268`).
- **105:** The revived Elder says he has reached his natural end, appoints Mūri before dying, and says the Namek balls will recover (06:01–09:23). Vegeta identifies a missing village as one he himself killed and claims it fell outside the wording about Freeza's group (07:01–07:25); preserve the source's wish wording and his attribution rather than treating any mass revival as unlimited (`C267`). Dende says the Namek dragon can revive the same person again, probably provided death was not natural (11:12–11:41). Goku tells Freeza to escape; after Freeza attacks again Goku retaliates (04:47–05:10, 23:11–24:22; `C268`).
- **106:** King Kai says Freeza has died and concludes Goku also failed to escape Namek; Yamcha carries that second assessment to the people on Earth (05:01–05:23, 14:22–17:36). These are characters' conclusions, not independently demonstrated final fates: episode 107 reveals Goku is alive. King Kai worries that reviving the apparently dead fighters where Namek was would leave them in space; the next episode tests an alternative (`C270`). Gohan refuses Vegeta's invitation, insists his father will return and resists him until Piccolo intervenes (20:52–24:22); preserve his agency without requiring this encounter in the branch (`C266`).
- **107:** Vegeta proposes moving the supposed dead fighters' souls to Earth first; after **130 days**, Porunga brings Krillin's soul, reports Goku alive, revives Krillin with his body restored, and says Goku refuses the attempted transport. Yamcha is then revived as the third successful wish (04:53–05:36, 10:13–10:38, 15:22–20:46). After another 130 days, Chiaotzu and Tien return individually and the Namekians relocate (21:53–24:14; `C270`). These are observed results and statements of this set at this time, not proof that every dragon honors every refusal or that all future resurrection uses identical slots. Gohan briefly worries about unfinished homework, and Chi-Chi's rescue party learns he is already back before launching; she reunites with him (07:02–09:04; `C250`). Education and protective concern coexist.

## Branch handoff and unresolved work

- `C137`, `C043`, `C250`, `C252`, `C253`, `C262` and `C263` retain their preceding values and now reflect the later evidence. New `C264`–`C271` record the team Genki-dama, contingent afterlife fight, Gohan's decisions, bounded wish plan, Goku's choices and mercy, the Z-49/Z-104 knowledge conflict, the later Namek wish sequence and the dramatic countdown. The technique ledger separates source uses from selected-branch abilities.
- Both boys have lived the full chamber year and comfortably outclass this Piccolo in ordinary spars by **author direction**, not by a sourced numeric multiplier. Gohan's judgment remains his own; Goshin's Talent is cumulative whole-being growth. His first eventual Great Ape is aware and controlled; the source's Super Saiyan scene does not reopen mandatory fear or loss of control.
- The accepted Chapter 20 original remains preserved; the selected correction and Chapters 19, 21–23 remain unaccepted. **No chapter prose was changed** by this source continuation.
