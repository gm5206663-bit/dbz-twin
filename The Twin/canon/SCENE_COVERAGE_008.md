## Additional context and authorized inventions

- `W-62` is an official **manga-based** account of Baba's earlier one-day return of Grandpa Gohan and his happy reunion with Goku. It does not establish that she knows the answer to Goku's current question or can guarantee a new visit. A request to carry a message is authored.
- `W-63` is a **later** episode-37 secondary report of Mr. Popo's flying carpet. Earlier use to get a message to Korin, if written, is an authored placement, not an episode-8 visual fact.
- `W-64` is a secondary report that Yamcha plays professional baseball in episode 10. Any earlier public clue in this branch is authored; no schedule or recruitment has happened merely because the future episode has one.
- Medical care by the village doctor, the present Senzu stock, named messengers' changed instructions, the family safety plan and any safe-lab procedure are **authored branch details**. A signal precaution is not proof an alien device cannot transmit.

## Historical branch carry-forward before drafting

Goku and Gohan are completely healed and fully restored by their Senzu. Goshin declined his bean; Roshi keeps it. The doctor has ordered rest, monitoring and follow-up for Goshin; nothing permits strenuous training yet. Goshin's deliberate training prioritizes mastery of what he already knows and serious foundations, with instinctive emergency copying permitted. His food rule is unchanged. The family knows Piccolo tried to seize the boys. They received a moon/tail warning and established precautions, but Goku is uncertain about Grandpa Gohan's death. No one has destroyed the moon in this branch. The Three- and Six-Star Balls remain at home, Gohan has the Four-Star Ball, Bulma has the Dragon Radar, and the Ox-King's truck is at the ninth road marker. Gohan's independent flight is not established; both boys have ridden Nimbus together. The Otherworld guide sent an inquiry through Baba, not an authorization or a stated road length. Chapter Nine is provisional; the next-chapter request is not acceptance of Chapters Four–Eight.

## Historical Chapter Nine treatment — superseded by the selected review manuscript

- **The year, the summons and a different telling.** §2 shows Popo observe Goshin ask Roshi about watching a Senzu grow; Kami then sends Popo to Korin, who sends Yajirobe. §§3–4 deliver changed messages. Krillin, Yamcha, Tien, Chiaotzu and Yajirobe are invited to gather at Korin Tower for Kami's training. Goku is asked to speak with Kami, and his family is invited when Goshin can travel. The aircar is a secondary report in `W-61`, and `W-60` lists a hovercar among episode objects; this specific journey, Popo's earlier carpet trip and the changed words are authored. The family already knows the truth, so Chi-Chi is not falsely told her living husband is dead. In the selected manuscript Baba conveys the guide's conditional inquiry to Roshi at Kame House while Goku and his family are elsewhere; Yemma has not granted it. Kami separately tells the family about the inquiry later. The manuscript does not show Baba giving Goku the message or discussing the road's length with him (`O114`, `O115`).
- **Grandpa and Goku's question — later correction.** An earlier version of this coverage claimed that selected Chapter Nine showed Goku ask Baba to relay his question and the children's names, and that Baba agreed. The selected manuscript and its protected pre-correction copy do not show that scene; the origin of the conflicting note is unknown. `O116` is therefore a historical note, not an on-page event. Chapter Eight does show Goku ask Roshi whether he might have hurt Grandpa, and Roshi says he does not know. `W-62` is a manga-based account of an earlier reunion, not an answer to that question.
- **Scouter.** §6 powers an externally supplied bench test in a mesh enclosure after Bulma disconnects suspected transmitter and microphone circuits. The old cell stays out. Bulma says she has learned what she can with the device off and explains why she is now undertaking a risk-disclosed powered test. She expressly states the enclosure may not contain an unknown alien transmission. Krillin consents to the limited test; the display shows **206** at rest and moves upward when he raises energy. This authored test echoes the episode's captioned 206, but the other source readings are deferred, no accuracy is established, and the scouter is switched off rather than certified safe for field use (`C124`, `O117`). The reported gun-to-wake-the-sleepers gag is not reproduced in the changed laboratory scene.
- **Return, care and food.** §§1 and 5–7 take the family home: Yajirobe carries Ox-King in the aircar to the ninth marker; both boys ride Nimbus beside Goku, who carries Chi-Chi; the family takes the truck home. A doctor authorizes only gentle travel with monitoring. She examines Goshin again at home, leaves warning signs in force and still forbids strenuous exercise, sparring and flight practice. Goku and Gohan remain completely healed and fully restored by their Senzu. Goshin has no bean, watches breakfast and lunch prepared, makes only a few doctor-permitted gentle foot placements and stops. Roshi retains the third bean (`O118`, `O120`).
- **Balls, studies and Gohan's agency.** §8 checks the Three- and Six-Star Balls at home; Gohan retains the Four-Star on his hat. The earlier promised schoolbag is brought out; the animal book and puzzle are additional gifts, not a replacement for it. No ball leaves the home, no wish is made, and any dispersal is a plan to discuss with those who would receive it (`O119`). §9 transfers the baseline's fearful-child/problem-solving motif into a different event: Gohan decides how to free a small bird, then voluntarily asks for and practises a short escape response. His studies remain part of the plan. Neither boy is made Piccolo's captive or given the original episode's sword and clothes (`O121`).
- **Moon, Piccolo and the deferred wilderness sentence.** §10 shows Piccolo training on his own and the family closing curtains at home. The moon remains; no present phase, Great Ape event, tail removal or certain explanation of Grandpa's death is created. Piccolo does not know the moon connection merely because an episode-8 flashback supplies it despite the source-order problem. Piccolo's six-month solitary sentence is not imposed on the children he failed to take. The age-four prohibition concerns early golden forms and god ki, not a categorical ban on Saiyan tail biology. Goshin's deliberate future work is mastery-first and foundation-oriented, while emergency copying remains possible; no technique is declared perfected (`O120`, `O121`).
- **Remaining leads.** Bulma knows of Yamcha's baseball from a public report; Krillin leaves her lab planning to look at the city stadium, but has not yet found or recruited Yamcha, Tien or Chiaotzu (`W-64`, `O117`). The episode-9 robot preview is not an episode-8 occurrence. Chapter Nine's own preview promises only a book and a road; a new episode-9 inventory remains necessary. Chapters Four–Nine are provisional, not accepted by implication.
