# Goku's growth — canon research and Chapter 13 revision plan

**Prepared before editing the copied story, 2 November 2026.** The user wants Goshin to understand during the spar that Goku has built and expanded his abilities through his own work and refusal to stop at apparent limits. The user also distinguishes Goshin's unusual innate potential from Goku's. This is a targeted, separate review candidate, not acceptance or replacement of the previously delivered Chapter 13 or the protected first five chapters.

## What the sources support

1. **Training is central, and Goku himself keeps choosing it.** The official [young Goku profile](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_23.html) says he and Krillin undergo harsh Kame School training and Goku continues training after the tournament. The [official story retrospective](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_3850.html) describes Roshi's training, his subsequent training with Korin and Kami, and the importance of food, rest and study alongside physical effort. The [official Kami profile](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_1657.html) identifies Goku's three years of training under Kami before the 23rd tournament.
2. **A particularly good, already-past example is Korin's water.** The [official Korin profile](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_1638.html) says young Goku climbed the tower and spent several days pursuing water from Korin; the water was ordinary, while the climbing and pursuit actually made him stronger. It describes Goku's persistence and resourcefulness. This first, ordinary **Super Holy Water** must not be confused with the separate, later potential-releasing **Super Water of the Gods** described by the [official Goku profile](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_478.html). Goku has already experienced both before this Saiyan-era branch.
3. **Goku is not merely talentless.** The official [technique compendium](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_140.html) says young Goku learned a Kamehameha after watching Roshi once, developed ideas during combat and learned moves from Grandpa Gohan. The [official young Goku profile](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_23.html) also describes his childhood superhuman strength. A [translated Toriyama Q&A](https://www.kanzenshuu.com/translations/saikyo-jump-january-2018-we-asked-akira-toriyama-sensei-saiyan-special-qa/) says most Saiyans have an innate aptitude for battle. Thus 'Goku has no gifts' would be a false canon assertion, just as 'his work is irrelevant' would be.
4. **Rank at birth is not a proven lifetime ceiling.** In the later Saiyan battle, Goku argues that a low-born fighter can outdo an elite through hard work; the [official manga retrospective](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_3864.html) quotes the line. The battle and that actual speech are later than the present branch, so Goshin cannot hear that speech here. It supports the larger theme, not a preordained result in this story.
5. **Skill, control, inventiveness, increased strength, recovery, innate capacity and teachers are distinct contributors.** The [official Goku technique compendium](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_140.html) gives examples of improvisation and applying techniques, while a [translated Toriyama interview](https://www.kanzenshuu.com/translations/chozenshu-1-we-asked-akira-toriyama-2013/) discusses increasing and controlling ki. These do not establish a numeric equation by which training raises an inherited 'talent stat', an infinite automatic growth rate, a permanent upper bound or a guaranteed winner. His Saiyan biology, help from teachers and other events matter too; training is neither trivial nor the sole source of every gain. Goku's ability to grow beyond his previous abilities must remain open and dramatically consequential.
6. **Why he does it matters.** In a [translated 1997 interview](https://www.kanzenshuu.com/translations/wired-japan-1997-akira-toriyama-interview/), Toriyama emphasizes Goku's desire to face strong opponents. This is contextual characterization, not a rule that he cannot care for his sons. Keep him eager and playful, not lecturing solemnly or delaying training until a family plan is settled.
7. **Later continuity is evidence, not a present event.** The [official Super recap](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_800.html) attributes later Ultra Instinct improvement to training with Whis. This illustrates continuing development but does **not** mean Whis, Ultra Instinct, divine ki or that later sequence has happened in this Saiyan-era chapter. Similarly, the pending King Kai inquiry cannot be written as already granted.

## Story interpretation and intended edits

- Goshin's exceptional, broad, innate Talent and greater inborn potential are the user's story premise, **not canon information** about an existing DB character. Canon cannot measure Goku against this authored son. Honor the premise without demoting Goshin to ordinary copying, making him less capable than Gohan, declaring Goku has no gifts, or fixing either character's eventual ceiling or ranking.
- Goku's own training, choices, battle sense, effort and desire to improve help him build on and expand his abilities. Show that this can continue: he reacts inventively to Goshin's attack and practises an adjustment after losing. Do not make him a passive prop whose only purpose is to praise Goshin, or suggest this one ring-out proves a permanent difference in their strength.
- After the match, have the four-year-old ask a concrete question and connect Goku's chosen effort to his growing strength. Goku can give an accessible account of the already-past Roshi and Korin training, with a grounded, in-character desire to keep training. Goshin then articulates the understanding and chooses to train too. Do not put knowledge of Goku's memories into the boy's head before Goku tells him; do not force a long abstract 'potential' lecture.
- Preserve the bout's actual outcome, the independent Gohan turn and agency, Chi-Chi's decisions, the earthquake, the waterfall storyline, C-6's consent, the source-order handoff and all established objects. The new scene does not rewrite Chapters 1–5 or any prior candidate.

A later source's retrospective or opinion is not proof that an event has already happened in this branch. No complete episode video was watched, no full manga reread was performed, and the inherited Chapter 13 episode-12 source limitations remain.
