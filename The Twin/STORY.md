## 1 · The laws

1. **Canon is the floor, not a track.** There is no parallel line to keep in step. He lives inside canon's world and changes it by being in it.
2. **Anime governs; manga corroborates.** Where they disagree, a ruling decides. **Never averaged** — a blend exists in neither source.
3. **Continuity:** DB + Z + Super + all films. Films need a verdict each (`OPEN-6`); the opening arcs touch none.
4. **He is native.** No foreknowledge, no meta-knowledge, ever. *(Declared exception to house rule F0.)*
5. **Presence has consequences.** Let Goshin affect events and relationships naturally without demanding that every scene revolve around him or discarding an otherwise useful quiet moment.
6. **Respect the actual scope.** Acceptance describes a particular version; it is not a permanent ban on later corrections. Apply the author's current request where it is relevant, without extending a restriction on one change to every chapter forever or requiring separate permission again for a correction already requested. Retain the prior version and identify new wording for review; a request to revise is not itself acceptance of the revised text. Respect any specific live hold the author actually gives.
7. **Goshin is never less capable than Gohan** (O056). Gohan also grows and retains agency. There is one divergent story, not a parallel track; do not force canon beats or a ceiling against Nappa.
8. **The gate.** He cannot take Super Saiyan God **as a form.** The ritual would adapt into a divine Talent and promote his baseline, with no way back down (`STATUS §12`, O031, O032). **Late, once, one direction — and the base afterwards is what he is.**
9. **The ladder is a floor, not a staircase.** Super Saiyan 1–3 are perfected, so they are never announced; **Rage** is his, **Super Saiyan 4** is earned at the ape, and **Blue is impossible** — and because the Talent **never lets a thing be lost** (`O041`), **every golden rung he reaches for comes back divine** — God 1, 2, 3, each louder than the last — crowned by **GOD 4**, the god worn inside the ape's body (`STATUS §13`).
10. **The promotion is total, and it subtracts nothing.** The Talent becomes divine and works on everything (`STATUS §14`): his forms, his techniques and **his children** — born divine, not mortal. **And he loses nothing** — temper, appetite, jokes, grief, mistakes. A secondary source describes a cool-headed form but does not prove emotion is erased in canon (`C119`); Goshin can be calm or emotional for contextual reasons without losing himself. (`O042`–`O044`)
11. **The era lock.** ~~At four, and through the Z-era arcs, he is mortal and ordinary, and the entire ladder is a locked ceiling.~~ **Clarified, 3 November:** he is mortal, not divine; he is already an exceptionally adaptive, growing Saiyan–Earthling child with ordinary ki, flight and techniques (`O045`). No early golden transformation or god ki. Natural Great Ape biology is not prohibited, though no ape event has happened; his first eventual transformation is conscious and controlled.
12. **Canon where canon speaks; ours where it does not — and ours says so.** Every rung canon printed is played by canon's rules. Every rung it did not print is declared in `STATUS`, with the mark that it is ours.
13. **The current abduction correction.** The user rules that Raditz cannot take Goshin conscious in this encounter. His ~400 is a snapshot and his output can rise in anger; he must fight and be knocked unconscious before Raditz carries him (`O080`). This is not a general equation between battle powers or an assertion of invulnerability.
14. **Master what he has first.** In deliberate training Goshin works toward mastering or perfecting the abilities he already has before pursuing new ones. The user clarified that instinctive copying in an emergency is still allowed; the priority concerns training choices, not a ban on protecting someone (`O104`).
15. **The foundation is serious work.** Goshin gives sustained attention to basics such as stance, balance, movement, guard, conditioning, breathing and ki control, as relevant to his skills and age. Show the practice and progress; do not replace his existing strength or rapid adaptation with a forced remedial stage (`O105`).
16. **The current post-chamber comparison.** After their accumulated training and full additional lived year, both boys are far beyond this era's Piccolo in an ordinary spar; he cannot make them winded by exchanging blows. Their restraint, Gohan's independent progress and Goshin's accumulated growth must appear in action. His Talent can also yield rapid, substantial in-fight development, but does not demand a new permanent gain in every exchange (`O170`, `O181`, `O188`). This is not a fixed numerical maximum, future universal ordering or forced result against Nappa or Vegeta.

## 5 · The cast — voice law and never-list

**GOKU** — short flat sentences; questions are sincere; no sarcasm, no explaining a feeling. Affection is *doing the task*. **Never:** boasts, withholds a discovery, uses a word he has no reason to know. *Around him power is ordinary — that is why a born-100 child stays likeable.*

**CHI-CHI** — fast and specific (times, costs, the rice, not "soon"); love arrives as a plan, fear as a rule; **she finishes her sentences.** **Never:** apologises for wanting them educated, is vague about money or time, or is written as a shouting joke. *Her plans and concern are serious, not a joke.*

**GOHAN** — polite and sensitive, but able to act decisively for people he loves; he trains, learns and grows. Do not reduce him to crying, a rage switch or his twin’s sidekick. His own choices can change with the divergence (`C151`).

**RADITZ** — contempt, entitlement and explicit threats. He explains the Saiyan mission and his ultimatum in ep. 2. He does not know about the boys before finding them; do not replace his canon dialogue with a permanently polite stranger.

**PICCOLO** — severe, direct and initially hostile, but capable of explaining a plan and later praising Gohan’s progress. Let his teaching and attachment develop through what happens; do not force him to stay unchanged throughout the arc.

**ROSHI** — an old man who has decided to be funny so he does not have to be frightened; asks the dangerous question sideways. **Never:** lectures, or states the plot.

**BULMA** — the only one who talks about herself; sharp, practical, emotional at the wrong moment. **Never:** a prize or a voice of exposition.

**KRILLIN** — brave first, sensible second. **Never:** comic relief in a scene where somebody is about to be hurt.

**GOSHIN** — visually, almost his father in miniature, with Goku's face and black spiked hair, Chi-Chi's eyes and his own tail (`O003`). He is an emotional, playful child whose words and choices grow from his relationships, knowledge and experience. He can ask, joke, disagree, describe a feeling, take initiative and make mistakes. The automatic word-for-word echo was assistant-invented and is withdrawn; no fixed lag, mandatory silence or sole emotional style replaces him. His exceptional whole-being growth and the responsibility of controlling its effects remain real (`STATUS §1–9`).

## 7 · The Talent

Present from conception, non-sentient and native to Goshin, the supernatural Talent supports **cumulative development of his whole being**, not merely the copying of techniques (`O004`, `O041`). His Saiyan/human body and ki, deepening Saiyan bloodline, recovery, senses, instincts, fighting and abilities can develop through life, training, combat, experience and recovery. Growth can be exceptionally rapid and substantial in battle, as the author directed; the gained foundation carries into later scenes. The Talent itself grows with its holder. Releasing existing power, concentrating a technique and actual development are different; a scouter cannot identify the cause of a reading on its own. Anger and being pushed hard are possible circumstances, not mandatory triggers, and no fixed rate or numerical ceiling is imposed (`O188`).

He also learns quickly from demonstrated movements and retains acquired skills. His first Kamehameha had more raw ki than Goku's wave in that scene but less practiced control (`O088`). In deliberate training he prioritizes mastery of what he already has and serious fundamentals (`O104`, `O105`); instinctive copying in an emergency remains allowed. Mastery is achievable (`R-26`). Fatigue, injury, suppression and distraction can affect performance without erasing a genuinely integrated gain. The physical chamber year is real training and lived time, not Chapter 18's mental exercise (`O155`). Its proposed results are written in unaccepted Chapter 22 and carry forward into unaccepted Chapter 23, where the landing follows later the same morning. The separately ruled late divine gate remains future (`STATUS §§12–14`).
