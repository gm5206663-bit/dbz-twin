# Goku and continuity — independent targeted review

**Owns: the scope, concrete corrections and unresolved risks of the 6 November Goku/continuity pass.**

## The author's correction

Goku must be recognizable in the story as someone eager to face strong or newly strengthened opponents and test himself. Show that through his initiatives and reactions, not merely in a personality note or a string of promises. His excitement does not erase his love for his family, his fighting judgment or the consequences of endangering other people (`O236`).

The prior selected story was not entirely missing this quality: Chapter 9 makes him eager to meet and train under King Kai, Chapter 11 repeats that desire, and Chapter 13 stages a lively match with Goshin and starts the promised Gohan match. Nevertheless, after his return in Chapters 28–31, his curiosity about Vegeta was mostly internal and the later match exchange relied on Gohan to initiate it. Goshin had said in Chapter 23 that he wanted to see what his father learned, but the family had not actually seen it. Those are scene-level shortcomings, not proof Goku was passive throughout the whole manuscript.

## Scope actually examined

- Inventoried all 31 selected chapter openings and visible episode references; all 31 have one. Inspected chapter headings and opening/ending handoffs across the manuscript, and searched the bodies for Goku's battle interest, moon and tail state, Senzu and scouter handling, power readings and relevant injuries.
- Examined the pertinent Goku scenes in Chapters 2, 4–5, 7–9, 11–15 and 22–23; the Snake Way episodes across Chapters 23–28; the scouter's Chapter 25–27 path; and the relevant reunion, guard, family, medical and ship scenes in Chapters 28–31. The existing voice, growth and scale research was consulted rather than treated as a substitute for the prose.
- This was **not** a fresh cover-to-cover, word-by-word reread of the roughly 180,000-word manuscript, nor a new complete episode audiovisual/source review. The earlier 1–29 full-body review is a different historical pass. Keyword scans, correct episode labels and integrity checks cannot establish that no further mistakes exist. The current audit is a substantial targeted pass, with wider source/style/scale review still open.

## Corrections made in the actual chapters

1. **Chapter 24:** Goku's urgent run is written at a more extraordinary visual scale, without assigning an exact speed or inventing an afterlife restriction. This corrects presentation only; it does **not** solve the timeline issue below.
2. **Chapter 29:** Goku physically tries Gohan's moon-shot sighting position, replacing two redundant descriptions of him following the same line. He offers the injured Vegeta a future match; Vegeta refuses and threatens the boys, and Goku immediately protects his family. There is no fight, release, forgiveness or agreed custody.
3. **Chapter 30:** After Goshin's genuine planet-capable, controlled display, Goku initiates the overdue match with Gohan and wants a turn with Goshin. He stops when Chi-Chi reminds him of the doctor-first rule. On his own, staying beside the guards, he briefly demonstrates the Kaiō-ken he already learned from King Kai in Chapters 22–23. Gohan wants to see it in their eventual match, and Goshin asks to learn. No match takes place, no victory or exact ranking is assigned, and Goshin has not yet learned the technique. Vegeta can witness the display.
4. **Scouter continuity:** Chapter 30 incorrectly said Vegeta had *discarded* his scouter. Chapter 25 actually has him remove it from his ear and clip it to his armour; Chapter 26 still has it clipped there, and Piccolo removes it in Chapter 27. Chapter 30 now recalls the clipping (`O237`). The remembered 19,400 remains a situational reading, not a maximum; the later ordinary-body gain is not erased.
5. **Chapter 31:** Goku asks whether the unnamed people Vegeta hints at are strong, while making clear he will stop them if they attack. Chi-Chi's request to offer the guarded Saiyans food and water is followed through: Vegeta refuses Goku's offer, while Nappa accepts what Gohan brings. Their guards stay in place; the offer is neither a release nor an alliance. Gohan's compassion, the family letter and the actual Jupiter/moon precautions remain.
6. **Separate medical and parental boundaries:** In Chapter 31 the doctor advises avoiding hard blows while sore. Chi-Chi separately forbids the boys to fight until they are no longer sore **and** she says so. The latter condition was missing from the central fact handoff. It is now recorded explicitly in `O233` and `O237`; no later permission or spar is invented. Gohan's push-ups stop at discomfort, not exhaustion.

The accepted original Chapter 20 was not edited. The revised/new prose remains unaccepted.

## Unresolved: Snake Way versus the author's speed interpretation

Chapters 23–28 establish that Goku leaves King Kai urgently, expects the Saiyans may arrive before him, and keeps travelling until after the fight, city response and initial aftermath. King Kai speaks to him during that return. Chapter 28 says he travelled as fast as he could. The text does not establish an exact elapsed return time or a new speed limit; a single visual description of fast movement does not reconcile the whole sequence with the author's early light-speed-or-greater interpretation (`O225`).

This pass makes the run feel faster but **does not claim to resolve its chronology**. A sound repair may require reconsidering departure, intercutting and/or the battle and reunion consequences across several chapters. Do not silently invent an afterlife speed restriction, pretend an exact length or time was established on-page, force an unsupported speed cap, or preserve the existing fight at all costs through an arbitrary delay. That larger rewrite and the broader earlier-story source/tone review remain open.

## Other active boundaries

- Gohan's and Goshin's matches with Goku and a possible Goku–Vegeta fight remain future possibilities, not decided results. Goku's solo display is not a match and Chi-Chi's Chapter 31 restriction is not yet lifted.
- Both boys and Vegeta retain their tails and their moon knowledge. Goshin's first Great Ape was conscious and controlled from the start and produced substantial retained body-and-ki growth; Gohan chooses independently. Goshin can create an artificial moon and his capacity for unaided space adaptation remains possible but presently unshown. Do not assign a new scouter number, fixed growth rate, training duration or ranking.
- The Namek route is tentative, the roster and Chi-Chi's consent are unsettled, safe custody and medical arrangements remain incomplete, and the family has not yet eaten supper together. No Chapter 32 is created by this pass.
- The retained captions and existing official/secondary references are bounded evidence, not complete audiovisual verification. No new full-caption or video/audio review was performed here.
