## Branch and craft decisions

1. Start at the previous chapter's village-side road after the rain, same afternoon. The family catches two fish for later and returns home in daylight; no unplanned full-moon exposure or transformation. Gohan keeps the Four-Star Ball on his hat. At home the Three- and Six-Star Balls can be checked, but no scouter is powered and no wish is made.
2. Goku is eager and impulsive about training, warm with his sons, and not a bureaucratic family planner. Chi-Chi speaks and acts for herself. Gohan wants study and training on his own terms. Goshin is no less capable than Gohan, acts independently, retains broad Talent and shown skills, and trains existing foundations rather than acquiring an arbitrary new move.
3. Preserve Goshin's meaningful habits: he watches the fish being cleaned and cooked before eating; repeats applicable instructions before compliance; takes the downhill side on a sloped path; places his hands behind his back when Chi-Chi checks him; waits for Goku's conversational invitation. Do not make every exchange mechanical or force a danger to display a rule.
4. C-6 remains awake at Capsule Corporation with Dr. Brief and an unrepaired bent arm; the family can plan the promised book visit without inventing his consent to an opening or a completed repair. Krillin keeps the last whole Senzu. Piccolo remains independent and hostile; the last sighting was his departure to train.
5. Vegeta, Nappa, Atla, Lemuria and Moai want different things. Show the prisoner's hope and Lemuria's own actions; establish the layout and results in each fight. A planet's destruction is an event on Arlia, not knowledge magically delivered to Earth. Keep the chapter in external-camera present tense with a cold open, title, hard cuts, eye-catch, loop-back and preview.
6. After drafting, check episode beats against this plan and disclose alterations.
