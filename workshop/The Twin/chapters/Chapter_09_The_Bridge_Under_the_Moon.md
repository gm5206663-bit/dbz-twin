# Chapter Nine — The Bridge Under the Moon

Beside a yellow cloud hovering above the grass, Goku holds Goshin while Chi-Chi closes a first-aid box.

**THE BRIDGE UNDER THE MOON**

**NARRATOR:** Goku and his sons have escaped Piccolo, but two stronger Saiyans are on their way. Chi-Chi wants a doctor to check Goshin after Raditz's blow.

---

## The Road to the Village

"There's a doctor in the village," Chi-Chi says. "We'll go there first."

Goku nods. "Show me the way."

Gohan climbs onto Nimbus while Chi-Chi takes the first-aid box and climbs onto Goku's back. Goku keeps one arm beneath Goshin and the other around Chi-Chi.

Krillin looks at Bulma and Roshi. "I can take both of you back to the island."

"And I can go to my lab from there," Bulma says. She checks that her toolkit is closed. The capsule containing the damaged aircraft is inside; the wrapped scouter and its cell are in separate pockets.

Roshi holds the medical case against his chest. "The Dragon Radar's still in here."

"And I've got the last bean." Krillin pats the bag inside his shirt. "I'll keep it safe."

Goku lifts off beside Nimbus. Krillin follows with Bulma on one arm and Roshi on the other. The clearing drops away beneath them.

At the river bend, Chi-Chi points toward a road between the hills. "The village is beyond that ridge."

A horn sounds below as a truck comes around the bend, and Ox-King waves from the window.

"There they are!" Gohan calls.

Goku descends beside the road, and Ox-King brakes and climbs down before the dust has settled. He reaches for Gohan, then stops with his arms open.

"May I?"

Gohan runs into him, and Ox-King lifts him and holds him close.

"I was looking for you," he says.

"We were looking for Mother."

Ox-King sets him down, looks at Goshin's bandaged hand, then leans toward him.

"I've brought a blanket. You can have it in the truck."

"Thank you, Grandpa."

"He needs the doctor first," Chi-Chi says.

"Then I'll follow you."

He gets back into the truck as Goku rises with Chi-Chi and Goshin, and Gohan brings Nimbus alongside.

At the ridge, Krillin turns toward the sea. Bulma waves to the boys; Roshi lifts one hand from the case. The truck continues along the road below the cloud.

## The Doctor's Rooms

A bicycle leans beside the doctor's open door, and a bell rings when Chi-Chi brings Goshin inside.

The doctor washes her hands at the basin and listens while Goku describes Raditz's blow and Chi-Chi tells her that Goshin was unconscious for an unknown length of time.

"He woke up in the round ship," Gohan says. "I was there."

The doctor kneels in front of Goshin. "Do you remember waking up?"

"Yes. Gohan was beside me."

"How does your head feel now?"

"It hurt before. It doesn't now."

Chi-Chi looks him over, and his hands go behind his back.

"May I look?" the doctor asks him.

Goshin nods. She examines his head gently, then holds up a finger.

"Follow my finger."

"Follow my finger."

His eyes follow it from side to side. She asks about sickness and dizziness; he tells her he has had neither.

The doctor unwraps his hand. Fresh pink skin covers the burn. She cleans it and turns his palm toward the window.

"When did this happen?"

"Today," Goku says.

"It was bleeding at the crater," Gohan adds.

The doctor looks at the bandage Roshi tied and lays it on the edge of the basin. Gohan describes how the cloth came loose beside the river, while Goku tells her that Goshin has been able to move his fingers.

"Was anything put on the burn?" she asks.

"Clean cloth," Chi-Chi says. "I wrapped it again after I found them."

The doctor looks again. "I've never seen a burn close that quickly."

When she asks about his ankle, Goshin stands on that foot, then turns it from side to side.

"Piccolo grabbed it," he says. "It doesn't hurt now."

"His answers are clear, and he isn't dizzy," the doctor tells Chi-Chi. "I can't tell everything from one examination after a blow like that. If he becomes sick or confused, or the pain returns, bring him back. If you're still in the village tomorrow, I'd like another look."

Chi-Chi nods. "I'll stay with him."

The doctor turns to Goshin. "Tell your parents if you feel sick or your head hurts."

"Tell your parents if you feel sick or your head hurts."

"Can I read?"

"Yes. And your hand can stay uncovered."

Gohan points to a calendar hanging beside the door, with a white circle printed in today's square.

"Is that the moon?"

"Yes. It's full tonight."

Goku looks at Chi-Chi. She closes the first-aid box.

"We'll keep them inside after dark," she says.

Gohan pulls his hat down a little, and Goshin watches him until Goku looks at him.

"What is it?" Goku asks.

"Does the moon come up over the village?" Goshin asks the doctor.

"It does. The sun's nearly down now."

Ox-King ducks through the door with his folded blanket. "I found a place for the truck. How is he?"

"He's feeling better," Chi-Chi says. "For tonight, we need a room with curtains."

"There's room at home," Ox-King says.

"There is. We also need to get there before the boys can see the moon."

"Then we'd best be quick."

He sets the blanket on a chair while Chi-Chi pays the doctor. Gohan walks to the window, but he stops below the sill and looks at the street instead.

A squirrel snatches an apple from a fruit seller's cart and runs toward the covered bridge at the bottom of the road.

"Hey!" the seller calls.

The squirrel vanishes beneath a wagon entering the bridge from the far bank. Gohan presses his lips together, then looks at Goshin.

"It didn't pay."

Goshin smiles. "It didn't wash it either."

## A Message for Korin

At the Lookout, Kami rests his staff against the stone railing.

"I will speak to Goku," he tells Popo. "Please ask Korin to gather Krillin, Yamcha, Tien and Chiaotzu. Yajirobe should come too."

"At Korin Tower?"

"Yes. They will need to prepare for the Saiyans."

Popo unrolls his carpet, and Kami watches it descend before stepping away from the railing and turning toward the village below.

At Korin Tower, Yajirobe is eating from a bowl when the carpet appears. He lowers the bowl as Popo lands.

Korin listens to the names and looks at Yajirobe.

"You heard him."

"I heard my name. That doesn't mean I like it."

"Krillin's friends need the message," Korin says. "You can take it to Kame House."

"I was enjoying my lunch."

Popo looks at the empty bowl. "You've finished."

Yajirobe tips it toward himself. One grain of rice remains. He picks it out with a finger, eats it and puts the bowl down.

"These Saiyans are stronger than Raditz, aren't they?"

"That's what Krillin told us," Korin says.

"And you want me to go near them?"

"I want you to prepare."

Yajirobe takes his sword from beside the pillar. "That's not much better."

Popo holds the carpet steady while Yajirobe steps onto it.

"If you can take me there," Yajirobe says, "why am I carrying the message?"

"Korin asked you to."

The carpet rises. Korin waves them away with his staff.

## The Island

Krillin lands on the sand at Kame House, and Bulma steps down first; Roshi follows with the medical case. Turtle comes up from the water.

"Did you find them?"

"All of them," Roshi says. "Chi-Chi is with Goku and the boys. They're taking Goshin to a doctor."

Turtle lets out a long breath. "Good. I told her what I saw, but I didn't know where they'd gone."

"You did right," Roshi says.

Bulma takes the wrapped scouter from her pocket and sets it on the table inside, then opens Roshi's repair kit and takes out a screwdriver.

"That one doesn't fit," Roshi says.

"Not yet."

She holds the tip against a file, then tries it in the casing after several strokes. This time the little screw turns.

Krillin leans over her shoulder. "Can you find out how strong I am?"

"I haven't even opened it."

"When you do?"

"When I know it can't tell the other Saiyans where we are."

Roshi puts the case on a chair. "And if it says Krillin is stronger than me?"

"Then you'll say it's broken," Krillin says.

"I might."

Bulma removes the cover. Fine wires cross the inside, and a small metal loop sits against the rim. She draws their positions on a piece of paper without connecting the cell.

"Is that the transmitter?" Krillin asks.

"It might be. Or it might do something else."

"Couldn't you just take it out?"

"Look here." She points to two thin connections beneath the loop. "They're joined to the display as well. If I cut the wrong part, we might lose the only thing we know it can do."

Krillin leans closer. "Can you tell what the other part does?"

"Not with these tools. I need my lab before I take it apart any further."

She puts the cover beside the drawing, leaving the cell wrapped in her other pocket.

"Goku has two Dragon Balls at his house," she says. "We need to talk to him about those."

"Raditz's friends heard about the dragon," Roshi says.

"Exactly. I don't know how much else they heard."

A voice calls from above the beach.

"Hey! Down there!"

Krillin goes to the door. Popo's carpet descends with Yajirobe standing on it, holding his sword and a corner of the carpet in separate hands.

"Yajirobe!" Krillin says. "Or was it Armadillo?"

"You know perfectly well what it is."

Popo brings the carpet down, and Yajirobe steps onto the sand and points at the house.

"Korin says he knows about the Saiyans. Krillin, Yamcha, Tien and Chiaotzu are to come to his tower. Kami will train you."

"All of us?" Krillin asks.

"And me."

"That's great!"

"For you, maybe. I said I didn't want to fight them. Nobody listened."

"Do you know where Yamcha and Tien are?" Bulma asks.

"No. Finding them sounds like your job."

Krillin looks at Bulma. "I'll start with Yamcha."

"I'll make some calls from my lab. Someone must have seen him."

Roshi strokes his beard. "Kami has chosen well."

Yajirobe looks at him. "Did he choose you?"

"No."

"Then of course you're pleased."

A round shadow crosses the doorway as Fortuneteller Baba descends on her crystal ball and floats into the room.

"Brother. You're home. That's unusual."

"Sister. We have company."

"I can see that. Where's Goku?"

"With his family," Roshi says. "A Senzu healed him."

"Good. An Otherworld guide told me Kami asked King Yemma whether King Kai might help a living Goku. Yemma said he would ask. He didn't promise an answer."

Krillin looks toward Popo. "Is that why Kami wants to train us?"

"He wants everyone to prepare," Popo says. "He's going to speak with Goku himself."

Baba glances at the open scouter. "Is that the thing the Saiyan wore?"

"It was," Bulma says.

"Can it hear us now?"

"The cell is out. I'm leaving it out until I know more."

"A sensible decision. I was afraid my brother had influenced you."

"I heard that," Roshi says.

"You were supposed to."

Popo bows to the group and leaves on the carpet while Yajirobe watches him rise.

"He could have waited to take me back."

"You could stay for a little while," Roshi says. "I was about to make tea."

"Is there anything to eat with it?"

Turtle turns toward the kitchen. "I'll see what we have."

Bulma puts the scouter cover back without reconnecting the cell, then wraps the device and pockets it separately from the cell again.

"My lab first," she tells Krillin. "Then we'll start looking for Yamcha."

Krillin offers her his arm, and they lift from the beach and turn toward the city, leaving Roshi with the medical case.

## Upstream

Piccolo stands on a narrow ledge above the river, with the pillar from his last shot lying broken in the ravine below.

He raises his new arm and fires at a second column of stone. The beam cuts through it, but the upper section twists instead of falling straight.

It strikes the bank and rolls into the water, breaking loose a tangle of fallen trees that turns downstream.

Piccolo watches the first trunk disappear around the bend. Far below, the river passes beneath a covered bridge at the edge of a village.

He lifts his head, sensing two familiar powers near the bridge with Goku close beside them.

"So you stopped running."

Piccolo drops into the trees above the river and follows the water.

## The Covered Bridge

Ox-King picks up his blanket from the chair as Goku carries Goshin out of the doctor's rooms. The truck is parked at the village end of the bridge, where the road is wide enough to leave it clear.

"Can I walk?" Goshin asks.

Goku sets him down. "I'll be beside you."

Gohan takes Goshin's hand as the road slopes toward the river, and Goshin keeps to its downhill side.

The fruit seller is still looking for her apple, and Gohan points toward the bridge.

"It went under that wagon."

"Then it's welcome to it," she says. "I'm not chasing it through the river."

Inside the covered bridge, a man pulls a small wagon toward the village. A girl sits behind him among parcels and a stack of books. One wheel catches on an uneven board, and the man stops to free it.

The sound of rushing water grows louder.

Goku turns toward the river. A trunk comes around the bend, with smaller trees tangled in its roots.

"The bridge!" Chi-Chi calls.

Goku flies over the bank. He catches the trunk just before it strikes the pier, but a thick fork wedges beneath a crossbeam. The force of the water twists the trunk against his arms.

The bridge shudders. Inside it, books tumble from the wagon.

"Mina!" the driver calls.

A roof beam breaks loose at the village entrance, and Ox-King plants his feet on the stone bank and catches its end against his shoulder. Chi-Chi pulls the driver clear as the beam comes down beside him.

"My daughter's in there!"

Through the entrance, Gohan sees a hole in the decking beside the wagon. Mina hangs from its edge, her legs above the river. A bag is caught around a splintered board near her shoulder.

"Father!" she cries.

"I'm here!"

The driver tries to go back. Another plank gives way beneath the wagon, and Chi-Chi catches his arm.

"Wait. The floor's breaking."

Chi-Chi starts across the first plank. It sags beneath her, and Mina's fingers slip along the broken edge. Chi-Chi steps back onto the stone bank.

"I can get underneath," Gohan says.

Goshin steps beside him. "I'll hold the board. I can fly with Gohan."

Chi-Chi looks from the hole to the water, where Goku is still holding the trunk against the pier.

"Stay together." She looks at the boys.

"Stay together."

She takes Goshin's hand for a moment. "If you feel unwell, tell me and come straight back."

"If you feel unwell, tell me and come straight back."

"We'll hold this end for you," Ox-King says.

Gohan calls Nimbus. The cloud slips through the gap below the wagon and carries him toward the broken floor. Goshin flies beside it, reaching for the loose board.

Mina sees them and pulls herself higher, but the board tilts under her fingers.

Goshin catches its broken end. He plants one foot against a bridge support and holds it level.

"Gohan."

"I see her."

Gohan guides Nimbus beneath the hole. Water splashes his shoes. He reaches up to Mina, but the strap of her bag has twisted around a splinter.

"It's stuck," she says.

"I can undo it."

"Will I fall?"

"I'll catch you."

"Where's my father?"

"At this end of the bridge," Chi-Chi calls. "He's right here."

"Mina, I can hear you," her father says.

Mina looks toward the voice. Her grip loosens, and Goshin pushes the board up beneath her chest.

Gohan keeps Nimbus under the opening while Mina tries to find a place for her other hand. The loose strap is stretched across the splinter, and each pull draws the bag a little closer to the water.

Goshin shifts his foot against the support so the board stays level. Gohan waits for it to stop moving before working the wet strap back along the wood.

"I'm holding it," he tells her.

Gohan takes hold of the strap. His fingers slip on the wet cloth. Above him, Mina's hands begin to slide.

A loose branch sweeps against the bridge support. Gohan steers Nimbus around it, then brings the cloud back beneath Mina.

"I'm still here," he says.

On the deck above them, the wagon begins to roll toward the hole. Chi-Chi reaches through the entrance and catches the rope tied to its handle. Mina's father takes the rope from her and winds it around a post on the bank. The wagon stops with one wheel against the broken boards.

"You can leave the bag," Goshin says.

"My book's in it!"

Gohan pulls at the knot again. "What's it about?"

"Machines. My father gave it to me."

"We'll get it."

The strap comes loose. Gohan catches the bag before it falls and tucks it beneath his arm. Mina reaches down to him, and he takes her around the waist.

"Hold my shoulder."

She clings to him as Gohan lifts her toward the unbroken decking, where Goshin guides her feet onto a plank.

The bridge jolts. Goshin's foot slips from the support, and his tail whips against the stone to steady him. The board jerks out of one hand. He catches it with the palm Raditz burned and holds it above Mina.

"Did that hurt?" Gohan asks.

"No. Keep going."

Goshin gets both hands under the board and keeps it level. Mina crawls onto the plank beside him.

Outside, Goku braces the trunk against the current as its fork presses harder against the bridge pier.

"The fork is caught under the beam," he calls. "If I pull it now, the bridge comes with it!"

"We're coming!" Gohan answers.

A second log jams against the trunk and tilts upward. Its branches catch the roof, tearing tiles and wooden boards into the river. Through the new opening, the full moon shines above the bridge.

Goku sees it from the water.

"Gohan! Goshin! Don't look up! The full moon can turn you into giant apes!"

"Don't look up!" Goshin repeats.

He lowers his face toward the planks. Gohan keeps his eyes on Mina and the cloud beneath him. At the entrance, Chi-Chi opens Ox-King's blanket, ready to cover the boys when they reach her.

"Come toward my voice," she calls.

"Come toward my voice," Goshin repeats.

Mina stands, but the tilted decking moves under her feet. Goshin releases the broken board and takes her hand. Gohan rises on Nimbus beside them, still holding the bag.

A wooden rail tears away and swings across their path. Gohan catches it before it reaches Mina. He pushes it back against a post while Goshin leads her past.

Chi-Chi reaches through the gap and draws Mina onto the stone bank. Her father wraps his arms around her.

Gohan passes the bag out after her, and Mina clutches it against her chest.

"My book!"

"I've got you too," her father says.

Ox-King keeps his shoulder under the fallen beam while Gohan climbs out. Goshin follows, looking down. Chi-Chi pulls the blanket over both boys' heads and shoulders.

From the willows beside the river, Piccolo watches the family. He heard Goku's warning.

"The full moon?"

In the water, Goku shifts his grip as the trapped fork pulls at the pier and a crack runs across the nearest support.

Gohan points down from the bank. "The branch is under that piece of wood."

Goku looks toward Goshin. "Can you see it?"

"Yes."

"Hit the branch without touching the bridge."

"Hit the branch without touching the bridge."

Goshin raises one hand. Gohan catches his sleeve.

"The pier is behind it from here."

Goshin lowers his hand and moves along the bank until the branch is in front of open water, then aims low, away from the moon.

A narrow flash strikes the fork. The branch breaks free, and Goku lifts the trunk above the pier. Water pours from its roots as he carries it to an empty gravel bar beyond the bridge.

The damaged deck shifts as the trunk comes free. The wagon tips onto its side, spilling several parcels through the hole, but the rope keeps it from following them. The current carries the parcels away beneath the bridge.

Ox-King lowers the roof beam carefully onto the bank, and Gohan helps him move it out of the road, then returns to Goshin.

"Did you see it?" Goshin asks.

"The moon? No."

"Neither did I."

Goshin sits on the stone edge of the road, and Gohan sits beside him with the blanket still over both their heads.

Gohan looks at Goshin's palm. "You caught the board with it."

"It didn't hurt," Goshin says.

As Chi-Chi kneels to look him over, Goshin puts his hands behind his back.

"And your head?"

"It doesn't hurt either."

"Show me your hand."

"Show me your hand."

Goshin brings it forward. The new skin is unbroken. Chi-Chi takes his hand for a moment, then lets him turn it over.

Goku lands beside them. He crouches so the boys do not need to raise their faces.

"You got her out. And that shot was narrower than the wave at the crater."

"Gohan saw the pier," Goshin says.

"You both found the right place for it."

Gohan peeks from beneath the blanket at Mina and her father. "She had a book."

"I heard."

Mina opens the bag and pulls out a wet volume, whose cover shows a machine with great digging arms.

"It's all right," she says. "Only the outside is wet."

Her father looks at the wagon, then at the books drifting downstream. "The others may be less fortunate."

"Can I help you find them?" Gohan asks.

"In the morning," Chi-Chi says. "The river isn't safe in the dark."

Mina holds the book out to him. "You can borrow this one."

Gohan takes it with both hands. "Thank you."

Goshin leans close enough to see the picture, and Goku looks at him.

"What would you like to know?"

"Does that machine eat rocks?"

"It digs them," Mina says.

"Then where do they go?"

Mina opens the book, and Gohan and Goshin bend over the page together.

Goku looks toward the willows. "Piccolo's nearby."

Chi-Chi draws the blanket closer around the boys, and Goku stands beside them until Mina and her father have gone back toward the village.

"Want me to carry you?" he asks Goshin.

"Yes."

Goku lifts him. No one comes out of the trees.

## The Other Bank

Piccolo remains beneath the willows as Goku leads the family toward the village. The blanket hides the boys' faces from the sky.

Piccolo looks up at the moon. Then he looks at Goku, who carries Goshin with one arm and keeps the other around Chi-Chi.

"A full moon can change them."

His eyes move to the boys' tails, visible below the edge of the blanket.

"Raditz had a tail too. If the other Saiyans can do the same, I need to be ready."

He rises above the river and turns toward the broken ridge.

## Behind the Curtains

The doctor meets them at her door. Chi-Chi tells her about the bridge while Goku brings Goshin inside. Gohan follows with Mina's book beneath his arm. Once the door is closed, Chi-Chi takes the blanket off the boys and folds it on a chair.

"Are you all right?" the doctor asks.

"We got Mina out," Goshin says. "My hand didn't hurt."

Ox-King fills the doorway behind them. "The road across the bridge isn't safe. I wouldn't drive the truck over it now."

"I have a room at the back," the doctor says. "You can stay until morning."

Chi-Chi looks toward the darkening street. "Thank you. We'll take it."

"I'll lock the truck," Ox-King says. He steps back outside.

Gohan carries the book into the room. He stops beside the window and waits while Chi-Chi closes the curtains. Then he sets the book on a low table.

There is a knock at the front door. Goku turns his head.

"That's Kami."

He goes to the door and returns with the guardian. Kami bows to Chi-Chi and looks at the boys.

"I'm glad to see you together."

"Kami helped train me," Goku tells them. "This is Chi-Chi, Gohan and Goshin."

"The boys have had a difficult day," Chi-Chi says.

"I know. Popo told me what he saw of Piccolo."

Kami sits across from Goku and rests his staff beside him.

"There is something you should know about Piccolo and me. Our lives are joined. If he dies, so do I. If I die, he dies as well."

Goku looks at the staff. "Because you were once the same person?"

"Yes. And the Dragon Balls depend on my life."

Gohan touches the ball on his hat. "Will the dragon go away?"

"One day it will. I have a feeling my life may have about a year left, but I cannot tell whether the danger is the Saiyans or my own age. I may be wrong."

Chi-Chi sits beside Gohan. "You don't know that there will be only one wish left."

"No. It is a possibility, not a certainty."

Goku looks at Goshin, who is watching Kami's mouth.

"Did you want to ask something?"

"Are you going to die?"

Kami's hand closes over the end of his staff. "I hope not soon. I don't know when."

Goshin moves closer to Chi-Chi. She puts an arm around him.

"Two of the Dragon Balls are at our house," Goku says. "Raditz's friends heard about the dragon. We should check them when we get home."

Gohan holds his hat against his chest. "Will they come after this one too?"

"They might," Chi-Chi says. "We can take it off your hat if you want."

Gohan turns the hat in his lap and looks at the stitches that hold the ball in place. A speck of mud from the bridge has dried beside one of the stars.

"Would they know it was here?"

"We don't know what else Raditz's device sent," Kami says. "It is safest to be ready for them to look."

Gohan rubs the mud away with his thumb while Goku waits for his answer. Chi-Chi keeps her hand on the edge of the hat rather than taking it from him.

"It's Grandpa's ball. I want to keep it."

Goku puts a hand on the hat. "Then keep it, Gohan. If those guys come for it, we'll stop them."

"Raditz nearly killed you today!" Chi-Chi says.

Goku rubs the back of his head. "Yeah. Those other two are even stronger, though. I'd sure like to see what they can do."

"Goku!"

"I know! I have to get stronger before they get here."

Gohan puts his hat back on.

"I have asked Korin to summon Krillin and the others," Kami says. "I will help them prepare. I also asked King Yemma whether King Kai might teach you. Yemma said he would ask, but he promised no answer."

"King Kai is stronger than you?"

"Much stronger."

Goku sits up. "Really? I want to meet him! Can we go now?"

"King Kai is in the Other World. I asked Yemma whether you could go there while alive, but he hasn't answered. King Kai has not agreed to teach you either."

"Aw, that's too bad. When Yemma hears from him, tell me, okay?"

Chi-Chi turns to Goku. "And what about Piccolo? He's still after our boys!"

"I know. We'll be ready if he comes back."

Goku looks at Gohan and Goshin. "Hey, how about training with me until we hear from King Kai?"

"With you?" Gohan asks.

"You bet! We can start tomorrow. Goshin, what do you say?"

"Can we practise aiming?"

"Sure!"

"And Gohan's studies won't stop," Chi-Chi says.

"Sure, sure! There's time for both."

Gohan runs one finger around the edge of his hat. "Roshi said the moon might change us. Could the Saiyans do that too?"

"They may be able to," Kami says. "We don't know."

Outside, Ox-King closes the truck door and comes up the path. Goku opens the room for him, and Chi-Chi hands him his folded blanket.

"The doctor says the boys can stay here tonight," Chi-Chi tells him.

"Then so can I."

He lowers himself onto a chair, but it creaks and he gets up again.

"I'll sit on the floor."

Kami rises and takes his staff. "I'll return to the Lookout. Popo can tell me when the others are ready."

"Thank you for coming," Goku says.

After Kami has gone, Chi-Chi goes to the kitchen with Goshin. The doctor has given her rice and eggs. Goshin watches her wash the rice, put it on the stove, crack the eggs and cook them in a pan.

"Are they ready?" he asks.

Chi-Chi turns the eggs. "Now they are."

She fills a bowl for him, and he carries it to the table and eats while Gohan opens Mina's book.

The first page shows a machine cutting a tunnel through stone, and the next shows a row of wheels, two metal arms and a scoop.

"It has more arms than Piccolo," Goshin says.

Gohan counts them. "Only two. That one is the scoop."

"It still looks like an arm."

Gohan reads the line beneath the picture. "The wheels carry it over the ground, and the scoop moves the rocks."

"What if the rocks fall on it?"

"I don't know." Gohan turns the page. "Maybe it says here."

A drawing shows a tunnel held open by wooden beams, and Goshin traces one of them.

"We could help it get out," he says.

"If it couldn't move," Gohan agrees.

Goku sits beside them. "What would you like to see?"

"One of those machines," Goshin says.

"I want to know how it moves," Gohan says.

"Maybe we can find one," Goku says.

Chi-Chi brings a second blanket from the cupboard. "When the river is safe, and we can go together."

Ox-King's head tips forward, and he catches himself, then lets it fall back against the wall.

"Goku," he murmurs. "There's another roast."

Gohan looks up. "He's asleep."

"He's still feeding Father," Goshin says.

Chi-Chi spreads the doctor's blanket over Ox-King's knees while Goku turns the page for the boys.
