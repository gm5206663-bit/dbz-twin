# Episode 2–3 scene coverage — 31 October repair

**Owns:** an ordered comparison of the available episode-2/3 evidence with the living story. It is an audit aid, not a claim that the original video has been watched in full. `W-30` is redistributed Japanese caption text of unverified broadcast provenance. `W-03`, `W-17`, `W-19` and `W-43` are secondary sources; selected stills in the episode-3 fan gallery were inspected, but their source/cut is unverified. Captions describe spoken material, not every silent movement.

**What the previous coverage check missed:** it claimed that the pod sequence was covered while omitting Gohan's anime-only hiding place under the farmer's truck (`C160`). It also skipped Goku calling Nimbus and the exchange about Piccolo's speed. A closer review found Chapter Two's Gohan/crab and stone-throw scenes missing, as well as the exchange explaining the Saiyans' business. The former claim that caption silence proved nobody threw stones was itself wrong (`R-17`).

**How to read the repair:** Chapter Two's accepted body remains intact. `chapters/Restored_Chapter_Two_Island_Moments.md` supplies five dramatized passages and identifies their positions, including one explicit paragraph replacement. Chapter Four is a replacement draft, not accepted by implication. Its opening replaces Chapter Two's conscious-capture passage. A remaining source-order debt in the accepted Chapter Two is recorded below; a scene inventory is not proof of perfect coverage.
