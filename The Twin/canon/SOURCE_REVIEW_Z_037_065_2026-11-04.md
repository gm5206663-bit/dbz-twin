# Continued canon source review · Z episodes 37–65

**Owns: what this continuation actually read, its episode-by-episode source findings and their limits.** Review date: 4 November 2026. This is research for *The Twin*, not a new chapter or an acceptance decision.

## Scope and evidence

- The complete **retained caption cue streams for Z episodes 37–65** were independently read in this continuation, including recaps, previews, music labels and appended fragments. The dialogue and narration support provisional text and order; silence, exact choreography, speaker identity where unlabelled and release provenance do not follow from a caption file. The times below are local SRT times, not verified broadcast timings.
- The source is `google_drive/Dragon_Ball_Z_Subtitle_Collection_001-291.zip`, SHA-256 `d77669700e812bd97cfce7338641f036146b2b377b594c08e46853e0dc997e50`. Earlier records attribute redistribution to Kitsunekko, but neither the retrieval trail, the broadcaster/official origin nor the release/cut has been independently verified (`W-30`). An appended fragment after the ending is not an episode event. Recaps and previews are identified as such, not treated as additional completed scenes.
- Earlier project records report full spoken-caption reads of 1–36, 49, 55, 59, 61, 65, 79–83, 183–184, 205–208 and 256–257. Together with this block, that produced **78 distinct episodes** at this checkpoint. The subsequent [66–78 review](SOURCE_REVIEW_Z_066_078_2026-11-04.md) raised coverage to 91; the later [79–90 review](SOURCE_REVIEW_Z_079_090_2026-11-04.md) raises it to **1–90, 183–184, 205–208 and 256–257: 98 of 291 Z episodes**. - No complete original episode video or audio was examined. Selected stills, the official and secondary episode synopses, and the short public episode-34 clip samples below are separate kinds of evidence. Original *Dragon Ball*, other Z episodes, Kai, manga, GT, *Super*, *Daima*, films and other material were not exhaustively reviewed by this pass.

## Other cross-version findings checked during the same continuation

1. **Episode 34:** the Japanese SRT's 08:13–08:18 Kamehameha call is not speaker-tagged. Sampled frames from a [26-second public fan-uploaded clip](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KmeVGvyqz2U) show Gohan's open mouth and both hands overhead beneath an embedded English “Kamehameha!” subtitle at clip +15 seconds, a yellow blast at +16, and Vegeta's reaction at +20. The following Vegeta line corresponds in sequence to the SRT. These observations strengthen contextual attribution to Gohan in that clip, but the upload's source/cut and original Japanese audio were not verified, and the frames do not establish when he learned the technique. A fan rewatch's different-dub claim remains unverified (`C242`, `W-90`, `W-101`). Three representative frames are retained in `../../Research/The Twin/Episode Stills/Episode 34/`; their SHA-256 values are `967e9ade5772bf1514fb86bf3a7c478559727b5316098b4673f81aace24030f7`, `20df2ab4257ea2539f0804bbd35dedbb09c2034e3aa8a4f48d2a4812d738cce6` and `5cc09aa8d6bc9adbe4f477b19fa2ad0d65afff335a11babca42f08706c0adc71` respectively. These are clip times, not episode timestamps.
2. **Manga edition:** two individually inspected cleaned comparison fragments show the chapter-196 age labels **３さい** and **４さい**. Kanzenshuu attributes them respectively to the original magazine and collected editions. The physical magazine and complete original pages were not inspected. An official reproduced chapter-197 illustration separately shows **４さい** and **孫**. The selected twins are four at the opening (`C243`, `C244`, `W-96`, `W-98`).
3. **Super Saiyan God:** Toei's [Super episode-9 synopsis](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragon_super/episode/9/) describes five righteous Saiyans pouring their hearts into a sixth righteous Saiyan and Videl's unborn child providing the needed Saiyan. It is a TV synopsis, not a complete viewing or film comparison. The authored Goshin divine-base rule is distinct; excluding *Battle of Gods* would not in itself erase the separate Super TV synopsis (`C117`, `W-100`, `O031`). Film integration remains open.
4. **English dub source:** `W-21` is a secondary quote page; the specific dub edition and audio have not been verified. Its reported episode-2 Goku quotation must not be presented as Japanese speech or Chi-Chi's own dialogue (`C063`, `C039`).
