## Standing instructions for future work

Read the actual relevant chapters and the author’s latest corrections. Goshin visually resembles Goku extremely closely, with Chi-Chi's eyes; the design is distinct from his habits and is not erased by the old R-22 prose note. The assistant-invented automatic repeat-back requirement is withdrawn; the author did not direct it. Older drafts repeated food-watching, paternal-invitation and maternal-inspection practices with incompletely recoverable provenance; the selected correction removes mechanical recurrence while retaining contextual choices, not unverified absolutes. Goshin is emotional, plays fully and has independent agency. He is never less capable than Gohan, while Gohan retains agency and growth. His Saiyan bloodline becomes purer and deeper as he grows, without assuming his human heritage disappears. Goshin’s first eventual Great Ape is aware and controlled from the start; the form and its future applications develop with him. Do not force canon outcomes, invent numerical ratings or mark drafts accepted without the author’s decision.

For delegated corrections, investigate and repair related issues autonomously; do not stop or require the author to reread each change. Keep revised text clearly unaccepted unless the author accepts it, but do not treat that status as a workflow gate. After a chapter or revision, update the foundation, timeline, relationships, status or status delta, world/knowledge and source notes that actually changed. Keep one clearly identified working version and preserve necessary earlier versions in the designated Research backup locations; existing older Drive copies are separate, not automatically synchronized. Check before deleting, replacing or syncing anything. Do not claim checks prove literary quality or full video review.

### Episode-index tool

`python3 -B 'The Twin/tools/index_eps.py'` reads the existing local 001–291 caption ZIP and checks whether it reproduces `canon/episodes.json`; it does not write by default. Run it from the workshop root. Use `--source` (or `DBZ_SUBS`) for another complete ZIP or extracted directory, `--output` for a new non-existing output file, and `--write` only after preserving the previous selected index and reviewing an intentional replacement. `--help` lists these options. The 29-minute window and source-provenance limitations are unchanged. The [pre-tool-alignment snapshot](../Research/The%20Twin/Chapter%20Research/Workspace%20synchronization/Before%20episode-index%20tool%20alignment%20%282026-11-03%29.zip) preserves the prior indexer and selected index. `python3 -B 'The Twin/tools/test_index_eps.py'` runs its isolated fixtures.
