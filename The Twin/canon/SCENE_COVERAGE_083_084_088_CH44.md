## Ordered branch events

1. **The Chapter 43 seam:** Freeza twists the wrist Goshin has caught with ×20 active. Piccolo turns a shot away and pulls Goshin out of another line of fire. Goshin keeps fighting and, when Freeza begins changing, actively raises his selected unboosted output with ×30 and strikes him before and during the change. The earlier ×30 direct advantage over the long-headed form is still meaningful; the fresh use is not ×30 of the prior ×20 (`O317`).
2. **Spring and warning:** Gohan follows the actual trail, meets Kado's companion and finds Muri, Dende, Cargo and the older resident at the spring. He says Freeza heard the healing report, admits that he did not tell Freeza what Dende looks like and does **not** claim certainty about what Freeza saw at the earlier house. Muri and the others choose the screened channel; the older resident carries the covered ball. Cargo and Kado's companion leave a stone direction marker. Dende chooses to stay with his people while remaining willing to help (`O319`).
3. **Two soldiers, not one:** The man Gohan passed at the grove remains last shown alive and temporarily tied there. The man who attacks Bulma and Kado at the inlet is the separate still, scouter-broken soldier whom they earlier passed under trees. Gohan hears the shot, reaches the ship, deflects the man's hand and renders him unconscious with a controlled physical strike; he checks that the man is breathing. Gohan and Kado tie him to a tree with a spare strap, not a guaranteed permanent restraint. Neither soldier is assigned an unshown escape, death or fresh report (`O320`).
4. **The report's physical route:** Puar flies from the Son house to Capsule Corporation, obtains the police-cordoned pod site's address through reception, and carries the note there. Chi-Chi, Yamcha and Dr. Brief hear only Bulma's time-bounded information. Brief's continued external readings do not prove safety or usability. Puar sets off back toward the home telephone; Krillin carries the information to the quarry, where Goku and Vegeta hear the reported Namek location and the same limits. Vegeta's earlier suspicion becomes confirmation. Receipt by the boys of Chi-Chi's earlier requested message remains unshown (`O321`, `O322`, `O330`).
5. **The ship and lake:** Bulma pilots around the shelf while Kado helps watch. Gohan and Kado discuss the missing worker; after Bulma, Kado and Gohan board, Bulma moves slowly toward the wider channel, not a certified evacuation or interplanetary rescue. At the lake Freeza emerges smaller, with the shoulder scrape shown. He and ×30 Goshin both land blows; Piccolo contributes through warning and positioning, and Goshin bends his established Kamehameha across a shot away from Piccolo and cuts it off. His body and ki genuinely develop during the contest. He briefly lets the red surge fall while the gain remains, then uses it again as Freeza moves (`O317`, `O318`).
6. **Earth watch and final approach:** At the quarry, after Krillin arrives, Vegeta conditionally proposes denying Freeza a possible Dragon Ball, while privately intending the wish for himself; Goku rejects giving him a ship or taking another people's ball. Goku asks King Kai to warn Piccolo if he can, and King Kai agrees to try; delivery is not shown. Tien asks Chiaotzu to seek help from Kami and Popo, and Goku asks him to pass on the bounded reports and the pod's uncertain usability. Tien changes positions to watch Nappa; Goku and Krillin stay by awake Vegeta. Chiaotzu leaves but has not arrived or obtained an arrangement. Freeza refers to the inlet report he actually received in Chapter 39 and moves toward it; Goshin and Piccolo pursue. On the moving ship Gohan senses Freeza approaching and warns Bulma. Water rises beside the craft; the cause and outcome are not stated (`O322`, `O323`).

The Namek and Earth sections are cross-cut, not a stopwatch or a claim of exact simultaneous cuts. Within each thread the shown actions above are ordered: Puar must reach the site before Krillin can relay, Gohan must hear the shot before reaching the inlet, and Freeza changes before the final chase. No specific speed or travel duration is assigned to make a cut work.
