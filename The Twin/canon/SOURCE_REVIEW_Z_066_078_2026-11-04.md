## Scope and limits

Every retained caption cue in episodes **66–78** was read, including recap, preview, music labels and appended fragments. A first combined tool output was truncated; the affected individual episodes **67, 68, 71, 72 and 75** were reread separately with untruncated outputs. The other episodes' complete cue streams were visible in the combined outputs. All times below are local SRT times. A preview is not an event completed in the current episode; post-ending fragments are not Z episode evidence.

The ZIP archive's source and cut are unverified (`W-30`), and captions alone do not show silent action or reliably assign an unlabelled cue. No complete original video, episode audio, manga run or movie was reviewed. Each newly checked official profile is a source-specific retrospective, not a substitute for the work itself; an official page can also make a mistake.

## Cross-source findings beyond the caption block

1. **The Namek wish limit changes.** The [official manga-based Porunga feature](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_2809.html) separately describes the initial one-person resurrection limit and a later upgrade removing it, as well as a 130-day Namek-ball recharge. This corroborates a useful version-specific distinction; it is not a complete manga reading or an Earth-Shenron rule (`W-103`, `C253`).
2. **Body Change has control costs.** The [official manga-based Ginyu feature](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_2068.html) identifies the 180,000 event with Kaiō-ken, describes his inability to release Goku's full power in the stolen body, and describes the frog interception. Local Z dialogue is recorded separately; neither source proves every body-swap method behaves identically (`W-104`, `C252`, `C256`).
3. **Nail, personality and source version.** The [official manga-based Nail profile](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_2250.html) calls him a lone warrior-type and describes the assimilated person surrendering body and personality. A separate [official Piccolo editorial](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_1365.html) interprets later behavior as possible influence by Nail. The Z captions have three earlier warrior-types and later Nail's last-survivor assertion. Do not flatten the separate moments, media and interpretations into an absolute single rule (`W-108`, `C254`, `C255`).
4. **Chi-Chi is a fighter as well as a protective mother.** A [manga-based official feature](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_1412.html) describes her preliminary advancement, fight with Goku and Roshi's favorable appraisal at the 23rd tournament. The Z-65 captioned rescue attempt is separate. Neither establishes her precise Z-era strength or requires identical branch choreography (`W-106`, `C250`, `C259`).
5. **An official film-labelled retrospective conflicts with a version comparison.** [Toei's Super episode-4 synopsis](https://lineup.toei-anim.co.jp/ja/tv/dragon_super/episode/4/) puts Bulma's birthday party on a luxury ship. [Kanzenshuu's comparison](https://www.kanzenshuu.com/episode/super/battle-of-gods/) and [film synopsis](https://www.kanzenshuu.com/movie/battle-of-gods/synopsis/) instead describe the 2013 *Battle of Gods* party at Capsule Corporation. An [official Whis feature explicitly labelled for the film](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_1663.html) also says cruise ship. This is a real **source conflict**, possibly a cross-version mix-up in the retrospective; the film was not viewed, so its actual venue remains unverified by this review. A movie-labelled profile is not automatically a complete or infallible account (`W-105`, `W-107`, `C258`). The movie, original Super TV and enhanced material remain separate version decisions.
