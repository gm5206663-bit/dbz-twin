## Corrections applied to the selected, unaccepted drafts

1. **Chapter 26, ambiguous demand.** Goshin told Vegeta “Let him go” although the immediate exchange did not show Vegeta holding a hostage. He now says “Leave everyone alone,” retaining his existing offer that Vegeta may take his ship. The fight and its outcome are unchanged.
2. **Chapter 36, unclear action.** At the access plate, the nearest preceding masculine name is Zākuro even though Goshin stops the catch. The sentence now names Goshin; no ability, action or outcome was added.
3. **Chapter 38, wrong father.** During the call, Bulma asked Chi-Chi to call “your father,” but the next Earth scene and surrounding request concern Bulma's father Dr. Brief. “My father” now matches the intended communication path. The downstream scene does not need to change.
4. **Chapter 43 and continuity records, Freeza's knowledge.** Chapter 39 explicitly has Freeza watch Gohan and the two children cross the exposed gap; Muri earlier names Dende and Cargo at the doorway, but Freeza's hearing of that remark is not established. In Chapter 42 he hears Gohan name Dende and report two acts of healing. The text does **not establish whether Freeza linked the name to a particular child or could later recognize Dende**. The current location of Muri's moved group is unknown to him. Earlier Chapter 42–43 handoffs, the `O307` active statement, the status panel and the Chapter 43 source note overstated this as face-ignorance; they now preserve the uncertainty. Chapter 44's Gohan already acknowledges it, so no story event was changed (`O339`).
5. **Chapter 45 continuation date.** The continuation's supplied date was 2026-11-09 UTC and the observed sandbox date was also 2026-11-09 UTC. Its current attribution incorrectly carried over 2026-10-30 Asia/Calcutta; the current Entry 146, its summaries and the Chapter 45 date note have been corrected. Older historical signals, existing archive labels and append order are preserved; this does not settle disputed earlier sessions.

## Open questions, not automatic errors

- **Namekian source/branch portrayal.** In the retained Z 51 captions, Dende describes eggs from the mouth and does not understand Bulma's words for “woman” or her proposed two-type distinction; Bulma infers a lack of a man/woman division. The selected branch uses she/her for Kado's companion from Chapter 42, calls her a woman in Chapter 43, and uses she/her for Sela in Chapter 45. This is a source/branch consistency question worth considering, not proof that feminine pronouns, an individual identity or a form of expression are impossible. Neither character has been recast or deprived of agency, and the divergent branch has not been made to repeat the source dialogue (`C325`). A definitive claim would require stronger, version-specific evidence or an authorial choice.
- **Prose and pacing.** Repeated reassurance about the precise guard arrangement and explicit statements that Goshin's growth remains can make successive Earth and Namek scenes feel formulaic. For instance, the cabin demonstration and later guns in Chapter 33 both separately explain the strength retained without red light; Chapter 34's meteor work and Chapters 35–37 similarly restate gains. These are editorial concerns, not continuity errors or evidence that growth should be removed. Any later line edit should retain the real cumulative whole-being and whole-hybrid development, distinguish it from Kaiō-ken, and keep Gohan and the other characters active.
- **Unresolved story outcomes are not mistakes.** The new lake clash, the damaged ship's condition and concealment, the separated groups and wider village, both temporarily bound soldiers, the downed but unsecured opponents, prior telemetry, the Earth pod and durable guard, Chi-Chi's earlier message, Raiti/Zākuro and the refugee children remain open. The whole Senzu was last shown with Krillin. Do not fill these gaps with unshown events or force source deaths.
