## Provisional starting position

This is not acceptance of Chapters 6–8. The family, Bulma, Roshi and Krillin are in the forest clearing after the pursuit. Goku and Gohan each ate a whole Senzu and are completely healed and restored; Krillin has one whole bean left. Goshin declined a bean he did not watch being prepared. His head, burned palm and grabbed ankle still hurt, although he can fly and has acted effectively; Chi-Chi and Goku intend an examination **today**. Goku is carrying Goshin, Gohan is with Chi-Chi, and Nimbus is waiting. Bulma has re-capsulated the damaged aircraft, kept the detached rod in her toolkit, and kept the wrapped, unpowered scouter and its cell separate. Roshi has the medical case with the Dragon Radar. Ox-King is still following by road.

The boys and parents know Piccolo tried to take the children, briefly stopped a dinosaur, then tried again; he withdrew and is training alone. Roshi has disclosed the moon/ape risk from his tournament experience. Goku wonders whether he caused Grandpa Gohan's death; no one has proved an answer. The moon's current phase has not been established. Kami feels, uncertainly, that his and Piccolo's linked lives might have about a year left and intends to speak to Goku. Yemma has promised only to ask King Kai about helping a living Goku, not granted permission. Popo returned to Kami. Baba is traveling to give Roshi the limited inquiry message. The Three- and Six-Star Balls are at the mountain home; the Four-Star is on Gohan's hat. No robot encounter or outcome has been set.

## Proposed story shape and checks before writing

- Resolve the actual clearing handoff promptly: show how the family, Roshi, Bulma and Krillin travel without making Nimbus carry someone who cannot ride it. Ox-King's road journey must meet the others by an intelligible route. Goshin's medical examination is today, not an invented instant cure; his choices and capability must continue to matter.
- Give both boys a real, shared danger with understandable geography. A damaged riverside bridge or similar obstacle can make the episode-8 high-place/small-creature motif matter while using Goshin's flight and Gohan's Nimbus rather than pretending they cannot fly or ride. Parents can be capable and occupied with other immediate hazards, not negligent or artificially absent. A warning about the full moon should change practical decisions in any exposed crisis.
- Piccolo's independent training may create consequences downstream or draw his attention, but establish the route. If he learns the moon danger, he must actually overhear or otherwise acquire the information; he was not at the original island conversation. No forced assault, redemption, moon destruction, tail loss or training custody.
- Deliver the Kami/Korin/Yajirobe recruitment and Baba inquiry through actual messengers, without repeating a false death report or a guaranteed King Kai answer. Bulma should actively investigate the separated scouter; she has promised not to restore its power while transmission is uncertain, so unsafe use or exact numbers are not compulsory. Keep the independent threads short enough that adventure stays central.
- If a medical professional recommends monitoring or avoiding exertion, preserve that advice and its consequences. Emergency help may still require Goshin to act, but nobody should silently declare him healed, medically cleared or less capable than Gohan. Do not spend the chapter only on transport and checkups; do not invent a hospital.
- Check word-for-word applicable instruction repeats before compliance, invitation into a father's conversation, downhill position beside Gohan on paths, hands behind his back when Chi-Chi looks him over, and watched food preparation. Let fear, humor, affection, cold rage and his four-year-old behavior coexist. In deliberate training, foundations and mastery of existing abilities come first; emergency copying remains possible. No extra numerical boost for observation.
- Keep Roshi's radar, the remaining Senzu, the separated scouter cell, all three known Dragon Balls, both tails and the living Goku straight. A future robot adventure must arise from an earned route; no adult-only rescue or predetermined robot fate.
