## Present first-form development and later paths

- **Great Ape:** his first transformation is staged in unaccepted Chapter 26 through his deliberate look at Vegeta's artificial moon, and is conscious and controlled from the beginning. The form and its power develop with Goshin and his deepening hybrid bloodline, including its Saiyan-linked traits, rather than remaining obsolete. He uses the form and shows further unnumbered growth in Chapter 27 before reverting. The older suggestion that the ape itself must frighten him does not override the later correction; further future development remains open.
- **Ape power in the ordinary body:** the first Great Ape experience already contributes to substantial, retained ordinary-body strength and ki (`O214`). The author also allows Goshin to access Great Ape power without the giant body and combine it with anger or, once earned, Super Saiyan. A separately activated mode, its exact mechanics and degree have not yet been shown; no mandatory delay is imposed. This is his own evolving power, not an automatic copy of Broly.
- **Golden line:** Super Saiyan 1, 2 and 3, and the distinctive Rage path, are later developments in the stored design. Their eventual perfected presentation is not an ability he possesses now. Their arrival must follow the author’s rulings and the story’s causes, not a defeat forced to preserve canon.
- **Ape road and Super Saiyan 4:** a later, earned route through the ape lineage; neither the golden ape nor SS4 has appeared in this branch.
- **Divine crossing:** the design reserves a late, one-way promotion to a divine baseline, followed by the authored God 1–3 and God 4 paths. Divinity must be genuinely reached through a valid path; the Talent does not conjure it from nothing. No god ki exists at the current checkpoint.
- **Excluded for Goshin:** Super Saiyan Blue. The future God 4 design is his own path, not an automatic copy of someone else’s Ultra state. The crossing must preserve his identity, emotions and relationships.

The detailed authored future design is in [`../STATUS.md`](../STATUS.md), especially §§11–16. Chapter 26 stages the first form; its substantial contribution to his ordinary-body baseline is now shown after reversion in the unaccepted continuation. A separate normal-sized mode and golden/divine scenes and their timing remain unwritten. Do not treat future plans as current feats.
