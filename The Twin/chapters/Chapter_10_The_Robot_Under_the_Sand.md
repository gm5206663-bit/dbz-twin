# Chapter Ten — The Robot Under the Sand

*Revised story draft; not yet accepted.*

Sand lies across a round glass eye. A thin line of morning light reaches the stone beside it.

## Morning at the Doctor's

Gohan wakes to the sound of a kettle. The curtains are open, and sunlight reaches the table where Mina's book lies beneath his hat. For a moment he watches a square of light creep across the cover.

Beside him, Goshin opens the hand Raditz burned. The new skin is less pink than it was yesterday.

"Does it hurt?" Gohan asks.

"No. My head doesn't hurt either."

Chi-Chi comes over, smoothing her hair back. "The doctor wanted another look if we were still here."

Goku folds a blanket while Ox-King wakes against the wall and looks down at his knees.

"I thought I had a roast."

"You offered it to Father," Gohan says.

"I hope he enjoyed it."

The doctor checks Goshin's hand, then holds up a finger. This time he follows it without turning his head.

"Stand on one foot."

Goshin stands on the ankle Piccolo gripped. His tail curls behind him as he balances, and the doctor gives him a nod.

"Any sickness or dizziness?"

"No. Can I put my foot down now?"

"Please do. The skin has healed even more," she tells Chi-Chi. "He can move normally. After a blow like that, tell me if anything changes."

"We'll stay close," Chi-Chi says.

The doctor looks at Goshin. "And if you feel sick or your head hurts?"

"I'll tell them."

Gohan points toward the book. "Can we help Mina find the others?"

"You may look from the bank," Chi-Chi says. "If the river is too high, we come back."

Goshin is already looking toward the door. "Then let's go before it gets higher."

"Breakfast first," Chi-Chi says. His stomach answers for her.

The doctor opens her kitchen cupboard for Chi-Chi. While Chi-Chi cooks rice and an egg, Goshin helps Gohan set the table. She fills Goshin's bowl, then washes and slices an apple and wraps it in paper.

"For later," she says.

As Goshin eats, Gohan opens Mina's book to an old stone carving above a drawing of an excavation.

"It says the carving is a vimana. People from Nemuria made it."

Goshin leans across the table. "Does it dig?"

"I think it flies."

"The one on the other page digs."

"That one has a scoop. See?" Gohan turns a page and points to the machine they studied the night before.

Goku looks over their shoulders. "I've seen flying things that look stranger than that."

Goshin points at the carving. "Raditz's ship had a chair in the middle. This one doesn't."

"Maybe the chair is inside," Goku says.

Gohan studies the picture again. "It might be. I wish it showed the other side."

"We can ask Mina if there's another picture," Chi-Chi says.

Gohan closes the book around a finger to keep the page.

At the door, Mina and her father are waiting with Ox-King, and Mina has a length of string in her hands.

"The other books were tied up with this," she says. "Father found it by the bridge."

"You can keep this one while we look," her father tells Gohan.

Gohan tucks the book into a cloth bag and puts his hat on while Chi-Chi adds the wrapped apple slices.

"Thank you," he says.

As the road slopes toward the river, Gohan walks beside Goshin. At the bottom, Nimbus waits above the grass.

Gohan climbs onto Nimbus with the bag. Goshin rises beside him, and Goku lifts Chi-Chi and follows the river. Ox-King stays with Mina and her father to search near the bridge.

## Downriver

Where the river leaves the wooded hills and bends between pale cliffs, sand has gathered along the inside bank. Two wet parcels lie against a fallen tree, and farther on a book rests beside a line of old stones.

Goku lands with Chi-Chi as Nimbus settles on the bank. Gohan climbs off, and Goshin lands beside him.

"Stay where I can see you," Chi-Chi says.

Goshin moves toward the fallen tree, keeping Chi-Chi in sight.

Gohan picks up one parcel. Water runs from the paper, but the string is still tied.

"This one's heavy."

Goku sets it on a dry rock. Farther along the bank, Goshin spots a book beside a line of old stones.

"There's another one!"

The boys start toward it. Gohan tests the sand with a stick before stepping out; the stick sinks only a little. He reaches down and lifts the book by a dry corner.

A crack races beneath his shoes.

Goshin catches his arm as the bank drops away, taking both boys with it. The wet book slips from Gohan's fingers into the river. Goku leaps forward, but a slab breaks loose between him and the boys; he catches it before it falls on them.

"Nimbus!" Gohan calls.

Goshin pulls his brother close and rises. Nimbus dives through the opening beneath them, but sand pours after the cloud, pushing it down a sloping passage.

"Gohan! Goshin!" Chi-Chi calls.

Sliding sand seals the opening behind them. Nimbus comes to rest in a chamber of dark stone, its yellow light reaching only partway up the carved walls. The air smells of damp earth and old dust. Goshin keeps hold of Gohan until the last pebbles have stopped falling, then lets him go.

"Did you hit your head?" Gohan asks.

"No. Did you?"

"No."

Gohan brushes sand from Goshin's hair while Goshin picks a pebble out of the fold of Gohan's hat. The Four-Star Ball is still fastened at the front.

Gohan looks up at the steep passage, now filled with sand where they entered. Nimbus rises to it, but loose grains spill down when the cloud touches the blockage.

"Father?" Gohan calls.

There is no answer through the sand. Goshin presses his palm against the loose wall where their parents disappeared. A small stream of grains runs over his fingers, and he pulls back before more can follow.

A narrow line of daylight comes from the far side of the chamber, and Goshin flies toward it. Gohan follows on Nimbus, keeping Mina's bag against his chest.

A carving covers the wall beneath the light, and Gohan opens the book to the page he marked.

"Goshin. Look."

Goshin leans closer to the page.

The carving in the book has the same curved wings and round windows.

"A vimana," Goshin says.

"A Nemurian one."

Something round and yellow projects from the sand beneath the carving, with a glass circle on its front. The paint is dulled and scratched; grit has settled in the seams around the glass. A stone beam lies across its lower half and reaches the arch behind it. One jointed metal arm sticks out at an awkward angle; the other is buried.

Goshin brushes sand from a small plate.

"There are letters."

Gohan steps off Nimbus and leans closer. "Three S's."

His finger touches a switch beside the plate, and a light blinks inside the glass.

"Who did that?" a voice asks.

Gohan jumps back onto Nimbus. Goshin moves in front of him, staring at the round glass eye.

"I'm Son Gohan," Gohan says.

"I'm Son Goshin."

"Two of you? Keep your voices down."

Sand trickles from the roof. Goshin lowers his voice. "Sorry."

"A little noise is enough to bring this place down. I was asleep."

Gohan leans forward. "Are you a robot?"

"What else would I be?"

"A digging machine?" Goshin suggests.

"I'm an SSS-series capsule robot."

Goshin looks at the beam across him. "Are you stuck?"

"No. I always keep a rock on my stomach."

Gohan opens his mouth, then closes it again.

"The exit is behind me," the robot says. "Take your questions with you, and turn my switch off."

"All right. Good night," Goshin says.

He reaches for the switch, and the light goes out.

"Thank you," Gohan whispers to the quiet robot.

## The Other Exit

Gohan guides Nimbus through a low arch behind the robot, and Goshin flies beside him on the lower side of the sloping passage.

The passage runs toward a cliff face, where a fallen stone blocks the opening. Daylight shines through a slit above it; the river is visible far below.

Goshin rises to the slit and puts his palm against the stone. Its upper edge shifts, and sand falls from the arch behind Gohan.

He takes his hand away. "The roof moves with it."

From Nimbus, Gohan can see a strip of sky, but the gap is too narrow for either boy. He calls through it above the rushing water. His voice comes back from the opposite cliff.

"I can't hear Mother."

"She was right there," Goshin says. "She and Father will be looking."

Gohan catches his brother's hand. He holds it until the last grains stop falling from the arch; then they return to the chamber. Gohan steps off Nimbus as Goshin touches the switch, and the glass lights again.

"You," the robot says. "I told you to turn me off."

"The exit is blocked," Gohan says. "There's a stone across it."

"There wasn't when I came in."

"When was that?"

"A long time ago."

Goshin looks at the sand around the robot. "Can you move?"

"Not with that beam across me. Now you know."

As Gohan walks around the exposed metal, the glass eye points past him.

"Can you see us?"

"I can see a light. And a shadow that won't stand still."

Goshin waves to the robot, but its light does not follow him.

"There are two shadows," Goshin says.

"Then both of them should leave me alone."

When a scrape sounds behind Gohan, he turns and sees a scorpion crawling out from beneath the edge of his bag.

"Ah!"

Sand rattles from the ceiling.

As Goshin lifts the bag clear by its strap, Gohan slides Mina's book beneath the scorpion and carries it to a crack in the wall. The scorpion crawls away.

Gohan watches until it is safely inside the crack. Only then does he notice that his hands are shaking; he grips the book against his chest until they settle.

"It didn't hurt you," Goshin says.

"I didn't know that when I saw it."

"It's a cave," the robot says. "There are scorpions."

Gohan closes the book. "Are there any more?"

"Would you like me to ask them?"

Goshin has to cover his mouth to keep from laughing. Gohan gives him a look, and the robot's light swings toward the sound.

"Now turn my switch off."

"Good night again," Goshin says, and the light fades.

While the boys sit beside the quiet machine, the line of sunlight above the blocked passage moves slowly across the wall.

## A Robot's Breakfast

Goshin's stomach makes a noise as he takes the paper packet from the bag.

"Mother's apple," he says.

Gohan helps him unfold it, revealing slices that are a little crushed but free of sand inside the paper.

"Would you like one?" Goshin asks.

"I'm not hungry yet."

When a stone taps the floor near the robot, Gohan looks up at the roof.

"Maybe he knows another way out."

Goshin looks at the dark glass. "He wants to sleep."

"We can ask once more."

Goshin turns the switch on, and the robot's light returns.

"What now?"

"We're sorry," Gohan says. "Is there another passage?"

"There was a stair on the other side of this room. It fell in before I got stuck. I don't know what remains of it."

Gohan takes the book to the far wall, where a row of marks runs along the stone with sand covering the bottom. He brushes a little away.

"They're not letters," he says. "They're numbers."

"Our measurements," the robot says. "We were drawing a plan of the rooms."

Goshin points beside the marks. "What was this metal bar for?"

"Part of a frame we used to measure the walls, until the ground shook it loose."

Gohan looks from the bar to the half-buried stairs. "The book only shows the outside, so it doesn't say where those stairs went."

"They went to another room, probably full of sand now," the robot says.

"Can we clean the sand off your eye?" Goshin asks.

"It won't do any good."

Another pebble drops beside the robot's arm.

"Where did that come from?" he asks.

Gohan points, then lowers his hand. "You can't see it."

The robot's light turns toward the sound of his voice. "Goshin, wipe the glass. Don't open the panel."

Goshin wipes the glass with his sleeve, keeping clear of the panel. The light brightens and steadies.

"I can see a little more," the robot says. "Two of you, all right, and something yellow behind you."

"That's Nimbus," Gohan says, "and he brought us down."

"Can he take you out?"

"Not through the stone."

The robot's light pauses on Gohan's hat. "Which one is Gohan?"

"I am."

"And I am Goshin," Goshin says.

"You look alike."

Gohan tips his hat forward. "You can tell by this."

"I can see the round thing on it."

"That's my grandfather's Dragon Ball."

"Then don't lose the hat, because I don't want to learn your voices all over again."

Goshin holds up the packet. "Mother packed an apple. Would you like some?"

"I don't eat. I can cook, though."

Gohan looks at his round metal body. "You can dig and cook?"

"I told you—I'm an SSS-series robot, C-6."

"C-6," Goshin says. "Can you make it like Mother does?"

"I've never met your mother. Chinese or Western?"

Gohan looks at Goshin. "What does Mother make?"

"Mother food."

"That's not a setting. Open the lid above my eye, Gohan."

Gohan lifts the small metal lid and finds a heating plate beneath it. He lays the apple on top, turning the slices as their edges begin to brown. A little bell sounds inside C-6.

Goshin picks up a slice, drops it and blows on his fingers. "It's hot!"

"I heated it."

He waits for it to cool, then takes a bite. His face puckers.

"It isn't like Mother's."

"You didn't tell me how your mother makes it."

Gohan tries a small piece. "Maybe she uses a different apple."

"Maybe she cooks for people who say thank you."

"Thank you, C-6," both boys say.

His light shifts from Gohan to Goshin. "You'll need more than one apple if you're here long."

Goshin folds the paper around the remaining slices. "Then I'll save these."

"I thought you didn't like them."

"I don't like them as much as Mother's."

C-6 makes a short clicking sound. "The survey crew used to complain too, but they still asked me to cook."

"Did you make it better?" Goshin asks.

"They stopped complaining when they were hungry."

The smell of warm apple lingers around the open lid. Goshin takes one last look at the slices before folding the paper tightly and putting them back in the bag.

Gohan looks toward the blocked passage. "Mother and Father will look for us."

"Then let them. Don't try to dig through that stone."

"Did someone look for you?" Goshin asks.

"There was a survey crew. We were digging at this temple when the ground shook. I found the ruin, and then the sand came down."

Gohan opens the book to the carving. "A Nemurian temple?"

"You know about it?"

"The book says people found pieces of one a long time ago."

"I found more than pieces."

"How long have you been here?" Goshin asks.

"About eighty years."

Gohan looks up from the page. "We're four."

"I can see that."

Goshin studies the sand packed around C-6. "Did your crew come back?"

"I don't know what happened to them. The ground was still falling when I lost sight of them."

The line of sunlight has moved across the carving. Gohan lays his hand on the old paper, keeping the page open.

"Do you remember them?"

"Of course. I remember every complaint they made about my cooking."

"Were you asleep all that time?" Goshin asks.

"Most of it. I wake when the ground moves. When the power runs low, I sleep again."

"Can you see the sky?"

"Not through that wall."

Goshin points toward the light at the arch. "There's a little bit through the other opening. We could show you more if you came out."

C-6's eye turns toward the light, then back to Goshin. "If I came out."

Goshin looks at the beam across C-6. "Couldn't we pull you out?"

Gohan looks up at the beam, following it all the way to the arch. He remembers how the wall moved when Goshin touched the stone at the exit.

"The roof is resting on that beam. Disturb the sand around me and you could bring the whole room down."

"What were their names?" Gohan asks.

"The surveyors? One was called Tama. She drew the plans."

"Like the one in the book?"

"More accurate than that one. She always made me hold the measuring frame while she checked it."

"What was she measuring?" Gohan asks.

"The distance from each door to the next, and the height of every carving, so she could draw what we found before anyone moved it."

Gohan looks at the marks he uncovered. "Then these are part of her plan."

"The beginning of it. She was still measuring this room when the ground began to shake, and the next wall was already coming down when I last saw her."

Goshin looks at the bar by the wall. "Did you mind holding the frame?"

"She said I was the right height for it."

"Were you?"

"Yes," C-6 says. "That wasn't the point."

Gohan picks up a twig from the sand. "Could I draw this room for Mina? She'd want to see where her book went."

"The flat patch by the wall isn't holding up the roof," C-6 says. "Draw it there."

Goshin kneels beside Gohan.

Gohan draws a wide arch and a circle as big as his head for C-6. Goshin adds two figures beside it, then uses the twig to mark the bar against the wall.

"That's you and me," Goshin says.

"Which is which?" C-6 asks.

Goshin puts a tiny hat on one of the figures. Gohan adds a tail to each.

"You've made me round," C-6 says.

"You are round."

"I was hoping you hadn't noticed."

Gohan adds a narrow line of sun to the top of his drawing. C-6 turns his eye toward it, although the marks in the sand are too small for him to read.

Gohan puts his hand on the exposed metal. "But you can't move."

"I'm saving energy. When I've stored enough, I'll get out myself. Don't dig around me."

"We'll leave you room," Goshin says.

He sits beside C-6 without touching the sand. Gohan closes the book.

## A Telephone Call

At Capsule Corporation, Bulma sets the scouter on her workbench. Its cell remains wrapped separately. Krillin stands beside the telephone with a small bag tucked inside his shirt.

"No answer," he says, replacing the receiver.

"Try the other number," Bulma says.

Krillin looks down at the list she has written. "Isn't this one yours?"

"It was. Yamcha stayed there for a while."

The telephone rings for a long time. Krillin sets the receiver down again.

"I'll go and look."

"I'll make some more calls," Bulma says. She puts the scouter cover beside her notes. "And this stays off until I know more about it."

Krillin checks the bag once, then heads for the door.

## The Fallen Beam

Gohan kneels beside the stone beam crossing C-6's body. A piece of wood is wedged beneath one end. Eighty years is a long time to be under a rock with only a little light.

"We could lift this," he says.

"No," C-6 says. "I told you what it holds up."

"What if we just move the wood?" Gohan puts a hand on its end.

Goshin looks up at the roof. "He said the sand could fall."

"I'll be careful."

Gohan draws the wood out a little. Sand runs into the space it leaves; the beam settles with a crack, and a stone drops from the roof.

Goshin flies up and catches it before it reaches Gohan. He sets it away from the beam, but more grains are already spilling between the blocks above.

"I said not to dig!" C-6 snaps.

Gohan pushes the wood back. Sand runs over his fingers.

"I'm sorry. I wanted to get you out."

The roof gives another low creak. Goshin lands beside his brother, looking up.

C-6's light wavers. "Something has pulled loose inside me. I can hear it."

A thin hiss comes from the robot's casing. Gohan takes his hand off the wood. He hadn't meant to hurt C-6; now he can't make the hissing stop.

Goshin crouches beside the robot and peers at the seam in his casing. Dust sifts down onto his hair. He looks up again, one hand hovering near his brother in case another stone comes loose.

From beyond the chamber comes a dull impact.

Gohan turns toward the passage. "Father?"

Another impact answers. Dust shakes from the arch and settles across C-6's glass.

"Don't hit it!" C-6 calls.

Goshin cups his hands around his mouth. "Don't hit it!"

The impacts stop.

## A Voice Through the Stone

Goku kneels beside the collapsed bank with Chi-Chi and sets down a rock he has loosened from the edge.

"I can feel both boys' ki below us, toward the cliff," he says. "I heard them too."

"Can you reach them?"

"Not from here without bringing more sand down."

As Chi-Chi holds her hand near a crack between two stones, a thread of air moves the edge of her sleeve.

"There's space under there," she says. "The cliff might have another opening."

Goku looks toward the river. "I'll take us along the face."

He lifts Chi-Chi and flies along the cliff face. A narrow stone shelf runs beneath the arch where the river has cut into the rock. Goku lands on it and sees a slit above a fallen slab.

"Gohan! Goshin!"

Inside, the boys hurry back through the passage. Gohan rides Nimbus, and Goshin flies beside him. They reach the slit as Chi-Chi calls their names.

"We're here!" Gohan says.

Goku puts his hand against the outside of the slab. "Are either of you hurt?"

"We didn't hit the ground," Gohan says. "Goshin caught me, and Nimbus came down."

"I didn't hit my head," Goshin says. "I'm all right."

Chi-Chi leans close to the slit. "We're going to get you out."

"There's somebody else," Gohan says. "A robot. He's buried in the room behind us."

The slab shifts under Goku's hand, and the arch above the boys grinds against it. Goku stops moving it and holds it where it is.

"If I pull this free, it might take the roof with it. Goshin, what is holding the other side?"

"A beam across C-6 reaches into the arch," Goshin says.

"Can you see how to support it?"

Goshin looks back into the chamber. "Maybe. There's a long metal bar by the wall."

"Show C-6," Gohan says. "He knows the place."

"He can tell us if it will hold," Goshin says.

They return to C-6. Gohan steps off Nimbus beside the arch, puts the book back in its bag and loops the strap over his shoulder. Then he carries the bar over and lays it beside the fallen beam.

"Father is holding the stone outside," he says. "Can we put this under the beam?"

C-6 studies the bar through his dim glass. "The floor by that wall is stone. If you set it there, it might hold for a little while."

"We can lift the beam together," Goshin says.

"You're supposed to get out."

"We want you to come too," Gohan says.

C-6's light stays on Gohan.

"I can't promise the roof will hold."

"Would you like to come with us?" Goshin asks.

A little sand slides off the beam.

"Yes," C-6 says. "If we can keep that stone from falling."

Goshin rises beneath the beam, puts his shoulder against it and braces the edge with his palm. Stone scrapes overhead as he lifts it enough for Gohan to slide the bar underneath.

"The bottom's sinking!"

Gohan pulls the bar back and scrapes away the loose sand until his fingers reach a flat stone. He plants the end against it, but the bar leans sideways.

"Can you keep holding it?"

"Yes. The roof's moving, though."

Goshin adjusts his shoulder while Gohan fits the bar into a notch between two blocks. He eases the beam down onto it. The bar bends, and sand trickles past Gohan's feet, but the roof stays up.

C-6's exposed arm moves a few inches, then stops.

"I need the reserve power. Gohan, open the panel in my head. Connect the blue wire to the red one."

Gohan finds the two loose ends beneath the glass. "Will it hurt you?"

"I've got an energy leak. The reserve will make it worse, and I don't know how much I'll have left."

Goshin looks at him. "You don't have to."

"I know. But you're both still in here." His eye steadies on Gohan. "The reserve will let my arm hold that beam. Connect them."

Gohan joins the wires. C-6's light flares, and his arm rises against the beam; the hiss inside his open panel grows louder.

"There," C-6 says. "Gohan, clear the sand from my left side."

Gohan scoops the sand away with both hands. Goshin takes the weight of the beam beside C-6's right arm while Gohan pulls a broken stone from beneath C-6's body. Sand slides from C-6's left arm, and he works it free.

Gohan sets the stone against the wall. Sand fills the hollow it left.

As a chip of stone drops toward Gohan, C-6 catches it in his newly freed hand, and Gohan takes it from him.

"Thank you," Gohan says.

"I don't want you dropping it on me."

Gohan sets the chip away from the beam. "That's the last one I can see."

"Then lift me toward the passage."

"Gohan, take that side. I'll take this one."

Goshin lowers himself beside C-6 and slips his arms under the metal rim while Gohan takes hold of the other side. They lift and pull him sideways from beneath the beam. Sand pours into the space he leaves.

The metal bar skids across the floor. Gohan catches the falling beam with both hands as Nimbus darts beneath him.

"Goshin! I've got it!"

Goshin rises with C-6 against his shoulder. The beam shifts against Gohan's palms, and Goshin reaches toward it; his ki catches the stone, taking some of the weight.

"I can move," Gohan says.

C-6 stretches his freed left arm toward him. "Let go. I'll catch you."

Goshin carries the robot clear of the beam's edge while C-6 keeps his right arm braced against it. Gohan lets go, and C-6 catches his wrist. Nimbus sweeps beneath his feet.

The weight bends C-6's right arm. Goshin holds the beam up with ki until Nimbus is clear, then releases it. The stone crashes down behind them.

Dust rolls through the arch. Goshin keeps the robot close, turning his body so C-6's damaged arm does not catch on the wall. Gohan guides Nimbus toward the light ahead.

As the arch shakes, Gohan pulls his arm free once he is balanced and guides Nimbus ahead of Goshin. He reaches the slit and calls to Goku.

"We're coming with C-6!"

Goku lifts the outer slab slowly, and stones scrape together above his hands. Chi-Chi moves back along the shelf, keeping one hand against the cliff.

As Goshin turns C-6 sideways through the widening opening, Gohan takes his arm from the other side and helps pull him clear.

A shower of sand falls through the gap. The boys come out after C-6, and Goku lowers the slab onto the shelf. The arch behind it folds into the passage.

For a moment, only the river can be heard. Fine dust settles on the narrow shelf and on C-6's round yellow casing. Beyond them, water flashes between the cliffs, and the open sky seems enormous after the low chamber.

## On the Shelf

Chi-Chi kneels beside the boys as the last sand falls from the passage. She pulls them close, then looks at Goshin.

"Did you hit your head?"

"No. I'm all right."

Sand covers his palms. Chi-Chi brushes it away and finds no blood, then catches Gohan's hand too. He is looking at C-6's bent arm.

"I shouldn't have moved the wood."

"I told you not to," C-6 says.

"I know. I'm sorry." Gohan rubs his eyes with the back of his hand.

C-6's light flickers. "Next time, listen."

"I will."

The passage behind them gives one last soft hiss as sand settles. Gohan keeps hold of his mother's hand.

Goku crouches beside C-6. "Can I carry you to the village?"

"If you keep hold of my body. That arm won't help."

Goku picks him up with both arms as Chi-Chi climbs onto his back, and Nimbus rises beside them with Gohan and Goshin aboard.

They stop at the bank long enough for Gohan to pick up the parcel Goku left on the dry rock, then the cloud follows Goku along the river.

C-6's glass eye turns toward the open sky.

"It's bright," he says.

Goshin leans close from Nimbus. "That's more than the little bit."

"I can see that."

## The Doctor's Yard

The doctor comes into the yard when Goku lands. Chi-Chi tells her what happened beneath the riverbank.

"There's shade under the eaves," the doctor says.

Goku sets C-6 there. His damaged arm hangs against his side, and the light behind his glass is dimmer than before.

The bent arm scrapes against the ground as Goku lets go.

"Is your energy still leaking?" Gohan asks.

"I can't hear it now. I don't know how much is left."

Goshin looks at the faint light in C-6's eye. "If you sleep, will you wake up again?"

"I don't know that either. I used the reserve because the two of you were under that roof, not because the wires would repair me."

"I don't want you to go to sleep," Goshin says.

C-6's light settles on him, but he doesn't answer. Goshin sits down beside the robot, close enough to see his eye without touching the open panel.

The thin light wavers again. Goshin waits for it to steady, watching so closely that the voices at the gate barely reach him.

Gohan rests the bag beside them. "We'll ask somebody who knows about robots."

Mina and her father arrive with Ox-King, and Gohan gives her father the parcel from the rock.

"Father put this parcel on a rock. Another was caught by the fallen tree, but the loose book fell into the river."

Her father unties the parcel and finds two damp volumes inside, turning the pages carefully.

"We can dry these. I'll look for the other parcel once they're safe. Thank you."

Mina stops in front of C-6. "Does he talk?"

"Of course I talk," C-6 says. "I'm not a wagon."

Mina looks at Gohan. "Where did you find him?"

Gohan opens her book to the carving. "Under the bank. He found the temple in the picture."

"More of it than the picture shows," C-6 says.

"Can you tell us about it?" Goshin asks.

"I could."

"Will you?"

C-6 looks at the open page. "My eye isn't good enough to see those words."

"I'll read them," Gohan says.

Goshin touches the edge of the page. "And I'll show you the pictures."

C-6's light moves between them. "I have a feeling neither of you is going to let me sleep."

"We can come back later," Goshin says.

C-6 is quiet for a moment. "Start with the page about the temple. The picture's wrong."

Gohan opens the book wider. "Can you tell us what's wrong?"

"I can if you stay long enough to listen."

Gohan sits beside Goshin and begins to read. Mina settles on the step with her father. Ox-King moves the empty chair aside and sits on the ground. The doctor goes inside, leaving the door open.

Goku looks at C-6's damaged arm. "My friend Bulma knows a lot about machines. We could ask her to look at it, if you want."

"Ask first," C-6 says. "Nobody opens my panel without telling me."

"We'll ask," Goku says.

As Gohan turns the page, the light behind C-6's glass remains on the book.
