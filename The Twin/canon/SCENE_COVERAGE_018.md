## Authored handoff and outstanding limits

- Goshin teaches stance, foot placement and ki control, demonstrating the already-developed Kamehameha's size, direction and stopping while he moves stones. He restrains this teaching demonstration deliberately. Gohan fires his own real first wave, repeats it, lets gathered light fade without firing and later aims at the bracket. The first-use qualification in `O088` is not a permanent limit (`O177`).
- The pod's voice knows the name Kakarot, but Bulma has not identified ownership, safely analysed the device or certified it inactive. She has taken the covered machine toward the laboratory. Its projected image is gone; no natural moon destruction or new transformation is shown.
- **Later source qualification:** the secondary episode-18 guide reports the source pod's destruction, and its inspected still `kanzenshuu_005.png` shows a beam striking the pod, not the full aftermath. In episode 46 Goku initially says his old ship was safe, but Brief later says he found it broken in pieces, with crucial parts left, and rebuilt almost all of it; Goku is surprised (`C245`). Salvage could reconcile those source episodes, but actual recovery and complete footage were not inspected. Do not call episode 46 an intact discovery or a proved contradiction; the preserved branch machine is its own altered fact.
- Goshin holds four pieces of falling rock with ki while using his existing wave to turn a separate falling slab onto an empty slope; he then lowers the held pieces away from the family. This is an actual physical branch event, not a numeric measurement. Chapter 18's prior large-wall feat remains a mental experience; Chapter 15 already showed four real stones plus a wave.
- Ox-King appears at the house in the morning, plans to mend the gate and sees the boys demonstrate their practice that evening. His prior Chapter 18 location and his later off-page movements are not assumed; the revision does not show him learning about the machine.
- In a telephone call the children confirm Pigero's allowed visit; permanent room arrangements are still unresolved. Hatch's original and the unfinished library drawing remain with Gohan, the larger drawing with the children, and Mina's bridge book with C-6 until he finishes. No new permission, Senzu transfer or C-6 repair is asserted.
- Goku has reached the little planet and met Bubbles; he has heard a blue man's voice and asked if he is King Kai. The family is not shown learning this. Both boys retain tails; Goshin's eventual first Great Ape must be aware and controlled from the start, but its timing is not fixed.
