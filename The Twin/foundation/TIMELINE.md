# THE TWIN — timeline and scene order

**Owns: chronology and relative scene order for the selected review branch.**

**Scope:** The selected Chapters 1–3 received substantive immersion rewrites, Chapter 4 extensive revisions, and Chapters 5–25 targeted revisions; their selected wording remains for review. An earlier hold is not a permanent ban on later requested corrections. The separately preserved pre-correction Chapter 20 is author-accepted; its selected corrected version is unaccepted. Acceptance of the earlier version does not accept earlier drafts. Chapters 21–30 are later unaccepted review candidates, not the accepted handoff. Events absent from a chapter remain unknown. The selected guidebook date of 12 October, Age 761 for Raditz's arrival is contested; neither is a verified on-page date. Chapter 20 shows three weeks of Goku's Bubbles training, not an exact number of days since Raditz or a Saiyan arrival countdown.

**Author direction and latest review staging, 3 November:** Goshin is to train for one full year **inside** the physical Room of Spirit and Time shortly before the Saiyans arrive; about one outside day passes (`O155`, `C217`). The revised unaccepted Chapter 22 first places an authored several-month outside interval after Goku's direct-lesson handoff in Chapter 21 and says nearly a year has passed since Raditz's warning (`O179`); this is not a source-proven exact count. Goku has practised both techniques, the family has maintained contact with the children, and their shared room has been renewed temporarily. Then Goshin and Gohan complete the continuous chamber year together while Chi-Chi stays outside. Both boys grow; the Saiyans have not landed at the Chapter 22 endpoint. Unaccepted Chapter 23 stages their landing later the same morning as the boys' exit. This is not Chapter 18's mental room, and it had not happened at the Chapter 19, 20 or 21 checkpoints. Before the landing, no exact arrival forecast is known on Earth; no present birthday or precise age is established.

Chapters 6–9 have targeted scene extensions and sentence revisions; their established sequence and outcomes below remain. At that earlier checkpoint Chapters 10–14 added source footers only, and Chapter 15 added a witness line; later contextual edits supersede that limited scope.
