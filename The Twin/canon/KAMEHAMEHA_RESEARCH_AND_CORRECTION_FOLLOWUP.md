## Snake Way: investigation, not a disguised fix

The audit identified a genuine presentation tension. Chapters 7 and 14 call the road's length a traditional **approximate** one million kilometres. In Chapter 23 Goku tells his family that he will return as fast as he can and starts urgently, while the Saiyans' landing, the battle, civilian response and a portion of the aftermath occur across Chapters 23–28 before he arrives. The author's standing working interpretation places earlier Goku at or above light speed; it is not an exact measurement that proves he always maintains that speed. If the approximate road were treated as exactly 1,000,000 km and covered at an average 299,792 km/s, travel alone would be about **3.34 seconds**. That calculation is a consistency diagnostic, not proof of a precisely measured road, a compulsory average velocity or an actual physical impossibility.

Possible substantial editorial approaches include: (a) revise the departure and the exact timing of King Kai's warning *and* the on-page intercuts so that the threat is understood at a different point, with all consequences made explicit; or (b) let Goku arrive earlier and revise who acts during the battle, civil rescue and family reunion across Chapters 23–28 and their continuity records. Either requires reconstructing the whole sequence so Gohan and Goshin retain meaningful independent victories and growth and Goku acts plausibly. Simply changing the road's reported distance, imposing an afterlife speed ceiling, inserting an unearned obstacle or time dilation, or deleting the urgent promise would hide or replace the problem rather than establish an earned solution. No such retcon is made in this pass. **OPEN-40 remains open for a coordinated, author-reviewable repair.**
