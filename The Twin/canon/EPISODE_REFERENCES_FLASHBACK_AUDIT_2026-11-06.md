# Episode references, flashbacks and prior decisions — 6 November 2026

**Owns: the scope, evidence and handoff for the local episode-reference and flashback correction.** All 30 selected chapters remain unaccepted; a source check or seal is not author approval.

## The author's direction

- A reader-visible anime episode reference belongs immediately after each chapter title. Name the material actually used, and qualify selected or brought-forward elements.
- There is **no one-chapter/one-episode rule**. A chapter may draw naturally from several episodes, and one episode may run through multiple chapters. Let the pacing, characters and consequences decide the division; do not omit scenes or force a break to satisfy a mapping.
- Dramatize supported source scenes, including memories and flashbacks, rather than quietly reducing them to a sentence. Distinguish characters' private memories from what others see or know.
- This is the living-character twin branch. Preserve Goku, Kami, Piccolo, Vegeta and Nappa as established; do not import source deaths, resurrections or other outcomes whose causes are absent. Goshin's first Great Ape experience is conscious and controlled, with substantial real retained growth; Gohan retains his independent choices. Current Goshin is Earth-destruction capable, and the author's extraordinary-speed framework applies without invented conversions or a combined-wave comparison.
- Chapter Three's external-camera restriction remains specific to that chapter. The compulsory repetition, food-watching, downhill, inspection and invitation habits in some historical plans were assistant inventions, not standing author instructions. Do not restore them as rules.

## Evidence, and what it cannot establish

The project has directly read retained Japanese captions for relevant episodes, with broadcaster origin and cut unverified. Captions capture spoken cues, not complete silent action, timing, music or performance. The earlier project count of 125 distinct full spoken-caption reads does not increase merely because a passage is reread. Official synopsis pages are brief; secondary pages and isolated stills are not a full audiovisual review.
