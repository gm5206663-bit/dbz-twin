## 12 · The gate — the one thing he cannot have

**He cannot achieve Super Saiyan God as a form. There is no form for him.**

The official **Super episode-9 synopsis** says five righteous Saiyans pour their hearts into a sixth righteous Saiyan, with Videl's unborn child supplying the needed Saiyan (`C117`, `W-100`). A secondary source separately reports a legend and time limit. This is not a full-episode or film review, and details may differ by version. For this serial, the idea of a mortal temporarily borrowing godhood is an **authored analogy**, not a universal canon rule about all later god-ki users.

**His Talent does not give it back.** Godly ki is not a bigger version of what he already has — it is a different substrate — and the Talent's one talent is that it **makes native what it adapts to**. So the ritual would not hand him a transformation. It would adapt, become a **divine** talent, and promote his **baseline**. **His base form would become Super Saiyan God** — permanently, at rest, with nothing to switch off.

| | ordinary ki | godly ki |
|---|---|---|
| what it is | an amplification of his base | a different substrate |
| what he can do with it | **enter and leave it** — the Talent makes it cheap and quick | **nothing. He can only become it** |
| the result of the ritual | — | **no transformation, no time limit, no way down** |

**It is a door, and it opens once, late.** In this serial his god state would be a *promotion*, and that authored promotion cannot be undone. So the gate is a **timing rule**, not a ban: **no god ki in the arcs we are writing** — and when the serial finally reaches that era, the crossing is **one event, one direction, and a change from mortal to divine in the project’s classification**. That classification does not by itself establish literal immortality or invulnerability. **It is not the end of him** (§14). After it, **Super Saiyan God is not a form he takes. It is his base** (§13). **Write the refusal for a hundred chapters. Write the crossing once, and never write it as a power-up** — and never write it as a loss of self (§14).

**Why it can matter:** the official Super episode-9 synopsis describes the ritual in terms of righteous Saiyans (`C117`, `W-100`); this serial separately establishes that Nimbus carries him (`O023`). That is not a measured ranking of every heart or proof of an inevitable invitation. If the family ever considers asking him, his response, relationships and reasons must arise from the story rather than an invented single fear (§7).

**What it does not touch:** before that crossing, his own line — Super Saiyan and whatever he earns above it — uses ordinary ki. His later perfected-rung presentation is separately ruled in §13. Ultra Instinct is a distinct technique/state from this project’s proposed God 4; its full canon mechanics vary by depiction. The project’s future ladder reserves a separate God 4 road rather than treating it as automatic possession of Ultra Instinct or Ultra Ego.

On that proposed ladder: he can be sensed at God 1, tracked at God 2, and heard coming at God 3. The earlier suggestion that he necessarily hates this visibility was assistant-authored, not a verified general emotional rule; how he responds belongs to the eventual story.

**GOD 4 — the authored crown and quieter signature.** The future project design imagines the ape body containing the divine base, with the leakage stopping: **fur as a vessel**. Its quieter energy signature is a proposed character-specific feature, not a canon rule or loss of speech or emotion. The earlier plan proposed calm instinct and a damage-fed rage as two expressions of the eventual form. The provenance of those specific mechanisms is unverified; they are not an obligatory mood switch or a description of his present personality. The form remains future and unwritten.

**The project calls it an Ultra-class crown, but it is not canon's.** “Ultra class” is project comparison language, not an established franchise taxonomy or a numerical equality to Ultra Instinct or Ultra Ego. His is his own proposed road; specific emotional or borrowed Ultra mechanics are not settled.

**Order matters.** The physical apex comes **first**, while he is still a boy with a tail. The crossing comes **late**. **God 4 is the last thing in the ladder and the only form in this serial that nobody else has a road to.**

**And the later golden and divine ladder has not happened yet.** Goshin is a child with growing hybrid abilities, including Saiyan-linked traits; his exact age after the outside interval and chamber year is not fixed. Great Ape is Saiyan biology, not an early golden or divine form, and is not barred by the era rule. His first aware, controlled Great Ape is staged in unaccepted Chapter 26 when he deliberately looks at the artificial moon; later paths remain unwritten.

## 14 · After the crossing — everything, promoted

**The Talent itself becomes divine, and it works on everything.** That is the whole content of the crossing: not a form gained, but a **substrate changed**, permanently, in every part of him at once.

| what it touches | what happens |
|---|---|
| his ki | divine at rest. There is no ordinary setting any more |
| his forms | every rung — Rage, Super Saiyan 4, God 1·2·3, God 4 — now runs on divine qi (O042) |
| his techniques | whatever he learns from that day is learned **divine**, first time, because the substrate is what he learns on |
| **his children** | **born divine, not mortal.** His line does not start at the ritual; it starts at him (O044) |

**And nothing is subtracted. This is the ruling that matters most on the page.**

A secondary source describes Super Saiyan God as cool-headed but also reports anger; it does not establish that canon godhood erases emotion or identity (`C119`). **His authored promotion preserves his identity and full emotional capacity** (`O041`, `O043`); calm is still possible when the scene warrants it:

- **He keeps his emotional life.** Anger, tenderness, laughter and other reactions remain possible; no single expression is compulsory.
- **He keeps his appetite, his jokes, his grudges, his grief.** He eats like a Saiyan at a divine substrate. He cries at the same things.
- **He keeps being wrong.** A god with a child's filing system and a mother's rules, still guessing.
- **He keeps loving people who can die.** That is the one that will hurt, and it is not a power.

**The continuity principle:** the planned crossing does not erase his identity or capacity for feeling (O043). A particular scene may still have him calm, serious, grieving, playful or distant for its own reasons.

**What it does not do.** No Blue (`§13`). No borrowed Ultra Instinct, no Ultra Ego — his Ultra class is God 4 and it is his own. No new rungs beyond the ones already named. **No automatic victory or numeric ceiling follows:** the future divine substrate is not a guarantee of any particular fight outcome.

## 15 · What none of it may mean

- **No free numeric boost from observation.** Training and adaptation can support growth; a demonstrated technique does not grant an arbitrary instant reading (O004).
- **Purity is not judgement.** The cloud certifies his heart, not his aim. He can be wrong all afternoon with a clean heart — **the cloud and the scouter disagree, and both are telling the truth.**
- **No meta-knowledge** (R6). He knows what that mountain knows.
- **Mastery is attainable.** The former claim that nothing he has can be finished is withdrawn: he works to master or perfect existing abilities first and retains what he has achieved (`O104`, `R-26`). His Talent supports rapid learning and mastery; fatigue or injury may affect a performance without erasing what he knows.
- **No god ki early.** Not a taste of it, not a spark, not "for a second" — until the crossing, which happens once (§12), and after which it is not a form but a floor (§13).
- **No Blue, ever.** This is Goshin’s authored structural rule: his perfected first rung is internal and his godly base is adapted permanently (§13). It is not a universal statement about every canonical Blue user.
- **He retains what he has learned** (O041), while injury, fatigue, suppression and distraction can interrupt performance; this does not erase the skill. Future divine-promotion rules remain as separately ruled.
- **He never announces a rung, and the narrator never numbers one.** No "God 3". The page may show the authored energy signature and its consequences without imposing a fixed mood, and lets the reader follow the action.
- **Nothing here is recited in prose.** This is a ledger. A chapter shows one line of it and says nothing about the rest.

## 16 · The brothers — capability and agency

**Standing authored direction:** Goshin is never less capable than Gohan (`O056`). Make this visible through effective action, learning, technique and growth—not a fixed hierarchy borrowed from a single number. Temporary fatigue, injury, suppression or a tactical mistake can affect what either child does in a scene without erasing learned capability.

**Gohan grows too.** His **1** after the Raditz outburst is a momentary low reading, not a permanent resting state. In the original arc his enraged Masenko reads **2,800** and the later Namek cue is interpreted in context as his attack exceeding **20,000 for an instant** (`C146`); neither is a permanent ceiling. The original six-month survival period and direct Piccolo training differ from this branch, where he trains with Goshin and others and then lives another full chamber year (`C130`, `C239`, `O166`). Dende’s healing and Vegeta’s explicit observation of a recovery-linked increase belong to the later original arc (`C144`, `C146`). Gohan learns, trains, chooses and contributes independently.

**Goshin grows too.** The ~400 island scan is a snapshot. His exceptional whole-being Talent and developing hybrid inheritance support cumulative growth in physical ability, ki, recovery, combat and skill through life, training, fighting and experience (`O004`, `O058`). His growth in a fight can be especially rapid and substantial. Do not write him as a static reading, a child with no techniques, or invulnerable to every method he has encountered.

This comparison is the user's serial rule, **not** a canonical law ranking him above Goten, Trunks, Piccolo, Nappa or all opponents (`O060`). Both brothers matter to the family, and neither is merely the other's prop. Let changed choices determine changed outcomes.
