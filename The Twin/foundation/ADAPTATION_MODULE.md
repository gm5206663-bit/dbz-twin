## 6. Progression law, not a plot promise

**Growth mechanism.** His extraordinary supernatural Talent continuously supports his entire living being. Saiyan/human constitution, maturing bloodline, ki, existing adaptations, experience and choices work together. Life, practice, fighting, environmental exposure, sleep and recovery are all relevant; observation and copying are only some inputs. The Talent develops with Goshin, and consolidated gains supply the foundation for later gains. It need not raise raw output every moment to remain active.

**Combat and recovery.** The author described his growth in a fight as “terrifying when fight”: allow rapid, substantial battle development in strength, ki, speed, response and skill rather than limiting growth to better technique. Distinguish that actual development from releasing existing reserves or concentrating a technique; a scouter cannot determine the cause by itself. Anger and being pushed hard are possible circumstances, not mandatory triggers; growth is not confined to extreme situations, a fixed rate or a numerical ceiling (`O188`). Recovery can be unusually fast and may also consolidate gains. A Saiyan recovery-linked power increase after serious injury is distinct from healing; the Raditz knockout does not by itself prove near death or an already-completed Zenkai. Neither injury nor a forced defeat is needed for growth.

**Bloodline and transformations.** His whole Saiyan–Earthling hybrid bloodline becomes purer, deeper and stronger through his developing Adaptation Talent as he grows, and his body, inherited traits and Great Ape can evolve with him (`O230`, `O243`). His first ape is staged conscious and controlled from the beginning in unaccepted Chapter 26. Its lived experience already contributes substantially to his normal-body strength and ki (`O214`). A separate ordinary-sized mode and combinations with anger or, once earned, Super Saiyan remain available directions whose precise mechanics and timing are not shown; do not impose a mandatory delay. Do not reduce the Earthling heritage to a passive remainder, prescribe a permanent multiplier, or import Broly's identity or loss of control.

**Training and time.** In deliberate training he prioritizes mastery of existing abilities and serious foundations; instinctive emergency copying is permitted. His Talent may refine, combine or contribute to new compatible abilities. Adaptation to a new condition or capability takes time, including the author's space-survival example (`O228`); that time can be short or long as appropriate, and no fixed duration or compulsory slow stage is imposed. Do not erase already-developed abilities or deny rapid learning and substantial combat growth. The directed year inside the physical Room of Spirit and Time is a full year of his own training and development, not a year outside or Chapter 18's mental exercise. It produces substantial shown gains in the unaccepted Chapter 22 candidate; the Saiyans have not landed at the Chapter 22 endpoint; unaccepted Chapter 23 stages their arrival later the same morning. The boys' first physical-room year uses roughly half of the stated cumulative lifetime room allowance; that environmental rule is not a power cap (`C234`, `O163`). No new form or numerical reading is asserted (`O155`, `O165`).

**Use in future scenes.** Preserve prior gains and let meaningful accumulated growth change what he can actually do, including raw power, not only how precisely he repeats a small task. At the post-chamber checkpoint both boys comfortably outclass this era's Piccolo in an ordinary spar, as the author specifically corrected (`O181`); do not treat that checkpoint as a permanent ranking over every later fighter. Track the context and cause, not a mandatory numerical schedule. Gohan continues growing with his own agency; Goshin is never less capable than him. No fixed ceiling, constant automatic boost, imposed immunity, arbitrary overload or forced canon defeat follows from this guidance.
